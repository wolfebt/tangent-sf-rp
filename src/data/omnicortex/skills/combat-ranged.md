# Ranged

The skill of using muscle-powered projectile weapons and thrown implements to strike targets at a distance.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility
- **Standard Attack Roll**: `2d10 + Ranged Rank + Agility Mod vs. Target Defense`.
- **Damage**: Weapon Base Damage Dice. (Strength modifier added to Thrown weapons, and composite bows up to weapon rating).
- **Range Increments**: Attacking beyond the weapon's base range increment imposes a cumulative `-2` penalty per increment.

### Specialties
- **Bows**: Traditional and high-tension compound bows.
- **Crossbows**: Crank and lever-operated mechanical projectile weapons.
- **Throwing Blades**: Weighted knives, throwing stars, and axes.
- **Boomerangs**: Aerodynamic returning and non-returning hunting clubs.
