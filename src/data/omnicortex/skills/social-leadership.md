# Leadership

Inspiring, organizing, motivating, and directing teams, military squads, corporate divisions, and ship crews under extreme pressure.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma
- **Inspire Allies**: DC 15 grants all allies within hearing a **+1 Morale Bonus** to saving throws and checks for 1 hour.
- **Rally Broken Troops**: DC 20 suppresses fear, panic, or confusion conditions among subordinates.
