# Bluff

The skill of lying convincingly, fabricating pretexts, disguising intent, fast-talking, forging credentials, and combat feinting.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma
- **Deceive Target**: Opposed check vs. target's **Insight**.
- **Combat Feint**: Standard Action in melee. Opposed check vs. target's **Insight** (or Defense). Success denies the target their Agility Defense bonus on the attacker's next strike.
- **Create Distraction**: DC 15 diverts guards' attention momentarily (allowing allies to slip past into Stealth).
