# Heavy Weapons

The operation of crew-served, vehicular, and squad-level heavy kinetic ordnance, including machine guns, rocket launchers, and artillery.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility (or Intellect for computed trajectory systems).
- **Launched Ordnance**: Direct targeting for grenades, mortars, and missiles.
- **Area Suppression (Blasting)**:
  - **General Area**: Targets within blast radius take base damage. For every **5 points** the attack roll exceeds **CR 10**, damage increases by **+2 Dice**.
  - **Focused Blast Zone**: Targets in the direct epicenter take **+100% Damage Dice** if attack roll exceeds **CR 15** by 10 or more.

### Specialties
- **Machine Guns**: Heavy suppression automatic weapons.
- **Rocket Launchers**: Shoulder-fired and vehicle-mounted missile systems.
- **Grenade Launchers**: Indirect and direct explosive projectile systems.
- **Vehicle Mounted**: Heavy kinetic cannons, autocannons, and gatlings.
