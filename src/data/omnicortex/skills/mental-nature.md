### **NATURE**

* **Ability:** Intellect (or Wisdom for practical application)  
* **Description:** Nature is the skill of understanding and interacting with the natural world. It reflects a character's ability to survive in the wilderness and understand the ecology of different environments. Nature is used whenever a character needs to navigate through the wilderness, whether it's to hunt for food or to avoid danger. This can include things like tracking animals, identifying plants, and surviving in extreme environments.   
* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Identify Creature:** CR 10 \+ Creature's Rarity/CR.  
  * **Foraging/Remedies:** CR 15 to find herbal antidotes or food.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Alien Botany:** Identifying toxic, medicinal, and nutritional properties of wild planetary plants.  
    * **Zoology & Beast Behavior:** Predicting predator attack vectors, nesting grounds, and pack dynamics.  
    * **Geology & Speleology:** Analyzing rock strata, seismic fault lines, mineral stability, and cavern layouts.  
    * **Meteorology & Weather Forecasting:** Predicting dangerous ionic storms, hurricanes, acid rain, and sudden blizzards.  
    * **Marine & Aquatic Ecology:** Navigating coastal shoals, identifying predatory sea life, and assessing tides.  
    * **Toxicology Sourcing:** Extracting potent venom, paralyzing secretions, and irritants from wild creatures.  
    * **Biome Tracking:** Identifying disturbed ground, broken twigs, scat, and animal territory scent posts.  
    * **Ecosystem Modeling:** Assessing ecological collapses, invasive species introductions, and bio-cycles.  
    * **Spelunking & Underground Survival:** Finding breathable air pockets, navigating cave-ins, and finding subterranean water.  
    * **Micro-Ecology & Spores:** Detecting airborne fungal infestations, micro-parasites, and spore clouds.
