# Nature

Knowledge of planetary biospheres: flora, fauna, geology, meteorology, ecosystem dynamics, and identifying toxic or beneficial alien life.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect (Scientific Theory) or Wisdom (Practical Ecology).
- **Identify Creature / Plant**: DC 10 + Creature CR/Rarity to recall weaknesses, behavior, and traits.
- **Foraging Herbal Remedies**: DC 15 to locate natural antiseptics, poisons, or edible biomass.
