# Salvager

Identifying, evaluating, breaching, and extracting valuable tech components and scrap metal from derelict ships, ruins, and battlefields.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom or Intellect
- **Salvage Valuation**: DC 15 estimates the scrap and component resale value of a derelict hull.
- **Delicate Extraction**: DC 20 strips a sensitive hyperdrive core or sensor array without damaging it.
