# Arcane

Arcane theory, esoteric lore, magical traditions, runes, and occult phenomena.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect
- **Discipline Focus**: Universal Arcana
- **Standard Check**: `2d10 + Arcane Rank + Intellect Mod vs. Target DC / Resistance`.
- **Prerequisite**: Unlocked when character possesses the **Awakened** feature or relevant Metaphysical discipline training.
- **Strain Resolution**: Failing an invocation check by 5+ points deals non-lethal strain fatigue.

### Specialties
- **Runic**: Runic Inscriptions
- **Planar**: Planar Cosmology
- **Esoteric**: Esoteric Relics
- **Magical**: Magical Traditions
- **Aetheric**: Aetheric Sigils
