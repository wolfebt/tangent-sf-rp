# Oratory

Public speaking, political rhetoric, delivering rallying speeches, pulpit preaching, courtroom advocacy, and commanding large crowds.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma
- **Inspire Audience**: DC 15 grants listeners a **+1 Morale bonus** to Willpower saving throws for 1 hour.
- **Persuade Mass Gathering**: Opposed check vs. the crowd's collective skepticism to sway public opinion on a crucial vote.
