# Language

Fluency in spoken, written, and signed languages, dialect translation, deciphering ancient scripts, and xenolinguistics.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect
- **Fluency Ranks**: Rank 1-2 (Broken basics), Rank 3-5 (Conversational / Fluent), Rank 6+ (Technical / Native accent).
- **Decipher Script**: DC 20 (Simple cipher) to DC 30+ (Ancient alien runic dialect).
