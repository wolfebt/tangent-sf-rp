# Dancing

Rhythmic physical expression to music: classical ballroom, ballet, club freestyle, traditional folk dances, and zero-G choreographies.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma (Expression) or Agility (Kinetic Technique).
- **Performance Evaluation**: Check result determines audience acclaim and social prestige.
- **Courtly Partnering**: DC 15 expertly leads a dance partner, granting them a **+2 bonus** to their own Social checks during the event.
