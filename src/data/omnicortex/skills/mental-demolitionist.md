# Demolitionist

The controlled handling, manufacture, placement, and defusal of explosive charges, shaped breaching ordnance, and structural blasting.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect (Trained Only).
- **Set Breaching Charge**: DC 15 cleanly blows a vault door without damaging contents; DC 25 brings down a multi-story building into its own footprint.
- **Defuse Explosives**: Base DC determined by device complexity (DC 20+). Failing by 5+ prematurely detonates the bomb.
