# Religion

Knowledge of galactic mythologies, sacred scriptures, divine pantheons, cosmic cults, holy relics, and religious ceremonies.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect
- **Identify Sacred Symbol / Rite**: DC 10 (Mainstream church) to DC 25 (Clandestine void cult).
- **Theological Debates**: Supported by Diplomacy or Oratory when interacting with devout factions.
