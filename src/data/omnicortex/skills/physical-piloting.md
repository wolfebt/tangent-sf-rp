# Piloting

The operation, handling, and tactical maneuvering of surface ground vehicles, aircraft, submersibles, mecha walkers, and starships.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility (Reflex / Evasive Maneuvers) or Intellect (Navigation & Ship Systems).
- **Standard Travel**: No check required for routine operation under benign conditions.
- **Combat & Stunts**: DC 15 (High-speed evasive turn), DC 25 (Threading asteroid gaps or tight urban canyons).
- **Dogfighting**: Opposed Piloting checks determine positioning, firing arcs, and evasion.

### Specialties
- **Ground Vehicles**: High-speed highway chases and rough terrain crawling.
- **Aircraft**: Atmospheric dogfighting and precision landing.
- **Spacecraft**: Orbital insertion, docking, and vacuum combat.
- **Mecha**: Gyroscopic balance and walker tactical positioning.
- **Watercraft**: Sub-surface nautical navigation.
