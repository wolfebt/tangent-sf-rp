### **Barter**

* **Ability:** Charisma (or Wisdom)  
* **Description:** Barter is the skill of trading goods and services without relying solely on currency. It reflects a character's ability to negotiate value, haggle for better prices, and reach mutually beneficial agreements in trade. Barter is used whenever a character needs to acquire items, services, or information through direct exchange. This includes assessing the relative value of goods, identifying market trends, and convincing others of the worth of a trade. The art of negotiation and exchange.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Negotiate:** Opposed check vs. the target's **Diplomacy** or **Insight**.  
  * **Appraise:** CR 15+ to determine the true value or rarity of an object or service.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Street Market Haggling:** Whittling down the asking price of street vendors and cantina fences credit by credit.  
    * **Bulk Discount Negotiation:** Securing large percentage-off price breaks when buying weapon crates or fuel tons in bulk.  
    * **Non-Monetary Trade Swapping:** Negotiating equal-value item-for-item swaps when credits are useless or unavailable.  
    * **Margin Squeezing:** Forcing suppliers to drop shipping, packaging, and handling fees from commercial sales.  
    * **Trade Goods Appraisal:** Accurately assessing what your own carried loot is realistically worth to a local buyer.  
    * **Closing the Deal:** Reading the precise moment a buyer or seller is on the fence and locking down the transaction.  
    * **Credit Terms & Financing:** Bargaining low interest rates and extended repayment timelines on borrowed capital.  
    * **Collateral Negotiation:** Convincing loan sharks or merchants to accept ships, deeds, or cyberware as trade collateral.  
    * **Pawn Valuation:** Squeezing the maximum instant credit payout from pawn brokers for stolen or hot goods.  
    * **Surplus Liquidating:** Dumping unwanted bulk inventory quickly onto frontier merchants without taking a total loss.
