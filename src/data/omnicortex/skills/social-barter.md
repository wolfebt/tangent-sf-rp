# Barter

The art of direct trade and negotiation: haggling prices, evaluating exchange parity, and structuring deals without cash currency.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma or Wisdom
- **Haggling Negotiations**: Opposed check vs. merchant's **Diplomacy** or **Insight**. Margin of success reduces purchase cost or increases sale value (typically by 10-30%).
- **Frontier Trade Parity**: DC 15 calculates fair trade ratios for commodities in non-currency economies.
