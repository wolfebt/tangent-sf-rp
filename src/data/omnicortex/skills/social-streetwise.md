# Streetwise

Navigating the criminal underworld, finding black markets, picking up street rumors, avoiding gang turf wars, and locating underworld fixers.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma or Wisdom
- **Informal Information Gathering**: DC 10 (Local gossip) to DC 20 (Targeted syndicate intelligence); requires 1d4 hours in local cantinas or back alleys.
- **Locate Safe House / Black Market**: DC 15 finds illicit merchants, fences, or unmonitored hideouts.
