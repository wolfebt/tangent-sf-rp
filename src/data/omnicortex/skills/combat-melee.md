# Melee

Proficiency with handheld close-quarters weaponry, from simple clubs and combat knives to energized power swords and heavy polearms.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Strength (Standard) or Agility (with Features like *Weapon Finesse*).
- **Standard Attack Roll**: `2d10 + Melee Rank + Strength Mod vs. Target Defense`.
- **Damage**: Weapon Base Damage Dice + Strength Modifier.
- **Reach Weapons**: Polearms and extended hafted weapons allow melee attacks from 10ft away.

### Specialties
- **Knives**: Lightweight concealable blades, daggers, and vibro-knives.
- **Swords**: Balanced bladed weapons including shortswords, broadswords, and mono-molecular katanas.
- **Staves**: Blunt wooden, composite, or energized pole weapons.
- **Axes/Hammers**: Heavy cleaving axes and crushing hammers optimized for breaking armor.
