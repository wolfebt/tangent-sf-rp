# Intimidate

Coercing others through fear, physical menace, psychological pressure, interrogation, and aggressive displays of authority.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma (or Strength for physical threats).
- **Coercion**: Opposed check vs. target's **Insight** or **Willpower**. Success forces short-term compliance (target may become resentful/hostile afterward).
- **Demoralize in Combat**: Standard Action. Opposed check vs. target's **Insight** or **Willpower**. Success inflicts the **Shaken** condition (`-2` penalty to attack rolls and checks) for 1 round.
