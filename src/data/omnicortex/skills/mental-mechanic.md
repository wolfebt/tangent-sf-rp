# Mechanic

Repairing, servicing, overhauling, and jury-rigging mechanical moving parts in vehicles, starships, droids, and industrial machinery.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility or Intellect
- **Mechanical Repair**: DC 15 (Standard replacement) to DC 30 (Rebuilding an engine block from scrap).
- **Jury-Rig Emergency Fix**: DC 20 creates a temporary mechanical patch with scrap parts (functions for 1d6 hours).
