# Defense

The art of self-preservation in combat. Measures a character's ability to avoid being hit, deflect blows, and position advantageously to mitigate harm.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility
- **Passive Defense**: Rank is added directly to static **Defense Score** (`10 + Agility + Defense Rank + Armor/Size Modifiers`).
- **Active Defense (Reaction)**: In tactical situations (such as *Total Defense*), roll a Defense check to replace static Defense score or negate combat maneuvers (Trips, Grapples, Disarms).

### Specialties
- **Evasion**: Dodging, weaving, and utilizing agile footwork to avoid attacks entirely (common for unarmored combatants).
- **Shield**: Proper angle and bracing of handheld shields to block incoming melee and ranged strikes.
- **Weapon Block**: Deflecting incoming blows using one's own weapon in close quarters.
