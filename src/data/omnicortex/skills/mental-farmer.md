# Farmer

Cultivating crops, managing large-scale hydroponics facilities, soil science, livestock animal husbandry, and agricultural production.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom or Constitution
- **Harvest Optimization**: DC 15 produces abundant food yields despite adverse weather or soil conditions.
- **Soil & Flora Diagnostics**: DC 20 assesses terraforming viability and determines optimal crop rotations.
