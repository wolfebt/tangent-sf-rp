# Culinarian

The culinary arts: preparing nutritious and gourmet food, baking, brewing, catering state banquets, and preparing alien ingredients.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom or Agility
- **Prepare Gourmet Meal**: DC 10 (Standard field meal) to DC 30 (Legendary banquet providing temporary morale buffs to all who partake).
- **Identify Ingested Toxins**: DC 15 detects poisons or spoilage by smell and subtle taste.
