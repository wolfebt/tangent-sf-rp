# Laborer

Heavy physical labor: mining, construction, dock cargo handling, demolition, operating heavy industrial machinery, and physical endurance.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Strength or Constitution
- **Endurance Shift**: DC 15 works continuous double shifts under harsh conditions without suffering Fatigue.
- **Operate Heavy Exosuits**: DC 10 operates industrial forklifts, plasma cutters, and mining rigs.
