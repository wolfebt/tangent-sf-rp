# Endurance

Physical stamina, resilience against bodily fatigue, hunger, extreme environmental exposure, and sustained grueling exertion.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Stamina / Constitution
- **Fatigue Checks**: DC 10 + 2 per hour of continuous forced exertion or environmental hazard.
- **Holding Breath**: Base rounds equal to Constitution score; DC 15 check extends duration by +1 round per point over DC.
- **System Integration**: In Tangent 1.07 rules, endurance is frequently tested via Fortitude resistance checks and the Athletics skill; this entry provides dedicated standalone vocational specialization.
