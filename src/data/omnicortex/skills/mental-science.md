### **SCIENCE**

* **Ability:** Intellect  
* **Description:** Science is the skill of understanding and applying the scientific method. It reflects a character's ability to understand the natural world and use that knowledge to solve problems. Science is used whenever a character needs to understand the behavior of natural phenomena, whether it's to design an experiment or to solve a mystery. This can include things like designing experiments, analyzing data, and interpreting scientific theories.   
* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Lab Work:** CR 15 to perform experiments.  
  * **Identify Substance:** CR 15 to CR 25\.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Inorganic Chemistry:** Synthesizing acids, industrial catalysts, synthetic polymers, and inert shielding reagents.  
    * **Biochemistry:** Analyzing cellular metabolism, amino acid structures, and enzymatic reaction triggers.  
    * **Genetics & Cloning:** Splicing DNA, sequencing alien genomes, and managing cellular degradation in clones.  
    * **Material Science & Metallurgy:** Testing tensile strength, molecular lattice bonding, and alloy fatigue.  
    * **Nanotechnology:** Programming molecular assemblers, managing gray-goo dampeners, and repairing nano-swarms.  
    * **Laboratory Procedure:** Operating centrifuges, mass spectrometers, clean-room environments, and electron microscopes.  
    * **Atmospheric Chemistry:** Analyzing toxic air balances, terraforming gas mixtures, and greenhouse gas saturation.  
    * **Radiological Science:** Measuring rems, half-lives, radioactive isotope decay, and managing fission shielding.  
    * **Synthetic Biology:** Designing artificial bacteria to consume plastics, scrub hydrocarbons, or excrete fuels.  
    * **Applied Spectroscopy:** Analyzing distant planetary atmospheres and compound solutions using optical light bands.
