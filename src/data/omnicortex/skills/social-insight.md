# Insight

Social perception and empathy: reading emotional states, detecting lies, discerning hidden motives, and picking up on social cues.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom (or Charisma for active social maneuvering).
- **Opposed Sense Motive**: Opposed check vs. target's **Bluff**. Success reveals deception or concealed intentions.
- **Read Room Dynamics**: DC 15 assesses hidden alliances, hostilities, and unspoken power dynamics in a gathering.
