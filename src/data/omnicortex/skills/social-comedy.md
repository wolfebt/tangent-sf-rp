# Comedy

Humor, stand-up, satire, roasting, defusing tense social confrontations with laughter, and provoking enemies in combat.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma
- **Defuse Hostility**: DC 20 shifts a Hostile NPC to Indifferent if they possess a receptive sense of humor.
- **Combat Taunt**: Opposed check vs. target's **Willpower**. Success forces target to fixate attacks on you, suffering a penalty to tactical discipline.
