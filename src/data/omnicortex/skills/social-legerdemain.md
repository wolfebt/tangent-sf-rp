# Legerdemain

Sleight of hand, stage magic, card tricks, pickpocketing, palming small objects, escaping ropes, and concealing small blades.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility (Manual Dexterity) or Charisma (Misdirection).
- **Pickpocket**: DC 20 (or opposed by target's **Alertness**) to lift a credit chip or keycard unnoticed.
- **Conceal Weapon**: DC 15 hides a knife or holdout blaster on your person, setting the DC for physical search checks.
