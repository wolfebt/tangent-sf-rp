# Broker

Commercial mediation: matching buyers and sellers, negotiating commissions, black market dealing, real estate, and financial transactions.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom or Charisma
- **Market Appraisal**: DC 15 determines if a commodity is selling above or below planetary market value.
- **Brokering Black Market Deals**: DC 20 connects with a discreet buyer for stolen goods or contraband.
