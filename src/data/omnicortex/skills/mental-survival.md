### **SURVIVAL**

* **Ability:** Wisdom  
* **Description:** Survival is the skill of surviving in extreme environments. It reflects a character's ability to find food, water, and shelter in the wilderness, and to avoid danger. Survival is used whenever a character needs to survive in the wilderness, whether it's to explore a new area or to escape from danger. This can include things like finding food and water, building shelter, and avoiding predators.   
* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Track:** CR varies by surface (Soft ground CR 10, Hard floor CR 20+).  
  * **Survival:** CR 15 to provide food/water for self; \+2 CR per additional person.  
  * **Endure Elements:** Grants bonuses to Fortitude saves against weather.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Foraging:** Sourcing edible roots, insects, fruits, and clean sustenance in untamed wilderness.  
    * **Water Procurement:** Locating hidden springs, building solar condensation stills, and testing water toxicity.  
    * **Extreme Cold (Arctic):** Building snow shelters, preventing frostbite, and enduring sub-zero blizzards.  
    * **Arid Wasteland (Desert):** Conserving bodily moisture, traveling by night, and finding shaded oases.  
    * **Jungle & Marshland:** Evading quicksand, combating rot, avoiding parasitic leeches, and crossing bogs.  
    * **Emergency Shelter Construction:** Rigging lean-tos, debris huts, windbreaks, and insulated thermal bivouacs.  
    * **Zero-G & Vacuum Survival:** Patching suit punctures, surviving depressurization, and conserving suit oxygen.  
    * **Subterranean Survival:** Navigating lightless deep caves, avoiding bad air pockets, and scaling internal chasms.  
    * **Fire-Building:** Starting cooking and survival fires using flint, friction, or battery sparks in rain and wind.  
    * **Wilderness Traps & Snares:** Constructing deadfalls, snare wires, and pit traps to capture wild game for food.
