# Survival

The practical knowledge of enduring in harsh, uncivilized, or deadly wilderness environments: tracking, foraging, shelter construction, and weather reading.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom
- **Tracking**: DC 10 (Soft mud/snow) to DC 25 (Hard metal decking / rocky scree).
- **Sustenance Foraging**: DC 15 provides basic food and water for oneself for 1 day (+2 DC per additional person).
- **Endure Elements**: Grants circumstance bonuses to Fortitude saves against extreme temperatures.
