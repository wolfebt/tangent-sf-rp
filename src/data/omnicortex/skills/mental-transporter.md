# Transporter

The commercial movement of passengers and cargo: space trucking, transit shuttles, cargo loading, hazardous transport, and smuggling routes.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Agility or Wisdom
- **Load Balancing**: DC 15 secures hazardous or heavy cargo to maximize hold efficiency and prevent shifts during high-G burns.
- **Smuggling Route Navigation**: DC 15 identifies undetected shipping lanes bypassing customs patrol cordons.
