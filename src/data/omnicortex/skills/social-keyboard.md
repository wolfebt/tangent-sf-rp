### **Keyboard**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Keyboard is the skill of playing a keyboard instrument, such as a piano or synthesizer. It reflects a character's ability to create music using a keyboard instrument. Keyboard is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instrument interfaces involving keys, buttons, or haptic pads. In a Sci-Fi setting, this ranges from classic pianos to complex holographic synthesizers.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Composition:* Write a piece of music (Intellect \+ Keyboard).  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Acoustic Grand Piano:** Performing complex classical sonatas, jazz improvisations, and concertos on grand pianos.  
    * **Pipe & Cathedral Organ:** Operating multi-manual organ keyboards, foot pedals, and stops in massive echoic halls.  
    * **Electronic Synthesizer Workstations:** Programming oscillators, low-pass filters, and playing electronic synth leads.  
    * **Holographic Light-Keys:** Playing futuristic musical consoles composed of projected, floating hard-light keys.  
    * **Keytar & Stage Performance:** Playing portable keyboard instruments while moving aggressively across live stages.  
    * **Harpsichord & Early Instruments:** Performing delicate, plucking keyboard compositions from archaic eras.  
    * **Haptic Touch-Pads:** Playing responsive, velocity-sensitive silicone pads and matrix controller surfaces.  
    * **Accordion & Squeeze-Box:** Operating bellow-driven reed key boxes while managing rhythmic bass buttons.  
    * **Ambient Soundscape Design:** Generating lush, evolving chordal pads and atmospheric sonic backdrops live.  
    * **Microtonal Synthesizers:** Navigating alien tuning scales and non-standard pitch intervals on custom boards.
