# Electrician

Installing, troubleshooting, and repairing power grids, high-voltage conduits, sub-surface wiring, lighting circuits, and micro-electronics.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect or Agility
- **Bypass Electronic Locks**: DC 20 hotwires an electronic keypad or security door.
- **Reroute Power**: DC 20 restores auxiliary power to life support or weapons systems on a crippled starship.
