# Elemental

Energy discipline: Thermal, electrical, fire, frost, and plasma generation and channeling.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Wisdom
- **Discipline Focus**: Energy
- **Standard Check**: `2d10 + Elemental Rank + Wisdom Mod vs. Target DC / Resistance`.
- **Prerequisite**: Unlocked when character possesses the **Awakened** feature or relevant Metaphysical discipline training.
- **Strain Resolution**: Failing an invocation check by 5+ points deals non-lethal strain fatigue.

### Specialties
- **Thermal**: Thermal / Pyrokinesis
- **Cryokinesis**: Cryokinesis / Frost
- **Electro-kinesis**: Electro-kinesis
- **Plasma**: Plasma Jets
- **Atmospheric**: Atmospheric Discharge
