# Artificer

The specialized craft of combining technology with metaphysical energies to create enchanted devices, psi-amps, resonant crystals, and golems.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect or Wisdom (Trained Only; requires Awakened or Metascience training).
- **Imprint Metaphysical Item**: DC 20 + Spell/Invocation Level binds an invocation into a physical matrix.
- **Analyze Artifact**: DC 15+ identifies the charges, command words, and resonance properties of a magical/psionic relic.
