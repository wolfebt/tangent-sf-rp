# Artist

Creating visual and physical artwork: painting, holographic sculpting, digital illustration, body modifications, and aesthetic crafts.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma (Aesthetic Expression), Wisdom (Inspirational Insight), or Agility (Precision Brushwork).
- **Create Masterpiece**: Check result directly determines market value and cultural renown of the work.
- **Artistic Forgery**: Opposed check vs. **Appraisal** to forge a master's signature or visual style.
