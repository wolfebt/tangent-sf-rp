### **TELEPORT FOCUS**

Relocation without traversing the intervening space.

* **Mechanic:** Difficulty is based on the Familiarity of the target location.  
* **Familiarity CR:** Very Familiar (5), Seen Once (15), Description Only (25), Blind (30).

**Invocations:**

* **Blink:** Short-range tactical hop.  
  * *Effect:* Move up to 10ft \+ (5ft per Rank) as a Move Action. Does not provoke Attacks of Opportunity.  
* **Gate:** Opens a stable portal for others to pass through.  
  * *Effect:* Sustained portal. Size 5ft x 5ft per Rank.  
* **Teleport Other:** Sends a target to another location.  
  * *Effect:* Touch attack to hit. Resisted by Will. Range determined by Discipline Rank.  
* **Spatial Distortion:** Warps space to increase distance/defense.  
  * *Effect:* Adds \+1 Defense per 2 Ranks against Ranged attacks (displacement).
