# Navigation

Terrestrial pathfinding, nautical orienteering, celestial astrogation, star chart plotting, and calculating FTL hyperspace trajectories.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect (Astrogation & Hyperspace) or Wisdom (Surface Orienteering).
- **Plotting Course**: DC 10 (Chartered star lanes) to DC 25 (Uncharted void anomaly sectors).
- **FTL Astrogation Calculation**: DC scales with jump distance and vicinity of gravitational wells.
