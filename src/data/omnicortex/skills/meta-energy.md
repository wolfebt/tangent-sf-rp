# Energy

The metaphysical discipline governing the manipulation of raw energy states, kinetic force, elemental plasma, electricity, acoustic waves, and telekinesis.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Key Ability (Requires Awakened feature).
- **Damage Scaling**:
  - *Elemental Focus*: `1d6 per Discipline Stage + 1d6 per Invocation Rank` (Applies critical status conditions).
  - *Force Focus*: `1d8 per Discipline Stage + 1d8 per Invocation Rank` (Force Damage).

### Focuses
- **Elemental Focus**: Area bursts, piercing bolts, and elemental barriers.
- **Force Focus**: Telekinetic object manipulation, homing force missiles, and force shields.
