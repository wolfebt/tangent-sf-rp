Vocal music and vocal performance: classical opera, modern rock/pop vocals, traditional ballads, Gregorian chant, and harmonic throat singing.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma (Performance) or Constitution (Breath Control & Projection).
- **Vocal Projection**: DC 15 allows clear vocal communication over the roar of gunfire, machinery, or planetary storms.
