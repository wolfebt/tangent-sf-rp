# Weaponsmith

Forging, machining, assembling, calibrating, and modifying melee blades, ballistic firearms, energy blasters, and heavy weapons.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Intellect or Strength (Trained Only).
- **Weapon Repair**: DC 15 fixes jammed, corroded, or broken firing mechanisms.
- **Weapon Crafting**: DC 15 (Sword/Pistol) to DC 25 (Plasma Rifle or Heavy Railgun).
- **Custom Weapon Modification**: DC 20 installs scopes, hair-triggers, extended magazines, or silencers.
