# Diplomacy

The art of peaceful persuasion, formal etiquette, mediating disputes, building alliances, and shifting the attitudes of non-player characters.

### Mechanics & Rules
- **Governing Attribute**: Charisma
- **Influence Attitude**: Shifting NPC attitudes (Hostile -> Unfriendly -> Indifferent -> Friendly -> Helpful). DC 15 (Indifferent to Friendly) to DC 25+ (Hostile to Indifferent).
- **Formal Information Gathering**: DC 15 gathers intelligence through high-society galas and official embassies.
