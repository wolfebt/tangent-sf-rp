# Description

Humans are the baseline standard in the galaxy—adaptable, ubiquitous, and deeply ambitious. Originating from Earth, their relentless drive has pushed them across the stars. They are not the strongest, the smartest, or the longest-lived, but their sheer versatility makes them unparalleled survivors.

## Traits & Characteristics
- **Lifespan**: 80-150 years (depending on access to standard cybernetics/medicine).
- **Diet**: Omnivore. Capable of digesting a wide variety of synthetic and natural proteins.

## Mechanics

Humans receive 2 bonus skill points and 2 bonus feature points at character creation to represent their varied backgrounds and adaptability. They have no inherent biological penalties.
