# Space Tourism Conglomerate (Generic Faction Template)

**Category**: Generic Faction Templates  
**Type**: Modular Organizational Template

## Overview
Luxury orbital liner operators, pleasure casino chains, and zero-g theme resort management.

## Customization Guidelines
- Assign Tech Level (1-5) and Meta Level (1-5) based on campaign scale.
- Choose 20 SP across 4-6 appropriate skills matching the organization's focus.
- Select 1-2 Recommended Features reflecting the faction's primary trade.


## Extended Lore & Background

These factions are characterized by their focus on promoting and facilitating space tourism for recreational purposes and providing unique experiences for their customers.   
**Attitudes**    
Adventurous, customer-focused, profit-driven, entrepreneurial, innovative, customer-oriented  
**Goals**    
Provide unique experiences for customers, promote space tourism, make money, develop space tourism technology, create unique space experiences, attract wealthy clients,   
**Strengths**    
Access to advanced spacecraft, skilled pilots and tour guides, ability to adapt to changing circumstances, access to cutting-edge technology, strong marketing and branding, ability to cater to high-end clientele,   
**Weaknesses**    
Difficulty maintaining customer satisfaction, tendency to prioritize profit over safety, high costs, reliance on technology, potential for accidents or disasters,   
**Technology** 3+  
**Skills** Knowledge and Vocation  
**Features** 2 General or Skill Features

Thrill-Seeker  
Galactic Guide  
Hospitality Specialist  
Event Planner
