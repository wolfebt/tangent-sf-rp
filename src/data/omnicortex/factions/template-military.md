# Military Junta (Generic Faction Template)

**Category**: Generic Faction Templates  
**Type**: Modular Organizational Template

## Overview
Planetary garrison, rogue admiral's battlefleet, or martial council governing through martial law.

## Customization Guidelines
- Assign Tech Level (1-5) and Meta Level (1-5) based on campaign scale.
- Choose 20 SP across 4-6 appropriate skills matching the organization's focus.
- Select 1-2 Recommended Features reflecting the faction's primary trade.


## Extended Lore & Background

These factions are characterized by their focus on military power.   
**Attitudes**    
Patriotic, disciplined, loyal, hierarchical  
**Goals**    
Conquer new territories, defend their borders, defend their own territories, maintain order  
**Strengths**    
Well-trained soldiers, advanced weaponry, strong leadership  
**Weaknesses**    
Rigid thinking, lack of creativity, tendency to rely on brute force, vulnerability to guerrilla tactics, potential for corruption, inflexibility in strategy  
**Technology** 3  
**Skills** Vocation and Combat  
**Features** 2 Skill or Combat Features

Soldier
