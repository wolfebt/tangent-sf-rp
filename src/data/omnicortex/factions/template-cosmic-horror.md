COSMIC HORROR
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Factions characterized by their connection to incomprehensible, eldritch entities. They seek to uncover the secrets of cosmic horror, harness the powers of the unknown, and spread their fanatical beliefs, often aiming to bring about the end of the universe.
* Key Themes: Madness, Eldritch Entities, Forbidden Knowledge, Cults.
* Relationship to Others: Universally hostile or parasitic.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Cult of the Void / The Awakened
* Colloquialisms: The Mad Ones / The Doomsayers
* Archetype: Eldritch Cults
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Dark corners of space, ancient ruins)
* Driving Mandate: Summon or control eldritch entities, destroy all life, harness the unknown.
* Motto: "The end is a beautiful beginning."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Insane, fanatical, nihilistic, mysterious, secretive, obsessed.
* Social Structure: Cult hierarchies led by those most touched by madness.
* View on Outsiders: Sacrifices, non-believers, or ignorant fools.
* Law & Order: Dictated by the whispered commands of dark entities.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Access to forbidden/eldritch knowledge and powers, ability to manipulate fear, strong devotion to their cause.
* Weaknesses: Vulnerability to madness, difficulty communicating with non-believers, potential for infighting, self-destructive tendencies.
* Military Doctrine: Terror tactics, psychic warfare, and unleashing uncontrollable horrors.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Often mutated or corrupted).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Discipline (+5)
   * Occult (+5)
   * Intimidate (+4)
   * Survival (+3)
   * Stealth (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Cultist: A fanatical follower willing to die for the dark cause.
   * The Occultist: Uncovering forbidden lore to summon eldritch entities.
   * The Doomsayer: Preaching the end of the universe to terrify the masses.
   * The Corrupted: Physically mutated by exposure to void energies.
   * The Void-Caller: Manipulating reality through incomprehensible magic.
   * The Awakened: An individual whose mind has been opened to horrifying truths.
   * The Sacrifice: A willing martyr prepared to bleed for the summoning ritual.
   * The Harbinger: Leading the vanguard of an eldritch invasion force.
   * The Eldritch Scholar: Translating non-Euclidean texts and dark scriptures.
   * The Mad Prophet: Seeing the future through the lens of utter insanity.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Fearless (Madness): Too insane to be terrified by mundane threats.
   * Discipline Focus (Entropy): Mastering the decay and shadow magics of the Void.
   * Darksight: Seeing perfectly in the unnatural dark of the cosmos.
   * Iron Will: Unbreakable devotion to their eldritch master.
   * Unsettling Aura: Projecting a subliminal sense of dread and wrongness.
   * Pain Tolerance: Ignoring physical trauma due to psychic detachment.
   * Eldritch Resistance: Immunity to the mind-breaking effects of cosmic horror.
   * Veiled Threat: Terrifying others with whispered, incomprehensible truths.
   * Wild Speech: Communicating with mutated beasts and horrors of the dark.
   * Awakened: Mind forcefully opened to powerful, unstable psionics.
