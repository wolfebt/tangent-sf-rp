# Rebel Resistance Cell (Generic Faction Template)

**Category**: Generic Faction Templates  
**Type**: Modular Organizational Template

## Overview
Guerilla insurgents, underground saboteurs, and freedom fighters resisting imperial or corporate occupation.

## Customization Guidelines
- Assign Tech Level (1-5) and Meta Level (1-5) based on campaign scale.
- Choose 20 SP across 4-6 appropriate skills matching the organization's focus.
- Select 1-2 Recommended Features reflecting the faction's primary trade.


## Extended Lore & Background

These factions are characterized by their opposition to a dominant power.   
**Attitudes**    
Idealistic, passionate, determined, independent, resourceful, defiant  
**Goals**    
Overthrow oppressive regimes, fight for freedom, protect the oppressed, establish a new order, protect the weak  
**Strengths**    
Resourceful, adaptable, skilled at guerrilla warfare, strong sense of purpose, ability to inspire others  
**Weaknesses**    
Lack of resources, internal divisions, difficulty maintaining order, vulnerability to infiltration, potential for infighting  
**Technology** 3  
**Skills** Knowledge and Vocation  
**Features** 2 General or Combat Features

Revolutionary  
Guerrilla Fighter  
Saboteur
