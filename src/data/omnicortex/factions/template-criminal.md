# Criminal Syndicate (Generic Faction Template)

**Category**: Generic Faction Templates  
**Type**: Modular Organizational Template

## Overview
Underworld cartel, smuggler ring, drug lab network, or protection racket controlling black markets.

## Customization Guidelines
- Assign Tech Level (1-5) and Meta Level (1-5) based on campaign scale.
- Choose 20 SP across 4-6 appropriate skills matching the organization's focus.
- Select 1-2 Recommended Features reflecting the faction's primary trade.


## Extended Lore & Background

These factions are characterized by their involvement in illegal activities.   
**Attitudes**    
Ruthless, cunning, opportunistic, secretive  
**Goals**    
Accumulate wealth, power, or influence through illicit means  
**Strengths**    
Connections to other criminal organizations, access to illegal resources, skilled at deception and manipulation, ability to operate outside the law  
**Weaknesses**    
Vulnerability to law enforcement, internal power struggles, potential for betrayal, difficulty maintaining loyalty  
**Technology** 3  
**Skills** any Physical and Manipulation  
**Features** 2 Skill or General Features

Crime Boss  
Con Artist  
Thief  
Hacker
