ENVIRONMENTAL
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Militant and passionate factions deeply focused on environmental preservation. They actively oppose destructive industrial activities, promoting sustainability and protecting natural habitats through direct, often violent, action.
* Key Themes: Eco-Terrorism, Nature Reclamation, Guerrilla Warfare, Sustainability.
* Relationship to Others: Hostile to major industrial powers (Syndicate, Coalition); allied with indigenous or agrarian societies.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Gaia Front / The Green Vanguard
* Colloquialisms: Eco-Militants / Tree-Huggers (derogatory)
* Archetype: Eco-Militants
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Often pristine or recovering garden worlds)
* Driving Mandate: Protect the environment, promote sustainability, oppose destructive activities.
* Motto: "The earth bleeds; we are the tourniquet."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Environmentalist, idealistic, passionate.
* Social Structure: Decentralized cells or tribal communes.
* View on Outsiders: Judged solely on their ecological footprint.
* Law & Order: Natural law; crimes against the environment are capital offenses.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Deep knowledge of the environment, strong community bonds, skilled at guerrilla tactics.
* Weaknesses: Lack of heavy resources, difficulty communicating with industrial factions, vulnerability to superpower militaries.
* Military Doctrine: Sabotage, ambush, and leveraging local flora/fauna in combat.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Elves, Humans prevalent).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Nature (+5)
   * Survival (+5)
   * Stealth (+4)
   * Mechanics (Sabotage) (+3)
   * Athletics (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Eco-Warrior: A militant fighter protecting the wilds with lethal force.
   * The Shaman: Drawing upon the spiritual energy of the planet.
   * The Saboteur: Destroying corporate logging and mining equipment.
   * The Terraformer: Healing scarred planets using advanced biology.
   * The Beastmaster: Fighting alongside tamed xeno-fauna and predators.
   * The Grove Warden: Standing as the final defense for sacred ecological sites.
   * The Scavenger: Repurposing corporate trash into functional survival gear.
   * The Outrider: Patrolling the deep wilderness to track corporate incursions.
   * The Herbalist: Utilizing local flora for advanced, natural medicine.
   * The Radical Activist: Inspiring protests and uprisings on industrial worlds.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Naturalist: Unmatched understanding of deep ecology and survival.
   * Animal Affinity: Syncing emotionally with local predators and beasts.
   * Wild Speech: Communicating basic intents with the natural world.
   * Tracker: Hunting corporate mercenaries across miles of unbroken wilderness.
   * Self Sufficient: Never starving while in a living biosphere.
   * Camouflage: Blending seamlessly into jungle or forest environments.
   * Tolerance: Extreme resistance to natural toxins, spores, and venoms.
   * Danger Sense: Primal instincts warning of approaching bulldozers or mecha.
   * Environmental Resistance: Thriving in extreme heat, cold, or humidity.
   * Climber: Navigating canopy cities and sheer cliffs effortlessly.
