# Resonance Crystal (Meta-Tech Enhancement Catalyst)

Naturally polarized noospheric mineral crystal that vibrates in harmony with Conscious Light. Required to anchor permanent passive Meta-Tech Enhancements into physical Sockets or dermal tattoos.

**Mechanics:** Exotic Meta-Tech catalyst worth 2,560 Cr (CR 20). Required for Passive Enhancements and Imbued Tattoos.

**Notes:** Meta-Tech fabrication requires Resonance Crystals for Passive Enhancements (such as Energy Sheath, Ghost-Strike, or Featherweight), constituting 50% of the final item value.
