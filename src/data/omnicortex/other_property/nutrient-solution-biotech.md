# Biotech Nutrient Solution (1-Dose Sustenance Bath)

Bio-rich enzymatic nutrient suspension required to sustain grown Auluran and Kitin heavy weapons and living carapace armor.

**Mechanics:** Prevents Broken (Starving) condition on grown Biotech heavy weapons and armor for 1 week.

**Notes:** Grown heavy weapons and armor must be submerged in 1 dose of Nutrient Solution once per week, or they degrade and gain the Broken (Starving) condition.
