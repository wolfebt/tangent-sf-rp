# Kitin Hive-Resin Structural Composite

Secreted organic bio-resin composite harvested by the Kitin Collective. Lighter than plasteel, extraordinarily rigid, and naturally self-sealing against micro-breaches.

**Mechanics:** Kitin factional material. Grants Lightweight trait on armor or +2 Stealth (acoustic dampening) in structures.

**Notes:** Armor crafted with Hive-Resin gains the Lightweight trait; architectural bulkheads lined with Hive-Resin grant +2 to Stealth (sound dampening) inside them.
