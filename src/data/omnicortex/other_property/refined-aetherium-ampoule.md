# Refined Aetherium Ampoule (Meta-Tech Imbuement Catalyst)

Hermetically containment-sealed ampoule of refined Aetherium—the hyper-conductive metaphysical element capable of hard-coding active Invocations into technological hardware.

**Mechanics:** Exotic Meta-Tech catalyst worth 10,240 Cr (CR 25). Required for Active Imbuements and Aether-Node fabrication.

**Notes:** Required as 50% raw material value to fabricate Active Meta-Tech Imbuements (such as Blink Suits or Sun-Fire Vambraces) and TL 4 Aether-integrated power cores.
