# Raw Polymatter Substrate Canister (Mekan Liquid Metal)

A magnetic-lattice containment cylinder holding true TL 5 Polymatter—a programmable liquid-metal substrate existing in a state of hyper-potentiality that reacts instantaneously to neural or digital commands.

**Mechanics:** Apex TL 5 exotic substrate worth 40,960 Cr (CR 30). Enables Morphic equipment fabrication and Base Substance Exchange.

**Notes:** Used in Tier 5 Genesis fabrication and Base Substance Exchange to forge Morphic weapons, armor, and implants.
