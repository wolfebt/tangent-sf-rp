# Full Auto (Long)

**Category**: Combat Maneuvers  
**Action Type**: Full Action

## Mechanics
- **Ammunition**: 10+ rounds.
- **Recoil**: **-1 Recoil Penalty** per 10 rounds fired.
- **Range Penalties**: All Range Penalties are **Doubled**.
- **Iterative Hits**: For every point the Attack Roll exceeds the Defense DC, an **additional bullet hits** (up to total rounds fired).
- **Damage**: Initial hit deals normal weapon damage; each extra hit deals **+1d Damage** (only if initial hit penetrated armor DR).
