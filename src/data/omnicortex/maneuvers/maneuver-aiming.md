# Aiming

**Category**: Combat Maneuvers  
**Action Type**: Standard / Movement Action

## Mechanics & Edge Bonus
- **Requirement**: Attacker spends an action to steady their aim and lead the shot; attacker **cannot move before shooting**.
- **Bonus**: Gain **+2 to Strike per round of Aiming**.
- **Maximum**: Capped at **1/2 the attacker's Skill Level** (up to Agility x 2 + 2 for moving attacker checks).
