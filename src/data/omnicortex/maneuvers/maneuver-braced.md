# Braced

**Category**: Combat Maneuvers  
**Action Type**: Stance Action

## Mechanics & Edge Bonus
- Set to receive a Charge or to stay in place.
- **Block Counter**: Block usable as a counter attack on getting hit (or with a readied weapon).
- **Anti-Advance**: Bonus is gained against an **Advancing Target**.
- **Recoil & Stability**: Negates penalties for heavy weapon recoil and grants **Advantage against being tripped or shoved**.
