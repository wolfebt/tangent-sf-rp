# Called Shot

**Category**: Combat Maneuvers  
**Action Type**: Standard Action

## Mechanics
- Attack a specific anatomical location or component at a targeted strike penalty:
  - Head (-2), Neck/Throat (-4), Eyes/Ears/Nose (-5)
  - Torso (-1), Chest (-2), Abdomen (-2), Groin (-4)
  - Arms (-2), Upper Arm (-3), Forearm (-3), Hand (-4), Digits (-5)
  - Legs (-2), Thigh (-3), Shin (-3), Foot (-4), Digits (-5)
  - Tentacles/Wings/Fins (-2 to -4)
- **Trauma**: If successful, damage is applied directly to that location for the purpose of Disabling or Destroying it.
