# Merchant Occupation

**Category**: Occupations  
**Starting Skill Points**: 20 SP (allocated to Professional Skills, max Rank 11, recommended Rank 6)  
**Recommended Features**: Skill (1 BP Discount)  
**Starting Traits**: 2 Free selections (Additional traits cost 1 CP each)

## Description
A merchant's occupation would involve trading, negotiating, and exploring uncharted regions of space for profit and adventure. As a merchant, the character would be skilled in various aspects of commerce, diplomacy, and resource management. They would need to be able to assess the value of goods, negotiate prices, and manage their finances. They would also need to be able to build relationships with other merchants and traders, and be able to navigate the political and social landscape of the space they are in. Additionally, they would need to be able to handle the physical and mental challenges of exploring uncharted space, such as dealing with dangerous environments and creatures, and making quick decisions in high-pressure situations. They might operate a store, be a traveling merchant, or even a merchant prince, who is a powerful and wealthy individual with a license to explore and trade outside the limits of their civilization.
