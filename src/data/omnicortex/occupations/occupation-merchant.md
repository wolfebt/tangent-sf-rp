# **MERCHANT**

A merchant's occupation would involve trading, negotiating, and exploring uncharted regions of space for profit and adventure. As a merchant, the character would be skilled in various aspects of commerce, diplomacy, and resource management. They would need to be able to assess the value of goods, negotiate prices, and manage their finances. They would also need to be able to build relationships with other merchants and traders, and be able to navigate the political and social landscape of the space they are in. Additionally, they would need to be able to handle the physical and mental challenges of exploring uncharted space, such as dealing with dangerous environments and creatures, and making quick decisions in high-pressure situations. They might operate a store, be a traveling merchant, or even a merchant prince, who is a powerful and wealthy individual with a license to explore and trade outside the limits of their civilization.

## **Traits**

### *Adaptability*

Merchants are adaptable and can quickly adjust their strategies to changing market conditions. They can thrive in different environments and make the most of any situation.

*\+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily*

### *Appraisal*

Merchants have the ability to accurately assess the value of items, whether it's rare artifacts, technology, or resources. They can quickly determine the worth of goods and negotiate fair prices.

*\+2 to any Appraisal check*

### *Business Acumen*

Merchants have a keen understanding of economics and market trends. They know how to identify profitable opportunities and make wise investments.

*\+2 Merchant Vocation*

### *Charismatic*

Merchants possess charisma and charm, making them persuasive and likable. They can easily win over potential customers and build long-lasting relationships.

*May take 10 on Diplomacy*

### *Cultural Alertness*

Merchants are culturally aware and sensitive. They understand different customs, traditions, and etiquette, allowing them to navigate diverse markets and build relationships with customers from various backgrounds.

*\+2 Culture Knowledge*

### *Knowledge (Language)*

Merchants often possess linguistic skills, enabling them to communicate with customers and suppliers who speak different languages. This skill can help them expand their reach and negotiate better deals.

*\+2 Knowledge (Language)*

### *Logistics*

Merchants have a strong understanding of logistics and supply chain management. They can efficiently transport goods, manage inventory, and optimize distribution to maximize efficiency and minimize costs.

*\+2 Wealth Checks*

### *Negotiation*

Merchants excel at bargaining and negotiating deals. They have the ability to persuade others and get the best prices for their goods or services.

*\+1 all Manipulation Skills*

### *Networking*

Merchants have a wide network of contacts and connections. They know the right people to get information, find buyers or sellers, and establish lucrative partnerships.

*Gather Information checks at Advantage*

### *Product Knowledge*

Merchants have in-depth knowledge about the products they sell. They understand their features, benefits, and potential uses, allowing them to effectively market and sell their goods.

*\+2 to a Knowledge or Vocation Skill*

### *Resourcefulness*

Merchants are resourceful and can find creative solutions to problems. They can adapt to unexpected situations, overcome obstacles, and turn challenges into opportunities.

*Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage*

### *Risk Management*

Merchants are skilled at assessing and managing risks. They can anticipate potential challenges and develop contingency plans to mitigate losses.

*\+1 Karma Point*
