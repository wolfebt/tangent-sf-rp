# Representative Occupation

**Category**: Occupations  
**Starting Skill Points**: 20 SP (allocated to Professional Skills, max Rank 11, recommended Rank 6)  
**Recommended Features**: Skill (1 BP Discount)  
**Starting Traits**: 2 Free selections (Additional traits cost 1 CP each)

## Description
A Representative's occupation could involve diplomacy, negotiation, and communication between various factions, planets, or organizations. They might be responsible for maintaining relationships, resolving conflicts, and advocating for their constituents' interests whether they are in a governmental or corporate arena.   A Governmental Representative might be responsible for representing the interests of their constituents in the legislative branch of government. They would do this by drafting and voting on legislation, and by participating in committee hearings and floor debates. They would also be responsible for communicating with their constituents and keeping them updated on the legislative process.   A Corporate Representative might be responsible for representing the interests of their company in dealings with other companies, government agencies, and the public. They would do this by negotiating contracts, resolving disputes, and providing information about the company. They would also be responsible for communicating with the company's employees and keeping them updated on the company's activities.   Representatives play an important role in maintaining order and cooperation between different groups. They use their skills in diplomacy, negotiation, and communication to build relationships, resolve conflicts, and advocate for the interests of their constituents.
