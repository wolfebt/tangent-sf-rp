# Synthetic

**Category**: Species Types (The Chassis)  
**Build Point Cost**: 15 BP  

## Description
Animated objects or artificially created creatures. Synthetics designed to emulate another type may have its form but may or may not have abilities to match.

## Senses
* Low-light vision [1] and Darkvision 60 feet [1].

## Immunities
* Mental Immunity (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, phantasms) [3]; Metabolic Immunity (ability damage, ability drain, fatigue, exhaustion, energy drain, nonlethal damage) [3]; Fortitude Immunity [3].



## Traits & Special Qualities
* Hardened Frame [2], Synthetic Strength [2], Repair Required [-3]


## Physiology
* Synthetics do not breathe, eat, or sleep, unless they want to gain some beneficial effect from one of these activities. [3]
