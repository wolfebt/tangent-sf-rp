# Verdant

**Category**: Species Types (The Chassis)  
**Build Point Cost**: 9 BP  

## Description
This type encompasses vegetable creatures. Note that regular plants lack Wisdom and Charisma scores and are not creatures, but considered living objects. Verdant may be applied to another corporeal type for plant based creatures.

## Senses
* Low-light vision. [1]

## Immunities
* Mental Immunity [3]; Physical Immunity (paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep effects, stunning) [3].



## Traits & Special Qualities
* Plant based biology


## Physiology
* Verdants breathe and eat, but do not sleep (unless for beneficial effects/spell regain). [2]
