# Planar

**Category**: Species Types (The Chassis)  
**Build Point Cost**: 4 BP  

## Description
A Planar is at least partially composed of the essence (but not necessarily the matter) of some plane other than the Material Plane. Planar creatures may have familiar forms of other types but may or may not have abilities to match.

## Senses
* Darkvision 60 feet. [1]

## Immunities
* Immune to effects which would pertain to Material Plane creatures. [3]



## Traits & Special Qualities
* Planar Origin [3]

## Planar Origin
* Not a creature of the material plane and immune to effects which would pertain to similar creatures. [3]
