# Climber

**Category**: Species Movement (ADVANCED)  
**Classification**: advanced  
**Target Mode**: Climbing  
**Cost**: +2 BP  
**Base Speed**: 30 ft / round  

## Description
Innate climbing adaptations granting Base Climb Speed 30 ft and +5 racial bonus on climbing checks.

### Tactical Movement Stages (Climbing)
- **Climbing**: 1/2 Walk (15 ft/rd baseline, 30 ft innate). Standard vertical ascent/descent pace. Easy (CR 10+), Moderate (CR 15+), Difficult (CR 20+).
- **Scaling**: 1x Walk (30 ft/rd). Rapid surface scaling with a -2 penalty to actions; Athletics (Climb) at -5 penalty.
- **Fast Ascent**: 2x Walk (60 ft/rd). Vertical sprint with a -4 penalty; Athletics (Climb) at -10 penalty.
- **Fast Descent**: 4x Walk (120 ft/rd). Rapid controlled vertical slide or abseil descent with a -4 penalty; CR 20 Athletics (Climb) or -10 penalty.
