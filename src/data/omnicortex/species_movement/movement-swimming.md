# Basic Swimming

**Category**: Species Movement (BASIC)  
**Classification**: basic  
**Target Mode**: Swimming  
**Cost**: +0 BP  
**Base Speed**: 15 ft / round  

## Description
Baseline swimming speed for non-aquatic species, moving at 15 ft/round (1/2 ground walking speed).

### Tactical Movement Stages (Swimming)
- **Swimming**: 1x Swim (15 ft/rd baseline, 30 ft aquatic). Standard cruise swim pace.
- **Glide**: 2x Swim (30 ft/rd baseline, 60 ft aquatic). Hurried swim stroke with a -2 penalty to stealth; Athletics (Swim) CR 10+.
- **Stroke**: 4x Swim (60 ft/rd baseline, 120 ft aquatic). Maximum power stroke sprint with a -4 penalty; Athletics (Swim) CR 15+.
- **Treading**: 1/2 Swim or less (7.5 ft/rd). Conserves stamina; grants +2 bonus to concentration; Athletics (Swim) CR 5+.
