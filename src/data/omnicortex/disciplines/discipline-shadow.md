# Shadow Focus (Illusion)

Infusing sensory illusions with quasi-real matter drawn from the Shadow Plane.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Illusion
- **Quasi-Reality**: Even when disbelieved via Will save, Shadow illusions retain 20% reality, solid impact, and damage.
- **Shadow Conjuration**: Duplicates other summon or evocation spells at 50% power.
