# Elemental Focus (Energy)

The conjuration and projection of raw thermodynamic and acoustic energies: Fire, Cold, Lightning, Sonic, and Acid.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Energy
- **Damage**: `1d6 per Discipline Stage + 1d6 per Invocation Rank`.
- **Critical Status Effects**: Pyro (lingering burn), Cryo (freeze/snare), Voltic (electric stun), Sonic (concussive stun), Corrosive (dissolve DR).
