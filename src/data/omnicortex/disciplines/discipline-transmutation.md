# Transmutation Focus (Matter)

Changing one physical substance into another, state changes (solid/liquid/gas), petrification, and biological polymorphing.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Matter
- **Polymorph**: Subject takes on the physical stat array of a beast or alternate species.
- **Petrification**: Fortitude save vs turning to stone; partial success inflicts Slowed condition.
