# Teleport Focus (Dimension)

Instantaneous physical relocation across space without traversing intervening terrain.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Dimension
- **Familiarity DC**: Very Familiar (DC 5), Seen Once (DC 15), Description Only (DC 25), Blind / Unknown (DC 30).
- **Tactical Blink**: Move `10ft + 5ft/Rank` as a Move Action without provoking Attacks of Opportunity.
