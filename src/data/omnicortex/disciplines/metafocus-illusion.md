# Illusion Discipline

**Category**: Metafocus Disciplines  
**Associated Skills**: Phantasm, Shadow  
**Sensory Signature**: Primary: Shimmering | Secondary: Oil-Slick  
**Texture & Form**: Mirrored surfaces, chromatic haze, sensor glitches, and light-bending refractive weaves.  

## Description
Manipulating photon refraction, sensory deception, holographic phantasms, mental glamours, and shadow weaving.

## Core Mechanics
- **Governing Attribute**: Selected at Awakening (Intellect for Arcane/Logic, Wisdom for Faith/Intuition, Charisma for Inherent/Dominance).
- **Damage Scaling**: Standard 1d6 per Stage achieved (Force Focus utilizes d8s as specific exception).
- **Conduit & Breadth**: Ranks in Phantasm and Shadow contribute directly to the character's **Essence Pool**.
