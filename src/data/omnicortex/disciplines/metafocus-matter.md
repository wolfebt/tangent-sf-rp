# Matter Discipline

**Category**: Metafocus Disciplines  
**Associated Skills**: Enhancement, Transmutation  
**Sensory Signature**: Primary: Earth Tones | Secondary: Metallic Sheen  
**Texture & Form**: Solidification, density shifting, transmutation, and diamond lattice reinforcement.  

## Description
Controlling physical density, atomic crystalline bonds, elemental transmutation, and structural reinforcement.

## Core Mechanics
- **Governing Attribute**: Selected at Awakening (Intellect for Arcane/Logic, Wisdom for Faith/Intuition, Charisma for Inherent/Dominance).
- **Damage Scaling**: Standard 1d6 per Stage achieved (Force Focus utilizes d8s as specific exception).
- **Conduit & Breadth**: Ranks in Enhancement and Transmutation contribute directly to the character's **Essence Pool**.
