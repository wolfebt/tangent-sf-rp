# Summoning Focus (Dimension)

The dimensional art of calling forth creatures, objects, and spatial matter across coordinates and planes.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Dimension
- **Duration**: 1 Round per Discipline Rank (Sustained). Spending 1 Karma Point extends duration to 1 Minute per Rank.
- **Summon Creature**: Summons entities with Build Point (BP) budget equal to `10 + (Invocation Rank x 5)`.
- **Create Object**: Manifests items of Simple complexity (CR 0); higher ranks increase complexity and Tech Level.
