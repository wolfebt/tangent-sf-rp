# Enhancement Focus (Matter)

Reinforcing structural integrity, hardening natural armor, sharpening blades, and boosting biological attributes.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Matter
- **Hardening / Barkskin**: Grants `+1 DR per 3 Ranks` to armor or natural skin.
- **Physical Boost**: Grants `+2` to Strength, Agility, or Stamina for 1 min/Rank.
