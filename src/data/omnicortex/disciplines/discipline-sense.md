# Sense Focus (Mental)

Receiving extrasensory information: clairvoyance, remote viewing, surface thought reading, and precognitive glimpses.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Mental
- **Precognition**: Grants Advantage on an upcoming Initiative roll or specific Defense check.
- **Clairvoyance**: Projects sight and hearing to any known location within range.
