# Force Focus (Energy)

The telekinetic manipulation of kinetic vectors, force fields, and invisible concussive constructs.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Energy
- **Damage**: `1d8 per Discipline Stage + 1d8 per Invocation Rank` (Force damage).
- **Telekinesis**: Lift and throw weight equal to `25 lbs x Rank`.
- **Force Field**: Grants Temporary Health equal to `5 x Rank`.
