# Phantasm Focus (Illusion)

Pure sensory deception: holographic glamer, active invisibility, auditory dampening, and phantom scenes.

### Mechanics
- **Parent Discipline**: Illusion
- **Disbelief**: Target making a successful Will save sees through and negates the illusion entirely.
- **Invisibility**: Bends light around subject (1 min/Rank); attacking breaks concealment.
