# Amphibious

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Breathe Air and Water equally well, +10 to Swim Speed.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Can breathe both air and water indefinitely without drowning or suffocation risk. Adds an innate +10 ft bonus to Swim Speed and grants the ability to operate submerged without standard underwater check penalties.
