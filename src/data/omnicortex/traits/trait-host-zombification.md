# Host Zombification

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**CP Cost**: 2  

## Description
The swarm can flow into, occupy, and puppeteer the corpses of other organisms or damaged biomorphic husks, utilizing them as ablative armored shells and adopting their physical size and strength.
