# Toxic Blood

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Copper-based hemocyanin blood contains defensive xenotoxins (Ingested DC 11 + 2xSta, Pain Incapacitation 1hr).

## Mechanics & Benefit
Ingested toxin: DC 11 + 2xSta Fortitude save or suffer Pain Incapacitation for 1 hour (Severe Nausea on save)
