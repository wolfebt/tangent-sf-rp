# Alter Form (Adv)

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Base Category, Change Appearance (+5 Disguise)/gender/adjust minor traits.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The character can fluidly alter their physical appearance, apparent biological sex/gender, vocal pitch, fingerprint patterns, and minor racial characteristics within their base morphological category. Grants a +5 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks and can mimic specific individuals with familiarity.
