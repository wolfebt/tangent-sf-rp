# Shadow Affinity

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Gain +5 to Stealth when in Shadowy or Dim area.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Innate metaphysical or light-absorbing dermal harmony with obscurity grants a +5 circumstance bonus on Stealth checks whenever operating within shadowy, dim, or poorly illuminated areas.
