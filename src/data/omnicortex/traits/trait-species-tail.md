# Tail

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
+2 to Trip and Balance Checks and usable as a Club.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Possesses a muscular, flexible tail that acts as a counterweight. Grants a +2 racial bonus on Balance (Acrobatics) and Trip combat maneuver checks, and can be swung as a natural bludgeoning weapon dealing 1d6 + Str kinetic damage (treated as a Club).
