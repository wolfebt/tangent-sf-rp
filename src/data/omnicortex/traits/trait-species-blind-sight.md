# Blind Sight

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Sensory  

## Description
Accurately target unseen objects in a 30 ft Radius or 60 ft Cone.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Total sensory spatial awareness using acoustic sonar, electro-magnetic field resonance, or ambient psychic echoes. The character can accurately target and interact with unseen, obscured, or invisible objects and creatures in a 30-foot radius or 60-foot cone with zero concealment miss chance.
