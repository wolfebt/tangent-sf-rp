# Synthetic Base Traits

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Elite  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**CP Cost**: 14  

## Description
The fundamental mechanical and digitized chassis of artificial sentient entities.

## Mechanics
Immune to Asphyxiation, Bleeding, Critical Damage, Starvation, Sleep, and biological ailments [9 CP]; Repair Required (cannot heal naturally without mechanical maintenance or bionics) [-3 CP]; Digitized Mind (+Advantage vs mental influence) [2 CP]; Robotic Strength (counts one size larger for lifting/grappling) [2 CP]; Resilient Design (DR 5/- vs physical damage) [2 CP]; Low-Light Vision [2 CP]. Total package value: 14 CP.

## Packaged Traits Sublist
* **Synthetic Biological Immunities** [9 CP / Elite]: Total immunity to asphyxiation, bleed, critical hit extra damage, starvation, sleep, disease, poisons, and biological needs.
* **Repair Required (No Vitality)** [-3 CP / Disadvantage]: Possesses no Vitality; Health is multiplied by 1.5 for Structure Points; cannot heal naturally and requires technical repairs.
* **Digitized Mind** [2 CP / Advanced]: Silicon neural substrate grants Advantage on all resistance saves against mind-affecting and psionic influence.
* **Robotic Strength** [2 CP / Advanced]: Heavy hydraulic actuators allow lifting, carrying, and grappling as if one size category larger.
* **Resilient Design (DR 5/-)** [2 CP / Advanced]: Hardened metallic alloy frame provides passive Damage Reduction 5/- against physical strikes.
* **Low-Light Optical Array** [2 CP / Advanced]: High-resolution multispectral optical sensors providing enhanced vision in low-light and infrared.
