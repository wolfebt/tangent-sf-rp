# Dragon Mind

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Make any Mental Resistance checks with Advantage. Req: Dragonkin, Wis 2.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Dragonkin lineage, Wisdom 2+. Fierce mental fortitude and draconic pride grant Advantage on all Mental Resistance, Willpower, and psychic intrusion checks.
