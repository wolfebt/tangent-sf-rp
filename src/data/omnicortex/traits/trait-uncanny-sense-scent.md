# Uncanny Sense (Scent)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Elite
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Sensory
**CP Cost**: 4

## Description
Hyper-developed olfactory receptors capable of pinpointing unseen targets and tracking chemical trails.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Uncanny Sense (Scent): +4 Alertness and Survival (Tracking) by scent; cannot be flanked or surprised by scent-bearing targets within 30 ft.
