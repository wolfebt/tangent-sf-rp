# Bodyform Heavy Armor

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Shapechange to gain a heavy protective layer (Doubles Bodyform Armor bonus to DR).

## Mechanics & Benefits
Dense calcified dermal plates and subcutaneous composite shielding double the Damage Resistance (DR) bonus provided by Bodyform Armor (granting +8 Natural DR / Armor).
