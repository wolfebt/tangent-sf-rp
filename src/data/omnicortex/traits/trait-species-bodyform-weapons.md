# Bodyform Weapons

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Shapechange to gain ‘Natural Weaponry’ based on Size

## Mechanics & Benefits
Allows extruding bone blades, talons, stingers, or maces dealing natural melee damage scaled to size category (1d6 for Medium, 1d8 for Large) with chosen damage type (slashing, piercing, or blunt).
