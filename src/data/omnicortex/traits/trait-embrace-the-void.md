# Embrace the Void

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Archetype Trait
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
Signature trait of The Doomsayer (Visionarie). Specialized expertise and intuition in resisting mental manipulation or maintaining composure in the face of terrifying phenomena.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You are immune to Void based psychic damage and gain +2 bonus on any checks related to resisting mental manipulation or maintaining composure in the face of terrifying phenomena
