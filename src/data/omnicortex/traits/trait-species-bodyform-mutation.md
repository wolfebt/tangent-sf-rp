# Bodyform Mutation

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical (Ranked)  

## Description
Adjust to a Racial Trait of which prerequisites are possessed. Ranked.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Allows temporary morphological adaptation to express any standard racial trait for which prerequisites are met. Lasts until the next rest. May be purchased multiple times (Ranked).
