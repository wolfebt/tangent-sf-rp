# Brutal

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Growths/Spurs doubling Str damage bonus to natural damage (Lethal).

## Mechanics & Benefits
Hardened bone spurs, jagged carapaces, or dense knuckles double the character's Strength modifier bonus when calculating damage for natural weapon and unarmed strikes (all damage is Lethal).
