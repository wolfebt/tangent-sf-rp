# Streetwise

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Urban  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Knows who controls each block, how to evade gang tolls, and where to fence hot cargo.

## Mechanics & Benefits
+2 to Streetwise checks (increases to +3 in character's home city).
