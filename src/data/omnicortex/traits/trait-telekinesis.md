# Telekinesis

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Special
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Innate psychokinetic manipulation allowing the entity to handle objects and interact with its environment without physical limbs.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Special Ability (Telekinesis): Manipulate objects and exert kinetic force at close range using Intellect in place of Strength for fine manipulation.
