# Vampiric Power

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Elite
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 4

## Description
Predatory life-drain metabolism that converts stolen vitality into raw physical power.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain +1 to a Physical Ability per 2 points of Stamina drained (Lethal at 3 + Sta)
