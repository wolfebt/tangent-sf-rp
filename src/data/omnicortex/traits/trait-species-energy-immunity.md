# Energy Immunity

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Completely Immune to specific Energy Damage. Req: Sta 2, DR 20 vs specific Energy.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Stamina 2+, DR 20 vs specific energy type. Complete molecular and harmonic immunity to one designated energy damage type (Thermal/Pyro, Cryo, Voltic, Sonic, or Corrosive). Suffers 0 damage from that energy type under all conditions.
