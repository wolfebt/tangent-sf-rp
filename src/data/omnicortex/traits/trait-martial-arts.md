# Martial Arts

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Combat
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Innate understanding of body mechanics, kinetic leverage, and unarmed natural combat styles.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Grants innate Martial Arts unarmed combat proficiency (1d6 unarmed strike damage, armed melee defense, and +2 to unarmed combat checks).
