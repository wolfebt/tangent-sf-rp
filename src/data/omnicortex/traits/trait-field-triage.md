# Field Triage

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Archetype Trait (The Field Medic (Sentinel))
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
Rapid application of derm-sealers, combat stimulants, and trauma clamps under fire.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Major Action: Make a Medicine check (CR 15) on a downed or critically wounded ally. On a success, restore Vitality equal to Wisdom x 5 and immediately restore consciousness without provoking attacks of opportunity.
