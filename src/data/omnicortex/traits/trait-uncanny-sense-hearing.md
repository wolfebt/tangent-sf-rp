# Uncanny Sense (Hearing)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Elite
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Sensory
**CP Cost**: 4

## Description
Hyper-developed auditory perception capable of detecting heartbeats, whispers, and spatial acoustics.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Uncanny Sense (Hearing): +4 Alertness (Listening); cannot be flanked or surprised by audible targets within 30 ft.
