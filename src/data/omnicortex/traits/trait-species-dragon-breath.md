# Dragon Breath

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Breath Weapon - 30 ft Cone or 60 ft Line of Energy [Str x d8 in Dmg].

## Mechanics & Benefits
Exhales an elemental tempest in a 30-foot cone or 60-foot line dealing Strength score × 1d8 elemental damage (Pyro, Cryo, Voltic, Sonic, or Corrosive). Targets may attempt a Reflex save (DC 10 + 1/2 Level + Sta mod) for half damage. Usable every 1d4 rounds.
