# Static Bonus Feat

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Trained  

## Description
Choose one feat with no prerequisites. All members gain this feat as a bonus feat.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The species' culture or universal biology instills a foundational discipline. Choose one standard feat with no prerequisites at species creation; all members of this species gain that feat as a permanent bonus feat.
