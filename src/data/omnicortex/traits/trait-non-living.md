# Non-Living

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Elite
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 4

## Description
Animated construct, elemental, or undead state devoid of standard organic biology.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Not classified as Living; immune to biological effects, bleed, sleep, poison, and disease
