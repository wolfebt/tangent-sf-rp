# Chameleon

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Changes color, +5 Stealth or may take 10 on Stealth checks.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Chromatophores across the epidermis adjust skin color and patterns in real-time to match surroundings. Grants a +5 racial bonus on Stealth checks or the option to Take 10 on Stealth even when observed or moving.
