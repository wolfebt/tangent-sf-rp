# Stable Footed

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Defensive  

## Description
+4 racial Stability bonus while standing on the ground.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Low center of gravity, gripping foot pads, or dense ground anchoring grants a +4 racial Stability bonus to resist Bull Rush, Trip, Reposition, and Overrun combat maneuvers while standing firmly on solid ground.
