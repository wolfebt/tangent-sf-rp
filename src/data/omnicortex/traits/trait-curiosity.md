**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: General

## Description
An insatiable drive to explore the unknown, analyze anomalous phenomena, and investigate new discoveries.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Regain 1 Karma Point on the first encounter when analyzing something entirely new in a session.
