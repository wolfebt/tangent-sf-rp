# Curiosity

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Origin Trait
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
An insatiable drive to explore the unknown, analyze anomalous phenomena, and investigate new discoveries.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Regain 1 spent Karma Point on the first encounter with a novel creature, location, or technology each session.
