# Hive Mind (Swarm)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Distributed swarm consciousness immune to single-target precision trauma and critical hits.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Cannot be killed unless >75% of swarm biomass is destroyed by area-of-effect damage
