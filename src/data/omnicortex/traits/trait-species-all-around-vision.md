# All-Around Vision

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Sensory  

## Description
+4 racial bonus on Perception checks and immune to flanking.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Multiple eyes, compound optical arrays, or wide optical placement grant a 360-degree field of view. Provides a permanent +4 racial bonus on Perception (Awareness) checks and complete immunity to being flanked in tactical combat.
