# Semi-Corporeal

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
DR30 vs physical, able to Phase through solid matter, Solidify at will.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The character's body exists in a quantum phase-state. Grants DR 30 against non-magical physical kinetic attacks, and allows phasing through solid physical obstacles (walls, bulkheads, barriers) at half movement speed, solidifying or phasing at will as a free action.
