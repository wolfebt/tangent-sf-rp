# Camouflage (Active)

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Elite  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**CP Cost**: 4  

## Description
Chitin can shift texture and hue to blend into their environment, shifting to a variety of colors as desired—including light-absorbing vanta-black and visual camouflage so effective they seem to fade into transparency (+8 to Stealth motionless, +4 moving).
