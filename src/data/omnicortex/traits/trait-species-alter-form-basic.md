# Alter Form (Basic)

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Base Category, Change Appearance only (+5 to Disguise).

## Mechanics & Benefits
The character can alter superficial facial features, skin tone, hair color, and vocal pitch at will within their base morphological category. Grants a +5 circumstance bonus on Disguise checks to pass as a different individual. Does not grant new appendages or alter size category.
