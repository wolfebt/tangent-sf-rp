# Shadow Blending

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
Dermal light-scattering or umbral resonance that obscures the character’s silhouette in low light.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Attacks against you in dim light suffer a 30% miss chance
