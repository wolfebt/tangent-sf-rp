# Non-Living

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Undead, Elementals and others not classified as Living by normal standards.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The entity does not possess a biological metabolism (Undead, Pure Energy Elemental, or Metaphysical Construct). Immune to suffocation, starvation, thirst, exhaustion, bleed effects, biological disease, and non-magical poison. Does not possess a biological pulse or nervous system.
