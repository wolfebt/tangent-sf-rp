# Resource Management

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Colony  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Meticulous rationing of food, water, battery cells, and structural spare parts.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Wealth and requisition checks for basic survival resources are made with Advantage.
