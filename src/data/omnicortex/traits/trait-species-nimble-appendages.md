# Nimble Appendages

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Usable as ‘main-hand’ with no penalties. Req: Additional Limbs/Tail.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Additional Limbs or Prehensile Tail trait. Complete neuro-muscular bilateral ambidexterity allows secondary limbs and prehensile tails to operate with full main-hand precision, suffering zero off-hand penalties on attack rolls, weapon handling, or complex technological operations.
