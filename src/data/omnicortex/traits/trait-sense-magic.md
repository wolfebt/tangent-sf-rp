# Sense Magic (30 ft)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Sensory
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Innate arcane resonance allowing perception of ambient magical auras and active spellwork.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Sense Magic (30 ft Radius): Automatically sense the presence and general intensity of active magic, arcane nexuses, and enchanted artifacts within 30 ft.
