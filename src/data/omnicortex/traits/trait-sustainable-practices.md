# Sustainable Practices

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Agricultural  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Deep understanding of ecological balance, crop rotation, and water conservation.

## Mechanics & Benefits
+2 to all Agriculture-based checks in a prepared grow zone and when preparing new soil.
