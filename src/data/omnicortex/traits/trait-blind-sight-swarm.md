# Blind Sight (Swarm)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Distributed resonance allows precise non-visual targeting in a 30 ft radius or 60 ft cone.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Accurately target unseen objects within 30 ft; completely immune to darkness and blinding effects
