# Enhanced Abilities

**Category**: Occupational Traits  
**Associated Occupation**: Adept  
**Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Adepts may be able to temporarily enhance their physical or mental abilities to go beyond what is considered normal for their species or race.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Able to spend a Karma Point to increase any one Ability Score by 1 point for 1 Minute, multiple points spent will increase a different Ability or the same.
