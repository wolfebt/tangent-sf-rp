# Sociable

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Social  

## Description
Diplomacy check to change attitude fails by 5 or more, try again within 24 hours.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Disarming demeanor and empathetic conversational recovery prevent social faux pas from becoming permanent grudges. If a Diplomacy check to influence attitude fails by 5 or more, the character may retry the check with that target within 24 hours rather than being locked out.
