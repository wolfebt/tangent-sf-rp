# Natural Armor

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Defensive  

## Description
+2 natural armor bonus.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Dense dermis, thick hide, or reinforced subdermal fascia grants a permanent +2 Natural Armor bonus to Armor Class (AC), which stacks with manufactured armor plating.
