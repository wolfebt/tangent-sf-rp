# Curiosity

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Trained  

## Description
+4 bonus on Diplomacy checks to gather information, and Knowledge checks.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Inquisitive neural wiring and academic drive grant a permanent +4 racial bonus on Diplomacy checks made to gather information and on all Knowledge (Academic/Lore) checks.
