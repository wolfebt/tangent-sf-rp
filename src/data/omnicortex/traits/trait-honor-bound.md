# Honor-Bound

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Militaristic  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Devotion to comrades and code of duty; willing to endure hardship for the unit.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Once per day, make a check with Advantage, provided the action directly protects or aids an ally.
