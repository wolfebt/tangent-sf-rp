# Silent Hunter

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Movement  

## Description
Reduce Stealth penalty for moving by 5 / Stealth checks while running at –20

## Mechanics & Benefits
Padded footfalls and hydrodynamic/aerodynamic motion dampen sound. Reduces the standard Stealth check penalty for moving at full speed by 5, and reduces the penalty for making Stealth checks while running to –20 (instead of automatic failure).
