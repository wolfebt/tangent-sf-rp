# Specialized Senses (Aquatic)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Sensory
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
Specialized aquatic sensory organs adapted for low-light abyssal visibility, pressure waves, and underwater detection.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Grants Low-Light Vision and Water-Sense (Blindsense 30 ft in shared body of water; +2 Alertness underwater).
