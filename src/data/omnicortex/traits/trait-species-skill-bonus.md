# Skill Bonus

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Trained  

## Description
Gain +2 racial bonus to divide amongst noted skills.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Grants a +2 racial skill bonus that may be divided among one or two designated species skills (+2 to one skill, or +1 to two skills) at character creation.
