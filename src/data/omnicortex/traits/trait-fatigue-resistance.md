# Fatigue Resistance

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Advanced
**Type**: Species Trait
**CP Cost**: 2

## Description
All Fatigue checks are made at Advantage and at twice the interval time.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Advantage on all saves/checks against fatigue, exhaustion, and prolonged exertion; interval duration is doubled
