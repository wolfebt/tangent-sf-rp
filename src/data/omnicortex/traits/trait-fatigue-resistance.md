# Fatigue Resistance

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**BP Cost**: 2  

## Description
All Fatigue checks are made at Advantage and at twice the interval time, allowing relentless sustained labor and prolonged forced marches.
