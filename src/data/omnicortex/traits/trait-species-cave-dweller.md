# Cave Dweller

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Trained  

## Description
+4 bonus on Survival checks made underground.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Innate subterranean orientation and cavern familiarity grant a +4 racial bonus on all Survival, Navigation, and Spelunking checks made underground or in enclosed tunnel networks.
