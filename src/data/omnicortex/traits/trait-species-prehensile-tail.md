# Prehensile Tail

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
+2 to Climbing and Balance checks and usable as an off-hand. Special Pants.

## Mechanics & Benefits
A fully articulated, muscular tail grants a +2 bonus on Climbing and Balance checks and can grasp objects, retrieve items, or wield light tools as an off-hand limb. Requires tailored pants.
