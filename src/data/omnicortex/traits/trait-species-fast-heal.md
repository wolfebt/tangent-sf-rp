**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)

## Description
Daily Recovery of Health and Vitality during a Light Rest (repeatable).

## Mechanics & Benefits
Accelerated cellular mitosis allows recovering 10% maximum Health and Vitality during a short Light Rest (15 minutes) once per day in addition to standard rest benefits.
