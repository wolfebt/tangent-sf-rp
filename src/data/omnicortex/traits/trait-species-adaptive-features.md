# Adaptive Features

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical (Ranked)  

## Description
May change between specific features during a Light Rest. Ranked.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The character possesses plastic biological structures allowing them to swap one chosen physical feature for another designated feature from their species list during a Light Rest. May be taken multiple times (Ranked) to allow additional swappable feature slots.
