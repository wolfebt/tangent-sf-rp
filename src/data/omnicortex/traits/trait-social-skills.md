# Social Skills

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Urban  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Navigates dense cultural melting pots, high-rise galas, and underground speakeasies with ease.

## Mechanics & Benefits
+1 to all Social Manipulation skills and +1 to Culture Knowledge.
