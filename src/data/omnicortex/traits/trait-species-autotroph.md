# Autotroph

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Does not require food/drink, may eat/digest elixirs for effects.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Generates complex biochemical energy directly through photosynthesis, radiation absorption, or chemosynthesis, eliminating all standard food and water requirements. Can still voluntarily consume and digest ingestible elixirs, potions, or medical compounds for their effects.
