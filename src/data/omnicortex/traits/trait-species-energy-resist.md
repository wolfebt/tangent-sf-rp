# Energy Resist

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical (Ranked)  

## Description
DR 10 vs Chosen Type (Pyro, Cryo, Sonic, Voltic, Corrosive). Multiple/Ranked.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Dense cellular shielding grants DR 10 against one chosen energy damage type (Thermal/Pyro, Cryo, Voltic, Sonic, or Corrosive). May be taken multiple times for different types or ranked to increase DR by +10 per rank.
