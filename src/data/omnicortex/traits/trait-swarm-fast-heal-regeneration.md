# Fast Heal & Regeneration (Swarm)

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**CP Cost**: 2  

## Description
The swarm constantly births new members to replace those lost, regenerating 2 Health/Vitality per round in combat and fully restoring mass during a Light Rest.
