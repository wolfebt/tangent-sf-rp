# Regeneration

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Will regrow lost Limbs and Organs with recovery of Health.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Incredible cellular stem-cell proliferation allows regrowing severed limbs, destroyed sensory organs, and ruptured internal vitals over 2d6 days as Health is recovered. Regenerates 1 Health per combat round while conscious.
