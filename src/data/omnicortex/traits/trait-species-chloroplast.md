# Chloroplast

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Gain sustenance from and Double Healing rate while in daylight equivalent light.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Photosynthetic organelles generate full metabolic sustenance from sunlight and double natural recovery rates of Health and Vitality whenever resting in daylight-equivalent illumination.
