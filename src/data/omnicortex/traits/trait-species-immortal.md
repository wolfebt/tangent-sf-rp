# Immortal

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Cannot die of Natural Causes, nor suffer damage from Poisons/Diseases. Req: Ageless.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Ageless trait. Perpetual bio-energetic renewal prevents death from natural causes or old age. The character is completely immune to non-magical and biological diseases, pathogens, venoms, and chemical poisons.
