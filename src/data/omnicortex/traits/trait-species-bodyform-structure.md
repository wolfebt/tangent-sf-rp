# Bodyform Structure

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical (Ranked)  

## Description
Adjust Physical Abilities in equal trade. Once per day for the entire day. Ranked.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Once per day, the character can redistribute physical ability points in an equal 1-for-1 trade (e.g., –2 Agility for +2 Strength) for 24 hours. Ranked.
