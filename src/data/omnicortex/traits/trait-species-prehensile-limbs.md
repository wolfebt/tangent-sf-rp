# Prehensile Limbs

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)

**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Fully Prehensile tentacles/off-hands/limbs, make certain checks with Advantage.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Fully prehensile tentacles or versatile extra limbs grant Advantage on Grapple maintenance, manual manipulation under zero gravity, and complex device rigging.
