# Cat's Luck

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Defensive  

## Description
Once per Long Rest make a Reflex Check at Advantage.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Once per Long Rest, when subjected to a hazard, trap, or area attack requiring a Reflex saving throw, the character may choose to make the check with Advantage (rolling twice and taking the higher result).
