# Frenzy

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
1/day, whenever taking damage, fly into frenzy for 1 min (+2 Con/Str, –2 AC).

## Mechanics & Benefits
Once per day, upon suffering damage in combat, the character may enter a battle frenzy lasting for 1 minute (10 combat rounds). While frenzied, gain a +2 bonus to Strength and Stamina (Con), but suffer a –2 penalty to Armor Class (AC).
