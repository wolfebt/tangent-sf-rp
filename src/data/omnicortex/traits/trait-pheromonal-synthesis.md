# Pheromonal Synthesis

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait

## Description
Biological scent-marking for hive coordination. Synthesizes persistent chemical trails lasting up to 72 hours (+2 Navigation/Survival to allies) and silent aerosol alarms out to 60 ft.
