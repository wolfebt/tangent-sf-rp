# Thermal Sight

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Sensory  

## Description
See Infra-Red/heat patterns, track passage of a warm target without light.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Infrared pit organs or thermal optics allow detecting heat signatures, warm conduits, and residual thermal footprints in absolute darkness, granting a +4 bonus to track warm-bodied creatures.
