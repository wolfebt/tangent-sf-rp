# Blind Sense

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Sensory  

## Description
Sense unseen objects in a 30 ft Radius or Cone of 60 ft.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Echolocation, sensory whiskers, or pressure sensors allow pinpointing the location of unseen creatures and objects within a 30 ft radius or a 60 ft directional cone. Total darkness and invisibility do not conceal targets within this range, though concealment penalties on attack rolls still apply unless converted to Blindsight.
