# Sense Evil (Uncontrolled)

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Elite
**Type**: Species Trait
**Classification**: Sensory
**CP Cost**: 4

## Description
60 ft radius uncontrolled sensory detection of entities bearing the Evil descriptor or active vile intent.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Sense Evil (60 ft radius, Uncontrolled): Detects targets with the Evil descriptor (such as Extreme Morality) or active vile intent through intrusive sensory cues.
