# Arcane Weaving

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Archetype Trait (The Magus (Savant))
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
Tracing geometric runes to warp the range, area, or elemental nature of an invocation.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Passive (Requires Awakened): When casting a metaphysical Invocation, you may take a -2 penalty to your Attune roll to double its range, expand its area of effect by 5 meters, or alter its energy damage type.
