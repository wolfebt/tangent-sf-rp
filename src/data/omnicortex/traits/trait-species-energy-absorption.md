# Energy Absorption

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta (Ranked)  

## Description
Heals 20% of damage ignored. Ranked. Req: Energy Immunity.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Energy Immunity trait. Whenever subjected to the designated energy damage type, the character converts 20% of the absorbed damage directly into restored Health and Vitality per rank. Ranked.
