# Ageless

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Does not suffer penalties nor show any signs of aging.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Immune to cellular senescence, aging debuffs, and physical attribute decay over time. The character cannot die of old age and does not show visual signs of deterioration.
