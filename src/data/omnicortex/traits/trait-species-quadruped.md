# Quadruped

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Movement  

## Description
Four legged, +4 Stability, +10 movement speed. Special Accommodations.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Four-legged locomotion grants +10 ft base movement speed and a +4 racial Stability bonus against Trip and Bull Rush attempts. Requires customized armor and vehicle mount accommodations.
