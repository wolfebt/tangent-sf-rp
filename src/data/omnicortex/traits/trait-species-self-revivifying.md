# Self Revivifying

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
1/day attempt to resurrect. Cost: 1 Karma, Con Check Diff 20. Req: Immortal.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Immortal trait. Once per day, upon death, the character may spend 1 Karma Point to attempt self-resurrection by making a Constitution check (DC 20). On a success, the body revives with 1 Health after 1 hour.
