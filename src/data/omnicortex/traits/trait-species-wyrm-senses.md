# Wyrm Senses

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Sensory  

## Description
Take Features from any Acute Sense Line. Req: Dragon Apotheosis.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Dragon Apotheosis trait. Ancient wyrm sensory transcendence grants immediate access to acquire any feature from the Acute Sense lines (Acute Hearing, Scent, Thermal, Micro-Vision) at a –1 BP discount.
