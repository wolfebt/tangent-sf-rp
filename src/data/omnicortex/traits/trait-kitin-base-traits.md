# Kitin Base Traits

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Elite  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**BP Cost**: 8  

## Description
The fundamental biological and psionic heritage shared across all Kitin Strains:
* **Awakened (Psychic) [2 BP]:** Conductors and receptors of the Hive psionic Chorus.
* **Biotechnology [2 BP]:** Innate affinity for manipulating living symbiotic technology.
* **Hive Connection / Hive-Link [2 BP]:** Constant telepathic connection to the Collective.
* **Torpor / Sleepless [2 BP]:** Requires only 4 hours of conscious torpor; immune to sleep effects.
