# Dragon Wings

**Category**: Species Traits (Elite)  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Movement  

## Description
Grow Leathery Wings - Fly Speed of 3x Ground Speed. Req: Dragonkin, Exoskeleton.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Requirements: Dragonkin lineage, Exoskeleton trait. Massive leathery, scaled wings sprout from the shoulder blades, granting a true Fly Speed equal to 3× base Ground Speed (90 ft/round for Medium) with Good maneuverability.
