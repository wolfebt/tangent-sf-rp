# Powerful Charge

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Combat  

## Description
Charge deals twice the number of damage dice plus 1-1/2 times Str bonus.

## Mechanics & Benefits
When executing a Charge action, the character deals double weapon damage dice plus 1.5× Strength modifier upon a successful hit.
