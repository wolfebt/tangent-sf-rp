# Protective Stance

**Category**: TRAITS
**Tier**: Basic
**Type**: Archetype Trait (The Protector (Sentinel))
**CP Cost**: 1

## Description
Digging in heels, raising a shield, and projecting an impenetrable interdiction zone.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Minor Action: Movement speed is halved, but you project a 2-meter Interdiction Zone. Enemies attempting to move through must succeed on an Athletics check opposed by your Intimidation or Melee skill or have their movement end and provoke a free melee strike.
