# Exoskeleton (Partial)

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
DR (Strength +2) x2: Str 2, Concealable - Leathery or Scaled.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Provides natural leathery, chitinous, or scaled epidermal plating. Grants Damage Resistance formula DR = (Strength + 2) × 2, requiring minimum Strength 2. The plating is concealable beneath standard clothing and light armor.
