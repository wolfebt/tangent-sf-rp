# G-Force Immunity

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**BP Cost**: 2  

## Description
Internal fluid dampening allows the organism to survive extreme maneuvers and turns that would turn a humanoid to jelly. Grants complete immunity to G-LOC, blackout, redout, and acceleration trauma.
