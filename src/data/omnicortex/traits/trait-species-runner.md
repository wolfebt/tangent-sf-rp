# Runner

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Movement  

## Description
+4 racial bonus on saves to avoid fatigue/exhaustion/ill effects from running

## Mechanics & Benefits
Cardiovascular endurance and oxygen-rich bloodstream grant a +4 racial bonus on all Fortitude saves and Constitution checks made to avoid fatigue, exhaustion, heat exhaustion, or muscle damage from sustained running, sprinting, or forced marches.
