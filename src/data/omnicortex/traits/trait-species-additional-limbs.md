# Additional Limbs

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Another pair of prehensile limbs; Arms, Tentacles or other.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The creature possesses an additional pair of functional prehensile limbs (arms, tentacles, or secondary manipulators). Allows holding additional objects, weapons, or gear, though standard multi-weapon attack limits and action economy still apply.
