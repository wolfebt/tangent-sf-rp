# Silver Tongued

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Social  

## Description
+2 bonus on Diplomacy and Bluff. Can shift attitude up to three steps.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Masterful cadence, vocal harmonics, and social charisma grant a +2 racial bonus on all Diplomacy and Bluff checks. When using Diplomacy to influence NPC attitudes, successful checks can shift attitude up to three steps (e.g., Hostile to Indifferent) rather than the standard two.
