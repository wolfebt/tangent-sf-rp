# Shards of the Past

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Pick two skills. Gain +2 racial bonus on both. Represents past lives.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Subconscious ancestral memories, reincarnation echoes, or genetic data traces surface in the mind. Choose any two skills at character creation; the character gains a permanent +2 racial bonus on both skills.
