# Emissary

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Social  

## Description
Once per day make a check at advantage for Bluff or Diplomacy.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Once per day (refreshing after a Long Rest), when making a Bluff (Deception) or Diplomacy (Persuasion) check during diplomatic exchanges or high-stakes negotiations, the character may make the roll with Advantage.
