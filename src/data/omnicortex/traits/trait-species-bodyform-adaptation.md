# Bodyform Adaptation

**Category**: Species Traits (Advanced)  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Physiology shifts to be compatible with the new environment.

## Mechanics & Benefits
Upon entering a new biome or harsh atmospheric zone, the character's physiology adapts over 10 minutes to grant environmental immunity (vacuum sealing, thermal regulation, or pressure resistance) for the duration of exposure.
