# Leadership Skills

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Basic  
**Type**: Origin Trait

## Description
Natural leadership ability that inspires and coordinates starship crews under critical void emergencies.

## Mechanics
+2 bonus on Leadership checks, and can grant allies Advantage on Morale saves once per encounter.
