# Peaceful Nature

**Category**: Origin Traits  
**Associated Origin**: Enlightened  
**Acquisition Cost**: Free (1 of 2 starting traits) or 1 CP per additional trait

## Description
Radiates serene composure that disarms hostility and calms tense standoffs.

## Mechanics & Benefits
+2 to Diplomacy checks during peaceful mediation and truce negotiations.
