# Camouflage

**Category**: Species Traits (Basic)  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Choose a favored terrain type. +4 bonus on Stealth checks within that terrain.

## Mechanics & Benefits
The species' epidermal pigmentation or chromatophoric cells naturally blend into one chosen terrain type (e.g., Jungle, Desert, Snow/Ice, Urban Concrete, Deep Space/Void). Grants a +4 racial bonus on all Stealth checks while operating within that favored terrain.
