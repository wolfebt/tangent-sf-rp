# Weaver

**Category**: TRAITS  
**Tier**: Advanced  
**Type**: Species Trait  
**CP Cost**: 2  

## Description
Abdominal spinnerets weave high-tensile silk filaments of Hive-Resin, creating adhesive snares to entangle enemies or constructing structural safety nets, hanging bivouacs, and anchor lines.
