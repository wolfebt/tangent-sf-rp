# **Illusion Invocations**

# **Illusion Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Illusion Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Illusion: Phantasm or Illusion: Shadow) for shaping, complexity, and believability.

## 

## 

## **PHANTASM INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Illusion

Specialization: Phantasm

Description: Create false imagery, sounds, and other sensations. These illusions exist in the minds of the viewers or as projections of light, but lack physical substance.

Standard Range: Medium.

Standard Area: 10 foot cube per Skill Level (Stage).

Standard Duration: 1 Minute per Level (Stage).

### 

### **ANALYZE ILLUSION**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Sensory | \[Cantrip\] \[Detection\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Sight  
* **Area:** Single Effect  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

The caster focuses on a visual or auditory anomaly to determine if it is false.

Benefit: All Investigation or Awareness checks made to analyze a possible illusion are made at Advantage.

### 

### **FACADE**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Personal\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** Personal  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Insight Check vs Attune

Effect Description:

A simple personal illusion that alters the caster's clothing, hair color, or minor facial features. It is purely visual and does not hold up to tactile inspection.

### 

### **GLIMMER**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility | \[Sensory\] \[Minor\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** 10ft Cube per Stage  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Creates a minor, static sensory illusion, such as a floating orb of light, a ghostly sound, or a faint smell.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Complexity:** Adds 1 sensory channel per Stage (Visual \-\> \+Audio \-\> \+Smell \-\> \+Thermal).  
* **Duration:** Can maintain without Concentration for 1 Minute per Stage.

### 

### **PROJECTED IMAGE**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Defense | \[Decoy\] \[Duplicate\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** Self or Target  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Creates illusory duplicates of the caster that mimic their movements, making it difficult for enemies to determine the true target.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Magnitude:** Number of Images \= 1 per Stage.  
* **Defense:** Each image grants a 10% Miss Chance (Max 50%).

### 

### **DISGUISE**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility | \[Complex\] \[Glamer\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Personal or Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature/Object  
* **Duration:** 10 Minutes/Level  
* **Resistance:** Insight Check vs. Attune CR

Effect Description:

Wraps a target in a complex illusion that alters their physical appearance (height, weight, clothing, species features). Unlike the minor Facade, this can include auditory and tactile elements at higher levels.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Complexity:** Disguise Check Bonus \= \+2 per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** At Stage 3+, tactile elements feel real to casual touch.

### 

### **INVISIBILITY**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility/Defense | \[Concealment\] \[Light Bending\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature/Object  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None (Detection requires scans)

Effect Description:

Bends light around the subject, rendering them completely unseen to the visual spectrum. Ends if subject attacks.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Stability:** At Stage 4 (Master), attacking does not break the invisibility (Greater Invisibility).  
* **Spectrum:** At Stage 3, invisible to Darkvision/Infrared.

### 

### **GLAMOUR**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility/Social | \[Complex\] \[Multi-Sensory\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** 10ft Cube per Stage  
* **Duration:** Concentration or Scene  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Creates a complex, moving scene involving visual, auditory, olfactory, and thermal elements. Used to distract, confuse, or entertain.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Area:** Cube size scales per Stage.  
* **Believability:** Save CR increases by \+1 per Stage.

### 

### **MIRAGE**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Environmental | \[Terrain\] \[Large Scale\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Full Round Action  
* **Range:** Long  
* **Area:** 100ft \+ Radius  
* **Duration:** Concentration \+ 1 Hour  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Alters the appearance of the terrain itself, hiding natural hazards, creating fake structures, or masking the presence of a camp.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Area:** Radius \= 100ft x Stage.  
* **Duration:** Persists for 1 Hour per Stage after concentration stops.

### 

### **COLOR BLAST**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Attack | \[Light\] \[Debuff\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** 15ft Cone  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Effect Description:

A sudden, overwhelming burst of clashing colors and hypnotic patterns designed to overload the optic nerves.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Stun Duration \= 1 Round per Stage.  
* **Area:** Cone Length \= 15ft \+ (5ft per Stage).

### 

### **BLUR**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Defense | \[Distortion\] \[Visual\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

The subject's outline becomes shifting and blurry. Attacks against the subject suffer a miss chance.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Defense:** Miss Chance \= 10% \+ (5% per Stage). Cap 35%.

### 

### **OBSCURE**

Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Debuff | \[Sensory\] \[Deprivation\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Effect Description:

Dulls or completely blocks a specific sense (Sight, Hearing, Smell) of a target.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Number of Senses blocked \= 1 per Stage.  
* **Power:** At Stage 3+, can blind special senses (Radar/Thermo).

## **SHADOW INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Illusion

Specialization: Shadow

Description: Conjuration of solid creatures and items from ether. May look realistic or not.

Standard Range: Close.

Standard Duration: 1 Round per Level (Stage). Spend 1 Karma Point to increase to 1 Minute per Level.

Object Limits:

* **Size:** Up to 2 ft per Skill Level (Stage) per side.  
* **Weight:** Up to 10 lbs x (Stage squared).

### 

### **LIGHT MANIPULATION**

Classification: Illusion (Shadow) | Environmental | \[Minor\] \[Control\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Adjusts the ambient illumination in the area. Can dim lights to create shadows or brighten them to reveal details.

Magnitude: Can change illumination level by 30% per Skill Stage.

### 

### **DARKNESS**

Classification: Illusion (Shadow) | Environmental | \[Obscurement\] \[Negative Light\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius  
* **Duration:** 1 Round per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Fills an area with supernatural shadow that blocks normal and low-light vision.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Integrity:** At Stage 3+, blocks Darkvision. At Stage 5, blocks Digital Sensors.  
* **Area:** Radius \= 20ft per Stage.

### 

### **SHADOW WEAPON**

Classification: Illusion (Shadow) | Attack | \[Creation\] \[Semi-Real\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Personal  
* **Area:** Weapon Item  
* **Duration:** 1 Round per Stage (or 1 Min/Stage with KP)  
* **Resistance:** Will (Disbelief for reduced damage)

Effect Description:

Solidifies shadows into a melee weapon. It deals real damage, though part of the trauma is psychosomatic.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 1d6 Cold/Psychic Damage per Stage.  
* **Reality:** Will Save CR to disbelieve increases by \+1 per Stage.

### 

### **SHADOW CONJURING**

Classification: Illusion (Shadow) | Utility/Attack | \[Summoning\] \[Quasi-Real\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Object or Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Round per Stage (or 1 Min/Stage with KP)  
* **Resistance:** Will (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Conjures a solid creature or item from ether. Creatures act as a temporary Companion (see Bestiary rules).

**Scaling Function:**

* **Size:** Max Dimensions \= 2ft per Stage per side.  
* **Weight:** Max Weight \= 10 lbs x (Stage squared).  
* **Reality:** Percentage of real HP/Damage \= 20% x Stage.

### 

### **SHADOW ASSAULT**

Classification: Illusion (Shadow) | Attack | \[Evocation\] \[Cold\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Single Target or Burst  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (Half) & Will (Disbelief)

Effect Description:

Evokes a blast of roiling shadow energy that mimics a physical attack.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 1d6 Shadow Damage per Stage.  
* **Debuff:** Targets taking damage are Entangled (Shadows cling) for 1 round per Stage.
