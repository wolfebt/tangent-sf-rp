# **ASI**

# **ASI**

This centralized codex establishes the overarching **Asi** classification system, a comprehensive taxonomic designation that officially supercedes all archaic historical listings of "Fey" within the standard operational data sets.

## ---

**1\. NOMENCLATURE & REGIONAL PHONETICS**

The designation of these entities varies significantly by sector, language family, and proximity to planar rifts. While compiled centrally under the designation **Asi**, field operatives, cartographers, and local populations utilize three primary regional variants:

* **Asi:** The standardized galactic and academic designation. It identifies the macro-classification of entities whose biological or energetic baselines are tethered directly to systemic meta-energies and planar anomalies.  
* **Aos:** The dominant phonetic variant utilized throughout the outer rim territories and sectors deep within old-growth planetary systems. It carries a heavy cultural connotation of ancestral permanence and localized world-binding.  
* **Ayz:** A sharp, localized variant frequently recorded within industrialized systems, asteroid settlements, and orbital hubs where atmospheric friction or localized slang has condensed the phoneme.

### **The Taxon Separation Protocol**

To prevent systemic errors in database indexing and diplomatic deployment, a strict demarcation is maintained between the macro-category and a specific endemic population:

1. **The Classification Name (Asi / Aos / Ayz):** Replaces "Fey" as the systemic category identifier. It describes any species whose morphology, origin, or core capability is derived from localized environmental generation or raw meta-attunement.  
2. **The Species Name (Fey):** The specific, traditional five-inch anthropomorphic humanoid lineage characterized by delicate, insectoid wings retains its distinct historical name: the **Fey** species. A member of this species is properly cataloged as a *Diminutive Asi of the Fey lineage*.

## ---

**2\. METAPHYSICAL & ECOLOGICAL FOUNDATION**

The species grouped under the **Asi** designation do not share a singular, monolithic genetic ancestry. Instead, they represent a highly variant collection of loosely related lineages bound by a shared phenomenon: **Environmental Synthesis and Aspect Embodiment**.

### **Environmental Genesis**

Every Asi lineage is inextricably linked to the specific ecosystem, planar tear, or atmospheric condition from which it first coalesced. They do not merely inhabit an environment; they are an extension of its internal logic. If an ecosystem possesses a high concentration of Meta-energy, it inevitably forces life to adapt along non-standard evolutionary paths, creating localized physical manifestations of abstract natural laws, physical forces, or ecological balances.

### **Mythical Level Incarnations**

Depending on their specific **Meta Level** (ranging from localized sensory adaptors to high-tier planar anomalies), Asi manifest as living embodiments of nature's varied facets:

* **Kinetic & Spatial Equilibrium:** Embodied by lineages that manipulate momentum, gravity, or physical orientation within extreme terrain (e.g., the Ferren and Sigal).  
* **Liminality & Twilight:** Embodied by shape-shifting lineages that exist between states of matter, light, and identity, acting as the boundary layers of sentient observation (e.g., the Whisper and Koban).  
* **Primal Animus & Camouflage:** Embodied by megafaunal or macro-scale humanoids that act as the active immune system of pristine biomes, shifting seamlessly out of standard visual spectra to safeguard natural structural balances (e.g., the Tozen).  
* **Arcane Macro-Ecosystems:** Embodied by minuscule or avian lineages that handle the micro-scale distribution of magical currents, record-keeping, and floral pollination across hidden folds of reality (e.g., the Frellin and Fey).

**4\. INDIVIDUAL SPECIES COMPENDIUM**

### **Whisper**

* **Classification:** Asi (Shapechanger)  
* **Size:** Medium / Fine  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Groundspeed / 30 ft Flightspeed  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Cha  
* **Innate Features:** Alter Form (choose 1 category) 2, Alternate Form (Wisp \- fine size) 2, Awakened (Nature Charisma) 2, Obfuscate 2, Silent 2  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *The Liminality of Twilight.* Whispers embody the transitional state between light and shadow, form and void. They coalesce in the quiet boundaries of primeval forests or planar rifts, existing natively as a faint, hovering mote of ethereal luminescence known as a Wisp. When interacting with external sentience, they assume an enchanting yet uncanny adolescent Elven form. This form is marked by an unnatural stillness and an eerie, detached grace, signaling that their physical presence is merely a projection of a deeper, fluid identity capable of shifting into any observed form or shadow.

### **Tozen**

* **Classification:** Quadruped Asi  
* **Size:** Medium (5 ft)  
* **Movement:** 40 ft Groundspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 1 | **Meta Level:** 5  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Agi, \+1 Wis  
* **Innate Features:** Awakened (Natural Wisdom), Obfuscate, Quadruped, Quick  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *The Sylvan Immune System.* Tozen are a mythical incarnation of the untouched, sovereign wilderness. Possessing a majestic, centauric morphology that seamlessly fuses a lithe humanoid torso with the powerful four-legged lower chassis of a deer or gazelle, they act as the silent guardians of ancient growths. They are so profoundly integrated into the natural rhythm of their home biomes that their stealth borders on structural phasing; they do not merely hide among the trees—they become a literal extension of the dappled light and shifting foliage, moving in plain sight completely unseen by unperceiving eyes.

### **Sigal**

* **Classification:** Elemental Asi  
* **Size:** Diminutive (6 to 12 inches)  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Flightspeed (Hover)  
* **Tech Level:** 0 | **Meta Level:** 5  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Int  
* **Innate Features:** Awakened (Arcane Intellect \- Energy), Awakened (Arcane Intellect \- Illusion), Flight (TK), Limbless, Non-Living, Photographic Memory, Telekinesis  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *The Crystalline Record and Spatial Force.* The Sigal are an inorganic manifestation of planetary pressure, mineral growth, and the structural mathematics of the cosmos. Taking the form of floating, diamond-cut gemstones that span the vibrant mineral spectrum from deep amethyst to fiery opal, they are entirely devoid of limbs or organic vulnerability. They interact with reality through flawless, innate telekinesis. Acting as living repositories of planetary memory, they dedicate their centuries of existence to the absolute preservation of knowledge, manipulating Force Magic to anchor environments and Shadow Magic to conceal their sacred archives.

### **Koban**

* **Classification:** Asi (Shapechanger)  
* **Size:** Small / Medium  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Groundspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 0 | **Meta Level:** 4  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Any  
* **Innate Features:** Climber, Alter Form (Humanoid), Awakened (Nature, Wis)  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *Faunal Calm and Social Adaptation.* True-form Kobans are diminutive, highly observant leporid-felid hybrids standing roughly 0.7 to 1.0 meters tall, featuring dense black-and-white coats, prehensile tails, and long, independently rotating ears. They embody the patient, calculating aspect of nature that observes without interfering. Viewing physical technology as a crude, temporary crutch, they rely entirely on direct Meta-manipulation. To navigate the wider, technologically obsessed galaxy, they manifest a permanent, specific Medium-sized humanoid "Guise," allowing them to operate as unassuming diplomats, spies, or traders who reveal their primal Asi nature only when crisis dictates.

### **Wildling *(Koban Lineage)***

* **Classification:** Asi (Shapechanger)  
* **Size:** Small / Medium  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Groundspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 0 | **Meta Level:** 4  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Ability of choice  
* **Innate Features:** Animal Affinity, Climber, Alter Form (Animal), Awakened (Nature, Wis)  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *Symbiotic Totemic Animus.* Sharing a close ancestral and genetic root with the Koban, the Wildlings represent a divergent ecological path focused outward into the faunal kingdom. Distinguished by mottled, earth-toned coats of fawn, cream, and russet, they bypass the humanoid guise entirely. Instead, they embody a deep, spiritual integration with the beasts of the wild, shifting their physical form into true animal shapes. They maintain a permanent, telepathic affinity with native wildlife, acting as the voice and collective consciousness of a planet's migratory herds and apex predators.

### **Frellin**

* **Classification:** Avian Asi  
* **Size:** Tiny  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Flightspeed / 10 ft Groundspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 1 | **Meta Level:** 5  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Int, \+1 Cha  
* **Innate Features:** Awakened (Arcane), Telepathy (Special), Flight (Wings), Darksight (Special)  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *Refracted Light and Scripted Fate.* Originating from the enigmatic, magic-soaked world of Frodleikr, the Frellin are tiny avian humanoids hatched directly from fruits engorged with raw planar energy. They are the living embodiment of cosmic air currents and pre-destined geometric order. Covered in radiant, multicolored plumage that generates its own shifting, internal arcane light, their piercing golden eyes possess hyper-perceptive sight. They worship Shal’Vir, the Scripted Fate utilizing advanced telepathy to observe, catalog, and subtly nudge the timeline of lesser species.

### **Fey**

* **Classification:** Diminutive Asi *(Species Name: Fey)*  
* **Size:** Diminutive (Maximum 5 inches)  
* **Movement:** 30 ft Flightspeed / 10 ft Groundspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 1 | **Meta Level:** 5  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Cha  
* **Innate Features:** Awakened (Nature \- Cha), Ageless, Obfuscate, Sleepless, Size (Diminutive), Flight (Insect Wings), Stigma (Xeno, Fae)  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *Micro-Scale Vitality and Ageless Nature.* This lineage represents the classic, historical baseline that preserves the traditional species title of **Fey**. Standing no taller than five inches, these anthropomorphic, ethereal humanoids are the direct embodiment of micro-scale organic life, hidden fungal networks, and the delicate vitality of budding flora. Propelled by shimmering, gossamer wings structurally identical to those of a dragonfly, they inhabit pocket dimensions and hidden macro-environments where hollowed roots and woven silk serve as advanced infrastructure, existing in a state of ageless, sleepless attunement to the planet's life cycle.

### **Ferren**

* **Classification:** Asi Humanoid  
* **Size:** Medium  
* **Movement:** 40 ft Groundspeed / 40 ft Climbspeed  
* **Tech Level:** 3 | **Meta Level:** 4  
* **Core Attribute Modifiers:** \+1 Agi  
* **Innate Features:** Prehensile Tail, Climber, Quick, Adapted (Arboreal)  
* **Aspect Embodiment:** *Kinetic Equilibrium and High-Canopy Sensory Flow.* Ferren are slender, elf-like sylvan humanoids who embody the chaotic, lightning-fast kinetic energy of the high forest canopy. They feature a unique, highly specialized prehensile split tail that acts as a dynamic counterweight, allowing them to pull off physics-defying leaps, course corrections, and vertical climbs. Often misjudged by outsiders as having a frantic, short attention span, their minds actually operate on a level of advanced parallel multitasking; a Ferren can simultaneously map structural branch integrity, track olfactory vectors of distant predators, and analyze local air currents without a single drop in spatial efficiency.
