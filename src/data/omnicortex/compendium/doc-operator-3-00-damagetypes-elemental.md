# **DAMAGE TYPES: VARIATIVE & ELEMENTAL**

## **VARIATIVE DAMAGE TYPES**

These are other damage types that are not of typical origins.

&nbsp;

### **Radiant / Light Damage**

This damage type focuses on the use of pure light and radiant energy to harm targets. It can cause immediate pain, burning sensations, and potentially long-term effects like blindness or disruption of energy fields. It is particularly effective against creatures vulnerable to light, such as those that thrive in darkness or have light sensitivity. Radiant damage can also disrupt or dispel certain magical effects, especially those associated with darkness or shadow.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Photokinesis: The ability to create and control light with the mind. This includes generating bursts of light, shaping light into various forms, and manipulating the intensity and spectrum of light. Photokinesis can be used for both offensive and defensive purposes, such as blinding enemies or creating barriers of light.  
* Light Evocation: Summoning entities composed of or empowered by light. These beings can unleash powerful radiant attacks, are often resistant or immune to light damage, and can provide tactical advantages in combat, such as illuminating dark areas or dispelling shadows.  
* Light Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with radiant magic. This can grant them the ability to emit blinding light, deal additional radiant damage, or even provide a measure of protection against darkness-based attacks.

Archaic Tech

* Mirrors and Reflectors: Using polished surfaces to focus and direct sunlight, creating intense beams of light that can burn or blind targets.  
* Lamps and Torches: Simple light sources that can be used to illuminate dark areas and potentially cause discomfort or minor damage to light-sensitive creatures.

Modern Tech

* Flashbangs and Stun Grenades: Devices that emit a blinding flash of light and a loud noise, disorienting and temporarily incapacitating targets.  
* High-Intensity Lights: Powerful spotlights or searchlights that can be used to illuminate large areas and potentially cause damage to light-sensitive creatures.

Advanced Tech

* Laser Weapons: High-energy laser beams can be focused to deliver intense bursts of light, causing both immediate and potential long-term damage.  
* Particle Beam Weapons: Weapons that fire streams of energetic particles, which can produce radiant energy upon impact, causing damage and potentially disrupting energy fields.  
* Holographic Projectors: Advanced devices that can create complex light patterns and illusions, potentially causing disorientation or even damage to targets with light sensitivity.

Additional Notes: Radiant damage can cause blinding effects, and some weapons may have additional effects like dispelling darkness or disrupting magical effects. Characters can use radiant weapons to target vulnerable enemies or to illuminate dark areas, providing tactical advantages in combat.

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

### **Umbral / Shadow Damage**

Shadow damage taps into the primal darkness and negative energy, inflicting harm by draining vitality, obscuring senses, and potentially causing fear or despair. It is effective against creatures vulnerable to darkness, negative energy, or those with strong connections to the physical world.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Umbrokinesis: The ability to manipulate shadows and darkness. This includes creating illusions, obscuring vision, and even solidifying shadows into tangible forms to attack or ensnare targets. Umbrokinesis can be used for both offensive and defensive purposes.  
* Shadow Evocation: Summoning entities composed of or empowered by shadow, such as shadow creatures or dark spirits. These beings can unleash shadow-based attacks, often with effects like draining life force, causing fear, or obscuring vision. They are often resistant or immune to shadow damage.  
* Shadow Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with shadow magic. This can grant them the ability to drain vitality on impact, deal additional shadow damage, or even create areas of darkness around the target.

Archaic Tech

* Darkness Powder: Alchemical substances that create clouds of obscuring darkness, disorienting and hindering opponents.  
* Shadow Traps: Mechanisms designed to create areas of intense darkness or to trigger illusions and fear effects.

Modern Tech

* Strobe Lights (Disorienting): While not directly shadow damage, rapidly flashing lights can create disorienting shadow effects, hindering vision and coordination.  
* Camouflage Systems: Advanced systems that manipulate light and shadow to create illusions and make targets difficult to detect.

Advanced Tech

* Dark Energy Weapons: Weapons that fire blasts of dark energy, draining vitality and potentially causing fear or despair.  
* Shadow Fields: Devices that generate areas of intense darkness or manipulate shadows to create illusions and disorient opponents.

Additional Notes: Shadow damage can cause effects such as draining vitality, obscuring vision, causing fear, or disorienting targets. Some attacks may also disrupt light sources or create areas of darkness. Characters can use shadow damage to weaken opponents, control the battlefield, or exploit vulnerabilities related to light and darkness.

### **Air Elemental Damage**

Air elemental damage is a unique form of energy damage that harnesses the raw power of wind, pressure, and atmospheric forces. It can manifest in several ways, including concussive blasts of air, debilitating gusts, or even the disruption of a target's internal equilibrium through pressure changes. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures that rely on flight or have vulnerabilities to wind-based effects.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Aerokinesis: The ability to manipulate air currents, creating powerful gusts, focused blasts, or even localized vacuum pockets. This can result in physical harm through blunt force trauma, suffocation, or disorientation.  
* Air Evocation: Summoning entities composed of or empowered by air, such as wind spirits or elementals. These beings can unleash powerful air-based attacks, often with effects like pushing, stunning, or even tearing at a target with high-speed winds.  
* Air Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with air magic. This can grant them the ability to create gusts of wind on impact, deal additional concussive damage, or even disrupt the target's balance and movement.

Archaic Tech

* Bellows and Fans: Simple devices used to create powerful gusts of air, which can be used to disorient or push back opponents.  
* Wind Traps: Mechanisms designed to channel and amplify natural wind currents, creating localized areas of high-pressure or dangerous gusts.

Modern Tech

* Compressed Air Cannons: Weapons that fire blasts of highly compressed air, delivering concussive force and potentially causing internal injuries.  
* Sonic Disruptors: Devices that use air vibrations to create disruptive frequencies, causing disorientation, nausea, or even physical harm.

Advanced Tech

* Vortex Generators: Advanced technologies that create localized vortices or miniature tornadoes, capable of tearing through objects or severely disrupting a target's stability.  
* Pressure Wave Emitters: Devices that emit focused waves of air pressure, capable of delivering concussive blasts or even causing internal damage through rapid pressure changes.

Additional Notes: Air elemental damage can cause effects such as being pushed back, knocked prone, disoriented, or even suffocated. Some attacks may also disrupt flight or other air-dependent abilities. Characters can use air elemental attacks to control the battlefield, manipulate enemy positions, or target vulnerabilities related to flight or balance.

### **Fire Elemental Damage**

Fire Elemental Damage focuses on the raw power of flame and heat to harm targets. It can cause immediate burning sensations, ignite flammable materials, and inflict ongoing damage over time. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures vulnerable to fire, such as those that are cold-based or made of wood/paper, and can create hazardous environmental conditions.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Pyrokinesis: The ability to create and control fire with the mind. This includes conjuring flames, shaping them into various forms, and directing their intensity. Pyrokinesis can be used for both offensive and utility purposes, such as creating a wall of fire or igniting a distant target.  
* Fire Evocation: Summoning fire elementals or spirits to fight on your behalf. These creatures can unleash powerful fire attacks, are often resistant or immune to fire damage, and can provide tactical advantages in combat.  
* Fire Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with fire magic. This can grant them the ability to ignite targets on contact, deal additional fire damage, or even provide protection against fire-based attacks.

Archaic Tech

* Torches, Arrows, and Grenades: The simplest forms of fire weaponry. Torches can be used for melee attacks, arrows tipped with flammable substances for ranged attacks, and grenades filled with incendiary materials for area-effect damage.  
* Flamethrowers: Primitive flamethrowers project a stream of ignited fuel, dealing continuous fire damage to targets in a cone or line. They are effective at close range and can cause widespread panic.  
* Fire Traps: Mechanisms that use fire to damage or deter intruders. These can range from simple pits filled with flammable liquids to complex traps that ignite automatically when triggered.

Modern Tech

* Flamethrowers: Modern flamethrowers are more advanced, with greater range, accuracy, and fuel efficiency. They can be mounted on vehicles or wielded by infantry, providing a powerful area denial weapon.  
* Incendiary Rounds: Ammunition for firearms can be designed to ignite upon impact, setting targets ablaze and causing ongoing fire damage. This is especially effective against flammable materials or creatures vulnerable to fire.  
* Fire Bombs: Explosive devices filled with incendiary compounds can be deployed to create large fires, damaging structures and causing widespread damage in an area.

Advanced Tech

* Laser Weapons: High-energy laser beams can be focused to ignite targets, causing both immediate and ongoing fire damage. Laser weapons are known for their precision and range, making them effective at targeting specific body parts or vulnerable areas.  
* Plasma Weapons: These weapons project superheated plasma, dealing devastating fire damage and potentially melting through armor. Plasma weapons are powerful but often have shorter ranges and may require cooling periods between shots.  
* Energy Pulse Weapons: Advanced energy weapons can emit pulses of intense heat, setting targets on fire and causing damage over time. These weapons can have various firing modes, including burst and spray, allowing for tactical flexibility.

Additional Notes: Fire damage can cause ongoing burning damage, and some weapons may have additional effects like armor-piercing or increased critical hit chance against flammable targets. Characters can use fire weapons to target vulnerable areas for increased damage or to create environmental hazards that can hinder or damage opponents.

### **Earth Elemental Damage**

Earth elemental damage harnesses the raw power of the earth's physical forces. It can manifest in various ways, including concussive force from rocks, tremors that disrupt balance, or even the crushing pressure of shifting earth and stone. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures vulnerable to physical trauma or those that rely on stable ground.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Geokinesis: The ability to manipulate earth and stone with the mind. This includes creating rock slides, causing tremors, shaping earth into barriers or weapons, and even manipulating the density of the ground. Geokinesis can be used for both offensive and defensive purposes.  
* Earth Evocation: Summoning entities composed of or empowered by earth, such as earth elementals or golems. These beings can unleash powerful earth-based attacks, are often resistant or immune to earth damage, and can provide tactical advantages in combat.  
* Earth Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with earth magic. This can grant them the ability to cause tremors on impact, deal additional bludgeoning damage, or even provide protection against earth-based attacks.

Archaic Tech

* Siege Weapons: Catapults, ballistae, and other siege engines that launch large rocks or debris, causing significant impact damage.  
* Traps: Pitfalls, rockfalls, and other traps that use the environment to inflict earth-based damage.

Modern Tech

* Explosives: Controlled explosions that can cause ground tremors, rockslides, and other earth-based damage.  
* Heavy Machinery: Bulldozers, excavators, and other heavy machinery that can be used to manipulate the environment and inflict crushing damage.

Advanced Tech

* Seismic Weapons: Devices that generate focused seismic waves, causing localized earthquakes or tremors.  
* Gravity Manipulation: Advanced technologies that can manipulate gravity fields, causing objects to be crushed or slammed into the ground with immense force.

Additional Notes: Earth elemental damage can cause effects such as being knocked prone, buried, crushed, or disoriented. Some attacks may also disrupt movement or cause structural damage to the environment. Characters can use earth elemental attacks to control the battlefield, create obstacles, or target vulnerabilities related to stability and physical integrity.

### **Water Elemental Damage**

Water elemental damage harnesses the raw power of water in its various forms. It can manifest as powerful jets of water, crushing pressure, or even drowning effects. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures vulnerable to water, such as those with a weakness to drowning or those susceptible to the force of rushing water.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Hydrokinesis: The ability to manipulate water, creating powerful jets, waves, or even localized floods. This can result in physical harm through bludgeoning, drowning, or being swept away.  
* Water Evocation: Summoning entities composed of or empowered by water, such as water elementals or spirits. These beings can unleash powerful water-based attacks, often with effects like pushing, drowning, or creating hazardous water conditions.  
* Water Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with water magic. This can grant them the ability to create jets of water on impact, deal additional bludgeoning damage, or even cause a drowning effect on the target.

Archaic Tech

* Water Wheels and Pumps: Simple devices used to create powerful jets of water, which can be used to disorient or push back opponents.  
* Water Traps: Mechanisms designed to channel and amplify natural water flow, creating localized areas of high pressure or dangerous currents.

Modern Tech

* High-Pressure Water Cannons: Weapons that fire blasts of highly pressurized water, delivering concussive force and potentially causing internal injuries or pushing targets back.  
* Water-Based Sonic Disruptors: Devices that use water vibrations to create disruptive frequencies, causing disorientation or nausea.

Advanced Tech

* Water Vortex Generators: Advanced technologies that create localized vortices or whirlpools, capable of tearing through objects or severely disrupting a target's stability.  
* Pressure Wave Emitters: Devices that emit focused waves of water pressure, capable of delivering concussive blasts or even causing internal damage through rapid pressure changes.

Additional Notes: Water elemental damage can cause effects such as being pushed back, knocked prone, disoriented, or even drowned. Some attacks may also disrupt movement or cause environmental hazards. Characters can use water elemental attacks to control the battlefield, manipulate enemy positions, or target vulnerabilities related to breathing or stability in water.
