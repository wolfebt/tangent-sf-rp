## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every rule, background package, and sociological profile in **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** adheres to these canonical mechanical laws (`/game_rules/operator/` authority):

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Math:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`) equals `-10 to Attack/Check Score` + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Allocations (0 CP Baseline):** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` across **Faction (`20 SP`) + Origin (`20 SP`) + Occupation (`20 SP`)**, alongside **4 Recommended Features** (`-1 CP` discount: `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and **4 Background Traits** at `0 CP` (`2 Origin Traits` + `2 Occupation Traits`; **Extra Traits cost `1 CP` each**).
* **Standardized Faction Wealth & Lore Anchors:** The **Kovian Tribunal (Nocturne Aeld)** is standardized at **`+3 WS`** (`TL 3 / ML 4`), and the **Radiant Impyrium** includes the canonical **Psycholith** psycho-reactive Aetherite-ceramic mineral codex (`+5 WS`, `TL 4+ / ML 4`).
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct characters combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum `+10 CP` gained from personal Hindrances during creation; Species-level disadvantages and cultural Stigmas are exempt.

PLANETARY
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Highly focused factions dedicated entirely to the development, exploitation, and defense of a single planet or local system. They possess limited interstellar reach but wield absolute, deeply entrenched power within their own gravity well.
* Key Themes: System Isolationists, Home-Field Advantage, Local Pride.
* Relationship to Others: Defensive and isolationist. They dislike interstellar meddling.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The [Planet Name] Directorate / Sovereign System
* Colloquialisms: Townies / Dirt-siders / Locals
* Archetype: System Isolationists
* Capital/Key World: Their single home planet
* Driving Mandate: Control their planet, protect resources, defend against external threats.
* Motto: "Our world, our rules."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Dominant, territorial, resourceful, protective.
* Social Structure: Variable (Planetary governors, local councils).
* View on Outsiders: Unwelcome tourists or potential invaders.
* Law & Order: Strict local laws designed to protect local resources.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Supreme knowledge of their planet, access to local resources, strong community bonds, deep infrastructure.
* Weaknesses: Limited interstellar capabilities, vulnerable to orbital bombardment or blockade, internal divisions.
* Military Doctrine: Static defense, planetary shields, and utilizing local weather/geography against invaders.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Locally adapted).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Survival (+5)
   * Knowledge (Local) (+5)
   * Mechanics (+4)
   * Athletics (+3)
   * Combat (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Governor: The supreme bureaucratic authority managing planetary affairs.
   * The Defender: A heavily armed militiaman protecting their home soil.
   * The Local Scientist: An expert in the specific, unique geology of the world.
   * The Explorer: Charting the dangerous, untamed wilderness of the home world.
   * The Militia Commander: Organizing local forces to repel interstellar invaders.
   * The Resource Tycoon: Controlling the vital mines or farms that keep the planet alive.
   * The Native Guide: Leading outsiders through deadly, localized terrain.
   * The Smuggler: Evading planetary blockades to bring in off-world goods.
   * The Separatist: Fighting to ensure the planet remains free from empire control.
   * The Agrarian: Managing the massive planetary farms that feed the population.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Self Sufficient: Surviving effortlessly on the unique flora/fauna of the world.
   * Home Field Advantage: Gaining tactical bonuses when fighting on familiar terrain.
   * Tracker: Hunting enemies across the specific biomes of the home planet.
   * Benefit (Status - Local): Wielding immense political power, but only on this world.
   * Connected (Local): Knowing every merchant, guard, and politician in the capital.
   * Endurance: Surviving the extreme, localized weather patterns of the planet.
   * Danger Sense: Knowing exactly when the local environment is about to turn deadly.
   * Gearhead: Keeping old planetary infrastructure and moisture vaporators running.
   * Ride/Pilot: Mastering the specific, rugged vehicles required for local transit.
   * Tough: Hardened by a lifetime spent under a harsh, localized sun.
