# 1.05.09 ORIGIN: RESEARCH (+2 WS)

* **Executive Summary:** University worlds, deep-space science stations, and classified black-site laboratories (New Aurelia, Altheria).
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Modifier:** **+2 WS**
* **Canonical Society Skills (20 SP Allocation):** Academics, Computers, Alertness, Knowledge (Investigation, Medicine, Physics, Science, Technology), Mental (Any)
* **Typical Archetypes:** Lab Scientist, Xenologist, Field Researcher, AI Programmer, Archivist, Scientist, Analyst, Theorist, Technician, Security Officer

---

# **RESEARCH**

A complex or habitat dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge. These worlds are focused on the systematic scientific investigation of a particular subject. Structures can range from prefabricated or mobile to large laboratory complexes, depending on need and resources.

## **Traits**

### *Adaptability*

The ability to adapt to new environments and situations is important for a research character, as they may encounter unexpected obstacles.

*\+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily*

### *Analytical thinking*

Researchers would be skilled at analyzing data and drawing conclusions from it. They would be able to identify patterns and trends in complex information.

*\+2 Reason (Intellect) Checks*

### *Attention to detail*

Researchers would have a keen eye for detail and be able to notice small nuances in data. They would be meticulous in their work and ensure that their research is accurate and precise.

*\+2 Alertness*

### *Collaboration*

Researchers would be able to work well with others, including other researchers and non-researchers alike. They would be able to communicate their findings effectively and work together to achieve common goals.

*additional \+1 to Aid bonuses received and granted*

### *Curiosity*

The character has a natural curiosity about the world around them, which drives them to explore and discover new things in the aquatic environment.

*Regain a Karma Point on the first encounter when dealing with something new during the session \- creature, area, technology, etc.*

### *Intellectualism*

Researchers would be knowledgeable and intelligent, with a deep understanding of their field of study. They would likely have a high level of education and expertise in their area of research.

*\+2 Academics and \+2 to a Mental Skill Specialization*

### *Persistence*

Researchers would be persistent in their pursuit of knowledge and discovery. They would not give up easily and would be willing to put in the time and effort required to achieve their goals.

*\+1 Karma Point*

### *Problem-solving*

Researchers would be adept at solving problems and overcoming obstacles. They would be able to think creatively and come up with innovative solutions to challenges.

*Once per Day may reroll a Mental Skill Check*

### *Studious*

Gained increased knowledge on a topic through diligent research.

*\+2 to a Knowledge of choice*

## Consolidated & Modernized Origin Traits (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)

*(Players may select their 2 free Origin Traits from either the narrative traits above or the consolidated mechanical traits below.)*

* **Analytical Mind:** Once per day, reroll a failed Knowledge (Investigation) or Knowledge (Science) check.
* **Data Synthesis:** Roll Academics (Research) checks in libraries or databases at Advantage.
* **Laboratory Safety:** +4 bonus to prototyping checks to avoid chemical or technological mishaps.
* **Methodical:** Increases the bonus from Meticulous time investment by +1 per step.
* **Peer Network:** Gather Information checks within academic, scientific, or medical communities are rolled at Advantage.
* **Tech Savant:** +2 to Computers and Knowledge (Technology) checks.

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis (CRISP 11-Faction Matrix)**

## **09\. RESEARCH**

*Science, discovery, labs, observatories, knowledge.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Digging up ancient tech. Dusty libraries of scrolls. Scientists who are also priests. Candlelight mixed with monitors.  
* **STYLE:** Archeotech Excavation, Gothic Science.  
* **PALETTE:** Parchment Yellow, Dust Brown, Candle Light, Monitor Green.  
* **TOKENS:** Scrolls, artifacts, digging, priests, magnifying glass.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Sterile, windowless labs. Human testing subjects. Top secret prototypes. Cold, unethical, advanced.  
* **STYLE:** R\&D Black Site, Unethical Lab.  
* **PALETTE:** Clinical White, Stainless Steel, Hazard Orange.  
* **TOKENS:** Glass, operating tables, prototypes, sterility.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Studying monsters and dragons. Cages, dissection tables, heavy chains. Biological research for war.  
* **STYLE:** Royal Bestiary, Frankenstein's Lab.  
* **PALETTE:** Blood Red, Biological Pink, Iron Black, Formaldehyde.  
* **TOKENS:** Cages, monsters, charts, chains, organs.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** A tent on an alien world. Muddy boots, sample cases, rugged laptops. Studying the unknown with duct-taped gear.  
* **STYLE:** Field Outpost, Xeno-Survey.  
* **PALETTE:** Tent Orange, Mud Brown, Alien Flora colors.  
* **TOKENS:** Tents, laptops, samples, mud, boots.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Basement meth-lab vibe but for high-tech drugs/weapons. Bubbling beakers, flickering lights, ventilator fans.  
* **STYLE:** Illegal Chem-Lab, Underground Tech.  
* **PALETTE:** Chemical Green, sickly Yellow, Shadow, Neon.  
* **TOKENS:** Beakers, fumes, fans, dirty, wires.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Cross-breeding plants. Grafting tables. DNA sequencing visualized as growing vines. Gentle, patient science.  
* **STYLE:** Genetic Garden, Mendel's Greenhouse.  
* **PALETTE:** Plant Green, Pollen Yellow, DNA Hologram Blue.  
* **TOKENS:** Plants, grafting, DNA, sunlight, growth.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Giant holographic maps of the galaxy. Studying the movement of stars. Magic used to calculate physics.  
* **STYLE:** Planetary Orrery, Stellar Cartography.  
* **PALETTE:** Star Black, Galaxy Purple, Hologram Gold.  
* **TOKENS:** Holograms, stars, maps, magic, silence.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Open-plan offices, glass walls, collaborative spaces. Robots and humans working together. Coffee shops and whiteboards.  
* **STYLE:** Innovation Hub, Silicon Valley.  
* **PALETTE:** Whiteboard White, Marker Colors, Coffee Brown, Glass.  
* **TOKENS:** Computers, collaboration, coffee, light, ideas.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** Studying other cultures. Artifacts on display. Translators working on ancient texts. Respectful and academic.  
* **STYLE:** Xenology Museum, Library.  
* **PALETTE:** Old Book Brown, Museum Lighting, artifact Gold.  
* **TOKENS:** Books, artifacts, translators, quiet, study.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** A room that is just one giant computer. Cooling pipes, blinking lights, heat. The research is purely simulation.  
* **STYLE:** The Great Calculation, Supercomputer.  
* **PALETTE:** Server Black, LED Red/Green, Cable Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Servers, cables, lights, heat, humming.

### **KITIN COLLECTIVE (THE STAR HIVE)**

* **CONTEXT:** A Genomatrix Evolutionary Laboratory where Manelli geneticists steer real-time embryonic adaptations inside translucent amber stasis cysts. Sensory pit arrays and living neural wetware sequence captured xeno-DNA.
* **STYLE:** Genetic Steering Sanctum, Biomorphic Wetware Lab.
* **PALETTE:** Stasis Amber, Proto-Flux Green, Synaptic Cyan, Glossy Obsidian.
* **TOKENS:** Pre-hatch stasis pods, DNA helix projections, Manelli brood-scientists, living microscopes, neural wetware.
