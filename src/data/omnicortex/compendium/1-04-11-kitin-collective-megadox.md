# 1.04.11 The Kitin Collective: Hive Hierarchy, Bio-Tech & Galactic Mandate

The **Kitin Collective** represents one of the most formidable, highly synchronized eusocial civilizations in known space. Composed of trillions of chitinous, multi-limbed individuals linked through biological pheromone relays and deep psychic sub-frequencies, the Collective functions as an integrated macro-organism.

---

## I. Hive Caste Taxonomy
The Kitin civilization is organized into specialized castes engineered from the egg for distinct biological functions:

| Caste Designation | Primary Morphological Function | Average Size | Key Biological Features |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Drones / Workers** | Hive excavation, bio-synthesis, brood nurturing, farming. | Small – Medium | Multi-tool limbs, high stamina, biological acid spit. |
| **Warriors / Soldiers** | Frontline combat, perimeter defense, suppression. | Medium – Large | Heavy chitin carapace (DR 8+), serrated scythe limbs, venom gland. |
| **Praetorians / Royal Guard**| Defense of the Hive Queen, elite shock assault. | Large – Huge | Reinforced heavy armor (DR 12+), dual bio-plasma cannons, fearless. |
| **Bio-Engineers / Weavers** | Genetic sculpting, architectural bio-resin synthesis. | Medium | Pheromone synthesis organ, delicate neuro-manipulators. |
| **Queens / Hive Matriarchs** | Consciousness relay, psychic beacon, reproduction. | Huge – Gargantuan| Telepathic command network, massive psychic presence, egg-chamber. |

---

## II. Bio-Technology & Symbiotic Weaponry
The Kitin do not manufacture cold metal firearms or silicon computers; their technology is entirely grown from genetically sculpted living tissue:

- **Bio-Acid Spitters:** Living symbiotic symbiotes mounted on forearms that fire pressurized streams of caustic bio-acid (3d8 Acid damage, corrodes 2 DR/round).
- **Chitinous Spore Launchers:** Organic projectile organs that fire burrowing needle-spores (2d10 Piercing damage, causes ongoing neurotoxin damage).
- **Bio-Resin Fortifications:** Rapid-hardening secretions that form high-DR walls (DR 20) in minutes.
- **Living Bio-Armor Carapaces:** Symbiotic exoskeletons that heal damage alongside the host (regenerates 2 HP/round).

---

## III. Pheromonal Language & The Deep Chord
- **Pheromonal Dispersion:** Kitin communicate instantly within 100 meters via airborne chemical cocktails transmitting emotional state, tactical targets, and spatial coords.
- **The Deep Chord:** A low-frequency metaphysical resonance that connects Queens across star systems, allowing coordinated hive movements that baffle standard electronic interception.

## Game Mechanics Rules
```
Hive Coordination: Kitin gain +2 to passive Defense and +1 to all attack rolls when within 30 ft of another conscious Kitin.
Bio-Acid Corrosive Effect: Hits reduce target's kinetic and energy Armor DR by 2 points until repaired.
```

## Gameplay Instructions
Kitin operatives who venture outside the hive are often independent Synapse Seekers or exiled bio-drones seeking trade and genetic archives.

## Designer Notes
The Kitin Collective maintains uneasy neutrality with the Free Worlds Coalition while fiercely defending their quarantine zones.
