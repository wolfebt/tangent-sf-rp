# **AULURANS**

# **AULURANS**

The Aulurans are a highly advanced feline species defined by profound loss and radical evolutionary adaptation. Following the catastrophic destruction of their original, verdant homeworld during an Impyrium Incursion, this species rejected traditional static industrialism to weaponize life itself, terraforming fringe worlds into virulent, hyper-accelerated jungles that serve as both impenetrable fortresses and pseudo-living machines. Physically, they are characterized by their feline attributes, possessing swiveling ears for acute sound detection, large expressive eyes, sharp retractable claws, and long prehensile tails, with fur ranging from stark white, cream, and fawn to copper, blue, and pitch black adorned with symmetrical patterns.

Operating at Tech Level 4 with a Meta Level of 4 using biotechnology and a telepathic web known as "The Chorus," Auluran society functions as a strict but meritocratic Caste-Based Bio-Technocracy. Their civilization is divided into Warrior, Mystic, and Scholar castes, which operate entirely independent of their three distinct biological sub-species: the psychic and humanoid Dar, the towering and digitigrade Koda tacticians, and the massive, quadrupedal Graa protectors. Because they grow their technology rather than build it—utilizing synthetic tissues, bio-ceramics, and mimetic polymers—their structural and vehicular assets, such as their massive "Biode" war-frames, function as living extensions of the Aulurans themselves, capable of rapid cellular regeneration and seamless environmental integration.

**Faction Identity:** The Aulurans (The Retaliators / The Great Gardeners)

**Government:** Caste-Based Bio-Technocracy

**Capital:** Unknown (Original Homeworld lost to Impyrium)

**Tech Level:** 4 (Advanced Bio-Technology)

**Meta Level:** 3+

The Aulurans are a species defined by profound loss and radical adaptation. Once a peaceful civilization, their history was shattered when the **Impyrium** invaded and destroyed their homeworld. This event, known as the "Incursion," fundamentally altered their cultural trajectory.

Instead of fighting back with traditional ballistics or steel, the Aulurans fought back with **Life**. They retreated to the fringes and weaponized their mastery of nature. Today, they are known as "The Great Gardeners" or "Retaliators"—terraforming worlds into virulent, hyper-accelerated jungles that serve as both home and fortress.

### 

### **Philosophy: Living Technology**

To an Auluran, a metal ship is a "dead coffin." Their philosophy rejects the static nature of industrial technology in favor of **Biotechnology**. They identified organisms with desirable traits, manipulated their genetics, and cultivated them on a massive scale in bio-farms.

&nbsp;

#### **Grown, Not Built**

Their structures are marvels of engineering grown from the earth, seamlessly blending with nature. They are made of living plants and fungi, creating vibrant facades that change with the seasons. Interiors are filled with light, air, and natural ventilation.

&nbsp;

#### **The Immune System**

They view the galaxy through a biological lens. Aulurans see themselves as the "white blood cells" of the universe. Allies are healthy cells; enemies (like the Impyrium) are pathogens to be purged.

### 

### **The Homeworld Environment**

Their original homeworld was a lush, verdant planet dominated by towering trees reaching heights of over a hundred meters, their gnarled trunks and branches reaching up like ancient guardians. The Aulurans have always been fiercely protective of these ecosystems. In response to the Incursion, they have terraformed several worlds to match this environment, creating wild, overgrown planets that are hostile to outsiders but home to them.

### 

### **General Species Features & Mechanics**

**Stigma:** Xeno

**Biotechnology:** Innate affinity and training with organic tech.

**Low Light Vision:** Adapted for low light environments.

**Prehensile Tail:** Used for balance and communication.

# **SOCIAL STRUCTURE: THE THREE CASTES**

Auluran society is organized into a rigid **Caste System**. Crucially, this system is **meritocratic and vocational**, not biological.

Historically, the sub-species drifted toward specific roles: Dar as advisors, Koda as warriors, and Graa as protectors. However, with the emergence of the **Scholar Caste**, these conventions have changed. A **Warrior Dar**, an **Awakened Koda**, or a **Scholar Graa** are now equally supported by their pursued Caste regardless of their biological form.

### 

## **The Warrior Caste**

**Focus:** Defense, Strategy, Martial Prowess, and Physical Cultivation.

**Role:** The immune response of the faction. They pilot the Bio-Frigates and lead ground assaults.

**Training:** Focuses on Combat Features and physical conditioning.

### 

## **The Mystic Caste**

**Focus:** Spirituality, The Meta (Psionics/Magic), Diplomacy, and Unity.

**Role:** They maintain the spiritual health of the Auluran people and communicate with the semi-sentient technology of their ships and cities.

**Training:** Focuses on Discipline Features and Meta-skills.

### 

## **The Scholar Caste**

**Focus:** Science, Bio-Engineering, History, and Genetics.

**Role:** The "Evolutionary Engine." Scholars are responsible for designing the next generation of Living Tech, preserving history, and ensuring genetic stability.

**Training:** Focuses on Skill Features and Academic knowledge.

# **SPECIES BIOLOGY (THE SUB-SPECIES)**

The Aulurans are a distinct race characterized by feline attributes. Their fur ranges in color from **stark white, cream, fawn, copper, and blue to pitch black**, often with a multitude of symmetrical patterns. They possess swiveling ears for sound detection, large expressive eyes, sharp claws, and long prehensile tails.

The species manifests in three distinct biological variations.

### 

## 

# **AESTHETICS & TECHNOLOGY**

## **Visual Aesthetic: "Biomorphic Solarpunk"**

**Atmosphere:** "Hunter's Moon." Lighting is often cool silvers, blues, and deep ambers.

**Architecture:** Honeycomb structures, vine-cables, bioluminescent lighting strips, and seamless organic forms.

**Colors:** Natural camouflage (Dark Greens, Browns, Ochers) accented with "Warning Colors" (Electric Blue, Toxic Green).

### 

## **The Great Garden (Tech Level 4\)**

**Weaponry:** Natural Weapons (Claws/Fangs) are standard. Tech weaponry includes Needle Rifles (neuro-toxins) and Acid-Spitters.

**Augmentations:** Biotech enhancements grant superhuman strength, enhanced senses, and rapid healing.

**Starships:** Void-Whales and Bio-Frigates. Constructed from genetically modified organisms, possessing regenerative abilities.

## 

## **Summary**

The Aulurans are a "high-concept" faction that subverts the "primitive cat-person" trope. They are highly advanced bio-engineers. Whether playing a psychic Dar Mystic, a towering 7ft Koda Warrior, or a massive Graa Scholar using telekinesis to operate a computer, the Aulurans represent the apex of biological evolution.

# **AULURAN IMAGE SYNTHESIS: THE BIOLOGICAL HUNTERS**

## 

## **I. Visual Philosophy & Ecosystem**

Archetype: The Biological Hunters / Interstellar Apex Predators

Core Design Philosophy: Predatory Biomimicry.

Unlike the Aeld (who sculpt art) or Humans (who build blocks), Aulurans grow and weave their technology. Their designs mimic the efficiency of biology: armor that flexes like skin, ships that swim through the void, and cities that photosynthesize.

### 

### **The Visual Ecosystem**

#### **The "Living" Silhouette**

Auluran tech avoids static shapes. Structures and vehicles are designed with muscular curvature, articulated joints, and textures resembling carapace, scales, or hardened wood.

&nbsp;

#### **Tactile & Olfactory Interfaces**

Screens are secondary. Auluran technology utilizes haptic feedback suits and even scent-synthesizers to convey data, leveraging their heightened senses.

&nbsp;

### **Materiality: Bio-Ceramics and Nano-Lattice Weaves.**&nbsp;

Their metal looks matte and textured, often coated in self-repairing resin. There is no rust, only "wither" or "bruising" on damaged tech.

### 

#### **Sensory Experience (Context Tokens)**

**Visual**

Vibrant alien jungles, glowing eyes in the dark, structures pulsing with life, weapons grown from bone/muscle.

&nbsp;

#### **Sound**

Chittering of living tools, roar of beast-ship engines, rustle of leaves, primal drums mixed with bio-acoustic hums.

&nbsp;

#### **Smell/Taste**

Musk, ozone, crushed herbs, iron (blood), sweet nectar, earthy scent of rapid growth.

## 

## **II. The Biological Chassis (Subspecies)**

*Select ONE chassis to define the physical body.*

### 

### **The Dar (The Spiritual Apex)**

**Structure:** Slender, elegant humanoids with feline features, **5–6 feet tall**. Long ear tufts and prehensile tails used for delicate gesturing.

**Visual Motif:** **"The Floating Eye."** Their tech often hovers (anti-grav), leaving their hands free for ritual somatic components.

**Fashion & Gear:** They wear **Neuro-Conductive Silks**—living robes that shimmer with data streams. Instead of heavy helmets, they wear "Halos" or circlets of floating crystals (**Aetherium**) that amplify their psychic reach.

**Key Tech:** **Thought-Loms.** Devices that obey mental commands, appearing as floating orbs or jewelry until activated.

### 

### **The Koda (The Tactical Hunter)**

**Structure:** Athletic, **digitigrade legs** (always emphasized in boot design), prominent muzzles, standing **7 feet tall**.

**Visual Motif:** **"The Pounce."** Their gear is designed for forward momentum. Jet packs or jump-jets are standard, integrated smoothly into the back armor.

**Fashion & Gear:** **Myomer-Exosuits.** Their armor replicates musculature on the outside, boosting their natural strength. It is segmented (like a crustacean) to allow for their acrobatic combat style. They favor retractable claws (energy or monofilament).

**Key Tech:** **Chameleon-Skin.** Active camouflage is standard issue, allowing them to vanish into urban or jungle environments instantly.

### 

### **The Graa (The Industrial Vanguard)**

**Structure:** Massive tiger-like **quadrupedal predators**, **9 feet long**, weighing 600lbs. Pure muscle.

**Visual Motif:** **"The Tank" / Industrial Symbiosis.** They are the heavy lifters and heavy hitters.

**Fashion & Gear:** They do not wear suits; they wear **Harnesses and Plating**. Heavy weapon or utility mounts are often strapped to their shoulders or backs.

**Key Tech:** Graa often carry several small repair or recon drones in their harness as well as manipulation **waldos / utility arms** to interact with technology despite being armless.

## 

## **III. The Caste Overlay (Role & Gear)**

*Apply ONE caste to define attire, equipment, and atmosphere.*

### 

### **The Warrior (The Protector)**

**Context:** The protectors. Heavy, biological shock troops.

**Visuals:** Heavy segmented plates of **matte bio-ceramic** (beetle shell aesthetic). Weapons that look like grown extensions of the arm (thorn-throwers). Imposing physical bulk, bestial features.

**Atmosphere:** A jungle patrol, a boarding pod (spore pod), a defensive nest. Dappled sunlight filtering through canopy, spores in the air, deep shadows, organic honeycomb structures, vibrant moss.

### 

### **The Mystic (The Enlightened)**

**Context:** The leaders who shape the "Living Tech".

**Visuals:** Robes of **woven living silk**, glowing amber crystal foci embedded in armor, fine fur texture, large iridescent eyes, serene meditation pose.

**Atmosphere:** The heart of a Grown Starship, an amber-lit genetic archive. "Hunter's Moon" lighting (Silver/Blue), translucent living walls, pulsing bioluminescent veins, floating genetic data-streams, humid and silent.

### 

### **The Scholar (The Studious)**

**Context:** The sedentary thinkers and historians.

**Visuals:** Heavy woven cloth and metal robes, complex geometric sigils, contemplative posture, holding illuminated scroll or data-crystals, multi-faceted compound eyes (goggles/tech), sensory deprivation gear.

**Atmosphere:** A vast, silent library, a geometrically complex data-storage vault. Dust motes in shafts of light, hushed and reverent silence, polished obsidian floors, glowing archival spheres, "Mourner's Sun" dimness.
