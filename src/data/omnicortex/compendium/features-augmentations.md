# Cybernetic Augmentations & Conversion Rules

Characters may enhance their biological bodies through mechanical cyberware, biological symbiotes, and full-body conversion.

## Augmentation Tiers & Credit Grants
- **Augmented (Tier 1)**: Base feature granting compatibility with standard cybernetics and granting **6 BP Augmentation Credit** toward cyberware/biotech systems.
- **Heavy Augmentations (Tier 2)**: Prerequisite: *Augmented*, *Stamina 2*. Allows installation of military-grade Heavy class cyberware; grants an additional **+6 BP Augmentation Credit**.
- **Extreme Augmentations (Tier 3)**: Prerequisite: *Heavy Augmentations*, *Stamina 3*. Full Body Conversion (FBC) or extreme genetic chimera grafts; grants an additional **+6 BP Augmentation Credit** (18 BP total credit across 3 tiers).

## Biological vs. Synthetic Physiology
- **Synthetic Limbs**: Gain **+50% Damage Threshold** before disabling or destruction, but cannot make Stamina recovery checks once disabled.
- **Biological Limbs**: Normal damage capacity; allows Stamina checks (DC 10 + Damage) to remain functional when disabled.
