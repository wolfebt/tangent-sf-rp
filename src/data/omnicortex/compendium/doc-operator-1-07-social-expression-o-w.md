### **Oratory**

  - **Ability:** Charisma
  - **Description:** Oratory is the skill of public speaking. It reflects a character's ability to use language and tone of voice to persuade and inspire others. Oratory is used whenever a character needs to convince a group to take a certain course of action, or to deliver a speech or other public address. The art of public speaking. Used to inspire crowds, deliver sermons, debate in court/senate, or give orders on a battlefield.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Inspire:* CR 15 to grant allies a +1 morale bonus to Willpower saves for 1 hour.
  - *Persuade Crowd:* Shift the attitude of a group (opposed by the group's collective Will or a set CR).

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Political Rally Speeches:** Delivering fiery, populist orations that sweep elections and energize masses of voters.
      - **Military Pre-Battle Speeches:** Delivering inspiring addresses that harden resolve and banish fear before bloody assaults.
      - **Religious Sermons & Preaching:** Delivering sermons that move congregations to tears, repentance, and zealotry.
      - **Courtroom Advocacy:** Presenting closing arguments to juries and judges with compelling narrative power.
      - **Eulogies & Funerary Orations:** Honoring the deceased with dignified, deeply moving reflections that comfort mourners.
      - **Crowd Riot Incitement:** Whipping an already angry protest crowd into an uncontrollable, barrier-smashing mob.
      - **Philosophical Lectures:** Explaining abstract ethical concepts and ideological frameworks to university halls.
      - **Diplomatic Keynote Addresses:** Addressing global assemblies with statesmanlike poise, vision, and gravitas.
      - **Acoustic Vocal Projection:** Throwing your voice without microphones to fill cavernous halls and outdoor stadiums.
      - **Debate Cross-Examination:** Dismantling an opponent's speech line-by-line with sharp rhetorical traps and counter-arguments.

  

### **Percussion**

  - **Ability:** Charisma
  - **Description:** Percussion is the skill of playing percussion instruments, like drums or cymbals. It reflects a character's ability to create music using percussion instruments. Percussion is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instruments that are struck, shaken, or scraped. This is often the foundation of musical timing and rhythm.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.
  - *Rhythm:* CR 15 to maintain a beat that helps others coordinate physical tasks (e.g., rowing, marching), granting a +1 bonus to their endurance-based checks.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Acoustic Drum Kits:** Playing intricate rock, jazz, and metal beats using snare, kick, tom-toms, and cymbals.
      - **Military War Drums & Snares:** Keeping the marching cadence, signaling battle lines, and drumming troops into action.
      - **Orchestral Timpani & Gongs:** Striking tuned kettle drums and massive orchestral gongs for dramatic climaxes.
      - **Hand Percussion (Congas/Bongos):** Slapping, popping, and palm-striking skin hand drums with polyrhythmic speed.
      - **Electronic Drum Pads:** Playing velocity-sensitive rubber pads triggering synthesized samples and sub-bass kicks.
      - **Tuned Mallet Percussion:** Playing wooden marimbas, steel xylophones, and vibraphones with precision mallets.
      - **Tribal Ritual Drumming:** Driving deep, hypnotic, relentless drum circles that induce trance states in participants.
      - **Bodhrán & Frame Drums:** Beating shallow Celtic-style frame drums with double-ended tippers while modulating pitch.
      - **Cymbals, Triangles & Chimes:** Accenting delicate sonic passages with crashes, rides, sizzling chimes, and iron bells.
      - **Rhythmic Rowing/Marching Cadence:** Maintaining a continuous, driving beat that coordinates team endurance tasks.

  

### **Singing**

  - **Ability:** Charisma (Performance) or Stamina (Breath Control)
  - **Description:** Singing is the skill of using one's voice to create music. It reflects a character's ability to use pitch, tone, and rhythm to convey emotions and communicate effectively. Singing is used whenever a character needs to entertain others or perform in a musical production. The use of the voice as an instrument. This creates a powerful connection with audiences without the need for equipment.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.
  - *Project Voice:* CR 15 to be heard clearly over loud noise/combat.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Classical Opera:** Projecting high, unamplified vibrato vocals across full orchestras with crystalline resonance.
      - **Rock & Metal Belting:** Singing powerful, gritty, overdriven lead vocals with intentional rasp and chest-drive.
      - **Choral & Sacred Hymns:** Blending harmonious soprano, alto, tenor, or bass lines into cathedral choirs.
      - **Lounge Jazz & Torch Songs:** Singing breathy, smoky, intimate vocal melodies into close microphones in clubs.
      - **Folk Ballads & Sea Shanties:** Singing narrative tales and rhythmic work songs that crowds can easily chant along with.
      - **Overtone & Throat Singing:** Producing two distinct pitch frequencies simultaneously through vocal-tract resonance.
      - **Sub-Harmonic Vocalizations:** Dropping into deep bass registers that rattle ribcages and project primal authority.
      - **Vocal Whistling & Warbling:** Executing intricate melodic trills and whistle registers mimicking birds or wind.
      - **Battle Hymns & Cadences:** Projecting rhythmic vocal cadences over gunfire to keep allied morale unshakeable.
      - **Microtonal Alien Chants:** Navigating vocal melodies that slide between traditional half-step and quarter-step scales.

  

### **String**

  - **Ability:** Charisma
  - **Description:** String is the skill of playing string instruments like guitar or violin. It reflects a character's ability to create music using strings. String is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production.Proficiency with instruments that produce sound via vibrating strings, whether plucked, strummed, or bowed.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Electric Guitar:** Shredding high-gain lead solos, tapping fretboards, and chugging heavy distorted rhythm riffs.
      - **Acoustic Guitar:** Fingerpicking, flatpicking, and strumming steel-string and nylon acoustic guitars.
      - **Violin & Fiddle:** Bowing high-speed country fiddle breakdowns and expressive classical violin concertos.
      - **Cello & Double Bass:** Playing rich, deep, resonant bowed basslines that provide orchestral weight.
      - **Concert Harp:** Plucking cascading arpeggios and glissandos across large, multi-pedal classical harps.
      - **Lute & Mandolin:** Plucking rapid, delicate tremolos and Renaissance melodies on doubled-string instruments.
      - **Electric Bass Guitar:** Locking into the drummer's groove with slap-and-pop basslines and heavy walking rhythms.
      - **Exotic Alien Zithers:** Plucking flat, resonant soundboards equipped with alien sympathetic drone strings.
      - **Banjo Playing:** Executing high-speed three-finger Scruggs-style rolls and clawhammer rhythm patterns.
      - **Bowed Rebec & Archaic Strings:** Playing ancient, gut-stringed instruments with historical bows and rustic tunings.

  

### **Style**

  - **Ability:** Charisma
  - **Description:** Style is the skill of creating a unique and distinctive appearance. It reflects a character's ability to use clothing, accessories, and other techniques to create a unique and memorable look. Style is used whenever a character needs to make a good impression or to stand out from the crowd. The knowledge of fashion, grooming, and personal aesthetics. It is the ability to make an impression before speaking a word. It involves dressing appropriately for high society or looking intimidating in street armor.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *First Impression:* CR 15 to grant a +2 bonus to the first **Diplomacy** or **Intimidate** check made in an encounter.
  - *Blend In:* Dress to match a specific social caste or group (opposed by Culture/Streetwise).

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **High-Society Elegance:** Selecting tailored suits, noble sashes, and jewelry that radiate immense wealth and status.
      - **Streetwear & Cyberpunk Chic:** Styling leather jackets, neon hair, combat boots, and decals for maximum street cred.
      - **Corporate Executive Polish:** Dressing in crisp, minimalist, intimidating boardroom suits that project pure competence.
      - **Military & Uniform Crispness:** Ironing razor-sharp uniform creases, polishing boots to mirrors, and aligning ribbons.
      - **Intimidating Mercenary Look:** Layering battered combat rigs, scars, and holster wear that scream hardened killer.
      - **Subtle Blend-In (Gray Man):** Selecting ultra-nondescript, beige, forgettable clothing that leaves zero mental impression.
      - **Avant-Garde Alien Couture:** Pulling off radical alien silhouettes, kinetic fabrics, and exotic off-world color clashes.
      - **Gala Glamour:** Wearing head-turning, showstopping formal eveningwear that dominates room attention instantly.
      - **Retro & Nostalgic Vintage:** Rocking period-accurate antique threads, vintage flight jackets, and classic hats.
      - **Personal Grooming & Scent:** Mastering hair styling, facial hair trimming, and signature scents that leave lasting impressions.

  

### **Wind**

  - **Ability:** Charisma
  - **Description:** Wind is the skill of playing wind instruments, like a flute or trumpet. It reflects a character's ability to create music using wind instruments. Wind is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instruments that require breath to play, including woodwinds and brass.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Brass Trumpet & Cornet:** Blasting clear, ringing fanfares, jazzy muted solos, and piercing high-register brass lines.
      - **Saxophone (Alto/Tenor/Baritone):** Playing soulful, expressive jazz melodies, blues runs, and smooth vibrato riffs.
      - **Flute & Piccolo:** Flying through rapid, airy classical scales, birdlike trills, and breathy folk melodies.
      - **Clarinet & Reed Instruments:** Navigating woody, warm, wide-ranging melodies across complex key mechanisms.
      - **Bagpipes & War Pipes:** Driving continuous double-drone pipes and chanter melodies that carry for miles across fields.
      - **Harmonica & Blues Harp:** Bending reeds, cross-harping, and chugging train rhythms using cupped hands.
      - **Trombone & Low Brass:** Moving slide mechanics to execute smooth glissandos, punchy bass lines, and orchestral power.
      - **Hunting Horns & Bugles:** Sounding natural, valveless brass calls that carry across forests and battlefields.
      - **Alien Double-Pipes:** Playing exotic instruments requiring circular breathing to produce unceasing melodic loops.
      - **Didgeridoo & Drone Wind:** Producing deep, guttural, cyclical drone vibrations infused with vocalized animal growls.
