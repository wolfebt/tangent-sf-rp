SPACE PIRATE
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Lawless factions characterized by their involvement in piracy, smuggling, and deep-space crime. Operating on the fringes, they prey on trade routes, control interstellar black markets, and live a life of high-risk freedom.
* Key Themes: Void Corsairs, High-Seas in Space, Rebellious Freedom, Black Markets.
* Relationship to Others: Hunted by authorities (Cosmic Justice, Military) but frequently utilized by Corpos for deniable black-ops.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The [Name] Corsairs / The Free Fleet
* Colloquialisms: Pirates / Raiders / Scum
* Archetype: Void Corsairs
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Hidden asteroid bases, lawless drift stations)
* Driving Mandate: Acquire resources, gain power, control black markets, live free.
* Motto: "Take what you can. Give nothing back."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Reckless, adventurous, greedy, ruthless, opportunistic, cunning.
* Social Structure: Ship-based democracies or brutal captain-led dictatorships.
* View on Outsiders: Marks, victims, or the Law.
* Law & Order: The Pirate Code. Mutiny is common if profit dries up.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Elite pilots, fast and advanced modified spacecraft, access to black markets, highly skilled at deception and boarding actions.
* Weaknesses: Hunted by law enforcement, potential for betrayal/infighting, reliance on stolen resources.
* Military Doctrine: Hit-and-run, boarding actions, disabling engines, and zero-G close-quarters combat.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Lawless individuals from all species).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Pilot (Space) (+5)
   * Combat (+5)
   * Intimidate (+4)
   * Streetwise (+3)
   * Mechanics (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Captain: The charismatic and ruthless leader of a pirate vessel.
   * The Swashbuckler: A skilled combatant excelling in zero-G melee boarding actions.
   * The Quartermaster: Distributing the loot and maintaining discipline among the crew.
   * The Navigator: Finding the hidden asteroid bases and slip-streams to evade the law.
   * The Boarding Specialist: Breaching enemy airlocks and securing hostages.
   * The Smuggler: Moving the stolen cargo past military blockades to sell on the black market.
   * The Gunner: Operating the ship's heavy weaponry to disable (not destroy) prey.
   * The Corsair: A privateer secretly paid by a corporation to raid their rivals.
   * The Mutineer: Always plotting to overthrow the captain and take the ship.
   * The Scrapper: Stripping disabled vessels down to the bolts for profit.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Spacer: Unmatched mobility and combat effectiveness in zero-gravity environments.
   * Zero G Training: Ignoring all penalties associated with floating or decompressed combat.
   * Gifted Pilot: Pushing illegal, souped-up interceptors past their redline safely.
   * Sneak Attack: Striking vulnerable spots during the chaos of a boarding action.
   * Intimidating Presence: Terrifying civilian crews into surrendering without a fight.
   * Weapon Focus (Cutlass/Blaster): Mastery of the traditional weapons of the void corsair.
   * Quick Draw: Winning the inevitable cantina standoffs.
   * Evasiveness: Dodging incoming security fire and shrapnel.
   * Danger Sense: Knowing exactly when a target is actually a military bait-ship.
   * Opportunist: Taking immediate advantage of an enemy's tactical mistake.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Rebellious, kinetic, and dangerously customized.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Fast interceptor ships, skull motifs, boarding actions, zero-g combat, stolen cargo.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Void Corsairs, Space Piracy, Rebellious.
   * CONTEXT: Fast interceptor ships, skull motifs, boarding actions, zero-g combat, stolen cargo.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Chaotic, dangerous, thrilling, lawless, kinetic.
