| *Doctor:*  A healer and a caregiver, dedicated to ensuring the health and well-being of your community. You are skilled in medicine, surgery, and pharmacology. Skills:  Alertness, Insight, Knowledge (Nature, Technology), Medicine, Diplomacy, Technology, Vocation (any) Features: Medical Expertise: You gain a \+2 bonus to Medicine checks to diagnose and treat injuries, illnesses, and other medical conditions. Empathetic: You have a natural ability to connect with patients and provide them with comfort and support, gaining a \+2 bonus on Insight and any diagnosis checks. |

## **Doomsayer**

| *Doomsayer* A nihilistic prophet who believes that the end of the universe is inevitable and seeks to hasten its arrival. Skills: Intimidation, Diplomacy, any four Disciplines Features: Harbinger of Doom: Your presence instills fear and despair in others, gaining a \+2 bonus to Intimidation checks and \+2 bonus on any checks related to demoralizing opponents or spreading panic. Embrace the Void: You are immune to Void based psychic damage and gain \+2 bonus on any checks related to resisting mental manipulation or maintaining composure in the face of terrifying phenomena. |

## **Dragoon**

| *Dragoon* Formidable dragon blooded knights, skilled in aerial combat, reconnaissance, and rapid deployment. \[Must be Draconic Species (Dragon Blooded Human)\] Skills: Acrobatics, Athletics, Alertness, and any one Combat skill. Features: Melee Superiority: You gain a \+2 bonus to close-combat attack rolls.  Dragoon Tactics: Gain \+1 bonus on any check using draconic abilities or tactics. |

## **Drop Marine**

| *Drop Marine* A heavily augmented special forces soldier employed by the Syndicate for high-risk missions requiring extreme force and resilience. They are cybernetically enhanced for superior combat capabilities and equipped with cutting-edge weaponry. \[Requires Augmentation and Heavy Augmentation Features\] Skills: Athletics, Intimidation, Technology, and any two Combat skills. Features: Cybernetic Warfare: You gain a \+1 bonus to a Combat skill. Augmented Resilience: Incredible Fortitude Feature |
