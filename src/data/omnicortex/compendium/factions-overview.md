# Factions System Overview & Alignment

Factions represent the major galactic polities, corporate conglomerates, monastic federations, and regional alliances that govern the sectors of the known galaxy.

## Character Affiliation & Benefits
Choosing a Faction affiliation provides:
1. **Faction Skill Package (20 SP)**: 20 Skill Points dedicated to the faction's cultural and operational core skills.
2. **Bonus Feature Discounts**: 1 BP discount on signature faction features (e.g., *Benefit (Status)* for Dynasty, *Benefit (Wealth)* for Syndicate).
3. **Cultural Identity & Aesthetics**: Concrete aesthetic, architectural, and visual semiotics defined by the CRISP framework.
