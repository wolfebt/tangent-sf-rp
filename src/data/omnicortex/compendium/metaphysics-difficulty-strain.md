# Metaphysic Difficulty Checks, Surges & Criticals

- **Critical Success (Nat 20)**: +30 bonus to check, dramatic amplification of effect.
- **Critical Mistake (Nat 1)**: -10 to check and dangerous metaphysical backfire.
- **Energy Surge (Attune ≤ 0)**: Uncontrolled explosion or transposition into unintended targets.
- **Internalized Strain**: Physical backlash inflicting 1 non-lethal damage per 5 points under target DC.
