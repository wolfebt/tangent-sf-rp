# Terminology Distinction: Flaws vs. Disadvantage

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Do not confuse Character Hindrances with the Rolling with Disadvantage mechanic.**

## 1. Hindrances / Disadvantages (Capital D / Character Flaws)
Permanent or semi-permanent character traits chosen during character creation (e.g., *Blindness*, *Addiction*, *Massive Debt*, *Adversary*). They grant bonus BP and provide role-playing hooks.

## 2. Rolling with Disadvantage (lowercase d / Dice Mechanic)
A temporary tactical or situational dice mechanic where a player must roll two d20 dice and take the **lowest result**.

*Note: Certain Character Hindrances (like Impaired Sense or Major Addiction withdrawal) may trigger the situational Rolling with Disadvantage mechanic on specific checks.*
