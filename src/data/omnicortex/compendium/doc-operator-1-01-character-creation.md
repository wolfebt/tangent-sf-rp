# **1.01 CHARACTER CREATION**

Players embark on a thrilling, customizable adventure across the Tangent universe where they hold the power to shape their operator's capabilities, allegiances, and destiny. This customization system grants players the freedom to craft unique and unforgettable characters that resonate with their playstyles and preferences.

A diverse range of species awaits players, each possessing distinct attributes and abilities that influence the course of their journey. From Humans, known for their adaptability and resilience, to mystical Alterian Elves with an innate affinity for the Resonance, the choice of species sets the biological and cultural foundation for a character's strengths and weaknesses.

Furthermore, players delve into a rich array of planetary and habitat origins, uncovering the profound impact of their character's past on their present and guiding their future. Whether raised in a bustling urban megalopolis, an agricultural frontier, a deep-space flotilla, or an enlightened enclave, each origin bestows specialized skills and formative traits that shape a character's worldview.

The game further immerses players in a vibrant galaxy populated by competing factions and diverse occupations. Aligning with a faction grants access to exclusive resources, cultural aesthetics, training packages, and recommended features, allowing players to forge alliances and shape the interstellar political landscape. Choosing an occupation provides professional skills, career traits, and baseline wealth tailored to specific operational roles—from front-line soldiers and covert agents to diplomats, builders, and scholars. Every decision made during character creation has a lasting mechanical and narrative impact.

---

## **1. CONCEPT**

Before allocating Character Points, envision a cohesive concept for your character across three foundational dimensions:

* **Background:** What is the character's upbringing and formative history? This includes their family, social status, education, and life experiences, shaping their personality, skills, and worldview.
* **Personality:** What kind of person is the character? Are they brave, analytical, cunning, compassionate, or pragmatic? Personality traits guide the character's decisions and interpersonal interactions.
* **Role:** What operational specialty will the character fulfill within the squad and the wider galaxy (e.g., diplomat, combat vanguard, technomancer, infiltrator, starship commander)? This influences their goals, motivations, and tactical priorities.

*Example:* *Xy'larra is from a merchant clan of Alterian Elves; she is a seasoned diplomat with a thirst for exploration, driven by a deep curiosity about the vastness of the galaxy and a desire to forge connections with newly contacted cultures.*

## **2. CHARACTER POINTS (150 CP STARTING BUDGET)**

Players begin character creation with **150 Character Points (CP)**—which may be allocated freely via custom point-buy or structured via an **80-CP Playable Archetype Chassis + 70 CP Customization**—in addition to the foundational **60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits** granted at **0 CP** by their Faction, Origin, and Occupation packages:

* **Ability Scores (`5 CP` per `+1` / `-5 CP` per `-1`):** The six primary attributes (*Strength, Agility, Stamina, Intellect, Wisdom, Charisma*). Human average is `+0`. Both inside a **Species Package** (`1.03 SPECIES.md`) and during post-species character customization, raising an attribute costs a straight **5 CP per +1** and penalties refund **-5 CP per -1** (zero package discount; max raw score `+4` before species or augmentation modifiers; racial paragon ceiling is `5 + Species Modifier`).
* **Vitality, Health & Structure (`1 CP` per `+5` points — Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with a flat base of **30 Vitality** and **30 Health** (Synthetics and Constructs combine both into **Base 60 Structure Points [SP]**), increased strictly at a rate of **+5 points per 1 CP spent** (Suggested Starting Maximum: **60 Vitality / 60 Health**, or **120 SP**). **Stamina NEVER increases Vitality or Health pools;** instead, the Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming wound damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Species Package (`Variable CP`):** Covers innate biological traits, attribute adjustments (`5 CP` per `+1` / `-5 CP` per `-1`), and species skill ranks (`5 CP` per `5 Ranks` / `1 CP` per `1 Rank`) (`1.03 SPECIES.md`).
* **Faction, Origin & Occupation (`0 CP`):** Faction (`20 SP`) + Origin (`20 SP`) + Occupation (`20 SP`) = **60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits at 0 CP** to ground the character in their established background. Extra traits beyond the four free selections cost **1 CP each**.
* **Technology Level (`0 CP` at `TL 3`):** No CP cost at the galactic baseline of **Tech Level 3 (Space Age)**; costs **+10 CP for TL 4**, **+20 CP for TL 5**, or grants **+10 CP per level below TL 3** (`-10 CP` at TL 2, `-20 CP` at TL 1, `-30 CP` at TL 0).
* **Skills (`1 CP` per `1 Rank` / `5 CP` per `5 Ranks`):** Represent learned proficiencies and expertise (`1.07 SKILLS.md`). Both inside a Species Package and during post-species customization, skills cost a straight **1 CP per 1 Skill Rank** (`5 CP` per `5 Ranks` — zero package discount; maximum starting rank is **Rank 11 [Expert]**).
* **Features (`3 CP` Standard / `2 CP` Recommended):** Special talents, combat maneuvers, karma abilities, and metaphysical awakenings (`1.08 FEATURES.md`). Features have a base cost of **3 CP**, reduced by **1 CP** (to **2 CP**) if listed as Recommended by the character's Species, Faction, Origin, or Occupation (minimum cost of **1 CP** per feature).
* **Personal Hindrances (`Up to +10 CP Maximum`):** Personal flaws and drawbacks (`1.09 HINDRANCES.md`) grant bonus Character Points up to a hard cap of **+10 CP** during creation. **Species-level disadvantages and Xeno Stigmas are exempt from this 10 CP cap.**
* **Augmentations (`3 CP` Augmented Feature + Node/CP Costs):** Permanent cybernetic, biological, or metaphysical implants (`2.00.Augmentations.md`). Requires the **Augmented** Feature (3 CP, granting a 6 CP augmentation credit per tier) and anatomical **Node** capacity (or 0 CP / 0 Nodes for commonplace civilian implants).
* **Meta Disciplines (`Awakened` Feature + Skill/Invocation CP):** Developing metaphysical capabilities (`4.00 METAPHYSICS.md`). The **Awakened** Feature unlocks **Attune** and the two Focus Skills of a chosen Metaphysic Discipline; **Special Ability** allows standalone invocation effects without full discipline training.
* **Property & Wealth Score (`EUFT`):** Equipment (`2.00 ECONOMATRIX.md`) is acquired via **Wealth Score** (Auto-Buy any personal item where `Crafting CR <= WS`), **Liquid Credits / Wealth Check Financing** (`Liquidity Gap = Value[Item CR] - Value[WS]`), **Crafting** via the Productivity Engine, **Conjuration** (temporary scene creations cost 0 CP; permanent conjured items require CP), or the **Benefit** Feature.

*Example:* *Xy'larra has 150 CP to allocate across her character creation pipeline.*
