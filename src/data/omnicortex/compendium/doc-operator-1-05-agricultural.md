# **AGRICULTURAL**

# **AGRICULTURAL**

Agri-Worlds are essential for mass food production. These worlds typically have low development and population. Most communities range from small houses and other farm-related structures, including barns, silos and the like, to expansive mega-structure Arcologies. They also include most Preservation/Green Worlds, those purposefully left undeveloped but with self-sustaining small communities. Farming and preserved wilds are the focus of these worlds.

## **Traits**

### *Animal Husbandry*

Expertise in raising and caring for livestock. Understands animal behavior, breeding, and nutrition. Can manage a farm with a diverse range of animals.

*\+2 to Animal Handling*&nbsp;

&nbsp;

### *Botanical Knowledge*&nbsp;

Extensive knowledge of plants, crops, and agricultural techniques. Understands the science behind growing and cultivating various types of vegetation.

*\+2 to Botany Checks*

&nbsp;

### *Community Building*

Values community and may have skills in building and maintaining relationships with other farmers, suppliers, and customers. Can negotiate deals, organize cooperative efforts, and foster a sense of camaraderie among fellow agriculturalists.

*Once per Day may reroll a Social Skill Check*

&nbsp;

### *Green Thumb*

Natural talent for nurturing plants and can make them thrive even in challenging environments. Has a knack for identifying the needs of different plants and providing the necessary care.

*Checks involving nurturing and helping plants may be made at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Mechanical Skills*

Mechanical skills related to farming equipment and machinery. Can repair and maintain farming tools, vehicles, pumps, conveyors and other automated systems used in agricultural operations.

*\+2 to Mechanical Skill Checks*

&nbsp;

### *Resourcefulness*

The character is able to make the most of limited resources and find creative solutions to problems, which is especially important in an environment where resources may be scarce.

*Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Survival Skills*

Survival skills honed from living and working in remote or harsh environments. Can navigate and forage for resources, making them self-sufficient in challenging situations.

*\+2 Survival Skill Checks*

&nbsp;

### *Sustainable Practices*

Knowledgeable about sustainable farming practices and strives to minimize environmental impact. May prioritize organic farming, crop rotation, and water conservation techniques.

*\+2 to any Agriculture based checks in a prepared grow area and to prepare and maintain an area.*

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis**

## **01\. AGRICULTURAL**

*Food production, farming, nature tamed, growth.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Endless golden wheat fields stretching to the horizon, interrupted by massive, brutalist white marble granaries that resemble ancient temples. Hover-barges with red banners float silently over the crops.  
* **STYLE:** Feudal Grandeur, Monumental Agrarian, Greco-Roman Sci-Fi.  
* **PALETTE:** Wheat Gold, Marble White, Imperial Red, Deep Blue Sky.  
* **TOKENS:** Aqueducts, stone silos, statues of Emperors, heat haze, "Bread and Circuses."

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Towering, windowless white monoliths in a grid pattern. Inside, sterile purple-lit hydroponic racks are tended by drone swarms. Outside, the grounds are manicured, geometric orchards.  
* **STYLE:** Corporate Utopia, High-Tech Sanctum, Sterile Efficiency.  
* **PALETTE:** Clinical White, Hydroponic Purple, Synthetic Green, Chrome.  
* **TOKENS:** Hexagonal towers, glass walkways, aeroponics, artificial sunlight, silence.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Terraced rice-paddy style fields climbing steep, misty mountains, protected by stone keeps with anti-air turrets. Farmers use bio-engineered beasts of burden.  
* **STYLE:** Draconic Feudalism, Highland Agrarian.  
* **PALETTE:** Emerald Green, Slate Grey (Stone), Mist White, Torch Orange.  
* **TOKENS:** Stone walls, banners, dragons in the sky, smoke from hearths, verticality.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** Scrappy homesteads made of corrugated metal fighting back the dust. Rusty, repurposed rovers pull plows. Moisture vaporators and lonely windmills creak in the wind.  
* **STYLE:** Dust-Bowl Frontier, Space Western.  
* **PALETTE:** Rust Red, Dust Brown, Galvanized Steel, Dry Grass Yellow.  
* **TOKENS:** Windmills, patch-jobs, fences, vast empty horizons, grit.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Industrial sharecropping in a muddy, rainy basin. Heavy, pollution-belching harvesters churn the earth. Pre-fab worker shantytowns are guarded by mercenary watchtowers.  
* **STYLE:** Industrial Noir, Gritty Realism.  
* **PALETTE:** Mud Brown, Diesel Smoke Black, Neon Bar Signs (distant), Grey Rain.  
* **TOKENS:** Mud, rain, heavy machinery, chain-link fences, floodlights.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** A symbiotic food-forest where trees are the habitation. Giant bioluminescent fruits hang from canopy webs. The "machinery" is grown, moving plant matter.  
* **STYLE:** Biomorphic Solarpunk, Organic Symbiosis.  
* **PALETTE:** Deep Forest Green, Bioluminescent Cyan/Amber, Earth Brown.  
* **TOKENS:** Living tree-homes, vine-bridges, giant flowers, dappled sunlight.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Floating crystalline trellises holding void-grapes. Elegant, Art Nouveau glasshouses with solar-sail collectors. Automated golems tend to delicate herbs.  
* **STYLE:** Arcane Vinery, High-Fantasy Agriculture.  
* **PALETTE:** Lavender, Gold, Crystal Blue, Starlight White.  
* **TOKENS:** Floating islands, magic-infused plants, elegant glass curves, sparkling lights.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Sleek, curve-linear communal hubs nestled in rolling green hills. Solar-glass domes, friendly robotic assistants, and clean water features integrated with crops.  
* **STYLE:** Utopian Eco-Community, Solarpunk.  
* **PALETTE:** Bright White, Sky Blue, lush Green, Solar-Panel Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Clean lines, wind turbines, community parks, bright optimism.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** Multi-level terraced gardens built into trade spires. Waterfalls recycle irrigation systems. Market stalls are integrated directly into the growing zones.  
* **STYLE:** Sustainable Trade-Garden, Hanging Gardens of Babylon.  
* **PALETTE:** Teak Wood, Turquoise Water, White Ceramic, Vibrant Flowers.  
* **TOKENS:** Waterfalls, vertical farming, busy markets, colorful awnings.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Infinite hexagonal pools of nutrient paste. No plants, just algae vats and protein synthesizers. Liquid metal harvesters move on magnetic rails.  
* **STYLE:** Fractal Nutrient Grid, Mathematical Farming.  
* **PALETTE:** Algae Green, Neon Blue (Grid), Matte Grey, Steel.  
* **TOKENS:** Geometry, silence, efficiency, protein vats, drones.
