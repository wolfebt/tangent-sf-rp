# 1.04. ARCHETYPAL FACTION: 04. CRIMINAL CARTEL (+2 WS)

## Executive Summary & Nov 2 Canonical Game Data
* **Archetypal Faction Classification:** 04. Criminal Cartel
* **Tech / Meta Level:** TL 3 / ML 1
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Score Modifier:** **+2 WS**
* **Canonical 20-Point Faction Skill Package (0 CP):** Streetwise (+5), Stealth (+5), Bluff (+4), Intimidate (+3), Ballistic (+3)
* **Canonical Recommended Features (-1 CP Discount):** Ambidextrous, Benefit (Identity), Black Market Connections, Blind-Fight, Connected, Contacts, Deceitful, Nimble Fingers, Sneak Attack, Veiled Threat
* **CRISP Visual Semiotics Key:** Neon-lit back-alley dens, concealed subdermal weapons, encrypted cred-sticks.

CRIMINAL
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Syndicates, cartels, and mafias operating in the shadows of galactic civilization. They are characterized by their involvement in illegal activities, accumulating wealth, power, and influence through illicit means while avoiding the gaze of official authorities.
* Key Themes: The Underworld, Black Markets, Betrayal, Smuggling.
* Relationship to Others: Parasitic or symbiotic; they operate within the borders of larger factions, bribing or fighting them as needed.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: [Name] Syndicate / Cartel / Family
* Colloquialisms: The Mob / The Underworld / The Outfit
* Archetype: Underworld Cartels
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Black market hubs, lawless stations)
* Driving Mandate: Accumulate wealth, power, or influence through illicit means.
* Motto: "Everything has a price in the dark."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Ruthless, cunning, opportunistic, secretive.
* Social Structure: Loyalty-based hierarchies, families, or gang structures.
* View on Outsiders: Marks to be exploited or cops to be avoided.
* Law & Order: Street justice, Omertà, and violent reprisal for snitches.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Connections to other criminal organizations, access to illegal resources, skilled at deception, ability to operate outside the law.
* Weaknesses: Vulnerability to law enforcement, internal power struggles, potential for betrayal, difficulty maintaining loyalty.
* Military Doctrine: Ambushes, drive-bys, assassinations, and intimidation.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Highly diverse.
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Streetwise (+5)
   * Intimidate (+4)
   * Stealth (+4)
   * Mechanics (Security) (+4)
   * Combat (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Crime Boss: Organizing rackets and maintaining control over territory.
   * The Con Artist: Swindling marks out of credits using elaborate schemes.
   * The Enforcer: Breaking legs and ensuring debts are paid on time.
   * The Smuggler: Moving contraband past customs blockades in hidden compartments.
   * The Slicer: Hacking security grids to bypass alarms during a heist.
   * The Fence: Appraising and liquidating stolen goods quickly.
   * The Hitman: Executing targeted assassinations for a high price.
   * The Getaway Driver: Evading law enforcement in high-speed chases.
   * The Extortionist: Running protection rackets on local businesses.
   * The Bouncer: Providing muscle and security for illegal underground clubs.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Connected (Underworld): Knowing exactly who to talk to for illegal goods.
   * Sneak Attack: Striking vulnerable spots when enemies are caught off guard.
   * Deceitful: Enhancing innate manipulative and lying abilities.
   * Intimidating Presence: Projecting an aura of violence that terrifies others.
   * Black Market Access: Acquiring restricted weapons and military-grade tech.
   * Nimble Fingers: Picking locks, disarming alarms, and picking pockets.
   * Street-Smart: Never getting lost or ambushed in a slum or back alley.
   * Uncanny Dodge: Reacting instantly to unexpected danger or betrayal.
   * Hidden Pockets: Smuggling small weapons or data drives past pat-downs.
   * Danger Sense: A paranoia-honed instinct warning of immediate peril.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Gritty, shadowed, and neon-lit.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Smoke-filled cantinas, neon-lit alleys, illegal chop-shops, unmarked crates.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Underworld Syndicate, Gritty Cyberpunk, Noir.
   * CONTEXT: Neon-lit alleys, smoke-filled cantinas, illegal chop-shops, shadowed warehouses.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Dangerous, grim, secretive, volatile, desperate.
