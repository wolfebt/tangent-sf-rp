# Hindrances System Overview & Economy

In the Tangent Role-Playing System, **Hindrances** (also called Flaws or Disadvantages) are negative traits, chronic disabilities, social liabilities, or psychological compulsions that a character possesses.

## Build Point (BP) Grant Economy
Unlike standard Features and Skills which consume Build Points, Hindrances **grant additional Build Points (BP)** during character creation.
- **Severity-Based Value**: Each Hindrance grants between **1 BP and 30 BP** based on how severely it impedes the character.
- **Recommended Cap**: The standard recommended limit is **no more than 15 BP** of Hindrances. Exceeding this requires explicit GM approval and signifies a deeply flawed, tragic, or heavily disabled protagonist.
- **GM Discretion & Campaign Context**: A flaw that carries no realistic penalty in a campaign (e.g., *Hydrophobia* in a desert wasteland, or *Technologically Impaired* in a primitive feral society) may be disallowed or have its BP value reduced by the GM.
