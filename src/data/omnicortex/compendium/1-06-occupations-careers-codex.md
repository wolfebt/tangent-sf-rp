# 1.06 Occupations & Careers Codex

An Occupation represents a character's career, formal trade, and role in society. It supplies professional skills, discounted recommended features, and career traits.

---

## Canonical Career Archetypes

### 1. Adept (Metaphysic Specialist)
- **Role:** Reality manipulation, psionic warfare, spiritual counseling.
- **Professional Skills:** Attune, Known Metafocus Disciplines.
- **Recommended Features:** Awakened Discipline, Meta Focus, Energy Channeling.

### 2. Soldier (Tactical Combatant)
- **Role:** Direct fire, heavy weapon deployment, squad coordination.
- **Professional Skills:** Modern Weapons, Archaic Weapons, Tactics, Athletics, Armor.
- **Recommended Features:** Toughness, Point Blank Mastery, Heavy Armor Training.

### 3. Scout (Recon & Exploration)
- **Role:** Pathfinding, infiltration, sniper overwatch, planetary survey.
- **Professional Skills:** Stealth, Alertness, Navigation, Survival, Piloting.
- **Recommended Features:** Lightning Reflexes, Camouflage Mastery, Eagle Eye.

### 4. Scholar / Scientist (Research & Analysis)
- **Role:** Forensics, astrophysics, xenobiology, historical deciphering.
- **Professional Skills:** Academics, Science, Physics, Computers, Medicine.
- **Recommended Features:** Mastermind, Fast Hacking, Analytical Deduction.

### 5. Scoundrel (Subterfuge & Infiltration)
- **Role:** Security bypassing, street economics, black-market procurement.
- **Professional Skills:** Stealth, Deceit, Streetwise, Lockpicking, Sleight of Hand.
- **Recommended Features:** Slippery, Hidden Pocket, Quick Draw.

---

## Occupational Rules

- **Professional Skill Pool:** **20 Skill Points** allocated to occupational skills.
- **Occupational Traits:** Select **Two Traits** from your career archetype.
- **Cross-Training (Background Trait):** Take the *Background* trait to acquire training from a secondary occupation.
