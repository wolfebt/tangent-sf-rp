# **CIVILIZATION ASPECTS & TECHNOLOGICAL DOMAINS**

## **CIVILIZATION ASPECTS & DOMAINS**

Detailed progression tracks for specific aspects of society.

### 

### **AGRICULTURE**

From hunting and gathering to planetary engineering.

* **0: Hunting and Gathering.** Early human societies relied on hunting animals and gathering plants for sustenance.  
* **1: Farming.** Domestication of plants and animals, leading to the development of agriculture.  
* **2: Advanced Farming.** Modern farming techniques, including mechanization, irrigation, and fertilization.  
* **3: Terraforming.** Manipulation of the environment to create more suitable conditions for agriculture.  
* **4: Planetary Engineering.** Large-scale manipulation of entire planets for agricultural purposes.  
* **5: Matter Synthesis.** Direct conversion of energy to food/organic matter (Post-Scarcity/Replicators).

### 

### **ARCHITECTURE**

From simple structures to self-assembling habitats.

* **0: Simple Structures.** Huts, tents, and basic masonry structures.  
* **1: Advanced Masonry.** Early steel structures and basic urban planning.  
* **2: Skyscrapers.** Advanced urban planning and sustainable architecture.  
* **3: Smart Cities.** Advanced building materials and energy-efficient designs.  
* **4: Advanced Space Habitats.** Self-assembling structures and advanced terraforming techniques.  
* **5: Megastructures.** Dyson Spheres, Ring Worlds, and Stellar Engineering.

### 

### **BIOTECHNOLOGY**

From selective breeding to retro-genetic engineering.

* **0: Herbalism.** Use of simple medicine and herbs; natural remedies.  
* **1: Pharmacology.** Improvements in medical knowledge, effective treatments, and the beginnings of pharmacology (eugenics possible).  
* **2: Early Biotech.** 3D printable materials, focused medical sciences, and the beginnings of nanotechnology.  
* **3: Genetic Engineering.** Living machinery, self-healing, and morphic functionality.  
* **4: Gene Editing.** Programmable materials, advanced living machinery, and bio-integration.  
* **5: Advanced Nanobiotechnology.** Advanced morphic equipment, enhanced genetic tailoring, clinical immortality via digital consciousness, and retro-genetic reengineering.

### 

### **COMMERCE**

From barter systems to post-scarcity economics.

* **0: Barter Systems.** Simple trade networks and basic economic practices.  
* **1: Currency Systems.** Early financial institutions and basic market economies.  
* **2: Modern Financial Systems.** Global trade networks and advanced market economies.  
* **3: Digital Currencies.** Advanced financial technologies and global economic integration.  
* **4: Advanced Economies.** Decentralized financial systems and post-scarcity economics.  
* **5: Post-Scarcity.** Substance Interchange renders currency obsolete; economy based on energy allocation and prestige.

### 

### **COMMUNICATION**

From pictograms to quantum signaling.

* **0: Verbal & Pictograms.** Spoken language and simple drawings.  
* **1: Published Text & Signaling.** Written language, printing, and advanced signaling.  
* **2: Digital Networks.** Audio/Video broadcast, radio, television, and internet.  
* **3: Holographic & FTL.** Holographic displays and faster-than-light communication technologies.  
* **4: Mnemonic Transceivers.** Thought-based communication using technological devices.  
* **5: Quantum Signaler.** Real-time communication using quantum entanglement (Zero Latency).

### 

### **DEVICES**

From simple machines to holophotonics.

* **0: Simple Mechanical.** Levers, pulleys, and wheels.  
* **1: Steam & Electric.** Steam-powered machines, early electrical devices, and basic electronics.  
* **2: Integrated Circuits.** Personal computers, smartphones, and modern electronics.  
* **3: Robotics & AI.** Advanced robotics, AI-driven devices, and quantum computing.  
* **4: Brain-Computer Interfaces.** Advanced nanotechnology devices and molecular machines.  
* **5: Holophotonic Projected.** Devices projected from a library within one unit using solid-light technology.

### 

### **ENERGY**

From biomass to zero-point energy.

* **0: Biomass.** Wind, water, and organic materials.  
* **1: Fossil Fuels.** Coal, oil, and natural gas.  
* **2: Nuclear & Renewables.** Fission, solar, and wind turbines.  
* **3: Fusion Power.** Advanced renewable systems and energy storage technologies.  
* **4: Antimatter.** Advanced energy harvesting and antimatter reactors.  
* **5: Zero-Point & Quantum.** Harvesting potential energy of reality itself.

### 

### **MANUFACTURING**

From handmade goods to polymatter.

* **0: Handmade.** Basic goods produced by hand.  
* **1: Crafted & Foundry.** Fire harnessed for metalworking and craftsmanship.  
* **2: Mass Industrialization.** Large-scale production and additive manufacturing.  
* **3: Memory Materials.** Nanotechnology and materials with memory properties.  
* **4: Programmable Material.** Materials programmed to change properties or shape.  
* **5: Polymatter.** Highly adaptable materials able to change to numerous forms and functions as needed.

### 

### **MATERIALS**

From natural resources to self-assembling matter.

* **0: Natural Materials.** Wood, stone, and metals.  
* **1: Synthetic Materials.** Plastics and advanced alloys.  
* **2: Advanced Composites.** Smart materials and nanomaterials.  
* **3: Metamaterials.** Programmable materials and advanced biomaterials.  
* **4: Nanotechnology.** Self-assembling materials with high adaptability.  
* **5: Polymatter.** Advanced adaptable properties for instant reshaping.

### 

### **MEDICINE**

From herbs to retro-genetic engineering.

* **0: Simple Medicine.** Natural remedies and herbs.  
* **1: Basic Medicine.** Pharmacology and basic medical knowledge.  
* **2: Focused Medical Sciences.** Specialized fields and advanced treatments.  
* **3: Augmentations.** Regenerative medicines, longevity treatments, and cloning.  
* **4: Clinical Immortality.** Enhanced genetic tailoring and life extension.  
* **5: Retro-Genetic Engineering.** Reversing genetic changes and engineering new traits instantly.

### 

### **META SCIENCES**

From folklore to integrated science.

* **0: Folklore.** Superstition and simple phenomena recognized.  
* **1: Early Exploration.** Basic practices and parapsychology.  
* **2: Systematic Study.** Scientific parapsychology and advanced practices.  
* **3: Technology.** Practical applications and the beginnings of meta-technology.  
* **4: Advanced Research.** Universal meta abilities and advanced metaphysical understanding.  
* **5: Integrated.** Meta knowledge is fully integrated into society as commonplace technology.

### 

### **SCIENCE**

From observation to transcendent theory.

* **0: Empirical.** Simple observations and early theories.  
* **1: Systematic.** Scientific methods and basic instrumentation.  
* **2: Modern Disciplines.** Interdisciplinary research and advanced instrumentation.  
* **3: Advanced Theories.** Global scientific collaboration and cutting-edge research.  
* **4: Unified Theories.** Breakthrough discoveries unifying forces.  
* **5: Transcendent Theories.** Understanding of dimensional physics and trans-planar knowledge.

### 

### **SOCIETY**

From tribal to post-scarcity.

* **0: Tribal.** Simple social structures and basic cultural practices.  
* **1: Early Civilizations.** Complex social structures and diverse cultures.  
* **2: Modern Societies.** Global cultural exchange and advanced structures.  
* **3: Advanced Societies.** System-wide governance.  
* **4: Highly Interconnected.** Interstellar societies.  
* **5: Post-Scarcity.** Advanced systems with universal values, allowing for promotion of self and society without resource need.

### 

### **SYNTHETIC INTELLIGENCE**

From reactive machines to hyper intellects.

* **2: Reactive Machines.** Basic form. Programmed for reflexive operations. No memory or cognition. (Smart Devices).  
* **2 (Late): Limited Memory.** Can use past experiences to inform decisions. Temporary data storage. (Virtual Assistants, Simple Automata).  
* **3: Self-Aware Algorithm (Theory of Mind).** Possesses consciousness and self-awareness. Capable of understanding emotions and beliefs. (Advanced Automata, AI).  
* **4: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).** Indistinguishable from human intelligence. Capable of solving problems across different domains. (Androids, Sentient Machines, Intellect Constructs).  
* **5: Hyper Intellect (ASI).** Surpasses human intelligence in creativity, wisdom, and problem-solving. (Mekan Prime, Bastion).

### 

### **TRANSPORTATION**

From wagons to spatial transition.

* **0: Wheeled Wagons.** Simple boats and carts.  
* **1: Wind Propelled.** Sailing ships and simple aircraft.  
* **2: Rollers & Rockets.** Self-propelled wheeled vehicles and chemical rockets.  
* **3: GEV/Aerodyne.** Reaction mass drives and FTL capable craft.  
* **4: Force Wave.** Kinetic drives and Tangent Space traversal.  
* **5: Contra-Grav.** Gravitonic drives and instantaneous spatial transition.

### 

### **WEAPONRY**

From melee to disintegration.

* **0: Melee.** Simple bows and slings.  
* **1: Gunpowder.** Cannons and early explosives.  
* **2: Modern Firearms.** Guided missiles and advanced explosives.  
* **3: Directed-Energy.** Autonomous weapon systems and robotics.  
* **4: Nanotechnology.** Force-based weaponry and nano-assemblers.  
* **5: Gravitonic.** Weaponized gravity and disintegration energy pulses.
