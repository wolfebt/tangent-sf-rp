# **99.Tech.CivilizationDomains.md**

## **CIVILIZATION ASPECTS & TECHNOLOGICAL DOMAINS**

Detailed progression tracks across the **16 Civilization Domains** from Tech Level 0 (Stone Age) through Tech Level 5 (Galactic Age):

---

### **1. AGRICULTURE**
*From hunting and gathering to planetary engineering.*
- **TL 0 (Hunting and Gathering):** Early societies rely on hunting animals and foraging wild plants for sustenance.
- **TL 1 (Farming):** Domestication of plants and animals, leading to settled agriculture.
- **TL 2 (Advanced Farming):** Modern farming techniques, including mechanization, irrigation, and synthetic fertilization.
- **TL 3 (Terraforming):** Manipulation of planetary environments and sealed hydroponic domes to create suitable agricultural conditions.
- **TL 4 (Planetary Engineering):** Large-scale manipulation of entire planetary biospheres and atmospheric cycles for agricultural output.
- **TL 5 (Matter Synthesis):** Direct conversion of energy to food and organic matter (Post-Scarcity / Replicators).

### **2. ARCHITECTURE**
*From simple structures to self-assembling megastructures.*
- **TL 0 (Simple Structures):** Huts, tents, and basic masonry structures.
- **TL 1 (Advanced Masonry):** Early iron/steel framing and basic urban planning.
- **TL 2 (Skyscrapers):** Reinforced concrete/steel high-rises (up to 40 stories) and modern urban grids.
- **TL 3 (Smart Cities & Arcologies):** Plasteel domes, orbital habitats, and modular energy-efficient designs.
- **TL 4 (Advanced Space Habitats):** Nanocarbon 500-story Mega-Cities, planetoid super-stations, and self-repairing structures.
- **TL 5 (Megastructures):** Dyson Spheres, Ring Worlds, and Stellar Engineering.

### **3. BIOTECHNOLOGY**
*From selective breeding to retro-genetic reengineering.*
- **TL 0 (Herbalism):** Use of simple medicine, poultices, and natural remedies.
- **TL 1 (Pharmacology):** Improvements in anatomical knowledge, vaccines, and early pharmacology.
- **TL 2 (Early Biotech):** 3D-printed biological scaffolds, genetic sequencing, and focused medical sciences.
- **TL 3 (Genetic Engineering):** Cloned organs, longevity treatments, and basic symbiotic/adaptive bio-machinery.
- **TL 4 (Gene Editing & Living Machinery):** Programmable bioware, vat-grown vehicles/structures, and clinical immortality.
- **TL 5 (Advanced Nanobiotechnology):** Thought-responsive morphic bio-equipment, digital consciousness transfer, and instantaneous retro-genetic reengineering.

### **4. COMMERCE**
*From barter systems to post-scarcity economics.*
- **TL 0 (Barter Systems):** Simple trade networks and direct commodity exchange.
- **TL 1 (Currency Systems):** Precious-metal coinage, early banking houses, and maritime trade routes.
- **TL 2 (Modern Financial Systems):** Fiat/digital credit networks and global commodity exchanges.
- **TL 3 (Interstellar Digital Currencies):** System-wide and gate-relayed credit clearinghouses (Galactic Credits).
- **TL 4 (Advanced Mesh Economies):** Real-time subscription-reality ledgers and decentralized interstellar finance.
- **TL 5 (Post-Scarcity):** Base Substance Interchange renders scarcity obsolete; economy is based on energy allocation, complexity, and prestige.

### **5. COMMUNICATION**
*From pictograms to real-time quantum signaling.*
- **TL 0 (Verbal & Pictograms):** Spoken language, smoke/drum signals, and simple drawings.
- **TL 1 (Published Text & Signaling):** Printing press, optical semaphores, and early telegraphy/telephone.
- **TL 2 (Digital Networks):** Radio/flatvid broadcast, global internet, and orbital satellite relays.
- **TL 3 (Holographic & FTL Courier/Relay):** 3D holographic transceivers and Jump-Gate FTL data relays.
- **TL 4 (Mnemonic & Hyper-FTL Transceivers):** Thought-based neural communication and tachyon/wormhole FTL arrays.
- **TL 5 (Quantum Signaler):** Instantaneous real-time communication across any distance via quantum entanglement (Zero Latency).

### **6. DEVICES**
*From simple machines to holophotonic projections.*
- **TL 0 (Simple Mechanical):** Levers, pulleys, wedges, and wheels.
- **TL 1 (Steam & Electromechanical):** Clockwork, steam-powered machinery, and early CR/AC electrical devices.
- **TL 2 (Integrated Circuits):** Personal computers, smartphones, and silicon microelectronics.
- **TL 3 (Robotics & Quantum Computing):** Modular personal gear, autonomous drones, and quantum processors.
- **TL 4 (Brain-Computer Interfaces & Picotech):** Neural-linked devices, programmable matter canisters, and molecular machines.
- **TL 5 (Holophotonic Projected):** Weightless Fine emitters projecting solid-light tools, weapons, and enclosures from an internal design library.

### **7. ENERGY**
*From biomass to zero-point energy.*
- **TL 0 (Biomass & Elements):** Muscle, wood fire, wind sails, and waterwheels.
- **TL 1 (Fossil Fuels):** Coal, steam boilers, oil, and early internal combustion.
- **TL 2 (Nuclear & Renewables):** Fission reactors, hydrocarbons, and solar/wind grids.
- **TL 3 (Fusion Power):** Thorium/Helium-3 micro-fusion cores and high-density hydrogen fuel cells.
- **TL 4 (Antimatter & Aetherite):** Antimatter containment cores, kinetic force reactors, and refined Aetherite cells.
- **TL 5 (Zero-Point & Quantum):** Harvesting the vacuum potential energy of spacetime and dimensional siphons.

### **8. MANUFACTURING**
*From handmade goods to polymatter looms.*
- **TL 0 (Handmade — Tier 0 Tools `x1`):** Artisanal goods crafted by hand.
- **TL 1 (Crafted & Foundry — Tier 1 Tools `x10`):** Blast furnaces, machine lathes, and early assembly lines.
- **TL 2 (Mass Industrialization — Tier 2 Tools `x50`):** Robotic assembly lines and additive 3D printing.
- **TL 3 (Automated Factories — Tier 3 Tools `x200`):** Memory materials, constructor drones, and miniaturized fabricators.
- **TL 4 (Nanoforge & Bio-Cultivation — Tier 4 Tools `x1,000`):** Molecular assembly swarms and hyper-growth amniotic vats.
- **TL 5 (Genesis Loom — Tier 5 Tools `x5,000`):** Thought-responsive Polymatter looms and matter-energy replicators.

### **9. MATERIALS**
*From natural resources to self-assembling matter.*
- **TL 0 (Natural Materials):** Wood, bone, hide, stone, and worked bronze/iron (*Degrading* trait).
- **TL 1 (Synthetic Alloys):** Forged steel, brass, and cast iron (*Heavy* trait).
- **TL 2 (Advanced Composites):** Kevlar, titanium alloys, polymers, and ceramic plating (*Ballistic Weave* trait).
- **TL 3 (Metamaterials):** Plasteel, duranium, and shape-memory alloys (*Modular* trait).
- **TL 4 (Nanotechnology & Picotech):** Nanocarbon, psycholith, grown chitin, and superatomic crystals (*Self-Repairing* trait).
- **TL 5 (Polymatter & Hard-Light):** Quantum Wellstone liquid metal and holophotonic lattices (*Morphic / Weightless* trait).

### **10. MEDICINE**
*From herbs to retro-genetic reengineering.*
- **TL 0 (Simple Medicine):** Natural remedies, bone-setting, and herbalism.
- **TL 1 (Basic Medicine):** Germ theory, antiseptics, vaccines, and early pharmacology.
- **TL 2 (Focused Medical Sciences):** Antibiotics, MRI diagnostics, organ transplants, and microsurgery.
- **TL 3 (Augmentations & Cloning):** Vat-cloned replacement organs, cybernetic prosthetics, and regenerative stims.
- **TL 4 (Clinical Immortality):** PicoSurgeons, full-body resleeving/transplants, and genetic tailoring.
- **TL 5 (Retro-Genetic & Matter Reconstruction):** Resurrecting a body from a single cell or backed-up digital engram.

### **11. META SCIENCES**
*From folklore to integrated reality engineering.*
- **TL 0 (Folklore):** Superstition, shamanic rites, and uncatalogued wild talents.
- **TL 1 (Early Exploration):** Occult lodges and early parapsychological inquiry.
- **TL 2 (Systematic Study):** Controlled laboratory parapsychology and resonance measurement.
- **TL 3 (Meta-Technology Foundations):** Aetherium containment and early Magi-Tech/Psi-Tech prototypes.
- **TL 4 (Advanced Meta-Engineering):** Mass-produced Psi-Tech, Psycholith architecture, and chassis-scaled Amplifiers.
- **TL 5 (Integrated Reality Science):** Seamless unification of quantum physics and the Resonance Field.

### **12. SCIENCE**
*From empirical observation to transcendent theory.*
- **TL 0 (Empirical):** Astronomical calendars, geometry, and natural observation.
- **TL 1 (Systematic):** Classical mechanics, thermodynamics, and periodic chemistry.
- **TL 2 (Modern Disciplines):** Relativity, quantum mechanics, and molecular biology.
- **TL 3 (Advanced Interstellar Theories):** Tangent-space metric physics and xenobiology.
- **TL 4 (Unified Field Theories):** Gravitonics, picotechnology, and Unified Resonance Dynamics.
- **TL 5 (Transcendent Theories):** Non-Euclidean dimensional engineering and causal mechanics.

### **13. SOCIETY**
*From tribal bands to post-scarcity collectives.*
- **TL 0 (Tribal):** Kinship clans, nomadic bands, and oral custom.
- **TL 1 (Early Civilizations & Nation-States):** Feudal kingdoms, industrial empires, and written law.
- **TL 2 (Modern Global Societies):** Planetary nation-states, corporations, and digital mass culture.
- **TL 3 (System-Wide Governance):** Interplanetary federations, feudal dynasties, and colonial charters.
- **TL 4 (Highly Interconnected Sector Polities):** Mesh-integrated megacorporations, psionic consensus states, and multi-sector empires.
- **TL 5 (Post-Scarcity Collectives):** Dyson-scale collectives and transcendent civilizations freed from material scarcity.

### **14. SYNTHETIC INTELLIGENCE**
*From reactive machines to hyper-intellects.*
- **TL 2 (Early — Reactive Machines, PR 0):** Programmed for reflexive operations; no memory or independent cognition (Smart Devices).
- **TL 2 (Late — Limited Memory, PR 0):** Uses past data to inform decisions; temporary storage (Virtual Assistants, Simple Automata).
- **TL 3 (Self-Aware Algorithm / Theory of Mind, PR 1):** Algorithmic AI capable of tactical adaptation and basic autonomy (Standard Automata).
- **TL 4 (Artificial General Intellect [AGI], PR 2):** Heuristic AI indistinguishable from sapient cognition; Androids and Sentient Machines.
- **TL 5 (Hyper-Intellect [ASI], PR 4+):** Surpasses organic intelligence across all domains (*Mekan Prime, BASTION*).

### **15. TRANSPORTATION**
*From wheeled wagons to instantaneous spatial transition.*
- **TL 0 (Wheeled Wagons & Rafts):** Animal traction, oars, and simple sails.
- **TL 1 (Wind & Steam):** Clipper ships, steam locomotives, and early dirigibles/biplanes.
- **TL 2 (Rollers, Jets & Rockets):** Internal combustion/EV groundcars, supersonic jets, and chemical orbital rockets.
- **TL 3 (GEV, Aerodynes & Translux Gates):** Hovercraft, VTOL aerodynes, fusion torchships, and Jump-Gate FTL.
- **TL 4 (Force Wave, Repulsorlift & Tangent Drives):** Anti-grav skimmers, artificial gravity, and independent FTL warp drives.
- **TL 5 (Contra-Grav & Spatial Displacement):** Gravitonic flight, phase-shift drives, and personal matter transmitters.

### **16. WEAPONRY**
*From stone blades to gravitonic and disintegration emitters.*
- **TL 0 (Archaic Melee & Bows):** Clubs, spears, bows, and forged blades.
- **TL 1 (Blackpowder):** Muskets, flintlocks, cannons, and early explosives.
- **TL 2 (Modern Cased Firearms):** Automatic rifles, sniper rifles, and guided missiles.
- **TL 3 (Mag-Rail & Directed Energy):** Ferrous railguns, lasers, plasma projectors, and vibro-blades.
- **TL 4 (Particle, Disruptor & Mono-Edge):** Heavy particle beams, force-field disruptors, and monofilament weapons.
- **TL 5 (Gravitonic, Disintegrator & Holophotonic):** Gravity-pulse emitters, molecular disintegrators, and hard-light weaponry.
