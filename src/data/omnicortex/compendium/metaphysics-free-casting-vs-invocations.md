# Free-Casting vs. Codified Invocations

## 1. Codified Invocations (Rote Muscle Memory)
- Safe, stable execution allowing the caster to **"Take 10"** on Discipline checks.
- Add Invocation Level to the underlying Discipline skill.
- Costs 0 Essence in quiet sanctums.

## 2. Free-Casting (Spontaneous Reality Alteration)
- High flexibility allowing casters to narrate custom effects within their discipline rank.
- Requires rolling both Attune and Discipline checks.
- Volatile: Failing inflicts **Internalized Strain** (1 non-lethal damage per 5 points of failure).
