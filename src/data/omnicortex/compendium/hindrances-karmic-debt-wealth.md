# Karmic Debt, Negative Wealth & Severe Breakdowns

Several high-tier Hindrances introduce specialized persistent mechanical debuffs:

## 1. Karmic Debt (Unlucky Hindrance)
- When a character takes the **Unlucky (Major: 9 BP)** hindrance, their starting Karma Pool maximum is reduced by **-6 KP**.
- This can drop their starting Karma Pool into negative numbers (**Karmic Debt**).
- At the start of each session, a character in Karmic Debt owes the GM bad-luck triggers (free GM complications or involuntary fumbles) before they can begin accumulating positive Karma.

## 2. Negative Wealth & Garnishments (Poor Hindrance)
- **Poor (Major: 9 BP)** applies a **-12 penalty** to the character's Wealth Score.
- A negative Wealth score denotes active interstellar debt to syndicates, loan sharks, or predatory cartels.
- Any legitimate earnings or mission rewards are subject to immediate wage garnishment and bounty enforcement.

## 3. Severe Psychological & Emotional Breakdowns
- **Severe Emotional Issues (12 BP)**: Requires a **Will Save (DC 18)** at the start of any stressful turn. Failing inflicts random *Panicked*, *Paralyzed*, or *Staggered* states.
- **Severe Social Issues (12 BP)**: Complete social ostracization; all social rolls at Disadvantage plus a flat **-2 penalty** on all Charisma-based skills.
