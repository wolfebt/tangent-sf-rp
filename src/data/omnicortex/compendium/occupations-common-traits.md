# Common Occupational Traits Available to All

Regardless of chosen Occupation, all characters have access to 4 universal baseline traits:

## 1. Background
- **Effect**: Grants access to training from another secondary Occupation fitting the character's backstory.
- **Mechanics**: Grants **ONE (1) Trait** from a chosen secondary Occupation. Does not grant additional SP or Recommended Feature discounts. May be taken multiple times for different secondary traits.

## 2. Trade Tools
- **Effect**: Covers standard occupational gear, specialized toolkits, and field equipment.
- **Mechanics**: Grants a **+2 Equipment Allowance Bonus** during character creation.

## 3. High Pay
- **Effect**: Higher historical compensation, retainer fees, or executive salary for performing one's trade at an elite level.
- **Mechanics**: Grants **+2 Wealth Score**.

## 4. Professionalism
- **Effect**: High professional standing and widespread recognition for craftsmanship and ethical reliability.
- **Mechanics**: Grants **+2 Reputation Score** with industry peers, guilds, and clients.
