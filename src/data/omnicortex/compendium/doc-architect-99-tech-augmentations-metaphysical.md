# **Metaphysical**

# **01.09.02 Metaphysical Augmentations**

# **Enchantments, Psi-Crystals, Imbued Tattoos, and Magi-Tech Implants**

## **1\. OVERVIEW AND PHILOSOPHY**

In the Tangent universe, the division between technology and metaphysics is merely a matter of methodology. If the physical universe is an operating system running on the "Conscious Light" of the universal Resonance, then the body is a hardware chassis capable of processing metaphysical code.

Metaphysical Augmentations (Meta-Augs) represent the pinnacle of this integration. By surgically embedding conductive metamaterials (such as Aetherium or Resonance Crystals) into the nervous system or etching them into the epidermis, individuals can hardwire magical Invocations and Psionic phenomena directly into their biological matrix.

## **2\. INSTRUCTIONAL PRIMER: THE INTEGRATION OF META-AUGS**

To understand the application and deployment of Metaphysical Augmentations within the Tangent setting, the integration process is defined by the following foundational parameters:

* **Who uses them?** Factions operating at high Meta Levels (ML). The Ascendancy utilizes them extensively as "Psi-Tech" to amplify their innate telepathic and telekinetic abilities. The Alterian Enclave uses "Magi-Tech" crystal laminates. Minor factions like the Vajar or Kovian Tribunal utilize primal runic scarification or shadow-glass implants.  
* **What are they?** Physical modifications that contain or channel metaphysical patterns. They range from solid Resonance Crystals surgically fused to the pineal gland, to intricate geometric tattoos inked with Aetherium-laced pigments, to cybernetic neural sockets designed to run "spell subroutines."  
* **Where are they installed?** Implantation requires highly specialized facilities. Alterian "Crystalline Matrices," Ascendancy "Meditation Forges," or the sterile, high-end clinics of the Syndicate (who reverse-engineer ancient Impyrium relics). The process blends medical surgery with metaphysical Attunement.  
* **When are they acquired?** Players may acquire them during Character Creation by spending Character Points (CP) or during standard gameplay by expending Credits (Cr) and raw materials (Aetherium/Resonance Crystals), followed by a period of physical and spiritual recovery.  
* **Why choose Meta-Augs over standard Cybernetics?** While cybernetics provide physical output (strength, armor), Meta-Augs alter reality. They allow non-Awakened individuals to manifest specific supernatural effects, or they grant Awakened individuals massive amplifiers for their Essence and Discipline skills.  
* **How do they work?** They utilize the Universal Displacement Unit (UDU) hierarchy. **1 Socket of Meta-Tech requires 10 Nodes of biological capacity.** Once implanted, Passive enhancements are permanent, while Active Imbuements are triggered as a Free Action (Mental Trigger) and fueled by the host's Essence pool (1 Essence \= 1 Charge).

## **3\. MECHANICAL FRAMEWORK: THE FLESH AS A CIRCUIT**

Embedding magic or psionics into a living chassis requires navigating the intersection of Biology, Technology (TL), and Metaphysics (ML).

**Node to Socket Conversion:**

Standard Meta-Tech is scaled by Sockets. When implanting Meta-Tech into a biological body part, the augmentation consumes Node Capacity at a ratio of **1 Socket \= 10 Nodes**.

* *Example:* Imbuing a character's arm with a "Rank 10 Energy (Fire) Invocation" requires 1 Socket of capacity, which consumes 10 Nodes of space within that arm. Exceeding Node capacity causes dangerous physical and spiritual Rejection.

**Fabrication & Economics:**

Meta-Augs cannot be built from standard materials. They adhere to the Economic Unified Field Theory:

* **Passive Enhancements** (e.g., Tattoos of Protection) require *Resonance Crystals*. CR \= Base Item CR \+ (Sockets Used x 5).  
* **Active Imbuements** (e.g., Psi-Crystal Blasters) require *Aetherium*. Raw materials always cost 50% of the final market Value.

**Tech Level (TL) and Meta Level (ML) Synergy:**

* **TL 1-3 / ML 3-4 (Primal/Arcane):** Tattoos, scarification, and raw crystal embedding. Often painful, visually obvious, and spiritually taxing.  
* **TL 4-5 / ML 4-5 (Advanced Psi-Tech / Magi-Tech):** Seamless neural integration, microscopic Aether-circuits, and digitized consciousness.

## **4\. CATEGORIES OF METAPHYSICAL AUGMENTATION**

### **4.1 Imbued Tattoos & Runification (Metaphysical Scarring)**

The practice of injecting Aetherium-dust or Resonance-laced ink directly into the dermis. The body itself becomes the spell scroll.

* **Mechanic:** Primarily used for Passive Enhancements (Stat boosts, Damage Resistance, Unarmed attack upgrades).  
* **Limitation:** Tattoos consume Nodes on the specific body part they are inked upon. If the limb is destroyed or replaced with a mechanical prosthetic, the magic is lost entirely.

### **4.2 Psi-Crystals and Focal Implants**

Surgically embedding psycho-reactive crystal matrices into the skeletal structure, nervous system, or directly into the brain (pineal gland).

* **Mechanic:** Primarily used for Active Imbuements or Symbiotic Interfaces. The crystal acts as a hard drive for a specific Invocation or as an amplifier (Amp) for the user's innate Metafocus disciplines.

### **4.3 Cyber-Occult Neural Sockets**

The Syndicate and Impyrium method of mass-producing magic. Installing physical, TL4/TL5 metallic computer sockets that interface with the brain, allowing the user to "slot" metaphysical software.

* **Mechanic:** Allows for modular, hot-swappable magical abilities, treating supernatural Talents and Features as downloadable code.

## **5\. CATALOG OF METAPHYSICAL MATRIX**

Below is a standardized catalog of available Meta-Augs, detailing their Node requirements, difficulty to craft/install, and economic value.

### **Neural & Cognitive Implants**

**Psi-Implant (Third-Eye Node)**

* **Location:** Head (Cerebral Cortex) | **Nodes:** 10 (1 Socket) | **TL/ML:** 4/4 | **CR:** 20 | **Value:** 2,560 Cr  
* **Description:** A surgically grown and implanted psycho-reactive lobe that forcibly stimulates the pineal gland. Often visibly glowing beneath the skin of the forehead.  
* **Mechanic:** Grants the *Awakened (Mental)* feature and access to psionic abilities for a character who does not naturally possess them. If the character is already Awakened, it provides \+10 bonus Essence points specifically reserved for Mental Invocations.

**Aether-Node Implant**

* **Location:** Spine / Brain Stem | **Nodes:** 10 (1 Socket) | **TL/ML:** 5/4 | **CR:** 25 | **Value:** 10,240 Cr  
* **Description:** A pinnacle-tier synthetic connection directly linking the host's nervous system to the Universal Resonance (The Aether).  
* **Mechanic:** Grants access to one specific Metafocus Discipline (e.g., Energy, Matter, Dimension) without requiring the biological Awakened feature. The user utilizes their Intellect attribute for all power checks and scaling.

**Feat Plexus (Metaphysical Neural Socket)**

* **Location:** Head / Upper Spine | **Nodes:** 10 (1 Socket) | **TL/ML:** 5/5 | **CR:** 30 | **Value:** 40,960 Cr  
* **Description:** A supreme piece of Cyber-Occult engineering. A physical metallic port grafted to the spine that accepts encoded Aether-chips.  
* **Mechanic:** Allows the user to run up to 4 simultaneous "Feature Circuits." The user can swap out Aether-chips (requiring 1 minute) to temporarily gain access to high-tier metaphysical Talents, Skill Features, or Invocations that they do not permanently own.

**Temporal Stutter Drive**

* **Location:** Head (Cerebral Cortex) | **Nodes:** 15 (1.5 Sockets) | **TL/ML:** 5/5 | **CR:** 35 | **Value:** 163,840 Cr  
* **Description:** A localized reality disruption module. It uses immense Aetherium reserves to fracture the time stream directly around the user's localized consciousness.  
* **Mechanic:** Once per day, as a Swift Action, the user triggers a *Time Stop*. The user takes one complete extra turn while the rest of the universe is frozen. The user can move, use items, or cast buff/utility invocations, but cannot deal direct damage or move other creatures during this frozen turn.

* **Mechanic:** Grants *Uncanny Sense (Hearing)*. Additionally, the user can spend 1 Essence to actively "listen" to the psychic residue of a room, gaining impressions of strong emotions or telepathic conversations that occurred within the last 24 hours.

However, due to the nullifying nature of the ink, the user cannot be targeted by beneficial Invocations (Healing, Buffs) unless they take a Standard Action to mentally suppress the tattoos' effect for one round.

* **Location:** Chest or Back | **Nodes:** 10 (1 Socket) | **TL/ML:** 2/3 | **CR:** 15 | **Value:** 640 Cr
