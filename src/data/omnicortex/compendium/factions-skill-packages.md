# Faction Skill Packages & Feature Synergy

Each faction's 20 SP skill package reflects the civilizational training and priorities of its citizens:
- **Impyrium**: Heavy command, law, history, and military discipline.
- **Syndicate**: Computer systems, corporate trade, social influence, and high-tech vocations.
- **Dynasty**: Feudal knightly combat, chivalric honor, tactical warfare, and beast/mecha piloting.
- **Coalition**: Streetwise survival, repair, jury-rigging, and mercenary combat.
- **Alterian Enclave**: High metaphysics, arcane knowledge, linguistics, and art.
- **Ascendancy**: Mental discipline, diplomacy, zen philosophy, and scientific innovation.
