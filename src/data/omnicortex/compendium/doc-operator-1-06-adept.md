# **ADEPT**

# **ADEPT**

An adept is a person who has developed their skills and abilities to a high level in a particular area. This can be achieved through a combination of innate talent, training, and experience. Adepts may possess unique powers or abilities that set them apart from others in their field, making them valuable assets in various situations. These abilities could include mental abilities, arcane study, or some other source. Adepts are often sought after for their expertise and skills, and they can play a vital role in many different areas. For example, they may be used as consultants, trainers, or even soldiers. Adepts can be found in all walks of life, and they can use their skills for good or evil. Some adepts choose to use their powers to help others, while others use them for personal gain or to harm others. Adepts are often seen as mysterious and powerful figures, and they can be both feared and respected. Though many may have Awakened various Metafocus Skills and Abilities \- an Adept has a true understanding of the arts.

## **Traits**

### *Enhanced Abilities*

Adepts may be able to temporarily enhance their physical or mental abilities to go beyond what is considered normal for their species or race.

*Able to spend a Karma Point to increase any one Ability Score by 1 point for 1 Minute, multiple points spent will increase a different Ability or the same.*

&nbsp;

### *Heightened Senses*

Adepts may have enhanced senses, allowing them to perceive the world in ways that others cannot. This could include heightened vision, hearing, smell, or even the ability to sense energy or auras.

*Gains access to purchase Sensory Features \- Night Vision Line, Scent, Acute Senses and Sense Features (still must be purchased but Considered Recommended)*

&nbsp;

### *Martial Arts Mastery*

Many adepts are highly skilled in martial arts, using their physical prowess and combat techniques to overcome opponents. They may have trained in various styles and have a deep understanding of the body's mechanics.

*\+2 Unarmed Combat*

&nbsp;

### *Mastery of a Discipline*

Adepts may specialize in a specific discipline or school of thought. They have honed their abilities within this discipline and can utilize them more effectively.

*\+2 to a Metafocus Discipline*

&nbsp;

### *Mystical or Spiritual Connection*

Adepts may have a deep connection to mystical or spiritual forces. They may draw power from these sources having a heightened awareness of the unseen world.

*\+1 Attune and \+1 Insight*

&nbsp;

### *Resourcefulness*

Adepts are often resourceful characters who can think on their feet and find creative solutions to problems. They may have a knack for improvisation and using their environment to their advantage.

*Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Special Equipment*

Adepts may have access to specialized equipment or tools that enhance their abilities. This could include advanced weapons, cybernetic enhancements, or artifacts with unique properties.

*\+2 Equipment*

&nbsp;

### *Special Ability*

Adepts may have access to unique powers or techniques that set them apart from other characters. These could include energy manipulation, telekinesis, elemental control, or advanced hacking abilities.

May be taken multiple times for a different Special Ability.

*Special Ability at level 1 (which are afterwards improved as a skill)*

&nbsp;

### *Specialized Skills*

Adepts are often highly skilled in specific areas, such as combat, technology, or mystical arts. They may have extensive training and knowledge in their chosen field, allowing them to excel in their profession.

*\+2 to a Skill of Choice*

&nbsp;

### *Versatility*

Adepts are often versatile characters who can adapt to different situations. They may have a wide range of skills and abilities that allow them to handle various challenges and tasks.

*\+1 to Karma Point*

## **01\. ADEPT**

*Psionic, Mage, Mystic, Shaman, Monk. Focus: Power, Ritual, Inner Strength.*

* **IMPYRIUM:**  
  * **Context:** A **Psionic Centurion** floating slightly off the ground. Wearing pristine white robes over gilded ceramic armor. No blindfolds; they control their power through sheer discipline and a golden "Halo-Interface" hovering behind their head.  
  * **Style:** Enlightened Imperialism, Solar Mystic.  
  * **Palette:** White Silk, Burnished Gold, Psionic Cyan/White (not dark purple).  
  * **Tokens:** Halo, floating, serene face, perfect grooming, disciplined power.  
* **SYNDICATE:**  
  * **Context:** A **Psi-Ops Consultant** in a bespoke white suit. No mystical trappings, just sleek neural-amp headsets (like high-end headphones) and glowing blue eyes.  
  * **Style:** Corporate Transhumanism, Clean Psionics.  
  * **Palette:** Stark White, Hologram Blue, Silver, Slate Grey.  
  * **Tokens:** Data-gloves, smart-glass, floating charts, cold efficiency.  
* **DYNASTY:**  
  * **Context:** A **Dragon Shaman** wearing scale-mail and furs, channeling elemental fire or lightning through a totemic focus (bone/crystal).  
  * **Style:** Elemental Feudalism, Primal Magic.  
  * **Palette:** Charred Black, Magma Red, Ash Grey, Bronze.  
  * **Tokens:** Smoke, embers, dragon scales, heavy staff, raw energy.  
* **OUTWORLDS:**  
  * **Context:** A **Wasteland Mystic** in tattered ponchos and scavenged gear. Their "staff" is a piece of re-purposed alien tech duct-taped to a rod.  
  * **Style:** Space Western Shamanism, Scrap-Wizard.  
  * **Palette:** Dust Brown, Rust, Faded Turquoise, Unstable Energy (Yellow/Green).

  * **Tokens:** Living wood staff, flowers, symbiosis, gentle glow.  
* **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE:**

  * **Palette:** Royal Blue, Pearl White, Gold Filigree, Arcane Violet.  
  * **Tokens:** Floating books, crystals, tiaras, perfect geometry, grace.
