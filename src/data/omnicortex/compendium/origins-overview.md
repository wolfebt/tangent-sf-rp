# Origins System Overview & Trait Selection

A character's **Origin** in Tangent SF RP represents the foundational environment, planetary ecology, or cultural habitat that shaped their youth, physical constitution, and worldview.

## Skill Points & Trait Economy
- **Origin Skill Points (SP)**: Choosing an Origin grants a character **20 Skill Points (SP)** to invest strictly into the **Society Skills** listed for that Origin.
- **Starting Trait Selection**: Players select **TWO (2) Starting Traits** for free from the list associated with their chosen Origin.
- **Additional Traits (CP Rule)**: Players may purchase additional traits from their Origin's available list at a cost of **1 Character Point (CP)** per additional trait.
- **Secondary Origin**: Characters may choose a secondary Origin, expanding their accessible list of Society Skills and selectable Traits without granting additional starting SP.

## The 11 Planetary & Habitat Origins
1. **Agricultural**: Agri-worlds, rural farming cooperatives, hydroponic arcologies, and preservation biospheres.
2. **Aquatic**: Ocean planets, pelagic flotillas, and pressurized deep-sea abyssal habitats.
3. **Colony**: Frontier outposts, planetary terraforming ventures, and modular pioneer homesteads.
4. **Enlightened**: Worlds saturated with ley lines, metaphysical crystal resonance, and monastic academies.
5. **Hostile**: Death worlds, radioactive wastelands, toxic atmosphere installations, and extreme biomes.
6. **Industrial**: Factory worlds, automated manufacturing hives, strip-mining pits, and heavy refineries.
7. **Leisure**: Utopian paradise planets, casino orbital strips, luxury resort stations, and aesthetic spas.
8. **Militaristic**: Garrison fortress worlds, sector training bastions, and perimeter citadel systems.
9. **Research**: Planetary laboratories, xenological observatories, and classified R&D facilities.
10. **Spacer**: Generation ships, void-born asteroid belts, orbital transit gates, and free-trader flotillas.
11. **Urban**: Sprawling megalopolises, neon-drenched arcologies, dense multi-layered cyber-cities, and hive habitats.
