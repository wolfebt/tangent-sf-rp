# **URBAN**

# **URBAN**

People who live in cities, from large metropolises to smaller but highly developed areas, will experience the benefits and drawbacks of "civilized society," which includes a high population density. Large high-rises will be the norm, and communities will be close together. Ground and air vehicles, including GEVs and hovercraft, will be commonplace.

## **Traits**

### *Adaptability*

Urban environments are constantly changing and unpredictable. Urban characters are adaptable and quick to adjust to new situations and challenges.

*\+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily*

&nbsp;

### *Contacts*

Building a network of contacts is essential for urban characters. They have connections to informants, fixers, and other individuals who can provide valuable information or assistance.

*Gather Information checks at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Resourceful*

Surviving in a bustling city requires resourcefulness. Urban characters are adept at finding creative solutions to problems and making the most of their surroundings.

*Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Social Skills*

Interacting with diverse populations and navigating complex social dynamics is crucial for urban characters. They may possess charisma, negotiation skills, or the ability to blend into different social circles.

*\+1 all Social Manipulation Skills and Culture Knowledge*

&nbsp;

### *Street Fighting*

Urban characters may have combat skills tailored to close-quarters combat and navigating tight spaces. They excel in hand-to-hand combat.

*\+1 Unarmed Combat and Defense*

&nbsp;

### *Streetwise*

Urban characters are often well-versed in the ways of the city streets. They have knowledge of the local neighborhoods, underground networks, and criminal elements.

*\+2 Streetwise (additional \+1 in Home City)*

&nbsp;

### *Tech-Savvy*

In a futuristic urban environment, technology plays a significant role. Urban characters may have expertise in hacking, cybernetics, or using advanced gadgets and devices.

*\+2 to Knowledges of Computer and Technology*

&nbsp;

### *Urban Survival Skills*

Urban characters may possess skills that help them survive in a harsh urban environment, such as parkour and being unnoticed.

*\+2 Athletics and Stealth Skills (in Urban and developed areas)*

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis**

## **11\. URBAN**

*City, density, skyscrapers, crowds, traffic.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Massive avenues for parades. Statues taller than buildings. Brutalist government ministries. Citizens walking in orderly lines.  
* **STYLE:** Monumental Capital, Totalitarian Architecture.  
* **PALETTE:** Stone White, Red Banners, Grey Concrete.  
* **TOKENS:** Statues, broad streets, columns, order, scale.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Layers of city. Upper levels are pristine white and glass. Lower levels are bathed in neon ads. Holographic sky.  
* **STYLE:** Cyber-Corporate Arcology, Cyberpunk Core.  
* **PALETTE:** Neon Pink/Cyan, Glass White, Asphalt Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Holograms, skyscrapers, layers, flying cars, ads.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Walled city with concentric rings. Stone lower levels, high-tech spires on top. Narrow, winding streets.  
* **STYLE:** Citadel City, Minas Tirith Sci-Fi.  
* **PALETTE:** Stone Grey, Spire Silver, Lantern Orange.  
* **TOKENS:** Walls, spires, narrow streets, layers, history.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** Unplanned urban density. Houses stacked on houses. Cables everywhere. Street markets. Colorful, dangerous, alive.  
* **STYLE:** Favela Sprawl, Kowloon Walled City.  
* **PALETTE:** Mismatched Paint, Cable Black, Rust, Laundry colors.  
* **TOKENS:** Wires, stacking, density, chaos, life.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Perpetual rain. Steam from sewers. Detectives in trench coats. Diners with flickering signs. Art Deco decay.  
* **STYLE:** Noir Metropolis, Blade Runner.  
* **PALETTE:** Rain Grey, Neon Blue, Sodium Yellow, Shadow Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Rain, umbrellas, diners, steam, neon.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Buildings are giant hollow trees. Bridges of woven vines. Bioluminescent moss lighting the streets. No cars, just ziplines.  
* **STYLE:** Tree-Scraper City, Kashyyyk.  
* **PALETTE:** Bark Brown, Leaf Green, Moss Glow Green.  
* **TOKENS:** Giant trees, bridges, moss, height, nature.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Impossible tall, thin towers connected by bridges of light. Floating platforms. The city sings with wind through the spires.  
* **STYLE:** Crystal Spires, Asgard.  
* **PALETTE:** Crystal White, Gold, Sky Blue, Light Bridge Cyan.  
* **TOKENS:** Spires, light bridges, floating, elegance, height.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Clean public transit. Green roofs on every building. Pedestrian friendly. Efficient, bright, safe.  
* **STYLE:** Smart City, Solar-Utopia.  
* **PALETTE:** White, Plant Green, Glass Blue, Sunlight.  
* **TOKENS:** Monorails, parks, glass, pedestrians, clean.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** Architecture from every culture blended together. Diplomatic quarters. High-end restaurants. A melting pot of style.  
* **STYLE:** Cosmopolitan Hub, Galactic Senate.  
* **PALETTE:** Diverse colors, Gold, Glass, Warm Light.  
* **TOKENS:** Diversity, restaurants, flags, architecture blend.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** A city that looks like a circuit board. Traffic moves in perfect unison. Buildings are server stacks. No sidewalks.  
* **STYLE:** The Grid, Tron City.  
* **PALETTE:** Circuitry Black, Neon Line Blue/Orange.  
* **TOKENS:** Lines, light, traffic, geometry, speed.
