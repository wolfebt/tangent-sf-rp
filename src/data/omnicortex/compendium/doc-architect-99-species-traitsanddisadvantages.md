# **MODULAR SPECIES MATRIX: TRAITS CATALOG & DISADVANTAGES**

### **5\. Species Traits Catalog**

Traits are determined by the categories below (Basic, Advanced or Elite). Any Feature or Skill Group RECOMMENDED for the Species will be 1 point less than typical.
