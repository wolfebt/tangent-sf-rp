## **Assessment**

**Description:** The character has a practiced eye for sizing up opposition. By observing a target's stance, equipment, and demeanor, they can gauge the threat level.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 6, Sense Motive 6  
* **Benefit:** By observing a target for 1 full round, you can determine their relative threat level compared to yourself (Inferior, Equal, or Superior) and estimate their highest Skill Rank category (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle).  
* **Purpose:** Allows players to gauge if a fight is winnable before initiative is rolled.

### 

  ## **Analyze Weakness**

  **Description:** The character applies their assessment to combat, identifying chinks in armor or behavioral tics that can be exploited.

* **Prerequisite:** Assessment  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make an **Assessment Check** opposed by the target's **Bluff Check**. For every 5 points by which your result exceeds the target's, you gain a cumulative **\+1 bonus to Strike and Damage** against that specific target.  
* **Special:** This bonus lasts for the duration of the encounter or until the target successfully leaves your line of sight for more than 1 minute.

### 

## **Benefit (Authority)**

**Description:** The character holds a recognized rank within a law enforcement or military organization, granting them legal power and jurisdiction.

* **Prerequisite:** Associated Occupation (Soldier, Constable, Agent)  
* **Benefit:** You hold a specific rank.  
  * *Rank 1:* Deputy / Corporal (Local jurisdiction).  
  * *Rank 2:* Officer / Sergeant (Regional jurisdiction).  
  * *Rank 3:* Marshal / Lieutenant (Planet-wide jurisdiction).  
  * *Rank 4:* Agent / Commander (Interstellar/System-wide jurisdiction).  
* **Mechanics:** Grants **\+2 to Intimidate and Diplomacy** when interacting with law-abiding citizens or lower-ranking officials.

### 

## **Benefit (Clearance)**

**Description:** The character has official access to restricted information, government facilities, or corporate secrets.

* **Prerequisite:** Vocation or Faction standing  
* **Benefit:** You possess a security key or biometric clearance.  
  * *Rank 1:* Classified (Departmental info).  
  * *Rank 2:* Secret (Mission-critical info).  
  * *Rank 3:* Top Secret (Strategic info).  
  * *Rank 4:* Black Badge (Existential threats / Black Ops).

### 

## **Benefit (Equipment)**

**Description:** The character is sponsored by an organization that provides them with gear, rather than purchasing it with personal wealth.

* **Prerequisite:** Membership in a Faction or Corp  
* **Benefit:** You are issued a package of gear that does not count against your personal Wealth or Debt.  
  * *Rank 1:* Standard Kit (Personal Gear, Tools).  
  * *Rank 2:* Tactical Package (Weapons, Armor).  
  * *Rank 3:* Mobility Package (Personal Vehicle, Heavy Weapons).  
  * *Rank 4:* Command Package (Starship, Base of Operations).

### 

## **Benefit (Identity)**

**Description:** The character has a meticulously crafted alternate persona, complete with background records, credit history, and identification.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You gain one complete, verifiable Alternate Identity.  
* **Mechanics:** Checks to uncover your true identity are made at **Disadvantage**. If the identity is "burned" (exposed), this feature is lost until a new one is purchased/established.

### 

## **Benefit (Immunity)**

**Description:** The character possesses a legal shield against prosecution for specific actions, usually granted by a government or mega-corp.

* **Prerequisite:** Special (usually High Status or Agent)  
* **Benefit:**  
  * *Rank 1:* Order Immunity (Can claim "Following Orders" for minor infractions).  
  * *Rank 2:* Diplomatic Immunity (Cannot be prosecuted by local planetary law).  
  * *Rank 3:* Sanctioned (Authorized to commit felonies during mission parameters).  
  * *Rank 4:* License to Kill (Authorized to execute targets at discretion).

### 

## **Benefit (Status)**

**Description:** The character holds a title of nobility, corporate executive power, or high military command.

* **Prerequisite:** Special  
* **Benefit:** You gain a Title (e.g., Duke, CEO, Admiral). You gain a **\+2 Bonus to Wealth** checks.  
* **Special:** This acts as a \[Ranked\] feature; higher ranks denote higher titles and influence.

### 

## **Benefit (Wealth)**

**Description:** The character is independently wealthy, possessing vast investments, inheritance, or liquid assets.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** Increase your **Wealth Score by \+3**.  
* **Purpose:** Allows purchasing of higher Tech Level gear or luxury items during character creation and downtime.

### 

## **Connected**

**Description:** The character has friends in low (or high) places. They can call in favors for equipment, information, or safe houses.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1  
* **Benefit:** You have a network of contacts.  
  * *Rank 1:* Street/Soldier level (Gangs, Grunts).  
  * *Rank 2:* Management/Lieutenant level (Shop owners, Squad leaders).  
  * *Rank 3:* Officer/Executive level (City officials, Corp VPs).  
  * *Rank 4:* Boss/Director level (Planetary Governors, Crime Lords).  
* **Mechanics:** Make a Charisma check to call in a favor. Difficulty depends on the size of the request.

### 

## **Contacts**

**Description:** The character knows who to talk to. They don't necessarily have favors owed, but they know the flow of information.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1  
* **Benefit:** You may make **Gather Information** checks in **1/2 the typical time**.  
* **Mechanics:** You have a specific reliable contact in a chosen field (Medical, Legal, Criminal) who provides information at **Advantage**.

### 

## **Coordinated Assist**

**Description:** The character is a natural team player or squad leader, adept at setting up allies for success.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 1  
* **Benefit:** When using the **Aid Another** action (Skill Synergy), you grant an additional **\+1 Bonus** to your ally's check.  
* **Special:** This feature is \[Ranked\] by Intellect. (Rank 2 grants \+2, etc.).

### 

## **Climber**

**Description:** The character is at home on vertical surfaces, moving with the agility of a spider or monkey.

* **Prerequisite:** Athletics 1  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **Climb Speed** equal to half your base speed. You may **Take 10** on Climb checks even when rushed or threatened. You retain your Agility bonus to Defense while climbing.

### 

## **Distract**

**Description:** The character can cause a diversion so potent that it leaves opponents vulnerable.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1 or Intellect 1  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make a Bluff or Engineering (for tech distraction) check vs. the Target’s Will.  
* **Success:** The target's attention is totally consumed by the distraction. They are considered **Flat-Footed** against all attacks from characters other than you until the start of your next turn.

### 

## **Double Jointed**

**Description:** The character possesses extreme flexibility, able to dislocate joints or contort into impossible shapes.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **\+4 Bonus** on Escape Artist checks and any check involving fitting into tight spaces.

### 

## **Eidetic Memory**

**Description:** The character remembers everything they see or read with fair accuracy.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **\+4 Bonus** on Memory Checks. You may use **Knowledge Skills Untrained**, as you recall snippets of information you’ve encountered previously.

### 

  ## **Photographic Memory**

  **Description:** The character's mind is a perfect recorder. Visual and textual information is stored instantly and permanently.

* **Prerequisite:** Eidetic Memory, Intellect 4  
* **Benefit:** You recall images and texts perfectly. Study time for **Learning new Skills** is reduced by **50%**.

### 

## **Endurance**

**Description:** The character has exceptional cardiovascular health and stamina for long-term exertion.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **\+4 Bonus** on checks to resist non-lethal damage from exhaustion, starvation, thirst, or forced marches. You can hold your breath twice as long as normal.

### 

  ## **Diehard**

  **Description:** The character is too stubborn or tough to die when they should.

* **Prerequisite:** Endurance  
* **Benefit:** When your Health is reduced to 0 or lower (but not dead), you automatically **Stabilize**. You do not lose 1 HP per round. You may continue to take partial actions while at Death's Door, though you risk killing yourself if you take strenuous damage.

### 

## **Fast Heal**

**Description:** The character's metabolism repairs tissue at an accelerated rate.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** Your natural healing rate is **doubled**. You recover Vitality and Health twice as fast during Rest.  
* **Special:** This feature is \[Ranked\]. Rank 2 \= 3x healing, Rank 3 \= 4x healing.

### 

  ## **Regeneration**

  **Description:** The character's body knits flesh back together in real-time.

* **Prerequisite:** Fast Heal, Stamina 4  
* **Benefit:** You recover **1 Health point per hour** automatically without resting, provided you are not dead. You can regrow lost limbs and organs over 1d4 weeks.

### 

## **Favored Enemy**

**Description:** The character has studied a specific type of creature extensively, knowing their anatomy, tactics, and psychology.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 1  
* **Benefit:** Choose a creature type (e.g., Humanoid \[Human\], Beast, Synthetic, Aberration). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to **Strike**, **Damage**, **Bluff**, **Insight**, and **Survival** checks against them.  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] Can be taken for different creature types.

### 

## **Fearless**

**Description:** Whether through madness, conditioning, or bravery, the character is immune to terror.

* **Prerequisite:** Will 3+  
* **Benefit:** You are **Immune** to Fear effects (magical, psionic, or mundane intimidation).

### 

## **Jack-of-all-trades**

**Description:** The character has picked up a little bit of everything in their travels.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** You may attempt **any Skill check Untrained**, even those normally requiring training (like Medicine, Engineering, or Piloting).

### 

## **Lightning Calculator**

**Description:** The character is a living computer, able to perform complex mathematics instantly.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 1  
* **Benefit:** You perform mathematical calculations at **2x speed**. This applies to Astrogation, Engineering calculations, and Cryptography.

### 

  ## **Mental Alacrity**

  **Description:** The character thinks at hyperspeed, allowing them to perform mental tasks while others are still reacting.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2, Lightning Calculator  
* **Benefit:** You gain an additional **Mental Action** per round, provided you take no Physical actions that round.  
  * *Usage:* Useful for issuing commands, hacking, scanning, or using purely mental Psionics.

  ### 

    ## **Multiplexing**

    **Description:** The character's mind can process multiple parallel streams of thought.

* **Prerequisite:** Mental Alacrity, Intellect 3  
* **Benefit:** Gain **\+1 additional Mental Action** per round to your Mental Alacrity pool.  
* **Special:** \[Ranked\] Limited by Intellect modifier.

### 

## **Master Plan**

**Description:** The character is a tactical genius who prepares for every eventuality.

* **Prerequisite:** Logic 6, Intellect 3  
* **Benefit:** If you have time to prepare for an encounter (minimum 1 hour), make a **Logic Check**. For every point your result exceeds CR 10, you create a **Bonus Pool**.  
* **Usage:** During the encounter, you can distribute these points to allies (as a free action) to add to their Attack rolls or Skill checks.

### 

## **Natural Leader**

**Description:** The character attracts loyal followers and can manage a crew or organization effectively.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 3, Diplomacy 6  
* **Benefit:** You gain a number of **Followers** (NPCs) and potentially a **Cohort** (a loyal lieutenant). The number and quality depend on your Level and Charisma score. You gain a \+2 bonus to Organization/Administration checks.

### 

## **Nimble Moves**

**Description:** The character dances around obstacles that would slow others down.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2  
* **Benefit:** You ignore **10 feet** of difficult terrain penalties during your movement per round.

### 

  ## **Acrobatic Steps**

  **Description:** The character bounds over debris and uneven ground.

* **Prerequisite:** Nimble Moves, Agility 3  
* **Benefit:** You ignore **30 feet** of difficult terrain penalties during movement. You may move at full speed while balancing on narrow surfaces.

  ### 

  ## **Perfect Balance**

  **Description:** The character has perfect equilibrium.  
* **Prerequisite:** Acrobatic Steps, Agility 4  
* **Benefit:** You ignore **all** difficult terrain penalties. You are **Immune to the Prone condition** (you cannot be tripped or knocked down) unless you choose to be.

### 

## **Tough**

**Description:** The character has a high threshold for pain and minor trauma.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** You gain **Damage Resistance (DR) 5** specifically against **Non-Lethal** damage and Stun damage.

### 

  ## **Pain Tolerance**

  **Description:** The character can function through agony that would incapacitate others.

* **Prerequisite:** Tough, Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** You make all **Stun Resistance Checks** at **Advantage**. You do not suffer penalty modifiers from being wounded or fatigued (though you still take the damage).

### 

## **Runner**

**Description:** The character is trained for speed and distance.

* **Prerequisite:** Strength 1, Agility 1, Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** You Run at **5x** your base speed (instead of 4x) and Sprint at **7x** (instead of 6x). You gain \+2 to Athletics checks for running endurance.

### 

## **Trance**

**Description:** The character can enter a deep meditative state to conserve energy or feign death.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 1  
* **Benefit:** You can voluntarily slow your heartbeat and metabolism. You consume 1/10th the oxygen and food. You can appear dead to casual inspection (Medicine check CR 20 to detect life).

### 

## **Swimmer**

**Description:** The character is a strong swimmer.

* **Prerequisite:** Athletics 1  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **Swim Speed** equal to half your base speed. You may **Take 10** on Swim checks even in stormy water. You can hold your breath for 3x Constitution score in rounds.

### 

## **Tracker**

**Description:** The character is an expert hunter.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 1  
* **Benefit:** You can follow tracks using **Survival** or **Awareness** at **Full Movement Speed** without penalty (normally tracking reduces speed to half).

### 

## **Well Informed**

**Description:** The character keeps their ear to the ground and knows a little bit about every local scene.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** Upon entering a new settlement or region, you may make an immediate **Gather Information** check for free to learn the local news, rumors, and power structure.

### 

## **Quick**

**Description:** The character is naturally fleet of foot.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1  
* **Benefit:** **\+10 feet** to Base Movement Speed and **\+1 to Initiative**.

### 

  ## **Quicker**

  **Description:** The character is exceptionally fast.

* **Prerequisite:** Quick, Agility 2  
* **Benefit:** **\+20 feet** (total) to Base Movement Speed and **\+2 to Initiative**.

  ### 

  ## **Quickest**

  **Description:** The character moves as a blur.  
* **Prerequisite:** Quicker, Agility 3  
* **Benefit:** **\+30 feet** (total) to Base Movement Speed and **\+3 to Initiative**.

### 

## **Zero G Training**

**Description:** The character is trained to operate in microgravity environments without disorientation.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1  
* **Benefit:** You suffer **No Disadvantage** or penalties for operating in Zero Gravity. You gain a \+2 bonus to Acrobatics when maneuvering in Zero-G.

### 

## **Absolute Pitch**

**Description:** The character possesses an exaggerated structural asymmetry in the planum temporale, allowing them to instantly identify, categorize, and perfectly recreate auditory frequencies without a reference tone.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 1, Perform 1  
* **Benefit:** You can perfectly memorize and mimic voices, musical notes, or mechanical frequencies. You gain a +4 Bonus on checks involving auditory matching, impersonation (vocal), or acoustic security bypass.

### 

## **Algorithmic Cognition**

**Description:** The character's brain bypasses standard working memory bottlenecks, allowing them to perform hypercalculia and extreme high-speed mental arithmetic natively.

* **Prerequisite:** Lightning Calculator, Intellect 4  
* **Benefit:** You may perform complex mathematical calculations, cryptanalysis, and algorithmic decryption as a Free Action instead of a Standard or Full Round Action. You gain Advantage on all Logic checks involving mathematics or codebreaking.

### 

## **Dual-Hemispheric Processing**

**Description:** Due to a congenital anomaly (such as the agenesis of the corpus callosum), the character's brain hemispheres operate with extreme independence. They can read two pages simultaneously and process localized data without global interference.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 3, Special (Congenital Trait)  
* **Benefit:** You can read, scan, and process textual or visual information at 10x normal speed. Furthermore, you may perform two different mental tasks (e.g., hacking a terminal while calculating astrogation routes) without suffering a multi-tasking penalty.

### 

## **Echolocation**

**Description:** Through extreme cross-modal neuroplasticity, the character's visual cortex has been repurposed to process auditory data, acting as a high-resolution biological sonar.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 3, Must possess the Blindness flaw (or be visually deprived)  
* **Benefit:** By emitting sharp clicks, you gain Blindsight up to 60 feet. You ignore all concealment penalties caused by darkness, fog, or visual invisibility within this range. You cannot be Flanked by enemies within your sonar radius.

### 

## **Eidetic Constructor**

**Description:** The character possesses unparalleled visual-spatial memory. They retain "frozen frames" of complex visual stimuli and can perfectly recreate them in physical space or memory.

* **Prerequisite:** Eidetic Memory, Awareness 2  
* **Benefit:** After observing an area, building, or object for 1 full round, you memorize its exact structural layout, dimensions, and structural columns. You gain a +4 Bonus to Navigation, Architecture, or Tactical checks involving that specific location.

### 

## **Extreme Endurance**

**Description:** A rare point mutation (like the EPOR gene) causes the character's bone marrow to overproduce red blood cells, granting them a permanently maximized oxygen-carrying capacity.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 3  
* **Benefit:** You gain a +4 Bonus to all Stamina checks involving breath-holding, suffocation, or intense aerobic exertion. Your movement speed is not reduced when fatigued.

### 

## **Hyperthymesia**

**Description:** Highly Superior Autobiographical Memory (HSAM). The character involuntarily and flawlessly records every moment of their life, immune to the standard reconstructive decay of memory.

* **Prerequisite:** Eidetic Memory, Wisdom 2  
* **Benefit:** You automatically succeed on any Memory check related to events you have personally experienced. You are Immune to memory alteration, gaslighting, or mind-altering effects that attempt to erase or rewrite your past.

### 

## **Hypoxia Tolerance**

**Description:** Genetic variants (such as EPAS1/EGLN1) allow the character to thrive in environments with severely depleted oxygen without suffering altitude sickness or cardiovascular strain.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** You suffer no penalties for operating in high-altitude, low-oxygen, or thin-atmosphere environments. You require 50% less oxygen to survive than a standard member of your species.

### 

## **Lactate Immunity**

**Description:** The character's physiology clears lactic acid so efficiently that they effectively never hit a lactate threshold. Their muscles do not burn or fail from prolonged standard exertion.

* **Prerequisite:** Endurance, Stamina 4  
* **Benefit:** You are Immune to physical fatigue caused by running, swimming, or aerobic exertion. You can travel continuously at a run for days without requiring rest, provided you remain calorically fueled and hydrated.

### 

## **Myostatin Hypertrophy**

**Description:** A homozygous mutation in the MSTN gene renders the myostatin protein non-functional, removing biological brakes on muscle fiber proliferation. The character possesses massive, dense musculature without needing to train.

* **Prerequisite:** Strength 3, Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain a +2 Bonus to Might and Athletics checks. For the purposes of Grappling, lifting weight, and breaking objects, you are treated as if you are one Size Category larger.

### 

## **Nociceptor Dampening**

**Description:** A mutation in the transcription factors regulating peripheral pain signaling (such as ZFHX2) renders the character profoundly insensitive to noxious pain while retaining normal tactile sensation.

* **Prerequisite:** Pain Tolerance, Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** You are Immune to non-lethal Stun damage, shock, and pain-compliance techniques (including torture). You do not suffer any negative mechanical modifiers from being severely wounded or burned. Drawback: You must make Awareness checks to notice if you are taking damage from environmental hazards (like standing on a hot surface).

### 

## **Short Sleeper**

**Description:** A mutation in the DEC2 gene alters the character's circadian rhythm and orexin production. They experience highly concentrated, perfectly restorative sleep.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** You only require 4 hours of sleep to gain the full benefits of a Long Rest. You are highly resistant to magical or chemical sleep effects, gaining Advantage on saves against them.

### 

## **Synesthetic Savant**

**Description:** The character experiences profound, cross-modal sensory wiring (e.g., seeing numbers as colors and textures). This provides a permanent, involuntary mnemonic structure for data retention.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain Advantage on all Memory checks, Knowledge retrieval, and cryptography. Your unique perception makes you highly resistant to visual illusions, granting a +2 Bonus to disbelieve fake stimuli.

### 

## **Tetrachromacy**

**Description:** The presence of a fourth class of cone cell in the retina allows the character to perceive millions of hues invisible to a standard trichromatic human.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 2, Special (Genetically Female, or engineered variant)  
* **Benefit:** You gain a +4 Bonus on Perception, Investigation, and Tracking checks that rely on visual details. You can instantly spot metameric camouflages (e.g., artificial foliage vs. real foliage, or subtle skin discoloration from poisons/shapeshifting).

### 

## **Unbreakable Skeleton**

**Description:** A gain-of-function mutation in the LRP5 gene blocks inhibitory proteins, causing continuous, hyperactive bone formation. The character possesses a skeleton of extreme, unyielding density.

* **Prerequisite:** Tough, Stamina 3  
* **Benefit:** You gain Damage Resistance (DR) 5 specifically against Bludgeoning, Crushing, and Falling damage. You are completely immune to effects that specifically fracture or break bones (such as the Cripple Sneak Attack feature). Because of your extreme bone density, your unarmed strikes deal an additional +1 Damage.
