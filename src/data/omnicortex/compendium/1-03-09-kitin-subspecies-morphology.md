# 1.03.09 Kitin Morphologies & Playable Hive Subspecies

While most Kitin operate in direct consensus with the Hive Mind, unique solitary morphs—known as **Severed** or **Synapse Seekers**—possess independent sapience and travel the galaxy as freelance operatives, scouts, and heavy mercenaries.

---

## Playable Kitin Morph Attributes
- **Attribute Modifiers:** Strength +2, Stamina +2, Agility +1, Charisma -2.
- **Inherent Natural Armor:** Chitinous Exoskeleton granting **DR 4 Kinetic / DR 3 Energy**.
- **Multi-Limbed Physiology:** 4 arms (2 primary weapon limbs, 2 secondary utility limbs).
- **Wall Climbing:** Natural climbing pace equal to walking pace (30 ft / round).
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft, Pheromone Tracking (Scent DC 10).
- **Stigma:** High Social Stigma (-3 to social checks with xenophobic factions).

## Game Mechanics Rules
```
Species CP Cost: 15 CP
Inherent Armor: DR 4 Kinetic / DR 3 Energy
Climb Speed: 30 ft / round (No climb check required on textured surfaces)
Multi-Limbed: Can hold 2 two-handed weapons, or 1 weapon and 2 shields/tools.
```

## Gameplay Instructions
Choose between the Warrior Sub-morph (extra STR) and the Weaver Sub-morph (extra INT/Tech).

## Designer Notes
Severed Kitin often experience phantom pheromone sensations when separated from their native collective.
