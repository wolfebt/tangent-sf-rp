# 1.04.Archetypal.Corporate — 03. CORPORATE SYNDICATE (+3 WS)

## Executive Summary & Nov 2 Canonical Game Data
* **Archetypal Faction Classification:** 03. Corporate Syndicate
* **Tech / Meta Level:** TL 4 / ML 1
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Score Modifier:** **+3 WS**
* **Canonical 20-Point Faction Skill Package (0 CP):** Knowledge: Business (+5), Diplomacy (+5), Bluff (+4), Computers (+3), Insight (+3)
* **Canonical Recommended Features (-1 CP Discount):** Benefit (Wealth), Benefit (Clearance), Connected, Contacts, Deceitful, Educated, Influence, Master Plan, Persuasive, Well Informed
* **CRISP Visual Semiotics Key:** Glass-and-steel boardrooms, holographic stock tickers, tailored ballistic suits.

---

CORPORATE
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Factions characterized entirely by their focus on profit and market dominance. They operate as sovereign nations, wielding wealth and private security forces to control resources, expand their influence, and develop new technologies at the expense of ethics.
* Key Themes: Unfettered Capitalism, Corporate Espionage, Profit over People.
* Relationship to Others: Transactional. Everyone is a customer or a competitor.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: [Name] Megacorporation / Holdings / Enterprises
* Colloquialisms: The Suits / The Board / The Brand
* Archetype: Profit-Driven Syndicates
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Corporate HQ Worlds)
* Driving Mandate: Maximize profits, control resources, expand market share.
* Motto: "Growth is the only metric of success."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Greedy, ruthless, opportunistic, profit-driven, pragmatic, competitive.
* Social Structure: Rigid corporate hierarchy (Intern to CEO).
* View on Outsiders: Viewed through the lens of demographics and exploitability.
* Law & Order: Corporate policy enforced by private military contractors.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Wealthy, well-connected, advanced technology, skilled in business and management.
* Weaknesses: Lack of ethics, prioritizes short-term gains, vulnerability to public opinion and corporate espionage, corruption.
* Military Doctrine: Cost-effective deployment of mercenaries and high-tech private security.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Highly diverse (Humans prevalent).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Finance (+5)
   * Diplomacy (Negotiation) (+5)
   * Computers (+4)
   * Insight (+3)
   * Streetwise (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Executive: Making high-level decisions and organizing hostile takeovers.
   * The Fixer: Solving corporate problems through unsavory, off-the-books means.
   * The Auditor: Investigating internal theft, inefficiency, and corporate espionage.
   * The Corporate Spy: Stealing R&D secrets from rival megacorporations.
   * The PR Rep: Spinning disasters into positive media coverage.
   * The Security Chief: Managing PMC forces to protect high-value assets.
   * The Data Broker: Buying and selling valuable blackmail and demographic material.
   * The R&D Scientist: Developing the next profitable technological breakthrough.
   * The HR Enforcer: "Terminating" problematic or unionizing employees permanently.
   * The Asset Liquidator: Stripping down bankrupt colonies and selling the parts.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Benefit (Wealth): Access to deep expense accounts and vast capital.
   * Benefit (Clearance): Biometric access to restricted corporate servers and facilities.
   * Silver Tongue: The ability to close deals and navigate tense boardrooms.
   * Connected: A vast network of vendors, contractors, and middle-managers.
   * Well-Informed: Constantly receiving market telemetry and news feeds.
   * Golden Smile: Charmed charisma that disarms even the most hostile clients.
   * Legal Immunity: Protected by high-priced corporate lawyers and shell companies.
   * Platinum Personality: Dominating social situations with sheer expensive presence.
   * Corporate Sponsorship: Access to cutting edge, prototype gear before market release.
   * Ruthless Negotiation: Extracting the absolute maximum value from any trade.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Sleek, expensive, and intimidatingly clean.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Glass boardrooms, tailored suits, holographic stock tickers, neon advertising.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Corporate Noir, Sleek Cyberpunk, High-Status.
   * CONTEXT: Glass boardrooms, glowing data-tables, sharp suits with hidden armor, high-altitude city views.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Cold, wealthy, untouchable, tense, professional.
