# **Kitin ---**

The Kitin are a vast, multifaceted insectoid lineage unified through a deep psionic hive network known as the Chorus. Possessing innate biological biotechnology, telepathic communion, and sleepless vigilance, they range from covert humanoid envoys to specialized subterranean hunters and colossal aggregate swarms.

**Kitin Base Heritage (8 CP base):**
* **Awakened (Psychic) [2 CP]:** Conductors and receptors of the Hive’s psionic Chorus.
* **Biotechnology [2 CP]:** Natural affinity and interface with living technology and bio-grafts.
* **Hive Connection / Hive-Link [2 CP]:** Constant telepathic connection to all Kitin Strains.
* **Torpor / Sleepless [2 CP]:** Requires only 4 hours of conscious torpor; immune to sleep effects.
