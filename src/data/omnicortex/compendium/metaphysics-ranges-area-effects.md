# Ranges, Targeting & Area of Effect Shapes

## Range Modifiers
- **Melee / Point Blank (5 ft)**: +5 to Check
- **Close (100 ft)**: No Modifier
- **Medium (500 ft)**: -5 to Check
- **Long (2,500 ft)**: -10 to Check
- **Extended (Miles+)**: -15 to Check (requires sensory link)

## Area of Effect Shapes
- **Blast Sphere (10 ft radius)**: -5 to Focus Skill check
- **Line (5 ft wide × 60 ft long)**: -10 to Focus Skill check
- **Cone (90 ft long × 45 ft wide)**: -15 to Focus Skill check
