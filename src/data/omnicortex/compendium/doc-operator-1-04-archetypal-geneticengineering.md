# 1.04. ARCHETYPAL FACTION: 08. GENETIC ENGINEERING LAB (+3 WS)

## Executive Summary & Nov 2 Canonical Game Data
* **Archetypal Faction Classification:** 08. Genetic Engineering Lab
* **Tech / Meta Level:** TL 4 / ML 2
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Score Modifier:** **+3 WS**
* **Canonical 20-Point Faction Skill Package (0 CP):** Knowledge: Medicine (+5), Knowledge: Science (+5), Knowledge: Nature (+4), Computers (+3), Knowledge: Investigation (+3)
* **Canonical Recommended Features (-1 CP Discount):** Analytical Sense, Augmented, Biotechnology, Biotechnologist, Educated, Fast Heal, Medic, Regeneration, Researcher, Scholar
* **CRISP Visual Semiotics Key:** Sterile white cloning vats, glowing emerald nutrient fluid, helical holo-displays.

GENETIC ENGINEERING
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Factions wholly consumed by the pursuit of biological perfection and biotechnology. They experiment relentlessly to create new life forms, enhance existing species, and achieve biological dominance, often viewing the organic body as merely a starting point.
* Key Themes: Playing God, Bio-Horror, Transhumanism, Mutagenics.
* Relationship to Others: Viewed with deep suspicion or ethical disgust by purist factions.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Bio-Ascendancy / The Genesis Bloc
* Colloquialisms: The Splicers / Vat-Grown
* Archetype: Biologic Ascendants
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Sterile lab-worlds or hyper-mutated jungles)
* Driving Mandate: Create new life forms, enhance existing species, achieve biological dominance.
* Motto: "Flesh is a canvas; we are the artists."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Ambitious, experimental, controversial, scientific.
* Social Structure: Meritocracy based on genetic perfection and scientific output.
* View on Outsiders: Unoptimized genetic material waiting to be upgraded.
* Law & Order: Regulated by peer review and genetic patent law.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 4
* Strengths: Skilled scientists, ability to adapt biologically to circumstances, advanced genetic engineering, ability to create powerful life forms.
* Weaknesses: Ethical controversies, unintended consequences, uncontrollable creations, reliance on bio-tech over mechanical infrastructure.
* Military Doctrine: Deployment of engineered bioweapons, mutants, and enhanced super-soldiers.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Heavily Mutated or Gene-Forged species.
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Medicine (+5)
   * Science (Biology) (+5)
   * Vocation (Lab Tech) (+4)
   * Computers (+3)
   * Insight (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Geneticist: Pushing the boundaries of DNA manipulation.
   * The Bioengineer: Grafting biological weapons onto living subjects.
   * The Enhanced Warrior: A vat-grown super-soldier built for extreme combat.
   * The Test Subject: A survivor of brutal experiments wielding unstable powers.
   * The Mutant: An unpredictable evolution displaying radical physical changes.
   * The Gene-Splicer: Combining the traits of multiple species into one form.
   * The Bio-Hacker: Writing new viruses and manipulating living code.
   * The Vat-Grown Clone: A perfect, disposable replica designed for specific tasks.
   * The Evolutionary Purist: Believing only the genetically perfect deserve to live.
   * The Apex Specimen: The flawless, terrifying result of generations of breeding.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Augmented (Bio): Heavy biological modifications replacing standard organs.
   * Fast Heal: Rapid cellular regeneration common in engineered flesh.
   * Tough: Bone density increased to shrug off kinetic impacts.
   * Enduring: Enhanced stamina filtering fatigue toxins instantly.
   * Disease Immunity: Flawless immune system rejecting all natural pathogens.
   * Heightened Senses: Engineered eyes or ears pushing past natural limits.
   * Biometric Bypass: Fooling genetic locks using altered DNA signatures.
   * Pain Tolerance: The ability to switch off pain receptors during combat.
   * Natural Weapons: Retractable bone-blades or acidic spit glands.
   * Adaptive Physiology: Quickly growing gills or thick hide when needed.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Clinical, organic, and unsettling.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Stasis tanks, sterile labs, organic technology, surgical theaters, biological perfection.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Biologic Ascendants, Clinical Sci-Fi, Body Horror.
   * CONTEXT: Stasis tanks, sterile labs, organic technology, surgical theaters, unsettling biological perfection.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Clinical, unsettling, pristine, unnatural, quiet.
