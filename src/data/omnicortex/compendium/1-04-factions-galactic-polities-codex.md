# 1.04 Factions & Galactic Polities Codex

Factions in Tangent represent the interstellar sovereign powers, megacorporate syndicates, monastic orders, and mercenary guilds that vie for control of the galaxy.

---

## Major Canonical Galactic Polities

### 1. The Dracon Dynasty
- **Archetype:** Feudal Space Monarchy / Dragon-Lord Technocracy.
- **Core Philosophy:** *Protection, Patience, and Long-Term Stability.*
- **Society Structure:** High Nobility (Draconic descendants) ruling over Knightly Dragoons and protected Commoners.
- **Key Skills:** Politics, Piloting, Archaic Weapons, History, Etiquette.

### 2. The Arcology Syndicates
- **Archetype:** Hyper-Capitalist Megacorporate Coalition.
- **Core Philosophy:** *Efficiency, Profitability, and Market Dominance.*
- **Society Structure:** Board of Directors, Executive Officers, and Contracted Citizens.
- **Key Skills:** Business, Computers, Technology, Modern Weapons, Deceit.

### 3. The Cyber-Guilds of Technomancers
- **Archetype:** Transhumanist Cyber-Collective.
- **Core Philosophy:** *Transcendence through Synthesis of Flesh and Machine.*
- **Society Structure:** Fabricators, Circuit-Monks, and Neural Enclaves.
- **Key Skills:** Technology, Computers, Science, Cybernetics, Physics.

### 4. The Holy Federation of Light
- **Archetype:** Theocratic Galactic Concordat.
- **Core Philosophy:** *Spiritual Purity, Cosmic Harmony, and Divine Law.*
- **Society Structure:** Patriarchs, Inquisitors, Templars, and Faithful Laymen.
- **Key Skills:** Religion, Metaphysics, Diplomacy, Fortitude, Will.
