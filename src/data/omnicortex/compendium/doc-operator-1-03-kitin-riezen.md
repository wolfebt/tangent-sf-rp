# **RIEZEN**
**Divergent Swarm**
**Classification:** Insect Ooze (Swarm)
**CP Cost:** 26 CP

**Description:**
The Riezen are close relations of the original insectoid genome. While the Kitin Collective followed a path of steered evolution under the Manelli, the Riezen are the result of raw, unmanaged survival following a planetary-scale NBC (Nuclear, Biological, Chemical) warfare event on their homeworld.
They do not possess a singular body; instead, a "Riezen" is a collective swarm of thousands of fine-sized insects acting as a singular psionic entity.

They are not part of the Kitin Collective and are considered a biological "heresy" by the Manelli. Their very presence creates "Psionic Static," a discordant frequency that physically and mentally disrupts the Manelli’s ability to coordinate their own hive.

**Technical Profile:**
* **Size:** Fine (Individual insects) / Large (Manifested Swarm)
* **Movement:** 30 ft Flightspeed
* **Tech Level:** — (Utilizes biological zombification of host technology)
* **Meta Level:** 3 (Resonant / Chaotic)
* **Stigma:** Severe Xeno, Monstrous (-6 CP when inhabiting hosts)

**Species Features:**
* **Hive Mind Swarm:** The swarm is a single consciousness. It cannot be "killed" unless the majority of the swarm is destroyed. [9 CP total w/ Swarm Body]
* **Telepathic:** Innate psychic communication within the swarm and with external minds. [2 CP]
* **Host Zombification:** The Riezen can inhabit and "pilot" the corpses of other species or damaged biomorphic husks, utilizing them as armored shells. [2 CP]
* **Psionic Interference (The Static):** Their psionic frequency is "out of tune" with the Manelli. Any Manelli within psychic range of a Riezen swarm suffers a -2 penalty to all Psionic and Wisdom checks due to mental feedback. If the Riezen are in the immediate vicinity, any communications between Kitin are severely disrupted, with the Chorus manipulated or used at a Disadvantage. [2 CP]
* **Fast Heal & Regeneration:** The swarm constantly births new members to replace those lost, allowing it to heal rapidly. [4 CP]
* **Honey & Paper Manufacture:** Ability to produce biological adhesives and pulp for nest building and restraint. [1 CP]
* **Sleepless:** Does not require rest; the swarm operates in constant rotation. [2 CP]
* **Adapted Features:** [2 CP]

**The Great Disruption: Riezen vs. Manelli**
The Manelli view the Riezen as a "cancer" on their genetic legacy. Because the Riezen share the same original genome but lack the "Chorus" protocols, they act as a void in the Kitin network.
* **The Counter-Chorus:** Riezen can instinctively sense the Manelli's steering signals and "jam" them. In combat, a Riezen swarm causes any "Chorus" communications to be disharmonized, with checks made at Disadvantage and ranges reduced by erratic static waves.
* **Interaction:** While the Kitin Collective is orderly and "glossy obsidian," the Riezen are chaotic, often appearing as a pulsating, multicolored cloud of toxic greens, deep violets, and amber oozes.
