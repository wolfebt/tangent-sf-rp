# Combat Maneuvers & Tactical Actions

## Standard Combat Actions
- **Attack**: Single melee, ranged, or heavy weapon strike.
- **Active Defense**: Immediate reaction replacing static defense DC.
- **Aiming**: Spending an action to track a target, gaining +2 Strike per round.
- **Called Shot (-5 Strike)**: Directing attack to a specific limb, sensory organ, or mechanical subsystem.
- **Feint**: Opposed Bluff vs. Sense Motive; success bypasses target defense for a Sneak Attack.

## Movement Actions
- **Evasive Movement**: Moving at high speed while dodging (+1 Defense +1 per 10ft moved).
- **Braced**: Setting a heavy weapon or stance against charges; negates recoil penalties.
- **Subtle Movement**: Stalking quietly in shadows to maintain stealth during maneuvers.
