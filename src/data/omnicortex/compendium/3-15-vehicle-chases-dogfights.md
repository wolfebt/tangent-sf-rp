# 3.15 Vehicular Combat, Dogfights & System Failures

Vehicular combat escalates the core 2d10 rules across 4 Domains of War (*Land, Nautical, Atmospheric, Interstellar*).

---

## 1. Initiative & The Crew Turn
- **Initiative Formula:** $2\text{d}10 + \text{Agility Mod} + \text{Alertness} + \text{Handling Modifier}$.
- **Pilot:** Controls Movement, Evasion (Defense), and Fixed Forward Weapons (45° arc).
- **Gunner(s):** Control Turrets and Pintle mounts (360° arc); track independent actions.
- **Engineer / System Operator:** Manages Shields, Sensors, EW, and Field Repairs.

---

## 2. Tactical Dogfights & Stalling
- **The Dogfight:** Opposed Pilot checks at start of round.
  - *Winner:* Gains **Advantage** on attack rolls this round and chooses positioning (**Tail / Flank**).
  - *Loser:* Considered **Flat-Footed** against the winner.
- **Stalling:** Flying slower than Stall Speed forces a **Pilot Check (DC 20)** or craft enters a terminal fall.

---

## 3. System Failure Table (d6)
Rolled when a vehicle suffers a Critical Hit (Natural 20 or exceed Defense by 10+) or reaches **50% SP**:

| d6 Roll | System Failure | Mechanical Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Motive System** | Speed reduced by **50%** (treads thrown, wing clipped). |
| **2** | **Weapon System** | One random weapon disabled or jammed. |
| **3** | **Sensor / Comms** | Blinded or silenced; **Disadvantage on all targeting**. |
| **4** | **Crew Compartment** | Cockpit breached; crew takes **half the damage** dealt to vehicle. |
| **5** | **Power Plant** | Smoke and internal fire; vehicle takes **1d6 Burn damage per turn**. |
| **6** | **Catastrophic Leak** | Fuel/reactor unstable; **explodes in 1d4 rounds** unless repaired. |

---

## 4. Vehicle Destruction & Explosion
- **0 SP (Disabled):** Vehicle stops. Systems offline. Can be repaired.
- **Negative SP > 50% Max (Destroyed):** Total loss, scrap wreck only.
- **Catastrophic Explosion:** Detonates if destroyed by Fire or Explosive damage.
  - *Radius:* **10 feet per Size Category**.
  - *Damage:* **1d6 per 10 SP** of the vehicle's maximum structure.
