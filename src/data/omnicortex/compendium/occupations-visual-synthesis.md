# CRISP Visual Synthesis Guide for Occupations

The **CRISP Framework** maps all **13 Occupations** against the **10 Major Factions** (130 visual synthesis matrices) for **BASTION** and **AIME** visual generation.

## Faction Architectural & Attire Integration
Each matrix grounds the character's occupational tools, armor, cybernetics, and garments within their faction's distinct cultural aesthetic:
- **Impyrium**: Monumental solar grandeur, white marble, gold embroidery, Roman-inspired discipline.
- **Syndicate**: Minimalist corporate transhumanism, sterile lab aesthetics, smart-fabrics, AR visors.
- **Dynasty**: Feudal draconic heraldry, scale-mail mesh, magma forges, stone battlements.
- **Outworlds**: Scavenged rust-tech, leather dusters, patchwork gear, frontier grit.
- **Coalition**: Cyber-noir street grime, rainy neon alleys, industrial heavy hazard gear.
- **Auluran**: Biomorphic grown tools, living wood/chitin symbiotes, spore lighting.
- **Alterian Enclave**: High arcane elegance, floating hard-light mandalas, silk robes, crystal focus.
- **Ascendancy**: Zen futurism, clean lines, white/grey solarpunk eco-tech.
- **Entari Combine**: Cosmopolitan trade opulence, saffron textiles, multi-cultural artisan wares.
- **Mekan**: Mathematical fractal chassis, hard-light interfaces, server grid efficiency.
