# 1.04. ARCHETYPAL FACTION: 22. SPACE RELIGION — LUMINISM (+2 WS)

## Executive Summary & Nov 2 Canonical Game Data
* **Archetypal Faction Classification:** 22. Space Religion (Luminism)
* **Tech / Meta Level:** TL 3 / ML 4
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Score Modifier:** **+2 WS**
* **Canonical 20-Point Faction Skill Package (0 CP):** Knowledge: Metaphysics (+5), Knowledge: Religion (+5), Attune (+4), Diplomacy (+3), Insight (+3)
* **Canonical Recommended Features (-1 CP Discount):** Awakened, Balanced Center, Believer, Centering, Devout, Discipline Focus, Harmonious Center, Iron Will, Karmic Blessing, True Faith
* **CRISP Visual Semiotics Key:** Starlight observatory temples, white-and-gold vestments, harmonic crystal choirs.

SPACE RELIGION
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Factions characterized by deeply held religious beliefs related to space, the cosmos, or celestial phenomena. They seek to explore the spiritual nature of the universe, protect their holy sites (often unusual celestial bodies), and recruit followers to achieve enlightenment.
* Key Themes: Celestial Mysticism, Cosmic Devotion, Star-Cults, Holy Pilgrimages.
* Relationship to Others: Generally peaceful, but will launch holy wars if their celestial sites are threatened by miners or explorers.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Celestial Church / The Path of Stars
* Colloquialisms: The Devout / Star-Gazers
* Archetype: Cosmic Devout
* Capital/Key World: Variable (A highly significant celestial body or ancient temple-world)
* Driving Mandate: Spread their faith, protect holy sites, achieve spiritual enlightenment.
* Motto: "The stars are the eyes of the divine."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Devout, spiritual, mystical, contemplative.
* Social Structure: Clergy hierarchies (Oracles, High Priests, Pilgrims).
* View on Outsiders: Lost souls in need of the cosmos' light.
* Law & Order: Strict adherence to holy texts and celestial alignments.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Strong community bonds, unity, access to unique spiritual powers (psionics/magic), ability to inspire unshakeable devotion.
* Weaknesses: Vulnerability to persecution, tendency to prioritize spiritual goals over practical survival, schisms over doctrine.
* Military Doctrine: Defensive zealotry; deploying highly trained temple guards or warrior-monks.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Deeply spiritual species).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Discipline (+5)
   * Attune (+5)
   * Academics (Cosmology) (+4)
   * Diplomacy (+3)
   * Insight (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Pilgrim: Traveling the galaxy to visit sacred celestial bodies.
   * The Missionary: Spreading the word of the cosmos to unenlightened worlds.
   * The Mystic: Meditating on the void to unlock deep psychic or magical powers.
   * The Oracle: Reading the alignment of the stars to predict the future.
   * The Star-Monk: A highly disciplined martial artist defending the holy temples.
   * The Void-Priest: Leading the congregation in worship of the nebulas.
   * The Celestial Knight: A heavily armed holy warrior protecting the pilgrimage routes.
   * The Harmonizer: Using song and frequency to align technology with the divine.
   * The Astromancer: Utilizing celestial magic drawn directly from the suns.
   * The Zealot: A radical believer willing to die to protect a sacred comet.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Awakened: The fundamental connection to the mystical energies of the cosmos.
   * Centering: Maintaining perfect inner peace to empower spells and psionics.
   * Magical Aptitude: A profound, natural resonance with celestial magic.
   * Iron Will: Unshakeable faith protecting the mind from doubt and manipulation.
   * Zero G Training: Viewing weightlessness as a holy state of being.
   * Spacer: Moving with divine grace through the vacuum of space.
   * Insightful Reason: Understanding the deeper, spiritual meaning behind events.
   * Discipline Focus: Mastering a specific, holy aspect of metaphysical power.
   * Telepathy: Communicating mind-to-mind as a form of pure communion.
   * Harmonious Center: Existing in flawless equilibrium with the universe.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Reverent, mystical, and celestial.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Celestial temples on asteroids, starlight illuminating altars, floating monks, glowing artifacts.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Cosmic Devout, Celestial Mysticism, Reverent.
   * CONTEXT: Celestial temples on asteroids, starlight illuminating altars, floating monks, glowing artifacts.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Serene, holy, mystical, vast, quiet.
