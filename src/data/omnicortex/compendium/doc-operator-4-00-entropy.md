# **Entropy Invocations**

# **Entropy Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Entropy Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Entropy: Chaos or Entropy: Order) for shaping, intensity, and effect resolution.

## 

## 

## **CHAOS INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Entropy

Specialization: Chaos

Description: Amplification of entropic forces and damaging to most everything. Utilizes Negative Energy.

Standard Range: Short.

Base Damage: 2d6 per Stage (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle) \+ 1d per Level of Invocation used.

Effect: Most armors are ignored or harmed by Entropic Effects.

### 

### **CULL**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Minor\] \[Negative Energy\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Causes 1 point of negative energy damage. If the target is near death (0 Vitality or wounded), it hastens their demise.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Increases to 1d4 damage at higher Stages against dying targets.

### 

### **TOUCH OF DEATH**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Necrotic\] \[Lethal\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 25  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Partial/Negates Death)

Effect Description:

Channels pure entropic energy into a living being, attempting to instantly sever their life force.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 2d6 Negative Energy Damage per Stage.  
* **Lethality:** Death Threshold (HP) \= 10 x Stage. If target HP is below threshold, save or die.

### 

### **WOUND**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Necrotic\] \[Bio-Degradation\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Half)

Effect Description:

Accelerates the decay of cells and tissue, inflicting open wounds.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 2d6 Negative Energy Damage per Stage.  
* **Debuff:** Inflicts Bleeding (1 dmg/round) per Stage.

### 

### **FEAR**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Debuff | \[Psychic\] \[Emotion\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Rounds/Level  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Effect Description:

Induces a primal, irrational terror in a creature.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Stage 1 (Shaken) \-\> Stage 3 (Frightened) \-\> Stage 5 (Panicked).  
* **Duration:** 1 Round per Stage.

### 

### **CURSE**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Debuff | \[Fate\] \[Misfortune\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Permanent (until removed)  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Effect Description:

Afflicts a creature with a metaphysical burden that hinders their actions.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Penalty to Rolls \= \-1 per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** Remove Curse CR \= Base \+ (2 per Stage).

### 

### **BLIGHT**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Environmental | \[Decay\] \[Area\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Full Round Action  
* **Range:** Short (Origin)  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius \+  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous / Permanent (to plants)  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Withers plants, rots food, and befouls water within an area.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Area:** Radius \= 20ft per Stage.  
* **Damage:** 2d6 Necrotic Damage per Stage to living matter.

### 

### **CORROSION**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Acid\] \[Destruction\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Target  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous (Secondary effects linger)  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (Half)

Effect Description:

Projects a stream of entropy that physically corrodes matter.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 2d6 Corrosive/Entropic Damage per Stage.  
* **Sunder:** Ignores 5 points of Object Hardness per Stage.

### 

### **NAUSEATE**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Debuff | \[Biological\] \[Sickness\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Rounds/Level  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Disrupts the target's biological equilibrium, inducing violent nausea.

* **Severity:** Stage 1-2 (Sickened: \-2 penalty) \-\> Stage 3+ (Nauseated: Move actions only).

### 

### **NULLIFY**

Parameters:

* **Area:** Single Effect  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Opposed Check

Effect Description:

Negates the effects of a specific environmental, elemental, or metaphysical condition.

**Scaling Function:**

### 

### **SUFFOCATE**

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Removes air from the target's lungs or prevents them from breathing.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 2d6 Non-Lethal per round (Scaling to Lethal).  
* **Duration:** Can maintain for 1 round per Stage without concentration.

### 

### **CHAOTIC BLAST**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Evocation\] \[Raw Chaos\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** 20ft Burst  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (Half)

Effect Description:

An explosion of multicolored, discordent energy.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Damage:** 2d6 Chaos Damage per Stage.  
* **Wild:** On a Crit, adds a random effect (Knockdown, Blind, or \+1d6 Dmg).

## **ORDER INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Entropy

Specialization: Order

Description: Manipulation and repairing patterns of most anything. Utilizes Positive Energy.

Standard Range: Short.

Base Healing: 2d6 healed per Stage (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle) \+ 1d per Level of Invocation used.

Note: If dealing with Undead or Negative Energy creatures, inverse the Damage/Heal effects.

### 

### **VITAL SIGHT**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Sensory | \[Cantrip\] \[Diagnosis\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Sight  
* **Area:** Single Subject  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Detects the presence and general location of 'living' subjects. Senses current physical condition and any type of illness, damage, injury, ailment, or allergy.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Precision:** Detail of diagnosis increases per Stage (Presence \-\> Condition \-\> Exact HP/Disease Strain).

### 

### **STABILIZE**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Healing | \[Minor\] \[Emergency\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Touch heals 1 point of damage and stabilizes a wounded patient immediately.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Range:** At Stage 3+, can be used at Short Range.

### 

### **CURE**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Healing | \[Restoration\] \[Positive Energy\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Will (Harmless) / Fortitude (if Undead)

Effect Description:

Channels positive energy to knit flesh and heal physical damage.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Healing:** 2d6 Health recovered per Stage.  
* **Bonus:** Adds \+1 healing per die at Stage 3+.

### 

### **LIFE CONTROL**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Utility/Healing | \[Vitality\] \[Transfer\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will (Negates)

Effect Description:

Manipulates the flow of life energy. Transfer HP or bolster Vitality.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Magnitude:** Max HP Transferred \= 5 x Stage.  
* **Efficiency:** At Stage 3, transfer is 1:1 (no loss).

### 

### **RESTORATION**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Healing | \[Cleanse\] \[Status Removal\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** 1 Minute  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Cures severe conditions such as Ability Damage, Blindness, Deafness, or Disease.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Complexity:** Removes Conditions of Severity equal to Stage.  
* **Attribute:** Heals 1d4 Ability Damage per Stage.

###

Ends active magical or metaphysical effects by imposing natural laws.

### 

### **REGENERATE**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Healing | \[Over Time\] \[Regrowth\]

* **Base Difficulty:** 25  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Rounds/Level  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Kickstarts the body's natural healing. Restores HP over time and regrows limbs.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Healing:** Fast Healing (HP/Round) \= 2 per Stage.

### 

### **EXORCISM**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Attack/Utility | \[Banishment\] \[Spirit\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Full Round Action (Maintained)  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature (Possessed)  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous (after success)  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Possessor)

Effect Description:

Drives out possessing spirits, demons, or entities.

**Scaling Function:**
