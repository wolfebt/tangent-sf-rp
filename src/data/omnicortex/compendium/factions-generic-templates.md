# The 25 Generic Faction Templates Guide

GMs and players can construct custom factions or minor local factions using the 25 standardized templates:
1. Alien Enclave, 2. Artificial Life Network, 3. Corporate Subsidiary, 4. Criminal Syndicate, 5. Cosmic Justice Order,
6. Cosmic Horror Cult, 7. Environmental Restorationists, 8. Genetic Engineering Directorate, 9. Hive Mind Collective, 10. Interstellar Diplomatic Corps,
11. Mercenary Company, 12. Military Junta, 13. Mutant Outcast Enclave, 14. Planetary Government, 15. Political Movement,
16. Post-Apocalyptic Warlords, 17. Rebel Resistance Cell, 18. Religious Cult, 19. Sentient AI Matrix, 20. Space Exploration Survey,
21. Space Pirate Fleet, 22. Space Faith Pilgrimage, 23. Space Tourism Conglomerate, 24. Supernatural Order, 25. Technological Innovators.
