# The 7 Categories of Hindrances

To help structure character backgrounds and balance narrative complications, Hindrances are categorized into 7 primary domains:

1. **Background Hindrances**: Past entanglements, sworn bounties, legal warrants, and dark secrets (*Adversary, Secret*).
2. **Mental Hindrances**: Cognitive barriers, intellectual blocks, learning disabilities, and traumatic fixations (*Mental Issues, Nightmares, Technologically Impaired*).
3. **Metaphysical Hindrances**: Cosmic curses, magical vulnerability, and luck deficits (*Unlucky, Extreme Morality*).
4. **Physical Hindrances**: Missing limbs, sensory loss, chronic illness, and non-prime age (*Disability, Lost Sense, Impaired Sense, Bodily Age, Mute*).
5. **Psychological Hindrances**: Compulsions, addictions, severe phobias, and rigid codes (*Addiction, Covetous, Hatred, Honorable, Emotional Issues*).
6. **Resource Hindrances**: Extreme poverty, crushing debt, and lack of vital maintenance (*Poor*).
7. **Social Hindrances**: Bad reputations, burdensome wards, societal oppression, and social ineptitude (*Clown, Dependent, Humorless, Minority, Obligation, Social Issues*).
