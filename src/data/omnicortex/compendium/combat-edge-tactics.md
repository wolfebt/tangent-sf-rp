# Tactical Edge & Battlefield Situational Modifiers

**Edge** represents tactical advantages gained through smart positioning, squad coordination, and environmental exploitation:

| Tactical Situation | Defender Modifier | Attacker Modifier | Tactical Impact |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Aiming** | — | **+2 Strike / round** | Up to 1/2 Skill Rank bonus; requires steady line of sight. |
| **Flanking** | — | **+2 Strike** | When 2+ allies engage a target from opposite arcs. |
| **High Ground** | — | **+2 Strike, +2 Crit** | Elevated positioning over target. |
| **Prone (vs Melee)** | -4 Defense | +2 Strike (High Ground) | Grounded target is vulnerable to close-quarters melee. |
| **Prone (vs Ranged)** | **+2 Defense / category** | — | Low profile offers superior ground cover against ranged fire. |
| **Charging** | -2 Defense | **+1d Damage, -1 Strike** | Rush movement (10+ ft); adds +1 Impact per 10ft speed. |
| **Advancing Target** | — | **Free Attack at -5** | Firing at a target running in the open without cover. |
| **Surprise / Ambush** | **No Defense Allowed** | **Damage at Advantage** | Attack made at -5 Strike; target receives zero defense roll. |
