# CRISP Visual Synthesis Guide for Factions

Factions define the architectural shape language, material finishes, and color palettes across the galaxy:
- **Dynasty**: Triangular plates, gunmetal grey, dragon scales, hearthfire orange.
- **Syndicate**: Hexagons, adaptive grey, stark white, holographic blue.
- **Impyrium**: Marble pillars, gold eagles, imperial red banners, searchlight white.
- **Outworlds**: Rust, corrugated metal, dust tan, space western grit.
- **Coalition**: Cyber-noir rain, neon pink/cyan, industrial steel catwalks.
- **Auluran**: Living bio-domes, moss glow green, living wood bridges.
- **Alterian Enclave**: Ivory and crystal spires, gold filigree, light bridges.
- **Ascendancy**: Solarpunk white/sky-blue, curving eco-parks, zen minimalism.
- **Entari Combine**: Saffron and gold bazaar spires, hanging silk awnings.
- **Mekan**: Fractal circuits, server stacks, neon data grids.
