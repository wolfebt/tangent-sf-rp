# Essence Pool Economy, Costs & Recovery

## Essence Pool Formula
```text
Essence Pool = (Sum of all 6 Ability Scores) + (Attune Skill Rank) + (Total Ranks in all Known Discipline Skills)
```

## Environmental Stress & Essence Activation Costs

| Difficulty Context | Base DC | Essence Cost per Invocation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Very Easy** (Sanctum / Lab / Library) | DC 5 | **0 Essence** |
| **Easy** (Casual / Non-Hostile) | DC 10 | **0 Essence** |
| **Average** (Combat / Vigorous Movement) | DC 15 | **1 Essence** |
| **Difficult** (Extreme Activity / Freefall) | DC 20 | **2 Essence** |
| **Very Difficult** (Environmental Catastrophe) | DC 25 | **3 Essence** |
| **Nearly Impossible** (Reality Ruptures) | DC 30 | **4 Essence** |

## Essence Recovery & The Burn
- **Light Rest**: Recovers Essence equal to Key Ability Mod (min 1) per hour.
- **Full Rest (6-8 Hours)**: Restores the entire Essence Pool to maximum.
- **The Burn**: Spending Essence when the pool is at 0 deals **2 direct Health damage** per point burned.
