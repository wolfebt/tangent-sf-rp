# Hit Locations, Called Shots & Limb Trauma

## Hit Location Table (d10 on Critical Hits or Called Shots)

| Roll (d10) | Hit Location | Called Shot Penalty | Immediate Trauma Condition |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | **Head** | -4 to -5 | **Knockout**: Reason Save or Stunned; fail by 10+ = Unconscious. |
| **2 – 4** | **Torso** | -1 to -2 | **Winded**: Fortitude Save or -2 on actions; fail by 10+ = Incapacitated. |
| **5 (R) / 6 (L)** | **Arm** | -2 to -3 | **Disarmed**: Reflex Save or drop held gear; fail by 10+ = Crippled Arm. |
| **7 – 8 (R) / 9 – 10 (L)** | **Leg** | -2 to -3 | **Hobbled**: Might Save or 1/2 Speed; fail by 10+ = Crippled Leg. |

## Limb Damage Thresholds
- **Disabled (1/3 Max HP)**: Limb becomes useless. Stamina check (DC 10 + Damage) to remain functional.
- **Destroyed (2/3 Max HP)**: Limb is mangled beyond repair; requires cybernetic or biological replacement.
- **Synthetic Limbs**: Possess **+50% Damage Threshold** before disabling, but receive no Stamina save when disabled.
