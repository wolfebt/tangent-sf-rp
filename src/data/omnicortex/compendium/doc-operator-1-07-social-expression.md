## **EXPRESSION SKILLS**

**Core Mechanic:** **2d10 \+ Skill Rank \+ Charisma Mod vs. Challenge Rating (CR)**

* **Performance/Busking:** The result determines the quality of the show and potential tips/wealth earned.  
  * *CR 10:* Amateur performance (Standard success).  
  * *CR 20:* Professional/Memorable performance.  
  * *CR 30:* Legendary performance.  
* **Influence:** You can use these skills to alter the attitude of an NPC (Make them Friendly/Helpful).

### 

  ### **Acting**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Acting is the skill of portraying a character or role in a performance. It reflects a character's ability to use body language, facial expressions, and tone of voice to convey emotions and communicate effectively. Acting is used whenever a character needs to deceive or persuade others, or to perform in a play or other theatrical production. The ability to embody a character, fake emotions, or improvise a persona. Unlike Disguise (which is visual), Acting is behavioral. It allows a character to pass as a soldier, a doctor, or a beggar through mannerisms and speech patterns alone.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Impersonation:* Opposed check vs. **Insight**.  
  * *Distraction:* Create a scene to draw attention away from allies (CR 15).  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Voice & Accent Mimicry:** Replicating the specific cadence, regional dialect, and vocal pitch of specific individuals.  
    * **Improvisational Roleplay:** Reacting organically in character when an undercover cover story goes completely off the rails.  
    * **Method Immersion:** Living, thinking, and behaving completely as an assumed identity for months at a time.  
    * **Stage & Holo-Screen Acting:** Delivering powerful, nuanced, and tearful dramatic performances before cameras or live crowds.  
    * **Feigning Injury & Death:** Realistically collapsing, faking seizures, and playing dead to fool passing guards.  
    * **Physical Mannerism Masking:** Changing one's walk, posture, head tilt, and physical gait to match a completely different persona.  
    * **Theatrical Stage Combat:** Executing convincing punches, sword swings, and falls that look lethal without causing harm.  
    * **Dramatic Monologuing:** Holding an entire audience or dining hall spellbound through dramatic pacing and projection.  
    * **Musical Theatre:** Seamlessly integrating song, stage dialogue, and stylized movement into a single performance.  
    * **Comedic Timing:** Delivering humorous acting cues, physical slapstick, and reaction beats on stage with perfection.

  ### 

  ### **Comedy**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Comedy is the skill of making people laugh or feel amused. It reflects a character's ability to use humor and wit to entertain others. Comedy is used whenever a character needs to lighten the mood or entertain others, or to perform in a comedy show or other comedic production. The art of humor, satire, and timing. Used to entertain, defuse tense situations, or provoke an enemy into making a mistake (Taunt).  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Entertain:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Defuse Hostility:* CR 20 to turn a Hostile NPC to Indifferent (if they have a sense of humor).  
  * *Taunt:* Opposed check vs. **Willpower**. Success causes the target to focus on you or suffer a penalty to Logic/Discipline due to anger.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Stand-Up Routines:** Delivering written comedic material, setups, and punchlines to pack cantinas and clubs.  
    * **Combat Taunts & Jeering:** Enraging an enemy soldier with sharp, humiliating barbs so they break cover and attack wildly.  
    * **Political Satire & Parody:** Mocking corrupt corporate moguls, planetary governors, and imperial policies through sharp humor.  
    * **Hostility Defusal Through Humor:** Cracking the perfect joke during an armed standoff to disarm tension and get laughs.  
    * **Roasting & Heckler Put-Downs:** Brutally shredding an abrasive heckler or arrogant rival with instant, razor-sharp wit.  
    * **Slapstick & Physical Humor:** Executing hilarious pratfalls, exaggerated double-takes, and physical gags for laughs.  
    * **Dark & Gallows Humor:** Using grim, morbid humor to keep spirits up and relieve stress in horrific combat zones.  
    * **Deadpan Irony:** Delivering absurd statements with an absolute stone face to confuse and amuse listeners.  
    * **Impressionist Parody:** Exaggerating the vocal tics, pomposity, and flaws of public celebrities for comedic effect.  
    * **Crowd Banter & Crowd-Work:** Interacting spontaneously with cantina patrons to turn unexpected events into comedic gold.

  ### 

  ### **Dancing**

* **Ability:** Charisma (Expression) or Agility (Technique)  
* **Description:** Dancing is the skill of moving rhythmically to music. It reflects a character's ability to use body movements to express emotions and communicate effectively. Dancing is used whenever a character needs to entertain others or perform in a dance show or other performance. Rhythmic bodily movement to music. This skill covers everything from high-society ballroom dancing to club dancing or traditional folk dances. It is a display of grace, stamina, and social awareness.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Partnering:* CR 15 to expertly lead or follow a partner, granting them a \+2 bonus to their own Social checks during the event.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Ballroom & Court Waltz:** Gliding gracefully through formal waltzes and minuet dances at aristocratic high galas.  
    * **Club & Rave Freestyle:** Moving fluidly to pounding electronic music, light-shows, and underground dance beats.  
    * **Zero-G Aerial Dance:** Executing three-dimensional pirouettes, flips, and spins in microgravity chambers.  
    * **Classical Ballet:** Performing disciplined, highly athletic leaps, toe-spins, and technical classical routines.  
    * **Folk & Cultural Dances:** Executing the traditional, historic footwork, line-dances, and circle-dances of worlds.  
    * **Sensual & Seductive Dance:** Performing alluring, captivating movements designed to mesmerize and distract patrons.  
    * **Martial Katas & War Dances:** Executing weapon dances, spear-stepping, and rhythmic intimidations before combat.  
    * **Breakdancing & Urban Acrobatics:** Executing head-spins, ground-freezes, and explosive popping and locking on street corners.  
    * **Choreographed Group Pop:** Synchronizing complex, high-energy group dance routines with background performers.  
    * **Tap & Percussive Footwork:** Creating complex acoustic rhythms and syncopated beats purely through iron-shod shoe taps.

  ### 

  ### **Disguise**

* **Ability:** Charisma (Acting the part) or Intellect (Creating the prop)  
* **Description:** Disguise is the skill of changing one's appearance to look like someone else. It reflects a character's ability to use makeup, costumes, and other techniques to alter their appearance. Disguise is used whenever a character needs to deceive or impersonate someone else, or to hide their true identity. The application of props, makeup, prosthetics, and costumes to change one's physical appearance. While Acting covers behavior, Disguise covers the visual fabrication.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Create Disguise:* The result of your check becomes the **CR** for anyone trying to see through it using **Alertness** or **Investigation**.  
  * *Speed Disguise:* Rushing a disguise (-5 penalty) takes 1d4 rounds. Proper application takes 10-30 minutes.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Prosthetic & Latex Application:** Applying false noses, cheekbones, brow ridges, and synthetic skin appliances.  
    * **Cosmetic Makeup & Contouring:** Using shading, highlights, and foundation to drastically alter perceived face shapes.  
    * **Specific Individual Cloning:** Replicating the exact face, wrinkles, scars, and hair of a known living person.  
    * **Cross-Species Camouflage:** Using silicone masks, false ears, and body padding to pass as a different humanoid alien race.  
    * **Quick-Change Techniques:** Shedding an entire outer costume and wig in three seconds inside a crowd or alley.  
    * **Aging & De-Aging Effects:** Simulating liver spots, graying hair, wrinkles, or youthful skin with paints and glues.  
    * **Body Shape Padding:** Wearing fat suits, muscle pads, or slouch braces to alter height, build, and posture completely.  
    * **Cyberware Concealment:** Using synthetic flesh and dermal plating to hide chrome cybernetic limbs and data jacks.  
    * **Camouflage Makeup:** Painting matte disruptive patterns across faces and gear to blend into jungle or night terrain.  
    * **Holographic Disguise Tuning:** Calibrating holographic light-emitters to project a false digital identity shell over gear.

  ### 

  ### **Keyboard**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Keyboard is the skill of playing a keyboard instrument, such as a piano or synthesizer. It reflects a character's ability to create music using a keyboard instrument. Keyboard is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instrument interfaces involving keys, buttons, or haptic pads. In a Sci-Fi setting, this ranges from classic pianos to complex holographic synthesizers.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Composition:* Write a piece of music (Intellect \+ Keyboard).  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Acoustic Grand Piano:** Performing complex classical sonatas, jazz improvisations, and concertos on grand pianos.  
    * **Pipe & Cathedral Organ:** Operating multi-manual organ keyboards, foot pedals, and stops in massive echoic halls.  
    * **Electronic Synthesizer Workstations:** Programming oscillators, low-pass filters, and playing electronic synth leads.  
    * **Holographic Light-Keys:** Playing futuristic musical consoles composed of projected, floating hard-light keys.  
    * **Keytar & Stage Performance:** Playing portable keyboard instruments while moving aggressively across live stages.  
    * **Harpsichord & Early Instruments:** Performing delicate, plucking keyboard compositions from archaic eras.  
    * **Haptic Touch-Pads:** Playing responsive, velocity-sensitive silicone pads and matrix controller surfaces.  
    * **Accordion & Squeeze-Box:** Operating bellow-driven reed key boxes while managing rhythmic bass buttons.  
    * **Ambient Soundscape Design:** Generating lush, evolving chordal pads and atmospheric sonic backdrops live.  
    * **Microtonal Synthesizers:** Navigating alien tuning scales and non-standard pitch intervals on custom boards.

  ### 

  ### **Legerdemain**

* **Ability:** Agility (Dexterity) or Charisma (Misdirection)  
* **Description:** Legerdemain is the skill of performing sleight of hand tricks and illusions. It reflects a character's ability to use misdirection and other techniques to deceive others. Legerdemain is used whenever a character needs to deceive or entertain others such as switching items, stealing or performing a stage or table magic show. Sleight of hand, stage magic, and manual dexterity. While often used for entertainment, it is the primary skill for pickpocketing, palming small objects, and concealing weapons.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Pickpocket:* CR 20 to take an item unnoticed (or opposed by Alertness).  
  * *Conceal Item:* CR 15 to hide a small weapon/item on your person (opposed by Search).  
  * *Stage Magic:* Perform a trick to entertain or distract.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Pickpocketing:** Slitting purse strings, slipping wallets from coats, and lifting keycards unnoticed.  
    * **Palming & Small Item Concealment:** Hiding daggers, datachips, coins, or vials in the palm or sleeve seamlessly.  
    * **Card & Coin Manipulation:** Executing flawless shuffles, coin-rolls, false cuts, and close-up stage magic tricks.  
    * **Stage Illusions & Escapology:** Slipping out of padlocked chains, straitjackets, and water tanks before an audience.  
    * **Quick-Draw Palming:** Snapping a hidden derringer or holdout blade from a sleeve straight into a firing grip.  
    * **Item Swapping:** Switching an authentic relic or keycard with an exact fake right before an inspector's eyes.  
    * **Shell Game & Three-Card Monte:** Manipulating cups, shells, and cards so fast that onlookers cannot track the prize.  
    * **Wax Impression Key Duplication:** Pressing a guard's physical security key into soft wax in a single casual handshake.  
    * **Distraction Flairs:** Snapping fingers, dropping coins, or generating sparks with one hand while the other steals.  
    * **Planting Evidence:** Slipping hot contraband, tracking bugs, or stolen narcotics into an unsuspecting mark's pocket.

  ### 

  ### **Oratory**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Oratory is the skill of public speaking. It reflects a character's ability to use language and tone of voice to persuade and inspire others. Oratory is used whenever a character needs to convince a group to take a certain course of action, or to deliver a speech or other public address. The art of public speaking. Used to inspire crowds, deliver sermons, debate in court/senate, or give orders on a battlefield.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Inspire:* CR 15 to grant allies a \+1 morale bonus to Willpower saves for 1 hour.  
  * *Persuade Crowd:* Shift the attitude of a group (opposed by the group's collective Will or a set CR).  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Political Rally Speeches:** Delivering fiery, populist orations that sweep elections and energize masses of voters.  
    * **Military Pre-Battle Speeches:** Delivering inspiring addresses that harden resolve and banish fear before bloody assaults.  
    * **Religious Sermons & Preaching:** Delivering sermons that move congregations to tears, repentance, and zealotry.  
    * **Courtroom Advocacy:** Presenting closing arguments to juries and judges with compelling narrative power.  
    * **Eulogies & Funerary Orations:** Honoring the deceased with dignified, deeply moving reflections that comfort mourners.  
    * **Crowd Riot Incitement:** Whipping an already angry protest crowd into an uncontrollable, barrier-smashing mob.  
    * **Philosophical Lectures:** Explaining abstract ethical concepts and ideological frameworks to university halls.  
    * **Diplomatic Keynote Addresses:** Addressing global assemblies with statesmanlike poise, vision, and gravitas.  
    * **Acoustic Vocal Projection:** Throwing your voice without microphones to fill cavernous halls and outdoor stadiums.  
    * **Debate Cross-Examination:** Dismantling an opponent's speech line-by-line with sharp rhetorical traps and counter-arguments.

  ### 

  ### **Percussion**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Percussion is the skill of playing percussion instruments, like drums or cymbals. It reflects a character's ability to create music using percussion instruments. Percussion is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instruments that are struck, shaken, or scraped. This is often the foundation of musical timing and rhythm.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Rhythm:* CR 15 to maintain a beat that helps others coordinate physical tasks (e.g., rowing, marching), granting a \+1 bonus to their endurance-based checks.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Acoustic Drum Kits:** Playing intricate rock, jazz, and metal beats using snare, kick, tom-toms, and cymbals.  
    * **Military War Drums & Snares:** Keeping the marching cadence, signaling battle lines, and drumming troops into action.  
    * **Orchestral Timpani & Gongs:** Striking tuned kettle drums and massive orchestral gongs for dramatic climaxes.  
    * **Hand Percussion (Congas/Bongos):** Slapping, popping, and palm-striking skin hand drums with polyrhythmic speed.  
    * **Electronic Drum Pads:** Playing velocity-sensitive rubber pads triggering synthesized samples and sub-bass kicks.  
    * **Tuned Mallet Percussion:** Playing wooden marimbas, steel xylophones, and vibraphones with precision mallets.  
    * **Tribal Ritual Drumming:** Driving deep, hypnotic, relentless drum circles that induce trance states in participants.  
    * **Bodhrán & Frame Drums:** Beating shallow Celtic-style frame drums with double-ended tippers while modulating pitch.  
    * **Cymbals, Triangles & Chimes:** Accenting delicate sonic passages with crashes, rides, sizzling chimes, and iron bells.  
    * **Rhythmic Rowing/Marching Cadence:** Maintaining a continuous, driving beat that coordinates team endurance tasks.

  ### 

  ### **Singing**

* **Ability:** Charisma (Performance) or Constitution (Breath Control)  
* **Description:** Singing is the skill of using one's voice to create music. It reflects a character's ability to use pitch, tone, and rhythm to convey emotions and communicate effectively. Singing is used whenever a character needs to entertain others or perform in a musical production. The use of the voice as an instrument. This creates a powerful connection with audiences without the need for equipment.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
  * *Project Voice:* CR 15 to be heard clearly over loud noise/combat.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Classical Opera:** Projecting high, unamplified vibrato vocals across full orchestras with crystalline resonance.  
    * **Rock & Metal Belting:** Singing powerful, gritty, overdriven lead vocals with intentional rasp and chest-drive.  
    * **Choral & Sacred Hymns:** Blending harmonious soprano, alto, tenor, or bass lines into cathedral choirs.  
    * **Lounge Jazz & Torch Songs:** Singing breathy, smoky, intimate vocal melodies into close microphones in clubs.  
    * **Folk Ballads & Sea Shanties:** Singing narrative tales and rhythmic work songs that crowds can easily chant along with.  
    * **Overtone & Throat Singing:** Producing two distinct pitch frequencies simultaneously through vocal-tract resonance.  
    * **Sub-Harmonic Vocalizations:** Dropping into deep bass registers that rattle ribcages and project primal authority.  
    * **Vocal Whistling & Warbling:** Executing intricate melodic trills and whistle registers mimicking birds or wind.  
    * **Battle Hymns & Cadences:** Projecting rhythmic vocal cadences over gunfire to keep allied morale unshakeable.  
    * **Microtonal Alien Chants:** Navigating vocal melodies that slide between traditional half-step and quarter-step scales.

  ### 

  ### **String**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** String is the skill of playing string instruments like guitar or violin. It reflects a character's ability to create music using strings. String is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production.Proficiency with instruments that produce sound via vibrating strings, whether plucked, strummed, or bowed.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Electric Guitar:** Shredding high-gain lead solos, tapping fretboards, and chugging heavy distorted rhythm riffs.  
    * **Acoustic Guitar:** Fingerpicking, flatpicking, and strumming steel-string and nylon acoustic guitars.  
    * **Violin & Fiddle:** Bowing high-speed country fiddle breakdowns and expressive classical violin concertos.  
    * **Cello & Double Bass:** Playing rich, deep, resonant bowed basslines that provide orchestral weight.  
    * **Concert Harp:** Plucking cascading arpeggios and glissandos across large, multi-pedal classical harps.  
    * **Lute & Mandolin:** Plucking rapid, delicate tremolos and Renaissance melodies on doubled-string instruments.  
    * **Electric Bass Guitar:** Locking into the drummer's groove with slap-and-pop basslines and heavy walking rhythms.  
    * **Exotic Alien Zithers:** Plucking flat, resonant soundboards equipped with alien sympathetic drone strings.  
    * **Banjo Playing:** Executing high-speed three-finger Scruggs-style rolls and clawhammer rhythm patterns.  
    * **Bowed Rebec & Archaic Strings:** Playing ancient, gut-stringed instruments with historical bows and rustic tunings.

  ### 

  ### **Style**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Style is the skill of creating a unique and distinctive appearance. It reflects a character's ability to use clothing, accessories, and other techniques to create a unique and memorable look. Style is used whenever a character needs to make a good impression or to stand out from the crowd. The knowledge of fashion, grooming, and personal aesthetics. It is the ability to make an impression before speaking a word. It involves dressing appropriately for high society or looking intimidating in street armor.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *First Impression:* CR 15 to grant a \+2 bonus to the first **Diplomacy** or **Intimidate** check made in an encounter.  
  * *Blend In:* Dress to match a specific social caste or group (opposed by Culture/Streetwise).  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **High-Society Elegance:** Selecting tailored suits, noble sashes, and jewelry that radiate immense wealth and status.  
    * **Streetwear & Cyberpunk Chic:** Styling leather jackets, neon hair, combat boots, and decals for maximum street cred.  
    * **Corporate Executive Polish:** Dressing in crisp, minimalist, intimidating boardroom suits that project pure competence.  
    * **Military & Uniform Crispness:** Ironing razor-sharp uniform creases, polishing boots to mirrors, and aligning ribbons.  
    * **Intimidating Mercenary Look:** Layering battered combat rigs, scars, and holster wear that scream hardened killer.  
    * **Subtle Blend-In (Gray Man):** Selecting ultra-nondescript, beige, forgettable clothing that leaves zero mental impression.  
    * **Avant-Garde Alien Couture:** Pulling off radical alien silhouettes, kinetic fabrics, and exotic off-world color clashes.  
    * **Gala Glamour:** Wearing head-turning, showstopping formal eveningwear that dominates room attention instantly.  
    * **Retro & Nostalgic Vintage:** Rocking period-accurate antique threads, vintage flight jackets, and classic hats.  
    * **Personal Grooming & Scent:** Mastering hair styling, facial hair trimming, and signature scents that leave lasting impressions.

  ### 

  ### **Wind**

* **Ability:** Charisma  
* **Description:** Wind is the skill of playing wind instruments, like a flute or trumpet. It reflects a character's ability to create music using wind instruments. Wind is used whenever a character needs to create music or perform in a musical production. Proficiency with instruments that require breath to play, including woodwinds and brass.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * *Performance:* Standard performance mechanics.  
* **Specialties:**   
  * **Brass Trumpet & Cornet:** Blasting clear, ringing fanfares, jazzy muted solos, and piercing high-register brass lines.  
    * **Saxophone (Alto/Tenor/Baritone):** Playing soulful, expressive jazz melodies, blues runs, and smooth vibrato riffs.  
    * **Flute & Piccolo:** Flying through rapid, airy classical scales, birdlike trills, and breathy folk melodies.  
    * **Clarinet & Reed Instruments:** Navigating woody, warm, wide-ranging melodies across complex key mechanisms.  
    * **Bagpipes & War Pipes:** Driving continuous double-drone pipes and chanter melodies that carry for miles across fields.  
    * **Harmonica & Blues Harp:** Bending reeds, cross-harping, and chugging train rhythms using cupped hands.  
    * **Trombone & Low Brass:** Moving slide mechanics to execute smooth glissandos, punchy bass lines, and orchestral power.  
    * **Hunting Horns & Bugles:** Sounding natural, valveless brass calls that carry across forests and battlefields.  
    * **Alien Double-Pipes:** Playing exotic instruments requiring circular breathing to produce unceasing melodic loops.  
    * **Didgeridoo & Drone Wind:** Producing deep, guttural, cyclical drone vibrations infused with vocalized animal growls.
