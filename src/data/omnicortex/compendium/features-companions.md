# Companions, Familiars & Neural Bonds

Characters can acquire loyal companion NPCs, animal mounts, combat familiars, and psychic bonded partners.

## Companion NPC Budget Rules
- **Companion Feature (Ranked)**: Grants a dedicated, loyal NPC built with **40 Build Points (BP)**. Each additional purchase can add +40 BP to an existing companion or grant an additional companion.
- **Familiar Feature**: Prerequisite: *Companion*, *Bonded*. Adds **+20 BP** to companion budget and allows exact sensing of companion's distance and direction.
- **Mind Link Feature**: Prerequisite: *Familiar* or *Bonded*. Adds **+10 BP** to companion and establishes full bidirectional telepathic communication.
- **Shared Senses Feature**: Prerequisite: *Mind Link*. Adds **+10 BP** to companion and allows perceiving through the companion's sensory organs across planetary distances.
