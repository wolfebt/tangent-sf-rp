# **SHA’NOR (LINEAGE OVERVIEW)**

Known across the galaxy as Harbingers of Entropy, Dark Hungers, and Smoke Demons, the Sha’Nor are an ancient extradimensional spectral aberration composed simultaneously of shifting organic matter and dark matter. Operating advanced spiritual Void Magi-Tech (TL 5 / ML 5), the lineage encompasses the baseline vampiric Sha’Nor, the demi-dimensional order-attuned Tenen offshoot, and the Sha’Nor-infused corrupted creatures known as the Eakren.
