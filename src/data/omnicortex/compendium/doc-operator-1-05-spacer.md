# **SPACER**

Some people do not have a home planet, instead choosing to travel the stars. They spend most of their time on ships and stations in space, and some have never set foot on a planet.

### *Combat Training*

Skill in combat, whether it's hand-to-hand combat or using weapons in zero gravity.

*\+2 to a Combat Skill of choice*

*\+1 Karma Point*

*\+2 Leadership Checks*

### *Pilot Skills*

*\+2 Piloting*

&nbsp;

Ability to adapt to unexpected situations and make the most of limited resources.

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis**

## **10\. SPACER**

*Void-born, zero-g, stations, ships, habitats.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** A starship interior that looks like a gothic cathedral. Stained glass force-fields. Stone floors in space. Vaulted ceilings.  
* **STYLE:** Cathedral Ship, Warhammer 40k Navy.  
* **PALETTE:** Stone Grey, Stained Glass Colors, Candlelight, Void Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Arches, stone, stained glass, vastness, incense.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Sleek space station with view of the planet. Carpeted floors, boardroom tables, gravity generated by spin.  
* **STYLE:** Orbital Office, Elysium.  
* **PALETTE:** Carpet Grey, Earth Blue (window), Table Wood, White.  
* **TOKENS:** Windows, desks, clean, corporate, view.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** A fortress floating in an asteroid belt. Thick armor plating. Dragon-skiffs docking. Torches burning oxygen (magically maintained).  
* **STYLE:** Void-Castle, Space Fortress.  
* **PALETTE:** Armor Grey, Torch Orange, Asteroid Brown, Space Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Armor, docking bays, torches, stone, heavy.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** Cramped, dirty spaceship interior. Stickers on the console. Fuzzy dice. Hull groaning under stress. Patch jobs.  
* **STYLE:** Belter Skiff, Millennium Falcon.  
* **PALETTE:** Worn Metal, Warning Light Red, Grease, Personal items.  
* **TOKENS:** Cockpit, clutter, stickers, coffee cup, wear.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** A shady space station docking ring. Steam venting, crates being loaded, shadows to hide in. Flickering gravity plates.  
* **STYLE:** Smuggler's Dock, Omega Station.  
* **PALETTE:** Steam White, Shadow Black, Warning Orange, Industrial Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Crates, steam, shadows, docking clamps, grime.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** The ship is alive. Rib-cage corridors. Veins pumping coolant. The walls are warm to the touch. Soft, organic lighting.  
* **STYLE:** Bioship, Living Vessel.  
* **PALETTE:** Flesh Pink, Bone White, Vein Blue, Amber Glow.  
* **TOKENS:** Ribs, organic texture, warmth, pulsing, soft light.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Open decks protected by force fields. Golden sails catching starlight. Elegant wood and brass controls.  
* **STYLE:** Solar Sailer, Treasure Planet.  
* **PALETTE:** Solar Gold, Wood Brown, Starlight White, Void Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Sails, deck, wood, stars, wind (solar).

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Massive, rotating habitat drum. Parklands curving up the walls. A self-contained world traveling through the void.  
* **STYLE:** Generation Ship, O'Neill Cylinder.  
* **PALETTE:** Sky Blue (Artificial), Grass Green, Cloud White.  
* **TOKENS:** Curve, parks, clouds inside, artificial sun.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** Massive ring-station. Ships queuing to dock. Customs drones. Billboards advertising goods. Busy traffic control.  
* **STYLE:** Trade Gate, Spaceport.  
* **PALETTE:** Traffic Light Red/Green, Ad Neon, Metal, Space Black.  
* **TOKENS:** Ships, ads, traffic, docking, busy.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Living on the surface of a Dyson sphere. Infinite horizon of solar panels. No up or down, just the grid.  
* **STYLE:** Dyson Node, Solar Grid.  
* **PALETTE:** Solar Panel Black/Blue, Sun White, Metal.  
* **TOKENS:** Solar panels, infinite horizon, sun, grid.
