# **Energy Invocations**

# **Energy Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Energy Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Energy: Elemental or Energy: Force) for shaping, intensity, and effect resolution.

## 

## 

## **ELEMENTAL INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Energy

Specialization: Elemental

Description: The conjuration and manipulation of various forms of energy.

Base Damage: 1d6 per Stage.

**Elemental Critical Effects:**

* **Pyro (Fire):** Burns on Crit. Target takes ½ Damage for the next 2 rounds.  
* **Voltic (Electric):** Shocks on Crit. Target is **Stunned**.  
* **Cryo (Ice):** Freezes on Crit. Target is **Slowed**.  
* **Sonic (Sound):** Booms on Crit. Target is **Stunned**.  
* **Corrosive (Acid):** Dissolution on Crit. Target takes ½ Damage for the next 2 rounds.

### 

### **ANALYZE ENERGY**

Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Sensory | \[Cantrip\] \[Analysis\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Sight  
* **Area:** Single Source/Effect  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Analyzes an energy source or active effect to determine the amount of energy available (potential) or currently being used (kinetic/active).

**Scaling Function:**

* **Precision:** Detail of analysis increases per Stage (Type \-\> Exact Magnitude \-\> Origin).

### **SPARK**

Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Utility/Attack | \[Minor\] \[Cantrip\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Touch/Close  
* **Area:** Single Target  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Causes 1 point of elemental damage of the chosen type. This triggers the associated minor elemental effect (e.g., lighting a candle, a static shock, freezing a cup of water).

## **FORCE INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Energy

Specialization: Force

Description: The ranged manipulation of kinetic energy. Damage is purely kinetic.

Base Damage: 1d8 per Stage.

### 

### **ANALYZE MOMENTUM**

Classification: Energy (Force) | Sensory | \[Cantrip\] \[Analysis\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Sight  
* **Area:** Single Target  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Instantly determines a target's precise Mass and Velocity.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Precision:** Can analyze multiple targets or predict trajectory at higher Stages.

### 

### **NUDGE**

Classification: Energy (Force) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Cantrip\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Free or Move Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Applies up to 10 pounds of force. Cannot cause direct damage. Used to close doors, push buttons, or tip small objects.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Range:** Range increases slightly per Stage.

###

A distinct "shove" of telekinetic force. Precise and invisible.
