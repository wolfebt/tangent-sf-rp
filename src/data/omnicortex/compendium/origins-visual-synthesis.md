# CRISP Visual Synthesis Guide for Origins

The **CRISP Framework** (Context, Resolution, Intent, Style, Palette) defines high-fidelity visual generation prompts for the **OMNICORTEX** database and AI synthesis engines (**BASTION** and **AIME**).

## Structure of CRISP Synthesis Entries
Every Origin × Faction combination (11 Origins × 10 Major Factions = 110 combinations) contains dedicated visual tokens:
- **Context**: The environmental setting, architecture, and lighting conditions.
- **Style**: Aesthetic influences, architectural movements, and cultural motifs.
- **Palette**: Exact primary, secondary, and accent colors for rendering.
- **Tokens**: Critical physical assets, weather conditions, props, and environmental story cues.

## Rendering Engine Standards
All visual outputs conform to AAA cinematic game engine standards (Unreal Engine 5 PBR shaders, ray-traced reflections, volumetric atmospheric lighting, and deep chiaroscuro).
