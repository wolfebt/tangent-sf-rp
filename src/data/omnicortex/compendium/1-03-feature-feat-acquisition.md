# 1.03 Feature & Feat Acquisition Rules

Features (also termed Feats or Perks) represent distinct capabilities, combat maneuvers, specialized augmentations, and metaphysical awakenings.

---

## Cost Structure & Discounts

- **Standard Base Cost:** **3 Build Points (BP)** per Feature.
- **Recommended Feature Discount:** If a Feature is listed in your **Occupation** or **Faction** recommended list, its cost is reduced by **1 BP** (Final Cost: **2 BP**).
- **Minimum Cost:** A Feature can never cost less than **1 BP**, regardless of stacking discounts.

---

## Feature Classifications

1. **Ability Features:** Enhance core physical or mental prowess (e.g. *Great Fortitude, Lightning Reflexes, Potent Might, Iron Will*).
2. **Combat Features:** Unlock tactical weapon masteries, critical thresholds, and advanced maneuvers (e.g. *Point Blank Mastery, Dual Wielding, Cleave*).
3. **Meta Features:** Awaken metaphysical disciplines or unlock advanced Invocations (e.g. *Awakened Discipline, Meta Resonance, Overchannel*).
4. **General Features:** Broad utility perks covering wealth, connections, cybernetics, and familiars.
5. **Ranked Features:** Features that can be purchased multiple times. Each purchase stacks benefits, capped by the character's linked Attribute or Skill Tier.
