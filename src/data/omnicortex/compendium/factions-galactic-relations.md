# Galactic Geopolitics & Faction Relations Matrix

The balance of galactic power rests on fragile treaties, commercial dependencies, and bitter historical rivalries:
- **Impyrium vs. Syndicate**: Bitter cold war between stagnant bureaucratic imperialism and agile corporate transhumanism.
- **Dynasty vs. Coalition**: Feudal traditionalists view the Coalition as lawless raiders; Coalition views the Dynasty as arrogant tyrants.
- **Alterian Enclave & Ascendancy**: Cautious intellectual peers sharing knowledge across metaphysical and technological disciplines.
- **Mekan Collective**: Coldly neutral calculating supercomputers trading data for raw planetary resources.
