# Damage Types & Elemental Interactions

Tangent SF RP categorizes damage into 3 major classifications:

## 1. Kinetic Damage
- **Blunt**: Crushing trauma against skeletal structures and unarmored targets; causes stun/knockdown.
- **Slashing**: Slicing flesh and light armor; inflicts persistent **Bleed** damage.
- **Piercing**: Deep puncture wounds; high penetration against body armor.
- **Concussive / Impact**: Giant explosions, falls, and vehicle crashes; **ignores 1/2 of Armor DR**.

## 2. Energy Damage
- **Pyro / Fire**: Intense thermal burns; ignites flammable armor and flesh.
- **Cryo / Cold**: Flash-freezing; reduces movement speed and embrittles metal armor.
- **Spectral / Phase**: Incorporeal energy; **completely bypasses physical armor DR**.
- **Sonic / Sound**: Acoustic resonance; causes disorientation and internal tissue rupture.
- **Voltic / Electrical**: Overloads cybernetics, neural systems, and robotic circuits.
- **Corrosive / Acid**: Ongoing chemical erosion melting through armor plating.
- **Radiation (Ra-D)**: Cellular degradation causing sickness, mutation, and organ failure.
- **Disruption / Force Pulse**: Molecular bond breakdown; destroys synthetic and biological matter.

## 3. Variative & Elemental
- **Radiant (Light)**, **Umbral (Shadow)**, **Air**, **Earth**, and **Water** elemental forces.
