# 1.05 Origins & Habitats Codex

A character's Origin defines their birthworld, native planetary biome, or orbital habitat. Origin environments instill instinctive survival mechanisms and physical conditioning.

---

## Canonical Origin Environments

| Origin Name | Environment Profile | Key Society Skills | Example Origin Traits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agricultural (Agri-World)** | Low development, fertile plains, farm arcologies | Piloting, Alertness, Nature, Survival | *Green Thumb, Animal Husbandry, Farm Mech* |
| **Arcology (Mega-City)** | Dense urban towers, neon corridors, artificial atmosphere | Computers, Stealth, Technology, Streetwise | *Urban Stalker, Grid Runner, Dense Crowd Evasion* |
| **Asteroid Belt (Void Habitat)** | Zero-g mines, pressurized domes, cold vacuum | Piloting, Physics, Technology, Alertness | *Zero-G Acclimation, Vacuum Intuition, Seal Repair* |
| **Death World (Hostile Ecology)** | Extreme predators, toxic flora, volatile tectonic cycles | Survival, Athletics, Alertness, Medicine | *Apex Predator Reflexes, Toxin Tolerance, Iron Gut* |
| **High-Gravity World** | Crushing gravity, dense planetary core | Athletics, Might, Fortitude, Intimidation | *Dense Muscle Mass, Impact Resistance, Heavy Stride* |
| **Low-Gravity / Void Station**| Microgravity, centrifugal rings, orbital platforms | Acrobatics, Piloting, Physics, Computers | *Light-Footed, Kinetic Drift, Micro-Thruster Mastery* |

---

## Origin Allotment Rules

- **Origin Skill Pool:** **20 Skill Points** distributed among the origin's listed skills.
- **Origin Traits:** Choose **Two Traits** from your native origin list at character creation.
- **Secondary Origin:** An Operator may select a secondary origin to broaden trait options without receiving additional points.
