# Damage Calculation, Armor DR & Penetration

## Master Damage Formula
```text
Total Damage Inflicted = (Weapon Base Dice + Ability Mod + Precision/Skill Bonuses) - (Target Armor DR + Target CON Mod)
```

## Damage Mitigation Components
1. **Weapon Base Dice**: Inherent kinetic, thermal, or electromagnetic energy delivered by the weapon.
2. **Ability Modifier**: Strength for heavy melee/thrown weapons; Agility for precision weapons; Intellect/Charisma for Metaphysics.
3. **Precision Damage**: Flat bonuses from high skill stage (+2 to +7) and Sneak Attack dice.
4. **Armor Damage Resist (DR)**: Physical plating or energy shields absorbing incoming force.
5. **Target Constitution (CON)**: Biological density, bone structure, and pain threshold reducing damage.
