## **PART II: COMPLETE UNABRIDGED PROFILES & RULES ENTRIES (COMPLETE UNABRIDGED FEATURE PROFILES & MECHANICS)**

## **Meticulous**

* **Description:** The character is exceptionally thorough. When they take their time, they rarely fail.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** The bonus gained from taking extra time on a skill check is improved.
  - **Mechanics:** For every +50% time added to a task, you gain a **+3 Bonus** (normally +2). This bonus scales with stages (+3, +6, +9 etc.).

  

## **Naturalist**

* **Description:** The character is at home in the wild.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Nature** (Knowledge) and **Survival** checks.

  

## **Nimble Fingers**

* **Description:** The character has excellent manual agility for fine manipulation.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Legerdemain** and **Knowledge (Technology)** (Security/Mechanics) checks.

  

## **Persuasive**

* **Description:** The character is convincing and compelling.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Diplomacy** and **Intimidate** checks.

  

## **Perceptive**

* **Description:** The character notices things others miss.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Perception** checks (Alertness) and a **+1 Bonus** to **Insight** checks.

  

## **Researcher**

* **Description:** The character knows how to navigate data archives and libraries.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Academics** and **Academics (Archivist / Research)** checks.

  

## **Scholar**

* **Description:** The character is an academic with a broad base of learning.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **ALL Knowledge Skills**.

  

## **Self Sufficient**

* **Description:** The character can take care of themselves in the wild without support.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Survival** checks and a **+1 Bonus** to **Fortitude** checks.

  

## **Silver Tongue**

* **Description:** The character knows exactly what to say to defuse a situation or make a friend.

  - **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1, Diplomacy 1
  - **Benefit:** When using Diplomacy to change an NPC's attitude, you improve it by **one additional step** (e.g., Hostile becomes Indifferent instead of Unfriendly).

  

## **Golden Smile**

* **Description:** The character's charm is disarming and risky, but often rewarding.

  - **Prerequisite:** Silver Tongue, Charisma 2, Bluff 6, Diplomacy 6
  - **Benefit:** You may choose to **Take 10** on Bluff and Diplomacy checks, OR you may roll **1d10** and add it to the 2d10 result (instead of a static modifier), allowing for potentially higher highs but lower lows.

  

## **Platinum Personality**

* **Description:** The character radiates trustworthiness and charisma.

  - **Prerequisite:** Golden Smile, Charisma 4, Bluff 11, Diplomacy 11
  - **Benefit:** You may make all **Bluff** and **Diplomacy** checks with **Advantage**.

  

## **Skill Focus**

* **Description:** The character has dedicated significant effort to mastering a specific skill.

  - **Prerequisite:** Chosen Skill Rank 1
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to the chosen skill.
  - **Special:** This feature is **[Multiple]** and **[Ranked]**. You may purchase it again for the *same* skill each time you reach a new Skill Stage (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master) to stack the bonuses. (e.g., A Master (Rank 16) with 4 ranks of Skill Focus has a total +8 bonus from this feature).

  

## **Skill Mastery**

* **Description:** The character is so confident in their skills that they cannot fail under pressure.

  - **Prerequisite:** Skill Focus (any two), Focused Skills at Rank 11+ (Expert)
  - **Benefit:** You may **Take 10** on checks with your Focused Skills even when rushed, threatened, or in combat.

  

## **Skill Specialization**

* **Description:** The character drills deep into a specific niche of a broad skill.

  - **Prerequisite:** Chosen Skill Rank 6 (Trained)
  - **Benefit:** Choose a sub-category of the skill (e.g., *Ballistic: Sniper Rifles* or *Computers: Hacking*). You gain a separate pool of ranks in this specialization equal to your base skill rank (up to +10).
  - **Mechanics:** When making a check in that specialization, you add your **Base Rank + Specialization Rank**. This allows you to exceed the normal Skill Rank cap of 20 (reaching Pinnacle+).
  - **Special:** [Multiple] Can be taken for different skills or different sub-categories.

  

## **Social**

* **Description:** The character is a social butterfly.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+1 Bonus** to **ALL Charisma Skills** and **Etiquette** checks.

  

## **Spacer**

* **Description:** The character was born or raised in the void; zero-g and vacuum are second nature.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Starship Operation** (Vocation/Pilot) and any **Space Survival** checks.

  

## **Steady**

* **Description:** The character has excellent balance and steady hands.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Acrobatics** and **Athletics** (Balancing/Climbing) and a **+1 Bonus** to **Reflex** checks.

  

## **Stealthy**

* **Description:** The character is a shadow.

  - **Prerequisite:** Agility 2
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Acrobatics (Escapology)** and **Legerdemain**; Gain a **+4 Bonus** to **Stealth** checks.

  

## **Stout**

* **Description:** The character is built like a tank, broad and sturdy.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+1 Bonus** to **ALL Stamina & Stamina Skills**, as well as **Might** and **Fortitude** checks.

  

## **Trustworthy**

* **Description:** The character has an honest face and a reliable demeanor.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Manipulation Skills** (Bluff/Diplomacy) and a **+1 Bonus** to **Culture** checks (fitting in).

  

## **Veiled Threat**

* **Description:** The character can menace someone so subtly that bystanders don't notice, and the target is too scared to retaliate.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intimidate 6
  - **Benefit:** When you use Intimidate to force a target to act, the target **does not become Hostile** toward you afterwards (they remain Indifferent or Unfriendly, but terrified). Bystanders must make an Insight check vs. your Intimidate to even realize a threat was made.

  

## **Versatile Curator**

* **Description:** The character is a font of trivia and data.

  - **Prerequisite:** Knowledge 6, Intellect 2
  - **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Knowledge category. For every point of Intellect Bonus you possess, you gain 1 Rank in a Knowledge skill you do not technically have, allowing you to make Trained checks.

  

## **Versatile Performer**

* **Description:** The character is a multi-talented artist.

  - **Prerequisite:** Perform 6, Intellect 2
  - **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Performance category. For every point of Intellect Bonus, gain access to another Performance type (Singing, Dancing, Oratory) at your base rank.

  

## **Versatile Professional**

* **Description:** The character knows every job on the crew.

  - **Prerequisite:** Vocation 6, Intellect 2
  - **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Vocation category. For every point of Intellect Bonus, gain access to a related Vocation sub-skill without spending CP.

- *Example:* A Soldier with Int +2 might gain Virtual Ranks in *Armorer* and *Demolitions*.
