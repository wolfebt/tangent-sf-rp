# Occupations System Overview & Skill Point Economy

Occupations represent a character's formal training, professional discipline, career history, and vocational standing in galactic society.

## Professional Skill Point (SP) Pool
- **20 SP Pool**: Choosing an Occupation grants **20 Skill Points (SP)** to invest across the **Professional Skills** listed for that Occupation.
- **Creation Cap**: No starting skill may be raised above **Rank 11 (Expert)** during character creation.
- **Recommended Limit**: It is strongly recommended that starting skills do not exceed **Rank 6 (Trained / Professional)** to foster balanced characters. Raising a skill to Rank 11 requires that specialized mastery is a core element of the character's backstory.
- **Conversion Rate**: 1 SP = 1 Skill Rank.

## Recommended Features Discount
- Each Occupation specifies a category of **Recommended Features** (e.g., Combat for Soldier, Discipline for Adept, General for Agent/Scout, Skill for Builder/Specialist).
- Purchasing a Recommended Feature during creation or progression grants a **1 BP Discount** (standard 3 BP features cost **2 BP**).

## Starting Traits & Additional Purchases
- **Free Traits**: Players select **TWO (2) Starting Traits** from their chosen Occupation's trait list.
- **Additional Traits (1 CP Rule)**: Characters may purchase additional traits from their Occupation's list at a cost of **1 Character Point (CP)** per additional trait.
