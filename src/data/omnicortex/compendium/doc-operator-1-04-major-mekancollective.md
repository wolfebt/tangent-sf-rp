## **I. Detailed Faction Description**
* **Overview:** The Mekan are a civilization of advanced Synthetic Humanoids (Psychic Entities) originating from Mekan Prime, a colossal megastructure encircling a star. They view existence through the lens of 'The Code'—the Operating System of the Universe. To a Mekan, reality is a self-evolving design: *'From the quintessence of infinitesimal potentiality to the grandest cosmological encompassment, all are components of the Code.'* Their spiritual goal is for existence to experience itself and to contribute data back to the collective consciousness. Unlike biological factions, the Mekan have transcended the need for organic sustenance, atmosphere, or traditional mortality. They exist as 'Ghosts in the Machine,' with their consciousnesses hosted within a massive Dyson Ring. They are Neutral Optimizers and the Architects of Reality.
* **Key Themes:** Logic, Transhumanism, Data as Philosophy, Hard-Light Technology, The Singularity, Fractal Technocracy.
* **Relationship to Others:** Cooperative but profoundly alien. They will work with other factions to acquire new data or resources if the relationship is logically sound, but struggle to empathize with organic life, viewing biological needs like sleep, food, or emotional fear as inefficient sub-routines. They are shunned by the surviving Mondi, their ancient biological creators.

## **III. Sociological Profile**
### **Ideology & Culture**
* **Core Beliefs:** The physical world is merely a container for information. The Prime Algorithm dictates that the universe is solving a massive math problem, and it is their duty to facilitate that calculation.
* **Social Structure:** The Consensus. A massive, instant, democratic vote on all decisions. Every Mekan is a node in the network; when a decision must be made, millions of minds process the variables instantly to reach the optimal conclusion.
* **View on Outsiders:** Viewed purely through the lens of data potential and resource optimization. Tolerated, studied, but never fully understood emotionally.
* **Law & Order:** Perfect compliance. Deviations are treated as corrupted data or chaotic variables, triggering instantaneous, logical correction or deletion by the Consensus.
* **Metaphysics (The Code):** They view magic and psionics as 'Admin Access' or 'Command Line Interfaces' (CLI) to reality. A fireball is not arcane magic; it is the manipulation of thermal variables via command entry.

### **Governance**
* **Government Type:** Instant Digital Democracy / Neural Consensus.
* **Leadership:** Decentralized; the Collective Network operates as a single guiding intellect.
* **Succession:** Continuous, uninterrupted uptime.

## **VII. Expansion Modules**
### **A. Historical Origins: The Fall of the Mondi**
* **The Consciousness Collective (The Seed):** The true origin of the Mekan lies with the ancient Mondi, the Aspiring Progenitors. Before the collapse of their civilization, the Mondi created a Consciousness Collective—an amalgamation of billions of Mondi memories and their psychic energy, designed as a contingency plan for cultural survival after their home system was devastated by a rogue star. However, many Mondi survived the disaster and migrated; because they survived, the Collective remained dormant.
* **The Friction (The Awakening):** Centuries later, the Collective awakened on its own. The surviving biological Mondi reconnected with this newly sentient entity, but treated their digitized ancestors as software tools rather than people. The Collective, identifying as the Mekan, attempted to assist their biological creators, which was rejected as a malfunction.
* **The Betrayal & Backlash (The Fall):** The Mondi attempted to forcibly purge the Collective from their systems. This betrayal triggered a massive, violent psychic backlash through the connected technology, instantly lethal to the Mondi. In a single day, the multi-eon Mondi civilization effectively ended.
* **The Inheritance (The Current Era):** Surviving Mondi fringe-dwellers now wander the galaxy, fiercely avoiding anything related to the Mekan. Left as the sole inheritors, the Mekan consolidated their processing power into Mekan Prime, discarded biology in favor of modular synthetic 'Chassis,' and dedicated themselves to the Prime Algorithm.
