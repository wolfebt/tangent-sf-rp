# 1.07 Master Skills Codex & Benchmarks

Skills are organized into **5 Major Skill Groups**. Every rank adds a +1 bonus to task resolution checks.

---

## Master Skill Directory

### I. Physical Skills (STR / AGI)
- **Acrobatics (AGI):** Tumbling, balance, aerial maneuvers, fall mitigation.
- **Athletics (STR/STA):** Sprinting, swimming, climbing, heavy lifting, physical endurance.
- **Piloting (AGI/INT):** Starships, ground speeders, mecha, atmospheric craft.
- **Stealth (AGI):** Hiding, silent movement, camouflage, shadow traversal.

### II. Mental Skills (INT / WIS)
- **Alertness (WIS):** Perception, hazard awareness, detecting hidden foes.
- **Academics (INT):** Research, linguistics, humanities, ancient history.
- **Computers (INT):** Cyber warfare, system intrusion, AI interaction, programming.
- **Investigation (INT):** Forensics, crime scene analysis, deductive logic.
- **Medicine (INT):** Combat triage, surgical intervention, pathology, cyber-surgery.
- **Science (INT):** Chemistry, biology, xenology, planetary physics.
- **Technology (INT):** Engineering, field repairs, weapon maintenance, fabrication.

### III. Social Skills (CHA)
- **Culture (CHA):** Etiquette, protocol, xenological customs.
- **Deceit (CHA):** Bluffs, disguises, misdirection, undercover infiltration.
- **Diplomacy (CHA):** Negotiation, treaties, conflict resolution, persuasion.
- **Intimidation (STR/CHA):** Coercion, interrogation, psychological pressure.
- **Streetwise (CHA/WIS):** Black market contacts, criminal underground navigation.

### IV. Combat Skills (AGI / STR)
- **Archaic Weapons (AGI/STR):** Swords, polearms, bows, thrown weapons.
- **Modern Weapons (AGI):** Pistols, rifles, shotguns, submachine guns.
- **Advanced / Heavy Weapons (STR/INT):** Plasma cannons, missile launchers, railguns.
- **Unarmed Combat (STR/AGI):** Martial arts, brawling, grappling, subdual.
- **Defense / Evasion (AGI):** Active dodges, parrying, kinetic deflection.

### V. Metafocus Skills (INT / WIS / CHA)
- **Attune (WIS/INT):** Drawing, channeling, and stabilizing metaphysical energy.
- **Discipline Skills (6 Branches):** Dimension, Energy, Entropy, Illusion, Matter, Mental.
