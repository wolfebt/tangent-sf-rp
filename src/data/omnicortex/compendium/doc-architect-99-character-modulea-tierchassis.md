## **MCM REFERENCE MODULE A: THE TIER CHASSIS (TIERS 0 – 20)**

During character generation, assigning a Tier instantly dictates the entity's baseline competence, raw health, action economy, and expected damage resistance before any specific skills or items are applied.

## **EXPANDED TIER DESCRIPTIONS**

- **Tiers 0–2 (Civilian / Adept / Militia):** Everyday populace, unaugmented wildlife, or standard grunt-level security. They possess rudimentary to basic professional training in their daily vocation but are largely ineffective in a sustained firefight against true professionals. Dangerous in groups, manageable alone.
- **Tiers 3–5 (Trooper / Standard / Professional):** Career soldiers, experienced bounty hunters, and advanced predatory wildlife. They utilize cover, flanking maneuvers, and fully understand their weaponry. They represent the standard threat level for an engaged and active combat zone. *(Note: Tier 5 is the mathematical equivalent of a starting 150 CP Player Character).*
- **Tiers 6–9 (Veteran / Specialist / Elite / Operative):** Hardened survivors, squad commanders, special forces operatives, and master martial artists. Their high attributes and Expert-level skills make them highly lethal one-on-one. They often possess Augmentations, advanced Features, and superior-tier gear.
- **Tiers 10–13 (Champion / Warlord / Commander / General):** The threshold of Boss-level adversaries or legendary Allies. Faction generals, ancient beasts, or heavily modified cyborgs. They have vast health pools (+50 to +65, doubled for Boss status) and specialized, devastating attacks. Their mastery of skills means they dictate the terms of engagement and rarely miss.
- **Tiers 14–16 (Paragon / High Lord / Heroic):** Characters that can subjugate entire cities alone. Grand Masters of metaphysical disciplines, apex Dragons, or highly advanced Synthesis constructs. Beings that require an entire coordinated strike team and extensive, multi-stage planning to successfully engage.
- **Tiers 17–19 (Legend / Ascendant / Demigod):** Myths made manifest and beings of immense, localized power. Avatars of the U'Nonn, rogue AI that have consumed planetary computing networks, or ancient figures whose very presence warps the local environment.
- **Tier 20 (Cosmic / Mythic):** Reality-warping beings. The sleeping Ila, Cosmic horrors, or literal gods. They possess Pinnacle skills (Rank 30+) and operate completely beyond the standard laws of physics, mortality, and the battlefield.

## **DERIVING CANONICAL STATS FROM THE BASE CHASSIS**

- **Skill Allocation:** The chassis provides a **Primary Skill Rank** and a **Secondary Skill Rank**. Apply the Primary Rank to the first "Key Skill" listed in the character's chosen Role Package (Module B). Apply the Secondary Rank to all remaining Key Skills in that package.
- **Initiative:** `2d10 + Agility Mod + Alertness Rank`.
- **Attack (Strike) Bonus:** `2d10 + Primary Offensive Attribute Mod + Key Combat/Attune Skill Rank` (as dictated by Role).
- **Defense Score:** `10 + Agility Mod + Defense Skill Rank + Armor/Shield/Size Modifiers`.
- **Saving Throws (Fortitude, Reflex, Willpower):**
  - **Active Save Roll:** `2d10 + 2 + (2 * Attribute Score) + Feature Bonus`
  - **Passive Save CR (Take 10):** `12 + (2 * Attribute Score) + Feature Bonus`
- **Base Movement:** 30 ft Groundspeed (unless modified by Species Scale, Faction traits, or Role features such as the Mobility Specialist).
- **Damage Mitigation (Armor DR + Base Toughness):** Subtract **Effective Armor DR** first, then subtract the character's **Stamina (STA) Modifier** point-for-point (**Base Toughness**).
- **Wealth Score (WS):** A character's base Wealth Score is equal to double their Threat Tier (`Base WS = 2 * Threat Tier`), representing their escalating influence and access to superior tier gear. *Exceptions:* Companions do not generate a Wealth Score; they are completely dependent on the Player Character for equipment and liquidity. Furthermore, feral beasts, mindless constructs, and cosmic anomalies do not utilize Wealth Scores.
- **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):**
  - **Biological Entities:** Begin with **30 Base Vitality** and **30 Base Health**, adding the **Vit/Hea Bonus** from the Tier Chassis table directly to each pool (representing +5 points per 1 CP invested). **Stamina never increases Vitality or Health.**
  - **Synthetics, Constructs & Mecha:** Combine total Vitality and Health into a single **Structure Points (`SP = Vitality + Health`)** pool (`Base 60 SP + [2 * Tier Bonus]`).
- **Size & Designation Multiplier Stacking:**
  1. First, multiply final calculated Vitality and Health (or Structure) totals by the creature's **Size Scale Multiplier** (*Tiny x0.5, Small x2/3, Medium x1, Large x2, Huge x5, Gargantuan x10, Colossal x20*).
  2. Second, if the character is a **Boss (`x2`)** or **Mastermind (`x3` to `x5`)**, apply the Designation Multiplier to the size-scaled pool. (Multipliers are multiplicative).
- **Invocation Stages:** When calculating the damage or scale of a metaphysical Invocation, convert the character's Discipline Skill Rank (20 maximum) plus the Invocation Rank (10 maximum) into Stages:
  - **Stage 1 (Novice):** Ranks 1–5
  - **Stage 2 (Trained):** Ranks 6–10
  - **Stage 3 (Expert):** Ranks 11–15
  - **Stage 4 (Master):** Ranks 16–20
  - **Stage 5 (Grand Master):** Ranks 21–25
  - **Stage 6 (Pinnacle):** Ranks 26–30+
- **Essence Economy:** Manifesting an Invocation at its base learned Stage costs 0 Essence (it is powered by ambient resonance). Pushing an Invocation to cast at a *higher* Stage than the character's current Skill Rank (any Invocation check over CR 10 difficulty), or maintaining multiple sustained effects simultaneously, costs 1 Essence per additional Stage/Effect per round.
