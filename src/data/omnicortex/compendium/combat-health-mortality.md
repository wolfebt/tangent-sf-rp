# Health, Vitality & The Mortality State (0 HP)

## The Mortality State (0 Hit Points)
When a character's HP drops to 0, they do not immediately die; they enter the **Mortality State**:
1. **Unconscious & Incapacitated**: The character immediately falls Prone and is unable to take actions.
2. **Bleeding Out**: At the start of each of their turns, the character suffers **1 point of Stability Damage**.
3. **Stability Threshold**: Total Stability Points equal **Constitution Score + 5**.
4. **Death**: When Stability Points reach 0, the character is permanently dead.
5. **Medical Stabilization**: A successful **Medicine Check (DC 15)** or metaphysical healing stabilizes the patient and stops bleeding out.
