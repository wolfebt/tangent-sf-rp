# 1.00. INTRODUCTION: TANGENT SCIENCE FANTASY ROLE-PLAYING SYSTEM

* **Focus Statement:** The primary player-facing introduction to the **TANGENT Science Fantasy Role-Playing Engine (Tangent SF RP)**, establishing the core narrative pillars, the 150-CP Operator creation architecture, the 2d10 vs. Challenge Rating (CR) resolution curve, the complete Core System Glossary, and official publishing credits.

## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Role-Playing Games (RPGs) provide a unique and interactive form of entertainment where a group of **Operators (Players)** and an **Architect (Game Master)** come together to weave imaginative stories. **Tangent SF RP** invites players to explore a captivating science-fantasy universe where street-level cyberpunk, high-magic arcane orders, bio-engineered hive collectives, and Dyson-scale cosmic empires intertwine under a single deterministic rules engine. Players are empowered to create one-of-a-kind characters and embark on exciting adventures across the galaxy.

Every rule and subsystem introduced in **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** adheres to these foundational mechanical laws:

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.

* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.

* **Critical Hit & Fumble Resolution:**

  * **Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`):** `Rolled Dice + 10` (e.g., `20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`.

  * **Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`):** `Rolled Dice becomes flat -10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.

* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.

* **80-CP Archetype Chassis Breakdown:**

  * **Primary Attribute at +3:** `15 CP` (`5 CP` per `+1`)

  * **Secondary Attribute at +2:** `10 CP` (`5 CP` per `+1`)

  * **4 Essential Skills at Rank 6 (Trained):** `24 CP` (`1 CP` per Rank)

  * **6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3 (Novice):** `18 CP` (`1 CP` per Rank)

  * **2 Signature Traits (1 CP each) and 3 Recommended Core Features (9 CP):** `13 CP`

* **Background Skill Ranks:** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` as `20 SP Faction` + `20 SP Origin` + `20 SP Occupation`, alongside `4 Recommended Features` (`-1 CP` discount; `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and `4 Traits` at `0 CP` (`Extra Traits = 1 CP` each).

* **Universal Attribute & Skill Pricing:** Straight cost across all creation, species, and customization packages (`+5 CP` per `+1` Attribute, `-5 CP` refund per `-1` Attribute penalty, `1 CP` per `1` Skill Rank / `5 CP` per `5` Skill Ranks — zero package discount).

* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools:** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct characters combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.

* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Standard personal character hindrances have a `+10 CP` maximum limit; **Species-level hindrances and cybernetic Stigmas do NOT count toward the 10-point standard hindrance limit**.

## Part II — Complete Unabridged Profiles & Rules Entries

### 1. Welcome to the Tangent Universe (The Six Pillars of Character Identity)

* *You stand at the threshold of a boundless science-fantasy galaxy—where ancient arcane lineages, hyper-capitalist megacorporations, bio-engineered hive minds, and frontier drifters collide across thousands of star systems.*

* *"Every legend in the Star Atlas started with a hundred and fifty Character Points, a battered sidearm, and a reason to leave home."* — **Captain Vaelen Thorne, Outworlds Free Trader**

#### 1.01. The Role-Playing Engine (`1.00 INTRODUCTION.md`)

Role-Playing Games (RPGs) provide a unique and interactive form of entertainment where a group of **Operators (Players)** and an **Architect (Game Master)** come together to weave imaginative stories. **Tangent SF RP** invites players to explore a captivating universe where science fiction and fantasy intertwine. Players are empowered to create one-of-a-kind characters using a deterministic **150 Character Point (CP)** creation budget and embark on exciting adventures.

* **Core Resolution Mechanics:**

  * **Standard Check:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.

  * **Advantage / Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack and keep the highest 2 dice (Advantage) or lowest 2 dice (Disadvantage).

  * **Critical Hit (`Natural 20`):** `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`.

  * **Fumble (`Natural 2`):** `Rolled Dice becomes flat -10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.

#### 1.02. Species & Biological Heritage (`1.03 SPECIES.md`)

* *Your genetic or synthetic blueprint dictates how you perceive the cosmos, how your body withstands vacuum or plasma fire, and how the societies of the galaxy react when you walk through an airlock.*

* *"Aeld live six centuries and still hurry; Mekan live forever and never waste a microsecond."* — **Xenobiologist's Field Note**

Tangent offers an intricate character customization system, granting players the freedom to shape their character's destiny. The journey begins with selecting a **Species** (or Sub-Species) for your character. Will you choose to be one of the ancient **Alterians** (`1.03.Aeld.Alterian.md`), wielding potent arcane metaphysics passed down through generations across 600-year lifespans? Or perhaps you will opt for the technologically advanced **Cyberforms** (`1.03.Humans.Cyberform.md`) or **Mekan** (`1.03.Synthetics.Mekan.md`), renowned for their superior intellect and cybernetic or synthetic enhancements? Each species in Tangent possesses distinct attributes and abilities, influencing your character's strengths, weaknesses, and potential in the game world.

* **Species Mechanical Parameters:**

  * **Universal Straight CP Pricing:** Species attribute modifiers cost `+5 CP` per `+1` and refund `-5 CP` per `-1`; innate species skill allowances cost `1 CP` per `1 Rank` (`5 CP` per `5 Ranks`) with zero package discount.

  * **Recommended Features:** Each species provides a curated list of **Recommended Features** eligible for the `-1 CP` discount (`2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`).

  * **Species Hindrance Exemption:** Innate species-level hindrances and cultural Stigmas do **not** count toward the `+10 CP` personal hindrance cap.

#### 1.03. Origins & Upbringing (`1.05 ORIGINS.md`)

* *Before you ever joined a faction or learned a trade, the gravity, atmosphere, and social order of your homeworld forged your earliest survival instincts.*

* *"Show me how someone treats clean water and open sky, and I will tell you which world raised them."* — **Coalition Colonial Administrator**

Beyond species selection, Tangent delves into the rich tapestry of your character's **Origins**. Your background and planetary or habitat upbringing play a significant role in shaping who you are. Were you raised in the humble surroundings of a rural **Agricultural** (`1.05.Agricultural.md`) or **Colony** (`1.05.Colony.md`) planet, learning the value of community and resourcefulness? Or did you experience a privileged childhood in a sprawling **Urban** (`1.05.Urban.md`) or **Leisure** (`1.05.Leisure.md`) metropolis, surrounded by advanced technology and political intrigue? Your Origin will not only influence your character's skills and abilities, but also contribute to their personality, starting Technology Level (`TL`), Metafocus Level (`ML`), and worldview.

* **Origin Mechanical Allocation (`0 CP` Baseline):**

  * **Background Skill Points:** Grants **`20 Skill Points (SP)`** distributed across core upbringing skills.

  * **Origin Feature & Trait:** Grants **`1 Origin Recommended Feature`** and **`1 Origin Trait`** at `0 CP`.

#### 1.04. Factions & Galactic Polities (`1.04 FACTIONS.md`)

* *Great powers carve the stars into spheres of influence, offering doctrine, patronage, and high-technology arsenals to those who carry their banner.*

* *"In the Core Worlds, allegiance is currency. On the Rim, allegiance is who shows up when your distress beacon fires."* — **Dracon Dynasty Envoy**

**Factions** represent powerful groups, civilizations, megacorporations, religious orders, or organizations that hold sway over the game world. Aligning with a faction can grant your character access to valuable resources, specialized training, and unique missions that shape their path. Choosing a faction that resonates with your character's beliefs and aspirations adds depth to their identity and opens doors to exciting opportunities. Whether you are drawn to the mystical **Alterian Enclave** (`1.04.AeldProvidence.AlterianEnclave.md`), the technologically advanced and hyper-capitalist **Syndicate** (`1.04.Major.Syndicate.md`), the politically influential noble houses of the **Dracon Dynasty** (`1.04.Major.DraconDynasty.md`), the psionic **Radiant Impyrium** (`1.04.Impyrium.Radiant.md`), or the fiercely independent **Coalition** (`1.04.Major.Coalition.md`) and **Outworlds** (`1.04.Major.Outworlds.md`), your choice of faction will significantly impact your character's journey and the challenges they face. Careful consideration of each faction's values, goals, and resources will ensure that your character finds their place in the intricate tapestry of Tangent's universe.

* **Faction Mechanical Allocation (`0 CP` Baseline):**

  * **Background Skill Points:** Grants **`20 Skill Points (SP)`** in culturally favored skills.

  * **Faction Feature & Trait:** Grants **`1 Faction Recommended Feature`** and **`1 Faction Trait`** at `0 CP`, plus access to factional CRISP visual skins and equipment lists.

#### 1.05. Occupations & Careers (`1.06 OCCUPATIONS.md`)

* *Your profession is how you earned your living, sharpened your expertise, and established your credit standing before stepping onto the galactic stage.*

* *"A title on a dossier means nothing. What matters is what your hands know how to do when the lights go out."* — **Specialist Guildmaster**

Your character's **Occupation** represents their chosen profession and area of expertise. This choice not only adds flavor and depth to their identity, but also grants them specific skills and abilities that reflect their training and experience. Whether you envision your character as a brave **Soldier** (`1.06.Soldier.md`), a wise **Scholar** (`1.06.Scholar.md`), a covert **Agent** (`1.06.Agent.md`), a cunning **Criminal** (`1.06.Criminal.md`), or a resourceful **Drifter** (`1.06.Drifter.md`), selecting an occupation that aligns with their personality and aspirations is crucial. Each of the 13 canonical Occupations in Tangent offers a unique set of skills, traits, Wealth Score modifiers, and **Recommended Features** that help define your character's capabilities and playstyle. By carefully considering the advantages and trade-offs of each occupation, you can create a character that is not only well-suited to their chosen path, but also capable of overcoming the challenges they will face in the game world.

* **Occupation Mechanical Allocation (`0 CP` Baseline):**

  * **Background Skill Points:** Grants **`20 Skill Points (SP)`** in vocational and career skills (completing the **`60 SP` total background skill allocation**: `20 SP Faction + 20 SP Origin + 20 SP Occupation`).

  * **Occupation Features & Traits:** Grants **`2 Occupation Recommended Features`** and **`2 Occupation Traits`** at `0 CP` (completing the **`4 Recommended Features` and `4 Traits` foundational background package**).

#### 1.06. Archetypes, Personality, Bonds & Progression (`1.01` & `1.02`)

* *Statistics measure what an Operator can accomplish; personality traits, flaws, and bonds reveal who they are when tested by the void.*

* *"Numbers build a chassis. Scars and promises build a person."* — **Architect's Matrix Commentary**

To bridge abstract Character Points and immediate table playability, Operators may select a pre-calculated **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`1.02 ARCHETYPES.md`)—defining their Primary Attribute at `+3` (`15 CP`), Secondary Attribute at `+2` (`10 CP`), 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6 (`24 CP`), 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3 (`18 CP`), 2 Signature Traits (`2 CP`), and 3 Recommended Core Features (`9 CP`, totaling `13 CP`)—and spend their remaining **70 CP** to select their Species, customize their Saving Throws (`Active Save = 2d10 + 2 + [2 * Attribute] + Feature Bonus`), expand their **Vitality** and **Health** pools (`Base 30` each, increased strictly by `+5 points per 1 CP spent` with zero Stamina scaling), and acquire gear via the **Economic Unified Field Theory (EUFT)** (`2.00 ECONOMATRIX.md`).

The character creation system in Tangent empowers you to build characters that are not merely collections of stats and abilities, but also possess intriguing personalities and captivating backstories. You can select from a variety of personality traits, personal hindrances (up to `+10 CP`), and bonds (`1.01.PipelineIdentity.md`), adding layers of depth and complexity to your character. These choices will not only make your character feel more alive and relatable, but also influence their interactions with other characters and the world around them.

As you embark on your adventures, your character will face numerous challenges and opportunities that will test their mettle. The decisions you make in response to these situations will shape the course of the story and determine your character's ultimate fate. Will you rise to become a hero, using your abilities to protect the innocent and fight for justice? Or will you succumb to temptation, using your powers for personal gain and domination? The choice is yours.

Whether you are a seasoned enthusiast or a newcomer to the RPG genre, Tangent offers an immersive and engaging experience that encourages you to unleash your creativity and strategic thinking. Gather your friends, roll the `2d10` dice, and prepare to embark on an unforgettable adventure in the boundless universe.

### 2. Core System Glossary (`./introduction/1.00.Glossary.md`)

* *Every Operator and Architect relies on a shared deterministic lexicon to adjudicate resolution rolls, displacement units, wound thresholds, metaphysical channeling, and interstellar trade.*

* *"Define your terms before you draw your weapon."* — **Kovian Tribunal Legal Code**

#### I. Core Metrics (The Math)

These terms define the fundamental mathematics of the **Tangent SF RP** engine:

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Range / Formula | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Core Roll** | Resolution | The universal resolution roll for skill checks, attacks, and active saves. | `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Mod + Modifiers vs. CR` | 
| **Advantage** | Resolution | Roll an extra `+1d10` per stack of Advantage and keep the **highest 2 dice**. | Cancels 1-for-1 with Disadvantage | 
| **Disadvantage** | Resolution | Roll an extra `+1d10` per stack of Disadvantage and keep the **lowest 2 dice**. | Cancels 1-for-1 with Advantage | 
| **Critical Hit** | Resolution | **Natural 20** (double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`). | `Rolled Dice + 10` (e.g., `20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Critical Effect` | 
| **Fumble** | Resolution | **Natural 2** (double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`). | `Rolled Dice becomes flat -10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication` | 
| **Rank** | Skills | The numerical value assigned to a Skill, representing formal training and practice. | `0` (Untrained) to `30+` (Pinnacle) | 
| **Score** | Attributes | The raw modifier of one of the 6 Primary Attributes (`STR`, `AGI`, `STA`, `INT`, `WIS`, `CHA`). | `-5` to `+10` (Caps vary by Species/Tier; `+5 CP` per `+1`) | 
| **CP** | Creation | **Character Points**. The universal currency used for Character Creation. | Standard Starting Budget: **`150 CP`** (`80 CP` Chassis + `70 CP` Custom) | 
| **AP / XP** | Progression | **Award Points / Experience Points**. Post-creation advancement points spent 1-for-1 like CP. | Tier 1: `150–199 CP`, Tier 2: `200–299 CP`, Tier 3: `300–449 CP`, Tier 4: `450+ CP` | 
| **CR** | Mechanics | **Challenge Rating**. The target difficulty number a `2d10` check must meet or exceed. | `CR 0` (Scrap/Simple) to `CR 50+` (Transcendent) | 
| **Active Save** | Defense | Dynamic saving throw rolled against hazards, toxins, or invocations. | `2d10 + 2 + (2 * Attribute) + Feature Bonus` | 
| **Passive Save CR** | Defense | Static threshold an attacker's check must meet when targeting a character passively. | `12 + (2 * Attribute) + Feature Bonus` | 
| **Karma (KP)** | Resources | Meta-currency pool used to roll with Advantage, seize initiative, or trigger Karma Features. | Base `3 KP` (Refreshes per Session; `1 CP = +1 Max KP`) | 
| **Tier** | Scale | Broad category of power or complexity for Characters, Threat Templates, and Infrastructure. | Tier 0 (Civilian) to Tier 5 (Cosmic / Post-Scarcity) | 

#### II. Physics & Capacity (The UDU System)

* **Canonical 10:1 UDU Ratio:** `1 Module (10 Tons) = 10 Mounts (1,000 kg) = 100 Sockets (100 kg) = 1,000 Usable Nodes (10 kg)`

| Term | Domain | Definition | Mass & Capacity Equivalence | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **UDU** | Physics | **Universal Displacement Unit**. The master logarithmic hierarchy governing mass, volume, and slot capacity across augmentations, personal gear, mecha, and architecture. | `10 Nodes = 1 Socket` | `10 Sockets = 1 Mount` | `10 Mounts = 1 Module` | 
| **Node (Tier 0)** | Augmentations | Microscale installation slot for biological, cybernetic, and nanofiber augmentations. | `1 Usable Node (< 10 g) = 0.1 Socket` (`10 Nodes = 1 Socket`) | 
| **Socket (Tier 1)** | Equipment | Personal-scale customization slot for weapons, armor suits, and portable drones. | `1 Socket (< 1 kg) = 10 Usable Nodes` | 
| **Mount (Tier 2)** | Mecha & Vehicles | Vehicular/Mecha hardpoint slot for heavy weapons, engines, and tactical subsystems. | `1 Mount (< 100 kg) = 10 Sockets` *(Garaging: `1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier`)* | 
| **Module (Tier 3)** | Architecture | Macroscale pre-fabricated room, starship bay, or facility block (`20x20 ft`). | `1 Module (< 10 Tons) = 10 Mounts = 100 Sockets` | 

#### III. Survival & Combat Integrity

Defining the states of life, structural resilience, and damage mitigation:

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Formula / Limit | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Vitality** | Integrity | Physical stamina, luck, and glancing-blow avoidance. Absorbs initial/non-lethal damage before Health. | **Base `30`**; increased strictly by **`+5 per 1 CP`** (Suggested Max: `60`). **Never scaled by Stamina.** | 
| **Health** | Integrity | Biological structural integrity and life force. Damage here represents physical wounds and trauma. | **Base `30`**; increased strictly by **`+5 per 1 CP`** (Suggested Max: `60`). **Never scaled by Stamina.** | 
| **Structure (SP)** | Integrity | Combined integrity pool (`Vitality + Health`) for Synthetics, Constructs, Vehicles, Mecha, and Buildings. | **Base `60 SP`** for Synthetics (`+5 SP per 1 CP`; Suggested Max: `120 SP`). | 
| **Base Toughness** | Mitigation | Point-for-point damage reduction granted by a character's **Stamina (STA) Modifier**. | Subtracts `STA Mod` from incoming damage *after* Armor DR. | 
| **DR** | Mitigation | **Damage Resistance / Reduction**. Flat value subtracted from incoming damage before Base Toughness and Vitality. | `0` (Clothing) to `60+` (Capital Ship). **Armor Layering Cap:** Only the single highest DR across bio, cyber, and worn armor applies. | 
| **Penetration** | Mitigation | Weapon armor-piercing rating that reduces the target's effective Armor DR on that strike. | `Effective DR = max(0, Highest Single Domain DR - Weapon Penetration)` | 
| **Defense** | Combat | Static target number an attacker's `2d10` Strike roll must meet or exceed to hit. | `10 + Agility + Defense Skill Rank + Shield/Size/Cover Modifiers` | 
| **Mortality State** | Survival | Triggered at `0 Health`. Character gains **Stability Points = `Stamina Score + 5`** and loses `1` per turn until stabilized (`CR 15 Medicine` or `CR 15 Fortitude Save`). | Death occurs at `0 Stability Points` or `-Stamina Score` Health; revivification inflicts `-5 XP Debt` and drains Karma to `0`. | 
| **Stigma** | Social | Social prejudice and Alien Mind Factor penalty resulting from species, synthetic, or cultural bias. | `-1` (Minor), `-2` (Typical), `-4` to `-8` (Severe/Monstrous) to Social checks with biased groups (exempt from `+10 CP` personal hindrance cap). | 

#### IV. Metaphysics & Technology

The intersection of Resonance Manipulation (Magic/Psionics) and Scientific Engineering:

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Range / Formula | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **TL** | Technology | **Technology Level**. The sophistication of scientific engineering and manufacturing. | `TL 0` (Stone/Primitive) to `TL 5` (Polymatter / Post-Scarcity) | 
| **ML** | Metaphysics | **Metafocus Level**. The societal integration and sophistication of Resonance manipulation. | `ML 0` (Unawakened/Null) to `ML 6` (Deific / Progenitor) | 
| **Attune** | Metaphysics | The universal conduit skill for channeling Resonance energy. Sets target Save CRs and attack accuracy. | Active Check = `2d10 + Attune Rank + Key Attribute Mod` (`Passive CR = 10 + Attune + Key Mod`) | 
| **Discipline** | Metaphysics | One of the **6 Primary Metaphysic Schools** (`Dimension`, `Energy`, `Entropy`, `Illusion`, `Matter`, `Mental`), each split into **2 Focus Skills** (`12 Foci` total). | Determines Range, Area, Duration, and Base Stage Dice (`+1d6` to `+4d6`). | 
| **Invocation** | Metaphysics | A specific spell, psionic power, or technique learned within a Discipline (`1 CP` per Rank). | Adds `+1 Effect Die` per Invocation Rank to Base Discipline Stage Dice. | 
| **Essence** | Metaphysics | Internal bio-spiritual energy reserve used to empower high-strain invocations or Meta-Tech. | `Essence Pool = Sum of All 6 Attribute Scores (STR + AGI + STA + INT + WIS + CHA) + Attune Rank` | 

#### V. Economy & Wealth (EUFT)

Bridging abstract Wealth Scores (`WS`), Crafting Challenge Ratings (`CR`), and liquid Credits (`Cr`):

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Formula / Ratio | 
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | 
| **Wealth (WS)** | Economy | **Wealth Score**. Abstract rating of financial leverage, credit access, and auto-buy threshold (`WS 0` to `WS 80+`). | **Golden Rule:** Auto-buy any personal item where `Item CR <= Character WS`. | 
| **Credits (Cr)** | Economy | Liquid currency used to pay the **Liquidity Gap** when purchasing items where `Item CR > WS`. | `Liquidity Gap = Value(Item CR) - Value(Character WS)` | 
| **MCr** | Economy | **Mega-Credit**. Macro-economic unit used for starships, orbital stations, and planetary trade. | `1 MCr = 1,000,000 Credits` | 
| **Crafting CR** | Economy | The "Prime Mover" of item valuation (`Purchase CR = Crafting CR`). | Base intervals of 5: `Value (Cr) = 10 * 4 ^ (CR / 5)` | 
| **Micro-Modifiers** | Economy | Linear multipliers applied to the nearest base interval of 5 for intermediate CRs (`+1` to `+4` and `-1` Flaw). | `+1 CR = x1.30` | `+2 CR = x1.75` | `+3 CR = x2.30` | `+4 CR = x3.00` | `-1 CR Flaw = x0.75` | 
| **Material Ratio** | Crafting | Raw material cost required to initiate fabrication via the Productivity Engine. | **Pristine Materials:** `50%` of Credit Value | **Scavenged Scrap:** `10%` value (requires `5x` Item Credit Value in raw scrap mass) |
