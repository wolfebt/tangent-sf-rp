# **1.00 INTRODUCTION: TANGENT SCIENCE FANTASY ROLE-PLAYING SYSTEM**

---

## **1. Welcome to the Tangent Universe**

Role-Playing Games (RPGs) provide a unique and interactive form of entertainment where a group of **Operators (Players)** and an **Architect (Game Master)** come together to weave imaginative stories. **Tangent SF RP** invites players to explore a captivating science-fantasy universe where street-level cyberpunk, high-magic arcane orders, bio-engineered hive collectives, and Dyson-scale cosmic empires intertwine under a single deterministic rules engine. Players are empowered to create one-of-a-kind characters using a **150 Character Point (CP)** creation budget and embark on adventures across the galaxy.

### **Species & Biological Heritage (`1.03 SPECIES.md`)**

Tangent offers an intricate character customization system, granting Operators the freedom to shape their character's destiny. The journey begins with selecting a **Species** (or Sub-Species) for your character. Will you choose to be one of the ancient **Alterian Aeld**, wielding potent arcane metaphysics passed down across 600-year lifespans? Or perhaps you will opt for the technologically advanced **Cyberforms** or **Mekan**, renowned for their superior intellect and synthetic or cybernetic enhancements? Each species in Tangent possesses distinct attribute modifiers (priced universally at `5 CP` per `+1` and `-5 CP` per `-1`, with skill allowances at `5 CP` per `5 Ranks` / `1 CP` per `1 Rank` — zero package discount), innate biological traits, and cultural stigmas that shape your character's strengths, vulnerabilities, and role in the galaxy.

### **Origins & Upbringing (`1.05 ORIGINS.md`)**

Beyond species selection, Tangent delves into the rich tapestry of your character's **Origin**. Your planetary or habitat upbringing plays a foundational role in shaping who you are, granting **20 Skill Points**, **1 Origin Feature**, and **1 Origin Trait** at **0 CP**. Were you raised in the humble surroundings of an **Agricultural** or **Colony** world, learning the value of community and self-sufficiency? Or did you experience a privileged childhood in a sprawling **Urban** or **Leisure** metropolis, surrounded by advanced technology and political intrigue? Your Origin influences not only your character's skills and abilities, but also their starting Technology Level (TL), Metaphysics Level (ML), and worldview.

### **Factions & Galactic Polities (`1.04 FACTIONS.md`)**

**Factions** represent powerful civilizations, megacorporations, religious orders, or independent syndicates that hold sway over the galaxy, granting **20 Skill Points**, **1 Faction Feature**, and **1 Faction Trait** at **0 CP**. Aligning with a faction grants your character access to specialized training, cultural networks, and operational mandates. Whether you are drawn to the mystical **Alterian Enclave**, the hyper-capitalist **Incorporated Planetary Syndication (The Syndicate)**, the noble houses of the **Dracon Dynasty**, the monolithic **Radiant Impyrium**, or the fiercely independent **Coalition** and **Outworlds**, your choice of faction significantly impacts your character's journey and the challenges they face.

### **Occupations & Careers (`1.06 OCCUPATIONS.md`)**

Your character's **Occupation** represents their chosen profession and area of expertise, granting **20 Skill Points**, **2 Occupation Features**, and **2 Occupation Traits** at **0 CP** (completing the **60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits** foundational background allocation). Whether you envision your character as a frontline **Soldier**, an inquisitive **Scholar**, a covert **Agent**, or a frontier **Drifter**, each of the 13 canonical Occupations offers a tailored suite of professional skills, starting Wealth Score synergies, and recommended features.

### **Archetypes, Personality & Progression (`1.01` & `1.02`)**

To bridge abstract Character Points and immediate table playability, Operators may select a pre-calculated **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`1.02 ARCHETYPES.md`)—defining prioritized attributes, trained skills, and signature traits—and spend their remaining **70 CP** to select their Species, customize their Saving Throws (`Active Save = 2d10 + 2 + [2 * Attribute] + Feature Bonus`), expand their **Vitality** and **Health** pools (`Base 30` each, increased strictly by `+5 points per 1 CP spent`), and acquire gear via the **Economic Unified Field Theory (EUFT)** (`2.00 ECONOMATRIX.md`).

As you embark on your adventures, every check is resolved using the **2d10 Bell-Curve Resolution Engine** (`2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating [CR]`). Gather your crew, calibrate your gear, and prepare to enter the Tangent universe.
