# **DAMAGE TYPES & CRITICAL INJURY MATRIX**

Tangent categorizes all offensive trauma into three overarching families—**Energy**, **Kinetic**, and **Variative (Elemental, Planar & Psychic)**—comprising **23 distinct Damage Types**. Each damage type interacts uniquely with armor resistances, environmental conditions, and anatomical hit locations, and features its own dedicated **Critical Injury Severity Table (1–20+)**.

| Damage Family | Damage Type | Primary Trauma Mechanism | Typical Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
