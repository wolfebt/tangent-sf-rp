# Technologist & Tech Level Progression

The Technologist feature represents advanced engineering mastery across specialized scientific disciplines.

## Effective Tech Level (TL) Progression
A character who takes the **Technologist** feature gains an **effective Tech Level +1** in their chosen field of study for crafting, modifying, prototyping, and repairing advanced technology.

## The 17 Technologist Fields of Study:
1. **Agriculture**: Hydroponics, atmosphere processors, automated farming rigs.
2. **Architecture**: Hab modules, structural shielding, space elevator engineering.
3. **Biotechnology**: Living tools, organic armor, cloning, tissue grafts.
4. **Commerce**: Financial encryption, automated logistics networks.
5. **Communication**: Sub-space transceivers, quantum entanglement relays.
6. **Devices**: Micro-gadgets, sensors, smart tools, personal electronics.
7. **Education**: Neural memory implants, holographic simulation pedagogy.
8. **Energy**: Fusion reactors, antimatter taps, solar array collectors.
9. **Manufacturing**: Nanoforges, 3D molecular printers, automated assembly.
10. **Materials**: Hyper-dense alloys, carbon nanotube weaves, meta-materials.
11. **Medicine**: Cyber-surgeries, genetic mutagens, neural prosthetics.
12. **Meta Sciences**: Psi-amplifiers, resonant crystal matrices, arcane capacitors.
13. **Science**: Laboratory spectrometers, particle colliders.
14. **Society**: Arcology social engineering, civic surveillance matrices.
15. **Synthetic Intelligence**: Neural matrices, sentient droids, combat automata.
16. **Transportation**: FTL hyperdrives, sub-light grav-drives, repulsorlifts.
17. **Weaponry**: Particle beams, plasma cannons, railguns, power fields.
