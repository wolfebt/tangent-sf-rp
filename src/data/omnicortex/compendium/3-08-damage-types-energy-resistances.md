# 3.08 Master Damage Types Directory & Energy Profiles

Tangent features a rich ecosystem of damage types categorized into Kinetic, Energy, and Variative/Elemental profiles across Archaic, Modern, Advanced Tech, and Metaphysical vectors.

---

## Master Damage Types Directory

| Damage Type | Category | Primary Mechanical Effect | Common Vector / Weaponry |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Blunt** | Kinetic | Crushing trauma, bone fractures | Clubs, maces, hammers, power gauntlets |
| **Slashing** | Kinetic | Deep lacerations; crits inflict **Bleeding** | Swords, axes, vibroblades, monomolecular edges |
| **Piercing** | Kinetic | Pinpoint organ penetration; crits inflict **Bleeding** | Spears, daggers, AP rounds, railguns, flechettes |
| **Force** | Kinetic Subtype | **Ignores 1/2 of Target Armor DR** | Concussive kinetic fields, gravitic crushers |
| **Concussive / Impact**| Kinetic | Dispersed whole-body trauma; **50/50 Vitality/Health split** | Falls, crashes, explosives, siege engines |
| **Bleed** | Kinetic Effect | **1d4 damage/turn, ignores Armor DR** | Slashing/piercing critical wounds |
| **Pyro / Fire** | Energy | Burns and incinerates; inflicts **Burning** (1d6/round) | Flamethrowers, incendiary ammo, plasma, pyrokinesis |
| **Cryo / Cold** | Energy | Thermal drain slowing speed; inflicts **Freeze** (Speed 0) | Cryo-guns, cryo-lasers, liquid nitrogen, cryokinesis |
| **Spectral / Phase** | Energy | **Ignores physical Armor DR entirely** to disrupt essence | Phase rifles, ghost touch, spirit weapons |
| **Sonic / Sound** | Energy | **Ignores most physical armor** via vibrational acoustic shock | Sonic cannons, saser beams, flash-crackers, sonokinesis |
| **Voltic / Electrical**| Energy | Stuns nervous systems, overloads robotics and electronics | Tasers, arc cannons, EMP devices, electrokinesis |

| **Force Pulse / Disruption** | Energy | Destabilizes molecular bonds; disintegrates targets on crits | Disruptor pistols/cannons, molecular disruptors |
| **Air Elemental** | Variative | Atmospheric gales knocking foes **Prone** and suffocating | Compressed air cannons, vortex generators, aerokinesis |
| **Fire Elemental** | Variative | Primal volcanic flames scorching terrain and applying Burning | Fire evocation, incendiary saturation |
| **Earth Elemental** | Variative | Crushing lithic weight and tremors knocking foes Prone | Seismic cannons, rockfalls, geokinesis |
| **Water Elemental** | Variative | Hydraulic pressure and drowning trauma | High-pressure waterjets, vortex emitters, hydrokinesis |
| **Radiant / Light** | Variative | Pure solar photons inflicting the **Blinded** condition | Flashbangs, coherent lasers, photokinesis |
| **Umbral / Shadow** | Variative | Void darkness draining vitality and inflicting Exhaustion | Dark energy projectors, shadow evocation, umbrokinesis |
| **Celestial** | Variative | Transcendent sacred energy smiting fiends and undead | Consecrated weapons, holy water, celestial evocation |

| **Dimensional** | Variative | Reality tearing that **completely bypasses Armor DR** | Phase rifts, spatial cannons, pocket dimension collapses |
| **Psyche / Mental** | Variative | Direct psionic neural trauma **ignoring physical Armor DR** | Neural disruptors, psychic assaults, telepathy |
