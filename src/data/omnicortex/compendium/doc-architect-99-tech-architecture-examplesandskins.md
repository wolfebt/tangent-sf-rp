## **XIV. THE CULTURAL SKIN (FACTION ARCHITECTURAL PARADIGMS)**

The same statistical building block functions differently depending on the faction that built it. The "Agnostic Chassis" provides the math, but the "Skin" is the aesthetic and narrative focus.

### **The Syndicate (Corporate Utopia / High-Tech Sanctum)**
* **Aesthetic:** "The Apple Store Cathedral." Designs are hyper-efficient, seamless, and sterile. Materials are matte white composites, brushed aluminum, and holographic interfaces.
* **Philosophy:** Technical Facilitation. Structures and habitats are integrated seamlessly into the "Mesh" network. Citizens in good standing enjoy the optimum capabilities of these automated systems. For non-citizens, basic habitational features are often subscription-based; with failure to tithe resulting in "Penance Mode" (reduced structural functionality and locked modules).
* **Signature:** Hexagonal tiling, cyan data-streams, anti-gravity elevators, expensive, clean and highly modular construction.

### **The Impyrium (Stagnant Grandeur / Heirloom Industrial)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Celestial Neo-Sumerian." Massive, brutalist structures clad in gold filigree and polished white marble. Buildings look solid and imposing, designed to intimidate.
* **Philosophy:** Enduring Dominance. Infrastructure is maintained by ritual as much as technical understanding. Facilities rely on "Relic Sockets" that accept only ancient, authorized archeotech components, violently rejecting modern or alien modifications.
* **Signature:** Solar motifs (8-pointed star), heavy physical plating, load-bearing statuary, and slow but unstoppable construction momentum.

### **The Dracon Dynasty (Feudal Protection / Warmth)**
* **Aesthetic:** "High-Fantasy Industrial." Heavy, blocky silhouettes reminiscent of castles or keeps. Warm lighting (orange/gold), tapestries in great halls, and prominent heraldic crests.
* **Philosophy:** Noblesse Oblige. Architecture is built to protect the inhabitants and project absolute authority. They prioritize structural integrity and extreme durability over speed of construction or efficiency.
* **Signature:** Dragon-inspired motifs (scales, crests), heavy kinetic/thermal defensive hardpoints, and "Void Castle" architectural designs that prioritize fortress-like integrity.

### **The Ascendancy (Enlightened Innovation / Solar Renaissance)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Smart City Solarpunk." Clean curves, glass canopies, and white facades with blue energy accents. A hopeful, forward-looking design language.
* **Philosophy:** Adaptive Progress. Unlike the Impyrium, they innovate rapidly. Their architectural technology is user-friendly, heavily shielded, and designed for modular agility and precision.
* **Signature:** Variable-geometry roofs and spires, high-efficiency ion power grids, and non-lethal structural defense options integrated alongside lethal ones.

### **The Coalition & Outworlds (Used Future / Scrappy Industrial)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Space Western Noir." Scavenged parts, exposed wiring, welded patch-jobs, and hazard stripes. Nothing is new; everything is repurposed.
* **Philosophy:** Survival Pragmatism. If it works, live in it. Redundancy is key because maintenance is sporadic.
* **Signature:** Asymmetrical layouts, ballistic defensive turrets (utilizing cheap ammo), oversized generators bolted onto exterior frames, and "Franken-habs" made of mismatched structural modules.

### **The Alterian Enclave (Magi-Tech Refinement / Crystal Spires)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Interstellar Art Nouveau." Impossible grace, solar sails (for energy gathering), white ceramic fused with living wood, and towering crystalline matrices.
* **Philosophy:** The Long View. Structures are treated as works of art designed to last centuries. Magic is treated as a rigorous science integrated flawlessly into structural engineering.
* **Signature:** Silent environmental systems, energy-based defensive weaponry (Sun-Lances), and open, airy atrium designs protected by force fields rather than physical glass.

### **The Auluran (Symbiotic Growth / Bioluminescent Jungle)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Living Tech." Structures and arcologies are grown, not built. Chitinous walls, vascular corridors, and bioluminescent moss lighting.
* **Philosophy:** Biological Imperative. The structure is a living organism bonded to its inhabitants. It actively heals structural damage (Regeneration) rather than requiring mechanical repair.
* **Signature:** No hard angles, fluid/organic shapes, spore/acid-based defensive weaponry, and neural-link facility interfaces that require biological compatibility.

### **The Mekan (Geometric Logic / Pure Code)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Fractal Perfection." Perfect geometric shapes (spheres, tetrahedrons), floating architectural components held by magnetism, and shifting "polymatter" surfaces.
* **Philosophy:** The Code. Form follows function instantaneously. A building might reconfigure its entire floorplan mid-siege to adapt to a threat or logistical shift.
* **Signature:** Liquid metal facades, hard-light structural projections, and "Swarm" modules where many small, independent rooms act as a single large entity.

### **The Entari Combine (Cosmopolitan Trade / Eco-Luxury)**
* **Aesthetic:** "Galactic Senate." A blend of the best features from other factions, smoothed over with a luxury finish. High-end materials, comfortable interiors, and non-threatening silhouettes.
* **Philosophy:** Ethical Efficiency. Infrastructure designed for mass trade, diplomacy, and elite comfort. Extremely high reliability and zero emissions.
* **Signature:** Universal transit/docking rings, advanced translation/comms suites built into the walls, and entirely concealed structural weaponry (maintaining a peaceful appearance with deadly defense capabilities).

## **XV. ECONOMIC INTEGRATION: THE TANGENT STANDARD ECONOMICS**

The most significant advancement in the Tangent Framework is the complete eradication of economic dissonance—the historically persistent fracture between the narrative abstraction of wealth and the mechanical reality of crafting and buying items. The Tangent Standard Economics posits a singular, mathematically rigorous solution: **Complexity Determines Value.** The market price of a structure or item is simply the societal aggregate of the skill (Crafting CR) and time required to produce it.

### **15. The Tangent Standard Curve (TSC)**

Because the gap between a simple survival tent and a sprawling moon base is logarithmic, the cost scaling must be exponential. The market price of every physical asset within the Tangent galaxy is generated using the Tangent Standard Curve (TSC) formula.

This formula dictates that an item's value in Credits equals a baseline value of 10 Credits (representing the simplest manufactured good or scrap metal at CR 0) multiplied by a growth factor of 4, raised to the power of the item's Crafting CR divided by 5. The growth factor of 4 ensures that value inherently quadruples across every tier interval of 5 CR.

> **Value (Credits) = 10 * 4^(CR / 5)**

### **15. Wealth Score and Purchasing Power**

Character economic power is quantified by the **Wealth Score (WS)**, a static rating of economic leverage representing credit rating, active investments, salary, and social capital.

The integration of WS and the TSC operates on **The Golden Rule of Tangent Wealth:** A character may automatically purchase any item or fund any construction project with a Crafting CR equal to or less than their Wealth Score without depleting liquid Credits or reducing their baseline Wealth Score. This aligns the abstraction of "lifestyle" with the concrete math of acquisition: Purchase CR = Crafting CR.

### **15. The Liquidity Gap and Friction Mechanics**

**The Liquidity Constraint (The Gap Rule):**

When a character seeks to build or buy an asset with a CR exceeding their Wealth Score, their passive income cannot cover the transaction. They must bridge the gap with liquid Credits:

> **Cost (Liquid) = Value (Item CR) - Value (Wealth Score)**

*Example:* A Wealth Score 15 character (Auto-buy limit 640 Cr) wants to construct a Secure Frontier Outpost (CR 18, Value 1,472 Cr via 640 * 2.3). They use their WS 15 leverage to cover the base material cost, leaving 832 Credits that must be paid in liquid cash from their adventuring savings or corporate backing.

**Liquidity Drag (The Fence Rate):**

* **Legal goods and registered Real Estate:** Sell at 50% of Total Value.
* **Black market, stolen goods, or unregistered Habs:** Sell at 20-25% of Total Value.
* **Scrap and Salvage:** Sells at 10% of Total Value (requires 5x the item's Credit Value in unrefined scrap mass to meet the 50% crafting material requirement).

### **15. The Productivity Engine and Macro-Economics**

> **Daily Progress (PP) = (Craft Check Result - 10) * Tool Tier Multiplier**

Under this engine, a Mastercraft Arcology Module (CR 30, Target PP = 40,960) would take a hobbyist with Basic Tools (x10 multiplier) over 409 days to build. However, an advanced engineering team utilizing a Nanoforge (x1,000 multiplier) with a high check result can materialize the identical structure in less than two days.

### EUFT & Matrix Systems Audit Retrofits (Canonical Reference)

* **Tangent Standard Curve (Base CR Intervals of 5):** `Value (Credits) = 10 * 4 ^ (CR / 5)`.
* **Linear Micro-Modifiers (+1 to +4 CR):** Non-multiple-of-5 CR adjustments apply linear multipliers to the nearest base 5-CR tier rather than exponential compounding:
  * **+1 CR:** `x1.3` (+30% Base Value)
  * **+2 CR:** `x1.75` (+75% Base Value)
  * **+3 CR:** `x2.3` (+130% Base Value)
  * **+4 CR:** `x3.0` (+200% Base Value)
  * **-1 CR (Flaw):** `x0.75` (-25% Base Value)
* **Crafting Material & Scrap Ratio:** Pristine raw materials cost **50%** of an item's total Credit Value. Unrefined **Scavenged Scrap** retains **10%** value, requiring **5x the item's Credit Value** in raw scrap mass to satisfy the 50% crafting material threshold.
* **Armor Layering Cap:** Damage Resistance (DR) does not stack across biological, cybernetic, and wearable domains; only the single highest applicable DR applies against an incoming damage source.
* **Mecha Scale Multiplier & Garaging:** Structure Points (SP) in vehicle size tables are pre-calculated; apply the active **Scale Multiplier** only to weapon damage, movement speed, and range during tactical play. For garaging in ships and bases, **1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier** (`10 Mounts = 1 Module`; e.g., Medium x1 = 1 Mount / 0.1 Modules; Large x2 = 2 Mounts / 0.2 Modules; Huge x5 = 5 Mounts / 0.5 Modules; Gargantuan x10 = 10 Mounts / 1 Module; Colossal x20 = 20 Mounts / 2 Modules).
