# **1.09 HINDRANCES**

**Hindrances** in Tangent SF RP are personal character flaws, physical disabilities, psychological compulsions, or social liabilities that grant additional **Character Points (CP)** during character creation. Unlike standard Features which cost Character Points, Hindrances **grant** bonus CP during creation—adding narrative depth, realism, and role-playing opportunities while imposing concrete mechanical drawbacks or limitations.

---

## **1. System Overview & Core Hindrance Rules**

### **Design Philosophy**

* **Depth & Realism:** Hindrances flesh out a character's personality, physiology, and history. A character with a phobia, a crippling debt, or a missing limb is often more narrative-rich than a "flawless" operative.
* **Role-Playing Incentives & Architect Involvement:** Each Hindrance provides hooks for the **Architect (Game Master)** to introduce complications, moral dilemmas, and personal stakes. The Architect plays a crucial role in approving Hindrances and ensuring they are meaningful within the campaign setting (for example, a *Fear of Water* Hindrance grants no free points in an arid desert campaign and may be disallowed or reduced in value).
* **Example:** A character with a severe fear of heights (*Acrophobia — Moderate Emotional Issues*) gains **6 CP**, reflecting the significant impact this phobia has on their tactical actions, such as hesitating to climb a communications tower or requiring a **Willpower Save (`CR 15`)** when crossing a narrow chasm bridge.

### **The 10 CP Personal Hindrance Limit & Species Exemption (Canonical Rules)**

* **The 10 CP Personal Hindrance Cap:** While each Hindrance has a specific CP value (`2 CP` to `30 CP`) reflecting its severity, a character may gain a **maximum of `+10 Character Points (CP)`** from personal/typical Hindrances during character creation. Players may assign additional narrative flaws for roleplaying depth, or select a severe single Hindrance (such as *Quadriplegic Disability [18 CP]* or *Severe Emotional Issues [12 CP]*), but the bonus points added to their starting character creation pool cannot exceed **+10 CP** without explicit Architect campaign override (such as a tragic/high-flaw campaign variant capped at `15 CP`).
* **Species-Level Hindrance Exemption:** Innate **species-level disadvantages, biological flaws, and Xeno Stigmas** (`1.03 SPECIES.md` and `../architect/99. MODULAR SPECIES MATRIX.md`) are already factored into the net Species Package CP cost and **do NOT count toward the 10 CP personal Hindrance limit**.

### **Terminology Distinction (`Hindrance` vs. `Disadvantage`)**

* **Hindrance (Character Flaw):** A permanent or semi-permanent character trait chosen during character creation (e.g., *Addiction, Poor, Obligation, Impaired Sense*).
* **Disadvantage (Situational Dice Mechanic):** The core resolution mechanic where a player rolls an extra `d10` per stack and keeps the **lowest two dice** (`2d10` keep lowest 2, the exact inverse of **Advantage**). Several Character Hindrances (such as *Impaired Sense*, *Not-Disguisable Distinctive Features*, or *Major Social Issues*) cause a character to roll specific checks with **Disadvantage**.

---

## **2. Categories of Hindrances**

Hindrances are grouped into seven thematic categories that define the source of the limitation:

1. **Background Hindrances:** Relating to the character's past history, upbringing, or previous entanglements (*Examples: Secret, Adversary, Wanted by the Law, Non-Monetary Debt, Traumatic Past, Unwanted Legacy*).
2. **Mental Hindrances:** Relating to cognitive function, memory, or intellectual barriers (*Examples: Mental Issues, Nightmares, Technologically Impaired, Poor Memory, Paranoia*).
3. **Metaphysical Hindrances:** Relating to magic, psionics, the soul, or supernatural forces (*Examples: Extreme Morality, Unlucky / Karmic Debt, Curses, Divine Disfavor, Haunted, Magical Vulnerability*).
4. **Physical Hindrances:** Relating to the body, health, and sensory capabilities (*Examples: Bodily Age, Disability, Distinctive Features, Impaired Sense, Lost Sense, Mute*).
5. **Psychological Hindrances:** Relating to behavioral patterns, compulsions, and personality restrictions (*Examples: Addiction, Covetous, Emotional Issues, Hatred, Honorable*).
6. **Resource Hindrances:** Relating to assets, wealth, and equipment maintenance (*Examples: Poor / Indebted, Lack of Shelter, Dependency on Rare Substances*).
7. **Social Hindrances:** Relating to standing in society, reputation, and interpersonal relationships (*Examples: Clown, Dependent, Humorless, Minority / Xeno Stigma, Obligation, Social Issues*).
