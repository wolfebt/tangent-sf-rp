* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Math:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`) equals `-10 to Attack/Check Score` + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.

Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum `+10 CP` gained from personal Hindrances during creation; Species-level disadvantages and cultural Stigmas are exempt.

# **REPRESENTATIVE**

A Representative's occupation could involve diplomacy, negotiation, and communication between various factions, planets, or organizations. They might be responsible for maintaining relationships, resolving conflicts, and advocating for their constituents' interests whether they are in a governmental or corporate arena.

A Governmental Representative might be responsible for representing the interests of their constituents in the legislative branch of government. They would do this by drafting and voting on legislation, and by participating in committee hearings and floor debates. They would also be responsible for communicating with their constituents and keeping them updated on the legislative process.

A Corporate Representative might be responsible for representing the interests of their company in dealings with other companies, government agencies, and the public. They would do this by negotiating contracts, resolving disputes, and providing information about the company. They would also be responsible for communicating with the company's employees and keeping them updated on the company's activities.

Representatives play an important role in maintaining order and cooperation between different groups. They use their skills in diplomacy, negotiation, and communication to build relationships, resolve conflicts, and advocate for the interests of their constituents.
