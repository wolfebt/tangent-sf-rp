# Conditions, Status Effects & Severity Rules

Conditions represent physical trauma, sensory impairment, and psychological distress:
- **Bleeding Out**: Suffering 1 Stability damage per turn at 0 HP.
- **Blinded**: Automatic failure on visual checks; 50% miss chance in combat.
- **Crippled**: Limb severed or destroyed; movement halved or items dropped.
- **Disabled**: Limb incapacitated until medical surgery.
- **Flat-Footed**: Denied Agility bonus to defense; vulnerable to Sneak Attacks.
- **Hobbled**: Movement speed reduced by 50%.
- **Incapacitated**: Cannot take actions or reactions.
- **Prone**: Grounded; +2 defense vs ranged, vulnerable vs melee.
- **Sickened**: -2 penalty on all attack rolls, saving throws, and skill checks.
- **Stunned**: Drops held items, cannot take actions, Defense DC reduced by -4.
- **Winded**: Cumulative -2 penalty on physical actions.
