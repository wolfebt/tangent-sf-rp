# **Matter Invocations**

# **Matter Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Matter Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Matter: Enhancement or Matter: Transmutation) for shaping, complexity, and effect resolution.

## 

## 

## **ENHANCEMENT INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Matter

Specialization: Enhancement

Description: Improving the pattern of something. The reinforcement and optimization of existing physical properties.

Standard Duration: 1 Minute per Level (Stage).

Standard Effect: Add up to 5 Character Points (CP) worth of traits, stats, or features to a target per Stage.

### 

### **REINFORCED ITEM**

Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Buff\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch (Personal Item)  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** Scene  
* **Resistance:** None

**Effect Description:** A chosen personal item is improved by 5% per Enhancement Skill Rank. This can manifest as increased Toughness (Structure/Hardness), Damage output, or general efficiency.

### 

### **STONESKIN**

Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Defense | \[Buff\] \[Physical\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Toughens the skin of the target, granting it the texture and durability of stone or metal.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Integrity:** Grants DR 5 \+ (2 per Stage).  
* **CP Value:** Roughly equivalent to 5 CP of Armor/DR traits per Stage.

### 

### **ARCANE LOCK**

Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Utility | \[Security\] \[Seal\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Door, Chest, or Portal  
* **Duration:** Permanent (until dispelled/broken)  
* **Resistance:** None (CR to break)

Effect Description:

Magically secures an object, fusing the materials at the molecular level.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Security:** Break/Lockpick CR increases by \+5 per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** Object gains \+10 Hardness per Stage.

### 

### **PROTECTION**

## **TRANSMUTATION INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Matter

Specialization: Transmutation

Description: Altering the pattern of something. The fundamental alteration of physical form and substance.

Standard Duration: 1 Minute per Level (Stage).

Standard Effect: Manipulate up to 5 Character Points (CP) worth of traits or material volume per Stage.

### 

### **ANALYZE STRUCTURE**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Sensory | \[Cantrip\] \[Analysis\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Touch/Sight  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Analyzes an object's physical structure and elemental composition. Determines weak points, alloy mixtures, and purity.

### 

### **VERSATILE ITEM**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Polymorph\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch (Personal Item)  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Changes a chosen personal item into another item of the same cost or lower. (e.g., Turning a Knife into a Screwdriver, or a Rope into a Net).

### 

### **ENLARGE**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Buff | \[Size\] \[Growth\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates if unwilling)

Effect Description:

Increases the target's size category.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Magnitude:** Increases Size by 1 Category per 2 Stages (Max \+2).  
* **Power:** Strength Bonus \= \+2 per Category increased.

### 

### **REDUCE**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Utility/Debuff | \[Size\] \[Shrink\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Decreases the target's size category.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Magnitude:** Decreases Size by 1 Category per 2 Stages.  
* **Defense:** AC/Stealth Bonus \= \+2 per Category decreased.

### 

### **MORPH**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Utility | \[Polymorph\] \[Disguise\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** Personal  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Transforms the caster physically into another form (humanoid, animal, or object).

**Scaling Function:**

* **Complexity:** Allowed Forms expand per Stage (Humanoid \-\> Beast \-\> Object \-\> Elemental).  
* **Features:** Gains 1 Trait of the new form per Stage (e.g., Scent, Swim Speed) up to the CP limit (5 CP/Stage).

### 

### **PETRIFICATION**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Attack | \[Save or Die\] \[State Change\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 25  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Permanent  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Transmutes organic flesh into solid stone.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Severity:** Save CR increases by \+1 per Stage.  
* **Speed:** At Stage 1-3, takes 1d4 rounds to complete (Slowed condition). At Stage 4+, instant.

### 

### **GRAFTING**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Healing/Utility | \[Fusion\] \[Modification\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** 1 Hour (Ritual)  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Permanent  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Attaches or integrates a foreign object or limb onto a living body.

**Scaling Function:**

* **Complexity:** Reduces Rejection Penalty/Damage by 2 per Stage.  
* **Utility:** Allows grafting of higher TL items (TL 3 \-\> TL 4 \-\> TL 5\) per Stage.

### 

### **MUTATION**

Classification: Matter (Transmutation) | Buff/Utility | \[Alteration\] \[Biology\]

Parameters:

* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)

Effect Description:

Alters the physical form to produce a specific biological enhancement (gills, claws).

**Scaling Function:**

* **Magnitude:** Natural Weapon Damage \= 1d6 per Stage.  
* **Quantity:** Number of Mutations \= 1 per Stage (Max 5 CP worth per Stage).
