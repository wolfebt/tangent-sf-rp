# Ranged Combat, Automatic Fire & Suppression

Automatic weapons sacrifice ammunition and recoil control for devastating volume of fire. All range penalties are **Doubled** during full auto fire.

## 1. Short Burst (3-Round Burst)
- Grants a **+1 Strike Bonus** to hit without suffering recoil penalties.

## 2. Full Auto (10+ Rounds)
- **Recoil Penalty**: -1 Strike per 10 rounds fired.
- **Iterative Hits**: For every point the attack roll exceeds the target's Defense DC, **one additional bullet hits** (up to total rounds fired).
- **Burst Damage**: The initial bullet deals weapon base damage. Each subsequent hit adds **+1d Damage** (provided initial shot penetrated DR).

## 3. Area Suppression & Blast Zones
- **General Area Suppression**: Firing a cone or hallway. Targets within the zone suffer **+2 Damage Dice** for every 5 points the attack roll exceeds DC 10.
- **Focused Blast Zone**: Concentrated fire on a 5ft grid. Targets suffer **+100% Damage Dice** for every 10 points over DC 15.
