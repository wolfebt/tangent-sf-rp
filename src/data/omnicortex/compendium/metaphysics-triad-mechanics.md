# The Metaphysic Triad: Attune, Discipline & Invocation

## Potency & Effect Formula
```text
Effect Potency = Key Ability Modifier + Discipline Skill Rank + Invocation Level + 10 (or d20 roll)
```

- **Attune Check**: Determines the DC of resistance saves or the Strike attack roll.
- **Discipline Check**: Determines the severity of status effects, duration, and damage dice.
- **Key Abilities**: Intellect (Arcane / Psychic), Wisdom (Divine / Cosmic / Nature), or Charisma (Bardic / Innate).
