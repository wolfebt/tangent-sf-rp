# Feature Taxonomy & Mechanics

Features in Tangent SF RP fall into 4 specialized mechanical classifications:

## 1. Ranked Features
- **Stacking Purchases**: Ranked Features may be purchased multiple times, with their numeric bonuses or capacities stacking.
- **Rank Limits**: The maximum rank a character may purchase is strictly governed by their associated **Ability Score** (e.g., Stamina for *Great Fortitude*, Charisma for *Inspiring Personality*) or associated **Skill Stage** (1 at Novice, 2 at Trained, 3 at Expert, 4 at Master, 5 at Pinnacle).

## 2. Multiple Choice Features
- **Distinct Selections**: Features tagged **[Multiple]** must be chosen for a specific skill, weapon group, sense, saving throw, energy type, or field of study.
- **Independent Stacks**: You may purchase the feature multiple times, selecting a different sub-option each time (e.g., *Weapon Focus: Swords*, *Weapon Focus: Pistols*).

## 3. Progression Trees
- **Linear Upgrades**: Multi-tier feature chains (e.g., *Two-Weapon Fighting -> Double Slice -> Improved Two-Weapon Fighting -> Greater Two-Weapon Fighting -> Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting*).
- **Replacement vs. Additive**: Certain higher tiers replace the lower tier's bonus (e.g., *Centering Master* replaces *Centering Expert*), while others add incremental dice or attacks.

## 4. Special & Storyline Features
- **Narrative Prerequisites**: Marked **[Special]**; requires GM approval, specific background events, origin milestones, or cybernetic/genetic transformation.
