# **04.03.01 DRAGONS CODEX (TRUE DRAGONS, DRAGONKIN & DRACONIC FLIGHT)**

## **I. OVERVIEW: THE ANCIENT PROGENITORS OF THE DYNASTY**

In the Tangent universe, **Dragons** are not mindless beasts of myth; they are an ancient, hyper-intelligent, long-lived sapient species possessing innate mastery over the **Resonance Field** and cellular shapechanging. Over seventeen centuries ago, True Dragons established the **Dracon Dynasty**, uplifting and interbreeding with humanity to create the **Draconic (Dragon-Blooded)** noble houses and the **Draconian** martial/arcane order.

True Dragons possess two natural biological forms—their true **Dragon Form** and an innate humanoid **Alternate Form**—and are classified into six primary elemental/metaphysical lineages (**Aurum, Argent, Cuprum, Obsidian, Verdant, and Lapis**) plus rare **Variants**.

## **II. THE SIX GREAT DRAGON LINEAGES & VARIANTS**

Each Dragon lineage governs a primary **Attribute Score (`+2`)**, an innate **Energy Type** (dictating its *Energy Immunity* and *Dragon Breath*), a **20-Point Lineage Skill Package**, an innate **Awakened Metaphysic Discipline**, and a specialized **Sensory Mode**:

| Lineage | Known As | Attribute (`+2`) | Energy Type & Breath Shape | Lineage Skills (20 SP) | Awakened Discipline | Signature Sensory Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **AURUM** | Gold / Bronze | **Charisma** | **Pyro** *(Fire Spit [Line] or Flaming Spray [Cone])* | Bluff, Intimidate, Diplomacy, Knowledge (Any) | **Energy** | **Thermal Sight** *(Heat-Based)* |
| **ARGENT** | Silver / White | **Intellect** | **Cryo** *(Ice Shards [Line] or Freezing Mist [Cone])* | Academics, Knowledge (Culture), Alertness, Knowledge (Any) | **Mental** | **Ethersight** *(Psionic-Based)* |
| **CUPRUM** | Copper / Red | **Wisdom** | **Voltic** *(Lightning Arc [Line] or Cascade [Cone])* | Knowledge (Culture), Diplomacy, Alertness, Vocation (Any) | **Illusion** | **Blindsense** *(Electroreception)* |
| **OBSIDIAN** | Night / Black | **Agility** | **Any One Energy** *(Chosen at creation)* | Acrobatics, Alertness, Stealth, Combat (Any) | **Dimension** | **Ethersight** *(Dimensional-Based)* |
| **VERDANT** | Forest / Green | **Stamina** | **Corrosive** *(Acidic Gas [Cone] or Caustic Spray [Line])* | Knowledge (Medicine), Alertness, Knowledge (Survival), Expression | **Entropy** | **Scent** *(Biological-Based)* |
| **LAPIS** | Earth / Blue | **Strength** | **Sonic** *(Shattering Bark [Cone] or Roaring Shockwave [Line])* | Athletics, Alertness, Knowledge (Survival), Combat (Any) | **Matter** | **Blindsense** *(Echolocation)* |
| **VARIANTS** | Mixed / Offshoots | **Any One (`+2`)** | **Any One Energy** *(Chosen at creation)* | Any 4 Chosen Skills | **Any 1 Discipline** | Lineage-dependent |

## **III. FULL-BLOODED DRAGON SPECIES PACKAGE & PREREQUISITES (112 CP MINIMUM)**

While **Draconic Humans** (15 CP) and **Draconians** (23–28 CP) are standard playable species packages, constructing a **Full-Blooded Adolescent Dragon** requires the **46 CP Advanced Species Base** plus the purchase of all **12 Dragonkin Features** and their Attribute prerequisites (**Minimum Strength 4 and Stamina 2**), totaling a minimum of **112 CP**:

### **1. Mandatory Prerequisites & Core Package Breakdown (112 CP Total)**

- **Required Minimum Attributes (30 CP):**
  - **Strength 4** (20 CP at 5 CP per +1)
  - **Stamina 2** (10 CP at 5 CP per +1)
- **Base Dragon Species Chassis (46 CP):**
  - **Dragon Type Chassis (5 CP):** *Darksight 60 ft*, *Low-Light Vision*, and **Immunity to magical sleep and paralysis effects**.
  - **Lineage Attribute Bonus (10 CP):** **+2** to the lineage's primary Attribute Score (*Aurum: CHA, Argent: INT, Cuprum: WIS, Obsidian: AGI, Verdant: STA, Lapis: STR* — 5 CP per +1).
  - **Lineage Skill Package (20 CP):** **20 Skill Points** distributed across the lineage's 4 signature skill groups (5 CP per 5 Ranks / 1 CP per Rank).
  - **Innate Awakening (2 CP Recommended):** **Awakened** in the lineage's signature Metaphysic Discipline.
  - **Lineage Sensory Trait (2 CP):** *Thermal Sight*, *Ethersight*, *Blindsense*, or *Scent*.
  - **Exoskeleton (Partial + Light) (3 CP):** Scales granting `DR = Strength Score * 2` (`DR 8` at STR 4).
  - **Energy Resistance x2 (4 CP):** **DR 20** against the lineage's Energy Type (prerequisite for *Energy Immunity*).
- **Complete Dragonkin Feature Suite (36 CP — 12 Features at Recommended/Elite Costs):**
  1. **Alternate Form (2 CP):** Innate humanoid form; does not involuntarily revert if shapechange effects are disrupted.
  2. **Brutal (2 CP):** Claws, fangs, and horns **double the Strength damage bonus** on natural attacks (Lethal Slashing, Piercing, or Blunt).
  3. **Energy Immunity (4 CP Elite):** Complete **Immunity** to the lineage's Energy Type (*Req: Stamina 2, DR 20 vs. Energy*).
  4. **Natural Weapons (2 CP):** Bite (`1d8`), Claws (`1d6`), Tail Slap (`1d6`), and Gore (`1d8`), scaled by Size Category.
  5. **Tail (1 CP):** Grants +2 to Trip and Acrobatics (Balance) checks and functions as a natural weapon.
  6. **Dragon Eyes (2 CP):** Enhanced spectrum vision (*Low-Light, Darksight, Thermal/Ethersight*).
  7. **Dragon Senses (2 CP):** Rolls all **Alertness checks with Advantage** (*Req: Alertness 11*).
  8. **Dragon Mind (2 CP):** Rolls all **Mental Resistance / Willpower Saves with Advantage** (*Req: Wisdom 2*).
  9. **Dragon Might (2 CP):** Lifts, carries, and grapples as **1 Size Category larger** (doubled weight capacity and +4 Stability; *Req: Strength 4*).
  10. **Dragon Wings (4 CP Elite):** Leathery draconic wings granting **Fly Speed = `3x Ground Speed`** (*Req: Exoskeleton*).
  11. **Dragon Breath (4 CP Elite):** **30 ft Cone (10 ft wide)** or **50–60 ft Line** of lineage energy dealing **`(Strength Score)d8` damage** (**Reflex Save CR = `10 + Stamina + Stage`** for half).
  12. **Dragon Form (2 CP) + Ageless / Longevity (4 CP) + Exoskeleton (Heavy) (3 CP):** Assumes **Large-sized Dragon Form** (+2 STR, -2 AGI, -2 Combat Mod, `x2` Damage Dice, `x2` Speed, +4 Stability) with Heavy Scales (`DR = Strength * 3`).

## **V. DRAGONKIN HYBRIDS & DRACONIC SUB-SPECIES**

*\* Wyrms, Drakes, and Wyverns may also be bonded via the **Companion** feature (`99. MODULAR COMPANION MATRIX.md`)—serving as both a loyal cohort and a flying/ground mount if Large or Huge.*

- **Draconic (Human Hybrid — 15 CP):** Legendary Champion Knights and noble houses of the Dracon Dynasty (`operator/species/Humans/1.03.Humans.Draconic.md`).
- **Draconian (Alterian Hybrid — 23–28 CP):** Philosophers, arcane researchers, and martial scholars of the Draconian Order (`operator/species/Aeld/1.03.Aeld.Draconian.md`).
- **Sa Sa Si (Vassoth Hybrid):** Serpentine-draconic defenders of the Garden (*"Silver Creche"* of Entari Prime).
- **Wyrms `*` (Small or Medium Non-Shifting Dragons):**
  - **Size:** Medium or Small (non-shifting).
  - **Intellect:** Mid to High sapience (`0` to `+4` Intellect).
  - **Movement:** Winged Flight (`3x Ground Speed`), strong climbers and swimmers. Frequently mistaken for young True Dragons.
- **Drakes `*` (Large Bestial Non-Shifting Dragons):**
  - **Size:** Large (`x2` base speed and damage dice; non-shifting).
  - **Intellect:** Mid to Low animal/semi-sapient intelligence (`0` to `-4` Intellect).
  - **Movement:** Quick (`+10 ft` base ground speed) and Full-Speed Vertical Climbing, **OR** Winged Flight (`3x Ground Speed`).
- **Wyverns `*` (Large Pseudo-Drakes with Venomous Stinger):**
  - **Physiology:** Bipedal pseudo-drakes with no separate forelimbs, utilizing semi-manipulative bat-like wings and a **Stinger-Tipped Tail (Paralytic Neurotoxic Venom)**.
  - **Size:** Large (`x2` base speed and damage dice; non-shifting).
  - **Intellect:** Mid to Low intelligence (`0` to `-4` Intellect).
  - **Movement:** Quick (`+10 ft` base speed) and Climbing, **OR** Winged Flight (`3x Ground Speed`).

## **VI. DRACONIC FLIGHT MECHANICS & AERIAL PACES**

Draconic wings are significantly larger than typical avian wings, supported by dense thoracic musculature, solid mineralized bones, and reinforced patagia—making Dragons and their winged kin apex aerial flyers.

- **Base Ground Speed:** Medium = `30 ft/round` (`6 ft/sec` or `3.41 mph`); Large (`x2`) = `60 ft/round`; Huge (`x5`) = `150 ft/round`.
- **Dragon Wings Multiplier (`3x Ground Speed`):** Triples base ground speed to **90 ft** (Medium), **180 ft** (Large), and **450 ft** (Huge).

### **1. Master Draconic Flight Speed Table (`Speed * 0.1136 = mph`)**

| Aerial Pace | Speed Multiplier | Medium (`x1`) | Large (`x2`) | Huge (`x5`) | Stealth / Precision Mod* | Required Acrobatics Check |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Hover / Controlled Descent** | `0.5x Base` or less | 45 ft (~5 mph) | 90 ft (~10 mph) | 225 ft (~25 mph) | +0 | **Acrobatics CR 15** |
| **Gliding** *(Slowest Descent)* | Optimal Glide | 90 ft (10 mph) | 180 ft (20 mph) | 450 ft (50 mph) | **+2 Bonus** | **Acrobatics CR 10** |
| **Flight** *(Standard Cruise)* | `1x Fly Speed` (`3x Ground`) | **90 ft (10 mph)** | **180 ft (20 mph)** | **450 ft (50 mph)** | +0 | None |
| **Sail** *(Hurried Pace)* | `4x Fly Speed` (`5x` w/ *Soar*) | **360 ft (40 mph)** | **720 ft (80 mph)** | **1,800 ft (200 mph)** | **-2 Penalty** | Sustained check / min |
| **Soar** *(Fast Overland Pace)* | `8x Fly Speed` (`9x` w/ *Soar*) | **720 ft (80 mph)** | **1,440 ft (160 mph)** | **3,600 ft (400 mph)** | **-4 Penalty** | **Acrobatics CR 10** / min |
| **Diving** *(Requires Altitude)* | `2x Current Speed` | **up to 1,440 ft (160 mph)** | **up to 2,880 ft (320 mph)** | **up to 7,200 ft (800 mph)** | **-4 Penalty** *(cumulative)* | **Acrobatics CR (Base - 10)** |

### **2. Aerial Fatigue, High Ground & Diving Ram Rules**

- **Aerial Stamina & Fatigue Checks:** Acrobatics checks during sustained high-speed flight (Sail, Soar, Dive, Hover) are rolled once per minute (or per round in extreme turbulence) to ward off muscle strain. On a failure, the flyer suffers **1 point of non-lethal Vitality damage per 5 points missed**, followed by a **Willpower Save** against the same CR. If flight cannot be maintained, the flyer must execute a **Controlled Descent (CR 15)** or begin an uncontrolled plummet from current altitude with a cumulative `-1` penalty per subsequent recovery check.
- * **Precision & Stealth Modifiers:** Apply to all checks involving subtlety, Stealth, fine manipulation, or ranged precision while flying at that pace.
- **The *Soar* Feature:** Increases the speed multiplier of Sail and Soar by `+1` (to `5x` and `9x` Flight Speed) without increasing the precision penalty.
- **High Ground Advantage:** Any airborne combatant attacking a ground-bound target from superior altitude gains the **High Ground Bonus (`+2 Strike / +2 Critical Confirmation`)**.
- **Aerial Diving Rams:** A Ram executed by a flying Dragon or creature inflicts **+`1d` bonus damage per active Flight Stage** (*Flight +1d, Sail +2d, Soar +3d, Dive +4d*) **plus `+1 Impact Damage per 10 ft of current Speed`** to all participants (Attacker and Target[s]; vehicular Crash rules apply on structural failure).

### **Lineage Apotheosis Masteries**

- **Aurum Apotheosis (Solar Majesty):** Radiates a 30 ft *Aura of Command & Awe*; allies gain +2 morale bonus to attacks and saves, while enemies suffer Disadvantage on attacks against the Aurum Dragon unless they pass a Willpower Save.
- **Argent Apotheosis (Cryo-Psionic Sovereign):** Telepathic range expands to planetary scale; *Cryo* breath weapon additionally forces a **Willpower Save** or freezes the target's mental action economy (**Stunned / Slowed**).
- **Cuprum Apotheosis (Living Storm Conduit):** Can transform into a bolt of living lightning to teleport up to its Fly Speed as a Move Action, dealing `3d8` Voltic damage to all creatures along the line.
- **Obsidian Apotheosis (Void-Phase Wyrm):** Can shift **Semi-Corporeal (`DR 30` vs. physical)** as a Swift Action and step through dimensional shadows (*Blink / Long Step*) at will.
- **Verdant Apotheosis (Bio-Regenerative Progenitor):** Gains true **Regeneration** (5 Health per round) and can exhale a restorative *Order* mist that heals allies for `(Stamina)d8` Health instead of dealing Corrosive damage.
- **Lapis Apotheosis (Tectonic Titan):** Immune to all forced movement, petrification, and physical stunning; *Sonic* breath weapon shatters fortifications (**double Siege damage** to structures and armor DR).
