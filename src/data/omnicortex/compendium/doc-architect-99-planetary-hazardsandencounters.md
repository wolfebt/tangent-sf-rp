### **9.2 Adventure Hooks by World Type**

* **Agricultural: Crop Theft/Sabotage**  
  * *Setting:* Vast, open-air fields or sealed, massive greenhouses covering kilometers of the surface. Corporate security patrols are stretched thin. The atmosphere is tense, with locals viewing the corp security as oppressors.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to track the specific poison/saboteur (a rival scientist or a disgruntled employee). The theft is a false flag, meant to frame a local dissident group. The stolen crops are being used to feed an underground refugee camp.  
* **Agri-World: Bio-Threat**  
  * *Setting:* A lush, engineered jungle where the mutated forest is aggressively expanding, its carnivorous vines and spore-sacs creating a moving wall of danger. The corporate compound is the last safe haven, but resources are dwindling.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs need to retrieve the original, non-mutated seed bank from a facility now deep inside the forest. The mutation was intentional, a failed attempt at creating a bioweapon. A scientist believes they can reverse the mutation but needs a rare enzyme only found in the heart of the growth.  
* **Asteroid: Collision Warning**  
  * *Setting:* A high-tech, sparsely populated orbital habitat or a deep-space factory carved into a major asteroid. Life is regimented and focused on the work. The uncharted object is a fast-moving, massive chunk of ice or rock, its trajectory calculated to be a direct hit in under 72 hours.  
  * *Hooks:* The PC's main thrusters for the asteroid are sabotaged/offline, requiring a dangerous EVA to fix. The "uncharted object" is actually a cloaked, automated Progenitor defense platform, and diverting it will break a major treaty. A faction secretly wants the collision to destroy a rival's operations on the asteroid.  
* **Barren: First Contact**  
  * *Setting:* A desolate, wind-scoured world with little atmosphere, forcing all life into shielded, underground domes or heavily armored surface vehicles. The signal originates from a deep, unexplored cavern network.  
  * *Hooks:* The signal is a distress call from an ancient species, but following it leads to a deadly trap. The signal is causing mass hallucinations or paranoia in the nearest habitat. A rival corporation is already trying to weaponize or silence the source.  
* **Desert: Lost Colony**  
  * *Setting:* Endless dunes and massive, unpredictable sandstorms. Extreme heat during the day, freezing cold at night. The ruins are only accessible during a rare, temporary lull in the weather, and time is running out.  
  * *Hooks:* The pre-spaceflight tech is powered by a dangerous, unstable energy source. The colony was not lost, but intentionally buried to contain a dangerous artifact/entity. A local, isolationist nomad tribe views the ruins as sacred and is hostile to outsiders.  
* **Fluid Oceans: Geyser Disaster**  
  * *Setting:* A planet with a deep, pressurized liquid surface, making the habitat domes the only solid ground. The eruption is massive, sending scalding, corrosive fluid hundreds of meters high, threatening to breach the thin-shelled domes.  
  * *Hooks:* The "geyser" is not natural, but triggered by deep-sea seismic drilling. The PCs must use a small submersible to place a seal/explosive charge deep underwater. The breach is intentional, designed to expose a specific person or secret inside the domes.  
* **Garden: Political Intrigue**  
  * *Setting:* A paradise world, meticulously terraformed with perfect climate control and stunning aesthetics, masking deep social inequalities. The revolutionary is a charismatic figure giving fiery speeches in public squares, their message spreading rapidly through the populace and threatening the comfortable elite.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to either protect the current governor from the revolutionary's followers or to extract the revolutionary safely before the corp/government silences them. The revolutionary is secretly being funded by a rival system/corp. The populace is being unknowingly manipulated by a powerful mind-control drug in the air/water supply.  
* **High Pop: Cyborg Riots**  
  * *Setting:* Densely packed, vertical cities of billions, where the workforce is overwhelmingly synthetic. The riots are coordinated, brutal, and focused on strategic infrastructure targets. The human military/police are overwhelmed.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must infiltrate the main android assembly plant to shut down the "sentience signal." A former scientist/programmer is trying to help the cyborgs achieve full recognition, and the PCs must decide to assist or stop them. The cyborgs are only rebelling because they were infected by a powerful alien AI.  
* **High Tech: Espionage**  
  * *Setting:* A pristine, heavily secured corporate research campus—all glass, chrome, and hidden surveillance. Gen-E is a project involving advanced genetic manipulation, and the escaped subject is both incredibly valuable and highly dangerous, blending seamlessly into the population.  
  * *Hooks:* The Syndicate lab is trying to cover up the subject's escape, making the PCs' mission unofficial and unsupported. The prototype is not a monster, but a child who needs protection from the Syndicate. A third-party black market organization is also hunting the subject for their own nefarious purposes.  
* **Ice-Capped: Deep Ice Horror**  
  * *Setting:* A world of endless glaciers and sub-zero temperatures. Geothermal stations drill miles into the ice sheet. The awakened entity is causing unexplained power failures and strange signals from the depths.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must venture down the unstable drill shaft to find the creature or shut down the defense system before it targets the main colony. The creature is not hostile, but terrified, and is trying to communicate a warning. The Progenitor defense system is only active because the colony is violating an ancient territorial boundary.  
* **Industrial: Pollution Crisis**  
  * *Setting:* A smog-choked, eternally twilight world dominated by immense, belching factories and pipes. The air is toxic, and the populace is confined to breathing apparatuses. The "plague" is a fast-acting neurological virus.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must retrieve evidence from the factory's core to prove the corporate negligence, but the facility is locked down. The "plague" is not a side effect of pollution but a deliberate, slow-acting bioweapon to cull the workforce. The cure exists, but it's held by a black market cartel demanding an impossible price.  
* **Low Pop: Missing Persons**  
  * *Setting:* A remote, rustic, frontier-style planet with a handful of scattered settlements. Communication is unreliable. The vanishings are silent, leaving no trace.  
  * *Hooks:* The lone predator is a hyper-evolved chameleon-like alien that has been feasting on the population. The hidden cult is sacrificing people to an entity or artifact. The "missing people" are actually being secretly abducted by a government agency for a hidden purpose.  
* **Low Tech: Witch Hunt**  
  * *Setting:* A medieval-level or retro-futuristic society with strong superstition and minimal technology. The phenomenon (e.g., a psychic or psionic power) is creating real, destructive effects (fires, localized storms, visions).  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs, with their advanced tech, are mistaken for the 'demons' or 'witches' and become targets. The source of the phenomenon is an untrained youth whose powers are manifesting chaotically. The local religious/political leader is secretly using a small piece of lost tech to fake the 'magic' to consolidate their power.  
* **Mining World: Labor Dispute**  
  * *Setting:* A deep, unstable network of tunnels and shafts beneath the surface. The air is thick with dust and tension. The artifact is radiating a madness-inducing field, causing miners to turn violently on each other.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must enter the deepest, most dangerous shaft to contain the artifact, but the corporate security detail is simultaneously trying to seize it, causing a three-way conflict. The madness is actually a form of hyper-accelerated evolution or collective psychic awakening, not true insanity. The artifact is the key to unlocking the planet's actual, hidden purpose.  
* **Non-Agriculture: Resource War**  
  * *Setting:* A desolate, mostly empty planet with a few heavily fortified outposts near the single, viable resource (e.g., the last clear underground lake or a vein of rare earth metals). The two factions are using scavenged, old-world military tech.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to broker a peace treaty, but one side is actively sabotaging the negotiations. The resource is not water/metal, but an element that stabilizes an ancient space gate, and controlling it is the true prize. A third, larger power is waiting for the two factions to weaken each other before moving in to seize the resource.  
* **Non-Industrial: Bandit Fortress**  
  * *Setting:* A quiet, low-development world used by the gang because it has vast, unpoliced areas (canyons, dense forests, abandoned military bases). The fortress is a well-defended, camouflaged stronghold with multiple escape routes.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must find a way to disable the gang's main communication/defense array without alerting them. The gang is using the fortress to hold a major political/corporate hostage. The "bandits" are actually former freedom fighters who turned to crime out of desperation after a corporate takeover.  
* **Poor: Debt Slavery**  
  * *Setting:* Gritty, rundown urban sectors or dilapidated settlements. The air is heavy with desperation and fear. The corporate/crime boss's enforcers are omnipresent and brutal. The debt is impossible to repay due to predatory interest rates.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must find the original debt contracts in a secure vault to nullify them. The crime boss is using the indebted populace to work on an illegal, secret project. The PCs are hired by a rival corp not for altruistic reasons, but to destabilize the current power structure.  
* **Quarantine: Extraction**  
  * *Setting:* The surface of Omin II is a hostile, storm-wracked landscape, crisscrossed with automated turrets and patrols. The crash site is rapidly being compromised by the Phage virus, which turns living tissue into a mobile, fungal-like growth.  
  * *Hooks:* The VIP is infected and showing early symptoms; the PCs must extract them before the change is complete. The turrets are controlled by a rogue AI that needs to be deactivated first. The Phage virus is not natural; the VIP crash-landed to deliberately release it on the planet.  
* **Rich / Mining: Assassination Plot**  
  * *Setting:* Opulent, high-security penthouses and exclusive social clubs, juxtaposed with the dark, profitable mining operations below. The governor/CEO is a major figure, and their death would send shockwaves through the system.  
  * *Hooks:* The assassin is one of the PC's trusted contacts, and the group must choose between friendship and the mission. The plot is an inside job, orchestrated by the governor's own family/board of directors. The "assassination" is a ruse, a plan to fake the target's death and allow them to escape to a new life.  
* **Vacuum: Space Hulk**  
  * *Setting:* The immense derelict ship is an unstable environment—pitch-black, riddled with hull breaches, fluctuating gravity, and deadly debris. Navigation is a nightmare. The Space Hulk is rumored to be haunted.  
  * *Hooks:* The riches are guarded by a surviving, heavily armed security team that went mad decades ago. The "deadly threat" is a rapidly multiplying, aggressive swarm of alien parasites. The Space Hulk contains a fully operational, but highly unstable, FTL drive that a faction desperately needs.  
* **Water World: Monster**  
  * *Setting:* Cities that float on the deep ocean surface, connected by bridges and submersibles. The Jorni variant is massive, capable of crushing an entire city module. The available "Jaeger" defenses are old, under-maintained models.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs need to pilot an old Jaeger or find a way to lure the Jorni away from the cities. The monster is not an animal, but a heavily engineered bio-weapon that has gone rogue. The only way to stop it is to retrieve a sonic dampener from a sunken, older city.  
* **Ice World: Env. Failure**  
  * *Setting:* A massive, sealed habitat dome on a perpetually frozen planet. The sabotage is total, and the massive primary heating arrays are offline. Frost is rapidly creeping up the inner walls, and essential systems are failing.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs have to traverse the frozen, unstable service tunnels to find the terrorists and restore power before the main reactor freezes over. The "terrorists" are actually a rescue team trying to expose a faulty life support system that was already slowly killing the population. The sabotage was triggered by a corporate rival to force the colony to relocate.

### **9.3 Encounter Tables (d10)**

**1\. Planetary Research Station Encounter** \[Tags: Research Origin, High-Tech (Ht), Ascendancy or Syndicate\]

1. System Malfunction: Life Support or power grid failure due to sabotage, weather, or an unknown invasive lifeform.  
2. Rogue Experiment: A containment breach of a genetically engineered creature or a powerful, sentient AI.  
3. Deserter/Saboteur: A disgruntled scientist or security officer is attempting to steal sensitive data or destroy the station.  
4. Supply Drop/Resupply Ship: An expected or unexpected vessel arrives, bringing new personnel, needed supplies, or an unwelcome corporate auditor.  
5. Ancient Artifact Discovery: Dig team unearths a Progenitor device or alien relic, causing strange psychic or physical effects.  
6. Xeno-Contagion: A newly discovered or recently mutated virus/parasite is spreading through the station population.  
7. Unsettling Silence: The communications array goes quiet; the exterior hatches are sealed, and no one answers the intercoms.  
8. Corporate Takeover: A rival corporation's security team arrives unannounced to seize control of the station's research or resources.  
9. Geological Event: A sudden seismic shift or volcanic eruption threatens the station's structural integrity.  
10. Pre-Collapse Data Core: Finding a hidden, intact data archive containing invaluable secrets, maps, or schematics.

&nbsp;

**2\. Corporate Citadel/Skyscraper Encounter** \[Tags: Urban Origin, Syndicate (Gov 1), High-Tech (Ht)\]

1. Executive Bodyguard Detail: A heavily armed, unyielding security team patrols the floor, enforcing a strict lockdown or search procedure.  
2. Hacker Intrusion: Systems are running erratically due to an outside or inside cyberattack targeting critical financial or research data.  
3. Covert Meeting: PCs stumble upon a clandestine meeting between rival executives or a corrupt official and a criminal element.  
4. Synthetic Rebellion: A group of labor or domestic androids have reached sentience and are coordinating a quiet revolt.  
5. Drone Swarm Malfunction: A fleet of security or maintenance drones goes haywire, attacking personnel indiscriminately.  
6. Political Assassin: A skilled killer is loose in the building, targeting a high-profile individual the PCs must protect or apprehend.  
7. Data Heist in Progress: Another group of thieves or mercenaries is attempting to steal the same objective as the PCs.  
8. Internal Audit/Interrogation: A brutal internal affairs team is questioning employees about a leak, possibly implicating the PCs.  
9. Vertical Emergency: A sudden power surge or sabotage traps the PCs in a high-speed elevator or collapses a skybridge.  
10. A Glimpse of the Underbelly: A hidden vent, access panel, or maintenance shaft leads to the ignored, impoverished, or dangerous lower levels.

&nbsp;

**3\. Frontier Mining Colony Encounter** \[Tags: Colony/Industrial Origin, Asteroid (As), Outworlds (Gov 0)\]

1. Tunnel Collapse: A mining accident traps workers/equipment, requiring immediate, dangerous rescue efforts.  
2. Claim Jumpers: Heavily armed bandits attempt to seize control of the most profitable mine or resource deposit.  
3. Atmospheric Contamination: A poisonous gas leak or dust storm makes the surface hazardous, requiring a scramble for sealed shelter.  
4. Mutated Fauna: Local wildlife, adapted to the harsh mining environment, has become aggressive and is attacking the outpost.  
5. Shipment Ambush: A vital transport ship is attacked en route to or from the colony, necessitating an immediate recovery mission.  
6. Water Scarcity: The primary water recycler or reservoir fails, leading to desperation and potential conflict among the colonists.  
7. Labor Strike: Mine workers, fed up with conditions, stage a violent protest, blocking access to the mines or control center.  
8. Alien Signal Interference: Strange energy pulses from the ground or a distant celestial body disrupt communications and sensor readings.  
9. A Desperate Trader: A lone merchant arrives, selling extremely valuable, but possibly cursed or illegal, resources/technology.  
10. Local Sheriff/Warlord: The true power in the colony demands a steep "tax" or confiscates a PC's valuable equipment.

&nbsp;

**4\. Orbital Station/Habitation Encounter** \[Tags: Vacuum (Va), Asteroid (As), Trade Hubs\]

1. Hull Breach: A micrometeoroid or stray debris causes a catastrophic leak, requiring immediate sealing and zero-G movement.  
2. Psychic Disturbance: A sudden, shared hallucination or panic attack spreads among the station's crew/populace.  
3. Docking Bay Mishap: A massive transport ship attempts an uncontrolled docking, threatening to cripple the station.  
4. Infestation: A fast-breeding biological or mechanical pest is clogging vents and disrupting life support.  
5. The Black Market: PCs stumble upon a hidden area being used for illegal gambling, smuggling, or illicit body modifications.  
6. Station Security Lockdown: All internal hatches seal due to a perceived threat, isolating the PCs from their objective or ship.  
7. Sabotage by Rival Faction: An operative is attempting to remotely detonate a critical component or steal a high-value asset.  
8. Artificial Gravity Failure: The entire area suddenly goes into zero-G, complicating combat and movement.  
9. Lost Drone: A massive, powerful defense or construction drone has broken free of its programming and is patrolling erratically.  
10. Diplomatic Crisis: Two opposing faction ambassadors meet on the station, and the PCs are caught in the middle of a tense negotiation that turns hostile.

&nbsp;

**5\. Jungle/Swamp Planet Encounter** \[Tags: Garden (Ga), Auluran Collective, Agricultural (Ag)\]

1. Territorial Megaplants: Carnivorous flora, toxic spores, or a rapidly spreading, aggressive root system blocks the path.  
2. Ambush Predator: A massive, camouflaged creature attacks from the canopy or the murky water (requires a difficult Spot/Survival check).  
3. Ancient Temple/Stone Ruins: The crumbling remnant of an old civilization, possibly protected by automated defenses or spirits/ghosts.  
4. Weather Shift: A sudden, torrential downpour or a flash flood turns the jungle floor into a river.  
5. Tribal Natives: A hostile or highly suspicious indigenous species/group confronts the PCs, demanding tribute or warning them away.  
6. Bogged Vehicle/Equipment: Heavy mud or quicksand traps a vehicle or a critical piece of equipment.  
7. Poisonous Insect Swarm: Millions of biting, stinging, or disease-carrying insects attack, requiring a scramble for protective gear.  
8. Hidden Stash: Discovering a forgotten military bunker, a drug lord's lab, or a long-lost pirate treasure.  
9. Luminous Phenomenon: Strange glowing spores, lights, or energy fields confuse senses and possibly cause mutations.  
10. Rival Scientific Team: A group of researchers who are either desperate for the PCs' help or fiercely competitive over a local discovery.

&nbsp;

**6\. Post-War Battlefield Encounter** \[Tags: Barren (Ba), Impyrium (Gov 11), Coalition\]

1. Active Landmines/UXO: The ground is littered with unexploded ordnance, requiring a difficult Tech or Perception check to cross safely.  
2. Automated Sentinel: A long-deactivated tank, mech, or defense turret suddenly reactivates and opens fire.  
3. Scavenging Gangs: Warring factions of heavily armed survivors fighting over scrap, resources, or territory.  
4. Psychic Residue/War Ghosts: The lingering trauma of battle manifests as terrifying illusions, cold spots, or maddening whispers.  
5. A Secret Bunker: A well-hidden, fortified underground base still containing resources, weapons, or a captive.  
6. Bio-Weapon Contamination: A pocket of toxic gas, chemical runoff, or irradiated sludge blocks a key route.  
7. Field Hospital/Refugee Camp: A desperate collection of wounded and civilians in need of immediate medical aid, supplies, or protection.  
8. Trapped Vehicle: A military transport or APC is stuck, and its crew needs help fighting off attackers or extracting the vehicle.  
9. Decoy Distress Call: A signal leads to a well-laid trap, either by local bandits or a still-active military unit.  
10. Relic Hunter: A well-funded, armed collector is systematically looting the area for valuable wartime technology and artifacts.

&nbsp;

**7\. Neo-Feudal Colony Encounter** \[Tags: Low-Tech (Lt), Dracon Dynasty (Gov 5), Agricultural (Ag)\]

1. Baron's Enforcers: A local lord's heavily armed, often brutal troops demand immediate loyalty and a crushing tariff.  
2. Peasant Revolt: The common folk are rising up against the local aristocracy, and the PCs are forced to choose a side.  
3. Medieval Trial/Execution: Witnessing a public, brutal display of justice that the PCs might feel compelled to interfere with.  
4. Religious Zealots: A fanatical cult preaching a doctrine that is either dangerously manipulative or surprisingly accurate about the setting's history.  
5. Outlaw Attack: Highwaymen on horseback or crude ground vehicles ambush the PCs' transport or camp.  
6. Mysterious Plague: A low-tech, easily spread disease is devastating the community, and the PCs' medical expertise is desperately needed.  
7. Royal Decree: An absolute, binding command from the highest authority forces the PCs to perform a specific, dangerous task.  
8. Witch/Heretic Hunt: A local mob is hunting an individual accused of using forbidden technology or magic.  
9. The Hidden Vault: Rumors of a forgotten pre-Collapse tech vault, locked away by ancient superstition or decree.  
10. Forced Labor: The PCs are temporarily conscripted for a major public work project or military fortification.

&nbsp;

**8\. Deep Sea/Underwater Habitat Encounter** \[Tags: Water World (Wa), Aquatic Origin\]

1. Pressure Breach: A critical structural failure in the habitat or the PCs' submersible, requiring rapid repair before implosion.  
2. Giant Marine Fauna: A massive, powerful creature adapted to the pressure attacks the habitat or vehicle.  
3. Geothermal Vent/Eruption: A sudden, violent release of superheated steam and minerals disrupts systems and visibility.  
4. Rival Submersible: A heavily armed research, military, or pirate submarine shadows or confronts the PCs.  
5. Artifact on the Seabed: Discovery of a crashed ship, a sunken city, or a Progenitor device in the deep trenches.  
6. Lost Drone/Probe: A valuable automated system is lost in the darkness and must be recovered before its secrets are exposed.  
7. Communication Blackout: A strange energy field or geological anomaly prevents all radio and sensor contact with the surface.  
8. Mutated/Aggressive Biota: A unique, potentially hostile lifeform is drawn to the habitat's lights or heat signature.  
9. Sabotage: Evidence of tampering with the habitat's power source or environmental controls by an internal or external agent.  
10. Resource Harvester: A massive, automated mining platform is disrupting the environment, and its security drones are hostile to intruders.

&nbsp;

**9\. Interstellar Market/Bazaar Encounter** \[Tags: Entari Combine, Rich (Ri), Trade Hubs\]

1. Customs Inspection: An aggressive security force performs a thorough, invasive search of the PCs and their cargo (Law Level check).  
2. Assault on a Vendor: A merchant selling rare or illicit goods is robbed or attacked, forcing the PCs to intervene or flee.  
3. Information Broker: A shadowy figure offers a vital clue or a forged document for a steep price or a dangerous favor.  
4. Gambling Debt Collector: A ruthless individual or gang is attempting to forcibly collect a massive debt from an acquaintance of the PCs.  
5. A Hidden Stowaway: A desperate refugee, a spy, or a dangerous criminal attempts to secretly board the PCs' vessel.  
6. Exotic Creature Display: A vendor is showing off a rare, possibly illegal, alien animal that suddenly breaks containment.  
7. Bar Fight/Brawl: A misunderstanding or territorial dispute escalates into a massive, multi-faction brawl.  
8. Conspiracy Theorist: A seemingly mad individual rants about a massive, galaxy-spanning conspiracy, offering a nugget of real, secret information.  
9. Illegal Arms Deal: PCs witness a secretive exchange of powerful, restricted, or dangerous weaponry.  
10. Pickpocket Master: A highly skilled thief attempts to steal the most valuable, and possibly critical, item the PCs possess.

&nbsp;

**10\. Alien Ruins/Progenitor Site Encounter** \[Tags: Anomalous/Precursor, Any TL/ML, Explorer Origin\]

1. Automated Defenses: A dormant security system (lasers, robots, energy fields) suddenly activates when the PCs touch a specific object.  
2. Time Warp/Temporal Echo: The PCs witness an event from the distant past playing out, which may alter the environment or their gear.  
3. Psionic Trap: A mind-altering field or telepathic entity attempts to control the PCs or drive them mad.  
4. Energy Drain: The site draws power from all nearby technology, disabling weapons and vital equipment.  
5. Biological Anomaly: A strange, crystalline, or fluid lifeform has adapted to the ruins and acts as a security system.  
6. A Trapped Observer: Another explorer, researcher, or mercenary has been injured or captured by the ruins' defenses.  
7. Illusion/Labyrinth: The site physically or psychologically manipulates the environment, leading the PCs in circles.  
8. Guardian Construct: A massive, powerful, unique robotic entity tasked with protecting the core secrets of the site.  
9. Self-Destruct Sequence: The PCs accidentally trigger a countdown, requiring them to solve a complex puzzle or escape quickly.  
10. The Grand Archive: Discovering a central data repository containing the history, technology, and perhaps the ultimate weakness of the Progenitors.
