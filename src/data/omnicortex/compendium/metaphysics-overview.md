# Metaphysics System Overview & Reality Manipulation

In Tangent SF RP, metaphysics is the deliberate restructuring of universal constants through focused willpower. It operates on a skill-based triad: **Attune, Discipline, and Invocation**.

## The Metaphysic Triad
1. **Attune**: The universal conduit skill used to draw, focus, and channel metaphysical energy (determines Attack rolls and Resistance DCs).
2. **Discipline**: The manipulation pattern skill governing specific laws of reality (determines Effect Severity and Damage potency).
3. **Invocation**: Codified rote formulas functioning as discipline specializations, granting safe "Take 10" execution.
