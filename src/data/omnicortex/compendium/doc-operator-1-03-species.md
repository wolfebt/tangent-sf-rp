# **1.03 SPECIES CATALOG**

Player characters can choose from a diverse range of species and sub-species across the Tangent galaxy, each possessing distinct biological traits, attribute adjustments, cultural stigmas, and technological/metaphysical baselines.

### **Species Package CP Valuation Rules (Canonical Rule #9 — Universal Straight Pricing)**
* **Species Attribute Modifiers:** Positive species attribute bonuses cost **5 CP per +1** and negative species attribute penalties grant **-5 CP per -1** (identical to character creation customization — straight values with zero package discount).
* **Species Skill Ranks:** Cost **5 CP per +5 Skill Ranks** (**1 CP per 1 Skill Rank**; 10 CP for +10 Ranks, 20 CP for +20 Ranks, 40 CP for +40 Ranks — straight values with zero package discount).
* **Species Trait Tiers:** Categorized as **Basic (1 CP)**, **Advanced (2 CP)**, or **Elite (4 CP)** (`99. MODULAR SPECIES MATRIX.md`).
* **Recommended Features Discount:** Features listed under a species' **Recommended Features** receive a **1 CP discount** when purchased (2 CP instead of 3 CP, minimum 1 CP; stacks with Occupation recommendations down to 1 CP minimum).
* **Species Hindrances & Stigmas (Canonical Rule #10):** Innate biological disadvantages and social Stigmas reduce the net CP cost of the Species Package and **do not count toward the character's 10 CP personal Hindrance limit**.
* **Vitality, Health & Synthetic Structure (Canonical Rule #8):** Biological species start with **Base 30 Vitality and Base 30 Health** (increased strictly by +5 points per 1 CP; Stamina never increases pools and instead grants Base Toughness damage reduction). Synthetics and Constructs combine both pools into **Base 60 Structure Points (SP)** (+5 SP per 1 CP, Suggested Max: 120 SP).

## Part II — Complete Unabridged Profiles & Rules Entries

# **SPECIES FORMAT**  
  
## **Character Points (CP) Cost**  
  
Each species has a CP cost, which is deducted from the player's starting CP pool. This cost reflects the species' relative power and uniqueness.  
  
  
  
## **Ability Modifiers**  
  
Species provide possible modifiers to one or more of the six core ability scores: Strength, Agility, Stamina, Intellect, Wisdom, and Charisma. These modifiers can either enhance or hinder a character's capabilities in areas related to those abilities.  
  
  
  
## **Species Traits and Features**  
  
Certain traits and features are granted directly as typical for the members of the species. These are unique abilities that further define the species' characteristics. These can range from natural weapons and physical adaptations to innate magical or psychic powers.  
  
  
  
## **Technology**  
  
Each species has a designated technology level, which influences the types of equipment, augmentations, and skills available to characters of that species. This reflects the species' technological advancements and societal norms.  
  
  
  
## **Stigmas**  
  
Some species may have stigmas, which are social modifiers that reflect how other species perceive and interact with them. Stigmas can lead to discrimination, prejudice, or other social challenges for characters of that species \- Minor \-1, Typical \-2, Severe \-4 (adjust as needed depending on individual/group).  
  
This Modifier also determines the Alien Mind Factor for Mental Contact (penalty to the initiating) and for a possible additional modifier for use with certain Alien Technologies).  
  
  
  
Stigmas listed are guidelines based on human-sided views and likely adjusted between different species. Individuals of certain species just may not care to bother with stigmas regardless of typical views or the other side when there is stigmas and discrimination within one’s own species. Players may chose their own views but it may be an issue.  
  
  
  
## **Awakened Species**  
  
Some species are naturally awakened, meaning they have an innate connection to metaphysical forces. This is represented by a designated ability score (Intellect, Wisdom, or Charisma) and a source of power (Arcane, Psychic, or Nature). Awakened species will often have access to a variety of metaphysical abilities and disciplines.
