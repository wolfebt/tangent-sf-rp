## **III. DETAILED SIGNATURE FEATURE TACTICAL PROFILES (`3 CP Standard | 2 CP Recommended`)**

### **1. Bureaucratic Efficiency (The Bureaucrat)**

* **Aesthetic:** A flurry of authorized permits and supply-chain re-routing.
* **Classification:** Operational Sentinel Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Bureaucrat)
* **Activation:** Passive (During downtime or resource acquisition)
* **Effect:** Whenever the party attempts to purchase gear, secure legal transit permits, or acquire faction assets, the **Challenge Rating (CR)** of the administrative/requisition task is reduced by **5**. Additionally, fiscal write-offs and financial loopholes allow the team to recover **15 percent** of all liquid Credits spent on gear or repairs at the end of a mission.

### **2. Guardian's Ward (The Warden)**

* **Aesthetic:** Roots and glowing spiritual energy weave into a defensive barrier.
* **Classification:** Metaphysical Sentinel Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Warden)
* **Activation:** **1 Reaction** (Triggered when an ally within `30 ft` [`10 meters`] is targeted by an attack)
* **Effect:** The Warden projects a barrier of protective life-force. The targeted ally gains **+3 to Defense** against the triggering attack. If the attack still hits, the net damage is halved, and the remaining half is transferred to the Warden's **Vitality** instead.

### **3. Black Market Connections (The Munitions Magnate / Smuggler)**

* **Aesthetic:** Cryptic handshakes and back-alley dealings.
* **Classification:** Social Operative Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Magnate/Smuggler)
* **Activation:** Passive
* **Effect:** You bypass local legal restrictions (Law Level) when acquiring weapons, armor, and cybernetics. You can purchase restricted or illegal items up to **Tech Level 4 (`TL 4`)** regardless of local jurisdiction, and receive a permanent **10 percent discount** on liquid Credit costs when buying from non-aligned syndicates or black-market brokers.

### **4. Protective Stance (The Protector)**

* **Aesthetic:** Digging in heels, raising a shield, and physically blocking the path.
* **Classification:** Combat Sentinel Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Protector)
* **Activation:** **1 Move / Minor Action** (On your turn)
* **Effect:** While active, your movement speed is halved, and you project a **`5 ft` (`2-meter`) Interdiction Zone** (all adjacent squares). Any enemy attempting to move through this zone must succeed on an **Athletics** check opposed by your **Intimidate** or **Melee** skill. On a failure, their movement immediately ends and you may make a **free Unarmed or Melee Opportunity Attack** against them.

### **5. Field Triage (The Field Medic)**

* **Aesthetic:** Rapid application of derm-sealers, stimulants, and trauma clamps under fire.
* **Classification:** Medical Sentinel Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Field Medic)
* **Activation:** **1 Standard / Major Action**
* **Effect:** Make a **Knowledge (Medicine)** check (**CR 15**) on an adjacent wounded or downed ally. On a success, the target immediately stabilizes, regains consciousness if at `0 Health` (restoring `1 Health`), and recovers **Vitality equal to `5 * your Wisdom Score`** (minimum `5 Vitality`). Using Field Triage in high-threat environments does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.

### **6. Stage Presence (The Thespian / Virtuoso)**

* **Aesthetic:** Commanding the room's attention with posture, projection, and cadence.
* **Classification:** Social Operative Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Thespian/Virtuoso)
* **Activation:** **1 Standard / Major Action**
* **Effect:** Perform a dramatic display, song, or authoritative declaration. All hostile targets within **`50 ft` (`15 meters`)** who can see or hear you must make an **Insight** check opposed by your **Expression (Performance)** or **Bluff** check. On a failure, they suffer a **-2 penalty to all Attack rolls and Alertness checks for 3 rounds** while their attention is locked onto you.

### **7. Silent Killer (The Silent Blade)**

* **Aesthetic:** Slipping behind a target unseen, striking with surgical precision.
* **Classification:** Combat Operative Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Silent Blade)
* **Activation:** Passive (Triggered when attacking a completely unaware target)
* **Effect:** When you strike a target while completely hidden/undetected, your hit automatically counts as a **Critical Hit** (`2x` base weapon dice + `30` to Attack Score) and bypasses the target's **Armor DR** entirely. If the target is an NPC of equal or lower Threat Tier, they must also pass a **Fortitude Save (`CR = your Attack Roll`)** or be rendered **Unconscious / Incapacitated**.

### **8. Cyber Warfare (The Decker / Slicer)**

* **Aesthetic:** Code cascading down an interface screen as firewalls crumble.
* **Classification:** Tech Savant Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Decker/Slicer)
* **Activation:** **1 Standard / Major Action** (Targeting a networked electronic system, vehicle, drone, or cybernetic implant within `60 ft` or network range)
* **Effect:** Execute a rapid intrusion packet by rolling **Knowledge (Computers) or Knowledge (Technology) (`CR = 10 + [Target Tech Level * 2]`)**. On a success, you seize complete control of the target system for **3 rounds**—disabling a cybernetic implant, forcing a cyber-limb to drop a held weapon, or flipping an automated turret/drone's IFF targeting so it treats enemies as allies and allies as enemies.

### **9. Arcane Weaving (The Magus)**

* **Aesthetic:** Tracing geometric runes in the air as reality warps.
* **Classification:** Metaphysical Savant Feature (`3 CP`, or `2 CP` if Magus)
* **Prerequisite:** *Awakened* Feature
* **Activation:** Passive (Declared when casting an Invocation)
* **Effect:** When casting a Metaphysic Invocation, you may voluntarily take a **-2 penalty to your Attune check** to apply one of the following metamagic alterations:
  1. **Double (`2x`)** the Invocation's range;
  2. **Expand** its area of effect radius/dimensions by **+15 ft (`5 meters`)**; or
  3. **Transmute** its energy damage type to another known Energy or Kinetic Impact subtype.
