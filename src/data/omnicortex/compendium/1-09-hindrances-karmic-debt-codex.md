# 1.09 Hindrances & Karmic Debt Codex

Hindrances are character flaws, phobias, debts, and physical disabilities that grant bonus **Build Points (BP)** at character creation.

---

## Master Hindrance Value Directory

| Hindrance | Bonus BP | Severity & Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Addiction** | 3 / 6 / 9 | Minor (3), Moderate (6), or Severe/Crippling (9) chemical dependency |
| **Adversary / Nemesis**| 1 – 10 | Individual stalker (1–5) or powerful interstellar Syndicate (5–10) |
| **Bodily Age (Child/Venerable)**| 5 / 10 | Physical penalties to STR/AGI/STA in exchange for social/knowledge perks |
| **Covetous / Greed** | 3 / 6 / 9 | Compulsive greed, ambition, or treasure obsession |
| **Dependent / Ward** | 3 / 6 / 9 | Innocent family member, AI core, or pet requiring constant protection |
| **Disability (Limb)** | 3 / 6 / 9 / 18| Lost Hand/Foot (3), Full Limb (6), Multiple Limbs (9), Quadriplegic (18) |
| **Distinctive Features** | 3 / 6 / 9 | Cybernetic scars, mutant pigmentation, or glowing biometric implants |
| **Extreme Morality** | 4 / 8 / 12 | Rigid ethical code (Good, Lawful, Evil, Chaotic) that cannot be breached |
| **Impaired / Lost Sense**| 3 / 6 / 9 | Lost Smell/Taste (3), Lost Touch/Hearing (6), Total Blindness (9) |
| **Poor / Severe Debt** | 3 / 6 / 9 | -4 / -8 / -12 to starting Wealth; negative scores represent bounty debt |
| **Unlucky (Karmic Debt)**| 3 / 6 / 9 | -2 / -4 / -6 penalty to maximum Karma pool |

---

## Rules on Hindrances

- **Recommended Limit:** No character should take more than **15 BP** of Hindrances.
- **Overcoming Hindrances:** A character may buy off a Hindrance during a campaign by paying its BP cost in experience points and completing a relevant story arc.
