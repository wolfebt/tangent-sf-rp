# Major Polities vs. Generic Organizational Templates

The Tangent galaxy divides organizational entities into two broad categories:

## 1. Major Galactic Polities (The Core 10-12)
Sovereign galactic superpowers controlling multiple star systems, standing starfleets, and distinct civilizational philosophies (e.g., *Dracon Dynasty, Radiant Impyrium, Incorporated Planetary Syndication, Coalition of Independent Worlds*).

## 2. Generic Organizational Templates (The 25 Archetypes)
Modular organization templates for local, planetary, or sector-level groups such as *Mercenary Companies, Rebel Cells, Megacorporate Subsidiaries, Piratical Fleets, and Cultist Enclaves*.
