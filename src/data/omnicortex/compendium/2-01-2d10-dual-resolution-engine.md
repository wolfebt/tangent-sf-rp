# 2.01 The 2d10 Dual Resolution Engine

The Tangent Roleplaying System is driven by a canonical **two-ten-sided-dice (2d10)** bell-curve resolution mechanic for resolving all uncertain actions, combat strikes, social interactions, and environmental hazards.

---

## The Core Resolution Formula

$$\text{Check Result} = 2d10 + \text{Skill Rank} + \text{Linked Attribute Modifier} + \text{Situational Modifiers}$$

- **Success:** If the total strictly exceeds the Target Challenge Rating (**DC** / **CR**) or Opposed Defense score, the action succeeds.
- **Failure:** If the total is less than or equal to the Opposed Defense score, or less than the DC/CR, the action fails.
- **Natural Roll Extremes:**
  - **Natural 20 (Double 10s):** Critical Success. Doubles base weapon damage dice, adds $+30$ to base attack score, or triggers special surge.
  - **Natural 2 (Double 1s):** Critical Fumble. Subtracts $-10$ from base check score and inflicts weapon jam, loss of action, or complication.

---

## Opposed vs. Unopposed Checks

### 1. Opposed Checks (Contested Actions)
When an action is actively resisted by another conscious entity (e.g. Combat Strike vs. Active Defense, Bluff vs. Insight, Stealth vs. Perception):
- **Attacker Rolls:** $2d10 + \text{Combat Skill} + \text{Relevant Attribute Modifier} + \text{Situational Modifiers}$
- **Defender Rolls:** $2d10 + \text{Defense Skill} + \text{Agility Modifier} + \text{Situational Modifiers}$
- **The Tie-Breaker Rule:** **DEFENDER WINS ALL TIES**. The attacker must strictly exceed the defender's total to score a hit or success.

### 2. Unopposed Checks (Static Environmental Tasks)
When a character interacts with a static object, bypasses security, or targets a stationary, unaware, or surprised foe:
- Attacker rolls against a Challenge Rating (**CR**) or Challenge Rating (**DC**):
  - Baseline for an unaware/stationary medium target within short range: **CR 15 (Average)**.
  - Baseline CR 15 is modified by target Size (e.g. Tiny $-4$, Large $+2$, etc.), Range, and Movement Speed.
