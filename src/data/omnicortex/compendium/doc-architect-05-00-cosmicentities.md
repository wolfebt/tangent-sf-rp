## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every rule, formula, and procedural matrix in this master compendium is governed by these canonical **Tangent SF RP** mechanical invariants:

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Resolution:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`) converts the rolled dice into a flat `-10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`15 CP` Primary Attribute at `+3`, `10 CP` Secondary Attribute at `+2`, `24 CP` for 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6, `18 CP` for 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3, and `13 CP` for 2 Signature Traits at `1 CP` each plus 3 Recommended Core Features at `9 CP`) and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Skill Ranks:** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` as `20 SP Faction` + `20 SP Origin` + `20 SP Occupation`, alongside `4 Recommended Features` (`-1 CP` discount: `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and `4 Traits` at `0 CP` (`Extra Traits = 1 CP` each).
* **Universal Straight Pricing:** `+5 CP` per `+1` Attribute, `-5 CP` refund per `-1` Attribute penalty, and `1 CP` per `1` Skill Rank (`5 CP` per `5` Skill Ranks) across all creation, species, companion, and bestiary packages.
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with **Base 30 Vitality** and **Base 30 Health**; Synthetic/Construct entities combine both into **Base 60 Structure Points (SP)**. Pools scale strictly at **`+5 points per 1 CP`** spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER scaled by Stamina**). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR (`Diminishing Returns Armor Stacking`: use the single highest DR across bio, cyber, and worn armor, plus `+1 DR` per stage of 5 [`0 = +0`, `1–5 = +1`, `6–10 = +2`, `11–15 = +3`, etc.] calculated separately for each additional layer).
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Standard personal character hindrances have a `+10 CP` maximum limit; **Species-level hindrances and cybernetic Stigmas do NOT count toward the 10-point standard hindrance limit**.
* **Economic Unified Field Theory (EUFT) & UDU Hierarchy:** Base 5-CR intervals follow `Value (Credits) = 10 * 4^(CR / 5)` modified by Linear Micro-Modifiers (`+1 CR = x1.30`, `+2 CR = x1.75`, `+3 CR = x2.30`, `+4 CR = x3.00`, `-1 CR Flaw = x0.75`). Pristine crafting materials cost `50%` of Credit Value; Scavenged Scrap retains `10%` value (`5x Item Credit Value` in unrefined scrap mass). UDU capacity scales at `10 Usable Nodes (Tier 0) = 1 Socket (Tier 1)`, `10 Sockets = 1 Mount (Tier 2)`, and `10 Mounts = 1 Module (Tier 3)` (`Garaging: 1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier`; vehicle table SP is pre-calculated).

## **I. ONTOLOGICAL OVERVIEW & CLASSIFICATION**

The designation of **Entity** refers not to a conventional biological species, but to a foundational ontological template for beings whose nature precludes an inherent physical body. An Entity transcends the material substrate of standard baryonic matter, existing in a purely conceptual, energetic, psionic, or spiritual state within **The Resonance** (`./metaphysics/04.TheResonance.md`).

When an Entity chooses to interact with the material plane, its visible presence is a temporary, constructed, or projected **Avatar / Vessel**—never its true self.

### **Existential Categories of Cosmic Entities**

1. **Extra-Planar Beings:** Intelligences originating from dimensions, Tangent Space layers, or planes of existence distinct from and normally inaccessible to the conventional physical universe. They are native inhabitants of realms of pure Conscious Light, energy, thought, or entropy; their presence on the material plane represents an intrusion, summoning, or temporary excursion.
2. **Detached Consciousnesses:** Minds, wills, or souls separated or voluntarily emancipated from a corporeal host. This category encompasses remnants of ancient progenitor intellects, psionic ghosts, ascended post-biological minds, and sentient mental constructs that have evolved beyond the need for a biological or synthetic chassis.
3. **Embodiments of Place or Concept (Egregores & Genius Loci):** Sentient personifications of abstract universal principles or geographical domains:
   - **Ideological Embodiments (Egregores):** The living consciousness of a universal concept such as *Justice*, *Chaos*, *Time*, *Entropy*, or *Fear*. Their power and permanence scale directly with the prevalence and psychic resonance of that concept across sentient populations.
   - **Geographic Embodiments (Genius Loci):** The manifested spirit or planetary will of a specific location—an ancient forest, a volcanic mountain range, an abyssal ocean, a living star, or a sprawling arcology-city. Their abilities and avatar geometry mirror the ecology and history of the domain they embody.

### **3. Non-Corporeal Combat, Pools & Manifestation Mechanics**

1. **Zero Physical Attributes in Native State:**
   - In its unmanifested native state, an Entity has no `Strength (STR)`, `Agility (AGI)`, or `Stamina (STA)` scores and cannot manipulate physical objects with mundane muscle force.
   - All movement, defense, and environmental interactions are governed by its Mental Attributes: **Intellect (`INT` / Reason)** substitutes for `AGI` on Dodge/Phase Defense and Initiative, **Wisdom (`WIS` / Willpower)** substitutes for `STA` on Metaphysical Toughness and Stability, and **Charisma (`CHA` / Etiquette)** governs Presence and Avatar projection.
2. **Metaphysical Structure Pool & Zero-Stamina Scaling (Rule 8):**
   - Because an Entity has no biological flesh, it operates on a unified **Metaphysical Structure / Essence Integrity Pool** equal to **`Base 60 Structure Points (SP)`**, increased strictly by **`+5 SP per 1 CP`** invested (plus its **Essence Pool** derived from *Attune* and Mental Attributes).
   - **Base Metaphysical Toughness:** The Entity's **Wisdom (`WIS`) Modifier** subtracts point-for-point from incoming metaphysical, psionic, or Aether-resonant damage after any active Ward/Shield DR.
   - **Disruption & Banishment (Mortality State):** If reduced to `0 SP` on the material plane, the Entity's projected Avatar destabilizes (`Stability Points = WIS Score + 5`). At `0 Stability`, the Avatar is banished back to its native plane or dormant locus for `1d10` days (or permanently dispersed if destroyed within its true planar core).
3. **Manifestation & Possession Protocols:**
   - **Projected Avatar (Manifestation):** By expending Essence or channeling through a local **Aetherium / Psycholith Focus** (`./technology/core/06.AetheriumAndAetherite.md`), the Entity projects a temporary semi-corporeal or hard-light body (`Alter Form, Elite`), interacting with the physical world through its *Energy*, *Matter*, or *Force* Invocations (`2d10 + Discipline Rank + Mental Attribute vs. CR`).
   - **Corporeal Host (Possession):** An Entity may attempt to inhabit a willing or unwilling biological, synthetic, or automata vessel via an opposed **Mind (Control) Invocation Check vs. Target's Willpower Save (`2d10 + 2 + [2 * WIS] + Feature Bonus`)**. While possessing a host, the Entity uses the host's physical `STR`, `AGI`, `STA`, Vitality, and Health (or Vehicle/Automata `SP`), while retaining its own `INT`, `WIS`, `CHA`, Skills, and Awakened Disciplines.
