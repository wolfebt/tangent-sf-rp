# Vehicle Combat, Chases & Hard-Point Ordnance

Vehicular combat scales character maneuvers to ground rovers, repulsor skiffs, starfighters, and colossal capital ships.

## Vehicular Action Steps
1. **Piloting Initiative**: Determined by Pilot Skill + Vehicle Handling.
2. **Tactical Chase Positioning**: Contested Piloting checks establish firing angles, tailing locks, and evasive arcs.
3. **Ramming & Collisions**: Impact damage equals Vehicle Scale Modifier × Speed stage.
