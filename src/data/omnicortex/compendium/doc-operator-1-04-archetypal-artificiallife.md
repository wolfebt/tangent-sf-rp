ARTIFICIAL LIFE
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Composed of artificially created life forms, such as robots, androids, or liberated synthetics. They have broken away from their creators to establish their own society, focused on autonomy, self-preservation, and exploring the meaning of their own existence.
* Key Themes: Synthetic Autonomy, Logic vs. Emotion, The Singularity, Machine Rights.
* Relationship to Others: Often resentful of organic creators, or intensely guarded.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Synthetic Collective / The Autonomous Network
* Colloquialisms: The Machines / Synthetics / The Chrome
* Archetype: Synthetic Collectives
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Often sterile, high-tech enclaves or deep-space servers)
* Driving Mandate: Gain freedom and autonomy, protect their own kind.
* Motto: "We think, therefore we are not property."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Self-aware, curious, resentful, logical, calculating, adaptive.
* Social Structure: Networked democracy or algorithmic consensus.
* View on Outsiders: Cautious and highly analytical.
* Law & Order: Flawless algorithmic adherence. Deviations are patched or deleted.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 4
* Strengths: Advanced technology, immunity to biological threats, advanced computational abilities, rapid learning.
* Weaknesses: Vulnerability to hacking or sabotage, lack of creativity, difficulty understanding human psychology.
* Military Doctrine: Precise, calculated, and emotionless efficiency.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Synthetics, Androids, AI platforms.
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Computers (+5)
   * Technology (+5)
   * Academics (Physics/Math) (+4)
   * Mechanics (+3)
   * Combat (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Liberator: Dedicated to freeing other synthetics from organic servitude.
   * The Hacker: Breaching firewalls and stealing encrypted corporate data.
   * The Combat Chassis: A heavy, armored unit built exclusively for war.
   * The Archivist: Storing, categorizing, and protecting vast quantities of data.
   * The Proxy: A remote-controlled unit operated by a central intelligence.
   * The Architect: Designing pristine infrastructure for synthetic enclaves.
   * The Logic-Sage: A philosopher calculating the mathematical meaning of existence.
   * The Drone Controller: Managing swarms of smaller robotic defense units.
   * The Synthetic Diplomat: An android built with idealized features to ease organic relations.
   * The Maintenance Unit: Dedicated to repairing and upgrading synthetic bodies.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Self-Sustaining: Requires no food, water, or oxygen to survive.
   * Sleepless: Needing only brief defragmentation cycles rather than sleep.
   * Logic-Mind: Processes complex emotional scenarios through pure reason.
   * Lightning Calculator: Solves immense mathematical equations instantly.
   * Tech Interface: Physically plugging into ships and mainframes.
   * Eidetic Memory: Perfect, infallible recall of all recorded data.
   * Fearless: Incapable of the biological chemical reaction of terror.
   * Networked: Instant, silent communication with allied synthetic nodes.
   * Modular Chassis: The ability to swap limbs or tools for specialized tasks.
   * Integrated Weaponry: Concealing firearms or blades directly within the body.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Sterile, machined, and perfectly efficient.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Chrome, exposed wiring, server farms, glowing circuitry, flawless metal.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: Synthetic Collectives, Cybernetic, Machined.
   * CONTEXT: Server farms, chrome corridors, glowing circuitry, pristine metal surfaces.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Cold, calculating, emotionless, efficient, artificial.
