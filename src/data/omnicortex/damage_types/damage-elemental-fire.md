# Fire Elemental Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Fire Elemental damage embodies the primal fury of flame, heat, and volcanic power. It scorches flesh, melts plastics, and creates hazardous wall-of-fire terrain zones.

### Metaphysical
- **Pyrokinesis**: Conjuring elemental firestorms and manipulating flame shapes.
- **Fire Evocation**: Summoning salamanders and fire elementals immune to heat.
- **Elemental Fire Enchantments**: Adding persistent flame sheaths to weapons.

### Tech Applications
- Torches, flamethrowers, incendiary munitions, plasma repeaters, and laser ignition arrays.

### Tactical Notes
Shares rules with **Pyro / Fire Damage**, applying the **Burning status effect** to flammable targets.
