# Dimensional Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Dimensional damage alters, fractures, or rips the fabric of spacetime around a target. It bypasses physical armor by displacing sections of the target into other planes or subjecting matter to dimensional shearing.

### Metaphysical
- **Dimensional Rift Creation**: Tearing holes in reality that suck targets into the void or slice them between dimensions.
- **Dimensional Displacement**: Shifting portions of a foe out of phase to inflict devastating spatial shearing.
- **Reality Warping**: Altering fundamental constants locally to tear atomic bonds apart.

### Archaic Tech
- **Dimensional Artifacts**: Ancient relic boxes and strange stones causing localized gravitational anomalies.

### Modern & Advanced Tech
- **Spatial Displacement Cannons**: Weapons teleporting microscopic slices of enemy armor into hyperspace.
- **Phase Rifles**: Projectiles shifting through dimensions to materialize inside enemy cockpits.
- **Pocket Dimension Collapse Generators**: Weapons crushing targets by collapsing localized spatial envelopes.

### Tactical Notes
Dimensional damage completely bypasses Armor DR and forces saving throws to avoid being banished or displaced across the battlefield.
