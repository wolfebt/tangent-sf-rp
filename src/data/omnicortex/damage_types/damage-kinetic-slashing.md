# Kinetic: Slashing (Cutting)

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Slashing damage focuses on cutting and tearing targets, often causing wide, jagged wounds and potentially severing limbs or causing significant bleeding. It is particularly effective against unarmored or lightly armored opponents, and can also exploit vulnerable seams in heavier armor.

### Metaphysical
- **Blade Evocation**: Manifesting telekinetic or metaphysical cutting arcs that slice through matter.
- **Wind Blades**: Razor-sharp currents of accelerated atmospheric force.

### Archaic Tech
- **Axes and Hatchets**: Powerful chopping tools cleaving flesh and bone.
- **Swords**: Scimitars, katanas, and broadswords excelling at slicing sweeps.
- **Sickles and Scythes**: Curved blades designed for hooking and wide sweeping cuts.

### Modern Tech
- **Machetes and Kukris**: Heavy angled blades optimized for hacking and slashing through dense brush or armor weave.
- **Chain Weapons**: Segmented blades linked by high-tensile chains (chain whips, kusarigama).
- **Vibroblades**: High-frequency oscillating blades that rapidly shear through organic flesh and composite weave.

### Advanced Tech
- **Monomolecular Blades**: Single-atom-thick molecular edges that slice through almost any material with surgical ease.
- **Energy Swords**: Coherent photonic blades cutting through armor and cauterizing wounds.
- **Plasma Swords**: Superheated plasma arcs that melt armor plates and deal additional thermal damage.

### Tactical Notes
Critical strikes with slashing weapons trigger the **Bleeding status effect** (1d4 ongoing damage per round, ignoring Armor DR).
