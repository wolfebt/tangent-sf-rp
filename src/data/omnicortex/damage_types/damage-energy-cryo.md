# Cryo / Cold Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Cryo and Cold damage drains thermal energy rapidly, slowing neural impulses, chilling muscle fibers, and causing metal armor and biological carapaces to become brittle. Severe exposure inflicts the **Freeze status effect** (Speed = 0, Vulnerable to Physical & Sonic).

### Metaphysical
- **Cryokinesis**: Mentally generating chilling blasts, ice barriers, and subzero fields.
- **Ice Evocation**: Summoning glacial elementals unleashing freezing torrents.
- **Cold Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with freezing enchantments that frostbite targets on impact.

### Archaic Tech
- **Alchemical Cold Grenades**: Endothermic chemical mixtures that absorb heat rapidly upon detonation.
- **Environmental Ice Weapons**: Improvised weapons formed from subzero ice and snow.

### Modern Tech
- **Cryo-Grenades & Mines**: Canisters releasing expanding clouds of supercooled gas.
- **Cryo-Guns**: Firearms firing cryogenic dart canisters causing cellular embrittlement.
- **Cryo-Sprayers**: Continuous liquid nitrogen/helium stream projectors.

### Advanced Tech
- **Cryo-Lasers**: Photonic beams tuned to endothermic frequencies that instantly extract kinetic heat.
- **Cryo-Blades**: Melee weapons with supercooled chambers that shatter brittle enemy armor on contact.
- **Cryo-Mines**: Proximity devices flash-freezing entire choke points.

### Tactical Notes
Cold damage severely hinders enemy mobility and makes targets vulnerable to shattering physical and sonic strikes.
