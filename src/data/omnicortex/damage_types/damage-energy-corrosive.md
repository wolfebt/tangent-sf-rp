# Corrosive / Disintegration Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Corrosive damage dissolves matter through chemical reactivity or molecular bonding failure. It melts armor plates, reduces target Armor DR permanently, and burns through biological tissue.

### Metaphysical
- **Acid Manipulation**: Mentally generating caustic streams, acid pools, and dissolving barriers.
- **Acid Evocation**: Summoning toxic oozes and corrosive elemental entities.
- **Corrosive Enchantments**: Imbuing blades with virulent acid coatings.

### Archaic Tech
- **Alchemical Acids**: Vials of vitriol, aqua regia, and caustic alkalis thrown as splash weapons.
- **Natural Venoms**: Caustic secretions harvested from alien insects and predatory flora.

### Modern Tech
- **Acid Grenades & Bombs**: Pressurized canisters dispersing aerosolized hydrochloric or hydrofluoric acid.
- **Chemical Launchers**: Rifles firing acid-filled ampoules designed to breach armor seams.
- **Caustic Sprayers**: Continuous chemical sprayers dissolving fortifications and obstacles.

### Advanced Tech
- **Molecular Acid Projectors**: Synthetic hyper-acids breaking molecular bonds in milliseconds.
- **Corrosive Energy Blasters**: Plasma-chemical beams that dissolve material targets on contact.
- **Nanite Acid Swarms**: Microscopic nanite swarms programmed to consume metal armor and structural hulls.

### Tactical Notes
Corrosive damage permanently degrades target Armor DR by 1 point per 5 points of unsoaked damage taken, rapidly stripping defensive barriers.
