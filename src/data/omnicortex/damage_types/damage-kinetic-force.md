# Force (Kinetic Subtype)

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Force damage is a specialized kinetic subtype consisting of concentrated kinetic fields, telekinetic pressure, and directed gravitic displacement.
- **Armor Penetration**: **Ignores 1/2 of Target Armor DR** automatically, making it exceptionally effective against fortified armor plates and heavy carapaces.

### Metaphysical
- **Telekinetic Blast**: Direct concussive projections of pure mental willpower.
- **Repulsor Wave**: Concentrated force pulses that shatter structures and repel targets.

### Modern & Advanced Tech
- **Concussive Force Projectors**: Directed acoustic or gravitic shockwaves.
- **Kinetic Field Emitters**: Devices compressing atmospheric air into high-density kinetic projectiles.

### Tactical Notes
Because Force damage bypasses half of Armor DR, it serves as the premier anti-tank and anti-fortification kinetic damage vector.
