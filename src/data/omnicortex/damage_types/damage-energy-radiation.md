# Ra-D / Radiation Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Ra-D (Radiation) damage delivers ionizing gamma rays, neutron fluxes, and radioactive isotopes that tear through cellular DNA and cause immediate radiation sickness, nausea, and long-term mutation.

### Metaphysical
- **Radiokinesis**: Generating radiation fields, transmuting isotopes, and inducing acute radiation sickness.
- **Radiation Evocation**: Summoning glowing, irradiated entities emanating lethal rad-auras.
- **Radioactive Wards**: Irradiating objects to poison anyone handling them without shielding.

### Archaic Tech
- **Radioactive Minerals**: Crude weapons tipped with refined pitchblende or radium ores (*hazardous to users*).

### Modern Tech
- **Dirty Bombs & Radiological Dispersal**: Devices dispersing radioactive dust over battlefield sectors.
- **Radiation Guns**: Directed gamma-ray projectors penetrating light structures.
- **Rad-Mines**: Perimeter mines saturating areas with intense localized beta radiation.

### Advanced Tech
- **Neutron Cannons**: Particle beams penetrating heavy tank armor to kill organic crews while leaving hardware intact.
- **Gamma-Ray Lasers (Grasers)**: Relativistic laser weapons inflicting massive cellular necrosis.
- **Active Radiation Emitters**: Choke-point denial systems bathing corridors in lethal ionizing fields.

### Tactical Notes
Radiation damage ignores soft biological tissue protection and inflicts the Exhausted and Sickness conditions on failed Fortitude saves.
