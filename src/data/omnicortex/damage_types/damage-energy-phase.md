# Spectral / Phase Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Spectral and Phase damage (also termed ghost or incorporeal damage) bypasses physical armor entirely, directly disrupting the target's life force, neurological matrix, or ethereal essence.
- **Armor Bypass**: Completely **ignores physical Armor DR**.

### Metaphysical
- **Phasing**: Shifting between corporeal and incorporeal states to pass through barriers and deliver phase strikes.
- **Ghostly Touch**: Attacks passing through armor plates to drain vitality, inflict fear, or disrupt concentration.
- **Spirit Weapons**: Manifesting weapons of pure spiritual energy that strike directly at the soul.

### Archaic Tech
- **Blessed Weapons**: Consecrated or runic weapons capable of harming ghosts and bypassing physical armor.
- **Ghost-Banishing Wards**: Artifacts creating sacred geometric zones that repel phased entities.

### Modern Tech
- **Phase Disruptors**: Wave emitters forcing phased or incorporeal beings back into tangible reality.
- **Ghost-Detecting Sensors**: EMF and quantum fluctuation detectors tracking spiritual profiles.

### Advanced Tech
- **Phase Rifles**: Firearms projecting quantum-shifted bolts that phase through cover and armor.
- **Phase Blades**: Melee weapons passing harmlessly through outer plating to sever internal organs.
- **Phase Field Emitters**: Devices establishing spatial phasing bubbles for tactical infiltration.

### Tactical Notes
Phase damage is the ultimate counter against heavily armored tanks and juggernauts, resisted only by Willpower and active energy wards.
