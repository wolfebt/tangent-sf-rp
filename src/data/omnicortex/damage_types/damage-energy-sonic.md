# Sonic / Sound Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Sonic damage utilizes acoustic shockwaves and harmonic resonance to vibrate matter.
- **Armor Bypass**: **Ignores most physical armor** by transmitting vibrations directly through solid plates into internal organs and systems.

### Metaphysical
- **Sonokinesis**: Mentally generating sonic shockwaves, directional deafening blasts, and acoustic shields.
- **Sound Evocation**: Summoning resonance entities made of pure acoustic force.
- **Sonic Enchantments**: Imbuing melee weapons with high-vibration humming edges.

### Archaic Tech
- **War Horns and Battle Drums**: Acoustic intimidation and disorientation instruments.
- **Whistling Projectiles**: Piercing whistle arrows creating auditory distraction and disorientation.

### Modern Tech
- **Sonic Grenades & Flash-Crackers**: Devices rupturing eardrums and causing concussive inner-ear trauma.
- **Sonic Cannons & LRADs**: Directional acoustic weapons that incapacitate or disorient targets.
- **Acoustic Disruptors**: Continuous sound wave generators that disrupt radio comms and induce nausea.

### Advanced Tech
- **Sonic Lasers & Masers (Sasers)**: Coherent acoustic beams cutting matter at resonant frequencies.
- **Sonic Barriers**: Deflection shields using acoustic standing waves to deflect kinetic projectiles.
- **Active Acoustic Cloaking**: Advanced noise-cancellation matrices masking engine and movement signatures.

### Tactical Notes
Sonic damage is especially devastating against creatures with acute hearing or delicate sensory clusters, and bypasses rigid breastplates.
