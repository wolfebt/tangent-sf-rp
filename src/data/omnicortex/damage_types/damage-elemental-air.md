# Air Elemental Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Air elemental damage harnesses atmospheric force, pressure waves, and howling gales. It knocks combatants off their feet, throws them against obstacles, and disrupts aerial flight.

### Metaphysical
- **Aerokinesis**: Generating howling gales, vacuum pockets, and concussive air blasts.
- **Air Evocation**: Summoning sylphs, storm elementals, and living whirlwinds.
- **Air Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with pressurized air bursts that throw targets backwards.

### Archaic Tech
- **Bellows & Wind Traps**: Channeling natural air currents into defensive chutes and chutes.

### Modern & Advanced Tech
- **Compressed Air Cannons**: Weapons firing dense slugs of hyper-compressed atmospheric gas.
- **Vortex Generators**: Technological emitters creating miniature localized tornadoes.
- **Pressure Wave Projectors**: Shockwave generators causing severe internal trauma via explosive decompression.

### Tactical Notes
Air attacks force Might saves to resist being knocked **Prone** or pushed 10+ feet backwards.
