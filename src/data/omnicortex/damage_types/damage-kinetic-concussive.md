# Concussive / Impact Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Concussive damage represents physical impact from something generally larger than the target's main body (smashing, crashing, crushing, falls, explosions).
- **Whole-Body Dispersion**: Traumatic energy is dispersed across the entire target organism or structure.
- **Vitality / Health Split**: Damage can be **divided equally between Vitality and Health** if the character attempts an active defense or soak, regardless of whether the check succeeds.

### Archaic Tech
- **Crashing**: High-impact collisions with vehicles or terrain (*Half DR if Hard Armor without Crash Suit; Force Field DR is half effective*).
- **Falling Damage**: Falling from greater than character height (*Ignores DR of physical protection and force fields*).
- **Siege Weapons**: Catapults, trebuchets, and battering rams (*Half DR if Hard Armor; Force Field DR is half effective*).

### Modern Tech
- **Explosives**: Blast waves from grenades, mines, and artillery (*Half DR of Hard Armor without Blast Suit; Force Field DR is half effective*).

### Advanced Tech
- **Gravity Weapons**: Gravitational crushers and wells (*Ignores solid armor DR; Force Field DR is half effective*).
- **Weaponized Force Fields**: Crushing or repelling fields (*Ignores DR of solid protection; Force Field DR is fully effective*).

### Tactical Notes
Concussive damage is uniquely dangerous because it threatens permanent Health loss even while a character still possesses Vitality points.
