# Pyro / Fire Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Pyro and Fire damage incinerates targets, causing immediate agony, melting materials, and setting combustible targets ablaze. It frequently applies the ongoing **Burning status effect** (1d6 Fire damage/round + Will DC 12 panic check).

### Metaphysical
- **Pyrokinesis**: Creating and manipulating fire with the mind (fire walls, localized conflagrations).
- **Fire Evocation**: Summoning fire spirits or elementals resistant to heat that unleash flame streams.
- **Fire Enchantments**: Imbuing blades or projectiles with fiery ignition on contact.

### Archaic Tech
- **Torches, Fire Arrows & Incendiary Pots**: Simple combustion weapons for area denial.
- **Primitive Flamethrowers**: Liquid fuel siphon projectors producing continuous flame cones.
- **Fire Traps**: Pitch pits and trigger-ignited incendiary snares.

### Modern Tech
- **Advanced Flamethrowers**: High-pressure infantry and vehicle-mounted flamethrowers.
- **Incendiary Ammunition**: Bullets filled with white phosphorus or thermite igniting upon target impact.
- **Fire Bombs & Napalm**: Area-saturation incendiary munitions.

### Advanced Tech
- **Laser Weapons**: Focused high-energy coherent photonic beams causing thermal ignition.
- **Plasma Weapons**: Magnetic containment nozzles firing superheated plasma bolts that melt through vehicle armor.
- **Energy Pulse Emitters**: Thermal pulse cannons firing bursts or sweeping sprays of intense heat.

### Tactical Notes
Critical hits with Fire weapons automatically ignite targets, forcing them to spend a full action to Stop, Drop, and Roll.
