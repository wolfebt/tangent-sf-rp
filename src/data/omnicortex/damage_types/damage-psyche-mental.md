# Psyche / Mental Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Mental damage targets the conscious mind, neural synapses, and psyche rather than the physical flesh.
- **Armor Bypass**: **Completely ignores Physical Armor DR**. Resisted exclusively by mental discipline, **Willpower Saves**, and psychic wards.

### Metaphysical
- **Psychic Assault**: Projecting pure telepathic force into an adversary's brain, inflicting agony or unconsciousness.
- **Emotional Manipulation**: Flooding enemy minds with panic, despair, or uncontrollable frenzy.
- **Mental Intrusion & Mind Blast**: Shattering conscious thought, inducing amnesia, or seizing motor control.

### Advanced Tech
- **Psychic Amplifiers**: Neural headgear amplifying the psionic output of trained espers.
- **Neural Disruptors**: Electromagnetic microwave beams scrambling brain synapses to cause disorientation and blackout.
- **Mind Control Transceivers**: Cybernetic overrides commanding enemy nervous systems.

### Tactical Notes
Mental attacks induce the **Dazed** (1 action/turn, no reactions) or **Stunned** status on failed saves, making them devastating against heavily armored brutes with low mental defenses.
