# Bleed Damage (Status Effect)

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Bleeding is not an independent weapon damage type, but rather a persistent trauma effect inflicted by critical strikes from slashing and piercing weapons, or specialized shrapnel and serrated blades.

### Mechanical Effect
- Target suffers **1d4 Damage at the start of each turn**.
- **Armor Bypass**: Bleeding damage **completely ignores Armor DR**.
- **Cure / Removal**: Successfully stabilized with a **Medicine Check (DC 10)**, medical trauma pack, or any restorative metaphysical healing ability.

### Tactical Notes
Bleeding steadily depletes life force during prolonged tactical skirmishes and synergizes destructively with Exhausted or Dazed conditions.
