# Force Pulse / Disruption Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Force Pulse / Disruption damage directly attacks the molecular and subatomic forces holding matter together. Targets hit by disruption energy weaken, crack, and dissolve into dust upon critical failure.
- **Armor Bypass**: Bypasses conventional physical armor by destabilizing the atomic cohesion of the plates.

### Metaphysical
- **Disruption Field**: Projecting an aura that breaks atomic bonds on contact.
- **Force Manipulation**: Manipulating gravity, inertia, and molecular coherence to tear targets apart.
- **Disruptive Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with bond-shattering resonance.

### Modern Tech
- **Disruptor Pistols & Rifles**: Weapons firing bolts of disruption energy, making targets brittle and crumbling them.
- **Disruption Grenades**: Munitions creating localized shock-waves that weaken structural walls and vehicles.

### Advanced Tech
- **Disruptor Cannons**: Heavy ship-mounted beam weapons capable of disintegrating armored hulls.
- **Molecular Disruptors**: Surgical weapons tuned to specific atomic bonds, disintegrating armor without harming crew.
- **Reality Anchors**: Devices reversing disruptive fields or tearing localized spatial frameworks apart.

### Tactical Notes
Critical strikes with Disruption damage disintegrate targets reduced to 0 HP, leaving no remains for biological revival.
