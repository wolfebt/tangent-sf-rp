# Kinetic: Piercing (Puncturing)

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Piercing damage focuses on puncturing and penetrating targets, often causing deep, narrow wounds. It is particularly effective against unarmored or lightly armored opponents, but can also be used to exploit weak points in heavier armor.

### Metaphysical
- **Kinetic Darts**: Telekinetically propelled needles or shards launched at supersonic velocity.
- **Spike Evocation**: Projecting crystalline or spiritual spikes that impale target vitals.

### Archaic Tech
- **Arrows and Bolts**: Projectiles for bows and crossbows penetrating flesh and light armor.
- **Spears and Lances**: Polearms with sharp thrusting tips; lances deliver devastating mounted momentum.
- **Daggers and Stilettos**: Concealable blades engineered for close-quarters vital organ penetration.

### Modern Tech
- **Bayonets**: Rifle-mounted blades providing immediate thrusting lethality.
- **Flechettes**: Fin-stabilized aerodynamic steel darts fired from shotguns or canisters.
- **Armor-Piercing (AP) Ammunition**: Hardened steel or tungsten penetrator cores defeating armor plates.

### Advanced Tech
- **Gauss Rifles and Coilguns**: Electromagnetic acceleration arrays launching hypervelocity kinetic slugs.
- **Needle Guns**: Weapons firing microscopic, high-velocity darts delivering toxins or nanite payloads.
- **Railguns**: Electromagnetic sliding armature cannons achieving orbital-grade muzzle velocities.

### Tactical Notes
Piercing attacks are ideal for called shots against internal components, limb joints, and vital organs, and frequently induce the **Bleeding** condition upon critical trauma.
