# Water Elemental Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Water elemental damage utilizes the hydraulic impact and drowning lethality of rushing water, ocean pressure, and concentrated fluid streams.

### Metaphysical
- **Hydrokinesis**: Manipulating fluid pressure, conjuring tidal waves, and creating watery spheres.
- **Water Evocation**: Summoning water elementals and spirits that envelope and drown foes.
- **Water Enchantments**: Adding high-pressure aquatic sheaths to weapons.

### Archaic & Modern Tech
- **Water Wheels & Traps**: Diverting torrential flows into defensive funnels.
- **High-Pressure Water Cannons**: Riot water cannons and industrial waterjet cutters delivering concussive blasts.

### Advanced Tech
- **Water Vortex Generators**: Devices forming localized whirlpools that swallow small vehicles.
- **Hydraulic Pulse Emitters**: Pressurized fluid shockwave generators shattering internal organs.

### Tactical Notes
Water damage can induce the **Drowning** suffocation condition and extinguishes Burning status automatically.
