# Umbral / Shadow Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Umbral damage taps into the void, drawing strength from the absence of light to drain vitality, freeze blood, and erode psychic morale.

### Metaphysical
- **Umbrokinesis**: Solidifying shadows into blades, creating darkness fields, and snuffing light.
- **Shadow Evocation**: Summoning shadow wraiths and shadowy beasts that drain life on touch.
- **Shadow Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with vitality-leeching dark energy.

### Archaic & Modern Tech
- **Darkness Powders**: Chemical smoke absorbing light and plunging areas into darkness.
- **Disorienting Strobe Emitters**: Rapid-flash devices disrupting dark-adapted sight.

### Advanced Tech
- **Dark Energy Projectors**: Cannons firing entropy beams that drain battery power and life force.
- **Shadow Field Generators**: Devices bending light around installations to swallow incoming optics.

### Tactical Notes
Umbral damage targets biological and mental stamina, frequently applying the **Exhausted** or **Dazed** conditions.
