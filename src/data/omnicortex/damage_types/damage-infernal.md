# Infernal Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Infernal damage channels the destructive and corrupting energies of the infernal planes and the Stygian abyss. It combines hellish heat with spiritual corruption, leaving lasting scars upon the victim's mind and soul.

### Metaphysical
- **Infernal Evocation**: Summoning demons, devils, and hellish fiends to ravage enemy ranks.
- **Infernal Pacts & Curses**: Channeling dark planar bargains to strike down foes with agonizing soul-fire.
- **Hellfire Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with black flames that resist being extinguished.

### Archaic Tech
- **Infernal Relics**: Ancient cursed daggers and blood-forged talismans carrying planar corruption.
- **Ritual Implements**: Occult focus daggers used in sacrificial invocations.

### Advanced Tech
- **Interdimensional Plasma Weavers**: Experimental devices siphoning chaotic planar energy into weapons batteries.

### Tactical Notes
Infernal damage inflicts persistent Burning and may induce memory loss or spiritual degradation associated with the Stygian influence.
