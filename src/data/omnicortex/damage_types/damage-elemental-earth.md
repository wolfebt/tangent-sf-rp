# Earth Elemental Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Earth elemental damage channels the weight, density, and crushing mass of stone, soil, and tectonic movement. It delivers massive blunt trauma and destabilizes footing.

### Metaphysical
- **Geokinesis**: Hurl boulders, create stone barriers, open chasms, and trigger tremors.
- **Earth Evocation**: Summoning earth golems and lithic elementals to crush enemy lines.
- **Earth Enchantments**: Imbuing weapons with dense stone weight to smash through shields.

### Archaic Tech
- **Siege Engines**: Catapults, trebuchets, and ballistae hurling heavy stone projectiles.
- **Rockfall Traps**: Deadfalls and weighted stone counterweight traps.

### Modern & Advanced Tech
- **Seismic Explosives**: High-yield subterranean charges triggering localized earthquakes.
- **Seismic Cannons**: Directed tectonic emitters shattering concrete foundations.
- **Gravitic Ground-Slammers**: Devices multiplying local gravity to crush targets into the floor.

### Tactical Notes
Earth attacks force Reflex saves to avoid being knocked **Prone** or buried in difficult terrain.
