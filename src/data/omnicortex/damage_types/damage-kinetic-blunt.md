# Kinetic: Blunt (Crushing)

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Blunt damage focuses on crushing and bruising targets, often causing widespread trauma, broken bones, and internal injuries. It is particularly effective against unarmored or lightly armored opponents, and can also be used to damage structures or disable machinery.

### Metaphysical
- **Telekinetic Impact**: Hurling dense physical objects with mental force to crush barriers and enemies.
- **Gravity Compression**: Increasing localized gravitational mass to inflict blunt crushing pressure.

### Archaic Tech
- **Clubs and Staves**: Simple, yet effective weapons made from wood, bone, or stone. Clubs offer raw power; staves provide reach and versatility.
- **Maces and Flails**: Metal-headed weapons designed to crush armor and bone.
- **Hammers and War Picks**: Hammers offer blunt force trauma; war picks feature pointed ends to hook opponents.

### Modern Tech
- **Shock Batons and Truncheons**: Law enforcement tools delivering blunt strikes and non-lethal electrical shocks.
- **Telescopic Batons**: Collapsible steel batons providing reach and striking power.
- **Impact Weapons**: Weighted batons or spring-loaded kinetic impact devices.

### Advanced Tech
- **Power Fists and Gauntlets**: Mechanically augmented gauntlets amplifying punching force with built-in kinetic or energy projectors.
- **Force Hammers and Mallets**: Weapons utilizing localized force fields to amplify striking mass, crushing heavy armor.
- **Gravity Weapons**: Devices manipulating gravitational fields to crush targets.

### Tactical Notes
Blunt damage frequently inflicts stunning or knockdown effects, and excels at disabling robotic joints or concussing organic anatomy without piercing biological vitals.
