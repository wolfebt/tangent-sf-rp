# Celestial Damage

**Category**: Damage Types & Energy Profiles

## Overview
Celestial damage embodies divine light, higher-planar righteousness, and the transcendent energy of sacred domains.
- **Targeting**: Particularly effective against evil entities, demons, undead, and creatures of corruption.
- **Special Properties**: Often dispels curses, banishes otherworldly entities, or selectively harms foes while sparing righteous allies.

### Metaphysical
- **Divine Light Manipulation**: Directing rays of sacred energy that sear evil creatures.
- **Celestial Evocation**: Summoning angels, archons, and seraphic spirits of light.
- **Holy Enchantments**: Consecrating blades to smite evil and bypass infernal resistances.

### Archaic Tech
- **Consecrated Weapons**: Blades blessed in sacred temples and inscribed with sanctified runes.
- **Holy Water & Reliquaries**: Blessed fluids burning fiendish flesh like acid.

### Advanced Tech
- **Resonance Harmonics**: Quantum-aligned frequency cannons tuned to higher-dimensional planar vibrations.

### Tactical Notes
Fiends and undead suffer double damage or Disadvantage against Celestial attacks, while holy wards mitigate incoming corruption.
