# Incapacitated

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanics & Debuffs
When a character takes damage that reduces them to 0 Health:
- The character falls unconscious immediately.
- They drop anything they are holding and fall **Prone**.
- Any excess damage is applied to Vitality (if any remains).
- The character cannot take actions, move, or make active defense reactions.
- If Vitality is also depleted (0), the character enters the **Death's Door** state.
