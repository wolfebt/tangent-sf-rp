# Comatose

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanics & Debuffs
A character who is **Comatose** is in a state of profound unconsciousness:
- They are completely unaware of their surroundings.
- They cannot speak, take actions, or make active defense reactions.
- Automatically fails Reflex saves or active evasion checks.
- Melee attacks against a comatose creature have Advantage.
- Characters at Death's Door suffer from this condition until stabilized and healed.
