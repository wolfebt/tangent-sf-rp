# Prone

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanical Effect
Lying on the ground:
- **Ranged Attacks vs. Target**: Disadvantage (and +2 Defense per range category after Point Blank).
- **Melee Attacks vs. Target**: Advantage (grants High Ground bonus to melee/point blank foes).

## Cure / Removal
**Move Action** to stand up.
