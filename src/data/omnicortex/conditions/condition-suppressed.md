# Suppressed

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanical Effect
Pinned down by volume of fire. **Leaving cover triggers an immediate Attack of Opportunity** from the suppressing combatant.

## Cure / Removal
Enemy stops firing, reloads, or is neutralized.
