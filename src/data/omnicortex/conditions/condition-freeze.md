# Freeze

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanical Effect
Target is encased/chilled. **Speed = 0**. Target gains **Vulnerability to Physical & Sonic Damage**.

## Cure / Removal
**Strength Check (DC 20)** to shatter ice or applying **Fire Damage** to melt.
