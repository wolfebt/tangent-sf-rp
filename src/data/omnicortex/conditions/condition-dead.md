# Dead

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanics & Debuffs
Death is reached when the Death's Door clock expires, through massive damage, or catastrophic structural destruction:
- The character is lifeless and ceases all biological or synthetic life functions.
- Souls or cognitive matrix detachments may occur depending on setting and cosmology.

## Revivification ("The High Cost of Dying")
Return from the dead is rare and involves high-level Metaphysics or Tech (TL5). It carries a heavy toll:
- **Zero Karma:** A revived character loses ALL remaining Karma Points.
- **-5 Experience Debt:** The character suffers a -5 Experience Debt due to the trauma, taken as a reduction in a trait (or Traits) or as a reduction in accumulated/future experience until the debt is paid.
