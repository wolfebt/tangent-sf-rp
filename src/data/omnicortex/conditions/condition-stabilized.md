# Stabilized

**Category**: Tactical Conditions & Status Effects

## Mechanics & Debuffs
A character who has been **Stabilized**:
- The Death's Door countdown clock is permanently halted.
- The character is no longer actively dying.
- The character remains **Unconscious**, **Prone**, and **Incapacitated** at 0 Health until restored by medical attention, rest, or healing effects.
- If the character takes additional lethal damage while stabilized, they re-enter Death's Door.
