# The Relic Hunter

**Sphere**: Savants (The Rationals)  
**Core Concept**: Explorer / Lore Expert / Trap Specialist

## Overview
Unveils the secrets of ancient ruins and artifacts, reconstructing chronicles of civilizations long gone.
