## **Traits**

### *Combat Skills*

Defending themselves against hostile creatures or other threats.

*\+2 to a Combat Skill of choice*

### *Intuition*

The character may have a heightened sense of intuition, allowing them to anticipate danger.

*Re-Roll any one failed Alertness check per day*

### *Mental Toughness*

Withstanding stress, fear, and other psychological challenges that might arise in a hostile environment.

*\+2 Willpower Checks*

### *Physical Endurance*

Withstanding harsh conditions and resisting fatigue.

*\+2 Fortitude Checks*

### *Pilot Skills*

Experienced piloting various craft in hostile environments.

*\+2 Piloting*

### *Resourcefulness*

Adapting to changing circumstances and making the most of limited resources.

*Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage*

### *Survival Skills*

Finding food, water, and shelter in hostile environments.

*\+2 Survival Skill Checks*

### *Technical Skills*

Repairing or modifying equipment to suit the needs of a hostile environment.

*\+1 to Mechanical, Electrical and Engineering Skill Checks*

## Consolidated & Modernized Origin Traits (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)

*(Players may select their 2 free Origin Traits from either the narrative traits above or the consolidated mechanical traits below.)*

* **Apex Vigilance:** May Take 10 on Alertness checks even under stress or danger.
* **Environmental Resistance:** +2 to Fortitude Saves against poison, disease, radiation, and extreme weather.
* **Hardened Survivor:** +1 to Might and Fortitude Saving Throws.
* **Predator's Instinct:** +2 to Initiative checks.
* **Scavenger's Eye:** +2 to Vocation (Salvager) and Knowledge (Survival) checks to locate water, shelter, or usable parts.
* **Stalker:** May Take 10 on Stealth checks in natural or ruined terrain.

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis (CRISP 11-Faction Matrix)**

## **05\. HOSTILE**

*Dangerous, poisonous, extreme weather, survival, ruins.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Thick black obsidian walls holding back a hellscape. Guard towers, razor wire, prisoners mining toxic crystals. Ash falling like snow.  
* **STYLE:** Penal Legion Fortress, Gulag.  
* **PALETTE:** Obsidian Black, Ash Grey, Searchlight White, Lava Red.  
* **TOKENS:** Walls, chains, ash, suffering, guards, mining.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** A facility harvesting energy from a volcano or storm. Reinforced white ceramic stained by soot. Expendable drone workers. Shields flaring against acid rain.  
* **STYLE:** Geo-Thermal Plant, Hazardous Industry.  
* **PALETTE:** Soot-Stained White, Hazard Yellow, Acid Green, Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Shields, steam, drones, corrosion, warning lights.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** A fortified hunting lodge in a jungle of carnivorous plants. Trophies on the walls, heavy blast-shutters, flamethrower turrets.  
* **STYLE:** Wyrm-Hunt Camp, Death World Safari.  
* **PALETTE:** Jungle Green, Blood Red, Iron Grey, Flame Orange.  
* **TOKENS:** Trophies, heavy weapons, giant plants, rain, mud.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** A shelter made inside the ribs of a giant dead beast or crashed ship. Radiation storms outside. Makeshift air-scrubbers rattling.  
* **STYLE:** Wasteland Scavenger, Post-Apoc.  
* **PALETTE:** Bone White, Rust Red, Radiation Green, Dust.  
* **TOKENS:** Giant bones, scrap, sandstorms, survival gear.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Hidden inside a toxic asteroid or gas giant cloud. Corroded metal, leaky seals, darker and grittier than usual. Hiding from the law.  
* **STYLE:** Smuggler's Hideout, Toxic Base.  
* **PALETTE:** Corroded Green, Rust, Shadow, Flickering Light.  
* **TOKENS:** Gas masks, leaks, corrosion, shadows, hiding.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Aggressive terraforming plants fighting back against a toxic waste dump. Giant mutated venus flytraps acting as guards. Spores in the air.  
* **STYLE:** Reclamation Zone, Weaponized Nature.  
* **PALETTE:** Toxic Green, Violet Spores, Rot Brown, Vibrant Flowers.  
* **TOKENS:** Giant plants, thorns, spores, ruins, aggressive growth.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** A perfect, clean bubble in the middle of hell. Outside is fire and brimstone; inside is a calm garden. The contrast is stark.  
* **STYLE:** Void-Shielded Sanctuary, Bubble City.  
* **PALETTE:** Forcefield Blue, Garden Green, Hellscape Red (Background).  
* **TOKENS:** Bubble shield, contrast, garden, calm vs chaos.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** High-tech, aerodynamic bunkers designed to withstand hurricane winds. Underground hydroponics. Society thriving despite the planet.  
* **STYLE:** Extreme Survival Hab, Arctic Station.  
* **PALETTE:** Ice White, Wind Blue, Silver, Warm Interior Light.  
* **TOKENS:** Aerodynamics, snow/sand, wind, underground, tech.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** Armored viewing platforms for rich tourists to watch dangerous beasts. Force-fields, luxury interiors overlooking lava flows.  
* **STYLE:** Xeno-Safari, Danger Tourism.  
* **PALETTE:** Luxury Gold, Lava Red, Shield Blue, Safety Glass.  
* **TOKENS:** Viewing deck, champagne, monsters, shields, safety.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Machines stripping a hostile world to its core. They ignore the acid rain/heat. Rust-proof alloys, relentless automated strip-mining.  
* **STYLE:** Termination Zone, Planetary Strip-Mine.  
* **PALETTE:** Metal Grey, Strip-Mine Brown, Laser Red, Acid Green.  
* **TOKENS:** Excavators, pits, smoke, lasers, indifference.

### **KITIN COLLECTIVE (THE STAR HIVE)**

* **CONTEXT:** Radiation-lashed volcanic badlands colonized effortlessly by heavy-carapace Murex and Maantene strains. Thick obsidian resin domes shrug off acid rain and ionizing fallout while Spire-Sentries track apex megafauna.
* **STYLE:** Evolutionary Apex Fortress, Radiation-Hardened Hive.
* **PALETTE:** Scorched Obsidian, Acid Green, Fallout Amber, Chitin Black.
* **TOKENS:** Heavy radiation carapace, acid-slick resin, bio-plasma AA turrets, apex predator husks.
