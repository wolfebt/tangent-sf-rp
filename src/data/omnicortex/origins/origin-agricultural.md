# Agricultural Origin

**Category**: Origins  
**Habitat**: Planetary Biome  
**Starting Skill Points**: 20 SP (allocated to Society Skills)  
**Starting Traits**: 2 Free selections (Additional traits cost 1 CP each)

## Description
Agri-Worlds are essential for mass food production. These worlds typically have low development and population, ranging from small rural houses, barns, and silos to expansive mega-structure Arcologies and preservation biospheres.

## Society Skills (20 SP Pool)
- Piloting
- Alertness
- Knowledge (Nature)
- Knowledge (Survival)
- Knowledge (Technology)
- Vocation (Any)

## Typical Archetypes / Native Vocations
- Farmer
- Hunter
- Preservationist
- Pilot
- Technician
- Community Leader

## Origin Traits (2 Free Selections, Additional 1 CP Each)
### Animal Husbandry
Expertise in raising and caring for livestock. Understands animal behavior, breeding, and nutrition. Can manage a farm with a diverse range of animals.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to Animal Handling**

### Botanical Knowledge
Extensive knowledge of plants, crops, and agricultural techniques. Understands the science behind growing and cultivating various types of vegetation.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to Botany Checks**

### Community Building
Values community and may have skills in building and maintaining relationships with other farmers, suppliers, and customers. Can negotiate deals, organize cooperative efforts, and foster camaraderie.  
*Bonus*: **Once per Day may reroll a Social Skill Check**

### Green Thumb
Natural talent for nurturing plants and making them thrive even in challenging environments. Has a knack for identifying needs of different plants.  
*Bonus*: **Checks involving nurturing and helping plants may be made at Advantage**

### Mechanical Skills
Mechanical skills related to farming equipment and machinery. Can repair and maintain farming tools, vehicles, pumps, conveyors and other automated systems.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to Mechanical Skill Checks**

### Resourcefulness
The character is able to make the most of limited resources and find creative solutions to problems, especially important where resources may be scarce.  
*Bonus*: **Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage**

### Survival Skills
Survival skills honed from living and working in remote or harsh environments. Can navigate and forage for resources, making them self-sufficient.  
*Bonus*: **+2 Survival Skill Checks**

### Sustainable Practices
Knowledgeable about sustainable farming practices and strives to minimize environmental impact. Prioritizes organic methods and water conservation.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to any Agriculture based checks in a prepared grow area and to prepare and maintain an area.**


## Extended Lore & Background
### Origin Overview
Agri-Worlds are essential for mass food production. These worlds typically have low development and population. Most communities range from small houses and other farm-related structures, including barns, silos and the like, to expansive mega-structure Arcologies. They also include most Preservation/Green Worlds, those purposefully left undeveloped but with self-sustaining small communities. Farming and preserved wilds are the focus of these worlds.

### Society Skills (20 SP Pool)
Piloting, Alertness, Knowledge (Nature, Survival, Technology), Vocation (any)

### Typical Archetypes
Farmer, Hunter, Preservationist, Pilot, Technician, Community Leader
