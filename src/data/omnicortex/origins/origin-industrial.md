# 1.05.06. ORIGIN: INDUSTRIAL (+2 WS)

* **Executive Summary:** Foundry worlds, orbital shipyards, and automated manufacturing hives that forge the hardware of empires (Braken, Vulcanis).
* **EUFT Starting Wealth Modifier:** **+2 WS**
* **Canonical Society Skills (20 SP Allocation):** Athletics, Computers, Knowledge (Engineering, Technology, or Any), Vocation (Any)
* **Typical Archetypes:** Foundry Worker, Mecha Tech, Union Organizer, Demolitionist, Foreman, Miner, Factory Worker, Engineer, Security Officer, Corporate Executive

# **INDUSTRIAL**

These worlds are developed to exploit available resources and are designed for maximum efficiency. Buildings are often reinforced or mobile, with simple designs. These worlds typically have a non-green operating philosophy that prioritizes profit.

## **Traits**

### *Adaptability*

The ability to adapt to new environments and situations is important for an industrial character, as they may encounter unexpected obstacles.

\+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily

### *Mechanical Aptitude*

Industrial characters may have a natural talent for working with machines and technology, allowing them to repair, modify, and operate complex equipment.

\+1 to Mechanical, Electrical and Engineering Skill Checks

### *Physical Strength*

Industrial work can be physically demanding, so characters who work in this field may have above-average strength and endurance.

\+2 Might Checks

### *Practicality*

Industrial characters may be more focused on practical solutions than abstract or theoretical concepts.

\+2 Reason (Intellect) Checks

### *Problem-Solving Skills*

Excels at problem-solving and critical thinking

Once per Day may reroll a Mental Skill Check

### *Resourcefulness*

Industrial characters may be used to working with limited resources and finding creative solutions to problems.

Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage

### *Risk-Taking*

Working in an industrial setting can be dangerous, so these characters may be more willing to take risks and face challenges head-on.

\+1 Karma Point

### *Specialized Knowledge*

Depending on the setting, industrial characters may have specialized knowledge in areas such as engineering, metallurgy, or manufacturing.

\+2 to a Vocation Specialization

### *Teamwork*

Industrial work often involves collaborating with others to achieve a common goal, so industrial characters may have strong teamwork skills.

additional \+1 to Aid bonuses

## Consolidated & Modernized Origin Traits (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)

*(Players may select their 2 free Origin Traits from either the narrative traits above or the consolidated mechanical traits below.)*

* **Assembly Efficiency:** Reduces crafting time by 10 percent (or grants +2 to Crafting/Vocation checks).
* **Heavy Lifter:** Counts as +1 Strength for lifting, hauling, and operating industrial exo-frames.
* **Hazard Conditioning:** +2 to Fortitude Saves against smoke, chemical fumes, and industrial heat/noise.
* **Machine Affinity:** +2 to Knowledge (Technology) and +1 additional bonus from Vocation Tool Kits.
* **Shift Endurance:** Rolls Fortitude checks against Fatigue from long work shifts at Advantage.
* **Union Solidarity:** +2 to Social checks with working-class laborers, mechanics, and guild members.

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis (CRISP 11-Faction Matrix)**

## **06\. INDUSTRIAL**

*Manufacturing, mining, factories, pollution, machinery.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Planet-sized factories producing tanks. Gothic architecture mixed with heavy industry. Statues of workers holding hammers. Furnaces the size of cities.  
* **STYLE:** The Forge World, Dieselpunk Empire.  
* **PALETTE:** Soot Black, Furnace Orange, Iron Grey, Brass.  
* **TOKENS:** Smoke, fire, statues, gears, massive scale.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Endless clean white rooms with robot arms moving in perfect synchronization. No humans, just humming servers and pristine assembly lines.  
* **STYLE:** Automated Assembly, High-Tech Fab.  
* **PALETTE:** Sterile White, Robot Arm Orange, LED Blue, Chrome.  
* **TOKENS:** Robots, conveyor belts, sparks, clean rooms, speed.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Blacksmithing on a massive scale. Magma used to forge starship hulls. Sparks flying, sound of hammers, heat haze.  
* **STYLE:** Armory of the Dragon, Dwarven Forge.  
* **PALETTE:** Magma Red, Iron Black, Spark Yellow, Heat Haze.  
* **TOKENS:** Anvils, lava, chains, hammers, heat.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** Mountains of scrap metal and old ships. Cranes lifting rust-buckets. Sparks from cutting torches. Oil spills and grease.  
* **STYLE:** Salvage Yard, Scrapyard.  
* **PALETTE:** Rust Orange, Grease Black, Sky Blue, Metallic.  
* **TOKENS:** Junk piles, cranes, cutting torches, dismantling.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** A maze of pipes and distillation columns. Flares burning off gas. Leaking steam. Workers in grimy jumpsuits. It smells of sulfur.  
* **STYLE:** Refinery City, Petro-Chem.  
* **PALETTE:** Industrial Yellow, Pipe Grey, Steam White, Rust.  
* **TOKENS:** Pipes, steam, flares, gantries, catwalks.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Vats of bubbling green nutrient paste. Growing biological components (ship skins, armor) in giant pods. Wet, humid, smelling of yeast/earth.  
* **STYLE:** Bio-Synthesis Plant, Cloning Vat.  
* **PALETTE:** Vat Green, Flesh Pink, Amber Liquid, Organic Brown.  
* **TOKENS:** Vats, tubes, growth, organic shapes, humidity.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Looms of light weaving hard-light constructs. Delicate, precise, magical manufacturing. Floating crystals focusing energy beams.  
* **STYLE:** Aether-Weaving, Magic Loom.  
* **PALETTE:** Thread of Light Gold, Crystal Blue, Loom Wood.  
* **TOKENS:** Light threads, floating crystals, weaving, elegance.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Objects appearing out of "grey goo" baths. 3D printing at an atomic level. Clean, silver, fluid.  
* **STYLE:** Nanotech Foundry, Smart-Matter.  
* **PALETTE:** Liquid Silver, Nano-Grey, Clean White, Laser Blue.  
* **TOKENS:** Liquid metal, 3D printing, lasers, formation.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** High-end manufacturing. Individual workshops connected by trade floors. Quality over quantity. Display cases of finished goods.  
* **STYLE:** Artisan Guildhall, High-End Workshop.  
* **PALETTE:** Warm Wood, Brass, Workshop Lighting, Polished Metal.  
* **TOKENS:** Tools, workbenches, craft, detail, quality.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** The entire environment is the machine. Walls are gears, floors are conveyors. Infinite complexity. Drones flying everywhere.  
* **STYLE:** The Hive Factory, Inside the Watch.  
* **PALETTE:** Gunmetal, Gear Brass, Laser Red, Oil.  
* **TOKENS:** Gears, pistons, conveyor belts, endless motion.

### **KITIN COLLECTIVE (THE STAR HIVE)**

* **CONTEXT:** A colossal Hatchery-Foundry where biological war-frames (Apex-Cutters and Koleo-Primes) are grown in vertical Proto-Flux gestation vats. Murex weavers graft Syndess cores into living chitin chassis amidst rhythmic hydraulic pulses.
* **STYLE:** Biomorphic Bio-Foundry, Accelerated Organogenesis.
* **PALETTE:** Glossy Obsidian, Vat Emerald, Arterial Green, Amber Resin.
* **TOKENS:** Gestation vats, vein-piping cranes, Syndess integration pods, living mecha hulls, biological steam.
