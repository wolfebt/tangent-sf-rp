# Industrial Origin

**Category**: Origins  
**Habitat**: Industrial Heavy  
**Starting Skill Points**: 20 SP (allocated to Society Skills)  
**Starting Traits**: 2 Free selections (Additional traits cost 1 CP each)

## Description
These worlds are developed to exploit available natural resources and are designed for maximum efficiency. Heavy blast walls, automated smelters, toxic smokestacks, and modular worker complexes dominate.

## Society Skills (20 SP Pool)
- Knowledge (Technology)
- Knowledge (Science)
- Vocation (Any)
- Knowledge (Any)

## Typical Archetypes / Native Vocations
- Miner
- Factory Worker
- Engineer
- Foreman
- Security Officer
- Corporate Executive

## Origin Traits (2 Free Selections, Additional 1 CP Each)
### Adaptability
Adjusting rapidly to shifting manufacturing quotas, boiler hazards, and mechanical breakdowns.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily**

### Mechanical Aptitude
Natural talent for working with heavy machinery, hydraulic presses, automated assembly lines, and smelters.  
*Bonus*: **+1 to Mechanical, Electrical and Engineering Skill Checks**

### Physical Strength
Above-average muscle power developed through grueling shifts in ore refineries and foundries.  
*Bonus*: **+2 Might Checks**

### Practicality
Focused on functional, no-nonsense solutions over abstract or untested theories.  
*Bonus*: **+2 Logic Checks**

### Problem-Solving Skills
Diagnosing operational bottlenecks and fixing systemic failures on the plant floor.  
*Bonus*: **Once per Day may reroll a Mental Skill Check**

### Resourcefulness
Salvaging scrap parts and fabricating replacement gears on short notice.  
*Bonus*: **Once per Day make a Skill Check of choice at Advantage**

### Risk-Taking
Willing to take calculated physical risks in hazardous high-heat, high-voltage environments.  
*Bonus*: **+1 Karma Point**

### Specialized Knowledge
Deep technical specialization in metallurgy, chemical processing, or structural robotics.  
*Bonus*: **+2 to a Vocation Specialization**

### Teamwork
Flawless synchronization with crewmates operating heavy tandem machinery.  
*Bonus*: **additional +1 to Aid bonuses**


## Extended Lore & Background
### Origin Overview
These worlds are developed to exploit available resources and are designed for maximum efficiency. Buildings are often reinforced or mobile, with simple designs. These worlds typically have a non-green operating philosophy that prioritizes profit and output.

### Society Skills (20 SP Pool)
Knowledge (any), Vocation (any)

### Typical Archetypes
Miner, Factory Worker, Engineer, Foreman, Security Officer, Corporate Executive
