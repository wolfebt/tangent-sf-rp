# Construct Intelligence (Composite Invocation)

**Category**: Composite Invocations  
**Requisite Disciplines**: Mental + Energy

## Mechanics & Reality Manipulation
Imbues an inanimate computer network or robotic chassis with an awakened pseudo-sentient spirit.

## Invocation Rules
- Invocation Level is added to the **lowest** of the requisite Discipline Skills used.
