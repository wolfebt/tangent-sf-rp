# FIND FAMILIAR Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Dimension

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | \[Ritual\] \[Bond\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20 (Ritual)  
* **Time:** 1 Hour  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Permanent (until dismissed/killed)  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Summons a minor spirit or creature to serve as a permanent magical companion.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Bond:** Familiar gains 10 BP per Stage (aligning with Summoning core rules).  
* **Link:** Telepathic range increases by 1 mile per Stage.
