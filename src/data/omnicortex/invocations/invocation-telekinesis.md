# TELEKINESIS Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Utility/Attack | \[Manipulation\] \[Sustained\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** Single Object/Creature  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will (if creature) or Opposed Grapple
Effect Description:  
Lifting and moving objects with the mind.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Capacity:** Weight Movable \= (5 lbs x *Skill Rank*) squared.  
  * *Rank 1:* 25 lbs  
  * *Rank 2:* 100 lbs  
  * *Rank 5:* 625 lbs  
  * *Rank 10:* 2,500 lbs  
  * *Rank 20:* 10,000 lbs  
* **Velocity:** Movement speed of object \= 20ft x Stage.
