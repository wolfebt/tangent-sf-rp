# ETHEREALNESS Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Dimension

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Defense/Utility | \[Phase\] \[State Change\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Personal  
* **Area:** Self  
* **Duration:** Rounds/Level  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Shifts the caster's body partially out of phase, becoming invisible and incorporeal.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Duration:** 1 Round \+ (1 Round per Stage).  
* **Utility:** Can move through solid walls (5ft thick per Stage).
