# FORCE ARMOR Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Defense | \[Personal\] \[Buff\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self (or Touch at higher ranks)  
* **Area:** Personal  
* **Duration:** 1 Hour/Level  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Surrounds the caster in a skin-tight sheath of force.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Defense:** Grants \+4 Armor Bonus \+ (1 per Stage).  
* **Absorption:** Gains 5 Temporary HP per Stage.
