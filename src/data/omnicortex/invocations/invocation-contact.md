# CONTACT Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Sense) | Communication | \[Long Range\] \[Message\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Long / Planetary  
* **Area:** Single Mind  
* **Duration:** 1 Round  
* **Resistance:** None (Voluntary)
Effect Description:  
Allows brief mental contact with another known mind at very far distances.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Range:** Stage 1 (Planet) \-\> Stage 3 (System) \-\> Stage 5 (Galaxy).  
* **Bandwidth:** Message length increases per Stage.
