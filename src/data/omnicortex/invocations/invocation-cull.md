# CULL Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Entropy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Minor\] \[Negative Energy\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Causes 1 point of negative energy damage. If the target is near death (0 Vitality or wounded), it hastens their demise.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Severity:** Increases to 1d4 damage at higher Stages against dying targets.
