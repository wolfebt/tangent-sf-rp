# BLENDING Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Matter

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Utility | \[Camouflage\] \[Stealth\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Enhances the target's skin or clothing to mimic surrounding textures (Chameleon).  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Magnitude:** Stealth Bonus \= \+2 per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** At Stage 4, grants Concealment even while moving.
