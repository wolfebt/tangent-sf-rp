# Temporal Stasis (Composite Invocation)

**Category**: Composite Invocations  
**Requisite Disciplines**: Entropy + Dimension

## Mechanics & Reality Manipulation
Locks a target or zone in absolute time freeze, halting all physical degradation, bleeding, and chemical reactions.

## Invocation Rules
- Invocation Level is added to the **lowest** of the requisite Discipline Skills used.
