# CHARM Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Projection) | Social | \[Emotion\] \[Trust\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Extended  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Scene  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Makes a creature regard the caster as a trusted friend and ally.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Quantity:** Number of targets \= 1 per Stage.  
* **Duration:** Persists for 1 Hour per Stage after the scene ends.
