# STUN Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Projection) | Attack | \[Non-Lethal\] \[Overload\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Extended  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1d4 Rounds  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Psychically overloads a creature's sensory input, leaving them Stunned.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Duration:** 1 Round \+ (1 Round per Stage).  
* **Damage:** Inflicts 1d6 Non-Lethal Psychic Damage per Stage.
