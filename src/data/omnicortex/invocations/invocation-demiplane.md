# DEMIPLANE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Dimension

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Utility | \[Creation\] \[Sanctuary\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 30  
* **Time:** Ritual (1 Hour)  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Doorway  
* **Duration:** Permanent (Plane exists forever, door is temporary)  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Creates a small, finite pocket dimension tailored to specifications.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Area:** Plane Volume \= 10ft Cubed Side per Stage (10x10, 20x20, 30x30, etc).  
* **Complexity:** Environmental control (Air/Temp) quality scales with Stage.
