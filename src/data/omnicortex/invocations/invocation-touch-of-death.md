# TOUCH OF DEATH Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Entropy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Necrotic\] \[Lethal\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 25  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Partial/Negates Death)
Effect Description:  
Channels pure entropic energy into a living being, attempting to instantly sever their life force.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 2d6 Negative Energy Damage per Stage.  
* **Lethality:** Death Threshold (HP) \= 10 x Stage. If target HP is below threshold, save or die.
