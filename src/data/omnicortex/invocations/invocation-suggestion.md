# SUGGESTION Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Projection) | Control | \[Influence\] \[Subtle\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Extended  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Hours/Level  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Plants a reasonable suggestion in a creature's mind. The course of activity must sound logical.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Duration:** 1 Hour per Stage.  
* **Complexity:** At Stage 3+, suggestion can be unreasonable (but not suicidal).
