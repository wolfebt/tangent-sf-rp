# IMMOVABLE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Matter

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Defense | \[Stability\] \[Anchor\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** Personal  
* **Duration:** 1 Round (Maintained)  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Makes the caster resistant to being moved, knocked down, or grappled.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Severity:** CMD Bonus vs Trip/Bull Rush \= \+4 per Stage.  
* **Duration:** Can maintain for 1 additional round per Stage.
