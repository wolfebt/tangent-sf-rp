# NUDGE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Cantrip\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Free or Move Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Applies up to 10 pounds of force. Cannot cause direct damage. Used to close doors, push buttons, or tip small objects.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Range:** Range increases slightly per Stage.
### 
### **WIND GUST (Force)**
Classification: Energy (Force) | Utility | \[Kinetic\] \[Variative\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** Single Target or 5ft Square  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Strength Save vs. Spell DC
Effect Description:  
A distinct "shove" of telekinetic force. Precise and invisible.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Push:** Knockback distance \= 10ft per Stage.  
* **Damage:** Inflicts 1d4 Force Impact damage per Stage if target hits a wall/obstacle.
