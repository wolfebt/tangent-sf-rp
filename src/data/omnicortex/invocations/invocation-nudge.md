# NUDGE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Cantrip\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Free or Move Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Object  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None

Applies up to 10 pounds of force. Cannot cause direct damage. Used to close doors, push buttons, or tip small objects.  
**Scaling Function:**
