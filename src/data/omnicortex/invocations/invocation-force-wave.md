# FORCE WAVE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Attack/Crowd Control | \[Cone\] \[Push\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** 30ft Cone  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude Save (Half Damage & Negate Push)
Effect Description:  
A visible ripple of distortion that hits like a physical wall.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d8 Kinetic/Force Damage per Stage.  
* **Push:** Knockback distance \= 5ft per Stage.
