# VISIONS Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Sense) | Sensory | \[Minor\] \[Precognition\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)  
* **Range:** Personal  
* **Area:** Self  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous
