# Life Transfer (Composite Invocation)

**Category**: Composite Invocations  
**Requisite Disciplines**: Matter + Entropy

## Mechanics & Reality Manipulation
Siphons organic health and vitality from a target to immediately repair wounds on another ally.

## Invocation Rules
- Invocation Level is added to the **lowest** of the requisite Discipline Skills used.
