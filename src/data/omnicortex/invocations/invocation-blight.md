# BLIGHT Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Entropy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Environmental | \[Decay\] \[Area\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Full Round Action  
* **Range:** Short (Origin)  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius \+  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous / Permanent (to plants)  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Withers plants, rots food, and befouls water within an area.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Area:** Radius \= 20ft per Stage.  
* **Damage:** 2d6 Necrotic Damage per Stage to living matter.
