# MIND BARRIER Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Projection) | Defense | \[Shield\] \[Anti-Psionic\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Scene  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Protects a creature from mental intrusion, mind control, and psychic damage.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Integrity:** Grants \+2 Bonus to Will Saves per Stage.  
* **Absorption:** Grants DR 5 vs Psychic Damage per Stage.
