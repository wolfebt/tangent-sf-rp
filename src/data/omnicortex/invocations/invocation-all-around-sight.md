# ALL-AROUND SIGHT Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Sense) | Buff | \[Vision\] \[Defense\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Personal  
* **Area:** Self  
* **Duration:** Rounds/Level  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Grants 360-degree vision. Caster cannot be flanked.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage.  
* **Defense:** Grants \+1 Insight Bonus to AC per Stage.
