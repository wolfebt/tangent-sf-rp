# SUNBURST Invocation

## Description & Ritual Details

Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack/Debuff | [Radiant] [Variative]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 20
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Medium
  - **Area:** 20ft Radius Burst
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous (Blindness persists)
  - **Resistance:** Reflex (Half Damage) and Fortitude (Blindness)

Effect Description:

An explosion of searing light. Deals radiant damage and blinds sensors/optics/eyes.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Damage:** 1d6 Radiant Damage per Stage.
  - **Status:** Blindness duration = 1 Round per Stage.
