# SUNBURST Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack/Debuff | \[Radiant\] \[Variative\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius Burst  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous (Blindness persists)  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (Half Damage) and Fortitude (Blindness)
Effect Description:  
An explosion of searing light. Deals radiant damage and blinds sensors/optics/eyes.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Radiant Damage per Stage.  
* **Status:** Blindness duration \= 1 Round per Stage.

### **WIND GUST (Elemental)**
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Utility/Attack | \[Air\] \[Knockback\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** 60ft Line or 30ft Cone  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Strength/Athletics Check vs. Spell DC
Effect Description:  
Manipulates air pressure to create a violent thrust of wind.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Push:** Knockback distance \= 10ft per Stage.  
* **Mass:** Maximum creature size affected increases by 1 Category per 2 Stages (Medium \-\> Large \-\> Huge).
