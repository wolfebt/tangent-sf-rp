# STORM Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Environmental | \[Sustained\] \[Area\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Full Round Action  
* **Range:** Long (400ft \+ 40ft/Level)  
* **Area:** 40ft Radius Cylinder (Height 40ft)  
* **Duration:** Concentration (1 round/Level)  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (Half Damage each round)
Effect Description:  
Manifests a localized elemental storm (Rain of Fire, Hailstorm, Lightning Storm).  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Elemental Damage per Stage (applied each round).  
* **Duration:** Concentration \+ 1 Round per Stage.
