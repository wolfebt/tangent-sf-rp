# LIGHTS Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Utility | \[Radiant\] \[Creation\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 5  
* **Time:** Swift Action  
* **Range:** Touch or Close (25ft)  
* **Area:** Varies (Target Object or Point)  
* **Duration:** 1 Hour/Level  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Creates floating orbs of light or causes an object to glow.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Magnitude:** Radius of illumination increases by \+20ft per Stage.  
* **Quantity:** Number of independent lights \= 1 per Stage.
