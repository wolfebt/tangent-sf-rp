# BARRIER Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Utility/Defense | \[Creation\] \[Hazard\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Close (25ft \+ 5ft/Level)  
* **Area:** Wall (10ft long per Level, 1ft thick per Level)  
* **Duration:** Concentration (up to 1 minute/Level)  
* **Resistance:** Reflex (to avoid being trapped) / Fortitude (if passing through)
Effect Description:  
Creates a physical wall of the selected element. Provides Cover and blocks movement.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** Passing through the barrier deals 1d6 Damage per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** Wall has 20 Structure Points (SP) per Stage.  
* **Size:** Length increases by \+10ft per Stage.
