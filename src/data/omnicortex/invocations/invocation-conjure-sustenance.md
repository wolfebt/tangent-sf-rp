# CONJURE SUSTENANCE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Dimension

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Creation\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** 1 Minute  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Single Meal  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
Creates a simple drink or snack sufficient for one person. The food is nutritious but bland unless the caster has culinary skills.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Quantity:** Number of meals increases by 1 per Stage.
