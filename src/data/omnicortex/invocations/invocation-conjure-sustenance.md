## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | \[Minor\] \[Creation\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** 1 Minute  
* **Range:** Close  
* **Area:** Single Meal
