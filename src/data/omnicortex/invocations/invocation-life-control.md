# LIFE CONTROL Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Entropy (Order) | Utility/Healing | \[Vitality\] \[Transfer\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Manipulates the flow of life energy. Transfer HP or bolster Vitality.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Magnitude:** Max HP Transferred \= 5 x Stage.  
* **Efficiency:** At Stage 3, transfer is 1:1 (no loss).
