# GLIMMER Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Illusion

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Illusion (Phantasm) | Utility | \[Sensory\] \[Minor\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium  
* **Area:** 10ft Cube per Stage  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Disbelief)
Effect Description:  
Creates a minor, static sensory illusion, such as a floating orb of light, a ghostly sound, or a faint smell.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Complexity:** Adds 1 sensory channel per Stage (Visual \-\> \+Audio \-\> \+Smell \-\> \+Thermal).  
* **Duration:** Can maintain without Concentration for 1 Minute per Stage.
