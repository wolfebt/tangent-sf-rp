# ELASTICITY Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Matter

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Utility/Defense | \[Flexibility\] \[Reach\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** 1 Minute per Stage  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Enhances flexibility. Target can stretch limbs or compress.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Magnitude:** Reach increases by \+5ft per Stage.  
* **Integrity:** DR vs Bludgeoning \= 5 per Stage.
