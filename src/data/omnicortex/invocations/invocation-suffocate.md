# SUFFOCATE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Entropy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | \[Air\] \[Biological\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 20  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Short  
* **Area:** Single Creature  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Fortitude (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Removes air from the target's lungs or prevents them from breathing.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 2d6 Non-Lethal per round (Scaling to Lethal).  
* **Duration:** Can maintain for 1 round per Stage without concentration.
