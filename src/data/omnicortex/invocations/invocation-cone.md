# CONE Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack | \[Large Area\] \[Breath Weapon\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 18  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self (Origin)  
* **Area:** 60ft Cone  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex Save (Half Damage)
Effect Description:  
A massive projection of elemental force, significantly larger than the 'Spray'.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Elemental Damage per Stage.  
* **Critical:** Applies Elemental Critical Effect (Burn/Stun/Slow/Dissolve).  
* **Lingering:** At Stage 3+, creates Difficult Terrain for 1 round per Stage.
