# SPRAY Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack | \[Variable Element\] \[Cone\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** 15ft Cone  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex Save (Half Damage)
Effect Description:  
The caster projects a short-range fan of elemental matter. Designed for close-quarters clearing.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Elemental Damage per Stage.  
* **Critical:** Applies Elemental Critical Effect (Burn/Stun/Slow/Dissolve).  
* **Area:** Cone length increases by \+5ft per Stage.
