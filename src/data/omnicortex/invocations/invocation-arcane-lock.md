# ARCANE LOCK Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Matter

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Matter (Enhancement) | Utility | \[Security\] \[Seal\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 10  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Touch  
* **Area:** Door, Chest, or Portal  
* **Duration:** Permanent (until dispelled/broken)

Magically secures an object, fusing the materials at the molecular level.

* **Security:** Break/Lockpick DC increases by \+5 per Stage.
