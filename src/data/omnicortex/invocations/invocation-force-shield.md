# FORCE SHIELD Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Force) | Defense | \[Reaction\] \[Shield\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Reaction (Immediate Action)  
* **Range:** Self  
* **Area:** Personal Facing  
* **Duration:** 1 Round (or Instant)  
* **Resistance:** None
Effect Description:  
A temporary, floating disk of force that interposes itself to block attacks.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Defense:** Shield Bonus \= \+4 \+ (1 per Stage).  
* **Deflection:** Reduces incoming damage by 2 per Stage.
