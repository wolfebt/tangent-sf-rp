# BURST Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack | \[Area\] \[Explosive\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Medium (100ft \+ 10ft/Level)  
* **Area:** 20ft Radius Sphere  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex Save (Half Damage)
Effect Description:  
The classic "fireball" effect. A bead of energy travels to a point and detonates.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Elemental Damage per Stage.  
* **Critical:** Applies Elemental Critical Effect (Burn/Stun/Slow/Dissolve).  
* **Area:** Radius increases by \+5ft per Stage.
