# DETECT THOUGHTS Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Mental

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Mental (Sense) | Sensory | \[Telepathy\] \[Surface\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 12  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Extended Cone  
* **Area:** Cone  
* **Duration:** Concentration  
* **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)
Effect Description:  
Reads the surface thoughts of creatures in the area.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Range:** Cone Length scales with Extended Range table.  
* **Depth:** At Stage 3+, can probe for specific answers (1 question/round).
