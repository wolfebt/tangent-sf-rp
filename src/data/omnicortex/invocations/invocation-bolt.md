# BOLT Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack | \[Line\] \[Penetrating\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Long (400ft \+ 40ft/Level)  
* **Area:** 5ft Wide Line  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous  
* **Resistance:** Reflex Save (Half Damage)
Effect Description:  
Projects a stroke of lightning, jet of fire, or beam of cold in a straight line.  
**Scaling Function:**
* **Damage:** 1d6 Elemental Damage per Stage.  
* **Critical:** Applies Elemental Critical Effect (Burn/Stun/Slow/Dissolve).  
* **Range:** Line length increases by \+20ft per Stage.
