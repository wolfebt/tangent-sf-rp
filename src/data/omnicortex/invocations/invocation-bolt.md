# BOLT Invocation

**Category**: Invocations  
**Discipline**: Energy

## Description & Ritual Details
Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Attack | \[Line\] \[Penetrating\]  
Parameters:
* **Base Difficulty:** 15  
* **Time:** Standard Action  
* **Range:** Long (400ft \+ 40ft/Level)  
* **Area:** 5ft Wide Line  
* **Duration:** Instantaneous

Projects a stroke of lightning, jet of fire, or beam of cold in a straight line.
