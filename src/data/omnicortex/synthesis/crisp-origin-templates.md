# CRISP Form Templates

- **Template A (Master Shot)**: Standard 50mm lens, eye-level camera angle, balanced cinematic framing, mid-shot composition, seamless environmental integration, natural depth of field.
- **Template B (Close-Up / Portrait)**: Macro photography, 85mm lens, extreme shallow depth of field, micro-detail skin pores, magnified material definition, Rembrandt lighting.
- **Template C (Character Concept Sheet)**: Multi-angle reference sheet, deep neutral gray backdrop, 5-panel array (front, back, right, left, face close-up).
- **Template D (Architectural Establishing Shot)**: Wide-angle landscape photography, 24mm tilt-shift lens, sweeping 3-point perspective, immense atmospheric depth.
- **Template E (Vehicle / Mech Design)**: Industrial photography, 35mm lens, anisotropic metallic reflections, carbon fiber weave, hydraulic realism.
- **Template F (Interior / Diorama Space)**: Architectural interior, 50mm lens, light shafts, lived-in environmental storytelling.
