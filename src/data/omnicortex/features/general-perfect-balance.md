# Perfect Balance

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Acrobatic Steps, Agility 4

## Description
The character has perfect equilibrium.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You ignore **all** difficult terrain penalties. You are **Immune to the Prone condition** (you cannot be tripped or knocked down) unless you choose to be.
