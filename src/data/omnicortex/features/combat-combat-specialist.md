# Combat Specialist

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Combat Skill 6 (Trained)

## Description
The character knows how to inflict maximum trauma with a specific class of weaponry.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Increase the **Damage Die type** of one specific weapon group by one step (e.g., d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10).

## Special Rules
\[Multiple\] May be taken for different weapon groups.
