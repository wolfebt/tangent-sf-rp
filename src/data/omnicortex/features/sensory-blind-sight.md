# Blind Sight

**Category**: Sensory Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Non-Visual Sensory Organs  

## Description
The creature perceives its surroundings through distributed sensory receptors, electromagnetic resonance, micro-acoustic sonar, or chemical olfactory tracking, completely bypassing the need for optical vision.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Precise Targeting**: Accurately targets, tracks, and engages unseen or visually obscured targets within a **30 ft radius** (or a **60 ft cone**) without penalty.
- **Immunity to Visual Impairments**: Immune to visual penalties from pitch darkness, dense fog, blinding flashes, smoke grenades, and optical invisibility.
