# Multidextrous (Many-Limbed)

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Multi-segmented Larval Anatomy  

## Description
The Syndess possesses dozens of fine, hair-like limbs running along the ventral length of its multi-segmented body. Each limb ends in a hyper-sensitive synaptic filament capable of manipulating micro-valves, nerve clusters, and vascular channels simultaneously.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Distributed Subsystem Processing**: A single integrated pilot can manipulate internal controls equivalent to 4 standard crew members simultaneously (pilot, gunner, engineer, and sensor operator).
- **Fine Internal Maintenance**: Can perform emergency bio-patching, nutrient fluid rerouting, and internal system calibrations without disconnecting from the mecha node.
- **Off-Hand Negation**: Zero penalty for operating multiple interfaces or fine tools simultaneously.
