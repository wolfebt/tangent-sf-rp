# Indomitable Will

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Iron Will, Wisdom 2

## Description
The character's mind is an immovable object. Attempts to control or break their spirit are met with absolute defiance.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may roll all **Will Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll 2d10 with Advantage (roll twice and keep the highest result) when making a Will save.
