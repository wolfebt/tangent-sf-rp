# Greater Feint

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Improved Feint

## Description
The character's feints leave opponents completely off-balance.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Enemies you successfully Feint lose their Agility bonus to Defense for **1 full round** (until the start of your next turn), allowing allies to also benefit from the opening.
