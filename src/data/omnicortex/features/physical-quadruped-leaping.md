# Quadruped (Leaping)

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Four Lower Limbs  

## Description
A four-legged lower chassis featuring reinforced spring-tendons and broad clawed pads specifically adapted for rapid sprinting and explosive high-impact leaps across rugged planetary terrain.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Stability Bonus**: Grants a **+4 racial bonus to Stability** against trips, pushes, and knockdown effects while on any solid surface.
- **Enhanced Leap Velocity**: High-impact springs add **+10 feet** to maximum horizontal jump distance and **+5 feet** to vertical reach.
- **Tauric Sprint**: Base Groundspeed increases by **+10 feet** when operating in full quadrupedal gait.
