# Blind-Fight

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Awareness 1

## Description
The character has learned to fight using auditory cues, air pressure, and instinct, allowing them to engage unseen enemies.

## Mechanics & Benefit
When fighting a target that is concealed, invisible, or obscured (darkness/fog), you may roll your Strike check with **Advantage** to counter the miss chance or penalty usually imposed by the concealment.

## Special Rules
You do not lose your Agility bonus to Defense when attacked by an invisible attacker in melee.
