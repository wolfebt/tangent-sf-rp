# Psionic Interference (The Static)

**Category**: Meta Features  
**Cost**: 1 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Resonant / Chaotic Psionic Aura  

## Description
The divergent survival conditions of the swarm resulted in an abrasive, chaotic mental resonance that runs violently counter to the Kitin Chorus steering frequencies. This manifests as a palpable "psionic static" that creates painful mental feedback in orderly telepathic networks.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Manelli Disruption**: Any Manelli within telepathic range (100 ft) of the swarm suffers a **-2 penalty** to all Psionic, Attune, and Wisdom checks due to excruciating cognitive feedback.
- **Chorus Jamming**: When in the immediate vicinity (within 30 ft), all Kitin Collective communications are severely disrupted; Chorus coordination, telepathic relay checks, and brood commands must be rolled at **Disadvantage**.
- **Aura Static**: Non-Kitin psionic operators attempting to probe or read the swarm's thoughts take 1d6 mental shock damage.
