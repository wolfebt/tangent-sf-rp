# Medic

**Category**: Skill Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Medicine 1

## Description
The character is trained in emergency triage and trauma care.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain a **\+2 Bonus** to Medicine checks.

## Special Rules
You may perform **First Aid** (stabilize a dying character) as a **Standard Action** rather than a Full Round Action.
