# Shared Senses

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Familiar or Bonded

## Description
The character can project their consciousness into their bond-mate.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Your Companion gains **\+10 Character Points**. As a Standard Action, you can shift your sensory input to perceive through your companion's eyes/ears/senses. While doing so, your own body is blind/deaf.
