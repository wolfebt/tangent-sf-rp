# Technologist

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Intellect 3, Technology (Knowledge) 11

## Description
The character is ahead of the curve, understanding technology that shouldn't exist yet. For a character to advance in their (TL Tech Level) they will require different fields of advanced study, the feature will need to be selected multiple times for various fields (minimum of five).

## Mechanics & Benefit
You treat your effective **Tech Level** as one step higher for the purposes of crafting, repairing, or understanding advanced artifacts in a specific field of study.  
  * Agriculture  
  * Architecture  
  * Biotechnology  
  * Commerce  
  * Communication  
  * Devices  
  * Education  
  * Energy  
  * Manufacturing  
  * Materials  
  * Medicine  
  * Meta Sciences  
  * Science  
  * Society  
  * Synthetic Intelligence  
  * Transportation  
  * Weaponry
