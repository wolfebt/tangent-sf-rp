# Multidextrous

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Ambidextrous or Agility 4; Species with 3+ arms.

## Description
Applicable to species with more than two arms, this feature allows the coordination of multiple limbs in concert.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You suffer no penalties for using any of your off-hands. You may wield weapons in all available hands without the standard "off-hand" penalties to Strike.
