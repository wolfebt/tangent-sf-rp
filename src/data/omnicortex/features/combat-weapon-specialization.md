# Weapon Specialization

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Weapon Focus (in chosen weapon), Combat Skill 6

## Description
The character knows how to inflict maximum damage with their chosen weapon.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain **\+1 Die of Damage** with the chosen weapon. (e.g., A 2d6 weapon becomes 3d6).

## Special Rules
\[Multiple\] This bonus is significantly stronger than standard d20 (+2 static), reflecting the Tangent system's damage scaling.
