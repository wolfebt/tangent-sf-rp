# Uncanny Senses (Vibration)

**Category**: Sensory Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Insectoid or Arachnoid Physiology  

## Description
Microscopic tactile setae (trichobothria) distributed along the creature's limbs, pedipalps, and abdominal surface are attuned to the faintest mechanical tremors. This grants an extraordinary seismic awareness of movements occurring across solid floors, walls, and interconnecting web meshes.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Surface Tremorsense (60 ft)**: Pinpoint the location, size, and movement vectors of all creatures and mechanical devices contacting the same floor, ceiling, wall, or connected web network within **60 feet**, regardless of darkness, smoke, or optical invisibility.
- **Hive-Mesh Alert**: When in contact with webbed structures or Hive-Spire meshworks, this detection range extends to **150 feet**.
- **Ambushes Defeated**: The character cannot be caught flat-footed or surprised by ground-based or surface-scaling assailants.
