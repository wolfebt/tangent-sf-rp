### **Overview**
Grown directly from the stabilized Genomatrix, the operative's genome exhibits unbroken fidelity across generations. Their tissues harmonize instantaneously with living Hive-technology, organo-grafts, and biomorphic neural interfaces without suffering immune rejection or biological incompatibility.

### **Mechanical Benefits**
* **Genetic Stabilization:** The operative is immune to accelerated cellular aging, clone decay syndrome, telomere unraveling, and non-hive retroviral pathogens attempting to rewrite host DNA.
* **Organic Socket Affinity:** The character completely ignores Cellular Rejection checks when equipping, grafting, or slaving organic augmentations, biomorphic symbiotic suits, or living wetware sockets.
* **Biomorphic Intuition:** The operative gains **Advantage** on all Science (Biotechnology) and Medicine checks performed to maintain, graft, suture, or cultivate Hive-Resin structures and biomorphic machinery.
