# Mind Link

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Familiar or Bonded

## Description
The bond deepens into a direct mental highway.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Your Companion gains **\+10 Character Points**. You possess a full **Telepathic Link** with the companion. You may share Knowledge Skills; if one knows a fact or language, the other can access it instantly.
