# Incredible Fortitude

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Great Fortitude, Stamina 2

## Description
The character's physical resilience is legendary. When subjected to physical stress that would cripple a normal being, they find a reserve of strength to persevere.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may roll all **Fortitude Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll 2d10 with Advantage (roll twice and keep the highest result) when making a Fortitude save.

## Special Rules
This does not replace the bonus from Great Fortitude; you add the bonus to the highest die result.
