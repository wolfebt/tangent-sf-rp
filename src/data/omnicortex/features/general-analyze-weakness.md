# Analyze Weakness

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Assessment

## Description
The character applies their assessment to combat, identifying chinks in armor or behavioral tics that can be exploited.

## Mechanics & Benefit
As a Standard Action, make an **Assessment Check** opposed by the target's **Bluff Check**. For every 5 points by which your result exceeds the target's, you gain a cumulative **\+1 bonus to Strike and Damage** against that specific target.

## Special Rules
This bonus lasts for the duration of the encounter or until the target successfully leaves your line of sight for more than 1 minute.
