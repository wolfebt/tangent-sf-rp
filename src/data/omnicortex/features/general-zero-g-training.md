# Zero G Training

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Agility 1

## Description
The character is trained to operate in microgravity environments without disorientation.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You suffer **No Disadvantage** or penalties for operating in Zero Gravity. You gain a \+2 bonus to Acrobatics when maneuvering in Zero-G.
