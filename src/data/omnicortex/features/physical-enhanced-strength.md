# Enhanced Strength

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Strength 2  

## Description
High-density striated muscle tissue reinforced by internal lever geometry allows the creature to exert phenomenal mechanical leverage. Their physical frame supports loads far beyond standard species of their nominal size category.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Size Category Lift/Carry**: Count as **1 size category larger** (Medium counts as Large) when determining maximum lifting capacity, dragging limit, encumbrance thresholds, and grappling checks against larger foes.
- **Overburden Resilience**: Suffers no movement speed penalties when operating under heavy encumbrance loads up to their revised maximum threshold.
