# Enhanced Biotechnology

**Category**: Augmentation / General Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisite**: Kitin Lineage, Awakened, or Tech Level 4+  

## Description
The character possesses advanced physiological and neural compatibility with living technology, symbiotic carapace enhancements, and vat-grown biomorphic tools.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Symbiotic Attunement:** Living equipment, symbiotic armor, and bio-grafted weaponry integrate instantaneously without surgical rejection checks or biological acclimation penalties.
* **Living Technology Synergy:** Gain Advantage on checks made to calibrate, heal, or command biomorphic constructs, hive-resin structures, and bio-organic items.
* **Reduced Biomass Maintenance:** Biotech symbiotes and living gear draw sustenance directly and efficiently from the host's metabolic waste without causing fatigue or stamina drain.
