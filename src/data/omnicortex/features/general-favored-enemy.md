# Favored Enemy

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Awareness 1

## Description
The character has studied a specific type of creature extensively, knowing their anatomy, tactics, and psychology.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Choose a creature type (e.g., Humanoid \[Human\], Beast, Synthetic, Aberration). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to **Strike**, **Damage**, **Bluff**, **Insight**, and **Survival** checks against them.

## Special Rules
\[Multiple\] Can be taken for different creature types.
