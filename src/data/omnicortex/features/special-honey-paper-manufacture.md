# Honey & Paper Manufacture

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 1 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Insectoid Secretions  

## Description
The swarm's worker castes synthesize sticky amber ooze, regurgitated cellulose pulp, and biological resin. These substances can be rapidly extruded and layered into fortified nests, structural honeycombs, or sticky combat obstacles.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Nest & Barricade Building**: Can construct dense, paper-like chitinous hive nests, atmospheric seals, and hanging egg-sacs. In 10 minutes, can create a reinforced barricade offering **DR 5 and 30 Structure Points**.
- **Amber Ooze Entrapment**: Can coat a 10 ft area in sticky honeyed ooze (Difficult Terrain; DC 14 Athletics or Reflex save to avoid being stuck).
- **Nutrient Honey**: Excretes nutrient-rich amber honey capable of sustaining organic allies or zombified husks.
