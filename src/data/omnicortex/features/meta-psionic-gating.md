### **Overview**
Operating within the continuous mental carrier-wave of the Chorus, the operative's neural pathways are defended by active psionic frequency gating. Foreign thoughts and invasive psychic frequencies are intercepted and damped before they can compromise the conscious ego.

### **Mechanical Benefits**
* **Cryptographic Neural Filter:** Hostile telepathic probes, mind-reading attempts, illusions, and forced psychic interrogations targeting the operative suffer **Disadvantage** on their attack or activation check.
* **Elevated Mental Defense:** The character receives a **+2 bonus to Mental DR** (Damage Reduction against psionic/metaphysical trauma) and a **+2 bonus on all Willpower or Wisdom checks** made to resist mind control, psychic panic, or cognitive override.
* **Chorus Ping:** The moment an unauthorized external entity attempts to breach the character's mental threshold, an automated silent alert resonates through the local Hive-Link network.
