# Energy Resistance

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
The character's physiology is resistant to a specific form of energy (Fire, Cold, Electricity, Acid, Sonic).

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain **Resistance 5** against one chosen energy type. (Subtract 5 from all damage of that type).

## Special Rules
\[Ranked\] Stacks to Resistance 10, 15, etc., eventually becoming Immunity.
