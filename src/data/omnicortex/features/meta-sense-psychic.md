# Sense (Psychic)

**Category**: Meta / Sensory Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Awakened, Intellect 2, or Wisdom 2  

## Description
The character can directly perceive the resonance of psionic minds, metaphysical activity, and bio-energetic auras without requiring technological sensors or overt physical signs.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Psionic Perception Range:** Detect the presence and approximate direction of conscious minds, psionic channeling, or metaphysical manifestations within a **60-foot radius**.
* **Aura Reading:** By spending 1 action in focused concentration, determine whether a detected mind is calm, panicked, hostile, or under mental coercion.
* **Cover Penetration:** This sense bypasses physical barriers up to 3 feet of wood/dirt or 1 foot of stone/metal, though dense lead or dedicated psionic dampeners block detection.
