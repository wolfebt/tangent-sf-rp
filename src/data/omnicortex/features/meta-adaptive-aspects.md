# Adaptive Aspects (Hive Memories)

**Category**: Meta Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisite**: Hive-Link or Genetic Memory  

## Description
By entering a quiet trance during Torpor or a Light Rest, the character communes with the ancestral genomatrix and deep hive memories, adjusting their mental focus to mirror specialized forebears.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* During a Light Rest (or Torpor cycle), the character may reconfigure one designated adaptive aspect—selecting a situational bonus (+2 to a specific knowledge, engineering, or piloting specialization) drawn from ancestral experiences.
* This adapted specialization remains active until the next rest cycle.
