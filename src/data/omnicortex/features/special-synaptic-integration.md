# Synaptic Integration

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Pilot Expression Physiology  

## Description
The Syndess can "slot" directly into the neuro-synaptic junction or cerebral node of any Kitin biomorphic construct or mecha frame. Upon establishing a bio-neural handshake, the pilot's nervous system fuses seamlessly with the construct's vascular and motor networks.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Bio-Neural Handshake**: Establishing full synchronization requires 1 full round of bio-neural calibration with a dormant or active mecha shell.
- **Cognitive Control**: While integrated, the pilot uses their own **Intellect** and **Agility** ability modifiers for all vehicle handling, piloting maneuvers, gunnery targeting, and systems checks.
- **Inertial Negation**: Ignores all G-force, high-velocity deceleration, and angular vector penalties.
- **Sensory Fusion**: External vehicle sensors operate as natural biological skin, granting complete spatial awareness without blind spots.
