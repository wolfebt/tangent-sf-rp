# Quadruped

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Four or More Lower Limbs  

## Description
The creature moves on four or more sturdy lower limbs supporting a tauric or animalistic body plan. This broad stance distributes body mass effectively across any ground condition.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Stability Bonus**: Gain a **+4 racial bonus to Stability** against combat maneuvers such as trips, bull rushes, knockdown effects, and overruns.
- **Speed Increase**: **+10 feet** to Base Ground Movement speed.
- **Special Accommodations**: Requires custom armor, saddles, and suits tailored for non-humanoid or tauric body forms.
