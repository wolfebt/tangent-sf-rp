# Hive Mind (Swarm)

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Swarm Physiology  

## Description
The organism does not possess a centralized brain or vital organ. Instead, thousands of synchronized fine-sized insects share a unified resonant psionic consciousness.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Distributed Consciousness**: Immune to sleep, paralysis, stunning, flanking, and critical hits directed at specific anatomical weak points.
- **Swarm Resilience**: Single-target precision weapons, piercing attacks, and non-area kinetic strikes deal half damage or fail to impede the swarm.
- **Annihilation Threshold**: The entity cannot be killed unless more than 75% of its total biomass is dispersed or destroyed by area-of-effect trauma (fire, explosive, chemical, or psionic shockwaves).
