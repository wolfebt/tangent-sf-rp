# Mighty Surge

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Potent Might, Strength 2

## Description
In moments of intense physical exertion, the character can summon a surge of adrenaline to perform feats of herculean power.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may roll all **Might Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll 2d10 with Advantage (roll twice and keep the highest result) when testing Strength against an obstacle or opponent.
