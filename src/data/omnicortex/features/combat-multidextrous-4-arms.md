# Multidextrous (4 Arms)

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 4 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Agility 2, 4 Arms  

## Description
The character possesses four fully articulate upper limbs ending in sharp, reinforced clawed digits. Engineered for simultaneous fine motor manipulation and lethal close-quarters combat, all four limbs operate with primary-hand dexterity.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Zero Off-Hand Penalty**: The character suffers no off-hand attack or interaction penalties when wielding weapons, tools, or shields with any of their four arms.
- **Multi-Tasking Dexterity**: Can perform dual complex tasks simultaneously (e.g., firing a two-handed weapon while patching armor or operating a technical console with the second pair of arms).
- **Clawed Digits**: Natural attacks made with the hands deal **1d6 + Str Lethal (Slashing/Piercing)** damage.
