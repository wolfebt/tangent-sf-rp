# Venomous Saliva

**Category**: Combat / Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Toxic Blood, Stamina 2  

## Description
Distinctive rows of sharp, needle-like teeth are backed by specialized venom glands along the jawline, injecting a potent paralytic venom into punctured tissues during a bite.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Delivery:** Injected via natural bite attack or melee puncture attack.
* **Difficulty Class:** **DC 11 + (2 × Stamina)** Fortitude check.
* **Effect on Failure:** The victim is afflicted with **Paralytic condition** for **1 hour**, rendering motor systems immobilized.
* **Effect on Success:** On a successful Fortitude save, the victim is afflicted with the **Drugged condition** (-2 penalty to Agility, Reflex, and sensory checks, plus slowed movement) for 1 hour.
