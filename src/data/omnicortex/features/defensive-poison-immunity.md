# Poison Immunity

**Category**: Defensive / Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Stamina 1 or Insectoid/Synthetic Physiology  

## Description
The character possesses an extraordinarily specialized hepatic, renal, and hemolymph filtration system that neutralizes xenotoxins, chemical weapons, venoms, and ingested poisons instantly.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Automatic Save:** Automatically succeed on all saving throws and Fortitude checks against any poisonous substances, ingested toxins, injected venoms, or contact irritants.
* **Toxin Neutralization:** Toxins introduced to the bloodstream are safely metabolized into inert bio-organic compounds without causing biological damage or lingering impairment.
