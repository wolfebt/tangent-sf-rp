# Sleepless

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Synthetic, Elf, or Special

## Description
The character does not require sleep cycles (though they may need downtime for system diagnostics or meditation).

## Mechanics & Benefit
You are **Immune** to magical sleep effects. You do not need 8 hours of sleep; you remain conscious during rest periods, though you must still engage in light activity to gain the benefits of a Long Rest.
