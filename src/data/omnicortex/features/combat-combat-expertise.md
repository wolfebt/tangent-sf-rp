# Combat Expertise

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Intellect 2

## Description
The character fights with superior tactical caution, sacrificing aggression for protection.

## Mechanics & Benefit
When you declare an attack action, you may choose a number up to your Combat Skill Rank. Subtract that number from your **Strike** (Attack) and add it to your **Defense** until the start of your next turn.

## Special Rules
You forfeit any bonus attacks from high skill or other features when using this action; you make one strike per target.
