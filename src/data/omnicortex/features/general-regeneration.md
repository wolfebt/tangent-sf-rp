# Regeneration

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Fast Heal, Stamina 4

## Description
The character's body knits flesh back together in real-time.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You recover **1 Health point per hour** automatically without resting, provided you are not dead. You can regrow lost limbs and organs over 1d4 weeks.
