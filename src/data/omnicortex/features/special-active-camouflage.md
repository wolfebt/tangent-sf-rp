# Camouflage (Active)

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 4 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Chitinous or Biomorphic Dermis  

## Description
The organism's carapace is embedded with reactive chromatophores, micro-prismatic refraction facets, and light-absorbing melanin matrices. By instinct or conscious intent, the shell dynamically alters both color and surface micro-texture, adapting to arid sand, volcanic basalt, synthetic starship interiors, or deep darkness.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Dynamic Chromatic & Texture Shift**: Can alter coloration across all visible spectra to match surrounding terrain in 1 free action.
- **Vanta-Black & Transparency**: Capable of assuming a matte, light-absorbing vanta-black state in shadows or bending ambient light so effectively that the character appears semi-transparent.
- **Stealth Dominance**: Grants a **+8 bonus** to Stealth checks when motionless and **+4 bonus** while moving at half speed. Thermal and standard optical sensors suffer Disadvantage when attempting to detect or track the character.
