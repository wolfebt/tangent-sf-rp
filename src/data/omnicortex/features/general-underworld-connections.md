# Underworld Connections

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Streetwise 3

## Description
Access to illicit black market weapons, fences, and safehouses across all starports.

## Mechanics & Benefit
The character maintains deep ties with smuggling syndicates, fences, and illegal arms dealers. They can source restricted, military, and black-market items without police interference, sell contraband merchandise at standard fence rates, and secure covert safehouses in most major civilized settlements.

## Special Rules
Characters with this feature ignore black market availability markups by 10% and gain Advantage on Streetwise checks to locate contraband or black-market contacts.
