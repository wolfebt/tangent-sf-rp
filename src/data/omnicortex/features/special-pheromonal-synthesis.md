# Pheromonal Synthesis

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Insectoid Glands or Chemoreceptive Biology  

## Description
Specialized ventral and oral glands synthesize sophisticated chemical cocktails and aromatic markers. These markers linger in environments to transmit silent tactical instructions, alarm states, and navigation routes to hive kin.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Hive Scent Marking**: Can mark objects, tunnels, and structural paths with persistent chemical trails lasting up to 72 hours.
- **Coordination Boost**: Allied Kitin and chemoreceptive organisms following a laid trail gain a **+2 bonus on Navigation, Survival, and Initiative checks** while operating within the scented operational zone.
- **Silent Alarm**: Can release an instantaneous localized aerosol alert across a 60-foot radius to silently signal danger without making audible noise or psychic vibrations.
