# Venom Spit

**Category**: Combat / Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisite**: Toxic Blood, Stamina 2  

## Description
Pressurized chemical glands beneath the tongue allow the character to spit an aerosolized stream of corrosive, ocular-targeted venom at an approaching threat.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Delivery:** Contact (Ranged Attack with 20 ft range increment, up to 60 ft maximum range).
* **Difficulty Class:** **DC 11 + (2 × Stamina)** Reflex or Fortitude check.
* **Effect on Failure:** The victim is afflicted with **Blindness** for **10+ minutes** (or 10 + Stamina minutes), suffering full concealment penalties and loss of targeted visual combat capabilities.
* **Effect on Success:** On a successful save, the target is dazzled (-1 to attack rolls and visual perception) for 1 minute.
