# Well Informed

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
The character keeps their ear to the ground and knows a little bit about every local scene.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Upon entering a new settlement or region, you may make an immediate **Gather Information** check for free to learn the local news, rumors, and power structure.
