# Vital Strike

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Combat Skill 6

## Description
The character makes a single, devastatingly accurate attack rather than a flurry of blows.

## Mechanics & Benefit
As a **Standard Action**, make a single attack at your highest bonus. If it hits, deal **two times (2x)** the weapon's damage dice.
