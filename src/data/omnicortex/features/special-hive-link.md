# Hive-Link

**Category**: Meta Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Kitin Lineage or Hive Mind Archetype  

## Description
The organism possesses an innate bio-synaptic resonance with the Kitin Collective. Through this conduit, the individual maintains a continuous background link with the Chorus, bridging minds across planetary and orbital distances.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Constant Telepathic Connection:** Maintain uninterrupted telepathic communication with all Kitin Strains within local range (planetary surface or system hub).
* **Tactical Chorus Synchronization:** When fighting or operating alongside other Kitin Strains or Hive-Linked allies, the character cannot be surprised if at least one linked ally is aware of the threat.
* **Brood Coordination:** Allows the transmission of non-verbal tactical directives, sensorial feeds, and bio-status reports instantaneously without generating audible or electronic signatures.
