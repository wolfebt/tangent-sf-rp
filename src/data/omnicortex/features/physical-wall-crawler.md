# Wall-Crawler

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
The character possesses the uncanny physical adaptation to scale vertical inclines, sheer cliffs, and ceilings without specialized climbing gear.

## Mechanics & Benefit
The character gains a Climb Speed equal to their normal Ground Movement Speed. They do not need to make Athletics checks to climb typical sheer surfaces unless under direct duress or navigating frictionless, polished materials. They retain their full Defense while climbing and can adhere to surfaces with at least two free limbs.
