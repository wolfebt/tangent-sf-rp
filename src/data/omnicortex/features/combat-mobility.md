# Mobility

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Agility 2, Dodge

## Description
The character moves through the battlefield with ease, weaving through threatened areas.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You gain a **\+4 Dodge bonus to Defense** against Attacks of Opportunity caused by moving out of or through a threatened square.
