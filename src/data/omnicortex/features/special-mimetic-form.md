# Mimetic Form

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Kitin Manelli or Morphic Species  

## Description
The species possesses a thin, human-mimetic dermis and internal endoskeletal framework capable of masquerading as baseline humanity. Beneath this skin, emerald copper-based blood flows without an external carapace.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Disguise** or **Deception** checks to pass as human.  
* *Condition:* The organism's sensory cranial antennae must be concealed (e.g., woven into hairstyles, concealed within headwear, or tucked flat against the scalp). If the antennae or needle-like teeth are visibly revealed, this disguise bonus is forfeited and true xenotype is recognized.
