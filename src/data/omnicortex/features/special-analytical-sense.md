# Analytical Sense

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Acute Sense (in the corresponding sense), Intellect 2

## Description
The character’s senses function like a tricorder or forensic scanner. They don't just see or smell; they analyze chemical compositions, structural stress points, and thermal gradients naturally.

## Mechanics & Benefit
When using your chosen Acute Sense, you gain specific, detailed scientific information. A smell isn't just "almond-like"; it is "Cyanide, distinct trace."  
* **Mechanics:** You may use **Perception** in place of **Investigation** or **Science** checks to identify substances or analyze clues if the sensory input is available.
