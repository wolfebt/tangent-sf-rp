# G-Force Immunity

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Hydro-Cushioned Hemolymph Vascular System  

## Description
The Syndess utilizes a hydro-cushioned internal cavity filled with non-compressible, dense hemolymph. This biological dampening architecture insulates vital neural organs against rapid velocity shifts and violent angular momentum.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Total G-Force Immunity**: The organism is completely immune to blackout, redout, G-LOC (loss of consciousness), vertigo, and internal organ rupture caused by high-G turns or extreme acceleration.
- **Extreme Evasive Maneuvers**: In vehicle combat, allows high-angle vector snaps, instantaneous retro-burns, and hard air-braking without requiring saving throws to resist physical incapacitation.
