# Natural Weapons

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
The character possesses claws, fangs, horns, or a tail striker.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You are always armed. Your Unarmed Strikes deal Lethal damage and the damage die is increased.  
  * *Small/Medium:* 1d4 or 1d6 damage.  
  * *Large:* 1d8 damage.

## Special Rules
\[Ranked\] Can be taken to increase the damage die size.
