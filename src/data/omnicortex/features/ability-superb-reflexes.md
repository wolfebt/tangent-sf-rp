# Superb Reflexes

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Lightning Reflexes, Agility 2

## Description
The character moves with a fluid, almost precognitive grace. They can weave through a hail of fire or debris with effortless speed.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may roll all **Reflex Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll 2d10 with Advantage (roll twice and keep the highest result) when making a Reflex save.
