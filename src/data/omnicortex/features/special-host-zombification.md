# Host Zombification

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Swarm Physiology  

## Description
The swarm can flow into the cavities of dead biological entities, synthetic cyber-frames, or damaged biomorphic husks, threading through muscle bundles and neural conduits to puppet the host body from within.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Puppet Husk**: Inhabiting a fresh corpse or husk takes 1 minute of uninterrupted burrowing. While inside, the swarm adopts the host's physical Size, Base Strength score, and native Armor DR.
- **Ablative Shell**: Attacks directed at the zombified form damage the host husk first (using the corpse's remaining Structure/Health). Once the husk is destroyed, the swarm emerges unscathed.
- **Technological Interface**: Can operate basic vehicles, tools, and weapons tailored to the host's anatomy.
