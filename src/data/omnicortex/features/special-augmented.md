# Augmented

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Available Technology Level (TL 3+)

## Description
The character has undergone invasive surgery to replace or enhance biological systems with cybernetics, biotechnology, or magical prosthetics.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You are compatible with **Standard Augmentations**.

## Special Rules
Upon taking this feature, you gain a "credit" of **6 Character Points (CP)** worth of Augmentations effectively for free (representing the initial suite of upgrades). Any cost beyond this must be paid with CP or Credits.
