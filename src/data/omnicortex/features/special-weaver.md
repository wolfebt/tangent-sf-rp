# Weaver

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Resin Secretion  

## Description
The character possesses spinneret nozzles capable of spinning high-tensile silk filaments spun from specialized, polymerizing Hive-Resin. These strands can be spun as ultra-sticky adhesive snares or non-adhesive structural cables.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Web Snare / Entrapment**: As a standard action, shoot a sticky webbing net at a target within **40 ft** (ranged attack vs Agility). On a hit, the target is **Entangled/Immobilized** (DC 15 + Sta Strength check to break free, or requires cutting through 25 Health webbing).
- **High-Tensile Tethers & Rappels**: Can produce up to 200 ft of continuous climb-line or anchoring wire capable of supporting up to 5,000 lbs.
- **Hive-Mesh Construction**: Can weave structural safety nets, hanging bivouacs, and tripwire alarm meshes across subterranean tunnels and ship corridors.
