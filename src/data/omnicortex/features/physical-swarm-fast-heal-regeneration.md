# Fast Heal & Regeneration (Swarm)

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Swarm Physiology  

## Description
The swarm maintains an internal breeding cycle where hundreds of micro-insects pupate, mature, and integrate every minute. Losses inflicted by combat or hostile environments are rapidly compensated through internal cellular reproduction.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Rapid Health Recovery**: Automatically recovers **2 Health/Vitality per combat round** so long as at least 25% of the swarm survives and has access to organic matter.
- **Light Rest Replenishment**: Completely replenishes lost Health and dispersed outer swarm layers over a single Light Rest (15 minutes).
- **Limb / Shape Reformation**: When manifested in a pseudo-humanoid form or inhabiting a host, the swarm can reshape damaged appendages within seconds.
