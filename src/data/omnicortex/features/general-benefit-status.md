# Benefit (Status)

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Special

## Description
The character holds a title of nobility, corporate executive power, or high military command.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You gain a Title (e.g., Duke, CEO, Admiral). You gain a **\+2 Bonus to Wealth** checks.

## Special Rules
This acts as a \[Ranked\] feature; higher ranks denote higher titles and influence.
