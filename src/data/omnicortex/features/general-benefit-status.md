# Benefit (Status)

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Special (Aristocratic or Corporate lineage)

## Description
High social standing, aristocratic titles, noble rank, executive board standing, and associated wealth stipends.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Grants significant social influence, aristocratic privilege, and +2 Wealth score per rank.

## Special Rules
Ranked: Higher ranks represent titles from Baron/Director to Archon/Grand Duke.
