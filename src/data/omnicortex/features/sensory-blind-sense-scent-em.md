# Blind Sense (Scent/EM)

**Category**: Sensory / Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisite**: Sensory Antennae or Electro-Receptive Physiology  

## Description
Prominent sensory antennae crowning the head serve as sensitive conduits for bio-energetic and electromagnetic fields. These delicate organs grant heightened spatial awareness and non-visual environmental navigation.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Perceptual Radius:** Detects moving organisms, electrical machinery, and biological entities within a **30-foot radius** (or 60-foot cone) regardless of lighting, darkness, visual obscurity, or total blindness.
* **Dual Sensory Channels:** Senses through subtle ambient air motion, olfactory plumes (scent), and electromagnetic emanations. Invisibility or visual camouflage confers no defensive concealment bonuses against this sensory sweep.
