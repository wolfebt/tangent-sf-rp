# Genetic Memory

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisite**: Kitin or Lineage with Cellular Inheritance  

## Description
Each new generation inherits a cumulative, albeit fragmented, memory bank of their ancestors, shaping their societal norms, skill sets, and instinctive reactions. This direct link to their past profoundly influences their culture, technical fluency, and resistance to historical revision.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Gain access to ancestral knowledge and Genomatrix blueprints:
* **Ancestral Recall:** The character gains a **+2 Bonus** on Lore, History, and Science checks relating to ancestral events, biological engineering, or collective history.
* **Genomatrix Blueprints:** The character intuitively comprehends biotechnological patterns and hive structural schematics without prior formal training.
