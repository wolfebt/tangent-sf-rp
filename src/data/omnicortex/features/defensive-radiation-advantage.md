### **Overview**
The operative's cellular biology and protective integument are reinforced by dense metalloid lattice structures or specialized radiotrophic pigments. This adaptation enables prolonged survival in high-radiation sectors, unshielded reactor spaces, and planetary fallout fields.

### **Mechanical Benefits**
* **Radiological Advantage:** The character rolls with **Advantage** (roll 2d10 and keep the highest result, or apply +3 bonus where opposed) on all Stamina, Survival, and physical checks made to resist the onset of radiation poisoning, sickness, or acute cellular degradation.
* **Rad Absorption / Soak:** Any raw Vitality or Stability damage directly inflicted by ambient ionizing radiation, solar flares, or dirty nuclear/antimatter detonation fields is reduced by **50% (halved)** after standard Damage Reduction (DR).
* **Zero Latent Mutation:** The character is immune to accidental non-beneficial mutagenic decay caused by environmental fallout.
