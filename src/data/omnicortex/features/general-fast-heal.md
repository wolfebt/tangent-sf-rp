# Fast Heal

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Stamina 2

## Description
The character's metabolism repairs tissue at an accelerated rate.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Your natural healing rate is **doubled**. You recover Vitality and Health twice as fast during Rest.

## Special Rules
This feature is \[Ranked\]. Rank 2 \= 3x healing, Rank 3 \= 4x healing.
