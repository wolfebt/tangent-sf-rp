# Inspired Reason

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Insightful Reason, Intellect 2

## Description
The character experiences flashes of brilliance and intuitive leaps of logic that allow them to solve the unsolvable.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may roll all **Logic Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll 2d10 with Advantage (roll twice and keep the highest result) when performing tasks requiring deduction or complex reasoning.
