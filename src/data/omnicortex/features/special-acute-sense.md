# Acute Sense

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Awareness 1

## Description
The character possesses a specific sensory organ that is highly developed, far exceeding the baseline for their species. This could be eagle eyes, a bloodhound’s nose, or sensitive auditory receptors.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Choose one sense (Vision, Hearing, Smell, Taste, or Touch). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to **Awareness Checks** utilizing that sense.

## Special Rules
\[Multiple\] You may take this feature multiple times, applying it to a different sense each time.
