# Toxic Blood

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Stamina 1, Poison Immunity  

## Description
The creature's emerald green circulatory system, powered by copper-based hemocyanin, carries potent endogenous chemical defense toxins. Anyone attempting to ingest the blood suffers swift biological distress.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Delivery:** Ingested (triggered when bitten, swallowed, or during predatory attacks involving oral contact).
* **Difficulty Class:** **DC 11 + (2 × Stamina)** Fortitude check.
* **Effect on Failure:** The victim suffers **Pain Incapacitation** for **1 hour**, unable to take offensive actions and doubled AP costs for movement.
* **Effect on Success:** On a successful Fortitude save, the victim instead suffers **Severe Nausea** (-2 on all d20 checks and actions) for 1 hour.
