# Leaper

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Athletics 1 or Agility 2  

## Description
The character possesses powerful digitigrade, mantis-like, or spring-loaded leg articulation engineered for explosive vertical and horizontal leaps.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Running Start**: Always counts as having a running start for all jumping and leaping distance calculations, even when initiating a leap from a dead stop or cramped stance.
- **Leap & Drop Advantage**: Make all Athletics or Acrobatics checks related to jumping across chasms, leaping over obstacles, or executing controlled high-altitude drops with **Advantage**.
- **Impact Absorption**: High-tensile chitinous shock dampers reduce falling damage by up to 20 feet.
