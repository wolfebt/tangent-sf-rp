# Double Slice

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Two-Weapon Fighting, Strength 1

## Description
The character applies full strength to both weapons when dual-wielding.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Add your full **Strength modifier** to damage rolls with your off-hand weapon (normally, off-hand weapons receive only 1/2 Strength modifier).
