# Myostatin Hypertrophy

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Strength 3, Stamina 2

## Description
A homozygous mutation in the MSTN gene renders the myostatin protein non-functional, removing biological brakes on muscle fiber proliferation. The character possesses massive, dense musculature without needing to train.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You gain a **+2 Bonus** to Might and Athletics checks. For the purposes of Grappling, lifting weight, and breaking objects, you are treated as if you are **one Size Category larger**.

## Special Rules
Prerequisite: Strength 3, Stamina 2.
