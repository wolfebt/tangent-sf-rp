# Torpor (Universal)

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisite**: Kitin or Non-Sleeping Species  

## Description
The species does not sleep in the conventional mammalian sense. Instead, individuals enter a 4-hour state of conscious torpor, maintaining environmental perception and alertness while metabolic and cellular maintenance occurs. They view the dreaming, subconscious minds of other species with profound distrust.

## Mechanics & Benefit
* **Conscious Rest:** Requires only **4 hours** of conscious inactivity to complete a Full Rest (recovering Vitality, Health, and Strain).
* **Sleep Immunity:** The character is completely immune to biological, magical, or psionic effects that induce involuntary sleep, stupor, or hypnotic unconsciousness.
* **Perceptual Vigilance:** While in torpor, the character remains aware of their immediate surroundings and suffers no penalties on passive Awareness or Perception checks.
