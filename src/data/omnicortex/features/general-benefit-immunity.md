# Benefit (Immunity)

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Special (Diplomatic or Imperial decree)

## Description
Legal immunity from prosecution under local planetary laws, customs tariffs, or extraterritorial arrest warrants.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Grants legal immunity (from Diplomatic Immunity to Inquisitorial License to Kill).

## Special Rules
Special: Requires sovereign or diplomatic faction affiliation.
