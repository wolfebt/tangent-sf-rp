# Environmental Adaptation

**Category**: Ability Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
Whether evolved in brutal frontier climates, engineered through genetic optimization, or tempered by void exploration, the character easily survives hazardous atmospheric, thermal, and barometric conditions.

## Mechanics & Benefit
The character suffers no penalties or exhaustion from extreme planetary temperatures (intense desert heat up to 60°C or sub-zero arctic cold down to -40°C) without special gear. When exposed to explosive decompression, thin atmospheres, or hard vacuum, they can sustain life without permanent lung damage or unconsciousness for up to 5 rounds (1 minute) by entering a controlled metabolic stasis.
