# Beginner's Luck

**Category**: Karma Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: The Skill being attempted must be at **Rank 5 (Novice) or lower**.

## Description
The character has an uncanny knack for succeeding at things they have no business doing. When inexperienced, they rely on instinct and fortune rather than training.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may spend **1 Karma Point** to gain **\+5 Temporary Ranks** in a specific skill for the duration of the current scene or encounter.  
  * *Mechanics:* This effectively treats an Untrained (Rank 0\) character as a Novice (Rank 5), or a Novice as a Professional (Rank 6-10) for a short time.
