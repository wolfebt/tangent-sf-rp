# Camouflage

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Stealth 1  

## Description
The character possesses passive epidermal coloration, matte chitinous properties, or biological patterning that naturally blends into shadows, subterranean cave walls, metallic ship framing, or rocky terrain.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Stealth Competence**: Grants a **+4 bonus** on all Stealth checks when remaining stationary or moving at half speed through dim light, shadows, subterranean environments, or favored hunting terrain.
- **Concealed Stalking**: Reduces the penalty for making Stealth checks while maneuvering around obstacles by 50%.
