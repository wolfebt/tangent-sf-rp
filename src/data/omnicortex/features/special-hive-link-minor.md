# Hive-Link (Minor)

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Kitin Genome or Swarm Attunement  

## Description
A streamlined neural antenna setup tuned primarily as a receiver for the psionic Chorus. While lacking the broadcasting potency of Cerebral strains, it enables instantaneous, unhesitating compliance with collective directives.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Command Receptivity**: Uninterrupted telepathic reception from any Manelli, Queen, or Hive Node within 5 miles.
- **Synchronized Readiness**: When allied hive leaders or commanders roll Initiative, the character may use the leader's Initiative result if it is higher than their own.
- **Mental Resistance**: Gains a **+2 bonus on saving throws against mental coercion, charm, or fear effects** originating from sources outside the Collective.
