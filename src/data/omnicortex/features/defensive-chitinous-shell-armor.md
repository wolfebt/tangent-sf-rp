# Armor (Chitinous Shell)

**Category**: Defensive Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Exoskeletal Physiology  

## Description
The body is enveloped in a hardened, glossy obsidian chitinous shell perpetually slick with microscopic natural polymer secretions. This heavy biomorphic armor dissipates impact energy, deflects shrapnel, and resists chemical burns.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Base DR 10**: Provides **Base 10 Damage Reduction (DR 10)** against physical trauma (kinetic, ballistic, slashing, piercing), direct energy attacks (thermal, voltic), and chemical caustic substances.
- **Environmental Buffering**: Grants advantage on saving throws against corrosive atmospheres and atmospheric pressure variations.
