# Blind Sense (EM/Vibration)

**Category**: Sensory / Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Electro-Receptive Array or Synaptic Vehicle Link  

## Description
Dominating the Syndess's anterior segment is a wrap-around sensory array and dual rows of antenna-cables. When slotted into a biomorphic husk or operating independently, these organs interpret electromagnetic waveforms and acoustic surface vibrations directly as tactile sensations.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Tactile Spatial Awareness**: Accurately detects and tracks moving entities, metallic masses, and energy sources within a **30-foot radius** (or 60-foot cone) without optical line of sight.
- **Hull Sensor Integration**: When integrated into a Kitin mecha, this sense expands across the vehicle’s entire outer hull surface, rendering the craft immune to flanking attacks and surprise strikes from concealed ground or atmospheric targets.
