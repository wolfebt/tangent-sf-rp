# Limited Flight

**Category**: Physical Features  
**Cost**: 2 BP  
**Prerequisites**: Athletics 1 or Agility 2  

## Description
The creature possesses lightweight dorsal elytra plates, membranous underwings, or pulsed bio-pneumatic vents capable of generating sustained aerodynamic lift for brief durations.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Burst Flight**: Can fly at **2x Ground Speed (80 ft/rd)** for up to 1 round of movement before needing to touch down.
- **Double Jump Distance**: Doubled jump distance when combining muscular leaps with wing assisted propulsion.
- **Slow Fall**: Immune to falling damage; can glide safely to the ground from any height at a rate of 30 ft per round.
