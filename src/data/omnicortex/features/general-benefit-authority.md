# Benefit (Authority)

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: Special (Law Enforcement background)

## Description
Legal enforcement authority granted by a governing body, planetary militia, or sector law enforcement agency.

## Mechanics & Benefit
Grants legal authority, jurisdictional powers, and arrest rights within designated sectors (from Deputy to Sector Marshal / Special Agent).

## Special Rules
Ranked: Higher ranks grant wider planetary and interstellar jurisdiction.
