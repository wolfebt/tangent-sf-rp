# Inventor

**Category**: General Features  
**Cost**: 3 BP (2 BP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 BP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
The character possesses an innate intuitive grasp of mechanical, electronic, and energetic principles, enabling them to fabricate bespoke solutions and jury-rig sophisticated hardware under impossible conditions.

## Mechanics & Benefit
The character reduces the time required to build, upgrade, or modify weapons, armor, and gear by 50%. Once per session, they can jury-rig a temporary gadget or override an incompatible system with scrap parts, functioning reliably for a scene before breaking down.
