# Balanced Center

**Category**: Discipline Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Centering Expert, Discipline Expertise (in all currently used Disciplines)

## Description
The character has achieved a perfect equilibrium between the channeling of power (Attune) and the shaping of it (Discipline).

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may **Take 10** on **Discipline Skill Checks** even when threatened, distracted, or in combat.  
* **Purpose:** Guarantees consistent Damage or Effect results. You no longer roll for power; you simply apply a reliable standard of devastation.
