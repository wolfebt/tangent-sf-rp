# Agile Wrestling

**Category**: Combat Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: Agility 1, Unarmed Combat 1

## Description
The character utilizes leverage, flexibility, and technique rather than brute force to control opponents in close quarters.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You may use your **Agility** modifier instead of your Strength modifier when making Grapple Checks (both to initiate and maintain a grapple).

## Special Rules
This applies to the grapple check only; damage dealt while grappling is still calculated using Strength unless another feature modifies it.
