# Non-Lethal Immunity

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
Through physiological adaptation, neural dampening, or synthetic resilience, the character does not register subdual trauma, stun effects, or pain-based incapacitation.

## Mechanics & Benefit
You are completely immune to **Non-Lethal Damage**. Attacks or hazards that deal exclusively Non-Lethal or Stun damage inflict 0 damage to you (though secondary physical or environmental effects still apply normally).
