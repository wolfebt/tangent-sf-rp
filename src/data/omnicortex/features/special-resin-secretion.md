# Resin Secretion

**Category**: Special Features  
**Cost**: 2 CP  
**Prerequisites**: Biomorphic Glands  

## Description
The organism possesses abdominal and thoracic dermal glands capable of synthesizing and extruding high-grade biomorphic resin. This compound rapidly cross-links upon exposure to air or moisture, forming an impermeable, shock-resistant seal.

## Mechanics & Benefit
- **Field Patching & Mecha Repair**: Can seal breaches in spacecraft hulls, mech armor plates, airlocks, and environmental suits. Restores **2d6 Structure Points** or patches critical integrity breaches in 1 round of sustained secretion.
- **Structural Adhesive**: The extruded resin cures in 10 seconds into a rigid polymer with tensile strength comparable to reinforced titanium alloys.
- **Airtight / Watertight**: Naturally seals atmospheric leaks, resisting vacuums and pressures up to 10 atmospheres.
