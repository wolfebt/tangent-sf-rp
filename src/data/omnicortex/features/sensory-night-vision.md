# Night Vision

**Category**: Sensory Features  
**Cost**: 3 CP (2 CP if Suggested Feature, minimum 1 CP)  
**Prerequisite**: None

## Description
Your character can see in the dark, giving them an advantage in low-light conditions and deep shadows without requiring an external illumination source.

## Mechanics & Benefit
The character ignores darkness and dim-light penalties to Awareness and Combat attack rolls within medium range (up to 30 meters). They can discern movement and shapes in total non-magical darkness as if it were daylight, though fine color details may be perceived in monochrome shades.
