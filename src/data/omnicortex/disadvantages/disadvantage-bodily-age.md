# Bodily Age

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 5 / 10 BP  
**Type**: Physical

## Description
The character is outside the physical prime for their species, suffering bodily limitations.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Child (10 BP)**: 7-12ish typical human years. **-1 Strength, -1 Agility, -1 Stamina** (Choose two). Small stature, physically undeveloped.
- **Teen (5 BP)**: 12-16ish typical human years. **-1 Strength, -1 Agility, or -1 Stamina** (Choose one). Awkward growth phases.
- **Old (5 BP)**: 60-90ish typical human years. **-1 Strength, -1 Agility, or -1 Stamina** (Choose one). Aching joints and slower reflexes.
- **Venerable (10 BP)**: 90+ typical human years. **-1 Strength, -1 Agility, -1 Stamina** (Choose two). Physically frail but rich in experience.
