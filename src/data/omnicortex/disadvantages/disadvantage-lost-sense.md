# Lost Sense

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 CP  
**Type**: Physical

## Description
Complete biological absence or destruction of one of the character's primary sensory channels.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **3 CP**: Total loss of **Taste or Smell**. Immune to scent-based nausea/gas sickness, but cannot smell toxins or smoke.
- **6 CP**: Total loss of **Touch (Numbness)** or **Hearing (Deafness)**. `-4` to fine Dexterity (if Numb) or auto-fail all acoustic checks (if Deaf).
- **9 CP**: Total loss of **Sight (Blindness)**. Automatically fails visual checks; 50% miss chance in combat unless using blind-fight/meta senses.
