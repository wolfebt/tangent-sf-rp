# Addiction

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 CP  
**Type**: Psychological / Physical

## Description
A chemical, biological, or psychological dependency on a substance or intense stimulus.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 CP)**: A bad habit (tobacco, alcohol, mild gambling). Suffer **-2 to Will saves** if denied within 24 hours.
- **Moderate (6 CP)**: Serious dependency (hallucinogens, painkillers, hard narcotics). Going 24 hours without inflicts the **Sickened** condition (`-2` to all checks) until indulged.
- **Major (9 CP)**: Debilitating addiction (rare combat stims, neural-jacking). Withdrawal begins after 12 hours. The character rolls **all checks with Disadvantage** and takes **1 Constitution damage per day** until indulged or detoxed.
