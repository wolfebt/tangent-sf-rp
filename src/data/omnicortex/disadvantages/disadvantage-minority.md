# Minority

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 2+ BP  
**Type**: Social

## Description
Belonging to an oppressed, feared, or heavily stigmatized demographic, species, or creed in the sector.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Mechanics
- Gains the **Xeno / Social Stigma** modifier.
- Prices for goods/services are 20-50% higher; law enforcement treats character with extreme suspicion; initial NPC reactions start as *Unfriendly* or *Hostile*.
