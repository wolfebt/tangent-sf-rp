# Distinctive Features

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 CP  
**Type**: Physical / Social

## Description
Unmistakable physical marks, alien coloration, cybernetic glow, or monstrous traits that make blending in impossible.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Disguisable (3 CP)**: Large facial scar, distinct tattoo, exotic hair. Can be hidden with standard Disguise check.
- **Not-Disguisable (6 CP)**: Extra eye cluster, bioluminescent blue skin in human cities, 7-foot height. Disguise checks roll with Disadvantage.
- **Very Obvious (9 CP)**: Blazing energy aura, stone carapace, horrific mutation. Stealth and Disguise impossible in populated areas.
