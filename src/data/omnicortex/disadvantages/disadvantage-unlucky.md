# Unlucky

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Metaphysical / Karma

## Description
A profound cosmic jinx that drains the character's pool of heroic luck and causes probability to work against them.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **3 BP**: **-2 to Karma Pool** maximum.
- **6 BP**: **-4 to Karma Pool** maximum.
- **9 BP**: **-6 to Karma Pool** maximum. Drops character into **Karmic Debt**, granting the GM automatic complication triggers at the start of each session.
