# Fragile Frame

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted / Refund**: 2 CP  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
The Syndess has evolved strictly for symbiotic cybernetic integration. In their exposed larval state outside a biomorphic mecha chassis or sealed nutrient tube, their soft translucent dermis and slender segmental frame lack structural reinforcement against concussive forces, environmental shocks, and atmospheric pressures.

## Mechanics & Severity
* **Physical Save Penalty:** Outside of a mecha cockpit or armored containment pod, the character suffers a **-2 penalty to all physical saving throws** (Fortitude, Reflex, and Stamina-based endurance checks).
* **Vulnerable to Trauma:** When exposed directly to kinetic explosions or bludgeoning attacks, any knockdown or stagger condition applied to the character increases in duration by 1 round.
