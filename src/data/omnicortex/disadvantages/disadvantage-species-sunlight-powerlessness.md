# Sunlight Powerlessness

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (BP Reduction)  
**BP Refund**: +6 BP (Cost: -6 BP)  
**Type**: Meta  
**Prerequisites**: Undead / Half-Undead / Non-Living  

## Description
Staggered/Helpless in direct sunlight. Req: Undead/Half-Undead.

## Mechanics & Severity
Requirements: Undead / Half-Undead or Non-Living lineage. Exposure to direct solar radiation completely paralyzes internal energetic channels. While exposed to natural direct sunlight, the character is Staggered and Helpless, able to take only a single standard action per round with severe disadvantage.
