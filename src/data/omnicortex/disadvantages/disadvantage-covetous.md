# Covetous

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Psychological

## Description
An overwhelming compulsion toward Greed (Money), Lechery (Lust), Ambition (Power), or Conspicuousness (Fame).

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Compulsion Trigger**: When presented with the object of desire, make a **Will Save (DC 15 + Severity)** to avoid acting impulsively.
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Grumbles and complains bitterly if denied.
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Takes dangerous tactical risks or commits social faux pas to acquire the prize.
- **Major (9 BP)**: Will steal, betray allies, or enter combat to satisfy the craving.
