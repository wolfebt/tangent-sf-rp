# Obligation

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Social

## Description
Bound by contractual feudal duty, military service, syndicate blood oath, or patron servitude.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Weekly report-ins, minor tithes, or regular civic duty.
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Must perform dangerous missions on demand or surrender 25% of all earned revenue.
- **Major (9 BP)**: Total servitude. The character is a sworn thrall, indentured slave, or sacred avatar with zero personal assets.
