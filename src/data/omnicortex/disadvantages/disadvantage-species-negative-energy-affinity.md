# Negative Energy Affinity

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (BP Reduction)  
**BP Refund**: +4 BP (Cost: -4 BP)  
**Type**: Meta  

## Description
Alive, but harmed by positive/healed by negative energy (like undead).

## Mechanics & Severity
Though biologically alive, the creature's metaphysical matrix reacts inversely to life force energies: positive/radiant healing spells and standard medical nanites cause damage instead of healing, while negative/entropic/necrotic energy effects restore Health and Vitality.
