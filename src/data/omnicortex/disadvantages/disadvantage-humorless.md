# Humorless

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Social

## Description
Takes every statement literally and lacks the ability to parse sarcasm, dry humor, or double entendres.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Mechanics
- **-2 Penalty** to Insight and Social checks involving nuance, sarcasm, subtext, innuendo, or metaphors.
