# Mute

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Physical

## Description
Lacking vocal cords or biological capacity for verbal speech.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Mechanics
- Cannot speak aloud. Cannot cast invocations requiring Verbal components. Must communicate via sign language, datapad text, or telepathy.
