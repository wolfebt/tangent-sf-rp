# Mental Issues

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 BP  
**Type**: Mental

## Description
Neurodivergence, chronic amnesia, hallucinations, severe cognitive blocks, or traumatic brain injuries.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Mild distraction or fixation. `-2` to Concentration checks.
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Stress confusion. Suffer **-2 penalty to all mental checks** when under duress.
- **Major (9 BP)**: Hallucinogenic / Dissociative. Suffer **-4 penalty to Perception**; struggles to distinguish reality from delusion.
- **Severe (12 BP)**: Catatonic / Paralyzing Anxiety. When triggered, character cannot initiate attacks or complex actions, taking only defensive actions.
