# Dependent

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Social

## Description
Responsible for the continuous protection, feeding, and medical safety of a non-combatant NPC ward.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Capable (3 BP)**: Ward feeds and dresses themselves but needs protection in combat (spouse, teenage apprentice).
- **Needy (6 BP)**: Ward requires daily assistance, supervision, and guidance (young child, frail elder).
- **Dependent (9 BP)**: Ward is completely helpless or requires expensive medical upkeep/stasis pod support (infant, comatose patient).
