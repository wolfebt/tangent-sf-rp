# Emotional Issues

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 BP  
**Type**: Psychological

## Description
Severe emotional volatility, mood swings, rage triggers, or trauma-induced freezing under pressure.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Mood swings. `-2` penalty to social interactions when under stress.
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Outbursts or deep withdrawal. Make **Will Save (DC 15)** under duress or become *Stunned* for 1 round.
- **Major (9 BP)**: Debilitating hysteria or catatonia. Make **Will Save (DC 20)** at start of any round where damage is taken to act normally.
- **Severe (12 BP)**: Crippling instability. Permanent **-2 to Will saves**. Must pass **Will Save (DC 18)** at start of each combat turn or become *Panicked*, *Paralyzed*, or *Staggered*.
