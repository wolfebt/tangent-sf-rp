# Nightmares

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 2 BP  
**Type**: Mental

## Description
Plagued by psychic echoes, traumatic memories, or horrifying supernatural dreams.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Mechanics
- After every Long Rest, roll **1d10**. On a **1**, the character suffers night terrors and gains **no benefit from the rest**, starting the day **Fatigued** (`-2` to all actions).
