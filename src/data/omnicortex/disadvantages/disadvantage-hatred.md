# Hatred

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Psychological

## Description
Deep prejudice, visceral loathing, or blood-feud hatred directed against a specific species, faction, or creed.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Disgust & Prejudice. `-2` to all Social checks with target group.
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Loathing. Must pass a **Will Save (DC 15)** to assist, heal, or cooperate with them.
- **Major (9 BP)**: Violent animosity. Must pass a **Will Save (DC 18)** *not* to attack or sabotage them on sight.
