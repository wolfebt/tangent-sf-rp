# Honorable

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 CP  
**Type**: Psychological

## Description
Bound by an inflexible code of chivalry, bushido, rules of engagement, or strict honesty.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 CP)**: Gentlemanly conduct. Refuses to cheat at games or strike from behind.
- **Moderate (6 CP)**: Code of Honor. Won't attack an unarmed foe, won't tell deliberate lies, accepts formal surrenders.
- **Major (9 CP)**: Absolute Pacifism or Unbreakable Oath. Cannot break word or harm a living being even to save own life.
