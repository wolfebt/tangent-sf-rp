# Stigma: Minor Xeno (Hidden) / Severe Xeno (Revealed)

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted / Refund**: 2 CP (Minor Hidden) / 4 CP (Severe Revealed)  
**Type**: Social  

## Description
The character is an insectoid mimetic organism operating within predominantly human or conventional carbon-based societies. While superficially humanoid, unnatural biological traits (emerald copper blood, needle-like teeth, cranial antennae) mark them as an alien outsider.

## Mechanics & Severity
* **Concealed State (Minor Xeno -2):** As long as antennae, sharp needle teeth, and green blood remain concealed, the character incurs only a **Minor Xeno penalty (-1 to -2)** on social negotiations with suspicious outsiders or xenophobic factions.
* **Revealed State (Severe Xeno -4):** If the character's antennae are exposed, green blood is spilled in public, or their insectoid nature is discovered, their social penalty worsens immediately to **Severe Xeno (-4)**. Prices increase by 50–100%, authorities are notified, and initial NPC reactions become automatically Unfriendly or Hostile.
