# Psionic Static Susceptibility (Riezen Disruption)

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted / Refund**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Metaphysical / Neural  

## Description
Because the character's nervous system is tightly calibrated to the structured carrier wave of the Chorus, chaotic or discordant psionic broadcasts inflict acute neuro-biological shock. The discordant frequency emitted by Riezen swarms or unaligned psychic turbulence violently clashes with the character's mental pathways, triggering intense cranial agony and cognitive disorientation.

## Mechanics & Severity
* **Psionic Disruption:** While within telepathic broadcast proximity (typically within line of sight or within 100 meters) of an active Riezen swarm or an artificial psionic scrambler, the character suffers **Disadvantage** on all Concentration, Metaphysics, Insight, and Initiative checks.
* **Neuro-Sensory Agony:** Each round the character begins their turn within active Riezen static, they must make a **DC 12 Willpower / Stamina check** or suffer 1 point of non-lethal Mental Strain / bio-neural headache and lose their Minor Action.
* **Chorus Disconnect:** Direct exposure to concentrated static temporarily suppresses Hive-Link communication until the source is neutralized or out of range.
