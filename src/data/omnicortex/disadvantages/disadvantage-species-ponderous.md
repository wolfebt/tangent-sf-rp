# Ponderous (Disadvantage)

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (CP Reduction)  
**CP Refund**: +4 CP (Cost: -4 CP)  
**Type**: Movement  

## Description
Base Speed -20 feet. (CP Gain) *

## Mechanics & Severity
The species suffers a -20 foot penalty to their base ground movement speed (reducing standard 30 ft speed to 10 ft). Note: A species may only select one base speed modifier trait.
