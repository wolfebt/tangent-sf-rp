# Secret

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 BP  
**Type**: Background / Social

## Description
Hiding a dangerous truth or past crime that would result in public ruin, exile, or execution if exposed.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Reputation damage or public embarrassment (e.g., forged academic degree).
- **Moderate (6 BP)**: Legal prosecution, imprisonment, or exile (e.g., undercover enemy operative, escaped convict).
- **Major (9 BP)**: Immediate death warrant or sector-wide manhunt (e.g., fugitive heir to fallen dynasty, wanted serial assassin).
