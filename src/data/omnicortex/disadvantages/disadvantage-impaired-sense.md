# Impaired Sense

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Physical

## Description
One primary sensory organ is partially damaged or weak (e.g., Near-Sighted, Hard of Hearing, Nerve Damage).

## Mechanics & Severity
### Mechanics
- **Alertness and Perception Checks** relying on the chosen sense are permanently made with **Disadvantage**.
