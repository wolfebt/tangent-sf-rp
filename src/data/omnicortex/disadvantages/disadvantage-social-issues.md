# Social Issues

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 CP  
**Type**: Social

## Description
Severe social awkwardness, abrasive personality, inability to read rooms, or notorious reputation.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 CP)**: Introverted/Shy. `-2` to Persuasion checks.
- **Moderate (6 CP)**: Abrasive or Clueless. `-4` to Diplomacy; NPC starting attitudes drop one step lower.
- **Major (9 CP)**: Antisocial / Sociopathic. All social checks (Diplomacy, Deception, Intimidate) made with **Disadvantage**.
- **Severe (12 CP)**: Social Pariah. All social checks with Disadvantage plus a permanent **-2 penalty to all Charisma skills**.
