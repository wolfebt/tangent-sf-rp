# Disability

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 3 / 6 / 9 / 18 BP  
**Type**: Physical

## Description
Missing limbs, paralysis, or severe mechanical impairment of bodily motor function.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Minor (3 BP)**: Missing a Hand or Foot. `-2` to Athletics/Dexterity tasks with that limb; reduced movement if foot.
- **Major (6 BP)**: Missing a full Limb (Arm or Leg). Cannot use two-handed gear/shields (Arm); movement speed halved (Leg).
- **Severe (9 BP)**: Two Limbs missing or Paraplegic (Legs paralyzed). Requires wheelchair, hover-rig, or cybernetics.
- **Crippling (18 BP)**: Quadriplegic or all limbs missing. Requires full-body life-support chassis or prosthetics.
