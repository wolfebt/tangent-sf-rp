# Adversary

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted**: 1 to 10 BP  
**Type**: Social / Background

## Description
An individual rival or powerful organization actively hunting, harassing, or seeking to ruin the character.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **Individual Adversary (1-5 BP)**:
  - *1-2 BP*: Nuisance or rival of equal power level.
  - *3-5 BP*: Superior hunter, assassin, or nemesis who actively tracks the character.
- **Organizational Adversary (5-10 BP)**:
  - *5-7 BP*: Local gang, sector police precinct, or planetary guild.
  - *8-10 BP*: Galaxy-spanning megacorporation (Syndicate), Imperial Inquisition (Impyrium), or interstellar crime syndicate.
