# Armless

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (BP Reduction)  
**BP Refund**: +4 BP (Cost: -4 BP)  
**Type**: Physical  

## Description
Without Arms.

## Mechanics & Severity
The species completely lacks arms, forelimbs, tentacles, or manual manipulation appendages. The character cannot wield weapons, hold items, carry two-handed gear, or perform fine mechanical manipulation without specialized prosthetic rigs, mouth grips, or cybernetics.
