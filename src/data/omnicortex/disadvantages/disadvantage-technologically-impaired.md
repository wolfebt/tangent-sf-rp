# Technologically Impaired

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**CP Granted**: 10 / 20 / 30 CP  
**Type**: Mental / Background

## Description
Baffled by modern technology, computing devices, energy weapons, or automated starships.

## Mechanics & Severity
### Tiers & Effects
- **10 CP**: **-1 Tech Level**. Treats standard TL 3 tech as unfamiliar (-2 penalty).
- **20 CP**: **-2 Tech Level**. Struggles with basic industrial machinery (-4 penalty).
- **30 CP**: **-3 Tech Level (Luddite)**. Cannot operate computers, energy weapons, or starship controls without massive penalties.
