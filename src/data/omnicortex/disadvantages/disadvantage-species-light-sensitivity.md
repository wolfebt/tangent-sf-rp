# Light Sensitivity

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (BP Reduction)  
**BP Refund**: +2 BP (Cost: -2 BP)  
**Type**: Sensory  
**Prerequisites**: Darkvision / Dark Sight  

## Description
Dazzled in bright sunlight. Req: Darkvision.

## Mechanics & Severity
Requirements: Darkvision / Dark Sight. Optical photoreceptors are overly sensitive to solar radiation. While operating in direct, unshielded bright sunlight or equivalent daylight-level illumination, the character is permanently Dazzled (–1 penalty on attack rolls and sight-based Perception checks).
