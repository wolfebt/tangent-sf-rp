# Stigma: Severe Xeno / Monstrous

**Category**: Hindrances & Disadvantages  
**BP Granted / Refund**: 4 BP  
**Type**: Social  

## Description
The character is an overt alien aberration—such as an intimidating arachno-tauric organism with multi-jointed limbs, clicking mandibles, and an obsidian chitinous lower abdomen. Their appearance triggers immediate fear, revulsion, and instinctive panic in standard humanoid societies.

## Mechanics & Severity
- **Severe Social Penalty (-4)**: The character suffers a **-4 penalty** on all social negotiation, diplomacy, and persuasion checks with standard civil populations and non-hive factions.
- **Immediate Hostility**: Non-allied civilian NPCs generally react with terror or hostile defensiveness upon sight unless pacified through overwhelming psionic presence or diplomatic intermediaries.
- **Surcharges & Confinement**: Commercial vendors charge 50–100% surcharges, and municipal planetary laws often restrict monstrous aberrations from free transit without armed chaperones or permits.
