# Light Blindness

**Category**: Species Disadvantages (BP Reduction)  
**BP Refund**: +4 BP (Cost: -4 BP)  
**Type**: Sensory  
**Prerequisites**: Darkvision / Dark Sight  

## Description
Abrupt exposure to bright light blinds for 1 round; then dazzled. Req: Darkvision.

## Mechanics & Severity
Requirements: Darkvision / Dark Sight. Abrupt exposure to bright sunlight, flashbangs, or intense illumination permanently tuned to subterranean darkness blinds the creature for 1 full combat round. In subsequent rounds of continuous exposure, the character remains Dazzled (–1 penalty on attack rolls and sight-based Perception checks).
