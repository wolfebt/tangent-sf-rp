# Hit Location: Torso (-1 to -4)

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Targeting Modifiers & Trauma
- **Torso**: Most common hit area, containing vital internal organs. **Called Shot -1**.
- **Chest**: Upper torso, heart and lungs; risk of cardiac shock and gasping. **Called Shot -2**.
- **Abdomen**: Lower torso, stomach, intestines, bladder. **Called Shot -2**.
- **Groin / Pelvis**: Low weak point location; severe debilitating pain and mobility loss. **Called Shot -4**.
