# Vehicular Combat: Operational Fundamentals & Scale

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Operational Loop: Action, Reaction, Consequence
In the Tangent universe, vehicular combat is an escalation of the core 2d10 mechanics across all chassis sizes, from nimble hover-bikes to gargantuan void-cruisers.

---

## 1. The Tactical Round
Vehicular combat operates on the standard 6-second Combat Round, but the **Scale** of the action changes:

### Initiative Formula
$$\text{Vehicle Initiative} = 2\text{d}10 + \text{Agility Mod} + \text{Alertness} + \text{Handling Modifier}$$

- **Handling Modifier**: A stat intrinsic to the chassis (e.g., Fighter Jet has **+4**, Capital Ship has **-4**).

### The Crew Turn
Unlike personal combat where a single character acts, a vehicle is a platform for multiple actors:
- **Pilot**: Controls Movement, Evasion (Defense), and Fixed Forward Weapons.
- **Gunner(s)**: Control Turrets and Pintle-mounted weapons. Act on the Pilot's Initiative count but track their own Actions.
- **Engineer / System Operator**: Manages Shields, Sensors, Electronic Warfare, and Field Repairs.

---

## 2. Scale & The Size Modifier
A pistol cannot damage a battle tank, and a battleship's spinal battery cannot easily track a single infantryman.
- **Scaling Modifiers**: Governed by *01.01.09 Scaling*.
- **David vs. Goliath**: Small targets shooting Large targets get a bonus to Hit (large surface area), but must penetrate heavy vehicle Damage Resistance (DR).
- **Goliath vs. David**: Large weapons suffer tracking penalties to Hit smaller, nimble targets (Tracking Penalty).
