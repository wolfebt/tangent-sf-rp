# Precision Damage & Active Defense Counter

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics
- **Precision Damage**: A direct damage bonus derived from the skill level of the attacker (relative Combat or Focus Skill Rank).
- **Active Defense Counter**: A defender who is actively defending reduces incoming damage by their **Defense score** (acting as a direct reduction against precision bonuses).
- **Sneak Attack Activation**: Sneak Attack damage is added **only if at least 1 point of damage penetrates target Armor DR** (determined after Armor DR but before the target's Stamina/CON modifier).
