# Metaphysics Triad Framework

In Tangent SF RP, all reality-defying powers—whether magic, psionics, divine miracles, or quantum meta-tech—operate on a unified triad of skills:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
|                     THE METAPHYSICS TRIAD                   |
+------------------------------+------------------------------+
| ATTUNE (The Channel)         | Conduit capacity & Save DC   |
| DISCIPLINE (The Shape)       | Range, Area & Base Potency   |
| INVOCATIONS (The Effect)     | Specific Spells & Scaling    |
+------------------------------+------------------------------+
```

## 1. Attune (The Channel)
- Sets the Difficulty Class (DC) for target saving throws: `d20 + Attune + Ability Mod`.
- Penetrates target Spell Resistance (SR) and maintains Concentration.

## 2. Disciplines (The Shape)
- 6 Disciplines (Dimension, Energy, Entropy, Illusion, Matter, Mental), each split into 2 Focuses.
- Ranks dictate range brackets and base damage/healing dice (from Touch at Novice to Global at Pinnacle).

## 3. Invocations (The Effect)
- Specific rote techniques costing **1 CP per Rank**.
- Adds **+1 die of effect per rank** to the Discipline base dice.
