# Character Wealth Score (WS) & Financial Status Hierarchy

**Category**: Core Character Economy & Financial Systems  
**Core Reference**: `02 ECONOMY AND WEALTH.md`, `1.01 CHARACTER CREATION.md`, & `2.00 ECONOMATRIX.md`

## 1. Character Wealth Score Determination
A character's **Wealth Score (WS)** represents their abstract financial leverage, liquidity, creditworthiness, and commercial influence across civilized space.

$$\text{Starting Wealth Score (WS)} = \text{Occupation Base} + \text{Origin Mod} + \text{Faction Mod} + \text{Tech Level Mod} + \text{Vocation Skill Bonus} + \text{Custom Mod}$$

### I. Starting Occupation Base Wealth (WS 1–6)
* **Representative:** Base WS 6 (Apex political, corporate, or diplomatic standing)
* **Merchant:** Base WS 5 (Commercial enterprise, cargo lines & capital leverage)
* **Entertainer:** Base WS 5 (Celebrity patronage, high-tier public bookings)
* **Adept:** Base WS 4 (Metaphysical expertise, esoteric consulting fees)
* **Criminal:** Base WS 4 (Underworld liquidity, smuggling, black-market fencing)
* **Builder:** Base WS 3 (Technical trades, manufacturing contracts, fabrication)
* **Scholar:** Base WS 3 (Academic stipends, university endowments, institutional grants)
* **Specialist:** Base WS 3 (Contract engineering, xeno-research, field commissions)
* **Agent:** Base WS 2 (Operational expense accounts, covert field stipends)
* **Citizen:** Base WS 2 (Standard municipal wage, stable employment)
* **Drifter:** Base WS 1 (Transient day labor, gig economy, itinerant odd jobs)
* **Scout:** Base WS 1 (Frontier surveying, resource prospecting, hazard bounties)
* **Soldier:** Base WS 1 (Military standard-issue rations, enlisted field pay)

### II. Origin Wealth Modifiers (+0 to +3)
* **Leisure (+3):** Resort and luxury garden worlds; high disposable capital, service wealth.
* **Enlightened (+2):** Cultural and academic enclaves; robust institutional stipends.
* **Industrial (+2):** Manufacturing powerhouses; high demand for technical skill.
* **Research (+2):** High-tech laboratory worlds; lucrative corporate research grants.
* **Aquatic (+1):** Specialized oceanic biospheres; hydrogen and pharmaceutical export dividends.
* **Spacer (+1):** Orbital stations, asteroid habitats, asteroid-belt merchant exposure.
* **Urban (+1):** Dense metropolis and arcology hubs; active consumer finance.
* **Agricultural (+0):** Agri-world baseline; high food volume with stable agrarian prices.
* **Colony (+0):** Frontier settlements; self-reliant barter economies.
* **Militaristic (+0):** Garrison and fortress worlds; strictly controlled military rations.

### III. Faction Wealth Modifiers (+0 to +6)
* **Mekan Sovereign Dominion (+6):** Absolute automation, post-scarcity algorithmic wealth.
* **Free Trade Syndicate (+4):** Unregulated galactic banking, free enterprise monopolies.
* **Alterian Enclave (+3):** Ancient wealth reserves, crystalline energy monopolies.
* **The Ascendancy (+4):** Aristocratic merchant houses, imperial state treasures, sovereign innovation reserves.
* **Entari Confederation (+3):** High-yield trade agreements, resource syndicates.
* **The Impyrium (+3):** Military-industrial complexes, state tax revenues.
* **Auluran / Kitin (+2):** Communal hive production, bio-engineered exports.
* **Solarian Dynasty (+2):** Established corporate dynasties, trade monopolies.
* **Terran Coalition (+0):** Standard civic baseline, bureaucratic social security.
* **Outworlds Coalition (+0):** Frontier autonomy, uncentralized barter systems.

### IV. Origin Tech Level Modifiers (-4 to +8)
* **TL 0 (Stone Age):** -4 WS
* **TL 1 (Metal Age):** -2 WS
* **TL 2 (Data Age):** +0 WS
* **TL 3 (Space Age - Standard Baseline):** +2 WS
* **TL 4 (Stellar Age):** +4 WS
* **TL 5 (Galactic Age):** +8 WS

### V. Vocation & Trade Skill Rank Stage Bonus (+1 to +5)
Applied from the character's single highest qualifying professional or commercial skill (e.g., Vocation, Trade, Business, Appraisal, Logistics, Medicine, Artificer, Performance, Discipline):
* **Novice (Ranks 1–2):** +1 Wealth Score (Apprentice / Journeyman wages)
* **Practiced (Ranks 3–5):** +2 Wealth Score (Certified tradesperson, steady income)
* **Expert (Ranks 6–8):** +3 Wealth Score (Senior specialist, consulting rates)
* **Master (Ranks 9–10):** +4 Wealth Score (Renowned authority, master artisan)
* **Grandmaster (Ranks 11–12):** +5 Wealth Score (Guildmaster, sector-wide fame)

## 3. Auto-Buy Rules & The Liquidity Gap
* **Auto-Buy Principle:** Any equipment, weapon, cyberware component, or service with a market credit cost at or below the character's **Auto-Buy Threshold** is acquired automatically without financial rolls, asset liquidations, or lifestyle penalties.
* **The Liquidity Gap:** When an item's market value exceeds the character's auto-buy limit:
  $$\text{Liquidity Gap Cost} = \max(0, \text{Item Market Value} - \text{Auto-Buy Limit})$$
  The character must finance this difference through liquid credit caches, employer expense stipends, black-market loans, or credit debt financing.
