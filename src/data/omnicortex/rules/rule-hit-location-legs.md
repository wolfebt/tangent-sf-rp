# Hit Location: Legs (-2 to -5)

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Targeting Modifiers & Trauma

- **Thigh**: **Called Shot -3**.
- **Shin**: **Called Shot -3**.
- **Foot**: **Called Shot -4**.
- **Digits**: **Called Shot -5**.
