# Vehicular Combat: Tactical Interactions & Siege

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## 1. Pilot vs. Pilot (Vehicle vs. Vehicle)
- **Evasive Maneuvers (Action)**: The Pilot forfeits their Attack Action to impose **Disadvantage on all incoming fire** for the round.
- **Jinking (Reaction)**: Reaction triggered when fired upon. Pilot rolls **Pilot Skill vs. Attack Roll**. Success negates the hit completely, but costs movement on the vehicle's next turn.
- **Fixed vs. Turret Weapons**:
  - *Fixed Weapons*: Fire in a **45-degree forward arc**. Requires the pilot to orient the vehicle directly at the target.
  - *Turrets*: Fire with **360-degree coverage**, operated by Gunners independently of chassis movement.

---

## 2. Gunner vs. Mecha (Infantry vs. Vehicle)
- **The Armor Threshold**: Vehicles possess massive Damage Resistance (DR).
  - *Rule*: If incoming weapon damage does not exceed DR, **the shot bounces harmlessly (ping!)**. Small arms cannot penetrate heavy armor plate.
- **Called Shots (Weak Points)**: Infantry can bypass thick armor by targeting optic sensors, drive treads, or exhaust ports.
  - *Mechanic*: Attack made at a **-5 Strike Penalty**. On hit, **ignore 50% of Armor DR** or disable a specific system (e.g., "Immobilized").
- **Boarding**: Infantry can physically climb a slow or stationary vehicle.
  - *Mechanic*: **Athletics Check vs. Vehicle Speed/Defense**. Success places the unit directly on the hull, bypassing Scale penalties for all subsequent attacks.

---

## 3. Gunner vs. Structure (Siege Warfare)
- **Static Defense**: Stationary structures have **0 Agility** and an effective **Defense of 5** (auto-hit by almost all attacks).
- **Hardness (DR) & Modules**: Structures are divided into modular sections (e.g., 10x10 wall module).
- **Siege Damage**: Heavy Weapons (Explosive, Energy ordnance) deal **Double Damage** to stationary structures.
- **Breaching & Collapse**:
  - Reducing a Module to **0 SP** creates a **Breach** large enough for infantry assault teams.
  - Reducing a Module to **-50% SP** triggers catastrophic **Structural Collapse** of that section and overhead levels.
