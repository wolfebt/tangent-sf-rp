# Skill Modifiers & Stacking Rules

A skill check may be influenced by multiple factors:

1. **Ability Score Modifier**: The primary physical or mental stat.
2. **Skill Rank**: Direct training investment.
3. **Difficulty / DC**: The target baseline set by the GM.
4. **Competence**: Guidance from a mentor, manual, or database.
5. **Environmental**: Terrain, weather, lighting, gravity.
6. **Gear / Tools**: Quality and suitability of specialized equipment.
7. **Luck / Karma**: Karma Point expenditure or fortunate coincidences.
8. **Features**: Permanent biological, cybernetic, or martial perks.
9. **Meta**: Magic, psionic, or supernatural enhancement fields.
10. **Situational**: Stress, active aid, or tactical positioning.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Non-Stacking Rule**: A character can only benefit from **one Competence bonus, one Environmental bonus, and one Gear bonus** on a single check. Different modifier categories stack; multiple bonuses within the same category do not.
