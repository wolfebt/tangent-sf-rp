# Stability Threshold Points

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics
\text{Total Stability Points} = \text{Constitution Score} + 5

- Tracks remaining life force while Bleeding Out in the Mortality State.
- Loses 1 point per turn until stabilized or permanently deceased at 0.
