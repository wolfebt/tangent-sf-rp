# Rest, Recovery & Biological Cycles System

**Category**: Core Survival & Resource Management Rules  

## Strategic Role & Resource Balancing
In-game features or abilities that require rest add a layer of strategy and resource management to gameplay. Players must balance using these abilities with resting to ensure they are available when needed. This can be particularly challenging in fast-paced games or during intense battles, where players may be tempted to overuse their abilities without considering the consequences.

The need for rest can have a significant impact on gameplay:
- Choosing between using a powerful ability in the heat of battle or saving it for later.
- Immersive roleplaying opportunities: finding safe places to rest (inns, campsites, starship quarters), taking time to relax, talking to NPCs, and learning new information.

---

## Full Rest Mechanics
- **Standard Sentient Species**: 6 to 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep restores 100% of maximum Vitality, fully restores 100% of the character's **Essence Pool**, resets daily limited-use features and abilities, clears the **Exhausted** condition, and resets the daily Light Rest counter to 0.

### Species Exceptions
1. **Synthetics, Fae, and Insect Species**:
   - Possess unique physiological attributes that enable them to function without traditional sleep.
   - Evolved to require minimal rest; a brief period of **Light Rest is sufficient for them to fully refresh and maintain their energy levels** (granting Full Rest benefits, including full Essence restoration).
2. **Alterians and Mondi**:
   - Do not sleep in the biological sense; engage in contemplative meditations throughout the day.
   - Considered **Light Rest**, entering deep contemplation and reflection to recharge their mental and physical well-being.

---

## Light Rest Mechanics
- **Daily Limit**: Can be performed **up to 4 times per day**.
- **Removal of Exhaustion**: Successfully completing any Light Rest tier removes the **Exhausted** condition.
- **Essence Recovery**: Recovers **Essence equal to the character's Key Ability Modifier (minimum 1)** per hour of downtime.

### The Burn (Overchanneling without Reserves)
If an operative's Essence Pool is empty, they may "burn" their own life force to power an invocation:
- Each point of Essence needed deals **2 points of direct Health damage** (not absorbed by Armor DR or Stamina).
- Represents the physical destruction of biological or synthetic tissue acting as an unshielded conduit.

### Activity Tiers & Required Downtime
1. **Nap or Meditation (1 Hour)**:
   - Most restful period; ideal for resetting traits or features.
   - Allowed Activity: Do nothing else but rest and relax.
2. **Lounging (2 Hours)**:
   - Less restful than a nap or meditation but still beneficial.
   - Allowed Activity: Casual observation, light recreation, and non-laborious activities.
3. **Light Duty (3 Hours)**:
   - Least restful but still counts as a rest period.
   - Allowed Activity: Light recreation, casual work, and minimal labor activities (gear check, sensor watch).

---

## Strenuous Interruption & Degradation Rules
Any activities more strenuous than light recreation/casual work count against rest (physical labor, intense exercise, combat, mentally demanding tasks).

**Degradation Progression**:
Each time a strenuous activity is performed, the rest category degrades by one stage:
$$\text{Nap (1h)} \longrightarrow \text{Lounging (2h)} \longrightarrow \text{Light Duty (3h)} \longrightarrow \text{Not Rested (Cancelled)}$$

If degraded, characters must extend their downtime to meet the new required duration. If degraded beyond Light Duty, the rest fails completely.

---

## Karma Economy Integration: Second Wind
- A character may spend **1 Karma Point** to enter 1 minute of internal focus to gain a **Second Wind**.
- **Second Wind replaces a Light Rest**, refreshing short-rest features and recovering Vitality without downtime.
