# Concussive & Impact Damage Rules

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics & Nature
Concussive and Impact damage primarily focuses on massive kinetic energy from impacts generally larger than the target's body (smashing, crashing, falling, explosive blast waves).
- **Whole-Body Dispersion**: Concussive damage is heavily traumatic but dispersed over the entire body.
- **Vitality / Health Split**: Damage can be **divided equally between Vitality and Health** if the character attempts to reduce the damage, regardless of whether the defensive attempt succeeds.

---

## Armor DR & Force Field Interactions
- **Crashing**: High-impact vehicle/object collisions. Half DR if Hard Armor (without Crash Suit option); Force Field DR is half effective.
- **Falling Damage**: Inflicted when falling from greater than character height. **Ignores DR of physical protection and force fields**.
- **Siege Weapons**: Catapults, rams, ballistae. Half DR if Hard Armor; Force Field DR is half effective.
- **Explosives**: Blast waves from grenades, mines, and artillery. Half DR of Hard Armor (without Blast Suit option); Force Field DR is half effective.
- **Gravity Weapons**: Handheld or vehicle gravitational crushers. **Ignores solid armor DR**; Force Field DR is half effective.
- **Weaponized Force Fields**: Repelling or crushing target-wide force fields. **Ignores DR of solid protection**; Force Field DR is fully effective.
