# Skill Synergy System

Skill Synergy rewards characters for applying multidisciplinary knowledge to a task.

## The Flat Bonus Model
When an auxiliary skill has a clear, logical connection to the primary check:
- **Trained Secondary (Rank 5 to 9)**: Grants a flat **+2 Synergy Bonus** to the primary check.
- **Expert Secondary (Rank 10+)**: Grants a flat **+4 Synergy Bonus** to the primary check.

### Example
A character attempting a Reason (Intellect) check to decipher an ancient inscription who possesses 6 ranks in **Language** adds a flat **+2 synergy bonus** to their check.
