# Movement & Action: Iterative Attacks & Terrain

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics
- **Iterative Attacks**: If a combatant moves more than a 5-foot step during their turn, they are restricted to **1 Attack Action** regardless of higher skill rank unlocks.
- **Difficult Terrain**: Movement speed is reduced to **x1/2**. Imposes a **-2 penalty to attacks** while in motion due to jarring footing (**-4 penalty** if movement is reduced to 1/4).
