# EDGE Tactical Advantage Modifiers Overview

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

**Edge** refers to tactical advantage modifiers that characters can gain or lose based on their movements, positions, and the environment during combat. These modifiers can significantly influence the outcome of attacks and defense.
