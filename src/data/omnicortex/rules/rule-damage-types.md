# Comprehensive Damage Types Directory

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

Tangent features a rich ecosystem of damage types, categorized into Kinetic, Energy, and Variative/Elemental profiles:

---

## 1. Kinetic Damage Types
- **Blunt (Crushing)**: Heavy impact trauma against armor plates and skeletal structures.
- **Slashing (Cutting)**: Edged lacerations causing deep wounds and the **Bleeding** status effect.
- **Piercing (Puncturing)**: Focused needle/bullet penetration targeting weak points and internal organs.
- **Force**: Kinetic subtype consisting of concentrated kinetic fields that **ignores 1/2 of Target Armor DR**.
- **Concussive / Impact**: Whole-body traumatic energy from crashes, falls, and explosions. Split equally between Vitality and Health; ignores or halves DR.
- **Bleed Damage**: Persistent status effect (1d4 damage/turn, ignores DR) inflicted by slashing/piercing crits.

---

## 2. Energy Damage Types
- **Pyro / Fire**: Intense heat setting targets ablaze with the **Burning** status effect (1d6/round + panic).
- **Cryo / Cold**: Endothermic freeze draining speed and inflicting the **Freeze** condition (Speed 0, vulnerable).
- **Spectral / Phase**: Incorporeal energy that **ignores physical Armor DR entirely** to disrupt life essence.
- **Sonic / Sound**: Harmonic acoustic resonance that **ignores most physical armor** and deafens foes.
- **Voltic / Electrical**: Electrostatic arcs stunning targets and overloading cyberware, robotics, and power conduits.
- **Corrosive / Disintegration**: Acidic chemical dissolution permanently degrading armor DR and melting flesh.
- **Ra-D / Radiation**: Ionizing gamma rays inducing radiation sickness, cell necrosis, and mutations.
- **Force Pulse / Disruption**: Destabilizes molecular bonds, crumbling targets and disintegrating matter.

---

## 3. Variative & Elemental Damage Types
- **Air Elemental**: Concussive atmospheric gales knocking foes **Prone** and disrupting flight.
- **Fire Elemental**: Primal volcanic flame scorching terrain and applying Burning.
- **Earth Elemental**: Massive lithic crushing weight and seismic tremors.
- **Water Elemental**: High-pressure hydraulic torrents causing concussive trauma and drowning.
- **Radiant / Light**: Pure solar light blinding evil/undead entities with the **Blinded** condition.
- **Umbral / Shadow**: Primal void darkness leeching vitality and inflicting Exhaustion.
- **Celestial**: Sacred transcendent power smiting fiends and undead with radiant fury.
- **Infernal**: Corrupting hellfire and Stygian damnation eroding mind and soul.
- **Dimensional**: Spacetime tearing and spatial shearing that completely bypasses Armor DR.
- **Psyche / Mental**: Direct psionic and neural trauma **ignoring physical armor entirely**; resisted by Willpower.
