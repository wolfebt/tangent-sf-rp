# How Scaling Works in Combat

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

- **Attack Rolls**: Size doesn't directly affect attack rolls, but larger creatures might have reach advantages or penalties depending on target size.
- **Damage**: Weapon dice are multiplied by the **Scaling Modifier**. No damage is reduced due to target size as target structure scales proportionally (e.g., Huge creature with x5 Scaling deals 5x base damage dice with 5x baseline construct structure).
- **Defense**: The **Combat Modifier** adjusts a creature's Defense score based on its size relative to its target.
- **Distance**: Speed and ranges are multiplied by the **Scaling Modifier**, allowing larger entities to cover more tactical ground.
