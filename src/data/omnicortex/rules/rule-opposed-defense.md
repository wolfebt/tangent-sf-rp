# Opposed Defense Resolution

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Opposed Roll Mechanics
When a defender is aware and actively fighting back:
\text{Defender's Agility} + \text{Defense Skill} + \text{Situational Mods} \quad \text{vs.} \quad \text{Attacker's Ability} + \text{Combat Skill} + \text{Bonuses}

- **Attacker Wins (Attacker > Defender)**: Strike or success.
- **Defender Wins (Defender >= Attacker)**: Attacker misses or unsuccessful action.
- > [!IMPORTANT]
  > **DEFENDER WINS ALL TIES.**
