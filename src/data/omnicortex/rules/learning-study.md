# Learning & Study Mechanics

Characters can acquire new skills and features through dedicated study periods between adventures.

## Study Periods
- **Improving Existing Skill**: **1 day per current rank** to attempt gaining the next rank point.
- **Learning New Skill**: **1d4+1 days** to attempt gaining Rank 1.
- **Learning New Feature**: **1 week** (with instructor) or **2+ weeks** (self-taught).

## Study Check Resolution
At the conclusion of the study period, make an **Easy (CR 10) Intellect check**:
- **Self-Taught**: Success grants **0.5 Skill Points** per period (1 point per 2 periods).
- **Learning from an Instructor (Trained 6+)**: Grants **+1 to the check per instructor skill tier** and yields **1.0 Skill Point** on success.
- **Study Aids**: High-quality manuals/holo-courses grant **+1 to +5** bonus to the check.
