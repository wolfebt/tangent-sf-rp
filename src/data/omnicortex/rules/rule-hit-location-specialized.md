# Hit Location: Specialized Appendages

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Targeting Modifiers & Trauma
For xenotypes, synthetics, and beasts with non-humanoid anatomy:
- **Full Limb Size**: **Called Shot -2**.
- **Half-Limb Size or Thin**: **Called Shot -3**.
- **Hand Size or Fibrous**: **Called Shot -4**.
