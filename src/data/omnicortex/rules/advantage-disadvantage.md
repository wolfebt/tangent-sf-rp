# Advantage & Disadvantage Mechanics

## Rolling with Advantage
- Roll **2d20** (or more with multiple sources) and select the **highest result**.
- **Stacking Advantage**: Each additional source of advantage adds an extra d20 to the pool.
- **Karma Point Trigger**: Spending 1 Karma Point grants Advantage on any single roll.

## Rolling with Disadvantage
- Roll **2d20** and select the **lowest result**.
- **Stacking Disadvantage**: Multiple disadvantage penalties add additional d20s, taking the lowest of all rolled.

## Mutual Cancellation
Advantage and Disadvantage cancel each other out on a 1-for-1 basis. If a roll has 1 source of Advantage and 1 source of Disadvantage, roll a single standard d20.
