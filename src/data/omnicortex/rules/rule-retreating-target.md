# Edge: Retreating Target

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics
Target quickly leaving the area forgoing any evasion or cover at Jog/Run/Sprint Speed.
- **Extra Attack**: Opponents gain an Extra Attack on the target at **-5 to Strike**.
- **Initiative Reroll**: Re-roll Initiative.
