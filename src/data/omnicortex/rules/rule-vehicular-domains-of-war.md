# Vehicular Combat: Domains of War (Land, Nautical, Aerial, Space)

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## 1. Land (Terrestrial & Walker)
Defined by **Terrain** and **Cover**:
- **Cover**: Vehicles use buildings and terrain for Cover (**+4 to +10 Defense Bonus**). A **Hull Down** position (only turret visible) grants **+4 Defense**.
- **Ramming**: A desperate physical collision maneuver.
  - *Attack*: Pilot makes a melee attack roll using Pilot Skill.
  - *Damage*: **1d10 per 10ft of Speed**. Both vehicles suffer this damage; the heavier vehicle takes half damage.
- **Walker Mechanics**: Bipedal and quadruped mecha can **Stomp** infantry (Standard Melee Attack) and step over obstacles that block wheeled/tracked armor.

---

## 2. Nautical (Surface & Submerged)
Defined by **Depth** and **Detection**:
- **Sonar / Sensors**: Visual range is often zero. Combat relies on *System Ops (Sensors)* checks to acquire a **Target Lock**. Without a Lock, all attacks are made at **Disadvantage**.
- **Depth Bands**:
  - *Surface*: Standard operational envelope.
  - *Periscope Depth*: Concealment against surface radar and optics.
  - *Deep*: High hydrostatic pressure. Hull breaches at this depth are immediately catastrophic.

---

## 3. Atmospheric (Aerial)
Defined by **Altitude** and **Energy Management**:
- **The Dogfight (Pilot vs. Pilot)**: When two aircraft engage, they enter an opposed Dogfight contest.
  - *Mechanic*: Opposed Pilot checks rolled at the start of each round.
  - *Winner*: Gains **Advantage** on attack rolls this round and chooses positioning (**Tail / Flank**).
  - *Loser*: Considered **Flat-Footed** against the winner.
- **Stalling**: Flying slower than the craft's Stall Speed forces a **Pilot Check (DC 20)**; failure causes the craft to enter a terminal fall.

---

## 4. Interstellar (Space)
Defined by **Vectors**, **Heat Signatures**, and **3D Distance**:
- **Newtonian Drift**: In zero-G, a vehicle maintains vector momentum unless thrust is applied. A pilot can cut engines to **drift silently (Stealth)** while retaining full velocity.
- **Heat Signatures**: Firing energy weapons or igniting main thrusters flares the craft's heat signature.
  - *Passive Sensors*: Automatically detect flared ships.
  - *Active Sensors (Ping)*: Required to establish a Target Lock on "Cold" drifting ships.
- **Proximity Damage**: Capital ship spinal weapons are so colossal that near-misses still inflict concussive kinetic shockwave damage to fighter-scale craft.
