# Limb Damage: Destroyed Threshold (2/3 Health)

**Category**: Core Combat Rules & Tables

## Mechanics
- **Threshold**: Taking **2/3rds of Health Score** in damage to an area damages it **beyond ANY further use** (Brain Death** if Head).
- **No Check**: There is **NO saving throw or Stamina check**; the limb is mangled and useless.
- **Traumatic Shock**: Traumatic Damage Control is immediately required for shock, blood loss, and vital collapse.
- **Brain Death Discretion**: Brain death is at the GM's discretion (as any instant kill). If the character survives, they remain permanently disabled without intense recovery, surgery, and downtime.
