## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every rule, background package, and sociological profile in **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** adheres to these canonical mechanical laws (`/game_rules/operator/` authority):

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Math:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`) equals `-10 to Attack/Check Score` + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Allocations (0 CP Baseline):** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` across **Faction (`20 SP`) + Origin (`20 SP`) + Occupation (`20 SP`)**, alongside **4 Recommended Features** (`-1 CP` discount: `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and **4 Background Traits** at `0 CP` (`2 Origin Traits` + `2 Occupation Traits`; **Extra Traits cost `1 CP` each**).
* **Standardized Faction Wealth & Lore Anchors:** The **Kovian Tribunal (Nocturne Aeld)** is standardized at **`+3 WS`** (`TL 3 / ML 4`), and the **Radiant Impyrium** includes the canonical **Psycholith** psycho-reactive Aetherite-ceramic mineral codex (`+5 WS`, `TL 4+ / ML 4`).
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct characters combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum `+10 CP` gained from personal Hindrances during creation; Species-level disadvantages and cultural Stigmas are exempt.

## **Traits**

### *Adaptability*

The ability to adapt to new environments and situations is important for a colony character, as they may encounter unfamiliar planets or face unexpected obstacles.

*\+2 to a specific Skill Check, may change assigned Skill daily*

### *Adventurous Spirit*

An adventurous spirit and a willingness to explore can be advantageous for a colony character who may need to venture out into the unknown.

*One Skill Check at Advantage per day*

### *Community Building*

Skills in community building and social dynamics are essential for a successful colony, so a colony character should have skills in community building and social dynamics.

*Once per Day may reroll a Social Skill Check*

### *Diplomacy*

The ability to negotiate and maintain diplomatic relations with other colonies or factions can be crucial for the colony's success.

*\+2 Diplomacy*

### *Leadership*

Strong leadership skills to guide and make decisions for the colony.

*\+2 Leadership*

### *Problem Solving*

Skilled at solving problems and overcoming challenges that arise in the colony, whether it's dealing with environmental hazards or resolving conflicts among colonists.

*Once per Day may reroll a Mental Skill Check*

### *Resource Management*

Adept at managing resources such as food, water, energy, and materials to ensure the colony's survival and growth.

*Wealth Checks for Basic Resources made at Advantage*

### *Scientific Knowledge*

Knowledge in fields such as biology, engineering, or agriculture can be valuable for a colony character to develop sustainable systems and technologies.

*\+2 to Science Knowledge*

### *Survival Skills*

Possess survival skills to navigate and explore unknown territories, and to protect the colony from potential threats.

*\+2 Survival Skill Checks*

## Consolidated & Modernized Origin Traits (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)

*(Players may select their 2 free Origin Traits from either the narrative traits above or the consolidated mechanical traits below.)*

* **Adaptability:** +2 to one chosen Skill Check (may reassign daily).
* **Community Spirit:** +1 additional bonus when using the Aid Another action.
* **Frontier Grit:** +1 to Fortitude and Willpower Saving Throws.
* **Jack-of-All-Trades:** May attempt Untrained (Rank 0) skill checks without the -2 unskilled penalty.
* **Jury-Rigging:** +2 to Vocation (Mechanic) checks for improvised or emergency repairs.
* **Resource Management:** Roll Wealth Checks for basic survival necessities and rations at Advantage.
* **Self-Sufficiency:** +1 to maximum Karma Pool (or +2 Knowledge: Survival).

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis (CRISP 11-Faction Matrix)**

## **03\. COLONY**

*Frontier, prefab, expansion, new beginnings.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Rigid rows of identical grey barracks. A central statue of the Emperor erected before housing is finished. Concrete walls, searchlights, red banners.  
* **STYLE:** Forward Command Post, Roman Camp.  
* **PALETTE:** Concrete Grey, Imperial Red, Dust Brown, Searchlight White.  
* **TOKENS:** Uniformity, walls, flags, statues, military order.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Identical, white-plastic modular pods stamped with QR codes. Branding everywhere. Clean, but sterile and soulless. Vending machines on every corner.  
* **STYLE:** Corporate Company Town, Prefab Suburbia.  
* **PALETTE:** Plastic White, Brand Colors (Cyan/Magenta), Asphalt Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Logos, clean streets, identical houses, surveillance cameras.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Stone and timber longhouses reinforced with starship plating. A central keep being built on high ground. Heraldic banners marking territory.  
* **STYLE:** Border Fiefdom, Medieval Colony.  
* **PALETTE:** Wood Brown, Stone Grey, Banner Colors, Mud.  
* **TOKENS:** Construction, fortifications, keeps, mud streets, armor.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** Tents, shipping containers, and crashed ship hulls welded together. Muddy streets, bonfires, diverse species mixing freely. Chaotic layout.  
* **STYLE:** Scrap-Town, Shantytown Frontier.  
* **PALETTE:** Rust, Canvas Tan, Container Red/Blue, Smoke.  
* **TOKENS:** Junk, wires, makeshift repairs, mud, campfires.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Hastily erected corrugated steel shacks. Bars and gambling dens are the biggest buildings. Neon signs buzzing over mud. Armed guards.  
* **STYLE:** Mining Boomtown, Wild West Neon.  
* **PALETTE:** Corrugated Silver, Mud Brown, Neon Pink, Rust.  
* **TOKENS:** Bars, casinos, mud, rain, weapons, poverty.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Fast-growing bio-domes that look like giant seed pods. Vines are guided to form walls. The colony looks like it is sprouting from the ground.  
* **STYLE:** Seedling Grove, Grown Colony.  
* **PALETTE:** Pod Green, Vine Brown, Flower colors, Earth.  
* **TOKENS:** Round shapes, growth, vines, nature integration.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** A single, beautiful white tower placed in the wilderness, with energy fields expanding outward to tame the land. Elegant tents of silk and force-fields.  
* **STYLE:** Expeditionary Spire, Elven Camp.  
* **PALETTE:** White, Gold, Silk colors, Forcefield Blue.  
* **TOKENS:** Spires, magic fields, elegance, nature preservation.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** High-tech, deployable yurts with solar skins. Community centers, open spaces, advanced 3D printers building homes.  
* **STYLE:** Mod-Hab Village, Mars Base.  
* **PALETTE:** Solar White, Orange (Martian soil), Sky Blue.  
* **TOKENS:** Domes, solar panels, rovers, 3D printers, clean.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** A central bazaar surrounded by temporary habs. Landing pads for merchant ships are the priority. Colorful flags, piles of trade goods.  
* **STYLE:** Trading Post, Silk Road Outpost.  
* **PALETTE:** Sand, Market Brights, Tent Canvas, Trade Goods.  
* **TOKENS:** Cargo crates, ships, stalls, flags, dust.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Self-replicating hexagonal tiles consuming the landscape. Drones printing structures layer by layer. No residential comfort, just function.  
* **STYLE:** Expansion Node, Fractal Construction.  
* **PALETTE:** Construction Yellow, Grey, Laser Red.  
* **TOKENS:** Hexagons, printers, drones, lasers, no humans.

### **KITIN COLLECTIVE (THE STAR HIVE)**

* **CONTEXT:** A Stage 1–3 Hive Genesis site where an orbital spore-cyst has anchored into raw planetary crust. Murex excavation teams secrete glossy obsidian Hive-Resin foundations around a bubbling emerald Genomatrix pool.
* **STYLE:** Biomorphic Terraforming, Hive Genesis Outpost.
* **PALETTE:** Glossy Obsidian, Raw Earth Ochre, Proto-Flux Green, Cyan Synaptic Glow.
* **TOKENS:** Spore-drop craters, resin secretion arches, gestation pods, root-tendril drills, telepathic synchronization.
