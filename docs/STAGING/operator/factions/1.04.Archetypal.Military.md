MILITARY
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Standing armies and stratocracies characterized by a total focus on military power. Society is highly disciplined, hierarchical, and patriotic, geared toward conquering new territories, maintaining absolute order, and defending borders with overwhelming force.
* Key Themes: Discipline, Stratocracy, Heavy Armor, The War Machine.
* Relationship to Others: Imperialistic or highly defensive. Often view civilian governments as weak.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Armed Republic / The [Name] Empire
* Colloquialisms: The Army / The Boot
* Archetype: Standing Armies
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Heavily fortified garrison worlds)
* Driving Mandate: Conquer new territories, defend borders, maintain order.
* Motto: "Peace through superior firepower."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Patriotic, disciplined, loyal, hierarchical.
* Social Structure: Strict chain of command (Civilians often treated as second-class to Citizens/Soldiers).
* View on Outsiders: Potential threats to be monitored or conquered.
* Law & Order: Martial Law and Military Tribunals.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3
* Strengths: Well-trained soldiers, advanced weaponry, strong leadership, logistical supremacy.
* Weaknesses: Rigid thinking, lack of creativity, inflexibility in strategy, vulnerable to asymmetric/guerrilla warfare.
* Military Doctrine: Overwhelming force, rigid formations, massive artillery support.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Highly regimented species).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Combat (+5)
   * Vocation (Soldier) (+5)
   * Athletics (+4)
   * Tactics (+3)
   * Discipline (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Infantryman: The disciplined backbone of the grand army.
   * The Officer: Leading platoons and executing grand strategy.
   * The Special Forces Operative: Handling highly classified, deep-strike missions.
   * The Combat Engineer: Building bridges, clearing mines, and fortifying positions.
   * The Field Medic: Keeping the line holding by healing traumatic injuries.
   * The Pilot: Dominating the skies with close air support.
   * The Artillery Specialist: Raining devastating orbital or surface fire on coordinates.
   * The Drill Sergeant: Forging raw recruits into perfect weapons of the state.
   * The Quartermaster: Managing the massive logistical tail required for war.
   * The Recon Scout: Operating ahead of the main force to identify enemy positions.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Coordinated Assist: Operating flawlessly as part of a highly trained squad.
   * Defensive Combat Training: Holding the line against overwhelming charges.
   * Weapon Specialization: Intensive drill training with standard-issue rifles.
   * Iron Will: Unshakable patriotism and discipline in the face of terror.
   * Tough: Conditioned to endure immense physical punishment.
   * Combat Reflexes: Reacting with lethal efficiency to sudden threats.
   * Born Leader: Inspiring troops to push past their absolute breaking points.
   * Endurance: Surviving grueling forced marches with heavy packs.
   * Discipline Focus: Maintaining absolute focus despite explosions and chaos.
   * Marching Cadence: Moving efficiently and silently as a cohesive unit.
