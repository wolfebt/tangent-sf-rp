# 1.06. OCCUPATIONS & PROFESSIONAL CAREERS

* **Focus Statement:** The master career training, professional skill, and occupational trait reference for **Tangent SF RP**, governing the 20-SP Professional Skill pool (`Rank 11` creation cap, `Rank 6` recommended), Recommended Feature categories (`-1 CP` discount), Occupational Traits (`2 Free at 0 CP`, `+1 CP` per extra trait), EUFT Base Wealth Scores (`Base WS 1` to `Base WS 6`), and the complete 13-Occupation CRISP Character Visual Matrix.

## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every rule, background package, and sociological profile in **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** adheres to these canonical mechanical laws (`/game_rules/operator/` authority):

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Math:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`) equals `-10 to Attack/Check Score` + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Allocations (0 CP Baseline):** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` across **Faction (`20 SP`) + Origin (`20 SP`) + Occupation (`20 SP`)**, alongside **4 Recommended Features** (`-1 CP` discount: `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and **4 Background Traits** at `0 CP` (`2 Origin Traits` + `2 Occupation Traits`; **Extra Traits cost `1 CP` each**).
* **Standardized Faction Wealth & Lore Anchors:** The **Kovian Tribunal (Nocturne Aeld)** is standardized at **`+3 WS`** (`TL 3 / ML 4`), and the **Radiant Impyrium** includes the canonical **Psycholith** psycho-reactive Aetherite-ceramic mineral codex (`+5 WS`, `TL 4+ / ML 4`).
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct characters combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum `+10 CP` gained from personal Hindrances during creation; Species-level disadvantages and cultural Stigmas are exempt.

# 1.06 OCCUPATIONS

Occupations in Tangent represent a character's formal training, professional history, career network, and operational role in society.

## Mechanical Benefits of Occupation Selection

1. **Professional Skills (20 SP Pool):** Every character receives **20 Skill Points (SP)** to distribute among the skills listed under their chosen Occupation's Professional Skills list at **0 CP** (part of the 60-Rank Background Allocation across Faction + Origin + Occupation).
   * **The Creation Cap:** No skill may be raised above **Rank 11 (Expert)** using this pool during character creation.
   * **Recommended Creation Limit:** It is highly recommended that starting skills do not exceed **Rank 6 (Trained/Professional)** to ensure a balanced, versatile character sheet. A player may only raise a skill up to Rank 11 if dedication to a single, specialized discipline is a core pillar of the character's backstory and concept.
   * **Cost Note:** `1 SP = 1 Skill Rank`.
2. **Foundational & Recommended Features (-1 CP Discount):** Grants 2 foundational Features (as part of the 60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits background baseline) and unlocks a **1 CP discount** (`2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) when purchasing Features from the Occupation's Recommended Feature Category during character creation or progression.
3. **Occupational Traits (Select 2 Free):** Players choose **2 Occupational Traits** from their Occupation's trait list at **0 CP**. Additional traits may be purchased for **1 CP each** during character creation or via Experience progression (Origin and Occupation traits are interchangeable if appropriate to the character concept).
4. **Occupation Wealth Base (Base WS 1 to 6):** Establishes the character's foundational Wealth Score before Origin, Faction, Tech Level, and Vocation stage modifiers under the Tangent Economic Unified Field Theory (`02 ECONOMY AND WEALTH.md`):
   * **Representative:** Base WS 6 (Apex political/diplomatic standing)
   * **Merchant:** Base WS 5 (Commercial enterprise & capital leverage)
   * **Entertainer:** Base WS 5 (Celebrity patronage & public bookings)
   * **Adept:** Base WS 4 (Metaphysical expertise & arcane consulting)
   * **Criminal:** Base WS 4 (Underworld liquidity & black-market fencing)
   * **Builder:** Base WS 3 (Technical trades & manufacturing contracts)
   * **Scholar:** Base WS 3 (Academic stipends & institutional grants)
   * **Specialist:** Base WS 3 (Contract engineering & technical commissions)
   * **Agent:** Base WS 2 (Operational expense accounts & field stipends)
   * **Citizen:** Base WS 2 (Standard municipal wage & steady employment)
   * **Drifter:** Base WS 1 (Transient gig economy & day labor)
   * **Scout:** Base WS 1 (Frontier surveying & expedition bounties)
   * **Soldier:** Base WS 1 (Military standard-issue ration & field pay)

## Common Traits Available to All Occupations

### *Background*
The character may select additional training from another Occupation fitting the character's backstory. This does not grant any additional Professional Skill points or Recommended Features from the other Profession. May be taken multiple times, granting a different Trait in the chosen Background Occupation; traits may now be chosen from both the primary and background Occupation.
* **Benefit:** *Gain one Trait from a SECONDARY Chosen Occupation.*

### *Trade Tools*
Helps cover general gear the character would have for their Occupation. The character's additional equipment will depend on their specific job within their profession.
* **Benefit:** *+2 Equipment.*

### *High Pay*
Benefit for doing their job well and commanding superior compensation.
* **Benefit:** *+2 Wealth.*

### *Professionalism*
Benefit of being widely known to do their job with excellence and reliability.
* **Benefit:** *+2 Reputation.*

II. CONSOLIDATED OCCUPATIONAL TRAITS CATALOG (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)
Universal Occupational Traits (Available Across Multiple Occupations)
* Adaptability: +2 to one chosen Skill Check (may reassign the chosen skill daily after a Full Rest).
* Attention to Detail: +2 to Alertness checks (or rolls Mastercrafting Checks at Advantage for Builder).
* Combat Training: +2 to one Combat Skill of choice.
* Curiosity / Adventurous Spirit: Regain 1 spent Karma Point on the first encounter with a novel creature, location, or technology each session.
* Loyalty / Freedom: Roll Saving Throws against Domination and Mind-Control at Advantage.
* Networking: Roll Gather Information (Streetwise / Diplomacy) checks at Advantage.
* Problem-Solving: Once per day, reroll a failed Mental Skill Check (or +2 to Reason/Logic checks for Builder).
* Resourcefulness: Once per day, roll any one Skill Check of choice at Advantage.
* Stalker: May Take 10 on Stealth Checks even under pressure.
* Teamwork / Collaboration / Team-Oriented: Grants an additional +1 bonus when using the Aid Another action.
* Time Management: Reduces Rushed time penalties on Skill Checks by 2.
* Versatility / Guile / Critical Thinking / Risk-Taking: Permanently increases maximum Karma Pool by +1.
Specialized Occupation-Specific Traits
