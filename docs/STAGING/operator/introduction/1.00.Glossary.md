# 1.00.01. CORE SYSTEM GLOSSARY

* **Focus Statement:** The canonical alphabetical and domain-indexed terminology reference for the **TANGENT Science Fantasy Role-Playing Engine (Tangent SF RP)**, defining all core resolution metrics, Universal Displacement Units (UDU), combat integrity pools, metaphysical disciplines, and Economic Unified Field Theory (EUFT) equations.

## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every term defined in this glossary governs **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** (`operator/`) and **Volume II: The Architect's Matrix** (`architect/`) under these strict mathematical invariants:

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Math:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (e.g., `20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`) converts the rolled dice into a flat `-10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`15 CP` Primary Attribute at `+3`, `10 CP` Secondary Attribute at `+2`, `24 CP` for 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6, `18 CP` for 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3, and `13 CP` for 2 Signature Traits at `1 CP` each plus 3 Recommended Core Features at `9 CP`) and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Skill Ranks:** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` as `20 SP Faction` + `20 SP Origin` + `20 SP Occupation`, alongside `4 Recommended Features` (`-1 CP` discount) and `4 Traits` at `0 CP` (`Extra Traits = 1 CP` each).
* **Universal Straight Pricing:** `+5 CP` per `+1` Attribute, `-5 CP` refund per `-1` Attribute penalty, and `1 CP` per `1` Skill Rank (`5 CP` per `5` Skill Ranks) across all creation, species, companion, and bestiary modules.
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools:** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct entities combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. Pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Standard personal character hindrances have a `+10 CP` maximum limit; **Species-level hindrances and cybernetic Stigmas do NOT count toward the 10-point standard hindrance limit**.

## Part II — Complete Unabridged Profiles & Rules Entries

### I. Core Metrics (The Math)

* *Every action in the Tangent universe is adjudicated through a unified bell-curve engine where training and attributes shift the center of probability against a deterministic Challenge Rating.*
* *"Do not ask the Arbiter for mercy; ask what the Challenge Rating is and count your ranks."* — **Vanguard Tactical Primer**

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Range / Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Roll** | Resolution | The universal resolution roll for skill checks, attacks, and active saves. | `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Mod + Modifiers vs. CR` |
| **Advantage** | Resolution | Roll an extra `+1d10` per stack of Advantage and keep the **highest 2 dice**. | Cancels 1-for-1 with Disadvantage |
| **Disadvantage** | Resolution | Roll an extra `+1d10` per stack of Disadvantage and keep the **lowest 2 dice**. | Cancels 1-for-1 with Advantage |
| **Critical Hit** | Resolution | **Natural 20** (double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`). | `Rolled Dice + 10` (e.g., `20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Critical Effect` |
| **Fumble** | Resolution | **Natural 2** (double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`). | `Rolled Dice becomes flat -10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication` |
| **Rank** | Skills | The numerical value assigned to a Skill, representing formal training and practice. | `0` (Untrained) to `30+` (Pinnacle) |
| **Score** | Attributes | The raw modifier of one of the 6 Primary Attributes (`STR`, `AGI`, `STA`, `INT`, `WIS`, `CHA`). | `-5` to `+10` (Caps vary by Species/Tier; `+5 CP` per `+1`) |
| **CP** | Creation | **Character Points**. The universal currency used for Character Creation. | Standard Starting Budget: **`150 CP`** (`80 CP` Chassis + `70 CP` Custom) |
| **AP / XP** | Progression | **Award Points / Experience Points**. Post-creation advancement points spent 1-for-1 like CP. | Tier 1: `150–199 CP`, Tier 2: `200–299 CP`, Tier 3: `300–449 CP`, Tier 4: `450+ CP` |
| **CR** | Mechanics | **Challenge Rating**. The target difficulty number a `2d10` check must meet or exceed. | `CR 0` (Scrap/Simple) to `CR 50+` (Transcendent) |
| **Active Save** | Defense | Dynamic saving throw rolled against hazards, toxins, or invocations. | `2d10 + 2 + (2 * Attribute) + Feature Bonus` |
| **Passive Save CR** | Defense | Static threshold an attacker's check must meet when targeting a character passively. | `12 + (2 * Attribute) + Feature Bonus` |
| **Karma (KP)** | Resources | Meta-currency pool used to roll with Advantage, seize initiative, or trigger Karma Features. | Base `3 KP` (Refreshes per Session; `1 CP = +1 Max KP`) |
| **Tier** | Scale | Broad category of power or complexity for Characters, Threat Templates, and Infrastructure. | Tier 0 (Civilian) to Tier 5 (Cosmic / Post-Scarcity) |

### II. Physics & Capacity (The UDU System)

* *The Universal Displacement Unit (UDU) locks sub-dermal bioware, personal sidearms, mechanized walkers, and orbital habitats into a single logarithmic 10:1 capacity scale.*
* *"If it has mass and volume, it has a displacement tier."* — **Entari Combine Engineering Standard 10.1**

* **Canonical UDU Ratio:** `1 Module (10 Tons) = 10 Mounts (1,000 kg) = 100 Sockets (100 kg) = 1,000 Usable Nodes (10 kg)`

| Term | Domain | Definition | Mass & Capacity Equivalence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **UDU** | Physics | **Universal Displacement Unit**. The master logarithmic hierarchy governing mass, volume, and slot capacity across augmentations, personal gear, mecha, and architecture. | `10 Nodes = 1 Socket` \| `10 Sockets = 1 Mount` \| `10 Mounts = 1 Module` |
| **Node (Tier 0)** | Augmentations | Microscale installation slot for biological, cybernetic, and nanofiber augmentations. | `1 Usable Node (< 10 g) = 0.1 Socket` (`10 Nodes = 1 Socket`) |
| **Socket (Tier 1)** | Equipment | Personal-scale customization slot for weapons, armor suits, and portable drones. | `1 Socket (< 1 kg) = 10 Usable Nodes` |
| **Mount (Tier 2)** | Mecha & Vehicles | Vehicular/Mecha hardpoint slot for heavy weapons, engines, and tactical subsystems. | `1 Mount (< 100 kg) = 10 Sockets` *(Garaging: `1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier`)* |
| **Module (Tier 3)** | Architecture | Macroscale pre-fabricated room, starship bay, or facility block (`20x20 ft`). | `1 Module (< 10 Tons) = 10 Mounts = 100 Sockets` |

### III. Survival & Combat Integrity

* *Combat integrity separates glancing exhaustion from lethal structural trauma, applying single-domain Armor DR and Stamina Base Toughness to every incoming strike.*
* *"Armor stops the shrapnel. Stamina keeps your ribs from caving in. Vitality is just how many times you can duck before both are tested."* — **Drop Marine Field Manual**

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Formula / Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vitality** | Integrity | Physical stamina, luck, and glancing-blow avoidance. Absorbs initial/non-lethal damage before Health. | **Base `30`**; increased strictly by **`+5 per 1 CP`** (Suggested Max: `60`). **Never scaled by Stamina.** |
| **Health** | Integrity | Biological structural integrity and life force. Damage here represents physical wounds and trauma. | **Base `30`**; increased strictly by **`+5 per 1 CP`** (Suggested Max: `60`). **Never scaled by Stamina.** |
| **Structure (SP)** | Integrity | Combined integrity pool (`Vitality + Health`) for Synthetics, Constructs, Vehicles, Mecha, and Buildings. | **Base `60 SP`** for Synthetics (`+5 SP per 1 CP`; Suggested Max: `120 SP`). |
| **Base Toughness** | Mitigation | Point-for-point damage reduction granted by a character's **Stamina (STA) Modifier**. | Subtracts `STA Mod` from incoming damage *after* Armor DR. |
| **DR** | Mitigation | **Damage Resistance / Reduction**. Flat value subtracted from incoming damage before Base Toughness and Vitality. | `0` (Clothing) to `60+` (Capital Ship). **Armor Layering Cap:** Only the single highest DR across bio, cyber, and worn armor applies. |
| **Penetration** | Mitigation | Weapon armor-piercing rating that reduces the target's effective Armor DR against that attack. | `Effective DR = max(0, Highest Single Domain DR - Weapon Penetration)` |
| **Precision Strike** | Combat | Feature-gated Standard Action strike consolidation chain (`1.08.CombatFeatures.md`). | `Precision Strike` (`Skill 6`) = `2x` Base Weapon Dice; `Improved` (`Skill 11`) = `3x`; `Greater` (`Skill 16`) = `4x`. |
| **Defense** | Combat | Static target number an attacker's `2d10` Strike roll must meet or exceed to hit. | `10 + Agility + Defense Skill Rank + Shield/Size/Cover Modifiers` |
| **Mortality State** | Survival | Triggered at `0 Health`. Character gains **Stability Points = `Stamina Score + 5`** and loses `1` per turn until stabilized (`CR 15 Medicine` or `CR 15 Fortitude Save`). | Death occurs at `0 Stability Points` or `-Stamina Score` Health; revivification inflicts `-5 XP Debt` and drains Karma to `0`. |
| **Stigma** | Social | Social prejudice and Alien Mind Factor penalty resulting from species, synthetic, or cultural bias. | `-1` (Minor), `-2` (Typical), `-4` to `-8` (Severe/Monstrous) to Social checks with biased groups (exempt from `+10 CP` personal hindrance cap). |

### IV. Metaphysics & Technology

* *Whether forged on an industrial nanoforge or woven from the raw threads of the Resonance, advanced capabilities obey strict tier and focus thresholds.*
* *"At sufficiently high Challenge Ratings, engineering and invocation become indistinguishable."* — **Alterian Technomancer**

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Range / Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TL** | Technology | **Technology Level**. The sophistication of scientific engineering and manufacturing. | `TL 0` (Stone/Primitive) to `TL 5` (Polymatter / Post-Scarcity) |
| **ML** | Metaphysics | **Metafocus Level**. The societal integration and sophistication of Resonance manipulation. | `ML 0` (Unawakened/Null) to `ML 6` (Deific / Progenitor) |
| **Attune** | Metaphysics | The universal conduit skill for channeling Resonance energy. Sets target Save CRs and attack accuracy. | Active Check = `2d10 + Attune Rank + Key Attribute Mod` (`Passive CR = 10 + Attune + Key Mod`) |
| **Discipline** | Metaphysics | One of the **6 Primary Metaphysic Schools** (`Dimension`, `Energy`, `Entropy`, `Illusion`, `Matter`, `Mental`), each split into **2 Focus Skills** (`12 Foci` total). | Determines Range, Area, Duration, and Base Stage Dice (`+1d6` to `+4d6`). |
| **Invocation** | Metaphysics | A specific spell, psionic power, or technique learned within a Discipline (`1 CP` per Rank). | Adds `+1 Effect Die` per Invocation Rank to Base Discipline Stage Dice. |
| **Essence** | Metaphysics | Internal bio-spiritual energy reserve used to empower high-strain invocations or Meta-Tech. | `Essence Pool = Sum of All 6 Attribute Scores (STR + AGI + STA + INT + WIS + CHA) + Attune Rank` |

### V. Economy & Wealth (EUFT)

* *The Economic Unified Field Theory binds abstract character status, liquid Credits, salvage mass, and industrial fabrication speed to a single exponential curve.*
* *"Complexity sets the price; infrastructure sets the schedule."* — **Syndicate Commerce Code**

| Term | Domain | Definition | Canonical Formula / Ratio |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wealth (WS)** | Economy | **Wealth Score**. Abstract rating of financial leverage, credit access, and auto-buy threshold (`WS 0` to `WS 80+`). | **Golden Rule:** Auto-buy any personal item where `Item CR <= Character WS`. |
| **Credits (Cr)** | Economy | Liquid currency used to pay the **Liquidity Gap** when purchasing items where `Item CR > WS`. | `Liquidity Gap = Value(Item CR) - Value(Character WS)` |
| **MCr** | Economy | **Mega-Credit**. Macro-economic unit used for starships, orbital stations, and planetary trade. | `1 MCr = 1,000,000 Credits` |
| **Crafting CR** | Economy | The "Prime Mover" of item valuation (`Purchase CR = Crafting CR`). | Base intervals of 5: `Value (Cr) = 10 * 4 ^ (CR / 5)` |
| **Micro-Modifiers** | Economy | Linear multipliers applied to the nearest base interval of 5 for intermediate CRs (`+1` to `+4` and `-1` Flaw). | `+1 CR = x1.30` \| `+2 CR = x1.75` \| `+3 CR = x2.30` \| `+4 CR = x3.00` \| `-1 CR Flaw = x0.75` |
| **Material Ratio** | Crafting | Raw material cost required to initiate fabrication via the Productivity Engine. | **Pristine Materials:** `50%` of Credit Value \| **Scavenged Scrap:** `10%` value (requires `5x` Item Credit Value in raw scrap mass) |
| **Productivity Points (PP)** | Crafting | Unit of fabrication progress accumulated daily toward `Target PP = Item Credit Value`. | `Daily PP = (Craft Check Result - 10) * Tool Tier Multiplier * Crew Size` |
| **Fence Rate** | Economy | Liquidity Drag applied when reselling goods on the open or black market. | `Legal Goods = 50% Value` \| `Black Market / Stolen = 20%–25% Value` \| `Scrap = 10% Value` |
