# **Dimension Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Dimension Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Dimension: Summoning or Dimension: Teleport) for shaping, duration, and effect resolution.

  
  

## **SUMMONING INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Dimension

Specialization: Summoning

Description: The ability to call forth creatures, objects, or materials from other locations or planes of existence, as well as manipulating the local dimensional fabric to restrain or alter time.

  

### **CONJURE SUSTENANCE**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | [Minor] [Creation]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 10
  - **Time:** 1 Minute
  - **Range:** Close
  - **Area:** Single Meal
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Creates a simple drink or snack sufficient for one person. The food is nutritious but bland unless the caster has culinary skills.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Quantity:** Number of meals increases by 1 per Stage.

  

### **SUMMON CREATURE**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility/Combat | [Conjuration] [Minion]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 15
  - **Time:** Full Round Action
  - **Range:** Close
  - **Area:** Single Point
  - **Duration:** 1 Round per Rank (Stage). *Spend 1 Karma Point to increase to 1 Minute per Rank.*
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Calls a known existing creature from another plane or location to serve the caster as a temporary companion.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Power:** Total Creature Character Points (CP) = 10 CP per Stage achieved.
  - **Quantity:** Can split the total CP budget among multiple creatures.

  

### **CREATE OBJECT**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | [Creation] [Temporary]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 15
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Close
  - **Area:** Single Object
  - **Duration:** 1 Round per Rank (Stage). *Spend 1 Karma Point to increase to 1 Minute per Rank.*
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Manifests equipment, gear, or materials from raw dimensional matter or retrieves them from a storage cache.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Value:** Maximum Item Wealth Rating Equivalent = 5 Wealth per Stage achieved.
  - **Complexity:** Tech Level limitations may apply based on caster's knowledge.

  

### **FIND FAMILIAR**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Utility | [Ritual] [Bond]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 20 (Ritual)
  - **Time:** 1 Hour
  - **Range:** Touch
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Permanent (until dismissed/killed)
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Summons a minor spirit or creature to serve as a permanent magical companion.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Bond:** Familiar gains 10 CP per Stage (aligning with Summoning core rules).
  - **Link:** Telepathic range increases by 1 mile per Stage.

  

### **WEB**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Crowd Control | [Restraint] [Hazard]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 12
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Medium
  - **Area:** 20ft Radius
  - **Duration:** Scene
  - **Resistance:** Reflex (Negates) / Strength (Break)

Effect Description:

Fills an area with sticky, dimensionally-anchored strands.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Severity:** Escape/Break CR = Base CR + (1 per Stage).
  - **Integrity:** Strands have 5 HP per Stage.

  

### **BANISHMENT**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Attack/Defense | [Displacement] [Planar]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 20
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Close
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Effect Description:

Forces a creature back to its home dimension or shunts it to a pocket dimension.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Power:** One Target per Stage
  - **Duration:** If temporary shunt, duration = 1 Round per Stage.

  

### **SPATIAL CONTROL**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Environmental | [Gravity] [Warping]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 18
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Medium
  - **Area:** 30ft Radius
  - **Duration:** Concentration
  - **Resistance:** Reflex or Fortitude (varies by effect)

Effect Description:

Manipulates spatial dimensions (gravity, orientation) within an area.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Severity:** Gravity Penalty/Bonus = 2 point modifier per Stage.
  - **Area:** Radius increases by +10ft per Stage.

  

### **HASTE**

Classification: Dimension (Summoning) | Buff | [Temporal] [Speed]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 20
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Touch
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Rounds/Level
  - **Resistance:** Fortitude (Harmless)

Effect Description:

Manipulates the temporal flow around a target, speeding them up.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Magnitude:** Speed Bonus = +10ft per Stage.
  - **Defense:** Dodge Bonus to AC = +1 per Stage.

  
  

## **TELEPORT INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Dimension

Specialization: Teleport

Description: The mastery of spatial transit, moving instantly from one point to another without crossing the intervening space.

  

### **MEASURE**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Utility | [Cantrip] [Analysis]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 5
  - **Time:** Swift Action
  - **Range:** Sight
  - **Area:** Single Object/Area
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Instantly determines the precise dimensions of an area or object, including distance, height, width, and length.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Precision:** Accuracy and range of measurement increase per Stage.

  

### **VANISH OBJECTS**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Utility | [Minor] [Sleight]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 10
  - **Time:** Free Action (At Will)
  - **Range:** Touch/Personal
  - **Area:** Single handheld object
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

**Effect Description:** Teleports a small object (keys, coin, dagger) about the caster's body or into another position/pocket/possession the caster is touching, instantly. Grants a bonus to Sleight of Hand to hide items equal to the Teleport Skill, and gone.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Mass:** Maximum object size increases per Stage (Fine -> Diminutive -> Tiny).

  

### **TELEPORT**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Movement | [Long Range] [Transport]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** Variable (See Below)
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Touch (Self + Others)
  - **Area:** Personal + Passengers
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** Will (if unwilling)

Effect Description:

Relocation of position without the subject traveling between the points of space. Difficulty is based on familiarity of target area after all modifiers.

**Familiarity Difficulty Table:**

  - **Very Familiar** (or clearly visible): CR 5
  - **Well Known** (or seen recently): CR 10
  - **Visited** (or seen once): CR 15
  - **Known** (heard or seen a poor image of): CR 20
  - **Unknown**: CR 25

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Capacity:** Passengers carried = 1 per Stage.
  - **Safety:** Reduces Familiarity CRs by 2 per Stage.

  

### **LONG STEP**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Movement | [Personal] [Short Range]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 12
  - **Time:** Move Action
  - **Range:** Self
  - **Area:** Personal
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Teleports the caster a short distance within line of sight.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Range:** Distance = 30ft + (10ft per Stage).

  

### **PLANE SHIFT**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Movement | [Planar] [Travel]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 25
  - **Time:** Full Round Action
  - **Range:** Touch
  - **Area:** Personal + Group
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** Will (if unwilling)

Effect Description:

Transports the caster and allies to a different plane of existence.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Accuracy:** Arrival error distance reduces by 10% per Stage.
  - **Capacity:** Passengers = 1 per Stage.

  

### **ETHEREALNESS**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Defense/Utility | [Phase] [State Change]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 20
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Personal
  - **Area:** Self
  - **Duration:** Rounds/Level
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Shifts the caster's body partially out of phase, becoming invisible and incorporeal.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Duration:** 1 Round + (1 Round per Stage).
  - **Utility:** Can move through solid walls (5ft thick per Stage).

  

### **DEMIPLANE**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Utility | [Creation] [Sanctuary]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 30
  - **Time:** Ritual (1 Hour)
  - **Range:** Close
  - **Area:** Doorway
  - **Duration:** Permanent (Plane exists forever, door is temporary)
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Creates a small, finite pocket dimension tailored to specifications.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Area:** Plane Volume = 10ft Cubed Side per Stage (10x10, 20x20, 30x30, etc).
  - **Complexity:** Environmental control (Air/Temp) quality scales with Stage.

  

### **GATE**

Classification: Dimension (Teleport) | Utility/Summon | [Portal] [Two-Way]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 25
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Medium
  - **Area:** 10ft to 20ft Ring
  - **Duration:** Concentration
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Opens a stable, two-way wormhole connecting to another location.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Stability:** Duration without concentration = 1 Round per Stage.
  - **Size:** Portal diameter increases by +5ft per Stage.
