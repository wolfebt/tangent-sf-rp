# **Entropy Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Entropy Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Entropy: Chaos or Entropy: Order) for shaping, intensity, and effect resolution.

  
  

## **CHAOS INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Entropy

Specialization: Chaos

Description: Amplification of entropic forces and damaging to most everything. Utilizes Negative Energy.

Standard Range: Short.

Base Damage: 2d6 per Stage (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle) + 1d per Level of Invocation used.

Effect: Most armors are ignored or harmed by Entropic Effects.

  

### **CULL**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | [Minor] [Negative Energy]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 10
  - **Time:** Standard Action (At Will)
  - **Range:** Touch
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Causes 1 point of negative energy damage. If the target is near death (0 Vitality or wounded), it hastens their demise.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Severity:** Increases to 1d4 damage at higher Stages against dying targets.

  

### **TOUCH OF DEATH**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | [Necrotic] [Lethal]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 25
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Touch
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** Fortitude (Partial/Negates Death)

Effect Description:

Channels pure entropic energy into a living being, attempting to instantly sever their life force.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Damage:** 2d6 Negative Energy Damage per Stage.
  - **Lethality:** Death Threshold (HP) = 10 x Stage. If target HP is below threshold, save or die.

  

### **WOUND**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Attack | [Necrotic] [Bio-Degradation]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 12
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Short
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** Fortitude (Half)

Effect Description:

Accelerates the decay of cells and tissue, inflicting open wounds.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Damage:** 2d6 Negative Energy Damage per Stage.
  - **Debuff:** Inflicts Bleeding (1 dmg/round) per Stage.

  

### **FEAR**

Classification: Entropy (Chaos) | Debuff | [Psychic] [Emotion]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 15
  - **Time:** Standard Action
  - **Range:** Short
  - **Area:** Single Creature
  - **Duration:** Rounds/Level
  - **Resistance:** Will Save (Negates)

Induces a primal, irrational terror in a creature.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Severity:** Stage 1 (Shaken) -> Stage 3 (Frightened) -> Stage 5 (Panicked).
  - **Duration:** 1 Round per Stage.

  

### **CURSE**

## **ORDER INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Entropy

Specialization: Order

Description: Manipulation and repairing patterns of most anything. Utilizes Positive Energy.

Standard Range: Short.

Base Healing: 2d6 healed per Stage (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle) + 1d per Level of Invocation used.

Note: If dealing with Undead or Negative Energy creatures, inverse the Damage/Heal effects.

  

### **VITAL SIGHT**

Classification: Entropy (Order) | Sensory | [Cantrip] [Diagnosis]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 5
  - **Time:** Swift Action
  - **Range:** Sight
  - **Area:** Single Subject
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Detects the presence and general location of 'living' subjects. Senses current physical condition and any type of illness, damage, injury, ailment, or allergy.

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Precision:** Detail of diagnosis increases per Stage (Presence -> Condition -> Exact HP/Disease Strain).
