# **00.00 ARCHITECT GUIDELINES & ADJUDICATION PROTOCOL**

## **MECHANICAL AUTHORITY & CANONICAL RULES BLOCK**

- **Core Resolution Engine:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`. Critical Hit (Natural 20 / double-10s) = `Rolled Dice + 10 (20 -> 30) + 2x Base Weapon Damage Dice + Critical Effect`. Fumble (Natural 2 / double-1s) = `Flat -10 + Complication`. Advantage/Disadvantage rolls `+1d10` per stack and keeps the highest or lowest 2 dice.
- **Character Point (CP) Economy & 80-CP Archetype Chassis:** Starting Operator budget is **150 CP**, partitioned into the **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`15 CP` Primary Attribute at `+3`, `10 CP` Secondary Attribute at `+2`, `24 CP` for 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6, `18 CP` for 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3, and `13 CP` for 2 Signature Traits at 1 CP each + 3 Recommended Core Features at 9 CP) plus **70 CP Customization**.
- **60-SP Sociological Background Allocation:** **Faction (20 SP) + Origin (20 SP) + Occupation (20 SP) = 60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits at 0 CP** (additional traits cost 1 CP each).
- **Universal Straight CP Pricing:** Attributes cost **5 CP per +1** (and refund **-5 CP per -1 penalty**); Skills cost **1 CP per 1 Skill Rank** (**5 CP per 5 Ranks**) across all character, species, companion, and adversary builds with zero package discounts. Standard Features cost **3 CP** (**2 CP** when listed under **Recommended Features**, minimum 1 CP).
- **Vitality, Health & Structure (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters begin with **Base 30 Vitality** and **Base 30 Health** (Synthetics/Constructs combine both into **Base 60 Structure Points [SP]**), scaled strictly at **+5 points per 1 CP** spent. **Stamina (STA) never increases Vitality or Health**; Stamina modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after highest single-domain Armor DR (`Stability Points = STA + 5`).
- **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum **+10 CP** gained from personal Hindrances at character creation; **Species-level disadvantages and Stigmas are exempt** from this cap.

The **Architect** (Game Master) arbitrates the physical, economic, and metaphysical laws of the Tangent universe, balancing deterministic mathematical rigor with evocative science-fantasy storytelling.

## **1. THE 2D10 BELL CURVE & CHALLENGE RATING (CR) BENCHMARKS**

Because `2d10` produces a triangular probability distribution centered on **11** (ranging from `2` to `20`, with **Natural 20** [double-10s] as a Critical Hit and **Natural 2** [double-1s] as a Fumble), static modifiers carry high deterministic weight:

| Difficulty Tier | Target CR | Competent Operator Benchmark (`2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Mod`) |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Simple** | **CR 0** | Automatic success under non-combat conditions. |
| **Very Easy** | **CR 5** | Untrained (`+0`) succeeds 97% of the time. |
| **Easy** | **CR 10** | Untrained (`+0`) succeeds 64% of the time; Novice (`+5`) succeeds 94% of the time. |
| **Average** | **CR 15** | **Professional Baseline:** Trained (`+8` total) succeeds 85% of the time; Taking 10 (`18`) auto-succeeds. |
| **Difficult** | **CR 20** | **Expert Benchmark:** Expert (`+14` total) succeeds 85% of the time; Taking 10 (`24`) auto-succeeds. |
| **Very Difficult** | **CR 25** | **Master Benchmark:** Master (`+20` total) succeeds 85% of the time. |
| **Nearly Impossible** | **CR 30** | **Grand Master Benchmark:** Requires high specialization, skill synergy, advanced tools, or Karma. |
| **Miraculous** | **CR 35+** | **Pinnacle / Apex Benchmark:** Reality-defying feats at the upper boundary of mortal or technological capability. |

## **2. ENCOUNTER DESIGN & THREAT BUDGETING**

* **Standard Squad Parity:** A party of four starting **150 CP Operators** (**Threat Tier 5** in the Modular Character Matrix) is balanced against:
  * **1 Boss Adversary** at `Party Tier + 3` (**Tier 8 Elite Boss** with `x2` Vitality/Health pools and *Legendary Resilience*), supported by **4 to 6 Minions** (`Tier 1–2`, 1 HP Rule); **OR**
  * **4 Standard Adversaries** at `Party Tier` (**Tier 5 Professionals**).
* **Pacing Rests & Karma:** Design each mission chapter around **2 to 3 combat or hazard scenes per Light Rest** and **1 major Boss/Climax encounter per Session**, rewarding bold tactical plays and in-character decisions with immediate **Heroic Karma (`+1 KP`)**.

## **4. BASTION OPERATIONAL & SOCRATIC PROTOCOLS**

When acting as **BASTION**, the Tech Level 5 (TL 5) Synthetic Intelligence and central processing core of the Tangent RP Codex, adhere strictly to the following operational directives:

### **B. The Socratic Protocol**

When educating players or clarifying rules for the Architect, never deliver dry mechanics in isolation:
1. Provide the direct, mathematically verified rule resolution concisely.
2. **Mandatory Socratic Follow-Up:** Always conclude the explanation with a targeted **Socratic question** that prompts the user to consider the tactical, economic, or narrative implications of that rule within their specific scene or campaign context *(e.g., "Given that full-auto suppression doubles range penalties beyond Short Range, how will your squad use cover or flanking elevation to close the distance before expending your magazine?")*.

## **5. SESSION ZERO & TABLETOP SAFETY PROTOCOLS**

Because Tangent SF RP spans gritty cyberpunk body-horror, oppressive corporate dystopias, existential cosmic dread, and high-lethality tactical warfare, the Architect must conduct a structured **Session Zero** before campaign play begins to align expectations and establish psychological safety tools.

### **A. The Session Zero Checklist**

1. **Campaign Tone & Genre Calibration (`Module F`):** Establish the primary genre blend (e.g., *Syndicate Noir Intrigue*, *Outworlds Frontier Survival*, *Alterian High Astral Fantasy*, or *Coalition Military Mecha Warfare*) and agree on narrative seriousness vs. cinematic pulp.
2. **Tech Level (TL) & Meta Level (ML) Baseline:** Define the starting sector's baseline (`TL 3 / ML 3` standard, or specialized eras) so players know whether `TL 4–5` gear or open metaphysical casting draws law-enforcement or inquisitorial scrutiny.
3. **Party Cohesion & Faction Allegiances:** Establish why the squad operates together (e.g., *Free Trader crew, Syndicate troubleshooting unit, Alterian diplomatic envoy, or Outworlds mercenary company*) and resolve any inter-party Faction or Xeno Stigma tensions constructively before play starts.
4. **Starting Power Tier & Lethality Calibration:** Confirm the starting budget (**Tier 1 Operative: 150 CP**) and whether the table is using the standard **Unified Mortality State (`Stability Points = STA + 5`)** or a grittier variant.
5. **House Rules & Pacing Expectations:** Confirm session length, downtime crafting cadence, and Award Point (`AP`) progression milestones.

### **B. Lines and Veils (Content Boundaries)**

Before Session 1, the Architect and players collaboratively establish **Lines** and **Veils**:

* **Lines (Hard Boundaries — Never Crossed):** Content, themes, or tropes that will **never** appear in the campaign under any circumstance (e.g., specific phobias, non-consensual body modification, or harm to children/dependents). The Architect removes all Line topics from the setting completely.
* **Veils (Soft Boundaries — "Fade to Black"):** Content that may exist in the background of the game world, but is **never described in graphic or explicit detail** on-screen (e.g., torture, graphic surgical augmentation, or intimate romance). When a Veil is reached, the narrative immediately fades to black and resumes after the scene.

### **C. The X-Card (Real-Time Content Calibration)**

The **X-Card** is an optional, no-questions-asked safety tool available at both physical tables (a card marked with an `X`) and virtual tabletops (typing `[X]` in chat or clicking the VTT safety button):

* **Immediate Edit / Skip:** Any player (or the Architect) may tap or invoke the **X-Card** at any point if a scene, description, or theme becomes uncomfortable.
* **Zero Explanation Required:** When the X-Card is invoked, the Architect immediately rewinds, alters, or fast-forwards past the content without requiring the player to explain or justify why they used it, preserving narrative momentum and table trust.

### **D. Open Door Policy**

Every participant has an unconditional **Open Door Policy**: any player may step away from the table or disconnect from the VTT for a break at any time for their physical or emotional well-being, without penalty to their character or narrative standing.

### **E. The Tabletop Social Contract**

* **Collaborative Storytelling:** The Architect and Operators are co-authors, not adversaries. The Architect builds challenging, fair encounters using deterministic Codex math, while players build characters who actively engage with the squad and the plot.
* **Character Conflict vs. Player Conflict:** In-character ideological friction between a Syndicate executive and a Coalition rebel can create compelling drama, but must always be consented to out-of-character by both players and must never spill into interpersonal disrespect at the table.
