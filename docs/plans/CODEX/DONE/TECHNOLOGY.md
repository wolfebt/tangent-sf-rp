## **ADVANCEMENT LEVELS & DEVELOPMENTAL PHILOSOPHY**

### 

### **The Framework of Eras**

The framework of "Eras and Comparative Technology" serves as the definitive guideline for understanding the availability and sophistication of technology across the Tangent universe. This model is not a rigid ladder, but a fluid spectrum influenced by resources, environment, and cultural philosophy.  
This structure functions primarily as a comparative tool—a baseline for gauging the relative standing between different entities. By comparing a society's capabilities against these defined Levels, one can establish context for trade, conflict, and social sophistication.

### 

### **Core Principles of Fluctuation**

1. **Uneven Development:** No two cultures progress at the same pace. A society may be TL 4+ in Ballistics (Warfare) but only TL 2 in Medicine.  
2. **Resource Influence:** Availability of materials like Aetherium or Rare Earths dictates the path. An aquatic species (e.g., Mobula) may skip Combustion (TL 1\) entirely for Chemical/Hydro-pressure tech.  
3. **Technological Stagnation:** High-tech does not mean "New." The Impyrium operates at TL 4 (Galactic) but refuses to innovate, relying on "Heirloom Technology" maintained by rote tradition rather than scientific understanding.  
4. **Non-Development:** Not every technology is universally adopted. A society might never develop the wheel or gunpowder, leading to divergent pathways.

### 

### **The Nuance of Progress (-, Standard, \+)**

To accurately represent the technological diversity of the galaxy, each Tech Level (TL) is divided into three distinct sub-strata.

* **Nascent (TL-):** The technology is new, expensive, bulky, and often unreliable. It is the domain of experimental military projects or wealthy early adopters.  
* **Standard (TL):** The technology is refined, mass-produced, and reliable. It is the baseline expectation for that era.  
* **Advanced (TL+):** The technology has been miniaturized, optimized, and perfected. It borders on the capabilities of the *next* Tech Level but is achieved through current-era methods.

## **TECH LEVEL 0: STONE AGE (Primitive / Pre-Industrial)**

Era: The Stone, Bronze, and Iron Ages.  
Power Source: Muscle, Fire, Wind, Water.

### 

### **Overview & Culture**

This tier describes cultures characterized by a near-total absence of complex mechanical technology. Their civilization operates entirely outside of technological dependence, often relying on inherent magic, fundamental survival strategies, or deep cultural dogma that rejects fragility in favor of organic strength.

* **Subsistence & Lifestyle:** Survival depends on **Hunting and Foraging** (hunting animals and gathering wild plants). A **Nomadic lifestyle** is common, requiring adaptation to different environments. The **mastery of fire** provides warmth, light, and a way to **cook food** (which revolutionizes diet). **Simple Medicines and herbs** (herbalism) are used to treat basic ailments, often made from plants found in the wild.  
* **Social Structure:** **Small Social Groups** (**families/clans**) form the unit of survival, providing support, protection, and cooperation. **Rituals and early Belief Systems** (often animistic) provide a framework for understanding the natural world. **Development of Simple Clothing** from animal skins and plant materials protects against the elements. **Early Art** (cave paintings, sculptures) is used for expression, often depicting daily life.  
* **Communication & Transport:** **Verbal Communication** is used generally, alongside **Pictograms** to represent objects or ideas. **Wheeled Carts and wagons** and **Simple Boats and rafts** are used for transporting goods and people.  
* **Tools & Technology:** **Basic Toolmaking** allows for the creation and use of **Rudimentary stone, bone, or wood implements** for tasks like cutting, scraping, and hammering. Large-scale mechanical construction is non-existent.

### **Demographics & Mechanics**

* **Associated Species:** **Fae, Koban, Thorns**.  
* **Education Bonus:** **Alertness \+2, Survival \+2, 1 Skill \+2** (Limited Selection).  
* **Limitation:** Skills associated with basic development only. Cannot select Piloting, Engineering, or Computer skills at character creation.

### 

### **Progression Track**

| Sub-Level | Description | Key Technologies |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **TL 0- (Nascent)** | **The Dawn of Tools.** Hunter-gatherer societies utilizing found objects. | Unworked stone, bone clubs, fire hardening, animal hides. |
| **TL 0 (Standard)** | **Agrarian & Metallurgical.** Settled agriculture, masonry, and soft metals. | Bronze/Iron tools, the wheel, sailing ships, written language, basic surgery (high mortality). |
| **TL 0+ (Advanced)** | **The Clockwork Edge.** Complex mechanical engineering without steam. High Renaissance. | Steel, intricate clockwork (Antikythera), complex architecture (domes/aqueducts), alchemy, plate armor, crossbows. |

## **TECH LEVEL 1: METAL AGE (Industrial / Mechanical)**

Era: The Age of Steam and Combustion.  
Power Source: Coal, Steam, Early Fossil Fuels.

### 

### **Overview & Culture**

The transition from the Iron Age to the Industrial Revolution. This era is marked by the harnessing of fire for **Foundries**, allowing for mass metallurgy and the creation of robust tools and weapons.

* **Basic Fabrication & Metallurgy:** People have learned to harvest and process natural materials (wood, stone, clay) to create useful objects. Fire is harnessed for foundries to melt and shape metals for complex tools and weapons.  
* **Industrialization:** The introduction of rudimentary machines systematizes production, dramatically increasing volume and uniformity and using basic machines to produce goods more quickly and efficiently.  
* **Maritime & Trade:** Sailboats and organized flotillas expand geographical reach, facilitating robust trade with other groups, the exchange of intellectual capital, goods, ideas, and technologies, which fosters cultural exchange and the spread of innovations.  
* **Communication & Writing:** Published text and signaling systems, along with the development of writing systems, allow for the preservation of communal memory, the recording of history and information, the sharing of ideas, and the coordination of large-scale activities (governance).  
* **Urbanization & State Formation:** The concentration of population leads to the formation of cities and organized states, providing organization, protection, and requiring infrastructure like roads and water systems.  
* **Agriculture:** Domestication of animals and plants provides stable food sources, supporting larger populations.  
* **Social Structure:** A social hierarchy and division of specialized roles for individuals allows for increased efficiency and productivity.  
* **Culture & Belief:** Art and Architecture reflect the society's beliefs, values, and cultural identity, often serving religious, political, or social purposes. Religion and belief systems explain the world and provide meaning, influencing social norms, laws, and governance.  
* **Medicine:** Basic Medicine & Pharmacology uses natural plants, substances, and other means to treat illnesses.

### **Demographics & Mechanics**

* **Associated Species:** **Caprians, Orlls, Truldan, Vassoth, Weti**.  
* **Education Bonus:** **2 Vocation skills \+2, 4 Skills \+2** (Limited Selection).  
* **Limitation:** Selection limited to Classic Age, Middle Age, to Renaissance equivalents. No advanced electronics or energy shielding.

### 

### **Progression Track**

| Sub-Level | Description | Key Technologies |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **TL 1- (Nascent)** | **The Steam Revolution.** The shift from muscle to machine. Dirty, dangerous, loud. | Stationary steam engines, printing press, early telegraph, germ theory (proposed), muskets/cannons. |
| **TL 1 (Standard)** | **The Combustion Age.** Mass production and mobility. | Locomotives, steamships, early internal combustion, photography, telephone, vaccines, rifled firearms. |
| **TL 1+ (Advanced)** | **The Electromechanical Dawn.** The verge of digital. "Dieselpunk" aesthetics. | DC Electricity, radio, early aircraft (biplanes), assembly lines, X-Rays, Dreadnoughts, chemical weapons. |

## **TECH LEVEL 2: DATA AGE (Digital / Information)**

Era: The Silicon and Plastic Age.  
Power Source: Advanced Hydrocarbons, Fission, Renewables.

### 

### **Overview & Culture**

A technological continuum comparable to Modern Earth, spanning from early telegraphy to the colonization of the local solar system.

* **Global Connectivity:** High-bandwidth audio/video broadcasts and digital networks connect the entire planet. Cybersecurity becomes a critical survival trait. Audio/video Radio Broadcast, flatvid and digital networks are used to communicate and share information. Cybersecurity has become a critical aspect of modern society with reliance on digital networks and technology and the importance of protecting sensitive information and systems, with governments, businesses, and individuals taking steps to safeguard their digital assets. Globalization and the Information Age has facilitated the rapid exchange of information, goods, and services across borders, leading to increased interconnectedness and interdependence among countries. Social media and Online Communication transforms the way people interact, share information, and form relationships. This has led to new opportunities for connection and collaboration, and challenges related to privacy, mental health, and the spread of misinformation.  
* **Energy:** High Yield Reactors (Fission/Early Fusion) form the cornerstone of infrastructure, powering massive cities and industrial operations. High Yield Reactors are used to generate electricity, and can be used to power a variety of devices and machines. Environmental Sustainability and increased awareness brings innovations in renewable energy, waste management, and resource conservation being developed to address the challenges posed by climate change and resource depletion.  
* **Transportation:** "Roller" vehicles (wheeled self-propelled) dominate land, while chemical rockets and specialized launch systems open the door to orbital operations. Roller vehicles (self-propelled wheeled) and various water and aircraft are used for transportation. These vehicles are able to travel over a variety of terrain, and can be used to transport people and goods long distances. Rockets used for space travel are used to launch satellites and other objects into space.  
* **Medicine:** Genetic engineering and advanced imaging (MRI) allow for precision diagnostics and the beginning of biological enhancement. Focused Medical Sciences are used to diagnose and treat diseases. Basic Biotechnology and genetic engineering have opened up new possibilities for medical treatments, agricultural improvements, and even the potential for human enhancement.  
* **Industry & Automation:** Mass Industrialization and manufacturing goods on a large scale. Basic Artificial intelligence and automation technologies have revolutionized various industries, leading to increased efficiency and productivity.

### **Demographics & Mechanics**

* **Associated Species:** **Brei, Dwergs, Prokoss, Qerics, Terran Humans**.  
* **Education Bonus:** **\+20 Skill Points** (Modern Education System).  
* **Limitation:** Selection limited to approximately Modern Earth equivalent.

### 

### **Progression Track**

| Sub-Level | Description | Key Technologies |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **TL 2- (Nascent)** | **Atomic & Transistor Era.** Splitting the atom and miniaturizing electronics. | Mainframes, jet engines, plastics, orbital rocketry, antibiotics, fission bombs. |
| **TL 2 (Standard)** | **The Information Age.** Global connectivity and micro-processing. | The Internet, smartphones, GPS, composites, EVs, reusable rockets, genetic sequencing, precision munitions. |

Power Source: Thorium, Hydrogen Fuel Cells.

The dawn of the interstellar age. Species break the bonds of their home star system to begin the arduous colonization of exoworlds.

**Technology Overview (Consolidated)**

  * Memory Materials (limited shape memory).  
  * Nanotechnology (molecular manipulation).  
  * Advanced Materials science (new materials with unique properties).  
  * Miniaturized Fabrication (3D printing, small-scale manufacturing).  
* **Energy:**  
  * Portable High Yield Energy Devices.  
  * Distributed Energy Generation and storage (solar, batteries, decentralized grids).

  * **Space Industry:** Space Industrialization (Stations, Habitats, etc.) and asteroid mining.

## **TECH LEVEL 4: STELLAR AGE (Galactic / The Warp Era)**

Era: The Age of Antimatter and Field Manipulation.  
Power Source: Antimatter, Cold Fusion, Aetherium Integration.

### 

### **Overview & Culture**

Enhanced technology characterized by a practical mastery over Matter and Force. Physics is no longer a constraint but a suggestion.

* **Programmable Materials:** Matter engineered at the nanoscale to be dynamic—hulls that self-repair and change aerodynamic profile on command. Also used to create various structures and objects with **Matter Compression and hyper tensile materials** for strong, lightweight construction.  
* **Gravity Mastery:** Ships utilize **Grav-Plating** (Artificial Gravity) and Inertial Dampeners, allowing for vertical designs and high-G maneuvers without liquefying the crew.  
* **Independent FTL:** Starships are equipped with **Tangent Drives** (Warp/Hyperdrive), allowing for travel independent of the fixed Gate network. Travel is also facilitated by **Jump Gates** that create artificial wormholes through hyperspace.  
* **Advanced Propulsion & Power:** Ships use **Kinetic Reactors** to provide power for force wave propulsion systems. Power is sustained through **Advanced Energy Harvesting** from sources like solar, fusion, and antimatter.  
* **Transhumanism:** "**Clinical Immortality**" is achievable through cellular reversal and revitalization treatments. The **Trans-Neural Interface** turns the brain into a supercomputer terminal, allowing direct connection to digital technology and is enhanced by **Mnemonic Transceivers** for thought communication. The development of immortality and enhanced abilities is driven by **Enhanced Genetic Tailoring**.  
* **Logistics & Infrastructure:** **Matter Transmission Nodes** allow for the transport of matter over long distances. **Optimized Resourcing, repurposing, and fabrication** ensures efficient resource use. **Superstructure Architecture** enables the construction of planetoid-sized super stations.  
* **Information & Simulation:** **Hyper FTL Transmission** enables faster-than-light communication. **Holophotonic Chambers** create realistic interactive simulations of environments, and **Synthetic Intellects** (AGI, Artificial Sentience) provide intelligent systems.  
* **Biological Advancements:** **Advanced Biotechnology** allows for the creation of living materials and machinery with the ability to heal, grow, and adapt.  
* **Expansion & Sustainability:** **Autonomous Exploration probes** gather data on distant star systems and habitable planets. **Advanced Terraforming** transforms planetary environments for colonization. **Advanced Environmental management** ensures long-term sustainability.  
* **Societal Elements:** **Interstellar Trade and diplomacy** foster cooperation. **Advanced Education and training systems** leverage VR/AI to prepare individuals for interstellar life.  
* **Defense:** **Advanced Defense and reaction systems** protect civilizations from hostile threats and natural disasters.

### **Demographics & Mechanics**

* **Associated Species:** **Impyrium, Aulurans, Manelli, Davae, Sefalin, Syndicate**.  
* **Education Bonus:** **\+40 Skill Points** (Highly Advanced Education System).

## **TECH LEVEL 5: GALACTIC AGE (Cosmic / The Singularity)**

Era: The Age of Reality Manipulation.  
Power Source: Dimensional Siphons, Matter-Energy Conversion.

### 

### **Overview & Culture**

The domain of "God-Like" power. The pillars of this era are **Matter-Energy Conversion** (limitless power/creation), **Dimensional Compression** (folding space), and **Gravitonic Control**.

* **Polymatter:** A synthetic, programmable substrate that exists in a state of hyper-potentiality. It shifts form, density, and state of matter based on direct mental interface. Used in **Advanced Morphic Equipment** for thought-responsive, hyper-adaptive materials and by **Advanced Personal Fabricators** for construction.  
* **Holophotonic Projection:** Projections of solidified energy creating interactive constructs, environment enclosures, and functional device simulations.  
* **Megastructures:** Structures like **Dyson Spheres and Rings** (**Megastructure Architecture**), encompassing entire stars to solve energy needs permanently. **Gestalted Synthetic Intellects** (Hyper Intellects and Superintelligence) are housed within grand constructs such as Mekan Prime (Dyson type stellar rings) and Bastion (city-sized education complex).  
* **Spatial Manifold Transition:** Instantaneous teleportation across thousands of light-years via **Jump Station projectors** or personal **Quantum Signallers** (**FTL in Real Time communication**). **Portable Matter Transmitters** (Personal Transporter Unit) allow for the teleportation of matter over long distances (target area must be unshielded from transmission).  
* **Digital Immortality:** Consciousness is fully digitized, backed up, and can be uploaded into biological clones, synthetic bodies, or virtual environments at will. Achieved with the transfer of consciousness into a digital form, which can then be uploaded into a new body.  
* **Gravitonic Harnessing and Generation:** Used for a variety of purposes, such as flight or the construction of megastructures.  
* **Dimensional Compression:** Allows for mass storage of physical matter as a digital medium and grants access to interdimensional areas from pockets, boxes, and rooms up to large hanger size.  
* **Base Substance Interchange:** The conversion of energy and matter at the atomic level, enabling a post-scarcity society with any needed resources.  
* **Retro-Genetic Reengineering:** Modification of active living genes, used to alter the subject within a very short time for dramatic changes.  
* **Advanced Governance:** Highly sophisticated systems capable of managing complex galactic societies, ensuring efficient resource allocation, and maintaining social order.  
* **Stellar Engineering:** Control of stars and other celestial bodies for various purposes, such as energy production, resource extraction, or the creation of habitable environments.  
* **Advanced Exobiology:** Leading to new insights into biology, medicine, and the potential for interspecies cooperation and communication.  
* **Advanced Energy Sources:** Capable of harnessing highly advanced theoretical energy sources like **Zero-Point Energy** and **Dark Energy**. Represent a paradigm shift in energy production, dwarfing even the output of antimatter reactors.  
* **Galactic-Scale Energy Networks:** Networks that span across star systems, providing abundant and sustainable power to countless worlds and civilizations.  
* **Advanced Defense:** Cutting-edge technologies and strategies to protect interstellar civilizations from potential threats, such as hostile alien species, rogue superintelligences, or natural disasters on a galactic scale.

## **CIVILIZATION ASPECTS & DOMAINS**

Detailed progression tracks for specific aspects of society.

### 

### **AGRICULTURE**

From hunting and gathering to planetary engineering.

* **0: Hunting and Gathering.** Early human societies relied on hunting animals and gathering plants for sustenance.  
* **1: Farming.** Domestication of plants and animals, leading to the development of agriculture.  
* **2: Advanced Farming.** Modern farming techniques, including mechanization, irrigation, and fertilization.  
* **3: Terraforming.** Manipulation of the environment to create more suitable conditions for agriculture.  
* **4: Planetary Engineering.** Large-scale manipulation of entire planets for agricultural purposes.  
* **5: Matter Synthesis.** Direct conversion of energy to food/organic matter (Post-Scarcity/Replicators).

### 

### **ARCHITECTURE**

From simple structures to self-assembling habitats.

* **0: Simple Structures.** Huts, tents, and basic masonry structures.  
* **1: Advanced Masonry.** Early steel structures and basic urban planning.  
* **2: Skyscrapers.** Advanced urban planning and sustainable architecture.  
* **3: Smart Cities.** Advanced building materials and energy-efficient designs.  
* **4: Advanced Space Habitats.** Self-assembling structures and advanced terraforming techniques.  
* **5: Megastructures.** Dyson Spheres, Ring Worlds, and Stellar Engineering.

### 

### **BIOTECHNOLOGY**

From selective breeding to retro-genetic engineering.

## **ADAPTIVE TECHNOLOGIES**

**Adaptive, Morphic, and Holophotonic Systems**  
This technology enables equipment to serve multiple functions by physically altering its form or creating a functional energy projection as needed.

* **Requirement:** Each equipment option must be loaded into the unit's **Design Library** and must be within the unit’s physical and technical capability.  
* **Limit:** Only **1 device** may be emulated per unit at a time.

### 

### **Reconfiguration Mechanics**

Changing functions is initiated by a simple switch command.

* **Trigger:** **Move Action** to trigger reconfiguration.  
* **Duration:** The time required for the device to become usable depends on the Tech Level:

| Tech Level | Technology Type | Time to Reconfigure |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **TL 3** | **Nanotech / Biotech** | **Minutes**\* |
| **TL 4** | **Programmable Matter** | **Rounds**\* |
| **TL 5** | **Polymatter** | **1 Round** |
| **TL 5** | **Holophotonics** | **Instant** (Trigger with Move Action, usable via Standard Action in same round) |

*\*Exact duration in Minutes or Rounds depends on the complexity of the device being emulated.*

### 

### **Schematics & Design Libraries**

Adding a new function to a device requires a digital schematic.

* **Integration:** Requires a **DC 20 Computer Check** to compile and implement the schematic into the unit's system.  
* **Constraints:** The emulating device is limited by its physical dimensions, maximum functionality, and the data size of its Design Library.

  #### 

  #### **Schematic Acquisition**

Schematics can be purchased on various markets or shared (if copy protections are absent). These files are also used for Fabricators. Social checks or Bartering may modify the base amount.  
**Market Value:**

* **COMMON:** 5x Base Cost of the item.  
* **UNCOMMON:** 10x Base Cost of the item.  
* **RARE/RESTRICTED:** 20x+ Base Cost of the item (if available).

Manual Creation:  
An industrious character with the proper skill set and design system can create their own schematics.

* **Procedure:** Requires a successful initial design and prototyping check (using Mechanical, Electrical, etc.) with a DC based on the device's complexity. This applies to creating new designs or blueprinting existing items.

## **THE TECHNOLOGIST FEATURE**

**Advanced Specialization Rule**  
Characters with the **Technologist** feature are ahead of the curve, understanding technology that shouldn't exist yet for their era.

* **Requirement:** To advance in a Tech Level (TL), a character requires multiple fields of advanced study. The feature must be selected multiple times for various fields (Minimum of 5 fields to fully progress to the next TL).  
* **Scope:** Tech 3+ characters may choose from TL 4 fields. Tech 4+ characters may choose from TL 5 fields. Tech 5 Technologists are generally NPCs unless the GM allows experimental TL X discoveries.  
* **Penalty:** Without the Technologist Feature in the appropriate field, take a **\-5 penalty** on any check to use, repair, or modify a device above your Technology Level (per level of difference).  
  * *Exception:* Some straightforward items (like weapons) may only incur a **\-1 per TL** penalty, but will require a check to wield any special options.

Fields of Study:  
Agriculture, Architecture, Biotechnology, Commerce, Communication, Devices, Education, Energy, Manufacturing, Materials, Medicine, Meta Sciences, Science, Society, Synthetic Intelligence, Transportation, Weaponry.
