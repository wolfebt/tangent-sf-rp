# **SPECIES FRAMEWORK & BUILD SYSTEM**

### **The Tangent Codex: Source of Truth**

## 

## **Introduction: The Xenological Blueprint**

In the Tangent universe, a "Species" is more than a cosmetic skin for a character; it is a fundamental chassis that defines their interaction with the physical, technological, and metaphysical laws of the galaxy.  
This framework acts as the Source of Truth for designing sentient lifeforms. It synthesizes the narrative architecture of the Tangent setting with the mathematical rigor of the Build Point (BP) system. When creating a species, you are engineering a biological or mechanical survivor.

## 

## 

## **I. The Component Hierarchy (Standardized Entry Template)**

Every species entry in the Tangent Codex must contain the following components to be considered complete. This hierarchy ensures that every lifeform is defined not just by its stats, but by its place in the galactic ecosystem.

### 

### **1\. Species Overview**

* **Species Name:** The formal designation (scientific/bureaucratic) and the common trade tongue name.  
* **Homeworld / Plane of Origin:** The specific planet, system, or dimensional plane where the species originated.  
* **Sentience Level:** The cognitive classification (e.g., Sapient, Hive Mind, God-like, Pre-Sentient).  
* **General Description:** A concise one-paragraph summary of the species, establishing its visual identity and primary narrative role.  
* **Core Concept / Archetype:** The "high-concept" elevator pitch (e.g., "Honor-bound Lizard Warriors," "Telepathic Gas Clouds," "Corporate Insectoids").

### 

### **2\. Biology & Physiology**

* **General Appearance:** Overall body shape, posture (bipedal, quadruped, amorphous), and average size/weight.  
* **Physical Composition:** The fundamental matter of the being (Carbon-based, Silicon-based, Energy, Polymatter).  
* **Skeletal Structure:** Support system (Internal Skeleton, Exoskeleton, Hydrostatic, Energy Lattice).  
* **Limbs & Appendages:** Number and type of arms, legs, tentacles, wings, or manipulators.  
* **Integument:** The outer covering (Skin, Scales, Chitin, Fur, Feathers, Energy Field).  
* **Coloration & Markings:** Typical skin tones, camouflage patterns, bioluminescence, or display colors.  
* **Head & Facial Features:** Sensory clusters, eye type (compound/binocular), mouth structure (mandibles/beak/jaw), and decorative features (horns/antennae).  
* **Diet & Metabolism:** What they consume (Omnivore, Photosynthetic, Lithovore) and how they process energy.  
* **Senses:** Standard sensory input (Sight, Sound) and unique capabilities (Echolocation, Thermal, Electroreception).  
* **Reproduction:** Methods of propagation (Sexual, Asexual, Budding, Construction) and associated rituals.  
* **Life Cycle & Maturation:** Stages of life (Larva, Pupa, Adult), growth rates, and average lifespan.  
* **Sleep/Rest Patterns:** Biological downtime requirements (Nocturnal, Diurnal, Hibernation, Sleepless).  
* **Vulnerabilities & Resistances:** Natural immunities (Poison, Radiation) or environmental weaknesses (Cold, Fire).

### 

### **3\. Psychology & Cognition**

* **Intelligence & Problem-Solving:** Cognitive style (Logical/Linear, Intuitive/Creative, Collective/Hive).  
* **Primary Method of Communication:** How information is exchanged (Verbal, Telepathy, Pheromones, Radio-Frequency).  
* **Dominant Instincts & Drives:** Core biological urges (Survival, Expansion, Hoarding, Protection).  
* **Temperament & Disposition:** General personality baseline (Aggressive, Passive, Curious, Xenophobic).  
* **Emotional Range:** Capacity for emotion (Human-like, Dampened, Alien/Incomprehensible).  
* **Memory & Learning:** Retention methods (Genetic Memory, Oral Tradition, Data Upload).  
* **Concept of Self:** Identity structure (Individualistic, Collectivist, caste-based).

### 

### **4\. Culture & Society**

* **Social Structure:** Organization of the population (Clans, Nations, Hives, Corporations).  
* **Government & Politics:** Ruling systems (Democracy, Magocracy, Tyranny, Anarchy).  
* **Laws & Ethics:** Moral compass and legal frameworks (Honor Codes, Survival of the Fittest, Profit-driven).  
* **Social Hierarchy:** Systems of rank and status (Caste System, Meritocracy, Hereditary).  
* **Gender Roles & Family Structure:** Definitions of family units and gender roles (if applicable).  
* **Language(s):** Spoken and written forms of communication.  
* **Technology Level:** Current advancement tier (Primitive, Industrial, Spacefaring, Transcendent).  
* **Arts & Aesthetics:** Cultural expressions in music, visual arts, and architectural style.  
* **Religion, Spirituality, & Mythology:** Belief systems, deities, and cosmological views.  
* **Traditions & Rituals:** Key cultural events, holidays, and rites of passage.  
* **Diet & Cuisine:** Preparation and consumption habits (Ritualistic feasting, nutrient paste, energy absorption).

### 

### **5\. Ecology & Habitat**

* **Homeworld Description:** Climate, geography, and atmospheric conditions of the origin world.  
* **Habitat/Niche:** The specific ecosystem role they fill (Apex Predator, Scavenger, Symbiote).  
* **Relationship with Other Species:** Interactions with local fauna and other sapient races (Predatory, Trade, Isolationist).  
* **Resource Needs:** Critical environmental requirements for survival (Water, Sunlight, specific radiation).  
* **Architecture & Habitations:** Construction style and settlement patterns (Underground hives, orbital rings, tree-cities).

### 

### **6\. Abilities & Traits (Game Mechanics)**

* **Inherent Abilities:** Natural powers defined by biology (Flight, Venom, Natural Armor).  
* **Species-Specific Strengths:** Mechanical bonuses to Attributes or Skills.  
* **Species-Specific Weaknesses:** Mechanical penalties or vulnerabilities.  
* **Unique Biological Features:** Distinct mechanics that defy standard categorization (Shapeshifting, Regeneration).

### 

### **7\. Notes & Concepts**

* **General Notes:** Plot hooks, naming conventions, and roleplaying tips.  
* **Inspiration Gallery:** Visual references and thematic touchstones for visualization.

## 

## **II. Construction Guide (Step-by-Step)**

Follow this protocol to build a species from concept to completion.

1. **Concept & Type Selection:** Select the Type from the Master Catalog below. This sets the base BP cost and fundamental physiology.  
2. **Sizing & Movement:**  
   * Select the Size. Medium is the baseline (0 BP). Smaller or Larger sizes cost BP due to combat advantages.  
   * Movement: Standard is 30ft Ground (0 BP). Special modes (Fly/Swim) cost extra.  
3. **Attributes & Skills:**  
   * Assign biological advantages.  
   * Abilities: Each \+1 bonus to an Attribute costs 4 BP.  
   * Skills: A bundle of \+5 Skill Points costs 4 BP.  
4. **Features & Traits:**  
   * Select special abilities from the Trait Catalog.  
   * Basic Feature: 1 BP.  
   * Advanced Feature: 2 BP.  
   * Elite Feature: 4 BP.  
   * Disadvantages: Reduce total BP cost.  
5. **The Build Point (BP) Tally:** Sum all costs.  
   * **Standard Species:** 10 \- 20 BP (Humans, Elves).  
   * **Advanced Species:** 25 \- 40 BP (Dragonkin, High-Tier Synthetics).  
   * **Monster/NPC:** 40+ BP (Dragons, Progenitors).

## **III. The Master Catalog (Source of Truth)**

### **1\. Species Types (The Chassis)**

Listed are the common traits to any species of that type. Individual species will have specialized abilities and traits depending on developmental, environmental and/or other factors.

#### 

#### **Aberration (1 BP)**

Aberrations have a bizarre anatomy, strange abilities, an alien mindset, or any combination of the three.

* **Senses:** Darkvision out to 60 feet. \[1\]  
* **Physiology:** Aberrations eat, sleep, and breathe.

#### 

#### **Beast (1 BP)**

A living, nonhuman creature, usually perceived as an animal and/or non-intelligent. Beastkin are hybrids of the Beast type and have similar traits and are added to another corporeal type.

* **Senses:** Low-light vision. \[1\]  
* **Physiology:** Beasts eat, sleep, and breathe.

#### 

#### **Dragon (5 BP)**

Dragons are intelligent reptilian creatures with metaphysical abilities. Dragonkin are hybrids of the dragon type and have similar traits and are added to another corporeal type.

* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 feet \[1\], Low-light vision \[1\].  
* **Immunities:** Magical sleep effects and paralysis effects. \[3\]  
* **Physiology:** Dragons breathe, eat, and sleep.

#### 

#### **Elemental (13 BP)**

Elementalkin are hybrids of the Elemental type and have similar traits and are added to another corporeal type.

* **Senses:** Darkvision out to 60 feet. \[1\]  
* **Immunities:** Poison, sleep effects, paralysis, and stunning. \[3+3\]  
* **Fortification:** Not subject to critical hits or flanking. \[3\]  
* **Physiology:** Elementals do not eat, sleep, or breathe. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Entity (24 BP)**

A non-corporeal being which may have never been alive \- such as embodiments, essence, avatars and others.

* **Incorporeal:** Immune to all physical conditional effects due to their incorporeal form. \[3\]  
* **Fortification:** Not subject to critical hits or flanking. \[3\]  
* **Immunities:** All mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, and phantasms) \[3\]; bleed damage, death effects, disease, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning \[3\]; nonlethal damage, ability drain, or energy drain; damage to physical ability scores (Con, Dex, Str); exhaustion and fatigue effects. \[3\]  
* **Senses:** Darkvision and Ether Sight out to 60 feet. \[1+2\]  
* **Planar:** Not a creature of the material plane and immune to effects which would pertain to similar creatures. \[3\]  
* **Physiology:** Entities do not eat, sleep, or breathe. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Fey (3 BP)**

A fey is a creature with supernatural abilities and connections to nature or to some other force or place. Feykin are hybrids of the Fey type and have similar traits and are added to another corporeal type.

* **Senses:** Low-light vision. \[1\]  
* **Traits:** Sleepless. \[2\]  
* **Physiology:** Fey breathe and eat.

#### 

#### **Humanoid (0 BP)**

Humanoid species have no special or supernatural abilities, but most can speak and have well-developed societies. Humanoids are usually Medium but Small and Large sized varieties are known.

* **Physiology:** Humanoids breathe, eat, and sleep.

#### 

#### **Mythical (5 BP)**

Beings that usually resemble beasts (or possibly other creature types), typically more intelligent and have special abilities or supernatural powers.

* **Senses:** Darkvision out to 60 feet and low-light vision. \[1+1\]  
* **Physiology:** Mythicals do not eat, sleep, or breathe. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Ooze (6 BP)**

Oozes are gelatinous / semi-solid amorphous creatures also used to describe swarms of small creatures. Many oozes use blindsight or other non-visual senses to navigate.

* **Amorphous:** Immune to all physical conditional effects due to the nature of their slippery form or base components. \[3\]  
* **Fortification:** Not subject to critical hits or flanking. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Planar (4 BP)**

A Planar is at least partially composed of the essence (but not necessarily the matter) of some plane other than the Material Plane. Planar creatures may have familiar forms of other types but may or may not have abilities to match.

* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 feet. \[1\]  
* **Planar Origin:** Not a creature of the material plane and immune to effects which would pertain to similar creatures. \[3\]  
* **Physiology:** Planars breathe, eat, and sleep.

#### 

#### **Synthetic (15 BP)**

Animated objects or artificially created creatures. Synthetics designed to emulate another type may have its form but may or may not have abilities to match.

* **Senses:** Low-light vision \[1\] and Darkvision 60 feet \[1\].  
* **Mental Immunity:** Immune to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns, and phantasms). \[3\]  
* **Repair Required:** Cannot heal damage on their own and must be repaired in some means (such as by physical maintenance, metaphysic repairs or an auto-repair augmentation). \[-3\]  
* **Metabolic Immunity:** Not subject to ability damage, ability drain, fatigue, exhaustion, energy drain, or nonlethal damage. \[3\]  
* **Fortitude Immunity:** Immune to any effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless). \[3\]  
* **Hardened Frame:** Synthetics are hard to destroy, and gain bonus hit points based on their size. \[2\]  
  * *Tiny:* — | *Small:* \+10 | *Medium:* \+20 | *Large:* \+40 | *Huge:* \+80  
* **Synthetic Strength:** For the effects of moving weights \- such as for Lifting/Carrying purposes and Grappling, is equivalent to a creature which is larger by one size (such as a Medium sized Synthetic will count as a Large creature). \[2\]  
* **Physiology:** Synthetics do not breathe, eat, or sleep, unless they want to gain some beneficial effect from one of these activities. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Undead (20 BP)**

Undead species are once-living creatures animated by spiritual or supernatural forces. Undead are based on another corporeal creature type and may or may not retain its previous traits.

* **Senses:** Darkvision 60 feet. \[1\]  
* **Mental Immunity:** Immune to all mind-affecting effects. \[3\]  
* **Physical Immunity:** Immune to bleed damage, death effects, disease, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning. \[3\]  
* **Metabolic Immunity:** Not subject to nonlethal damage, ability drain, or energy drain, and are immune to damage to physical ability scores (Con, Dex, Str), as well as to exhaustion and fatigue effects. \[3\]  
* **Negative Energy Affinity:** Harmed by positive energy and healed by negative energy. An undead creature with the fast healing special quality still benefits from that quality. (Note: Deathless are a variant Undead type with inverted energy traits). \[2\]  
* **Fortitude Immunity:** Immune to any effect that requires a Fortitude save (unless the effect also works on objects or is harmless). \[3\]  
* **Resurrection Limits:** Immune to raise dead/reincarnate. Resurrection and true resurrection can affect undead creatures. \[2\]  
* **Physiology:** Undead do not breathe, eat, or sleep, unless they want to gain some beneficial effect from one of these activities. \[3\]

#### 

#### **Verdant (9 BP)**

This type encompasses vegetable creatures. Note that regular plants lack Wisdom and Charisma scores and are not creatures, but considered living objects. Verdant may be applied to another corporeal type for plant based creatures.

* **Senses:** Low-light vision. \[1\]  
* **Mental Immunity:** Immune to all mind-affecting effects. \[3\]  
* **Physical Immunity:** Immune to paralysis, poison, polymorph, sleep effects, and stunning. \[3\]  
* **Physiology:** Verdants breathe and eat, but do not sleep (unless for beneficial effects/spell regain). \[2\]
