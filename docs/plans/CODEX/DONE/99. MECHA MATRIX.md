# **MECHA MATRIX**

## **THE STRUCTURE OF MOBILITY**

### 

### **The Definition of Mecha**

In the Tangent system, the term **MECHA** is an all-encompassing classification. It serves as the universal mechanical umbrella covering **all** varieties of vehicles, constructs, and mobility devices. This includes, but is not limited to: personal conveyances like skateboards and hoverbikes; autonomous or remote-controlled drones; civilian cars and rovers; aquatic boats and submersibles; atmospheric aircraft; heavy bipedal walkers; and colossal interstellar spaceships. Regardless of whether a construct is powered or non-powered, operates on land, water, in the air, or within the vacuum of space, and whether it is physically piloted, remotely operated, or governed by autonomous AI, it is built and classified under the Mecha Matrix.

**Autonomy and Sentience (The Automata Distinction):**  
Within this classification, any Mecha operating via an onboard AI control system is designated as an **AUTOMATA**. Automata possess low-level AI and autonomous programming (Reactive or Limited Memory) sufficient for independent operation, but they lack true sapience. If a construct houses a fully conscious, self-aware AI, it crosses the threshold from **equipment** to **entity**. Such beings are considered members of the **Synthetic** species (specifically an Intellect Construct or Sentient Machine, as appropriate for the design) and are governed by standard character and NPC creation rules.  
The study of this vehicular transport within the Tangent universe extends far beyond the utilitarian metrics of propulsion and cargo capacity; it is a discipline that intersects engineering, sociology, xenology, and political philosophy. A vehicle, in this context, serves as a mobile artifact of its originating civilization—a kinetic declaration of cultural values manifest in steel, ceramic, or grown biological matter.  
When analyzing the transportation assets of the major galactic powers, one observes a distinct bifurcation between the "Agnostic Chassis"—the fundamental, physics-compliant engineering skeleton—and the "Cultural Skin," the aesthetic and functional overlay that aligns the machine with a faction’s specific technological ethos.

### **The Matrix of Eras and Technological Sub-Strata**

The efficiency, durability, and operational methodology of an item's chassis are explicitly gated by its Tech Level (TL) and its resulting Complexity Stage. The matrix of "Eras and Comparative Technology" serves as the definitive guideline for understanding the availability and sophistication of equipment across the galaxy.  
Technological progress in Tangent is not a rigid ladder, but a fluid spectrum deeply influenced by resource availability, environmental pressures, and cultural dogma. To provide a baseline mechanical standard before exploring the nuanced sub-strata, all materials and passive traits are broadly categorized by their primary era.

| TL | ERA | MATERIAL COMPOSITION | PASSIVE TRAIT |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1-2 | Analog | Stamped Metal, Plastic | None |
| 3 | Digital | Aluminum, Composite | **Modular:** Universal ports; share batteries/charge. |
| 4 | Enhanced | Smart-Fabric, Nanocarbon | **Self-Repairing:** Regens 1 HP/hour. Mastery of Shields/Hover/Directed Energy. |
| 5 | Advanced | Polymatter, Hard-Light | **Morphic / Weightless:** Instant reconfiguration and functional mass projection. |

To accurately represent the nuance of innovation within these eras, each Tech Level is further divided into three distinct sub-strata: **Nascent (TL-)**, representing experimental and bulky prototypes; **Standard (TL)**, representing refined and mass-produced baselines; and **Advanced (TL+)**, representing optimized miniaturization bordering on the capabilities of the next era.

**TL 0: The Stone Age (Primitive / Pre-Industrial)** Operating entirely outside of complex mechanical dependence, TL 0 equipment relies on inherent magic, survival strategies, or deep cultural dogma that rejects fragility in favor of organic strength. Materials consist of unworked stone, bone, wood, and animal hides in the Nascent stage, advancing to bronze, iron, and masonry in the Standard stage. Equipment at this tier possesses the **"Degrading"** trait, breaking easily under sustained stress, and offers minimal Damage Resistance (DR). Vehicular propulsion relies entirely on biological traction or environmental wind. (Base Cost Modifier: N/A)

**TL 1: The Metal Age (Industrial / Mechanical)** Marking the transition from the Iron Age to the Industrial Revolution, TL 1 is characterized by the harnessing of fire for foundries, allowing for mass metallurgy. Power sources shift from muscle and wind to coal, steam, and early fossil fuels. Nascent technologies include intricate clockwork mechanisms akin to the Antikythera mechanism, complex architectural domes, alchemy, plate armor, and crossbows, as well as stationary steam engines and the printing press. Standard innovations introduce internal combustion, early photography, and rudimentary vaccines. Advanced TL 1+ borders on the digital, featuring direct current (DC) electricity, radio, biplanes, early X-Rays, and chemical weapons. Equipment here is heavy, highly durable, but imposes significant check penalties due to its unrefined ergonomics. (Base Cost Modifier: \-5 DC)

**TL 2: The Data Age (Digital / Information)** Comparable to modern Earth, TL 2 spans from early telegraphy to the colonization of the local solar system. Power generation relies on advanced hydrocarbons, fission, and renewable infrastructure. The era is defined by global connectivity, digital networks, and the miniaturization of electronics. Ground propulsion is dominated by Roller Vehicles (wheels and tracks), while atmospheric flight relies on airfoils and chemical jet turbines. Nascent tech includes jet engines, orbital rocketry, and fission bombs. The Standard era perfects GPS, reusable rockets, and genetic sequencing. The Advanced TL 2+ tier borders on cybernetic integration, featuring quantum prototypes, functional virtual reality, nascent artificial intelligence algorithms, and prototype railguns. Protective equipment utilizes kevlar, ceramic plates, and advanced alloys, possessing a **"Ballistic Weave"** trait that highly resists piercing kinetic trauma. (Base Cost Modifier: \-5 DC)

**TL 3: The Space Age (Stellar / The Gate Era)** The dawn of interstellar expansion, breaking the bonds of the home star system. Power is generated via Thorium reactors and Hydrogen fuel cells. A critical limitation of this era is that artificial gravity remains achievable only via spin or thrust; manipulation of gravitons is not yet mastered. TL 3 represents the galactic baseline for common trade, featuring reaction mass space flight (Ionic or Fusion), memory materials with limited shape retention, and molecular nanotechnology. Ground movement sees the introduction of GEV (Hover) technology and Mecha (Walkers) driven by myomer musculature. Advanced TL 3+ pushes the limits of thermodynamics with the discovery of Tangent Space for faster-than-light travel, holographic transceivers, and energy shields that impose massive power drains. TL 3 equipment is defined by the **"Modular"** trait, featuring universal data ports, standard charging protocols, and vacuum-ready hermetic seals. (Base Cost Modifier: \+0 DC)

**TL 4: The Stellar Age (Galactic / The Warp Era)** The domain of high-tier galactic factions (like the Impyrium and Syndicate), characterized by a practical mastery over matter and force where standard physics are treated as a mere suggestion. Power generation shifts to antimatter, cold fusion, and Aetherium integration. This era masters gravity with artificial grav-plating, inertial dampeners, and repulsorlift hover technology. Starships feature independent Tangent Drives for FTL travel without relying on fixed gate networks. Biological limitations are bypassed via "Clinical Immortality" cellular treatments and trans-neural interfaces that turn the biological brain into a supercomputer terminal. Material science is defined by programmable materials engineered at the nanoscale. Equipment utilizes smart-fabrics, nanocarbon weaves, and phase-shift armor. All TL 4 equipment inherently possesses the **"Self-Repairing"** trait, regenerating 1 Structure Point per hour due to localized nanotech. Kinetic weaponry becomes entirely obsolete unless chemically or magnetically hyper-accelerated to keep pace with directed energy and plasma. (Base Cost Modifier: \+5 DC)

**TL 5: The Galactic Age (Cosmic / The Singularity)** The realm of "God-Like" power (Mekan and Mondi entities), representing the apex of material science. The pillars of this era are matter-energy conversion (replicators), dimensional compression (folding space), and absolute gravitonic control. The distinction between hardware and software vanishes completely. Equipment at this tier relies heavily on two exotic technologies:

* **Polymatter:** Polymatter is a synthetic, programmable substrate existing in a hyper-potential state. It is composed of true liquid metal held in a magnetic suspension that reacts instantaneously to telepathic or digital commands. Equipment forged from Polymatter possesses the **"Morphic"** trait, allowing it to reshape as a Free Action into complex mechanical forms, such as shifting from a sensory scanner into a lethal weapon. Polymatter also utilizes Base Substance Exchange, integrating ambient foreign matter (rock, steel scrap) directly into its matrix to increase its mass or repair structural damage.  
* **Holophotonics:** Holophotonics, developed by highly advanced entities, consist of programmable hard-light constructs. Unlike visual illusions, these are tactile, functional photons capable of exerting kinetic force, bearing immense weight, and cutting through steel. Holophotonic gear grants the **"Weightless"** trait. The physical emitter is often the size of a piece of Fine jewelry (0 Sockets), carrying zero bulk when inactive, but projecting full functional mass when triggered. (Base Cost Modifier: \+10 DC)

### **Faction-Specific Design Philosophies**

**The Syndicate (Corporate Utopia / High-Tech Sanctum):**

* **Aesthetic:** "The Apple Store Cathedral." Designs are hyper-efficient, seamless, and sterile. Materials are matte white composites, brushed aluminum, and holographic interfaces.  
* **Philosophy:** Technical Facilitation. Vehicles are integrated into the "Mesh" network. Citizens in good standing have optimum capabilities of the system. For non-citizens the features are often subscription-based; with failure to tithe resulting in "Penance Mode" (reduced functionality).  
* **Signature:** Hexagonal tiling, cyan data-streams, anti-gravity propulsion, expensive, clean and modular construction.

**The Impyrium (Stagnant Grandeur / Heirloom Industrial):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Celestial Neo-Sumerian." Massive, brutalist structures clad in gold filigree and polished white marble. Vehicles look solid and imposing.  
* **Philosophy:** Enduring Dominance. Technology is maintained by ritual as much as technical understanding. Gear relies on "Relic Sockets" that accept only ancient, authorized archeotech components, rejecting modern/alien modifications.  
* **Signature:** Solar motifs (8 pointed star), heavy physical plating, slow but unstoppable momentum.

**The Dracon Dynasty (Feudal Protection / Warmth):**

* **Aesthetic:** "High-Fantasy Industrial." Heavy, blocky silhouettes reminiscent of castles or keeps. Warm lighting (orange/gold), tapestries in cockpits, and heraldic crests.  
* **Philosophy:** Noblesse Oblige. Vehicles are built to protect the pilot and project authority. They prioritize armor and durability over speed.  
* **Signature:** Dragon-inspired motifs (scales, crests), heavy kinetic/thermal weaponry, and "Void Castle" ship designs that prioritize structural integrity.

**The Ascendancy (Enlightened Innovation / Solar Renaissance):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Smart City Solarpunk." Clean curves, glass canopies, and white hulls with blue energy accents. A hopeful, forward-looking design language.  
* **Philosophy:** Adaptive Progress. Unlike the Impyrium, they innovate rapidly. Their tech is user-friendly, heavily shielded, and designed for agility and precision.  
* **Signature:** Variable-geometry wings, high-efficiency ion drives, and non-lethal weapon options integrated alongside lethal ones.

**The Coalition & Outworlds (Used Future / Scrappy Industrial):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Space Western Noir." Scavenged parts, exposed wiring, welded patch-jobs, and hazard stripes. Nothing is new; everything is repurposed.  
* **Philosophy:** Survival Pragmatism. If it works, fly it. Redundancy is key because maintenance is sporadic.  
* **Signature:** Asymmetrical hulls, ballistic weaponry (cheap ammo), oversized engines bolted onto frames, and "Franken-ships" made of mismatched parts.

**The Alterian Enclave (Magi-Tech Refinement / Crystal Spires):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Interstellar Art Nouveau." Impossible grace, solar sails, white ceramic fused with living wood, and crystalline matrices.  
* **Philosophy:** The Long View. Vehicles are works of art designed to last centuries. Magic is treated as a rigorous science integrated into engineering.  
* **Signature:** Silent propulsion, energy-based weaponry (Sun-Lances), and open, airy cockpit designs protected by force fields rather than glass.

**The Auluran (Symbiotic Growth / Bioluminescent Jungle):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Living Tech." Ships and mecha are grown, not built. Chitinous hulls, vascular corridors, and bioluminescent lighting.  
* **Philosophy:** Biological Imperative. The vehicle is a living organism bonded to the pilot. It heals damage (Regeneration) rather than requiring repair.  
* **Signature:** No hard angles, fluid/organic shapes, spore/acid-based weaponry, and neural-link control interfaces that require biological compatibility.

**The Mekan (Geometric Logic / Pure Code):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Fractal Perfection." Perfect geometric shapes (spheres, tetrahedrons), floating components held by magnetism, and shifting "polymatter" surfaces.  
* **Philosophy:** The Code. Form follows function instantaneously. A vehicle might reconfigure its entire shape mid-combat to adapt to a threat.  
* **Signature:** Liquid metal hulls, hard-light projections, and "Swarm" tactics where many small units act as a single large entity.

**The Entari Combine (Cosmopolitan Trade / Eco-Luxury):**

* **Aesthetic:** "Galactic Senate." A blend of the best features from other factions, smoothed over with a luxury finish. High-end materials, comfortable interiors, and non-threatening silhouettes.  
* **Philosophy:** Ethical Efficiency. Technology designed for trade, diplomacy, and comfort. High reliability and low emissions.  
* **Signature:** Universal docking rings, advanced translation/comms suites, and concealed weaponry (peaceful appearance, deadly defense).

**The Thorn (Primal Growth / Rooted Defense):**

* **Aesthetic:** Bark-plate, vine-wrapped tech, glowing chlorophyll veins.  
* **Philosophy:** Primal Growth. Vehicles and mecha are highly durable, rough, and naturally camouflaged with their native environments.  
* **Signature:** Rooted stabilizers (+2 to resist Trip/Bull Rush), woodland camouflage capabilities.

**The Kitin (Hive-Resin / Swarm Logic):**

* **Aesthetic:** Hive-resin composites, organic structures, green vein-piping.  
* **Philosophy:** The Swarm. Their engineering relies on incredibly rigid yet remarkably lightweight organic construction.  
* **Signature:** Secreted self-sealing resin, telepathic hive-mind communication integration, acoustically absorbent materials for stealth.

### **THE UNIVERSAL SCALE: MODULES VS. MOUNTS**

To ensure absolute mathematical consistency across personal inventory, vehicular engineering, and architectural design, the matrix relies upon the **Universal Displacement Unit (UDU)** hierarchy. When designing architecture, ships, or planetary macro-structures, builders must manage two distinct tiers of capacity: **Modules** and **Mounts**.

**Tier 3: The Module (Rooms & Facilities)** The **Module** is the baseline unit of macro-scale structural volume (representing roughly 400 square feet of functional space, or a 20x20 ft area, up to a Maximum 10 Tons displacement). In architectural terms, a Module represents a physical room or a massive internal facility—such as a generator room, a barracks, a medical bay, or a vehicle hangar. A building's total Size and Height determine how many Modules it can hold.

**Tier 2: The Mount (Hardpoints & Sub-Scale Gear)** The **Mount** is a sub-scale hardpoint (Maximum 100 kg displacement). In architectural and capital-ship design, Mounts are used for functional components that do not require an entire room. This includes exterior structural armor plating, energy shield emitters, defense turrets, sensor dishes, and localized utility nodes.

**The 10:1 Integration Rule** The UDU hierarchy relies on a strict 10:1 conversion ratio. **1 Module equals 10 Usable Mounts.** When constructing a base, an architect can dedicate a full Module to a room, or they can partition that Module into 10 external or internal Mounts to install heavy defenses, armor, and specialized gear.

| Scale Category | Architectural Application | Integration Bottleneck |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Tier 0: Nodes | Implant scale, biology. | 10 Usable Nodes \= 1 Socket |
| Tier 1: Socket | Personal gear, handheld items. | — |
| Tier 2: Mount | Hardpoints: Armor, Weapons, Shields, Sensors. | 1 Mount \= 10 Sockets |
| Tier 3: Module | Facilities: Rooms, Hangars, Cores, Barracks. | 1 Module \= 10 Mounts |

#### **Integration Rules**

* **Installing Personal Gear on Mecha:** A Vehicle/Mech can equip Personal-scale weapons or equipment. These take up negligible space. Up to 10 Sockets worth of items can be actively installed in a single 1 Mount hardpoint.  
* **Storing Personal Gear:** Up to 100 Sockets worth of items could be stored in the cargo/utility bay of 1 Mount.  
* **Garaging Mecha in Architecture:** Lower sized mecha is based on displaced mass of the Mecha vs the Module (at 10 Tons of capacity), at upper ranges (huge and larger) adapt the ratio of approximately 1 Module per Scale multiplier to garage the mecha. For large ships use the conversion of 10 Mounts to 1 Module for ease of calculation and economy of space.  
  * **Medium** (Human Size x1 Scale, \<8ft, \<500 lbs): Requires \~0.025 Modules (40 units fit in 1 Module).  
  * **Large** (X2 Scale, \<16ft, \<2 tons): Requires 0.50 Modules (2 units fit in 1 Module).  
  * **Huge** (x5 Scale, \<32ft, \<16 tons): Requires 5\* Full Modules (Hangar Bay) per unit.  
  * **Gargantuan** (x10 Scale, \<64ft, \<125 tons): Requires 10\* Full Modules (Heavy Bay) per unit.  
  * **Colossal** (x20 Scale, \<128ft, \<1K tons): Requires 20\* Full Modules (Launch Gantry) per unit.

#### **Equipment Modification and Mastercrafting**

Equipment in Tangent is inherently modular, capable of evolving alongside the character through the utilization of UDU Capacity (Sockets for Personal Gear, Mounts for Mecha, Modules for Architecture). Weapons, armor, and utility gear possess a baseline UDU Capacity derived from their Size Category.  
UDU Capacity allows for the installation of optics, barrel attachments, biometric locks, or payload alterations. Installing a Sniper Scope consumes 1 UDU Capacity slot and increases Range Increment by 50% while penalizing short-range strikes. Installing an Under-barrel Grenade launcher consumes 2 UDU Capacity slots.

* **Standard Upgrades:** Each mundane module costs its listed UDU Capacity amount and increases the Crafting DC by \+5 (or \+10 for TL4/TL5 items).  
* **Mastercrafting (Bonus Capacity):** High-quality manufacturing allows armor and weapons to exceed standard limits. For every 5 points an Acquisition or Crafting check exceeds the item's base DC, the item gains 1 Bonus UDU Slot (up to a maximum of 1 per TL of the item).

### 

### ---

**UDU Compression:** If a build requires more capacity than physically available, advanced engineering can compress the hardware at a severe premium.

* **Efficient Design:** \-25% UDU Capacity cost (Round up). Adds \+5 DC.  
* **Miniaturized:** \-50% UDU Capacity cost (Round up). Requires Tech Level \+1 over item base. Adds \+10 DC.  
* **Integrated:** 0 UDU Capacity (Fused to chassis). Cannot be removed. Adds \+5 DC.

### **The Mecha Forge Protocol (Instructional Guide)**

This section details the mechanical rules and procedural steps for constructing Mecha, Vehicles, Drones, and Starships. This protocol ensures that complexity directly correlates to financial value, build time, and battlefield capability.  
**Instructional Parameters:**

* **Who:** Game Masters constructing world assets, combat encounters, and loot drops; Players engaging in mechanical engineering, downtime crafting, or commissioning new builds.  
* **What:** A rigorous, sequential design matrix that explicitly links physical size, technological complexity, and tactical power to the Wealth Score (WS) requirements and Build Time parameters.  
* **Where/When:** Utilized during pre-game preparation, world-building phases, or at the crafting table during extended downtime sessions. Must be completed before a vehicle can be fielded in play.  
* **Why:** To ensure that all vehicular and macro-mobility assets remain mathematically viable, economically balanced, and narratively consistent within the Tangent Standard Economic Curve, preventing ludonarrative dissonance where heavy capital ships cost less than advanced sniper rifles.  
* **How:** Execute the following step-by-step construction protocol.

####

The Base Market DC represents the Crafting DC (and Wealth Score requirement) for a bare chassis of this size at Tech Level 3\.  
*Instructional Execution:* Select the desired size from the table below. Note the Scale Multiplier, as this will multiply the damage and range of all weapons equipped to this chassis.

#### 

#### **Step 2: Define Body Type & Configuration**

The physical form of the chassis dictates its utility, its Handling Modifier (crucial for Piloting checks and Initiative), and its Complexity Modifier (which is added to the Base Market DC).  
*Instructional Execution:* Select a Frame Type. Add the Complexity DC to your running total, and record the Handling Mod, which will be used in vehicular combat.

| FRAME | EXAMPLES | HANDLING MOD | COMPLEXITY |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Creature | Minibots, Drones, Biomimetics | \+2 | \+5 DC |
| Humanoid | Remote Bodies, Power Armor, Mecha | \+0 | \+8 DC |
| Industrial | Construction Bots, Cargo Haulers | \-2 | \-2 DC |
| Personal | Hoverboards, Bikes, Cars | \+0 | \+0 DC |
| Platform | Tanks, Buses, Boats, Stations | \-4 | \+2 DC |
| Racing | Interceptors, Speeders | \+4 | \+5 DC |
| Walker | Heavy Mecha, Spider Tanks | \-2 | \+5 DC |
| Winged | Fighters, Shuttles, Ornithopters | \+2 | \+2 DC |

*Valuation Example:* A Medium Humanoid Power Armor (TL 3). Calculation: Medium Base (DC 22\) \+ Humanoid (DC \+8) \+ TL3 (DC \+0) \= Final Base DC 30\. It possesses \+0 Handling.

#### 

#### **Step 3: Implement Core Chassis Configuration (Free Systems)**

To ensure the Mounts listed above are available for Payload/Weaponry, specific mandatory systems are considered integral to the chassis and DO NOT consume your Mount limit.  
*Instructional Execution:* Define the narrative source of the following free systems based on the Tech Level chosen:

* **Primary Engine:** Power core appropriate for Tech Level/Size (e.g., Combustion for TL2, Fusion for TL3).  
* **Basic Locomotion:** Standard movement type (Treads, Legs, Hover) providing base speed.  
* **Control System:** Cockpit (Pilot) or Computer Core (Remote/Automata).

#### 

#### **Step 4: Execute System Integration and Mount Allocation**

Allocate functional systems, weapons, and armor into the remaining chassis Mounts.  
*Instructional Execution:* Select components from Sections 1.1 through 2.4. Subtract their Mount cost from your chassis total. Add their individual DC modifiers to your running total if upgrading.

* **Weaponry Standard:** 1 Mount accepts 1 Weapon of the host's Size Category.  
* **Weaponry Battery:** 1 Mount can house 2 Weapons (1 size smaller) or 4 Weapons (2 sizes smaller).  
* **Spinal Weapons:** Requires All Available Mounts for an upscaled weapon, next size scale (Min. 4 Mounts).  
* **Logistics:** 1 Mount \= 100kg of dedicated cargo storage.  
* **Crew Capacity Rule:** A vehicle requires 1 Crew Station (1 Mount) per Tier of Scale above Medium to operate efficiently (Large \= 2 Crew, Huge \= 5 Crew, Gargantuan \= 10 Crew). This requirement can be mitigated by installing advanced AI Targeting/Piloting Computers.

#### 

#### **Step 5: Armor Layering & Defense Calculation**

Armor provides Damage Resistance (DR) but must be scaled to the size of the Mecha.  
*Instructional Execution:* Choose an Armor Plating type (Section 1.2). Multiply its base Mount Cost by your Mecha's Scale Modifier. Subtract this from your total Mounts. Then, calculate your vehicle's Defense.

* **Defense (DC) Formula:** Base 10 \+ Pilot's Agility Modifier \+ Vehicle Combat Mod (from Size) \+ Vehicle Handling Mod (from Body Type).

#### 

#### **Step 6: Finalization & The Productivity Engine (Economics)**

Sum the final DC of the chassis and all applied systems. Calculate the final Credit Value and Build Time.  
*Instructional Execution:* \* **Value Calculation:** Plug the Final DC into the formula: Value (Credits) \= 10 \* 4^(DC/5). If purchasing, verify the character possesses a Wealth Score (WS) equal to or greater than the DC. If not, they must pay the liquid credit difference.

* **Build Time:** If crafting, the Target Productivity Points (PP) required to finish the vehicle equals its Credit Value. Calculate Daily Progress: Daily PP \= (Craft Check Result \- 10\) \* Tool Tier Multiplier. Divide the Total Value by the Daily PP to find the number of days required to build the Mecha.

### **Variable Form Technology (VFT)**

Variable Form Technology allows units to reconfigure their physical chassis to adapt to combat conditions, specifically bridging the gap between high-mobility vehicle modes, static defensive structures, and versatile biomimetic (humanoid/animal) forms.  
**1\. VFT Tech Level Progression** The efficiency, speed, and method of these transformations are strictly gated by Tech Level (TL).

* **TL3: Mechanical Articulation (The "Hard" Shift):** Hydraulic actuators and sliding rails. Slow (Full Round Action), vulnerable during shift.  
* **TL4: Programmable Matter (The "Fluid" Shift):** "Smart-Matter" composites. Rapid (Movement Action), can occur mid-maneuver.  
* **TL5: Polymatter & Holophotonics (The "State" Shift):** Sub-atomic rearrangement or hard-light projection. Near Instantaneous (Swift Action).

**2\. Alternate Form Classifications**

* **Form Type A: Structural Integration (Bastion Mode):** Transformation from a mobile vehicle/mech into a static defensive emplacement. \+100% Armor, \-100% Speed.  
* **Form Type B: Biomimetic Configuration (Mecha/Beast Mode):** Transformation from a vehicle into a humanoid or animalistic form for terrain traversal. Gains Melee, Dodge/Evasion, \-50% Armor.  
* **Form Type C: Vehicular Configuration (Interceptor Mode):** Transformation into a streamlined vehicle for rapid transit. Optimized for Speed and Momentum.

**3\. VFT Design & Cost Protocols**

* **Multi-Form Efficiency:** 10% reduction of total available mounts for each EXTRA FORM.  
* **Differential Costing:** The most expensive design is the foundation price. ALL forms and functions prices are totaled for each form separately for credit value, apply a 20% reduction to the cost of additional forms and then summarize the values for WS total.

### **Meta-Tech Integration: Synthesizing Science and Sorcery**

In the Tangent Universe, the divide between science and sorcery is treated purely as a matter of origin, not effect. Meta-Tech represents the engineering necessary to accommodate reality-warping patterns within physical Sockets and Mounts. This integration is governed by the Meta-Conversion capacity system, which utilizes the Universal Displacement Unit (UDU) hierarchy. The type of UDU capacity used (Socket, Mount, or Module) dictates the Operational Scale of the effect, while the Power Level is determined by the Skill Rank installed during creation.

| PHYSICAL UNIT | OPERATIONAL SCALE | CAPACITY LIMIT FOR INVOCATIONS |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 Socket | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 10 (Trained Effect) |
| 2 Sockets | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 20 (Master Effect) |
| 3 Sockets | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 30 (Pinnacle Effect) |
| 1 Mount | Vehicle Scale | Holds any Rank up to Pinnacle (Rank 30\) |
| 1 Module | Strategic Scale | Holds any Rank up to Pinnacle (Rank 30\) |

### 

### ---

The **Principle of Scale** dictates that an Invocation installed in a Mount is fundamentally more potent than the identical spell in a Socket. A Rank 10 Teleport installed in a personal suit (1 Socket) moves an individual 5 miles; the same Rank 10 Teleport installed in a Mecha (1 Mount) moves a 10-ton vehicle 25 miles.  
**Passive Enhancement vs. Active Imbuement** Meta-Tech fabrication requires highly specialized conductive materials—such as Aetherium for active Imbuements or Resonance Crystals for passive Enhancements—which are extremely rare and always constitute 50% of the item's exorbitant final value. It serves three distinct engineering goals:

* **Enhancement (Passive):** The application of Matter or Energy disciplines to permanently improve the physical properties of a device. These effects are "Always On" and do not require an action to activate. Examples include an Energy Sheath (adding \+1d6 elemental damage to a blade), Ghost-Strike (allowing a weapon to phase through physical DR), or Featherweight (altering local gravity to make armor count as a lighter weight category). Each UDU Slot allocated to an Enhancement increases the item's Crafting DC by \+5, effectively quadrupling its market value.  
* **Imbuement (Active):** Hard-coding a specific metaphysical Invocation (spell) into a machine, allowing a non-Awakened user to cast the effect via a physical trigger. The device's statistics become fixed constants based on the engineering quality. The Crafting DC for an Imbuement is calculated as: 15 \+ Invocation Rank \+ TL Modifier (TL3: \+0, TL4: \+2, TL5: \+5). (Example: A "Blink Suit" imbued with a Rank 10 Teleport at TL 4 occupies 1 UDU slot and possesses a Craft DC of 27).  
* **Consumable Modifier:** If a Meta-Tech module is designed for a single use (e.g., a one-time emergency planar shift that burns out the crystals), the final Meta-Tech DC is reduced by 10\.  
* **Interface (Symbiotic):** Devices that amplify an Awakened user's natural metaphysical skills. When an Awakened user channels through an Amplifier (such as an Elemental Focus Cannon taking up 1 Mount), the Invocation's output is multiplied by the Chassis Scale. A spell cast through a Huge Mecha (x5 Scale) deals five times the damage and expands its area of effect proportionately.

### **Combat & Damage Mechanics: Offensive and Defensive Hardware Systems**

The Equipment Matrix provides a unified structure for the creation, classification, and modification of weapons and armor. Moving beyond simple item lists, the matrix defines the "DNA" of combat gear—its component parts, resource requirements, and capacity for evolution through Sockets and Mounts.  
**1\. Weapon Anatomy and Scaling Mechanics** Every weapon in the Tangent system is defined by a standard Stat Block detailing its Core Identity (Name, Classification, Size Category, Tech Level), Combat Statistics (Damage, Range, Penetration), and Logistics (Ammunition, Bulk, Mounts/Sockets, Craft DC).  
A critical component of the Tangent combat engine is the replacement of arbitrary "type-based" multipliers with "size-based" logic, determined by the Scaling Modifier. This scaling system determines how a creature or object's size affects its interaction with the world:

* **Damage and Structure:** Weapon damage dice are strictly multiplied by the Scaling Modifier. A Huge mecha (x5 Scaling) deals five times the base damage dice with its weapons, and its Structure Points are multiplied by five. Importantly, no damage is arbitrarily reduced due to a target being a larger size; larger targets simply possess proportionally increased Structure Points and Damage Reduction to absorb the impact.  
* **Distance and Area:** Movement speed, weapon ranges, and area-of-effect parameters are multiplied by the Scaling Modifier.  
* **Starship Proximity Damage:** At Enormous scales (x40) and above, weapons are so overwhelmingly powerful that they utilize Proximity Damage. Overwhelming attacks from capital ships often vaporize small targets instantly and deal 1/10th indirect damage (splash effect, debris) to small targets not directly hit, within a radius equal to half the attacker's Strength Modifier in feet.  
* **Relative Combat Modifiers:** The Combat Modifier adjusts an entity's Agility, Attack, and Defense scores based on the relative size of their opponent. When a Large attacker targets a Small defender, the attacker suffers a significant penalty (subtracting the target's modifier from their own), reflecting the difficulty of hitting a nimble, diminutive target with massive weaponry. Conversely, when a Tiny attacker targets a Small defender, they gain a moderate advantage.

**2\. Targeting Sub-Systems (Called Shots)**

* **Penalty:** \-5 Strike.  
* **Threshold:** \> 10% of Vehicle's Total SP in a single hit disables the system.

### **3.0 Automata Integration Protocol (Instructional Guide)**

When a Mecha is equipped with a Computer Core instead of a traditional Cockpit, it sheds the need for a biological pilot and transitions into an **Automata**. Because these vehicles operate independently, they must possess their own mechanical stat block representing their strength, logic, and operational skill sets.  
**Instructional Parameters:**

* **Who:** Game Masters designing automated encounters, combat encounters, or defensive perimeters; Players deploying autonomous drones, synthetic companions, or unmanned mecha.  
* **What:** A systematic, four-step procedure to calculate the physical and mental statistics of unmanned, AI-driven Mecha (Automata), transitioning them from static equipment into independent actors.  
* **Where/When:** Executed during the finalization phase of the Mecha Forge Protocol (Step 6), or utilized anytime a Player acquires a robotic companion.  
* **Why:** To ensure automated units operate with mathematically consistent attributes and skills tied strictly to their chassis size and processor rating, preventing arbitrary stat blocks and preserving Ludonarrative Consistency.  
* **How:** Execute the following four-step generation sequence to finalize the Automata's independent stat block.

#### 

#### **Step 1: Calculate Physical Attributes (The Chassis)**

An Automata's physical capabilities are entirely dictated by the hardware of its Frame and Size Category. They do not roll for these stats; they are intrinsic constants of the engineering.

* **Strength:** Equal to the Chassis's STR/CBT MOD (from the Step 1 Size Table). *Example: A Large Automata possesses a \+2 Strength modifier.* Upgrades like Myomer Musculature (+4 Strength) are added directly to this base modifier.  
* **Agility:** Base 0 \+ Handling Mod (from the Step 2 Body Type Table) \+ any localized mobility upgrades. *Example: A Racing Drone frame possesses \+4 Agility.*  
* **Stamina:** Automata do not possess Stamina or Vitality as they are inorganic constructs. They rely entirely on their Structure Points (SP) and Damage Resistance (DR). They are immune to biological effects, poisons, disease, and fatigue.

#### 

#### **Step 2: Calculate Mental Attributes (The Processor Core)**

Automata rely on their **Processor Rating (PR)** to determine their cognitive capability and reaction times. The PR is strictly gated by the Tech Level of the installed Computer Core (as defined in the Computational Assets ecosystem).

* **TL 2 (Basic Logic):** PR 0\. Intellect 0, Wisdom \-2, Charisma \-4. *Cannot adapt to unknown variables.*  
* **TL 3 (Algorithmic AI):** PR 1\. Intellect \+1, Wisdom \-1, Charisma \-2. *Standard trade and security drones.*  
* **TL 4 (Heuristic AI):** PR 2\. Intellect \+2, Wisdom 0, Charisma 0\. *Advanced combat automata capable of predictive tactics.*  
* **TL 5 (Persona AI):** PR 4\. Intellect \+4, Wisdom \+2, Charisma \+4 (Possesses a fully simulated, charming personality overlay).

#### 

#### **Step 3: Allocate Skill Matrices (Software)**

Automata do not possess natural life experiences; their skills are hard-coded into their logic matrices via Expert Software and Operation Software.

* **Base Skill Ranks:** An Automata receives a total number of Skill Ranks equal to (PR \+ 1\) \* 5. These are typically allocated primarily to Combat, Piloting, and Alertness.  
* **Hard-Coded Limits:** An Automata cannot possess a Skill Rank higher than PR \* 3 (Minimum cap of 5 for PR 0 and PR 1 drones).  
* **Targeting Computers:** If a specific Targeting Computer module is installed onto the chassis (e.g., Basic \+1, AI \+2), this bonus acts as an Equipment Bonus applied *on top* of their base calculated Attack skill.

#### 

#### **Step 4: Establish Action Economy & Initiative**

Automata roll their own Initiative and operate independently in the combat order, functioning identically to an NPC.

* **Initiative Formula:** d20 \+ Agility Mod \+ Alertness Skill \+ PR. (The PR represents their literal processing speed and ability to prioritize threats in milliseconds).  
* **Operational Directives:** Semi-autonomous Automata must be given commands (requiring a Move Action from a controller via a comm-link) to change their primary directive. Fully autonomous units follow their programmed threat-assessment matrices without the need for external input.

### **Specialized Gear and Utility Ecosystems**

Beyond combat and architecture, the Equipment Matrix codifies the rules for exploration, survival, and computational problem-solving, ensuring that all gear interfaces seamlessly with core gameplay loops.

**Advanced Grown Technologies (Biotech Protocols)** Factions utilizing grown biotechnology (like the Auluran and Thorn) subvert traditional battery mechanics, requiring distinct operational rules. Grown tech is subject to three core protocols:

* **Symbiosis (Bonding):** Living gear must genetically and bio-electrically "bond" with a host over a 24-hour attunement period. Failure to attune results in a persistent \-2 operational penalty across all checks.  
* **Sustenance (Feeding):** Grown technology cannot be recharged via a power grid; it requires nutrient consumption. Minor items (Diminutive/Tiny size) feed harmlessly on the user's waste heat and dead skin flakes. Heavy weapons and armor must be fully submerged in a Nutrient Solution (costing 10 Cr per dose) once per week, lest they degrade and gain the "Broken" (Starving) condition.  
* **Bio-Repair:** Damage to living gear is healed via Medicine or Xenobiology checks rather than Engineering. A healthy host naturally facilitates the automatic regeneration of 1 Structure Point per hour for their bonded equipment.

**Specific Factional Innovations:**

* **Empathic Carapace (Auluran):** Reactive armor that adapts when struck by energy (Fire/Cold/Elec), granting DR \+5 against that specific energy type for the remainder of the Scene.  
* **Chorist Node (Auluran):** A grown neural-graft transceiver. Allows Telepathy (1 mile radius) with other Node users and grants Advantage on Empathy checks.  
* **Sunglass Laminates (Alterian):** Crystalline structures grown from silica and light. Acts as a solar capacitor, recharging energy weapons automatically in sunlight.  
* **Hive-Resin (Kitin):** A secreted construction material lighter than plasteel but self-sealing. Structures grant \+2 to Stealth (sound dampening) inside them.

**Programmable Matter and Utility Devices** Beyond the organic growth of the Auluran, high-tech factions utilize advanced material science for everyday utility. **Programmable Matter**, often referred to as "Picotech" (TL 4, DC 25), consists of a canister of smart sand or fluid metal that can be commanded to reshape into any Simple tool, requiring user instruction to shift forms. Visual utility is provided by **Image Projectors**, which scale from TL 2 (DC 5\) units projecting 2D images with sound, up to TL 3 (DC 10\) Holo Emitters capable of projecting localized 3D holograms.

**Computational Assets and Software Systems** Computers and digital decks are defined by their Processor Rating (PR). A **Basic Processor** (TL 3, DC 10\) operates at PR 0, capable only of simple calculations and data storage. **Advanced Processors** (TL 5, DC 25\) reach PR 4 and can host robust Persona AIs, possessing pseudo-intelligence and a Charisma score equal to their PR. Computers run specialized software to aid users:

* **Expert Software:** (Ranging from DC 10 to DC 30\) Grants a direct \+1 to \+5 Aid Bonus to specific Skill checks.  
* **Operation Software:** Allows a user to automatically "Take 10" on specific skill checks when the software is active, critically reducing the risk of catastrophic failure during routine high-stakes operations, such as orbital astrogation or systemic hacking.

**Surveillance and Communications** Information gathering and transmission are categorized into three distinct operational spheres:

* **Passive Sensors:** Range from simple TL 2 (DC 5\) Rangefinders and Cameras to TL 3 (DC 10\) Holo-Cams.  
* **Comm Units:** Dictate connection scale: Audio/Visual radios (TL 2, DC 5\) handle standard comms, Holographic transceivers (TL 3, DC 10\) project 3D representations, and Telepathic arrays (TL 4/5, DC 25\) allow for planetary-scale communication requiring the Telepathy skill.  
* **Counter-Intel:** To protect this data, gear includes TL 2 (DC 10\) Scrambler Units and advanced TL 4 (DC 20\) Encryption Suites.

**Vocation Tools and Facilities** Vocation tools provide direct Equipment Bonuses to relevant skill checks, scaling rapidly with the facility's volumetric footprint and the number of users supported:

* **Basic Tools:** A portable belt or pack (DC 5\) that requires Trained skill but offers no intrinsic bonus, supporting 1 user.  
* **Full Set:** A semi-portable caddy (DC 10\) requiring Trained skill, granting a \+2 Equipment Bonus and supporting 2 users.  
* **Shop/Lab:** A dedicated room (DC 20\) requiring Expert skill, granting a \+4 Equipment Bonus and supporting 5 users.  
* **Facility:** A building or campus (DC 30\) requiring Master skill, granting a \+6 Equipment Bonus and supporting 10 or more users.

**Specific tool systems integrate directly into this hierarchy:**

* **Diagnostics Systems & Databases:** Grant scaling bonuses (+2 at TL 3, \+4 at TL 4, and \+6 at TL 5\) to Repair and Knowledge checks respectively.  
* **Multi-Tools:** Replicate non-powered tools at TL 3 (DC 10), powered tools at TL 4 (DC 15, \+2 Bonus), and become fully polymorphic at TL 5 (DC 25, \+4 Bonus).  
* **Medical Tools:** Follow exact scaling rules. A Field Surgery Pack acts as a basic kit (Tiny, DC 10\) granting a \+2 Equipment Bonus to Medicine. A Portable Nurse is a TL 3 unit running Expert Software acting as an automated secondary aid. An Autodoc is a TL 4 (DC 20\) autonomous robotic surgeon capable of running Operation Software independently. Operating Theaters are Medium-sized room installations (DC 20\) granting a \+4 Equipment Bonus. A Medical Lab requires a Facility-sized architectural Module (DC 30), granting a \+6 Equipment Bonus and supporting multiple trauma patients simultaneously.

### **Economic Integration: The Tangent Standard Economics**

The most significant advancement in the Tangent Matrix is the complete eradication of economic dissonance—the historically persistent fracture between the narrative abstraction of wealth and the mechanical reality of crafting and buying items. In legacy RPG systems, the cost to craft an item is derived from its market price, which is often assigned arbitrarily based on perceived combat utility rather than engineering complexity. This results in linear pricing models that fail catastrophically in science fiction settings; a starship is not merely a very large rifle, it represents an order-of-magnitude leap in manufacturing difficulty.  
The Tangent Standard Economics posits a singular, mathematically rigorous solution: **Complexity Determines Value.** The market price of an item is simply the societal aggregate of the skill (Crafting DC) and time required to produce it.

**1\. The Tangent Standard Curve (TSC)** Because the gap between a simple survival tool and a dimensional jump-gate is logarithmic, the cost scaling must be exponential. The market price of every physical asset within the Tangent galaxy is generated using the Tangent Standard Curve (TSC) formula. This formula dictates that an item's value in Credits equals a baseline value of 10 Credits (representing the simplest manufactured good or scrap metal at DC 0\) multiplied by a growth factor of 4, raised to the power of the item's Crafting DC divided by 5\. The growth factor of 4 ensures that value inherently quadruples across every tier interval of 5 DC.  
Value (Credits) \= 10 \* 4^(DC/5)  
By strictly adhering to this calculation, all pricing debates are eliminated. This mathematical rigidity allows Game Masters to value any new, homebrew, or precursor artifact instantly simply by assigning it a Crafting DC based on its operational complexity.

**2\. Wealth Score and Purchasing Power** Character economic power is quantified by the Wealth Score (WS), a static rating of economic leverage representing credit rating, active investments, salary, and social capital. The '99 \- AUGMENTATIONS MATRIX' utilizes Build Points (BP) for biological tolerance; the Equipment Matrix utilizes Wealth Score for material acquisition, translating a character's intrinsic potential into extrinsic economic power.  
The integration of WS and the TSC operates on **The Golden Rule of Tangent Wealth:** A character may automatically purchase any item with a Crafting DC equal to or less than their Wealth Score without depleting liquid Credits or reducing their baseline Wealth Score. This aligns the abstraction of "lifestyle" with the concrete math of item acquisition: Purchase DC \= Crafting DC.

**3\. The Liquidity Gap and Friction Mechanics** A common failure point in abstract wealth systems is the "infinite money loop," where players attempt to use their passive Wealth Score to acquire high-value items for free and immediately sell them for infinite cash. Tangent mitigates this via strict friction mechanics, ensuring "Loot" (liquid Credits) remains a vital gameplay reward at all tiers.

**The Liquidity Constraint (The Gap Rule):** When a character seeks an item with a DC exceeding their Wealth Score, their passive income cannot cover the transaction. They must bridge the gap with liquid Credits. The required liquid cost is calculated by subtracting the Credit value of the character's Wealth Score from the Credit value of the desired item's DC. Formula: Cost (Liquid) \= Value (Item DC) \- Value (Wealth Score) Example: A Wealth Score 15 character (Auto-buy limit \~640 Cr) wants a Stealth Suit (DC 18, Value \~1,470 Cr). They use their WS 15 to cover the base lifestyle cost, leaving 830 Credits that must be paid in cash from their adventuring savings.

**Liquidity Drag (The Fence Rate):** To further prevent market abuse, the economy imposes Liquidity Drag. In real-world economics and RPG simulations, the "Buy" price and "Sell" price are never identical due to fencing effort, market saturation, and legality. The Sell Price of an item is heavily reduced:

* **Legal goods:** Sell at 50% of Total Value.  
* **Black market or stolen goods:** Sell at 20-25% of Total Value.  
* **Scrap:** Sells at 10% of Total Value.

Because the raw materials required to craft an item also cost 50% of the item's Total Value, a character who buys materials and crafts an item for immediate resale operates at a 0% profit margin. Profit is only possible through adventuring, scavenging materials for free, or utilizing specialized merchant skills.

**4\. The Productivity Engine and Macro-Economics** Because the Tangent Standard Curve dictates that value scales exponentially, a linear crafting system (e.g., generating 50 credits of value per day) would require literal centuries to build high-end assets, ending campaigns before items are finished. Tangent solves the "Time vs. Cost" paradox through the **Productivity Engine**, converting effort into Productivity Points (PP).

**The Productivity Formula:** To craft an item, the creator must accumulate a Target PP equal exactly to the item's Credit Value. Daily progress is generated based on the crafter's skill variance and the tier of their infrastructure. A creator's Daily Progress in Productivity Points is calculated by taking their Craft Check Result, subtracting 10, and multiplying that number by the tool's Tier Multiplier.  
Daily Progress (PP) \= (Craft Check Result \- 10\) \* Tool Tier Multiplier  
The Tool Tier Multiplier is the critical variable that allows the timeline to scale exponentially alongside the item's value:

| TOOL TIER | MULTIPLIER | DESCRIPTION & EXAMPLES |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Tier 0 | x1 | **Improvised:** Bare hands, stone tools. |
| Tier 1 | x10 | **Basic:** Handheld power tools, garage kit. |
| Tier 2 | x50 | **Advanced:** Professional machine shop. |
| Tier 3 | x200 | **Industrial:** Automated factory lines. |
| Tier 4 | x1,000 | **Nanoforge:** Molecular assembly swarms. |
| Bio | x1,000 | **Cultivation:** Hyper-Growth Vats. Requires Medicine/Nature and Engineering checks. |
| Tier 5 | x5,000 | **Genesis:** Thought-responsive Polymatter loom, Holophotonics or metaphysical fabrication. |

Under this engine, a Mastercraft Titan Mech Suit (DC 30, Target PP \= 40,960) would take a hobbyist with Basic Tools (x10) over 409 days to build. However, an advanced engineering character utilizing a Nanoforge (x1,000) with a high check result can materialize the identical Mech Suit in less than two days.

**Macro-Scale Construction and Faction Labor Pools:**  
For astronomical projects like a Dreadnought (DC 50, Value \~10.5 Million Cr) or a Titanic Arcology (DC 60+, Value \~167 Million Cr), individual crafting is physically impossible. Factions utilize Labor Pools to execute macro-scale construction. The Daily PP output is simply the sum of individual workers.  
A shipyard employing 1,000 engineers operating Industrial (x200) tools can generate 1,000,000 PP per day, completing a capital ship in roughly 10.5 days. This macro-economic logic drives factional warfare; maintaining these shipyards requires an uninterrupted flow of massive liquid capital (5.25 Million Cr in raw materials per ship), forcing factions to fight for territorial resources to keep their production engines fed.

### **Standardization Protocols: Vehicle Entry Format**

When committing a new vehicle design to the master lore compendium or a character sheet, utilize the following format block for uniformity:

**Name:** Model Name / Designation  
**Classification:** e.g., Ground Transport, Aero-Fighter  
**Tech/Meta Level:** 0-5  
**Faction/Origin:** e.g., Syndicate, Dynasty  
**Cost/Wealth:** Crafting DC / Credit Value

**CORE STATISTICS**  
**Size Category:** Medium to Titanic  
**Scale:** x1 to x80  
**Structure (SP):** Total Structure Points (If reduced to 50% SP, roll on System Failure Table)  
**Mounts:** Total Capacity  
**Damage Resistance (DR):** Value  
**Defense (DC):** Base 10 \+ Pilot Agility Modifier \+ Vehicle Combat Mod (Scale) \+ Handling Mod (Body Type)

**MOVEMENT & PROPULSION**  
**Primary Mode:** Type  
**Engine Type:** Type  
**Speed:** Tactical / Travel  
**Handling/Maneuverability:** Modifier value applied to Initiative & Pilot Checks

**CREW & CAPACITY**  
**Pilot:** Required Mounts  
**Crew/Gunners:** Additional Stations (Minimum \= Scale Multiplier, unless AI equipped)  
**Passengers/Cargo:** Mounts Allocated

**SYSTEMS & LOADOUT**  
**Mounts:** Location  
**Weapon:** Name (Scale: Mecha/Personal, Dmg: X)  
**Defensive Systems:** Shields, Smoke  
**Utility/Sensors:** Radar, Drills

**AESTHETICS & VISUALS**  
**The Structure:** Silhouette  
**The Motif:** Theme  
**Texture/Material:** Surface detail  
**Lighting/Atmosphere:** Mood
