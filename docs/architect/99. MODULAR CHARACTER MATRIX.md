# **MODULAR CHARACTER MATRIX (MCM)**

# **UNIFIED PROTOCOLS FOR NPCS**

### **PHASE I: COMPREHENSIVE CONSTRUCTION PROTOCOL**

#### **1\. INTRODUCTION: THE MCM PHILOSOPHY**

In the architecture of tabletop role-playing games, the non-player character (NPC) serves as the primary interface between the player and the fictional world. Whether functioning as a monolithic antagonist, a disposable minion, a powerful political neutral, or a cherished ally, the efficacy of a character is determined by the depth of their internal logic and the specificity of their external presentation. The distinction between these roles is not a matter of physics, but of narrative alignment. All characters, regardless of their allegiance to the players, exist within the exact same mechanical reality and utilize the same scaling systems.  
To maintain Ludonarrative Consistency, this framework utilizes the Modular Character Matrix (MCM). This schema generates high-fidelity characters across multiple strata by synthesizing archetypes, lifepath methodologies, and motivational psychology into a modular "Stack" approach:

   **TIER CHASSIS (Power Level)** \[REFERENCE MODULE A: THE TIER CHASSIS\]  
**\+ DESIGNATION (Allegiance Rules)** \[REFERENCE MODULE B: COMPETENCY ROLES & MATRICES\]  
**\+ COMPONENTS (Species/Role)** \[REFERENCE MODULE C: SPECIES & BIOLOGY\]  
**\+ NARRATIVE (Soul)** \[REFERENCE MODULE D: NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE\], \[REFERENCE MODULE E: GENRE & SETTING MODULES\] and \[REFERENCE MODULE F: ASSETS, CYBERNETICS, & VISUAL SEMIOTICS\]  
**\= NPC** *\[For a full mechanical stat block Modules A, B and C will be used with Module D, E and F supplying the narrative content / flavor of the character.\]*

#### 

#### **2\. INSTRUCTIONAL WORKFLOW (THE CONSTRUCTION PROTOCOL)**

Follow this rigorous, step-by-step procedure to construct any non-player character, from a backwater scavenger to a Galactic Emperor.

**STEP 1: DEFINE CONCEPT & DESIGNATION**  
The absolute first step before calculating any numbers or mechanics is determining the narrative allegiance and mechanical function of the character in the game. Undertaken by the Game Master (GM) or the Player (if creating a Companion), this choice dictates how the character interacts with the core ruleset, preventing the over-engineering of simple background characters while ensuring major players have robust stats. These decisions are documented on the Character Stat Block. To begin, select one of four core Designations:

* **Designation A: ADVERSARY (Enemy):** Built to actively oppose the players. Utilizes the full combat mechanics of their assigned Tier.  
* **Designation B: ALLY (Independent Supporter):** Built to aid the players autonomously. Utilizes the full mechanics of their assigned Tier, but operates independently of player action economy.  
* **Designation C: COMPANION (Player-Bound):** A specific sub-set of Ally bound directly to a Player Character via a purchased Feature. Restricted strictly by a Build Point (BP) budget starting at 40 BP.  
* **Designation D: NEUTRAL (Civilian/Bystander):** Characters not actively engaged in the central conflict. While they possess a Tier Level dictating their professional competence, they are managed via simplified Narrative Blocks until violence is initiated.

**STEP 2: SELECT THE CHASSIS (POWER LEVEL)**  
Immediately after selecting the Designation, the Game Master assigns a Threat Tier (0 to 20\) to establish the character's baseline competence, health, skill modifiers, and action economy. This step provides the mathematical skeleton, ensuring that a Tier 6 Allied soldier is mechanically balanced against a Tier 6 Adversary creature. Consult **Reference Module A: The Tier Chassis** to apply the baseline numbers.  
*(Note: Companions do not use arbitrary GM Tiers; they scale strictly via Player BP investment).*

**STEP 3: APPLY COMPONENT PACKAGES**  
After the base numbers of the Chassis are recorded, the Game Master layers specific occupational and biological traits onto the base Chassis. This differentiates characters mechanically, ensuring a Tier 4 "Sniper" functions completely differently from a Tier 4 "Tank." Consult **Reference Module B (Competency Roles)** and **Reference Module C (Species & Biology)** to apply these traits:

1. **Apply Role Package:** Choose a specific tactical job (e.g., Brute, Healer, Spy) from Reference Module B. Apply the mandated Primary Attributes, Key Skills, and Tactical Behaviors to the character's stat block.  
2. **Apply Species Package:** Add Attribute Modifiers, biological features (e.g., Natural Armor, Flight), and adjust Size Scaling from the Tangent Species Codex.

**STEP 4: REFINE NARRATIVE & PSYCHOLOGY**  
As the final step to solidify the "Soul" of the character, the Game Master establishes their motivations, history, and visual aesthetic. Mechanics without narrative are sterile; this step dictates exactly why the character fights, negotiates, or surrenders.

1. Consult **Reference Module D** to define the character's "Want" (immediate goal), "Lie" (core flaw), "Ghost" (past trauma), and behavioral quirks.  
2. Consult **Reference Module E** to roll or select their developmental Lifepath.  
3. Consult **Reference Modules F and G** to outfit them with appropriate factional aesthetics, cybernetics, and physical assets.

### **PHASE II: UNIVERSAL CHARACTER PROTOCOLS (THE RULES)**

This phase details the specific mechanics governing how the Designations scale, behave, and engage with the game world based on their Tier.

#### **1\. THE UNIVERSAL THREAT HIERARCHY (TIERS 0-20)**

All characters (except Companions) use the Tier Chassis to instantly generate high-fidelity statistics. An effective campaign utilizes a tiered hierarchy of characters to pace the narrative scale. A character's complexity scales with their assigned Tier:

* **Tiers 0-2 (Minions & Commoners):** These characters use the **1 HP Rule**. They do not track Vitality or Health. If they take any damage that bypasses their Damage Resistance (DR), they are instantly incapacitated, killed, or forced to flee. They are faceless masses used to drain player resources or provide a sense of scale. In combat, groups of Minions can be clustered to act as a single "Swarm" unit. *(Note: The 1 HP Rule supersedes all biological Size Multipliers).*  
* **Tiers 3-9 (Standards & Specialists):** Fully realized characters representing elite units, professional merchants, or skilled mercenaries. They track standard Vitality and Health pools based on their Tier and utilize tactical Role Packages (e.g., Flanking, Suppressing Fire). They are dangerous but lack grand, world-altering ambition.  
* **Tiers 10-16 (Bosses & High Lords):** Major narrative figures, faction leaders, and lethal commanders. To survive the combined action economy of a player party, any Adversary designated as a Boss automatically multiplies their total Vitality and Health pools by **x2**. They also gain access to advanced action economy mechanics:  
  * *Legendary Resilience:* If the character fails a critical Saving Throw (Fortitude/Reflex/Will), they can choose to succeed instead. (Usable 1-3 times per Day, depending on Tier).  
  * *Lair Actions:* On Initiative count 20 (losing ties), the character can trigger a massive environmental effect in their territory (e.g., venting plasma, shifting gravity, summoning guards).  
* **Tiers 17-20 (Masterminds & Cosmic Entities):** Overarching threats or god-like allies with high agency and high resources. They multiply their total Vitality and Health pools by **x3 to x5**. Rarely encountered directly until major narrative climaxes, they possess "Plot Armor" (guaranteed narrative escape mechanisms until their final confrontation) and influence the setting at a macro-scale.

#### 

#### **2\. ALLIES vs. COMPANIONS**

While both support the players, they function under entirely different mechanical constraints.

* **The Ally (Independent):** A heavily armed mercenary or a powerful planetary governor. They are controlled by the GM. They possess a full Tier scaling (e.g., a Tier 12 Commander) and operate on their own initiative count. They will leave, betray, or demand payment based on their personal motivations.  
* **The Companion (Player-Bound):** A loyal pet, familiar, or customized drone purchased via the Companion Feature. They are controlled by the Player. To ensure survivability, Companions automatically gain the Base Actions, Vit/Health Bonus, and Expected DR of the Tier Chassis that their total BP budget equates to (e.g., a 100 BP Companion automatically gains the Tier 3 baseline stats). Their BP is then spent exclusively on customizing Attributes, Skills, and Features. They act on the Player's initiative count and possess unique bond mechanics (Empathic Link, Telepathic Mind Link, Symbiosis). *Note: Companions are completely dependent on their Player for gear and do not automatically generate a Wealth Score.*

#### 

#### **3\. THE NEUTRAL PROTOCOL**

Neutrals (merchants, bureaucrats, civilians) can exist at any power level. A bartender might be Tier 0, while a Corporate CEO might be a Tier 15 Administrator.

* **The Narrative Block:** To save Game Master prep time, Neutrals do not require full combat stat blocks. They are tracked using only three metrics: Concept, Key Skill (e.g., Administration Rank 22), and Disposition (Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile).  
* **Combat Trigger & Desperate Evasion:** If forced into violence, a Neutral utilizes the full Health and Vitality pools of their assigned Tier Chassis, making high-level politicians and scholars extremely durable. However, because they lack combat training, they must roll all attacks using the Untrained (Rank 0\) mechanics (Attack Roll \= d20 \+ Attribute Mod only). While their attacks are Untrained, they retain their full Tier Action Economy exclusively for Movement, Evasion, and activating defenses. A High-Tier Neutral taking the *Total Defense* or *Flee* action may substitute their highest Vocation or Knowledge Skill Rank for their Defense Rank. This represents their ability to intuitively trigger automated defenses, utilize advanced personal shielding, or expertly navigate their home terrain to avoid strikes.

#### 

#### **4\. TACTICAL BEHAVIOR PROTOCOLS**

To translate a character's narrative motivations into actionable gameplay, the Game Master must define their tactical behavior on the battlefield. During the final encounter design phase, this profile dictates exactly how they engage players, utilize their environment, and when they choose to retreat. This prevents every enemy from fighting to the death in a generic manner, ensuring combat dynamics directly align with the character's psychological profile and threat tier.

* **The Mastermind (Tiers 17-20):** Rarely fights alone. Uses the environment, elaborate traps, and loyal minions as shields. Flees well before their life is truly threatened. Attacks from extreme range, manipulates reality, or uses proxies (drones/mind-controlled thralls).  
* **The Boss / Officer (Tiers 10-16):** Coordinates others. Actively buffs allies (shouting orders, projecting auras). Engages the strongest hero or leader directly (The Lancer). Uses high-end tactical equipment and consumables immediately, saving nothing for later.  
* **The Specialist / Henchman (Tiers 3-9):** Focuses relentlessly on specific vulnerabilities or soft targets (The Healer, The Mage). Uses specialized attacks (sniping from cover, backstabbing). Will strategically retreat if the tide turns against them to fight another day.  
* **The Minion / Swarm (Tiers 0-2):** Attacks the absolute nearest target without tactical foresight. Uses "Pack Tactics" to flank for bonuses. Morale breaks easily when their numbers dwindle or their leader falls. Dangerous only through sheer action economy.

**Expanded Tier Descriptions:**

* **Tiers 0-2 (Civilian/Adept/Militia):** Everyday populace, unaugmented wildlife, or standard grunt-level security. They possess rudimentary to basic professional training in their daily vocation but are largely ineffective in a sustained firefight against true professionals. Dangerous in groups, manageable alone.  
* **Tiers 3-5 (Trooper/Standard/Professional):** Career soldiers, experienced bounty hunters, and advanced predatory wildlife. They utilize cover, flanking maneuvers, and fully understand their weaponry. They represent the standard threat level for an engaged and active combat zone. (Note: Tier 5 is the mathematical equivalent of a starting 150 BP Player Character).  
* **Tiers 6-9 (Veteran/Specialist/Elite/Operative):** Hardened survivors, squad commanders, special forces operatives, and master martial artists. Their high attributes and Expert-level skills make them highly lethal one-on-one. They often possess Augmentations, advanced Features, and superior-tier gear.  
* **Tiers 10-13 (Champion/Warlord/Commander/General):** The threshold of Boss-level adversaries or legendary Allies. Faction generals, ancient beasts, or heavily modified cyborgs. They have vast health pools (+50 to \+65, doubled for Boss status) and specialized, devastating attacks. Their mastery of skills means they dictate the terms of engagement and rarely miss.  
* **Tiers 14-16 (Paragon/High Lord/Heroic):** Characters that can subjugate entire cities alone. Grand Masters of metaphysical disciplines, apex Dragons, or highly advanced Synthesis constructs. Beings that require an entire coordinated strike team and extensive, multi-stage planning to successfully engage.  
* **Tiers 17-19 (Legend/Ascendant/Demigod):** Myths made manifest and beings of immense, localized power. Avatars of the U'Nonn, rogue AI that have consumed planetary computing networks, or ancient figures whose very presence warps the local environment.  
* **Tier 20 (Cosmic/Mythic):** Reality-warping beings. The sleeping Ila, Cosmic horrors, or literal gods. They possess Pinnacle skills (Rank 30+) and operate completely beyond the standard laws of physics, mortality, and the battlefield.

**Deriving Stats from the Base Chassis:**

* **Skill Allocation:** The chassis provides a Primary and Secondary Skill Rank. Apply the Primary Rank to the first "Key Skill" listed in the character's chosen Role Package. Apply the Secondary Rank to all remaining Key Skills in that package.  
* **Initiative:** Agility Mod \+ Alertness Rank.  
* **Attack Bonus:** Primary Attribute Mod \+ Key Offensive Skill Rank (as dictated by Role).  
* **Defense:** 10 \+ Agility Mod \+ Defense Skill Rank \+ Armor Modifiers.  
* **Saves (Will/Fort/Ref):** 10 \+ Attribute Mod \+ Feature Bonus.  
* **Base Movement:** 30ft Groundspeed (unless modified by Species Scale, Faction traits, or Role features such as the Mobility Specialist).  
* **Wealth Score:** A character's base Wealth Score is equal to their Threat Tier, representing their escalating influence and access to superior tier gear. *Exceptions:* Companions do not generate a Wealth Score; they are completely dependent on the Player Character for equipment and liquidity. Furthermore, feral beasts, mindless constructs, and cosmic anomalies do not utilize Wealth Scores.  
* **Vitality & Health (or Structure):** All characters possess a Base Vitality of 30 and Base Health of 30\. The "Vit/Health Bonus" column is added directly to both of these base values to determine maximum pools. Synthetics and Mecha do not possess Vitality; to calculate their Structure Points (SP), combine the total values of the Vitality and Health pools (Vitality \+ Health \= SP).  
* **Size & Multiplier Stacking:** The scaling multipliers from the Scaling Framework apply universally to all life pools. For biological characters, multiply their final calculated Vitality and Health totals by their Size Scale Multiplier (e.g., Tiny x0.5, Large x2, Huge x5). *Note:* If a character possesses multiple multipliers (e.g., a Large Boss), calculate the Size Multiplier *first* to establish the physical baseline, then apply the Designation Multiplier (Boss x2 / Mastermind x3+) to that new total. (Multipliers are multiplicative).  
* **Invocation Stages:** When calculating the damage or scale of a metaphysical Invocation, convert the character's Discipline Skill Rank into Stages: Novice (Stage 1), Trained (Stage 2), Expert (Stage 3), Master (Stage 4), Grand Master (Stage 5), Pinnacle (Stage 6).  
* **Essence Economy:** Manifesting an Invocation at its base learned Stage costs 0 Essence (it is powered by ambient resonance). Pushing an Invocation to cast at a *higher* Stage than the character's current Skill Rank, or maintaining multiple sustained effects simultaneously, costs 1 Essence per additional Stage/Effect per round.

#### **REFERENCE MODULE B: COMPETENCY ROLES & MATRICES**

During the mechanical finalization of a character, Game Masters and players utilize this matrix to assign specific skill suites, attributes, and combat behaviors based on the character's designated job on the battlefield. Rather than generating exhaustive, unfocused lists, skills are strictly categorized by Narrative Function. This ensures that when an encounter begins, the character possesses the exact tools necessary to execute their role efficiently, dictating precisely how they solve problems under duress.

##### 

##### **A. COMBAT ROLES**

**The Breaker (Frontline / Heavy Assault)** These characters are the vanguard of any hostile force, designed to absorb massive amounts of trauma and break enemy lines through overwhelming physical force.

* **The Tank:** Operating as the impenetrable vanguard, the Tank utilizes massive durability to anchor the front lines and protect fragile allies. Deployed precisely when an immovable object is required to hold a vital chokepoint, they solve battlefield problems by deliberately absorbing catastrophic damage and drawing enemy fire away from high-value targets. They accomplish this by closing the distance steadily, relying almost entirely on heavy armor shielding, immense Stamina, and the Intimidate skill to dominate the physical space of the encounter.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Stamina, Strength.  
  * *Key Skills:* Defense, Intimidate, Athletics.  
  * *Feature:* Guard (Intercept attacks meant for allies).

* **The Brute:** Functioning as the overwhelming physical hammer of a squad, the Brute utilizes raw, unadulterated strength to shatter enemy fortifications and break defensive lines. Dispatched when sheer kinetic force is necessary to breach a hardened position, they operate by crashing into the enemy ranks to inflict maximum structural and physical trauma. Relying heavily on the Athletics and Melee Combat skills, they crush their opposition through relentless, close-quarters brute-force maneuvers.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Strength, Stamina.  
  * *Key Skills:* Melee Combat, Athletics.  
  * *Feature:* Rage (Take damage to deal \+2 damage).

**The Striker (Precision / Damage Dealers)** Operating on the principle of maximum lethality with minimum exposure, Strikers are the designated executioners of the battlefield, specializing in the rapid neutralization of high-value threats.

* **The Melee DPS:** Serving as the close-quarters executioner, the Melee DPS is deployed to inflict maximum, targeted lethality with surgical precision directly on the front lines. They are utilized when high-value targets must be rapidly eliminated in hand-to-hand engagements before they can react or cast. By prioritizing Agility and Precision, they leverage fast-striking Melee Combat and Acrobatics to weave through defenses, dealing devastating localized damage while remaining in constant motion.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Agility, Strength.  
  * *Key Skills:* Melee Combat, Acrobatics, Evasion.  
  * *Feature:* Sneak Attack (+1d6 damage on advantage).

* **The Ranged DPS:** Operating as the primary source of sustained suppressive and lethal fire, the Ranged DPS controls the mid-range battlefield. They are positioned behind the vanguard when a squad needs to lay down a continuous hail of ballistics or energy blasts to keep enemies pinned or eliminate them as they approach. Relying on the Ranged Combat skill, they utilize heavy or rapid-fire weaponry to systematically dismantle enemy ranks from a safe distance.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Agility, Precision.  
  * *Key Skills:* Ranged Combat, Alertness.  
  * *Feature:* Burst Attack (Reduces penalties with Auto-Fire).

* **The Sniper:** Functioning as the unseen executioner, the Sniper is deployed to surgically eliminate high-value targets from extreme distances. They operate from elevated or deeply obscured vantage points when an enemy commander or heavy weapon operator must be neutralized before an engagement even truly begins. By heavily leveraging the Stealth and Ranged Combat skills, they dictate the terms of engagement from the shadows, ensuring their targets are dead before the sound of the gunshot arrives.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Precision, Agility.  
  * *Key Skills:* Ranged Combat, Stealth, Alertness.  
  * *Feature:* Sniper (Ignore cover penalties).

**The Skirmisher (Mobility / Asymmetric Warfare)** Skirmishers are masters of asymmetric warfare and battlefield traversal, engineered to exploit gaps in enemy formations and cause absolute chaos behind the front lines.

* **The Mobility Specialist:** Acting as the master of traversal, the Mobility Specialist focuses entirely on movement and positioning. They are dispatched to exploit sudden gaps in enemy formations, divide the enemy's attention, and secure vital objectives across the map. Relying heavily on Agility, Stamina, and Acrobatics, they ensure they control the tempo and location of the engagement, utilizing sheer speed to outmaneuver slower, heavily armored foes.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Agility, Stamina.  
  * *Key Skills:* Acrobatics, Piloting, Athletics.  
  * *Feature:* Nimble Moves (Ignore difficult terrain penalties).

* **The Flank:** Operating as the tactical positioning expert, the Flank is engineered to collapse defensive lines from the sides or rear. Deployed specifically to punish enemies who overcommit to a frontal assault, they operate by circling the main engagement to strike at exposed vulnerabilities. Utilizing Evasion and Stealth, they execute devastating hit-and-run tactics, dealing high burst damage before fading away to avoid sustained, direct-fire retaliations.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Agility, Precision.  
  * *Key Skills:* Stealth, Melee/Ranged Combat, Evasion.  
  * *Feature:* Hit & Run (Move after attacking without provoking reactions).

**The Caster (Metaphysics / Reality Warping)** Harnessing the fundamental metaphysical forces of the Tangent universe, Casters bend reality to serve their tactical needs.

* **The Combative Meta-User:** Operating as esoteric artillery and a reality-warping specialist, the Combative Meta-User is brought onto the battlefield when conventional ballistics fail. Required to provide area denial, bypass physical armor, or manipulate the environment via Psionics, Arcane magic, or Primal forces, they rely on deep Intellect or Wisdom. They use the Attune skill and specific Disciplines to manifest powerful offensive Invocations, fundamentally altering the physics of the encounter.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Intellect, Wisdom, or Charisma.  
  * *Key Skills:* Attune, Discipline (Specific).  
  * *Feature:* Awakened (Access to Metaphysical Invocations).

##### 

##### **B. SUPPORT & UTILITY ROLES**

**The Commander (Leadership / Force Multiplication)** The linchpins of any organized squad, Commanders act as the tactical brain and morale anchor for their subordinates.

* **The Buffer:** Serving as the morale and enhancement center of a unit, the Buffer elevates the operational effectiveness and resilience of everyone around them. Positioned safely in the mid-line during large-scale skirmishes, they are necessary to grant substantial defensive cohesion and offensive boosts to their frontline allies. Utilizing Charisma and specific support features, they actively project auras or targeted metaphysical enhancements that ensure their squad survives overwhelming odds.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Charisma, Wisdom.  
  * *Key Skills:* Diplomacy, Attune (Entropy-Order).  
  * *Feature:* Aura of Command (Allies within 30ft gain \+1 to Hit/Save).

* **The Leader:** Functioning as the tactical brain of an operation, the Leader dictates the flow of battle and prevents squad routing under heavy fire. They are deployed at the helm of military or corporate strike teams where coordination is the defining difference between victory and slaughter. Relying on the Tactics and Leadership skills, they survey the battlefield to issue precise, calculated commands, orchestrating complex maneuvers that turn chaotic skirmishes into methodical victories.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Intellect, Charisma.  
  * *Key Skills:* Tactics, Leadership, Insight.  
  * *Feature:* Master Plan (Create bonus pools through pre-planning).

**The Controller (Sabotage / Environmental Manipulation)** Controllers manipulate the operational environment, neutralizing enemy advantages through technological or metaphysical suppression.

* **The Debuffer:** Operating as a battlefield saboteur, the Debuffer systematically neutralizes enemy advantages through targeted suppressive effects. They are deployed when confronting heavily fortified or physically superior foes, stepping in to inflict status conditions, blind sensors, or cripple movement. Utilizing specific weaponry or metaphysical curses, their mastery of disruptive tactics allows them to turn formidable adversaries into helpless, compromised targets.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Wisdom, Intellect.  
  * *Key Skills:* Intimidate, Discipline (Entropy-Chaos).  
  * *Feature:* Suppressing Fire or Analyze Weakness.

* **The Technician:** Serving as the environmental manipulator and electronic warfare expert, the Technician controls the digital and mechanical battlefield. They are dispatched against technologically advanced factions to jam communications, hack cybernetics, or lock down automated defenses. Utilizing high Intellect and their mastery of Computers and Engineering, they rewrite the operational environment, turning the enemy's own security systems and hardware against them.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Intellect, Agility.  
  * *Key Skills:* Computers, Engineering, Mechanics.  
  * *Feature:* Jamming/Hack (Disable enemy tech).

**The Face (Social / Subterfuge)** Because not all battles are fought with kinetic weapons, Face variants specialize in the warfare of information, deception, and infiltration.

* **The Socialite:** Engaging in the subtle warfare of high society and negotiation, the Socialite operates in environments where drawn weapons would guarantee immediate failure. They are dispatched into corporate galas, diplomatic summits, and affluent urban centers to secure objectives purely through charm, blackmail, and misdirection. Relying heavily on Charisma, they utilize the Bluff and Etiquette skills to bypass security, extract secrets, and negotiate favorable outcomes without ever throwing a punch.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Charisma, Intellect.  
  * *Key Skills:* Bluff, Diplomacy, Etiquette.  
  * *Feature:* Connected (Call in favors).

* **The Spy:** Functioning as the covert infiltrator and intelligence gatherer, the Spy exists to uncover that which is deeply hidden. Deployed deep behind enemy lines or within rival corporate structures, they operate to extract highly classified data or sabotage operations unnoticed. Utilizing exceptional Wisdom and Agility, they leverage Insight, Stealth, and Streetwise to effortlessly blend into hostile environments, ensuring they are entirely forgotten the moment they leave a room.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Wisdom, Agility.  
  * *Key Skills:* Stealth, Insight, Disguise/Acting.  
  * *Feature:* Veiled Threat or Assessment.

**The Medic (Restoration / Triage)** Dedicated restorative anchors engineered or trained specifically to keep their allies alive and combat-effective under extreme duress.

* **The Healer:** Serving as the metaphysical restorative anchor for any active squad, the Healer is dedicated to preserving life under catastrophic duress through the Resonance. Positioned safely behind the primary combatants, they respond instantly to knit shattered bone and flesh when allies suffer critical trauma. Utilizing high Wisdom and the Attune skill, they channel Life and Entropic energies to deploy powerful healing Invocations, miraculously pulling their team back from the brink of death.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Wisdom, Charisma.  
  * *Key Skills:* Attune, Discipline (Entropy-Order).  
  * *Feature:* Life-Weaver (Access to Metaphysical Healing Invocations).

* **The Biochemist:** Operating as the conventional medical expert and tactical anatomist, the Biochemist keeps allies combat-effective using high-tech science and stimulants. They are deployed in high-attrition, technologically reliant environments to rapidly diagnose trauma and apply critical, physical life-saving techniques. Utilizing high Intellect, they apply the Medicine and Biology skills to deploy combat stims, auto-sutures, and antivenoms, while simultaneously identifying the physiological weaknesses of their enemies.  
  * *Primary Attributes:* Intellect, Wisdom.  
  * *Key Skills:* Medicine, Biology, Academics.  
  * *Feature:* Field Surgeon (Heal allies rapidly without provoking reactions).

#### **REFERENCE MODULE C: SPECIES & BIOLOGY**

During the physiological phase of character creation, the Game Master or player must define the biological baseline of the character. By consulting the Species Catalog, they apply innate attribute modifiers, natural features, and scaling adjustments directly to the character's stat block. This ensures that the character mechanically represents their evolutionary background and possesses the innate tools—such as low-light vision, flight, or natural armor—necessary to survive in their native environments.

* **Attribute Mods:** Add Species modifiers (e.g., \+2 Str for Kongi) to the Chassis base.  
* **Features:** Add innate biological traits (e.g., Low-Light Vision, Flight, Natural Armor, Prehensile Limbs).  
* **Size:** Apply Scaling modifiers (See Scaling Framework) for Tiny, Large, Huge, etc.

#### **REFERENCE MODULE D: NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE (THE SOUL)**

During the final stages of character creation, the Game Master or narrative designer must establish the psychological and visual architecture of the character, treating the Persona as the sensory interface between the player and the fictional world. While mechanics dictate how a character fights, the Narrative Architecture dictates exactly why they are fighting, how they visually signal their threat level, and what internal logic justifies their actions. This module synthesizes archetypes, somatic markers, and motivational psychology to generate high-fidelity characters grounded in both emotional reality and mechanical utility.

**1\. Archetypal Narrative Roles**

* **High-Agency Characters (Masterminds, Officers, and Key Allies):**  
  * **The Architect:** This archetype represents the intellectual who plays the "long game." Visually characterized by meticulous grooming and pristine environments of data or schematics. They speak in absolutes, rarely raising their voice, signaling that power derives from control rather than physical force. Their attire reflects high status—pristine suits in sci-fi or elaborate robes in fantasy—suggesting they are removed from the dirty work.  
  * **The Warlord:** Ruling through strength and fear, the Warlord seeks direct confrontation. Their Persona is built on physical dominance: heavy armor, visible scars, cybernetic combat enhancements, or an imposing physical silhouette. Their voice is resonant and commanding, designed to fill a room. They occupy space aggressively, forcing others to yield ground.  
  * **The Zealot:** Driven by ideology, faith, or a twisted moral code, the Zealot inspires fanatical loyalty. Their appearance incorporates religious or ideological iconography—vestments, symbols, or ritual scarring. Their gaze is intense, often described as "burning," and their speech patterns are rhythmic and sermon-like, utilizing repetition and rhetorical flourishes to captivate or intimidate.  
  * **The Corrupt Bureaucrat:** Representing the banality of evil, this archetype weaponizes systems and laws. Their Persona is deceptively unassuming: indistinct, gray clothing; a physically soft or weak appearance; and nervous ticks disguised as busywork. They deflect direct confrontation with jargon-heavy speech and bureaucratic obfuscation, making them frustratingly untouchable targets for combat-focused players.  
  * **The Shadow:** Operating through proxies, the Shadow is rarely seen until the climax. When they do appear, their features are obscured—masks, hoods, digital avatars, or deep shadows. Their voice may be modulated or synthesized, stripping away humanity and enhancing the aura of the unknown.  
* **Functional Characters (Henchmen, Mercenaries & Minions):**  
  * **The Specialist:** A character with a specific, dangerous skill set (sniper, hacker, assassin). They possess a professional demeanor and customized gear that sets them apart from the rank-and-file. They are often aloof, interacting with common minions only to give orders or express disdain. Identifying a Specialist early allows players to prioritize targets.  
  * **The Brute:** A physical impediment designed to absorb damage. Visually, they are characterized by an oversized physique, often enhanced by stim-injectors or cybernetics. They may be inarticulate, communicating through grunts or aggressive posturing, and their equipment is heavy, crude, and intimidating.  
  * **The Mook/Grunt:** These characters emphasize the scale of a faction. Their Persona is defined by uniformity: identical armor, standard-issue weapons, and rehearsed phrases ("Sector clear," "Moving to engage"). However, small deviations—a scratched helmet, a nervous tremor—can humanize them immediately before conflict.  
  * **The Comic Relief:** This archetype breaks the tonal tension. They are often physically clumsy, possess ill-fitting equipment, or complain about mundane issues like pay or food. They serve to ground an organization in a mundane reality, reminding players that even evil empires have payroll issues.  
* **Heroic Allies and Companions:**  
  * **The Lancer:** A foil to the party leader, the Lancer is competent, often arrogant, and visually distinct. If the party wears earth tones, the Lancer wears neon. Their body language is competitive, and their speech is confident, bordering on abrasive.  
  * **The Heart:** The emotional glue of the group. Their aesthetic is softer, open, and approachable. They tend to touch others to reassure them, speak with empathy, and carry sentimental mementos that anchor them to the world.  
  * **The Smart Guy:** The source of exposition and technical solutions. They are often tech-laden, wearing glasses or ocular implants, and exhibit a distracted demeanor. Their speech is fast, mumbling, and laden with technical details, and they are typically physically frailer than the combatants.  
  * **The Big Guy:** The protector and physical powerhouse. Often depicted as a "gentle giant," they move with slow, deliberate weight. Their voice is deep and resonant, and they may be of a large species or heavily augmented.

**2\. Physicality and Somatic Facilitation (The Visual Interface)**  
In speculative fiction, physical form is a narrative device. It tells the story of the world's technology, the character's personal history, and the environmental conditions of their upbringing. Game Masters consult the following facilitation lists during character generation to apply specific, evocative traits that hint at a character's origin and lifestyle, avoiding generic descriptions.

* **Dermatological Variants:**  
  * *Synth-skin Grafting:* Patches of flawless, unblemished plastic disrupting natural skin texture, indicating past trauma or cosmetic modification.  
  * *Bioluminescent Tattoos:* Subdermal ink that glows in low light, often marking gang affiliation or corporate ownership.  
  * *Chromatophores:* Skin pigment that shifts with mood or environment, common in gene-modded characters or specific alien species.  
  * *Radiation Burns/Scarring:* Weeping pores or keloid scars indicating survival of environmental disasters or industrial accidents.  
  * *Crystalline Growths:* Mineral protrusions resulting from exotic diseases or silicate-based biology.  
  * *Transparent Dermis:* A disturbing mutation where veins and muscle tissue are visible beneath a clear skin layer.  
* **Ocular Anomalies:**  
  * *Cyber-Optics:* Mechanical replacements ranging from sleek, glowing irises to bulky, multi-lens apparatuses bolted to the skull.  
  * *Compound Eyes:* Insectoid faceting, offering a wider field of view but creating an alienating effect on observers.  
  * *Rectangular/Horizontal Pupils:* Indicating prey-animal genetics or specific alien lineages.  
  * *Empty Sockets/Sonar:* The character lacks eyes entirely, relying on auditory or tech-assisted echolocation, often covered by a visor or cloth.  
  * *Milky Cataracts:* The "blind seer" trope, suggesting inner vision or reliance on the Force/Psionics.  
* **Structural Deviations:**  
  * *Elongated Limbs:* Indicates a life spent in low-gravity environments (Spacers, Belters).  
  * *Asymmetric Hypertrophy:* One arm or leg is significantly larger, perhaps due to specialized labor or partial gene-doping.  
  * *Hunchbacked/Spinal Curvature:* Resulting from the weight of heavy tech-harnesses or exoskeletons worn over decades.  
  * *Digitigrade Legs:* Backward-bending knees, often associated with speed or specific non-human species, changing the character's gait to a prowl.

**3\. Fashion and Attire Facilitation**  
Clothing signals status, profession, and faction. In sci-fi and fantasy, materials and utility are key descriptors.

* **Upper Class / Mastermind Attire:** Gravity-Defying Capes (smart-fabric that billows dramatically even without wind, projecting power). Kinetic Shielding (clothing that shimmers or ripples when the character moves, indicating active defense fields). Pristine White/Pastels (in grimy dystopian settings, wearing spotless light colors signals immense power—the ability to remain clean and distant from the filth). Ancient Organics (real wool, silk, or cotton is a supreme luxury in synthetic future worlds, signaling old money).  
* **Working Class / Minion Attire:** Utility Vests (covered in pouches, hard-points, and tools; implies a life of constant labor and readiness). Magnetic-Lock Boots (heavy, clanking footwear designed for zero-G work, grounding the character in an industrial aesthetic). Patched Vac-Suits (space suits repaired with differing materials, showing poverty and resourcefulness). Clan Colors (armor or jumpsuits spray-painted with crude symbols or gang tags).  
* **Underworld / Henchman Attire:** Sensor-Baffle Hoodies (clothing made of material that blurs faces on camera or blocks thermal scans). Mirror-Shades (concealing the eyes to prevent reading intentions, a staple of the "cool professional"). Trophy Jewelry (necklaces of teeth, circuit boards, or dog tags taken from victims). Integrated Weaponry (holsters that are part of the clothing, or coats tailored specifically to hide large guns).

**4\. Voice, Speech, and Mannerisms**  
A character’s voice anchors their reality in the auditory space of the game. Facilitation should focus on the texture and pattern of speech, not just the accent.

* **Vocal Textures:**  
  * *The Academic/Tech:* Speaks in precise jargon, correcting others' grammar. Uses passive voice ("Mistakes were made") to deflect blame. References statistics or probabilities rather than certainties.  
  * *The Grunt/Thug:* Mono-syllabic and harsh. Uses street slang or military brevity codes ("Oscar Mike," "Copy that"). Speaks with aggressive volume, interrupting frequently. Heavy breathing or spitting between sentences.  
  * *The Deceiver/Manipulator:* Silky, modulated tone that changes pitch to match the listener’s comfort zone. Uses rhetorical questions and dramatic pauses. Speaks sotto voce (whispering) to force others to lean in, asserting dominance.  
  * *The Alien/Other:* Refers to self in the third person ("This one thinks..."). Voices may contain dissonant harmonics (two tones at once) or staccato clicking. Biologically impossible vowel sounds create a sense of unease.  
  * *The Droid/Synthetic:* Flat affect, lack of contractions, or varying audio quality (static, skipping) depending on damage.

**5\. Psychological Architecture (The Engine)**  
While Persona is the surface, Psychology is the engine. A well-constructed character requires an internal logic that justifies their actions to themselves. Game Masters deploy this framework to establish this internal logic, proving they are the hero of their own story, driven by compelling intrinsic or extrinsic needs.

* **Primary Motivators & Core Drives:** Motivation drives the plot. In RPGs, motivations can be categorized as Intrinsic (internal needs) or Extrinsic (external goals). A character is rarely "evil for evil's sake"; they are driven by a compelling need.  
  * *Order/Control:* The desire to impose rigid structure on a chaotic universe. (Mastermind) Seeks to establish a totalitarian regime to end war or suffering. (Minion) Desires a strong leader to tell them what to do; fears autonomy and decision-making.  
  * *Chaos/Anarchy:* The belief that the current system is corrupt and must be destroyed to rebuild. (Mastermind) An accelerationist seeking to trigger the apocalypse to clear the slate. (Minion) Enjoys the freedom of looting and the lack of social consequences.  
  * *Acquisition/Greed:* The drive to possess resources, wealth, or people. (Mastermind) Wants to own a planet, a monopoly on FTL travel, or a unique artifact. (Minion) Motivated by the next paycheck or a shiny piece of loot.  
  * *Recognition/Legacy:* The need to be remembered or validated. (Mastermind) Wants to be written into history books, even as a monster. (Minion) Wants the boss to notice them; craves a promotion or a commendation.  
  * *Survival/Safety:* The primal drive to continue existing. (Mastermind) Fears a cosmic threat (aliens, entropy) and is willing to sacrifice freedoms for species survival. (Minion) Fighting because the alternative is starvation, execution, or exile.  
  * *Revenge/Justice:* A reaction to a perceived wrong. (Mastermind) Believes they were wronged by a system or hero and seeks to dismantle it entirely. (Minion) Hates a specific rival gang or authority figure for a personal slight.  
  * *Love/Protection:* Doing terrible things to save a loved one. (Mastermind) Willing to burn the world to save a dying spouse or child. (Minion) Fighting to send money back to a starving family.  
  * *Knowledge/Truth:* The pursuit of information regardless of the cost. (Mastermind) Uncovering forbidden secrets, unearthing eldritch gods, or breaching dimensional barriers. (Minion) Curiosity about how things work; the "tech guy" who just wants to solve the puzzle.  
  * *Redemption/Atonement:* Trying to erase a past sin through extreme measures. (Mastermind) "I destroyed my home; I will build a perfect new one, no matter who dies." (Minion) A criminal trying to "go straight" but trapped in the life by debts or threats.  
  * *Perfection/Purity:* The drive for an ideal state. (Mastermind) Genetic cleansing, cybernetic transcendence, or eliminating "inferior" species. (Minion) Obsessive about their gear, appearance, or adherence to protocol.  
* **The Internal Mechanics: The Lie and The Ghost:** Narrative depth is often achieved through the interaction of "The Lie" (a misconception the character holds about the world or themselves) and "The Ghost" (the traumatic past event that birthed the Lie). Antagonists often cling to their Lies until the bitter end, while Allies may overcome them during the campaign.  
  * *The Lie of Strength:* "Vulnerability is death. If I show weakness, I will be destroyed." This leads to isolation, tyranny, and the rejection of help.  
  * *The Lie of Control:* "If I can control every variable, nothing bad will ever happen again." This leads to micromanagement, fascism, and paranoia.  
  * *The Lie of Worth:* "I am only valuable if I am successful/powerful/useful." This leads to burnout, betrayal, and a willingness to be used by others.  
  * *The Lie of Justice:* "The system is broken, so I must break the people who built it." This leads to terrorism, collateral damage, and moral relativism.  
  * *The Lie of Victimhood:* "The world is against me, so I am justified in whatever I do to survive." This leads to selfishness, cruelty, and a lack of empathy.  
  * *The Lie of Superiority:* "I am the only one capable of saving them; they are sheep." This leads to a god complex and the dismissal of others' agency.  
  * *The Lie of Solitude:* "I am better off alone; people only hurt you." This creates the "Lone Wolf" archetype who sabotages their own alliances.  
* **The Ghost (Trauma):** The Ghost explains why the character believes the Lie. It provides the GM with the "wound" to press during social encounters.  
  * *Abandonment:* Left behind by parents or mentors during a crisis. (Result: Fear of attachment).  
  * *Betrayal:* Sold out by a partner or institution they trusted implicitly. (Result: Paranoia).  
  * *Failure:* Failed to save a loved one from death or ruin. (Result: Obsession with control).  
  * *Injustice:* Wrongly imprisoned or punished for doing the right thing. (Result: Hatred of authority).  
  * *Catastrophe:* Survived a planetary bombardment, plague, or massacre where everyone else died. (Result: Survivor's Guilt).  
  * *Humiliation:* Publicly shamed or stripped of rank/title. (Result: Obsession with reputation).  
  * *Conditioning:* Raised in a cult or military program that stripped away humanity. (Result: Lack of empathy).  
* **Fears, Phobias, and Vulnerabilities:** A character without fear is a plot device, not a person. Fears provide GMs with levers to manipulate character behavior during encounters.  
  * *Primal Fears:* Darkness (Nyctophobia), Fire (Pyrophobia), Drowning (Aquaphobia), Heights (Acrophobia), Spiders (Arachnophobia).  
  * *Social Fears:* Public humiliation, loss of rank, being forgotten, betrayal, intimacy, losing control of a situation.  
  * *Sci-Fi Specific Fears:* Vacuum Exposure (fear of hull breaches or EVA suits failing). Technophobia (fear of A.I. takeover or losing humanity to cybernetics). Xenophobia (fear of alien contamination or invasion). Teleportation (fear of their atoms being scattered or "dying" during transport). Time Dilation (fear of losing time with loved ones due to relativistic travel).  
  * *Secrets:* Hiding an addiction, a criminal past, an illegitimate child, working as a double agent, fraudulent credentials, contracting a terminal illness, or secretly lacking powers/magic.

#### **REFERENCE MODULE F: GENRE & SETTING MODULES**

To effectively place a generated character into the Tangent universe, the Game Master must apply the specific thematic layers of Science-Fiction and High Fantasy. This module provides specialized facilitation for Science Fiction settings, focusing on the implications of advanced technology, metaphysics, and environmental occupations required to contextualize a raw stat block within the galaxy. Additionally, it provides specific facilitation for mystery and investigation plots.

**1\. The Metaphysical Spectrum (Magic & Psionics)** For settings with "space magic" or mental powers, define the scope and limitation. In Tangent, all "magic" stems from manipulating the Conscious Light (The Resonance), but the cultural interface heavily dictates the visual and mechanical flavor of the character's abilities. When assigning the "Caster" Role, the Game Master must determine the character's Metaphysical interface to ensure the narrative aligns with the setting.

* **Psionics (Psi-Tech / The Mind):** Utilized primarily by the Ascendancy, Syndicate, and specific synthetics (Mekan). A cold, disciplined, highly intellectual manipulation of reality.  
  * *Disciplines:* Telepathy (mind reading, mental communication, memory alteration, emotion sensing/empathy, psychic attacks/mind blast, mind control). Telekinesis (moving objects, force push/pull, flight, force fields, crushing matter, deflecting projectiles). Clairsentience (precognition/seeing future, retrocognition/seeing past, remote viewing, danger sense, psychometry/reading object history). Biokinesis (healing, physical enhancement, shape-shifting, inducing pain/disease, body adaptation). Technopathy (interfacing with machines mentally, hacking via touch, controlling drones/robots directly). Teleportation (moving self or objects instantly across space, blinking/warping).  
  * *Visual Execution:* Manifests invisibly or with a sterile, high-frequency hum. Energy is represented by sharp, geometric crystalline light (Cyan or Violet) and requires deep concentration, glowing eyes, and absolute stillness.  
* **Arcane & Magi-Tech (The Weave / The Formula):** Utilized primarily by the Alterian Enclave, educated Elves, and scholars. Magic treated as a high-level physics equation, woven seamlessly into technology.  
  * *Disciplines:* Dimension (summoning creatures/objects from other planes, binding familiars, blinking, spatial folding, instantaneous teleportation). Energy (manipulating fire, cold, voltic, and sonic elements, energy blasts, kinetic projection, solid force constructs, force armor, hard-light weapons). Illusion (phantasmal mirages, sensory deception, dream manipulation, shadow-matter constructs, obfuscation, darkness manipulation).  
  * *Visual Execution:* Activated via complex vocalized frequencies (spells), geometric holographic runes, or by channeling energy through meticulously crafted Aetherium foci. Effects are elegant, flowing, and highly visible.  
* **Primal & Entropic (The Wild / The Void):** Utilized by the Vajar (Wild Elves), Sha'Nor (Entities of Entropy), and tribal factions. Magic drawn from the raw, chaotic lifeforce of a planet or the destructive vacuum of space.  
  * *Disciplines:* Entropy \- Chaos (culling the weak, inducing sickness/nausea, nullifying energies, suffocation, accelerated decay, negative energy attacks). Entropy \- Order (blessings, fortifying resolve, regenerating tissue, removing curses, stabilizing patterns, healing over time). Matter \- Enhancement (reinforcing items, boosting physical attributes, anchoring to space/immovability, granting flight/gravity manipulation). Matter \- Transmutation (morphing/disguise, size reduction/expansion, biological mutations, grafting foreign objects, altering physical states).  
  * *Visual Execution:* Brutal, instinctual, deeply connected to the environment. Glowing bioluminescent moss; visible, jagged magical auras; solid black smoke and cold void energy.

**2\. Operational Roles & Environments** A character's Origin heavily dictates their function. In space opera settings, characters often fill specific roles aboard a vessel. The Game Master must adapt the base Role Packages (from Module B) to fit the specific environment the players encounter them in.

* **The Starship Crew (Spacer Origin):**  
  * *Captain (Commander):* Command, diplomacy, tactics.  
  * *Pilot (Skirmisher/Utility):* Helm control, evasion, navigation.  
  * *Engineer (Controller/Builder):* Power management, repairs, damage control.  
  * *Gunner (Ranged DPS/Sniper):* Weapons systems, targeting.  
  * *Science Officer:* Sensors, scanning, medical, xenobiology.  
  * *Marine:* Boarding actions, security, ground combat in zero-G.  
* **The Frontier Laborers (Colony / Outworlds Origin):**  
  * *Prospector / Scavenger (Striker/Utility):* Utilizes heavy mining lasers, rugged environmental suits.  
  * *Field Medic (Support):* Operates with minimal supplies, relying on raw medical skill and practical grit.  
  * *Homesteader (Breaker):* Hardened survivor defending territory with low-tech ballistic weapons.  
* **The Urban Sprawl (Syndicate / Core Worlds Origin):**  
  * *Corporate Fixer (Face):* Navigates bureaucracy, negotiates black-market deals via the "Mesh" (AR network).  
  * *Street-Level Enforcer (Breaker):* Utilizes crude cybernetics, heavy blunt weapons, and intimidation.  
  * *Cyber-Doc (Controller/Utility):* Black-market surgeon specializing in illegal augmentations.

**3\. Mystery and Investigation (The Suspect Triangle)** For campaigns focusing on solving crimes or uncovering conspiracies, the character schema must include specific elements related to the crime. Game Masters construct this investigative framework prior to the session, mapping out the motives, means, and opportunities of specific NPCs. This creates a web of logical clues, fabricated alibis, and deliberate red herrings that players must actively unravel to identify the true culprit.

* **Motive Generation (Why?):**  
  * *Passion:* Jealousy, unrequited love, sudden rage, protecting a secret lover.  
  * *Greed:* Inheritance, insurance fraud, corporate theft, gambling debts, obtaining a rare artifact.  
  * *Fear:* Blackmail, preventing a secret from leaking, self-defense, fear of exposure/ruin.  
  * *Power:* Political assassination, removing a rival, silencing a whistleblower, gaining rank.  
  * *Fanaticism:* Ritual sacrifice, eliminating "sinners," fulfilling a prophecy, ideological cleansing.  
* **Means Generation (How?):**  
  * *Physical:* Blunt force (statue, tool), bladed weapon (knife, laser cutter), strangulation (cord, hands), firearm (slugthrower, blaster).  
  * *Subtle:* Poison (ingested, injected, contact), sabotage (vehicle malfunction, life support failure), induced accident (pushing, falling object).  
  * *Exotic/Sci-Fi:* Hacking cybernetics (heart attack), airlock ejection, nanobot swarm, psychic aneurysm, teleporter malfunction, radiation exposure.  
* **Alibi Generation (Why it "wasn't" them?):**  
  * *Solid (True):* Was publicly seen elsewhere; surveillance footage confirms location; was in custody/hospital.  
  * *Fabricated (False):* Tampered with logs/footage; bribed a witness; used a clone/android double; claims to have been alone sleeping; established by an accomplice.  
  * *Weak:* Claims to have been walking alone; was at a place with no witnesses; "can't recall"; claims to have been with the victim but left before death.  
* **Narrative Hooks and Secrets:** Every character in a mystery should have a secret, even if they aren't the killer.  
  * *Red Herrings:* Having an affair, embezzling money (but not murder), hiding a drug habit, hiding a criminal past.  
  * *The Artifact:* Character possesses an alien device that everyone wants.  
  * *The Derelict:* Character was found on a "ghost ship" with the crew missing.  
  * *The Clone:* Character discovers they are a clone of a famous/infamous figure.  
  * *The Virus:* Character is carrying a digital or biological plague.  
  * *The Time Displacement:* Character is from the past/future and warns of a timeline shift.

#### **REFERENCE MODULE G: ASSETS, CYBERNETICS, & VISUAL SEMIOTICS**

Following the assignment of tactical skills, the Game Master outfits the character with specific physical assets to dictate their power projection on the battlefield. During the final encounter preparation, these weapons, armor sets, vehicles, and factional aesthetics are selected to explicitly define the character's offensive damage output and defensive resilience, ensuring their equipment matches their Threat Tier, Wealth Score, and operational environment.

**1\. Assets, Resources, and Gear (Sci-Fi/Tech Database)** What does the character own or control? This defines their physical power projection.

* **Weapons:** Plasma rifles, monofilament katanas, railguns, sonic pistols, EMP grenades, thermal detonators, wrist-mounted lasers, smart-link firearms, gravity hammers, stun batons.  
* **Defense:** Personal force fields, powered armor, stealth suits (optical camo), vac-suits, ablative plating, neural shielding, holographic decoys.  
* **Gadgets/Tools:** Omnitools, medical nanobots, holographic projectors, jetpacks, grapple guns, portable scanners (tricorders), translation implants, hacking decks, drone controllers.  
* **Vehicles:** Starships (freighter, fighter, capital), hover-bikes, mechs/walkers, APCs, personal shuttles, drop-pods.

**2\. Cybernetic Enhancements Facilitation** To reflect the technological transhumanism of the Tangent setting, the Game Master may install cybernetic enhancements into the character's physical form. These augmentations are selected from the hardware tables and applied directly to the character's physiological systems prior to gameplay. This allows the character to interface with machines, process sensory data beyond human limits, or achieve superhuman strength, serving to elevate their baseline capabilities while visually reinforcing their faction's technological aesthetic.

| SYSTEM | ENHANCEMENT TYPES | FUNCTION/BENEFIT |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Neural** | Datajacks, Skillsoft slots, Reflex boosters, Memory banks, Comms-link. | Interface with machines; instant skill acquisition; reaction speed; secure communication. |
| **Sensory** | Cyber-eyes (IR/UV/Zoom), Cyber-ears (Parabolic/Damping), Olfactory boosters. | Enhanced perception; night vision; lie detection (voice stress analysis). |
| **Limb** | Replacement arms/legs (Strength/Agility), Hidden compartments, Built-in weapons. | Superhuman strength; concealment of contraband; built-in blades/guns. |
| **Dermal** | Subdermal armor, Chameleon skin, Temperature resistance. | Protection; stealth; survival in extreme environments. |
| **Organ** | Synthetic heart (Stamina), Filter lungs (Gas immunity), Detoxifiers. | Endurance; immunity to toxins/gases. |

**3\. Faction Aesthetics (Visual Semiotics)** During the final application of a character's physical assets, the Game Master or player must define the visual semiotics of their equipment by choosing a factional design language. This process occurs directly on the character sheet or NPC profile to instantly communicate the character's technological origin, cultural alignment, and socioeconomic status to observers. By applying specific materials, lighting motifs, and architectural styles to otherwise generic gear, you transform basic mechanical stat blocks into highly distinct, recognizable elements of the Tangent universe.

* **The Syndicate (Adaptive Neo-Futurism):** High-tech, transhumanist, and impeccably clean. Gear utilizes high-gloss white for civilian and corporate assets, or light-absorbing Vanta Black for stealth operatives. Materials include brushed aluminum and seamless cybernetics. Visuals are dominated by cerulean blue data-streams from "The Mesh," sterile cyan lighting, tailored high-collar suits, and weapons that integrate directly into armor or limbs without visible seams.  
* **The Dracon Dynasty (Feudal Fortress):** Fortified, angular, and deeply defensive. The aesthetic represents medieval armor upscaled to a high-tech reality. Gear features heavy "Dragon-Plate" powered armor, thick velvet capes, and massive ballistic weaponry or vibro-lances. The color palette relies heavily on stone grey, spire silver, and warm lantern orange lighting. Function always prioritizes defense and physical intimidation over sleekness.  
* **The Alterian Enclave (Cosmopolitan Magi-Tech):** Ergonomic, elegant, and timelessly handcrafted. Equipment seamlessly blends magic and technology, utilizing shimmering pearlescent whites, intricate gold filigree, and crystalline structures. Typical gear includes "Ballistic Silk" robes that act as armor, "Sunglass Laminate" solar-capacitor weapons, and everyday jewelry that functions as high-tier technology. The visual is defined by violet or cyan arcane light and fluid, curving geometries.  
* **The Aulurans (Predatory Biomimicry):** Organic, living technology that is inherently primal. Gear contains no hard corners or manufactured metals, relying entirely on deep jungle greens, bone, sinew, and hardened chitin. Weapons are living organisms, such as iron-wood rifles that fire pressurized thorns, and armor plates are grown directly from the character's skin. The visual atmosphere is filled with amber bioluminescence and spore-grown textiles.  
* **The Ascendancy (Psionic Purity):** Serene, minimalist, and enlightened. The faction rejects physical interfaces in favor of thought-controlled machinery. Characters wear flowing white robes and utilize crystalline floating interfaces without physical keyboards. Gear relies on white psycholith (marble) laced with conductive gold circuitry. The aesthetic is extremely clean, featuring repulsor-assisted armor that hovers slightly off the body and brilliant white or blue starlight energy.  
* **The Outworlds & Coalition (Scavenger Grit):** Dusty, highly functional, and heavily mismatched. This represents survival on the bleeding edge of the frontier. Gear is "Franken-tech," cobbled together from the ruins of other factions, featuring exposed wiring, duct tape, and heavy ballistic revolvers. The visual palette is defined by rust, worn leather, industrial safety orange, and the harsh, unfiltered lighting of dying suns.  
* **The Vajar & Primitive Factions (Wild Primal):** Earthy, ancient, and magically potent. Technology is eschewed for raw, elemental manipulation. Gear includes massive swords forged from folded iron and bone, inscribed with Matter-Enhancement runes to rival high-tech weaponry. Characters wear heavy cloaks of real fur and iron plating enchanted to shift with their form. The aesthetic heavily features living wood, alchemical steel, and glowing druidic auras.  
* **The Kovian Tribunal (Obsidian Futurism):** The shadow reflection of the Enclave. The aesthetic relies on sharp, jagged geometries crafted from matte-black obsidian (Shadow-Glass), dark chrome, and violet energy veins. Gear prioritizes stealth and supremacy, featuring form-fitting smart-leather, flowing capes of shadow-weave, and full-face masks designed to terrify rather than negotiate.  
* **The Premians (Organic Mysticism):** Clean, organic, and deeply connected to planetary preservation. Gear avoids visible, invasive cybernetics entirely, favoring high-collar earthen robes woven from ballistic silk. The visual language includes terracotta and slate tones, accented by bioluminescent blue or cyan circuit-tattoos and wooden staff weapons tipped with glowing crystals.

#### **CONCLUSION**

This facilitation framework provides the structural scaffolding necessary to build complex RPG characters. By rigorously defining the Persona (the outer shell), Psychology (the inner engine), Backstory (the trajectory), and Profile (the capabilities), a Game Master can generate antagonists and allies that are dynamic agents of narrative agency. Whether populating a sci-fi mystery or a space opera epic, these lists serve as a generative engine for storytelling, ensuring that every character encounter has the potential for depth, drama, and consequence. The combination of "Lie/Ghost" mechanics with concrete "Asset/Skill" lists ensures that characters are grounded in both emotional reality and mechanical utility, transforming them from mere statistics into memorable inhabitants of a living world.
