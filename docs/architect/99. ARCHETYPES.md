# **Tab 1**

# **ARCHETYPES**

These archetypes offer a framework that encompasses a character's narrative, personality traits, and mechanical abilities. Feel free to use the sample archetypes as is or adjust as desired.

# **Suggested skills**

* Listed as a guide *11 is the starting maximum skill rank.*  
* Benchmarks \- Skill ranks with appropriate designations:  
  * 1 \- 5 \- Hobbyist, casual interest  
  * 6 \- 10 \- Professional, formerly trained  
  * 11 \- 15 \- Expert, dedicated student  
  * 16 \- 20 \- Master

# **MBTI**

Myers-Briggs Type Indicator is a personality assessment that identifies 16 distinct personality types based on four key preferences:  
1\. How you focus your attention and get energy:

* Extraversion (E):   
  Energized by interacting with people and the external world.  
* Introversion (I):   
  Energized by spending time alone and reflecting on their inner world.

2\. How you perceive and take in information:

* Sensing (S):   
  Focus on concrete facts, details, and what they can perceive with their five senses.  
* Intuition (N):   
  Focus on patterns, possibilities, and the bigger picture.

3\. How you make decisions:

* Thinking (T):   
  Make decisions based on logic and objective analysis.  
* Feeling (F):   
  Make decisions based on values and how their choices affect others.

4\. How you approach the outside world:

* Judging (J):   
  Prefer structure, order, and making plans.  
* Perceiving (P):   
  Prefer flexibility, spontaneity, and keeping their options open.
