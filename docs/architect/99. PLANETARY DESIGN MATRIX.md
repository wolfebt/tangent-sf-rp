## **Introduction: The Geopolitics of the Void**

In the realm of speculative fiction role-playing games, the construction of a planetary setting is a discipline that transcends mere creative writing; it is an act of "ontological engineering"—the creation of a functional reality that must sustain the weight of mechanical interaction and narrative scrutiny. A planet is not merely a backdrop; it is a "Geophysical Chassis" that dictates the survival conditions, resource scarcity, and cultural evolution of its inhabitants.  
Within the Tangent universe, this process is elevated by the concept of "Kinetic Semiotics," where the physical artifacts of a civilization—its vehicles, architecture, and armored shells—serve as tangible manifestations of its cultural ethos. Therefore, the world-building process for Tangent cannot simply be a collection of random terrain features; it must be a rigorous synthesis of geophysical constants and sociological variables. It is the creation of a stage where the "Agnostic Chassis" of physics meets the "Cultural Skin" of factional ideology.  
This document synthesizes the Geophysical Chassis guidelines with the Planetary Design Matrix (PDM) to provide a definitive, exhaustively detailed framework. It integrates modular granularity, astrophysical rigor, and narrative fluidity into a unified procedural generation system.  
The framework operates on three layers:

1. **The Agnostic Substrate:** The physical reality of the star system, planet, and environment (Gravity, Atmosphere, Hydrology).  
2. **The Technocratic Overlay:** The infrastructure, economy, and legal systems imposed by the inhabitants (Tech Level, Government, Law Level, Economy, Meta Level).  
3. **The Narrative Texture:** The specific cultural and factional aesthetics that give the world its unique "flavor."

### 

### **The Component Hierarchy**

To ensure a World entry functions as a comprehensive setting, it must be constructed using the following six-part hierarchy:

1. **Cosmology & Fundamental Laws:** Defines the "Operating System" of reality (Universal Position, Physics & Metaphysics).  
2. **The Geophysical Chassis (Physical World):** The "Agnostic Substrate" (Stellar Mechanics, Planetary Statistics, Geography).  
3. **Chronological Stratigraphy (History):** The layers of time (Origin Myths, Eras & Cataclysms, Future Fate).  
4. **Biosphere & Demographics (Life):** The "Living Skin" of the planet (Ecology, Sentience).  
5. **Sociological Systems:** The "Cultural Software" (Technology & Metaphysics, Economy, Governance).  
6. **Narrative Aesthetics (Themes):** The "Vibe" and visual identity (Tone, Visual Semiotics).

## **1\. Stellar Context: The Architecture of Systems**

A planet’s character is fundamentally shaped by its primary star. The spectral class, luminosity, and mass of the star define the "Habitable Zone" (or Ecosphere), determining where liquid water—and thus standard life—can exist.

### **1.1 Stellar Classification and Life Zones**

The following table synthesizes the Morgan-Keenan classification with Tangent resource implications and system "moods."

| CLASS | COLOR | MASS (SOL=1) | SYSTEM IMPLICATIONS & RESOURCES |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **O** | Blue | \> 16.0 | Violent/Young. Systems are filled with debris, protoplanets, and high radiation. Likely mining outposts only; no native sophonts. Rare/Hostile. |
| **B** | Blue White | 2.1–16.0 | High Energy. Intense UV radiation requires "Reflective Shell" armor upgrades. Rare biosphere development. |
| **A** | White | 1.4–2.1 | Bright/Harsh. Settlements require polarized shielding. Often hosts "Pre-Garden" worlds or energy collection arrays (Dyson Swarms). |

### 

### **1.2 Orbital Zones and System Anatomy**

A system is divided into functional zones which dictate the type of encounters and infrastructure found.

* **The Inner Zone (The Furnace):** Inner orbits where water boils. Worlds here are "Geomorteus" (dead rock) or "Geoplastic" (molten).  
  * *Infrastructure:* Mining Outposts (Silica/Metal extraction) and Solar Power Arrays.  
  * *Survival:* Requires Heavy Energy Shielding (TL4).  
* **The Bio-Zone (Ecosphere):** The band permitting liquid water.  
  * *Infrastructure:* Home to "Garden" and "Ocean" worlds like Aquatica and Almathea. Prime real estate for Agri-Worlds and Capital Worlds (e.g., Premius, Draconis).  
* **The Outer Zone (The Deep / Cold Zone):** Outer orbits beyond the frost line.  
  * *Infrastructure:* "Cryo" or "Ice Giants." Fuel Refining (skimming hydrogen) and Secret Research Stations (hidden in asteroid belts).  
  * *Survival:* Requires constant thermal regulation and "Ice Farm" infrastructure.

## **2\. The Geophysical Chassis: Planetary Mechanics**

This layer represents the immutable physical reality of the world. These parameters are not merely flavor text; they are the primary antagonists in an environmental survival scenario.

### **2.1 Planetary Size and Gravity**

Gravity is the fundamental constraint of physical existence. It dictates movement speeds, carrying capacities, and structural integrity.

### **2.3 Hydrology and Terrain**

Hydrographics dictate planetary terrain and necessary vehicle types.

* **Desert/Arid (0-9%):** Requires "Water Reclamation Systems" (TL2). Vehicles use "Tracked" or "Hover." Society relies on Moisture Vaporators or Deep Aquifer Mining. E.g., Grilious.  
* **Dry (10-35%):** Small seas. Agriculture limited to Oases.  
* **Wet/Oceanic (36-85%):** Standard Earth-like.  
* **Water World (90-100%):** Requires "Hydro Induction" or "Water Propeller" propulsion. Settlements are "Floating" (TL3) or "Submersible" (TL4). Resources extracted from seabed. E.g., Aquatica.  
* **Ice Ball:** Water locked in ice caps. Requires "Thermal Regulators" and "Ice Farm" infrastructure. E.g., Krias.  
* **Anomalous Geography (High Meta Level):** Worlds boasting a Meta Level (ML) of 4 or higher exhibit physical manifestations of their metaphysical power. This includes strong ley lines, levitating geographical features (e.g., floating continents, suspended waterfalls), localized storms of raw Essence, or visible crystalline matrices growing naturally from the terrain. These features act as environmental hazards and strategic resources.

### 

### **3.2 Ecological Hazards**

The biosphere is rarely benign.

* **Virulent Pathogens:** "Weaponized Phage" (Type 6 Atmos) requires daily Fortitude saves (DC 15+) or "Full Environmental" suits.  
* **Predatory Fauna:** On "Savage" worlds, fauna is Aggressive. Tables feature "Pack" tactics (Kannid) or "Ambush" predators (Rakne).  
* **Psionic Ecology:** Worlds linked to Fey/Alterian influence may have "Awakened" biospheres (Plants with Telepathy/Obfuscate). Requires "Psi-Dampener Fields."  
* **Scaling and Mega-Fauna:** Planetary environments dictate the physical scale of native threats. Worlds with High/Extreme Gravity or Water World (100 percent) hydrographics frequently breed Mega-Fauna. Apply a Scaling Modifier (x2 to x10) to the Damage Dice and Structure Points of these native apex predators, directly reflecting the size class of the threat.

### 

### **3.3 Progenitor & Relic Specification**

When a world features "Fallen Empires" or ancient ruins, use the Progenitor Ruin Matrix to ensure narrative consistency across artifacts and visual descriptions.

* **The Ila (Elohim):** Ancient ruins feature levitating white ceramic structures, gold-laced polymatter, and psionic violet energy signatures (ML 6). Relics are often psychoreactive, responding only to the mental commands of Awakened users.  
* **The U'Nonn (Anunnaki):** Ruins consist of colossal, sculpted lapis lazuli and basalt ziggurats adorned with cuneiform holograms. Relics frequently feature extreme gravity manipulation, temporal distortion, and geometric force-field emitters.  
* **The Mondi:** Architectural remains are grown from crystalline consciousness archives and feature bioluminescent patterns embedded in ethereal technology. Relics are often post-biological psychic constructs or memory-storage matrices.

## **4\. The Civilizational Skin: Demographics, Tech, & Government**

The "Civilizational Skin" is the overlay of sentient will upon the planet.

### **4.1 Tech Level (TL) Overview**

* **TL 0 (Stone Age):** Fae, Koban, Vassoth. Wood/Bone/Stone. Muscle/Fire power.  
* **TL 1 (Feudal/Archaic):** Early Dynasty. Iron/Brick. Steam/Wind power. Castles.  
* **TL 2 (Industrial):** Coalition/Terran. Steel/Concrete. Combustion/Fission. Skyscrapers.  
* **TL 3 (Interstellar):** Standard Dynasty/Mercenary. Plasteel. Fusion. Arcologies/Domes.  
* **TL 4 (Stellar Age):** Syndicate/Impyrium. Nanocarbon. Antimatter. Mega-Cities (500+ stories).  
* **TL 5 (Exotic/Advanced):** Ascendancy/Mekan. Living Metal/Hard-Light. Zero-Point. Floating Cities.

*Building Limits:* A TL 2 world cannot support "Mega-Cities" (500 stories) because steel/concrete fail at that height; they are limited to "High-Rise" (40 stories).

### 

### **4.2 Metafocus Level (ML) Overview**

Comparable to the Technological Level of a society, the Metafocus Level determines the prominence, understanding, and integration of Metaphysics (Magic, Psionics, Dimensional manipulation) within a civilization.

* **ML 0 (Null):** Complete absence of active metaphysical manipulation. The concept of "magic" or "psionics" is a myth or entirely unknown.  
* **ML 1 (Rare):** Negligible ratio of Meta Users. They are likely to be harshly judged, feared, or subjected to intense societal expectations.  
* **ML 2 (Selective):** More 'in tune' individuals, but typically restricted to minorities, reclusive sects, or special operatives. Represents the early stage of Enlightenment.  
* **ML 3 (Cultured):** Uncommon but accepted Meta Usage. Adepts of various types are integrated into society without immediate fear.  
* **ML 4 (Awakened):** Magic and Psionics are formally studied, structured, and utilized as a science or primary defense mechanism. It is foundational to the society's infrastructure.  
* **ML 5 (Integrated):** Meta knowledge and its use are fully integrated into society as commonplace technology (Magi-Tech or Psi-Tech).  
* **ML 6 (Deific):** Transcendent entities that do not manipulate reality; they define and rewrite it. Usually reserved for Progenitor species or god-like entities.

### **5.2 Commodities & Wealth Mechanics**

A character's Wealth Score determines purchasing power.

* **Impoverished World (Wealth Mod \-2):** Goods are cheap, but high-tech items are unavailable.  
* **Wealthy World (Wealth Mod \+4):** High prices for basics, but abundant luxury/tech goods.  
* **Trade Code Modifiers:** Trade Codes directly modify the baseline cost and availability of specific items on the Commodities Exchange. For example, High Tech (Ht) worlds apply a \+2 to a character's effective Wealth Score when purchasing computers or cybernetics, but may impose a \-1 WS penalty when purchasing natural Foodstuffs. Agricultural (Ag) worlds invert this, providing a \+2 WS for food and organics.

**Purchasing Rules**

1. **The Golden Rule (Auto-Buy):** A character may automatically purchase any item with a Crafting DC equal to or less than their Wealth Score without depleting their liquid Credits or reducing their Wealth Score.  
2. **The Liquidity Constraint (The Gap Rule):** When a character wants an item with a DC higher than their Wealth Score, they cannot simply buy it using their status. They must bridge the gap with Liquid Credits (Loot).  
   * Cost Liquid \= Value (Item DC) \- Value (Wealth Score)  
   * *Example:* A Wealth Score 15 character (Auto-buy limit \~640 Cr) wants a Stealth Suit (DC 18). DC 18 Value is approx 1,470 Cr. Cost: 1,470 \- 640 \= 830 Credits. The character uses their WS to cover the "base" cost but must pay 830 credits from their savings to acquire the superior item.  
3. **The Market Availability Cap (TL/DC Cap):** The maximum Crafting DC of items natively available for purchase on a world is equal to (Planetary TL \* 5\) \+ 10\. Items above this DC are considered Imported or Contraband. Their liquid cost is doubled, and they must be acquired via the Black Market or specialized trading factions (e.g., Entari Combine).

### **6.2 Cultural Quirks**

Add flavor to any settlement by rolling or selecting from these quirks:

* **Face Concealment:** Masks/veils mandatory; showing face is taboo.  
* **Barter Economy:** Credits are insulted; trade in goods/favors only.  
* **Technophilia:** Cybernetics displayed as jewelry; "Organics" pitied.  
* **Nocturnal:** Society active at night to avoid heat/radiation.  
* **Dueling Code:** Legal disputes resolved through ritual combat.  
* **Caste Colors:** Clothing color strictly dictates social rank.  
* **Ancestor Worship:** Decisions made by consulting AI constructs of elders.  
* **Silence Vow:** Public speaking reserved for elite; commoners use sign language.  
* **Xenophobia:** Extreme distrust of non-humans ("Purists").  
* **Communal Property:** "Theft" doesn't exist; borrowing is standard.  
* **Ritual Scarification:** Marks of accomplishment or status earned through painful ceremonies.  
* **The Great Filter:** Historical events are framed as the weak being culled for the greater good.  
* **Holographic Pets:** Real animals are considered unclean; companionship is found in complex, custom-built holograms.  
* **Augmented Memory:** Critical information is stored in specialized brain implants, external notes are seen as unreliable.  
* **Gravity Worship:** High-altitude living or structures are status symbols, proximity to the ground is low-caste.  
* **Obsession with Symmetry:** Art, architecture, and even bodily modifications must adhere to strict symmetrical patterns.  
* **Consumption Rituals:** Food and drink are taken only after elaborate, time-consuming preparation and blessing.  
* **Personal Drones:** Every citizen is assigned a small, personal drone for surveillance, communication, and basic retrieval tasks.  
* **The Debt Clock:** A public, visible display tracks an individual's societal contributions and favors owed/received.  
* **No Permanent Residence:** Citizens move through a network of communal, temporary habitats on a fixed schedule.  
* **Genetic Purity Testing:** Regular mandatory screening to ensure no genetic "drift" or unauthorized modification.  
* **Weather Control Superstition:** Attempts to manipulate the planet's weather are done with elaborate, mystical ceremonies despite technological methods being available.  
* **Body Language Dialects:** Subtle shifts in posture and gesture form complex regional dialects, making written text secondary.  
* **Mandatory Leisure:** Every citizen is required to log a minimum number of hours per week in state-approved recreational simulations.  
* **The Sound Silence:** A specific, culturally important humming or low-frequency tone must be present in all public spaces; its absence is unsettling.  
* **One-Name Identity:** Individuals only use a single given name, surnames or titles are temporary and granted based on current achievement.

### **9.2 Adventure Hooks by World Type**

* **Agricultural: Crop Theft/Sabotage**  
  * *Setting:* Vast, open-air fields or sealed, massive greenhouses covering kilometers of the surface. Corporate security patrols are stretched thin. The atmosphere is tense, with locals viewing the corp security as oppressors.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to track the specific poison/saboteur (a rival scientist or a disgruntled employee). The theft is a false flag, meant to frame a local dissident group. The stolen crops are being used to feed an underground refugee camp.  
* **Agri-World: Bio-Threat**  
  * *Setting:* A lush, engineered jungle where the mutated forest is aggressively expanding, its carnivorous vines and spore-sacs creating a moving wall of danger. The corporate compound is the last safe haven, but resources are dwindling.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs need to retrieve the original, non-mutated seed bank from a facility now deep inside the forest. The mutation was intentional, a failed attempt at creating a bioweapon. A scientist believes they can reverse the mutation but needs a rare enzyme only found in the heart of the growth.  
* **Asteroid: Collision Warning**  
  * *Setting:* A high-tech, sparsely populated orbital habitat or a deep-space factory carved into a major asteroid. Life is regimented and focused on the work. The uncharted object is a fast-moving, massive chunk of ice or rock, its trajectory calculated to be a direct hit in under 72 hours.  
  * *Hooks:* The PC's main thrusters for the asteroid are sabotaged/offline, requiring a dangerous EVA to fix. The "uncharted object" is actually a cloaked, automated Progenitor defense platform, and diverting it will break a major treaty. A faction secretly wants the collision to destroy a rival's operations on the asteroid.  
* **Barren: First Contact**  
  * *Setting:* A desolate, wind-scoured world with little atmosphere, forcing all life into shielded, underground domes or heavily armored surface vehicles. The signal originates from a deep, unexplored cavern network.  
  * *Hooks:* The signal is a distress call from an ancient species, but following it leads to a deadly trap. The signal is causing mass hallucinations or paranoia in the nearest habitat. A rival corporation is already trying to weaponize or silence the source.  
* **Desert: Lost Colony**  
  * *Setting:* Endless dunes and massive, unpredictable sandstorms. Extreme heat during the day, freezing cold at night. The ruins are only accessible during a rare, temporary lull in the weather, and time is running out.  
  * *Hooks:* The pre-spaceflight tech is powered by a dangerous, unstable energy source. The colony was not lost, but intentionally buried to contain a dangerous artifact/entity. A local, isolationist nomad tribe views the ruins as sacred and is hostile to outsiders.  
* **Fluid Oceans: Geyser Disaster**  
  * *Setting:* A planet with a deep, pressurized liquid surface, making the habitat domes the only solid ground. The eruption is massive, sending scalding, corrosive fluid hundreds of meters high, threatening to breach the thin-shelled domes.  
  * *Hooks:* The "geyser" is not natural, but triggered by deep-sea seismic drilling. The PCs must use a small submersible to place a seal/explosive charge deep underwater. The breach is intentional, designed to expose a specific person or secret inside the domes.  
* **Garden: Political Intrigue**  
  * *Setting:* A paradise world, meticulously terraformed with perfect climate control and stunning aesthetics, masking deep social inequalities. The revolutionary is a charismatic figure giving fiery speeches in public squares, their message spreading rapidly through the populace and threatening the comfortable elite.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to either protect the current governor from the revolutionary's followers or to extract the revolutionary safely before the corp/government silences them. The revolutionary is secretly being funded by a rival system/corp. The populace is being unknowingly manipulated by a powerful mind-control drug in the air/water supply.  
* **High Pop: Cyborg Riots**  
  * *Setting:* Densely packed, vertical cities of billions, where the workforce is overwhelmingly synthetic. The riots are coordinated, brutal, and focused on strategic infrastructure targets. The human military/police are overwhelmed.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must infiltrate the main android assembly plant to shut down the "sentience signal." A former scientist/programmer is trying to help the cyborgs achieve full recognition, and the PCs must decide to assist or stop them. The cyborgs are only rebelling because they were infected by a powerful alien AI.  
* **High Tech: Espionage**  
  * *Setting:* A pristine, heavily secured corporate research campus—all glass, chrome, and hidden surveillance. Gen-E is a project involving advanced genetic manipulation, and the escaped subject is both incredibly valuable and highly dangerous, blending seamlessly into the population.  
  * *Hooks:* The Syndicate lab is trying to cover up the subject's escape, making the PCs' mission unofficial and unsupported. The prototype is not a monster, but a child who needs protection from the Syndicate. A third-party black market organization is also hunting the subject for their own nefarious purposes.  
* **Ice-Capped: Deep Ice Horror**  
  * *Setting:* A world of endless glaciers and sub-zero temperatures. Geothermal stations drill miles into the ice sheet. The awakened entity is causing unexplained power failures and strange signals from the depths.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must venture down the unstable drill shaft to find the creature or shut down the defense system before it targets the main colony. The creature is not hostile, but terrified, and is trying to communicate a warning. The Progenitor defense system is only active because the colony is violating an ancient territorial boundary.  
* **Industrial: Pollution Crisis**  
  * *Setting:* A smog-choked, eternally twilight world dominated by immense, belching factories and pipes. The air is toxic, and the populace is confined to breathing apparatuses. The "plague" is a fast-acting neurological virus.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must retrieve evidence from the factory's core to prove the corporate negligence, but the facility is locked down. The "plague" is not a side effect of pollution but a deliberate, slow-acting bioweapon to cull the workforce. The cure exists, but it's held by a black market cartel demanding an impossible price.  
* **Low Pop: Missing Persons**  
  * *Setting:* A remote, rustic, frontier-style planet with a handful of scattered settlements. Communication is unreliable. The vanishings are silent, leaving no trace.  
  * *Hooks:* The lone predator is a hyper-evolved chameleon-like alien that has been feasting on the population. The hidden cult is sacrificing people to an entity or artifact. The "missing people" are actually being secretly abducted by a government agency for a hidden purpose.  
* **Low Tech: Witch Hunt**  
  * *Setting:* A medieval-level or retro-futuristic society with strong superstition and minimal technology. The phenomenon (e.g., a psychic or psionic power) is creating real, destructive effects (fires, localized storms, visions).  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs, with their advanced tech, are mistaken for the 'demons' or 'witches' and become targets. The source of the phenomenon is an untrained youth whose powers are manifesting chaotically. The local religious/political leader is secretly using a small piece of lost tech to fake the 'magic' to consolidate their power.  
* **Mining World: Labor Dispute**  
  * *Setting:* A deep, unstable network of tunnels and shafts beneath the surface. The air is thick with dust and tension. The artifact is radiating a madness-inducing field, causing miners to turn violently on each other.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must enter the deepest, most dangerous shaft to contain the artifact, but the corporate security detail is simultaneously trying to seize it, causing a three-way conflict. The madness is actually a form of hyper-accelerated evolution or collective psychic awakening, not true insanity. The artifact is the key to unlocking the planet's actual, hidden purpose.  
* **Non-Agriculture: Resource War**  
  * *Setting:* A desolate, mostly empty planet with a few heavily fortified outposts near the single, viable resource (e.g., the last clear underground lake or a vein of rare earth metals). The two factions are using scavenged, old-world military tech.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs are hired to broker a peace treaty, but one side is actively sabotaging the negotiations. The resource is not water/metal, but an element that stabilizes an ancient space gate, and controlling it is the true prize. A third, larger power is waiting for the two factions to weaken each other before moving in to seize the resource.  
* **Non-Industrial: Bandit Fortress**  
  * *Setting:* A quiet, low-development world used by the gang because it has vast, unpoliced areas (canyons, dense forests, abandoned military bases). The fortress is a well-defended, camouflaged stronghold with multiple escape routes.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must find a way to disable the gang's main communication/defense array without alerting them. The gang is using the fortress to hold a major political/corporate hostage. The "bandits" are actually former freedom fighters who turned to crime out of desperation after a corporate takeover.  
* **Poor: Debt Slavery**  
  * *Setting:* Gritty, rundown urban sectors or dilapidated settlements. The air is heavy with desperation and fear. The corporate/crime boss's enforcers are omnipresent and brutal. The debt is impossible to repay due to predatory interest rates.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs must find the original debt contracts in a secure vault to nullify them. The crime boss is using the indebted populace to work on an illegal, secret project. The PCs are hired by a rival corp not for altruistic reasons, but to destabilize the current power structure.  
* **Quarantine: Extraction**  
  * *Setting:* The surface of Omin II is a hostile, storm-wracked landscape, crisscrossed with automated turrets and patrols. The crash site is rapidly being compromised by the Phage virus, which turns living tissue into a mobile, fungal-like growth.  
  * *Hooks:* The VIP is infected and showing early symptoms; the PCs must extract them before the change is complete. The turrets are controlled by a rogue AI that needs to be deactivated first. The Phage virus is not natural; the VIP crash-landed to deliberately release it on the planet.  
* **Rich / Mining: Assassination Plot**  
  * *Setting:* Opulent, high-security penthouses and exclusive social clubs, juxtaposed with the dark, profitable mining operations below. The governor/CEO is a major figure, and their death would send shockwaves through the system.  
  * *Hooks:* The assassin is one of the PC's trusted contacts, and the group must choose between friendship and the mission. The plot is an inside job, orchestrated by the governor's own family/board of directors. The "assassination" is a ruse, a plan to fake the target's death and allow them to escape to a new life.  
* **Vacuum: Space Hulk**  
  * *Setting:* The immense derelict ship is an unstable environment—pitch-black, riddled with hull breaches, fluctuating gravity, and deadly debris. Navigation is a nightmare. The Space Hulk is rumored to be haunted.  
  * *Hooks:* The riches are guarded by a surviving, heavily armed security team that went mad decades ago. The "deadly threat" is a rapidly multiplying, aggressive swarm of alien parasites. The Space Hulk contains a fully operational, but highly unstable, FTL drive that a faction desperately needs.  
* **Water World: Monster**  
  * *Setting:* Cities that float on the deep ocean surface, connected by bridges and submersibles. The Jorni variant is massive, capable of crushing an entire city module. The available "Jaeger" defenses are old, under-maintained models.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs need to pilot an old Jaeger or find a way to lure the Jorni away from the cities. The monster is not an animal, but a heavily engineered bio-weapon that has gone rogue. The only way to stop it is to retrieve a sonic dampener from a sunken, older city.  
* **Ice World: Env. Failure**  
  * *Setting:* A massive, sealed habitat dome on a perpetually frozen planet. The sabotage is total, and the massive primary heating arrays are offline. Frost is rapidly creeping up the inner walls, and essential systems are failing.  
  * *Hooks:* The PCs have to traverse the frozen, unstable service tunnels to find the terrorists and restore power before the main reactor freezes over. The "terrorists" are actually a rescue team trying to expose a faulty life support system that was already slowly killing the population. The sabotage was triggered by a corporate rival to force the colony to relocate.

## **10\. Synthesis: The Tangent World Profile (TWP)**

The Tangent World Profile (TWP) is the alphanumeric DNA of a planet. It utilizes a simple colon-separated numerical string to compress complex geophysical and sociological data into a compact format, allowing Game Masters to generate, record, and reference entire sectors with efficiency.

### 

### **10.1 The Universal World Profile (UWP) Structure**

The TWP is expressed as a string of pure numerical values (and one letter designation for the Starport). Values operate on a 0 to 15 scale.  
**The String Sequence:**  
Starport:Size:Atmosphere:Hydrography:Population:Government:Law Level:Tech Level:Meta Level  
*Example:* A:8:6:7:10:4:5:4:3 (A large, wet, high-tech Syndicate world with moderate law and cultured metaphysics).

### 

### **10.2 Procedural Generation Sequence (The "Build Loop")**

Follow this sequence to construct a world from the void up.  
**Mathematical Limits:** No generated UWP value can fall below 0 or exceed 15\. If a formula yields a negative, record 0\. If a formula exceeds 15, record 15\.

* **Step 1: Stellar Context**  
  * *Action:* Roll to determine the Primary Star (Section 1.1) and the Planet's Orbital Zone (Section 1.2).  
  * *Impact:* If the star is Type M (Red Dwarf) or the Zone is Inner/Outer, apply penalties to Atmosphere and Hydrography rolls (typically minus 2 or greater).  
* **Step 2: The Physical Chassis (Size, Atmos, Hydro)**  
  * *Planetary Size (S):* Formula: 2d6 minus 2\. Range: 0 (Asteroid Belt) to 10 (Super Earth). Effect: Sets Gravity Tier and limits Atmosphere.  
  * *Atmosphere (A):* Formula: 2d6 minus 7 plus Size. Constraint: If Size is 0, Atmosphere is 0\. Range: 0 (Vacuum) to 15 (Exotic/Thick).  
  * *Hydrography (H):* Formula: 2d6 minus 7 plus Atmosphere. Constraint: If Size is \< 2, Hydrography is 0\. If Atmosphere is 0 or 1, subtract 4 from Hydrography. Range: 0 (Desert 0%) to 10+ (Water World).  
* **Step 3: The Sociological Skin (Pop, Starport, Gov, Law)**  
  * *Population (P):* Formula: 2d6 minus 2\. (Roll 3d6 minus 2 if designated as a Faction Capital). Range: 0 (Uninhabited) to 15 (Quadrillions). Note: This is a base-10 logarithmic scale.  
  * *The Zero-Pop Override:* If Population is rolled as 0, the world is a Barren wasteland. Starport automatically becomes X, Government becomes 0, and Law Level becomes 0\. Disregard the formulas for these stats.  
  * *Starport (Sp):* Formula: 2d6 minus 7 plus Population. (A result of 0 or less \= X, 1-2 \= E, 3-4 \= D, 5-6 \= C, 7-8 \= B, 9+ \= A).  
  * *Government (G):* Formula: 2d6 minus 7 plus Population. Range: 0 (Anarchy) to 15 (Progenitor Control).  
  * *Law Level (L):* Formula: 2d6 minus 7 plus Government. Range: 0 (Lawless) to 15 (Absolute Suppression). Effect: Determines the "Banned Items" list and the difficulty of "Streetwise" checks.  
* **Step 4: The Factional Overlay Process**  
  * Once the raw world is generated, apply the "Faction Template" to align the math with the narrative. *Note: Faction Mandates are HARD OVERRIDES. They replace the generated math to ensure consistency.*  
  * *The Syndicate:* Mandate: If Pop is less than 8, override and set to 8\. Set Law to 5+ and TL to 4\.  
  * *The Dracon Dynasty:* Mandate: Change Government type to 5 regardless of roll. Increase Structure Points of all buildings by 20%.  
  * *The Coalition:* Mandate: Change Government to 4 or 7\. Reroll Law Levels above 6\. Coalition worlds value independence.  
  * *The Outworlds:* Mandate: Set TL to a maximum of 2, Law to a maximum of 3\. Apply "Scrap-Punk" aesthetic. Reduce effective Wealth of the world by minus 2\.  
* **Step 5: Technocratic Assessment (Tech Level)**  
  * *Base Tech Level (TL):* Baseline is 3 (Standard Interstellar, the most prevalent across 70+% of worlds). Roll 1d6. On a 1, subtract 1 from the Base. On a 6, add 1 to the Base. On a 2-5, the Base remains 3\.  
  * *Modifiers:* Starport A (+1), Starport X/E (-1); Size 0 or 1 (+1); Atmosphere 0-3 or 10+ (+1) (Survival demands tech); Population 10+ (+1); Government 0 (-1), Government 13 (-1).  
  * *Cap:* Floor at 0, Cap at 5\. Faction Overrides take final precedence.  
* **Step 6: Metaphysical Assessment (Meta Level)**  
  * *Base Meta Level (ML):* Formula: 1d6 minus 1\.  
  * *Modifiers:* Alterian Enclave presence (+2), Ascendancy presence (+2), Mekan presence (+1), Syndicate presence (-1), Impyrium presence (-1), Tech Level 0 (+1 for tribal shamanism/superstition).  
  * *Range:* Floor at 0 (Null) to 6 (Deific).

### 

### **10.3 Derived Attributes: Trade Codes**

Trade Codes are tags assigned based on the TWP string. They drive the economic simulation. See Section 5.1 for full code descriptions.

* **Ag (Agricultural):** Atmos 4-9, Hydro 4-8, Pop 5-7  
* **As (Asteroid):** Size 0, Atmos 0, Hydro 0  
* **Ba (Barren):** Pop 0, Gov 0, Law 0  
* **De (Desert):** Hydro 0  
* **Fl (Fluid Oceans):** Atmos 10+, Hydro 1+  
* **Ga (Garden):** Size 5+, Atmos 4-9, Hydro 4-8  
* **Hi (High Pop):** Pop 9+  
* **Ht (High Tech):** TL 4+  
* **Ic (Ice-Capped):** Atmos 0-1, Hydro 1+  
* **In (Industrial):** Atmos 0-2 or 4 or 7 or 9, Pop 9+  
* **Lo (Low Pop):** Pop 1-3  
* **Na (Non-Ag):** Atmos 0-3, Hydro 0-3, Pop 6+  
* **Ni (Non-Ind):** Pop 4-6  
* **Po (Poor):** Atmos 2-5, Hydro 0-3  
* **Ri (Rich):** Atmos 6 or 8, Pop 6-8  
* **Va (Vacuum):** Atmos 0  
* **Wa (Water World):** Hydro 10+

### **10.5 Ludo-Narrative Validation Checklist**

Before concluding the world-building process, verify the integration of your planet's mechanics and narrative details using this checklist:

* **Trade Code Math:** Are the applied Trade Codes (e.g., Ag, Ind, Hi) mathematically supported by the UWP generation scores?  
* **Faction Alignment:** Does the generated Tech Level (TL) and Meta Level (ML) accurately reflect the controlling Faction's mandated baselines, utilizing the Hard Overrides?  
* **Law Level vs. Architecture:** Are the local laws directly reflected in the visual aesthetics? (e.g., Does a strict Police State feature hidden black-market areas rather than open bazaars?)  
* **Market Economics:** Are the items natively available for purchase physically restricted by the Market Availability Cap formula: (Planetary TL \* 5\) \+ 10?  
* **Metaphysical Geography:** If the planet features a Meta Level (ML) of 4 or higher, are there clear Anomalous Geography features manifesting as environmental variables?  
* **Scale Integrity:** If the world is High Gravity or a Water World, have the native creatures been properly enhanced using the Scaling Multiplier (x2 to x10)?
