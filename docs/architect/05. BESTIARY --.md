# **BESTIARY**

**Subtitle:** The Creatures and Adversaries of the Tangent Universe

## **I. INTRODUCTION & CREATION RULES**

In the Tangent system, adversaries are not defined by arbitrary "Challenge Ratings" or "Levels." Like Player Characters, every creature, drone, and NPC is built using the **Build Point (BP)** economy. This ensures that the physics of the world remain consistent—a monster's strength comes from the same attribute costs as a hero's strength.

### 

### **The Threat Scale**

To gauge the difficulty of an encounter, compare the Adversary's **Total BP** to the Party's Average BP.

* **Fodder (0 \- 25 BP):** Weak creatures, untrained civilians, or basic drones. Dangerous only in swarms.  
* **Standard (26 \- 50 BP):** Competent professionals, dangerous predators, or soldiers. A 1-on-1 match for a starting character.  
* **Elite (51 \- 100 BP):** Veteran combatants, large alpha predators, or heavy mecha. Requires teamwork to defeat.  
* **Boss / Legend (101+ BP):** Major story antagonists, massive distinct entities, or master-tier NPCs.

### 

### **Creating Custom Adversaries**

1. **Concept:** Determine the creature's role (Tank, Striker, Controller).  
2. **Attributes:** Cost is **4 BP per \+1 modifier**. (e.g., \+3 Strength costs 12 BP).  
3. **Skills:** Cost is **1 BP per Rank**. (e.g., Stealth \+6 costs 6 BP).  
4. **Features:** Apply Species Features or Feats at their listed BP cost.  
5. **Equipment:** Add gear. For NPCs, equipment does not cost BP but increases their effective threat.
