**Description:** The designation of "Entity" refers not to a specific species or classification but to a fundamental template for a creature whose very nature precludes a conventional, inherent physical body. This is a category for beings whose existence transcends the material plane, representing a state of being rather than a biological or physiological structure.  
Such an entity is defined by its lack of physical permanence and its capacity to exist in a purely conceptual, energetic, or spiritual state. Their manifestation, if they choose to interact with the physical world, is often a temporary, constructed, or projected form—a mere vessel or avatar—not their true self.

The origins and nature of these entities are varied and encompass several existential categories:  
Extra-Planar Beings are the entities that originate from dimensions or planes of existence distinct from and inaccessible to the conventional physical universe. They may be native inhabitants of realms of pure energy, thought, or emotion, their presence on the material plane being an intrusion or temporary excursion. 

Detached Consciousness are minds, wills, or souls that have been separated or voluntarily departed from any corporeal host. This could be a remnant of a powerful intellect, a ghost, or a form of sentience that has evolved beyond the need for a biological substrate, existing as a self-aware mental construct.

Embodiment of a Place or Concept, an entity can be the living, sentient personification of an abstract or geographical element. This includes: Ideological Embodiments: The consciousness of a universal concept such as Justice, Chaos, Time, or Fear. Their existence is intrinsically tied to the continued presence of the concept itself. Geographic Embodiments: The spirit, genius loci, or manifested will of a specific location—a forest, a mountain, an ocean, or a city. Their power and form are often reflective of the nature and history of the place they embody.

In essence, an Entity is an existential force, a non-corporeal intelligence whose essence exists outside the typical constraints of mass, form, and conventional biology. They operate on principles of reality that are often mysterious and inherently powerful to material beings.
