# **99 \- AUGMENTATIONS MATRIX**

# **MODIFYING THE PHYSICAL FORM**

In the Tangent universe, the definition of "self" is fluid. The body is an agnostic chassis—upgradable, interchangeable, and capable of profound evolution. This catalog unifies the mechanics of Cybernetics (Replacements), Bioware (Enhancements), and Nanoware into a single matrix governed by Tech Levels (TL), Build Points (BP), and the Node System.

## 

## ---

## **1\. Introduction & Mechanics Overview**

The augmentation system provides a progression from basic prosthetics to god-like nanotech manipulation.

### 

### **1.1 The Economy of Self**

1. **Build Points (BP)**: Represents neurological and biological capacity. Standard mods cost 2 BP. Specialized traits or simple options cost 1 BP.  
2. **Nodes**: Represents the physical mass and room available in a limb or organ. If a body part's Node capacity is exceeded, the part malfunctions or suffers structural failure. Typically, Structure Points (SP) are matched 1-to-1 with Node Capacity.  
3. **Sockets**: Standardized hardpoints for modular gear and internal upgrades. Limb-based sockets are often pre-determined by the chassis.  
4. **Credits (Cr)**: The financial cost of hardware and surgery. Calculated via the formula: Value \= 10 \* 4^(DC/5).

### 

### **1.2 Tech Level (TL) Progression**

* **TL 1-2**: Primitive/Functional replacements. Often 0 BP (Prosthetic).  
* **TL 3 (Standard)**: Parity with biology. The galactic baseline.  
* **TL 4 (Enhanced)**: Bioware/Seamless. Mechanic: Immune to disablement from Massive Damage.  
* **TL 5 (Advanced)**: Nanotech/Metaphysical. Mechanic: Costs 1/2 BP (minimum 1).

### **12.1 Mechanics of the Wearable**

* **Capacity**: Pseudo-cybernetics provide their own internal Node capacity for hosting mods. These are external frames; mods are installed into the item's chassis.  
* **Node Calculation**: Set to **Half** the comparable anatomical location (Head=5, Torso=25, Gauntlet=8, Sabaton=10). A full body shell provides 100 nodes. **Note: Node capacities for Gauntlets and Sabatons are per item, not a total for the pair.**  
* **Standard Interface**: Wearables (0 BP) require an **Interface Port** (Internal or External) to sync with neural impulses. Without a port, the item suffers \-2 to all associated checks, checks at disadvantage or it is not usable at all, in addition to any security options.  
* **Limitations**: One wearable item per primary body location. External sockets cannot exceed 1 per 10 Nodes of base capacity.

# **Biological**

# **01.09.01 Biological Augmentations**

# **Symbiotes, Grafts, and Genetic Modifications**

## **1\. OVERVIEW AND PHILOSOPHY**

While mechanical cybernetics represent the brute-force integration of the "Agnostic Chassis" with inorganic technology, Biological Augmentations (Bioware) represent the harmonization of flesh, genetics, and living organisms. In the Tangent universe, flesh is not a limitation; it is a programmable canvas.

Bioware encompasses symbiotic organisms that bond with a host, limbs grown in amniotic vats and grafted onto bodies, and genetic retroviruses that fundamentally rewrite a creature's biological pattern. Factions such as the Aulurans (who view metal technology as "dead coffins") and advanced Syndicate Bio-Labs heavily utilize these methods over traditional chroming.

## **2\. INSTRUCTIONAL PRIMER: THE INTEGRATION OF BIOWARE**

To fully understand the application of Biological Augmentations in the Tangent setting, the process is broken down into the fundamental questions of implementation:

* **Who uses them?** Advanced factions operating at Tech Level 4 (Syndicate Elite, Ascendancy Science Officers) and civilizations built entirely on living technology, such as the Aulurans and the Thorn. It is also utilized by Adepts specializing in Matter (Transmutation) Invocations.  
* **What are they?** Living technology. This includes parasitic/symbiotic creatures engineered for specific tasks, organs cloned from enhanced DNA, and chemical/viral agents that mutate the host's cellular structure.  
* **Where are they installed?** Procedures take place in sterile Bio-Labs, Auluran "Deep Root Forges," or through the metaphysical touch of a Matter Discipline user.  
* **When are they acquired?** Characters can acquire them during Character Creation by spending Build Points (BP) or during a campaign by expending Credits (Cr) and undergoing significant surgical/incubation downtime.  
* **Why choose Bioware over Cybernetics?** Bioware is inherently stealthy (often undetectable by metal detectors or standard EMPs). Furthermore, TL4 Bioware is immune to disablement from Massive Damage, and it naturally interfaces with the host's Essence for Meta-Tech applications.  
* **How do they work?** They utilize the exact same Node and Capacity economy as mechanical augmentations. A biological arm graft still requires the corresponding Nodes in the shoulder. However, they rely on the host's metabolism, requiring increased caloric intake, Essence, or biological matter to function and repair.

## **3\. MECHANICAL FRAMEWORK: BIOWARE VS. CYBERWARE**

Biological augmentations follow the core rules established in the *01.09 MATRIX* matrix but possess unique operational physics.

**Tech Level 4 (Enhanced) Baseline:**

True bioware natively operates at TL4.

* **Mechanic:** Bioware is immune to disablement from Massive Damage (unlike TL3 mechanical limbs, which can be rendered useless by heavy trauma).  
* **Repair:** Bioware possesses the *Regeneration* trait. It recovers 1 HP/Structure Point per hour naturally. It cannot be repaired with an Engineering check; it requires a Medicine or Xenobiology check. Alternatively, the host can "feed" the bioware raw organic matter to instantly restore 1d4 HP.

**Node Capacity & Rejection:**

Biological augments consume Node Capacity exactly like mechanical ones. Exceeding Node capacity with bioware does not cause mechanical failure, but rather **Cellular Rejection**—resulting in chronic fatigue, immune system collapse, and systemic necrosis (manifesting as permanent penalties to Stamina and maximum Health).

Symbiotes are independent or semi-independent living organisms engineered to bond with a host chassis.

* **Integration:** Requires Biotech Paste and a surgical bonding process.

* **Vulnerability:** Symbiotes are susceptible to poisons, diseases, and biological toxins that target the host.

### **4.2 Grafts (Cloned and Spliced Additions)**

Grafts are vat-grown limbs, organs, or carapaces surgically attached to the host.

* **Integration:** Grafting can be performed via TL4 Medical Surgery (Requires 1d4+1 weeks of recovery) or instantly via the **Grafting Invocation** (Matter: Transmutation DC 20).  
* **Mechanic:** Grafts replace biological baseline parts. They are seamless and often indistinguishable from the host's natural body unless specifically engineered for a visibly alien aesthetic (e.g., grafting a Draconic arm onto a Human).

### **4.3 Genetic Modifications (Retroviruses & Mutations)**

Unlike physical additions, genetic mods alter the host's DNA to naturally develop new traits.

* **Integration:** Injected via retroviral serums or triggered by the **Mutation Invocation** (Matter: Transmutation DC 18).  
* **Mechanic:** Grants access to standard Species Features (e.g., *Low-Light Vision, Water Breathing, Claws, Exoskeleton*) for species that do not naturally possess them. These are paid for via BP or high Credit costs.

## **1\. OVERVIEW AND PHILOSOPHY**

In the Tangent universe, the division between technology and metaphysics is merely a matter of methodology. If the physical universe is an operating system running on the "Conscious Light" of the universal Resonance, then the body is a hardware chassis capable of processing metaphysical code.

Metaphysical Augmentations (Meta-Augs) represent the pinnacle of this integration. By surgically embedding conductive metamaterials (such as Aetherium or Resonance Crystals) into the nervous system or etching them into the epidermis, individuals can hardwire magical Invocations and Psionic phenomena directly into their biological matrix.

## **2\. INSTRUCTIONAL PRIMER: THE INTEGRATION OF META-AUGS**

To understand the application and deployment of Metaphysical Augmentations within the Tangent setting, the integration process is defined by the following foundational parameters:

* **Who uses them?** Factions operating at high Meta Levels (ML). The Ascendancy utilizes them extensively as "Psi-Tech" to amplify their innate telepathic and telekinetic abilities. The Alterian Enclave uses "Magi-Tech" crystal laminates. Minor factions like the Vajar or Kovian Tribunal utilize primal runic scarification or shadow-glass implants.  
* **What are they?** Physical modifications that contain or channel metaphysical patterns. They range from solid Resonance Crystals surgically fused to the pineal gland, to intricate geometric tattoos inked with Aetherium-laced pigments, to cybernetic neural sockets designed to run "spell subroutines."  
* **Where are they installed?** Implantation requires highly specialized facilities. Alterian "Crystalline Matrices," Ascendancy "Meditation Forges," or the sterile, high-end clinics of the Syndicate (who reverse-engineer ancient Impyrium relics). The process blends medical surgery with metaphysical Attunement.  
* **When are they acquired?** Players may acquire them during Character Creation by spending Build Points (BP) or during standard gameplay by expending Credits (Cr) and raw materials (Aetherium/Resonance Crystals), followed by a period of physical and spiritual recovery.  
* **Why choose Meta-Augs over standard Cybernetics?** While cybernetics provide physical output (strength, armor), Meta-Augs alter reality. They allow non-Awakened individuals to manifest specific supernatural effects, or they grant Awakened individuals massive amplifiers for their Essence and Discipline skills.  
* **How do they work?** They utilize the Universal Displacement Unit (UDU) hierarchy. **1 Socket of Meta-Tech requires 10 Nodes of biological capacity.** Once implanted, Passive enhancements are permanent, while Active Imbuements are triggered as a Free Action (Mental Trigger) and fueled by the host's Essence pool (1 Essence \= 1 Charge).

## **3\. MECHANICAL FRAMEWORK: THE FLESH AS A CIRCUIT**

Embedding magic or psionics into a living chassis requires navigating the intersection of Biology, Technology (TL), and Metaphysics (ML).

**Node to Socket Conversion:**

Standard Meta-Tech is scaled by Sockets. When implanting Meta-Tech into a biological body part, the augmentation consumes Node Capacity at a ratio of **1 Socket \= 10 Nodes**.

* *Example:* Imbuing a character's arm with a "Rank 10 Energy (Fire) Invocation" requires 1 Socket of capacity, which consumes 10 Nodes of space within that arm. Exceeding Node capacity causes dangerous physical and spiritual Rejection.

**Fabrication & Economics:**

Meta-Augs cannot be built from standard materials. They adhere to the Economic Unified Field Theory:

* **Passive Enhancements** (e.g., Tattoos of Protection) require *Resonance Crystals*. DC \= Base Item DC \+ (Sockets Used x 5).  
* **Active Imbuements** (e.g., Psi-Crystal Blasters) require *Aetherium*. Raw materials always cost 50% of the final market Value.

**Tech Level (TL) and Meta Level (ML) Synergy:**

* **TL 1-3 / ML 3-4 (Primal/Arcane):** Tattoos, scarification, and raw crystal embedding. Often painful, visually obvious, and spiritually taxing.  
* **TL 4-5 / ML 4-5 (Advanced Psi-Tech / Magi-Tech):** Seamless neural integration, microscopic Aether-circuits, and digitized consciousness.
