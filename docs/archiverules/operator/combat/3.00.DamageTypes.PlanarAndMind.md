# **DAMAGE TYPES: PLANAR & PSYCHIC**

### **Celestial Damage**

Celestial damage is a form of energy damage that embodies holy power, divine light, and the essence of higher planes. It is particularly effective against evil entities, undead, and creatures of darkness. Celestial damage can manifest as radiant energy, pure light, or a spiritual force that disrupts and banishes unholy beings. It often carries additional effects such as banishing, dispelling, or healing allies.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Divine Light Manipulation: The ability to generate and control holy light, creating blinding flashes, healing rays, or beams of pure energy that harm evil creatures.  
* Celestial Evocation: Summoning celestial beings or spirits of light to fight on one's behalf. These entities are often resistant or immune to celestial damage and can unleash powerful divine attacks.  
* Holy Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with celestial power, granting them the ability to deal additional celestial damage, harm evil creatures more effectively, or even provide protection against unholy forces.

Archaic Tech

* Consecrated Weapons: Weapons blessed or imbued with holy symbols and rituals, allowing them to harm evil entities or deal celestial damage.  
* Holy Water: Water blessed by religious rites, capable of burning or harming unholy creatures and dealing celestial damage.

Modern Tech

* (Limited applicability) While modern tech may not directly produce celestial damage, certain devices could be modified or used in conjunction with metaphysical abilities to amplify or channel holy energy.

Advanced Tech

* (Highly theoretical) Advanced technologies that could potentially harness or project divine energy, creating beams or pulses of celestial damage. This might involve manipulating energy fields or tapping into higher dimensional sources.

Additional Notes: Celestial damage is particularly effective against evil creatures and undead. It may have additional effects such as banishing, dispelling, or healing. Characters with strong faith or divine connections may be more effective at channeling or wielding celestial damage. Based on the "Celestial Template", creatures associated with the celestial alignment often have resistances to cold, acid, and electricity, as well as Damage Reduction against evil attacks. They may also possess spell resistance and the ability to "smite evil."

### **Infernal Damage**

Infernal Damage is a unique form of energy damage that channels the corrupting and destructive energies of the infernal planes. It is more than just fire, encompassing a blend of hellish flames, spiritual corruption, and the raw essence of damnation. Infernal damage is particularly effective against creatures of good alignment, those vulnerable to spiritual corruption, or those who have made pacts or deals with infernal entities. This damage type can cause both physical and spiritual harm, often leaving lasting scars or taints on the victim's soul.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Infernal Evocation: Summoning infernal entities, such as demons or devils, to fight on your behalf. These beings can unleash powerful infernal attacks, are often resistant or immune to infernal damage, and may have additional abilities to corrupt or torment their foes.  
* Infernal Pacts/Curses: Inflicting curses or pact-based damage that draws power directly from the infernal planes, causing both physical and spiritual harm to the target. These attacks may bypass normal defenses and inflict lasting penalties.  
* Infernal Enchantments: Imbuing weapons or objects with infernal power, granting them the ability to inflict infernal damage, corrupt the target's soul, or even bind them to infernal entities.

Archaic Tech

* Infernal Relics/Artifacts: Items imbued with infernal power or created in the infernal planes, capable of channeling infernal energy to harm or corrupt targets. These items may be dangerous to handle and may carry their own corrupting influence.  
* Ritualistic Implements: Tools used in dark rituals to invoke infernal powers or channel infernal energies, which can be used to inflict infernal damage or summon infernal entities.

Modern Tech

* (Limited applicability) Modern technology is unlikely to directly produce infernal damage, but certain devices could be modified or used in conjunction with metaphysical abilities to amplify or channel infernal energy.

Advanced Tech

* (Highly theoretical) Advanced technologies that could potentially harness or project infernal energy, creating beams or pulses of corrupting fire or spiritual force. This might involve manipulating interdimensional energies or tapping into the essence of the infernal planes.

Additional Notes: Infernal damage can cause ongoing burning, spiritual corruption, or the weakening of the target's soul. It may also inflict fear, despair, or other negative emotional effects. Some weapons may have additional effects such as binding the target to infernal entities, inflicting curses, or hindering healing. Characters with strong faith or divine connections may be more resistant to infernal damage, while those with corrupt souls or infernal pacts may be more vulnerable. Based on the context of document, infernal damage may be associated with the "Stygian" influence, which can cause memory loss and other dangerous effects.

### **Dimensional Damage**

Dimensional damage manipulates and disrupts the fabric of reality itself, inflicting harm by altering a target's connection to their current dimension, tearing at their physical form on an interdimensional level, or sending them to other planes of existence. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures that rely on a stable connection to their dimension, entities that phase or shift between realities, or those with magical or extradimensional origins. Dimensional damage often bypasses conventional defenses and can have unpredictable and reality-altering effects.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Dimensional Rift Creation: The ability to create tears or rifts in the fabric of reality, causing targets to be exposed to chaotic interdimensional energies, banished to other planes, or torn apart by the forces of dimensional instability.  
* Dimensional Displacement: Shifting a target's position or existence across dimensions, causing them to become phased out, lost between realities, or transported to another location or plane.  
* Reality Manipulation: Altering the fundamental laws of physics or reality around a target, causing them to suffer damage from the instability or contradictions created. This can include warping space, time, or even the target's own form.

Archaic Tech

* Dimensional Artifacts: Objects imbued with ancient or otherworldly powers that can disrupt dimensional boundaries, causing localized rifts or banishing effects. These artifacts are often dangerous and unpredictable.  
* Rituals and Incantations: Complex rituals and incantations that manipulate the vibrations or frequencies of reality, causing dimensional instability or opening temporary portals to other planes.

Modern Tech

* Phase Disruptors: Devices that emit fields or pulses designed to disrupt dimensional phasing or shifting, forcing entities back into a single reality and making them vulnerable to conventional attacks.  
* Spatial Displacement Devices: Experimental technology that can teleport or displace targets across short distances or even between dimensions, often with unintended consequences.

Advanced Tech

* Interdimensional Cannons: Powerful weapons that fire bolts of energy or matter infused with dimensional instability, causing targets to be torn apart, banished, or destabilized on an interdimensional level.  
* Reality Anchors: Devices that stabilize or disrupt the dimensional fabric, either preventing dimensional travel or causing localized reality distortions that inflict damage.  
* Pocket Dimension Generators: Technology that creates or manipulates pocket dimensions, which can be used to trap targets, store items, or unleash chaotic energies upon opponents.

Additional Notes: Dimensional damage can cause a wide range of effects, including banishment, phasing, disorientation, reality distortion, and even disintegration. Some attacks may also cause targets to become lost between dimensions or suffer lasting effects related to the disruption of their connection to reality. Characters with strong connections to their home dimension or those with abilities that stabilize reality may be more resistant to dimensional damage.

### **Psyche / Mental Damage**

Mental damage targets the mind and psyche, rather than the physical body. It can manifest in various ways, including psychic assaults, emotional manipulation, or the disruption of mental processes. This damage type is particularly effective against creatures with strong mental capabilities, those vulnerable to psychic attacks, or those whose minds are not adequately shielded. Mental damage can cause effects such as confusion, fear, disorientation, loss of control, or even permanent psychological trauma.

&nbsp;

Metaphysical

* Psychic Assault: Direct attacks on the mind, causing pain, disorientation, or even unconsciousness. This can be similar to a physical assault but targeted at the mental plane.  
* Emotional Manipulation: Manipulating the target's emotions, causing fear, despair, or rage. This can lead to the target being unable to function effectively or turning on allies.  
* Mental Intrusion: Invading the target's mind to steal information, plant suggestions, or control their actions. This can be subtle or overt, depending on the power and the target's defenses.

Archaic Tech

* (Limited applicability) Archaic tech is unlikely to directly cause mental damage, but certain rituals, substances, or artifacts might be used to induce altered states of consciousness or psychic vulnerability.

Modern Tech

* (Limited applicability) Modern technology might include devices that induce stress or disorientation, but it is less likely to cause direct mental damage unless combined with metaphysical abilities. However, some technology could be used to detect or amplify mental activity.

Advanced Tech

* Psychic Amplifiers: Devices that amplify psychic abilities, allowing for more potent mental attacks or intrusions.  
* Neural Disruptors: Technology that directly disrupts brain function, causing confusion, pain, or even memory loss.  
* Mind Control Devices: Advanced technology that allows for the direct control of a target's mind and actions.

Additional Notes: Mental damage can cause a variety of effects, including confusion, fear, loss of control, and psychological trauma. Some attacks may bypass physical defenses entirely, while others may be resisted by mental fortitude or psychic shielding. Characters with strong wills or psychic defenses may be more resistant to mental damage. Based on the "Power Profile \- Mental Powers.pdf", mental powers generally involve mental sensory effects or Will-resisted effects which target minds. Effects can include Emotion Control, Hallucination, Mental Blast, Mind Control, Mind Switch, Possession, Psychic Vampirism, and Psychic Weapon.
