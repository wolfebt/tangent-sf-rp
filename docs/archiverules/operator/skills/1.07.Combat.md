# **COMBAT** 

## 

## **ARCHAIC COMBAT SKILLS**

Game Mechanic for Attacks: 2d10 \+ Skill Rank \+ Ability Modifier vs. Target’s Defense  
Game Mechanic for Defense: Defense Skill Rank is added to the character's Static Defense Score (10 \+ Agility \+ Defense Skill \+ Armor/Size Mods).

### 

### **DEFENSE**

* **Ability:** Agility  
* **Description:** Defense is the art of self-preservation in combat. unlike other combat skills used to inflict damage, this skill measures a character's ability to avoid being hit, deflect blows, or position themselves advantageously to mitigate harm. It represents situational awareness, footwork, and the effective use of protective gear like shields.  
* **Game Mechanics:**  
  * **Passive Defense:** Your Rank in this skill is directly added to your **Defense Score**. (e.g., A character with Agility \+2 and Defense Rank 4 has a base Defense of 16).  
  * **Active Defense (Reaction):** In specific tactical situations (such as the *Total Defense* action), you may roll a Defense check to replace your static score or negate specific maneuvers like Trips or Grapples.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Evasion & Footwork:** Dodging, weaving, and ducking under melee swings and incoming kinetic projectiles.  
  * **Solid Shield Blocking:** Intercepting arrows, blades, and blunt trauma directly on a heavy metal or composite shield.  
  * **Energy Shield Deflection:** Angling hand-mounted hard-light or plasma shields to bounce energy bolts harmlessly away.  
  * **Weapon Parrying:** Using your own blade or staff to deflect an incoming melee strike off its attack line.  
  * **Total Defense Stance:** Sacrificing all offensive action to curl behind cover or shields, maximizing survivability.  
  * **Roll & Tuck (Impact Absorption):** Rolling with a direct concussive explosion or heavy blunt hit to cut damage in half.  
  * **Counter-Grapple Framing:** Posting forearms and hips to prevent an opponent from securing a takedown or chokehold.  
  * **High-Cover Bracing:** Maximizing cover benefits behind low concrete barriers, bulkheads, and sandbag walls.  
  * **Ranged Projectile Weaving:** Moving unpredictably in zig-zag patterns to spoil the aim of marksmen downfield.  
  * **Point-Blank Deflection:** Shunting an attacker's drawn gun barrel or blade tip away from your center of mass in CQB.

### 

### **MELEE**

* **Ability:** Strength (standard) or Agility (with specific Features like *Weapon Finesse*)  
* **Description:** This skill covers proficiency with hand-held weaponry designed for close-quarters combat. It encompasses everything from simple clubs to elegant rapiers. It reflects a character’s ability to strike accurately and forcefully with extended reach or heavy implements.  
* **Game Mechanics:**  
  * **Attack Roll:** 2d10 \+ Melee Rank \+ Strength Mod vs. Target Defense.  
  * **Damage:** Weapon Damage Dice \+ Strength Mod.  
  * **Reach:** Some melee weapons (Polearms) allow attacks from 10ft away.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Daggers & Combat Knives:** Swift, lethal slashes, throat cuts, and reverse-grip thrusts inside grappling range.  
  * **One-Handed Swords:** Fencing with rapiers, sabers, and arming swords utilizing precision point-work and lunges.  
  * **Two-Handed Greatswords:** Sweeping massive, heavy blades across wide arcs to cleave multiple targets and break lines.  
  * **Polearms & Spears:** Utilizing superior 10-foot reach, thrusting points, and hooking blades to keep foes at bay.  
  * **Blunt Staves & Bo Rods:** Executing rapid, spinning two-handed bludgeoning combinations, sweeps, and disarms.  
  * **Battleaxes & Cleavers:** Delivering crushing, chopping blows designed to split wooden shields, limbs, and light armor.  
  * **Warhammers & Maces:** Driving blunt armor-piercing kinetic force directly through heavy steel and plate armor.  
  * **Vibro-Blades:** Wielding high-frequency vibrating knives and swords that saw through metal plate effortlessly.  
  * **Energized Melee Weapons:** Slashing with superheated plasma blades and power-axes that melt armor on contact.  
  * **Flails & Flexible Weapons:** Whipping weighted chains and morningstars around an opponent's raised shield to strike.

### 

### **RANGED**

* **Ability:** Agility  
* **Description:** The skill of using muscle-powered projectile weapons or thrown objects to strike targets at a distance. This differs from *Ballistic* (guns) as it relies more on the user's physical trajectory calculation and force (for thrown items) or mechanical tension (bows).  
* **Game Mechanics:**  
  * **Attack Roll:** 2d10 \+ Ranged Rank \+ Agility Mod vs. Target Defense.  
  * **Damage:** Weapon Damage Dice (Strength mod is usually added to *Thrown* weapons, but not standard Bows unless they are composite/specialized).  
  * **Range Increment:** Each weapon has a range increment; attacking beyond this imposes a cumulative \-2 penalty per increment.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Recurve & Hunting Bows:** Drawing, anchoring, and loosing arrows rapidly at close-to-medium forest targets.  
  * **Heavy Longbows:** Drawing massive 100-pound pull bows to punch arrows through metal armor at extreme distances.  
  * **Compound Tech-Bows:** Utilizing modern pulley cams, stabilizer rods, and fiber-optic sights for pinpoint arrow shots.  
  * **Hand Crossbows:** Firing and rapid-cranking compact, concealable crossbows designed for close ambush work.  
  * **Heavy Siege Crossbows:** Operating foot-stirrup, crank-drawn heavy crossbows that hit with devastating kinetic shock.  
  * **Throwing Knives & Spikes:** Hurling balanced steel blades with precision into eyes, necks, and exposed joints.  
  * **Throwing Axes & Tomahawks:** Lobbing heavy, rotating axes that bury deeply into skull bone and wooden barricades.  
  * **Shuriken & Flechettes:** Flumping spreads of star-blades or balanced throwing darts in rapid succession.  
  * **Slings & Staff Slings:** Whirling lead bullets or river stones to hit targets at range with crushing blunt force.  
  * **Boomerangs & Returning Sticks:** Throwing aerodynamic curved timber sticks that strike hard or loop back if missed.

### 

### **UNARMED**

* **Ability:** Strength (for power/grappling) or Agility (for striking speed/precision)  
* **Description:** The ability to fight without artificial weapons, using only the body. This includes brawling, martial arts, wrestling, and the use of natural weaponry for non-human species.  
* **Game Mechanics:**  
  * **Attack Roll:** 2d10 \+ Unarmed Rank \+ Str/Agi Mod vs. Target Defense.  
  * **Damage:** Typically lower than weapons (1d4 or varies by size/species) \+ Strength Mod.  
  * **Non-Lethal:** Unarmed attacks can often be chosen to deal Stun/Non-Lethal damage without penalty.  
  * **Grapple:** Unarmed skill is used to initiate and maintain holds (Grapple checks).  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Boxing & Power Punches:** Throwing crisp jabs, hooks, crosses, and uppercuts with weighted hand wraps or bare fists.  
  * **Kicks & Leg Sweeps:** Snapping roundhouse kicks, front teep kicks, low shin strikes, and sweeping enemy legs.  
  * **Joint Locks & Submissions:** Snapping armbars, kimuras, and heel hooks to break enemy joints and force surrenders.  
  * **Grappling & Throws:** Executing hip throws, double-leg takedowns, and suplexes to slam targets into hard deckplates.  
  * **Claws & Talons (Natural):** Slashing and tearing flesh and armor with native species claws, finger spikes, and talons.  
  * **Biting & Fangs (Natural):** Clamping down with powerful reptilian or feline jaws, injecting venom, or tearing throats.  
  * **Horns & Headbutts (Natural):** Driving heavy head-crest horns or hardened skulls straight into an opponent's face.  
  * **Tail Strikes & Sweeps (Natural):** Whipping heavy muscular tails to trip enemies, strike backs, or shatter ribs.  
  * **Chokeholds & Strangles:** Sinking rear-naked chokes, guillotine holds, and crushing windpipes to knock foes unconscious.  
  * **Nerve Strikes & Pressure Points:** Striking vulnerable nerve clusters to temporarily paralyze limbs and drop guards.

## **MODERN COMBAT SKILLS (Combat)**

These skills govern the use of technologically advanced projectile weaponry, from personal firearms to heavy ordinance.

### 

### **BALLISTIC**

* **Ability:** Agility  
* **Description:** The operation of firearms and other handheld projectile weapons. This skill covers maintaining, reloading, and firing weapons that use combustion or magnetic acceleration to launch solid slugs at high velocity.

* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Standard Attack:** **2d10 \+ Skill Rank \+ Agility Mod vs. Target Defense**.  
  * **Burst Fire (Short):** Firing a controlled 3-round burst grants a **\+1 Bonus to Strike**2.

  * **Auto-Fire (Long Burst):** Firing longer bursts (10+ rounds) imposes a **\-1 Recoil Penalty** for every 10 rounds fired. However, for every point the attack roll exceeds the target's Defense, one additional round hits (up to the total shots fired). Each extra hit deals **\+1d damage** (if the initial hit penetrated DR)3.

* **Specialties:**   
  * **Semi-Automatic Pistols:** Double-tapping service handguns rapidly, clearing malfunctions, and drawing from holsters.  
  * **Heavy Revolvers:** Firing high-caliber magnum hand cannons with heavy recoil control and precision shot placement.  
  * **Tactical Shotguns:** Pumping or cycling 12-gauge buckshot spreads and armor-piercing slugs in room clearing.  
  * **Submachine Guns (SMGs):** Controlling high cyclic rates of fire, firing controlled bursts from compact machine pistols.  
  * **Assault Rifles & Carbines:** Engaging point and area targets from 50 to 300 meters using single shots and short bursts.  
  * **Designated Marksman Rifles:** Precision shooting at mid-to-long range using magnified optics and match ammunition.  
  * **Sniper Rifles:** Calculating windage, bullet drop, core Coriolis effect, and firing single-shot extreme range kills.  
  * **Automatic Fire Walking:** Firing long full-auto bursts while walking the muzzle stream onto moving enemy lines.  
  * **Kinetic Anti-Materiel Rifles:** Firing massive .50-caliber or 20mm kinetic rounds to destroy engine blocks and light armor.  
  * **Concealed Carry Quick-Draw:** Clearing clothing, drawing a hidden sub-compact pistol, and firing within one second.

### 

### **HEAVY WEAPONS**

* **Ability:** Agility (or Intellect for calculated trajectories/guided systems)  
* **Description:** The use of large-scale weaponry, often requiring two hands, a tripod, or a vehicle mount. This includes machine guns, rocket launchers, and artillery systems.

* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Launched/Thrown Ordinance:** Grenades and missiles use this skill for targeting.

  * **Area Effect (Blasting):** Attacks target a specific area rather than a single defense score.  
    * **General Area:** Targets within the blast radius take base damage. For every **5 points** the attack roll exceeds a **CR of 10**, the damage increases by **\+2 Dice**.

    * **Focused Blast Zone:** Targets in the center of the blast take **\+100% Damage Dice** if the attack roll exceeds a **CR of 15** by 10 or more.

* **Specialties:**   
  * **Squad Automatic Weapons (SAWs):** Firing belt-fed light machine guns to lay down suppressing fire on enemy tree lines.  
  * **Heavy Mounted Machine Guns:** Operating spade-gripped, tripod-mounted .50-caliber guns against light vehicles and infantry.  
  * **Unguided Rocket Launchers:** Shoulder-firing unguided RPGs and anti-tank rockets into bunkers and tank treads.  
  * **Smart Missile Systems:** Painting targets, locking thermal signatures, and firing fire-and-forget guided missile tubes.  
  * **Rotary Grenade Launchers:** Lobs of 40mm high-explosive, smoke, and tear-gas canisters downfield in rapid succession.  
  * **Vehicle-Mounted Cannons:** Operating auto-cannons (25mm-40mm) mounted in armored vehicle turrets using fire computers.  
  * **Rotary Miniguns:** Spooling electric gatling guns to unleash 4,000 rounds per minute, chewing through light fortifications.  
  * **Man-Portable Mortars:** Dropping indirect-fire mortar shells into tubes, setting baseplates, and adjusting quadrant sights.  
  * **Stationary Defense Emplacements:** Manning fixed bunker machine gun emplacements, managing ammunition belts and barrel swaps.  
  * **Anti-Air Kinetic Flak:** Tracking fast-moving atmospheric aircraft with high-angle, proximity-fused explosive flak cannons.

## **ADVANCED COMBAT SKILLS**

These skills govern the use of high-technology weaponry that utilizes directed energy rather than solid projectiles.

* **Mechanic:** **2d10 \+ Skill Rank \+ Agility Modifier vs. Target's Defense**

### 

### **ENERGY**

* **Ability:** Agility  
* **Description:** Proficiency with personal, directed-energy weapons such as pistols and rifles. These weapons discharge various forms of lethal energy, from searing plasma to destabilizing sonic waves. Unlike ballistic weapons, energy weapons often carry secondary effects based on their energy type2222.

* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Attack Roll:** Standard ranged attack roll.  
  * **Critical Effects:** In addition to increased damage, specific energy types apply status effects on a Critical Hit3:

    * *Pyro (Plasma):* Burns target for ½ damage in the following rounds.  
    * *Cryo (Icer):* Freezes target (Slow/Snare effect).  
    * *Voltic (Electricity):* Shocks target (Stun effect).  
    * *Sonic:* Concussive force (Stun effect).  
    * *Corrosive:* Dissolves armor/structure (½ damage for next 2 rounds).  
    * *Grav:* Impact force causing Knockback.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Laser Pistols & Carbines:** Firing instant-hit coherent light beams that burn through flesh and thin sheet metal.  
  * **Plasma Rifles:** Hurling bolts of superheated gas that melt through composite plate and ignite secondary fires.  
  * **Particle Beam Carbines:** Firing high-velocity subatomic particle streams that deliver immense penetrating radiation trauma.  
  * **Sonic Stunners & Shatterguns:** Firing concussive acoustic shockwaves that knock targets unconscious or rupture organs.  
  * **Cryo-Projectors:** Spraying endothermic chemical streams that flash-freeze target limbs and shatter armor plates.  
  * **Arc & Voltic Rifles:** Discharging branching lightning bolts that jump across multiple metal-armored targets and stun.  
  * **Corrosive Spray Weapons:** Spraying pressurized acidic chemical streams that dissolve physical armor and melt hulls.  
  * **Grav-Pulse Blasters:** Firing high-density gravitational shockwaves that throw armored targets backward off feet.  
  * **Disruptor & Disintegrator Pistols:** Firing exotic matter-collapsing energy that breaks molecular bonds down to ash.  
  * **Thermal Microwave Lances:** Focusing invisible microwave radiation that cooks organic targets from the inside out behind cover.

### 

### **HEAVY ENERGY**

* **Ability:** Agility (or Intellect if integrated into vehicle/ship gunnery systems)  
* **Description:** The operation of massive, high-yield energy cannons. These weapons are typically vehicle-mounted, ship-mounted, or require a tripod setup due to their size and power consumption. They are designed to destroy armored vehicles, starships, and fortifications4444.

* **Mechanics:**  
  * **Attack Roll:** Standard ranged attack roll, often with range penalties reduced by targeting computers (Smart Links).  
  * **Area of Effect:** Many heavy energy weapons (like Particle Beams) strike in a line or an area blast rather than a single point.  
  * **Penetration:** Heavy energy weapons typically possess high Penetration values, ignoring significant amounts of Damage Resistance (DR).  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Ship-to-Ship Laser Batteries:** Firing megawatt turrets to slice through starship hull plating and sensor domes.  
  * **Orbital Particle Lances:** Aligning spine-mounted spinal cannons to fire devastating lance strikes through the atmosphere.  
  * **Heavy Plasma Mortars:** Lobbing massive globes of burning thermonuclear plasma onto planetary siege fortresses.  
  * **Singularity Projectors:** Firing unstable microscopic gravitational vortexes that crush tanks and pull down walls.  
  * **Spinal Railguns & Coilguns:** Magnetically accelerating multi-ton slugs to relativistic speeds across solar distances.  
  * **Manned Fortress Energy Turrets:** Manning massive energy gun emplacements on planetary defense shields.  
  * **Tachyon Beam Cannons:** Firing high-energy faster-than-light particles that bypass conventional deflector shields.  
  * **Point-Defense Energy Arrays (PDC):** Tracking and vaporizing incoming torpedoes and starfighters with automated pulse lasers.  
  * **Macro-Ion Cannons:** Firing massive EMP and ion charges that disable starship power grids, engines, and shields without hull breach.  
  * **Coaxial Plasma Bombardment Cannons:** Directing planetary bombardment salvos from orbit to obliterate coastal cities.
