# **MCM REFERENCE MODULE E: BACKSTORY SCHEMA (LIFEPATH GENERATION)**

### **REFERENCE MODULE E: BACKSTORY SCHEMA (LIFEPATH GENERATION)**

A character's history determines their skills, assets, and connections. The Lifepath method is the gold standard for generating rich backstories. We categorize this into childhood, adolescence, and adulthood events to trace the character's trajectory. Game Masters and players utilize this sequence of chronological tables during character generation to systematically map the character's history. This process provides concrete assets, defines the psychological ghost, and grounds raw mechanics in lived experience.

&nbsp;

* #### **Childhood (0-12 Years) Facilitation Table**

During the initial phase of lifepath generation, Game Masters and players establish the foundation of the character's personality and their core psychological "Ghost" by choosing an option (or rolling a d10) from this facilitation table or describing their unique circumstance. These early events dictate their inherent cultural biases and primal survival instincts.

| D10 | EVENT ARCHETYPE | DETAILED NARRATIVE OUTCOME |
| ----- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | **Tragedy** | Parents killed in a raid, war, or accident. Home destroyed by orbital bombardment or natural disaster. Left an orphan or refugee. |
| 2 | **Privilege** | Born into a high-caste corporate or noble family. Genetically engineered for perfection. Tutored by A.I. or private masters. Sheltered from reality. |
| 3 | **Isolation** | Grew up on a lonely asteroid outpost, lighthouse, or deep forest. Sole survivor of a crash. Raised by machines or non-human entities. |
| 4 | **Labor** | Forced into child labor in mines, factories, or agricultural combines. Apprentice to a hard master. Street urchin scrambling for scraps. |
| 5 | **Discovery** | Found a strange artifact, secret passage, or hidden facility. Witnessed a crime or conspiracy that they shouldn't have seen. |
| 6 | **Mentorship** | Taken in by a master swordsman, hacker, or wizard. Raised by a religious order or guild. Groomed for leadership or a specific destiny. |
| 7 | **Wanderlust** | Family were nomads, traders, or travelers. Constantly moving between star systems or cities. Never had a real home or stable friends. |
| 8 | **Conflict** | Bullied relentlessly. Part of a youth gang. Had to fight to protect siblings or self. Learned violence early as a language. |
| 9 | **Anomaly** | Manifested strange powers (psionics/magic). Displayed genius-level intellect. Diagnosed with a rare condition or mutation. |
| 10 | **Normality** | A surprisingly mundane upbringing in a chaotic world. Anchors the character to "normal" life and provides a baseline to deviate from. |

&nbsp;

* #### **Adolescence (13-18 Years) Facilitation Table**

During the middle phase of lifepath generation, Game Masters and players determine the defining events of the character's teenage years by choosing an option (or rolling a d10) from this facilitation table or describing their unique circumstance. These events are recorded in the character's backstory profile to establish exactly where the character acquired their primary foundational skills, how they formed their core "Lie", and their initial stance toward authority and social bonds.

| D10 | EVENT ARCHETYPE | DETAILED NARRATIVE OUTCOME |
| :---: | ----- | ----- |
| 1 | **Military/Tactical Training** | Enrolled in a strict military academy or local militia. Gained discipline and weapons training, but subjected to intense indoctrination. |
| 2 | **Street Survival** | Abandoned or ran away to the lower city levels/streets. Learned stealth, theft, and survival, forming deep bonds with a local gang or syndicate. |
| 3 | **Corporate/Noble Education** | Attended an elite boarding school or private tutoring. Mastered bureaucracy, deceit, networking, and economics, but isolated from the struggles of the common classes. |
| 4 | **Arcane/Monastic Initiation** | Raised within a temple or metaphysical order. Taught history, meditation, martial arts, or arcane disciplines, forming a rigid moral or dogmatic worldview. |
| 5 | **Guild Apprenticeship** | Indentured or apprenticed to a trade guild or master artisan. Learned mechanics, crafting, or labor, building practical skills and a strong blue-collar network. |
| 6 | **First Love / Deep Betrayal** | Experienced a profound early romance that ended in tragedy or betrayal (e.g., partner died, revealed as a spy, or forced into an arranged political marriage). |
| 7 | **Radical Rebellion** | Rejected family tradition to join a radical political group or counter-culture movement. Hacked a server, protested, or experimented with illicit tech/substances. |
| 8 | **Early Criminal Record** | Forced into or willingly chose crime. Arrested for petty theft, killed someone in self-defense, or spent formative years in a juvenile detention facility. |
| 9 | **Exploration / Nomadic Duty** | Spent adolescence working on a freighter, scouting the frontier, or living with nomads. Learned piloting and navigation but struggled with permanent attachments. |
| 10 | **Tragic Awakening (Meta/Tech)** | Suddenly manifested uncontrollable psionic/magical powers or underwent a traumatic, non-consensual cybernetic augmentation, altering their life trajectory forever. |

&nbsp;

* #### **Adulthood/Career Events (19+ Years) Facilitation Table**

In the final stage of backstory creation, Game Masters and players finalize the character's current position, available resources, bitter enemies, and advanced skills by choosing an option (or rolling a d10) from this facilitation table or describing their unique circumstance. This establishes the immediate, logical context for exactly why the character is currently engaging the players.

| D10 | EVENT ARCHETYPE | DETAILED NARRATIVE OUTCOME |
| :---: | ----- | ----- |
| 1 | **War/Combat** | Fought in a major campaign. Decorated hero or traumatized veteran. Lost a limb (cybernetic replacement). Guilty of war crimes. Witnessed a massacre. |
| 2 | **Fortune** | Won a lottery. Inherited a ship or estate. Successful heist. Promoted rapidly. Found a valuable resource claim. Investments paid off. |
| 3 | **Disaster** | Financial ruin. Ship destroyed. Reputation destroyed by scandal. Addiction spiraled out of control. Faction collapsed. Medical debt. |
| 4 | **Betrayal** | Left for dead by a partner. Framed by a superior. Spouse took everything. Ideology proved false. Sold out to the enemy. |
| 5 | **Travel** | Explored unknown space. First contact with a new species. Spent years in cryo-sleep (time displaced). Exiled from homeworld. |
| 6 | **Connections** | Saved a powerful noble (Favor owed). Made a powerful enemy (Hunted). Joined a secret society. Recruited by intelligence services. |
| 7 | **Transformation** | Underwent major cybernetic augmentation. Religious conversion. Memory wipe/reprogramming. Extensive plastic surgery. |
| 8 | **Achievement** | Published a famous work. Invented a new tech. Founded an organization. Solved an impossible problem. Broke a record. |
| 9 | **Imprisonment** | Spent years in a penal colony. Enslaved. Prisoner of war. Trapped in a dimension/virtual reality. Under house arrest. |
| 10 | **Mystery** | A period of missing time. Wakes up with no memory. Receives anonymous messages. Unknowingly carrying a secret code or artifact. |
