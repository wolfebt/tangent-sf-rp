## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

> **PINNED MEMORY REFERENCE:** Keep this document in active context during all **BASTION** adjudication, Operator character creation, tactical combat resolution, and EUFT equipment, mecha, or architectural structure forging sessions.

Every calculation across **Volume I: The Operator's Manual** (`operator/`) and **Volume II: The Architect's Matrix** (`architect/`) is governed by these non-negotiable mathematical laws:

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.

* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.

* **Critical Hit & Fumble Resolution:**

  * **Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`):** `Rolled Dice + 10` (e.g., `20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`.

  * **Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`):** `Rolled Dice becomes flat -10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.

* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.

* **80-CP Archetype Chassis Breakdown:**

  * **Primary Attribute at +3:** `15 CP` (`5 CP` per `+1`)

  * **Secondary Attribute at +2:** `10 CP` (`5 CP` per `+1`)

  * **4 Essential Skills at Rank 6 (Trained):** `24 CP` (`1 CP` per Rank)

  * **6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3 (Novice):** `18 CP` (`1 CP` per Rank)

  * **2 Signature Traits (1 CP each) and 3 Recommended Core Features (9 CP):** `13 CP`

* **Background Skill Ranks:** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` as `20 SP Faction` + `20 SP Origin` + `20 SP Occupation`, alongside `4 Recommended Features` (`-1 CP` discount; `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and `4 Traits` at `0 CP` (`Extra Traits = 1 CP` each).

* **Universal Straight Attribute & Skill Pricing:** Across Species Packages (`1.03` / `99.Species`), Companion Packages (`99.Companion`), Bestiary Point-Buy (`04.03`), and Character Customization (`1.01`), `+1 Attribute = 5 CP`, `-1 Attribute Penalty = -5 CP refund`, and `1 Skill Rank = 1 CP` (`5 Skill Ranks = 5 CP`).

* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools:** Biological characters start with Base `30 Vitality` and Base `30 Health`; Synthetic/Construct entities combine both into Base `60 Structure Points (SP)`. All pools scale strictly at `+5 points per 1 CP` spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER** scaled by Stamina). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR.

* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Standard personal character hindrances have a `+10 CP` maximum limit; **Species-level hindrances and cybernetic Stigmas do NOT count toward the 10-point standard hindrance limit**.

#### 3. Wealth Score (WS), Liquidity Gap & Fence Rate

* **The Golden Rule of Wealth (Auto-Buy):** If `Item Crafting CR <= Character Wealth Score (WS)`, the character acquires the personal-use item automatically at `0` liquid Credit cost (`Purchase CR = Crafting CR`).

* **The Personal Use & Party Pooling Limits:**

  * Wealth Score covers personal equipment and reasonable squad support; bulk commercial acquisition requires speculative trade rules (`02.Economy.InterstellarTrade.md`) or triggers a Strain Check.

  * Operators **may pool liquid Credits** to buy shared assets, but **may NEVER add or combine their Wealth Scores** (four `WS 10` characters cannot combine WS to auto-buy a `WS 20` vehicle).

* **The Liquidity Gap Rule (`Item CR > WS`):** When purchasing an item whose Crafting CR exceeds the character's Wealth Score, the character pays the difference in liquid Credits (or rolls a `2d10 + WS vs. Item Cost CR` Wealth Check to finance the gap, risking `-2 WS` on delinquency):

  * `Liquid Cost = Value(Item CR) - Value(Character Wealth Score)`

  * *Worked Example:* An Affluent Operator (`WS 15`, Auto-Buy Value `640 Cr`) purchasing a `CR 18` Stealth Suit (`1,472 Cr`, or rounded `1,470 Cr`) pays `1,472 - 640 = 832 Credits` (or `830 Cr` rounded) in liquid cash.

* **Permanent Wealth Score Growth Formula:** Raising a character's permanent Wealth Score from `Current WS` to `Target WS` using liquid Credits requires investing the exact difference in tier values:

  * `Permanent WS Upgrade Cost (Credits) = Value(Target WS) - Value(Current WS)`

#### 3. Combat Mitigation, Precision Strike Gating & Mortality

* **Damage Mitigation Order & Diminishing Returns Armor Stacking:**

  * `Final Damage = max(0, Raw Damage - max(0, Effective Stacked DR - Weapon Penetration) - Target Stamina Modifier [Base Toughness])`

  * **Diminishing Returns Armor Stacking:** When multiple armor layers apply (across Biological/Natural, Cybernetic/Subdermal, Metaphysical, and Wearable domains), use the **single highest DR** as the base, and increase it by **`+1 DR` per stage of 5** for **each additional armor layer calculated separately** (`0 DR = +0`, `1–5 DR = +1`, `6–10 DR = +2`, `11–15 DR = +3`, `16–20 DR = +4`, i.e., `ceil[Layer DR / 5]` per additional layer).

  * **Weapon Penetration:** Weapon DR reduction is strictly termed **Penetration** (Tangent SF RP does not use `AP` for Action Points or Armor Penetration).

* **Feature-Gated Strike Consolidation (`Precision Strike` Chain):**

  * Strike consolidation is **not** a baseline combat option open to all combatants, and there is no Grazing Protocol or `+AP` consolidation mode.

  * Consolidating an attack into multiplied base weapon damage dice as a Standard Action requires possessing the **Precision Strike** Combat Feature chain (`1.08 FEATURES.md` / `1.08.CombatFeatures.md`):

    * **Precision Strike** (`Combat Skill 6` prerequisite): `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice`.

    * **Improved Precision Strike** (`Precision Strike, Combat Skill 11` prerequisite): `3x Base Weapon Damage Dice`.

    * **Greater Precision Strike** (`Improved Precision Strike, Combat Skill 16` prerequisite): `4x Base Weapon Damage Dice`.

* **The Mortality State & High Cost of Dying:**

  * At `0 Vitality`, overflow damage strikes **Health**.

  * At `0 Health`, the character enters the **Mortality State** with `Stability Points = Stamina Score + 5`, losing `1 Stability Point` per turn (plus additional points if taking damage while downed) until stabilized (`CR 15 Medicine`) or clinically dead at `0 Stability`.

  * **Revivification Penalty:** Returning from clinical death imposes **`-5 Experience Debt`** and drains the character's current **Karma** pool to `0`.
