### **2. Linear Micro-Modifiers (`+1` to `+4 CR` and `-1 CR` Flaw)**

For any Crafting CR that is not an exact multiple of 5, decompose `CR = Base_5_CR + Offset` and multiply the `Base_5_CR` Credit Value by the Linear Micro-Modifier:

* **`+1 CR` Offset:** `x1.30` (+30% of Base 5-CR Value)
* **`+2 CR` Offset:** `x1.75` (+75% of Base 5-CR Value)
* **`+3 CR` Offset:** `x2.30` (+130% of Base 5-CR Value)
* **`+4 CR` Offset:** `x3.00` (+200% of Base 5-CR Value)
* **`-1 CR` Flaw Offset:** `x0.75` (-25% of Base 5-CR Value)

* **Worked Example (CR 18 Item/Structure):** Decompose `CR 18 = CR 15 (640 Cr) + 3 CR (x2.30)`. Exact Value = `640 * 2.30 = 1,472 Credits`.

### **3. Wealth Score, Liquidity Gap & Fence Rate**

* **Auto-Buy Rule:** If `Item CR <= Wealth Score (WS)`, the character acquires the item automatically at `0` liquid Credit cost.
* **Liquidity Gap Rule:** If `Item CR > Wealth Score (WS)`, the character pays the difference in liquid Credits: `Liquid Cost = Value(Item CR) - Value(Wealth Score)`
* **Liquidity Drag (Fence Rate):**
  * **Legal Goods / Registered Real Estate:** Sells at **50%** of Credit Value.
  * **Black Market / Stolen Goods:** Sells at **20%–25%** of Credit Value.
  * **Scavenged Scrap / Salvage:** Retains **10%** of Credit Value (requires **5x** the target item's Credit Value in unrefined scrap mass to cover the 50% crafting material requirement).

### **4. The Productivity Engine**

* **Target Productivity Points (PP):** `Target PP = Item Credit Value`.
* **Daily Progress Formula:** `Daily PP = (Craft Check Result - 10) * Tool Tier Multiplier * Crew Size`
* **Tool Tier Multipliers:** Tier 0 Improvised (`x1`), Tier 1 Basic (`x10`), Tier 2 Advanced (`x50`), Tier 3 Industrial (`x200`), Tier 4 Nanoforge / Bio-Cultivation (`x1,000`), Tier 5 Genesis (`x5,000`).

### **2. Mecha Scale Multiplier & Garaging Rule**

* **Pre-Calculated Vehicle SP:** Structure Points (SP) listed in Vehicle/Mecha Size Tables are already pre-calculated for that scale. **Never multiply table SP a second time by the Scale Multiplier.**
* **Active Scale Multiplier:** Apply the chassis Scale Multiplier (`x1 Medium`, `x2 Large`, `x5 Huge`, `x10 Gargantuan`, `x20 Colossal`, `x40 Enormous`, `x80 Titanic`) strictly to **weapon damage, movement speed, and weapon range** during play.
* **Garaging Conversion (`1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier`):**
  * **Medium (`x1`):** `1 Mount` (`0.1 Modules`)
  * **Large (`x2`):** `2 Mounts` (`0.2 Modules`)
  * **Huge (`x5`):** `5 Mounts` (`0.5 Modules`)
  * **Gargantuan (`x10`):** `10 Mounts` (`1.0 Module`)
  * **Colossal (`x20`):** `20 Mounts` (`2.0 Modules`)

### **3. Combat Mitigation, Precision Strike Gating & Mortality**

* **Damage Mitigation Order:** `Final Damage = max(0, Raw Damage - max(0, Highest Single Domain DR - Weapon Penetration) - Target Stamina Modifier [Base Toughness])` *(Note: Biological, Cybernetic, and Wearable DR never stack; apply only the single highest DR. Weapon DR reduction is strictly termed **Penetration**—Tangent does not use `AP` for Action Points or Armor Penetration).*
* **Feature-Gated Strike Consolidation (`Precision Strike` Chain):** Strike consolidation is **not** a baseline combat option open to all combatants, and there is no Grazing Protocol or `+AP` consolidation mode. Consolidating an attack into multiplied base weapon damage dice as a Standard Action requires possessing the **Precision Strike** Combat Feature chain (`1.08 FEATURES.md`): **Precision Strike** (`Combat Skill 6` prerequisite -> `2x` Base Weapon Damage Dice), **Improved Precision Strike** (`Precision Strike, Combat Skill 11` prerequisite -> `3x` Base Weapon Damage Dice), and **Greater Precision Strike** (`Improved Precision Strike, Combat Skill 16` prerequisite -> `4x` Base Weapon Damage Dice).
* **The Mortality State & High Cost of Dying:**
  * At `0 Vitality`, overflow damage strikes **Health**.
  * At `0 Health`, the character enters the **Mortality State** with `Stability Points = Stamina Score + 5`, losing `1 Stability Point` per turn (plus additional points if taking damage while downed) until stabilized (`CR 15 Medicine`) or dead at `0 Stability`.
  * **Revivification Penalty:** Returning from clinical death imposes **-5 Experience Debt** and drains the character's current Karma pool to `0`.
