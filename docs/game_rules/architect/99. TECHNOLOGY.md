# **99. TECHNOLOGY FRAMEWORK & MASTER DIRECTORY**

## Overview & Mechanical Authority Block

Every rule, formula, and procedural matrix in this master compendium is governed by these canonical **Tangent SF RP** mechanical invariants:

* **Core Resolution Roll:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`.
* **Advantage & Disadvantage:** Roll `+1d10` per stack of Advantage (keep the highest 2 dice) or Disadvantage (keep the lowest 2 dice). Opposing stacks cancel 1-for-1.
* **Critical Hit & Fumble Resolution:** Critical Hit (`Natural 20` / double-10s on `2d10`, or expanded threat range `19`, `18`) equals `Rolled Dice + 10` (`20 -> 30`, `19 -> 29`) + `2x Base Weapon Damage Dice` + `Weapon/Elemental Critical Effect`. Fumble (`Natural 2` / double-1s on `2d10`, or expanded fumble range `3`, `4`) converts the rolled dice into a flat `-10` (`Base Score - 10`) + `Situational Complication`.
* **Character Point (CP) Economy:** `150 CP` starting Operator budget divided into an **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`15 CP` Primary Attribute at `+3`, `10 CP` Secondary Attribute at `+2`, `24 CP` for 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6, `18 CP` for 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3, and `13 CP` for 2 Signature Traits at `1 CP` each plus 3 Recommended Core Features at `9 CP`) and a **70-CP Customization Budget**.
* **Background Skill Ranks:** `60 Background Skill Ranks` allocated at `0 CP` as `20 SP Faction` + `20 SP Origin` + `20 SP Occupation`, alongside `4 Recommended Features` (`-1 CP` discount: `2 CP` instead of `3 CP`, minimum `1 CP`) and `4 Traits` at `0 CP` (`Extra Traits = 1 CP` each).
* **Universal Straight Pricing:** `+5 CP` per `+1` Attribute, `-5 CP` refund per `-1` Attribute penalty, and `1 CP` per `1` Skill Rank (`5 CP` per `5` Skill Ranks) across all creation, species, companion, and bestiary packages.
* **Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters start with **Base 30 Vitality** and **Base 30 Health**; Synthetic/Construct entities combine both into **Base 60 Structure Points (SP)**. Pools scale strictly at **`+5 points per 1 CP`** spent (Suggested Max: `60` each / `120 SP`; **NEVER scaled by Stamina**). Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR (`Armor Layering Cap`: only the single highest DR across bio, cyber, and worn armor applies).
* **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Standard personal character hindrances have a `+10 CP` maximum limit; **Species-level hindrances and cybernetic Stigmas do NOT count toward the 10-point standard hindrance limit**.
* **Economic Unified Field Theory (EUFT) & UDU Hierarchy:** Base 5-CR intervals follow `Value (Credits) = 10 * 4^(CR / 5)` modified by Linear Micro-Modifiers (`+1 CR = x1.30`, `+2 CR = x1.75`, `+3 CR = x2.30`, `+4 CR = x3.00`, `-1 CR Flaw = x0.75`). Pristine crafting materials cost `50%` of Credit Value; Scavenged Scrap retains `10%` value (`5x Item Credit Value` in unrefined scrap mass). UDU capacity scales at `10 Usable Nodes (Tier 0) = 1 Socket (Tier 1)`, `10 Sockets = 1 Mount (Tier 2)`, and `10 Mounts = 1 Module (Tier 3)` (`Garaging: 1 Mount = 1 Scale Multiplier`; vehicle table SP is pre-calculated).

## **2. ADVANCEMENT LEVELS & DEVELOPMENTAL PHILOSOPHY**

### **The Framework of Eras**

The framework of "Eras and Comparative Technology" serves as the definitive guideline for understanding the availability and sophistication of technology across the Tangent universe. This model is not a rigid ladder, but a fluid spectrum influenced by resources, environment, and cultural philosophy. By comparing a society's capabilities against these defined Levels, one can establish context for trade, conflict, and social sophistication.

### **Core Principles of Fluctuation**

1. **Uneven Development:** No two cultures progress at the same pace. A society may be TL 4+ in Ballistics (Warfare) but only TL 2 in Medicine.
2. **Resource Influence:** Availability of materials like Aetherium or Rare Earths dictates the path. An aquatic species (e.g., Mobula) may skip Combustion (TL 1) entirely for Chemical/Hydro-pressure tech.
3. **Technological Stagnation:** High-tech does not mean "New." The Impyrium operates with TL 4 Heirloom Technology maintained by rote tradition rather than scientific innovation.
4. **Non-Development:** Not every technology is universally adopted. A society might never develop the wheel or gunpowder, leading to divergent pathways.

### **The Nuance of Progress (`-`, Standard, `+`)**

Each Tech Level (TL) is divided into three distinct sub-strata:

- **Nascent (`TL-`):** The technology is new, expensive, bulky, and often unreliable. It is the domain of experimental military projects or wealthy early adopters.
- **Standard (`TL`):** The technology is refined, mass-produced, and reliable. It is the baseline expectation for that era.
- **Advanced (`TL+`):** The technology has been miniaturized, optimized, and perfected. It borders on the capabilities of the *next* Tech Level but is achieved through current-era methods.

## **3. ADAPTIVE, MORPHIC & HOLOPHOTONIC TECHNOLOGIES (TL 3 – TL 5)**

This technology enables equipment to serve multiple functions by physically altering its form or creating a functional energy projection as needed:

- **Requirement:** Each equipment option must be loaded into the unit's **Design Library** and must be within the unit's physical and technical capability.
- **Limit:** Only **1 device** may be emulated per unit at a time.

### **Reconfiguration Mechanics**

Changing functions is initiated by a simple switch command or neural impulse:

| Tech Level | Reconfiguration Tier & Technology Type | Action / Time to Reconfigure | Stored Blueprints & Operational Scope |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TL 3** | **Adaptive (Nanotech / Biotech / Mechanical)** | **1 Minute (10 Rounds)** *(or Full-Round Action for dual-mode mechanical shift)* | Pre-engineered dual-mode shift or molecular nanotech reconfiguration. |
| **TL 4** | **Morphic (Programmable Matter / Picotech)** | **Move Action / 1 Full Round** *(available next round/action)* | **3 Blueprints** (+INT w/ Neural Link); reshapes into any solid tool, weapon, or garment of equal mass. |
| **TL 5** | **Polymatter (Quantum Wellstone)** | **1 Standard / Move Action** *(available next action)* | Thought-responsive liquid-metal/quantum lattice reconfiguration. |
| **TL 5** | **Holophotonics (Hard-Light Projection)** | **Instant (Swift / Move Action)** *(usable via Standard Action in same round)* | **Unlimited Library** (PR 4 Core); instantaneous solid-light projection from a Fine emitter (0 Sockets when inactive). |

### **Schematics & Design Libraries**

Adding a new function to a device requires a digital schematic:

- **System Integration Check:** Requires a **Knowledge (Computers) Check** to compile and implement the schematic into the unit's system (`CR = 5 * Tech Level`: **TL 3 = CR 15**, **TL 4 = CR 20**, **TL 5 = CR 25**).
- **Schematic Market Acquisition (Social / Barter Checks):**
  - **Common Schematic:** `2x` to `5x` Base Cost of the item.
  - **Uncommon Schematic:** `5x` to `10x` Base Cost of the item.
  - **Rare / Restricted Schematic:** `10x` to `20x+` Base Cost of the item (if available).
- **Manual Blueprinting:** An industrious character with the proper skill set (`Vocation: Engineer/Weaponsmith` or `Knowledge: Technology`) can design and prototype their own schematic against the device's Crafting CR, followed by the system integration check.

## **4. THE TECHNOLOGIST FEATURE**

Characters with the **Technologist** feature (`operator/features/1.08.GeneralFeatures.md`) are ahead of the curve, understanding technology that shouldn't exist yet for their era:

- **Requirement:** To advance a full Tech Level (TL), a character requires multiple fields of advanced study. The feature must be selected multiple times across fields (**minimum of 5 fields** to fully progress to the next TL).
- **Scope:** TL 3+ characters may choose from TL 4 fields; TL 4+ characters may choose from TL 5 fields. TL 5 Technologists are generally NPCs unless the Architect allows experimental TL X discoveries.
- **Tech Disparity Penalty:** Without the Technologist Feature in the appropriate field, take a **-5 penalty per Tech Level of difference** on any check to use, repair, or modify a device above your native Technology Level. *(Exception: Straightforward items like basic weapons incur only a **-1 per TL** penalty to fire, but still require a check to wield special options).*
- **17 Fields of Study:** *Agriculture, Architecture, Biotechnology, Commerce, Communication, Devices, Education, Energy, Manufacturing, Materials, Medicine, Meta Sciences, Science, Society, Synthetic Intellect, Transportation, Weaponry.*
