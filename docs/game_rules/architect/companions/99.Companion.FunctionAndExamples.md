# **99.Companion.FunctionAndExamples.md**

## **MECHANICAL AUTHORITY & CANONICAL RULES BLOCK**

- **Core Resolution Engine:** `2d10 + Skill Rank + Attribute Modifier + Situational Modifiers vs. Challenge Rating (CR)`. Critical Hit (Natural 20 / double-10s) = `Rolled Dice + 10 (20 -> 30) + 2x Base Weapon Damage Dice + Critical Effect`. Fumble (Natural 2 / double-1s) = `Flat -10 + Complication`. Advantage/Disadvantage rolls `+1d10` per stack and keeps the highest or lowest 2 dice.
- **Character Point (CP) Economy & 80-CP Archetype Chassis:** Starting Operator budget is **150 CP**, partitioned into the **80-CP Archetype Chassis** (`15 CP` Primary Attribute at `+3`, `10 CP` Secondary Attribute at `+2`, `24 CP` for 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6, `18 CP` for 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3, and `13 CP` for 2 Signature Traits at 1 CP each + 3 Recommended Core Features at 9 CP) plus **70 CP Customization**.
- **60-SP Sociological Background Allocation:** **Faction (20 SP) + Origin (20 SP) + Occupation (20 SP) = 60 Skill Ranks, 4 Features, and 4 Traits at 0 CP** (additional traits cost 1 CP each).
- **Universal Straight CP Pricing:** Attributes cost **5 CP per +1** (and refund **-5 CP per -1 penalty**); Skills cost **1 CP per 1 Skill Rank** (**5 CP per 5 Ranks**) across all character, species, companion, and adversary builds with zero package discounts. Standard Features cost **3 CP** (**2 CP** when listed under **Recommended Features**, minimum 1 CP).
- **Vitality, Health & Structure (Zero Stamina Scaling):** Biological characters begin with **Base 30 Vitality** and **Base 30 Health** (Synthetics/Constructs combine both into **Base 60 Structure Points [SP]**), scaled strictly at **+5 points per 1 CP** spent. **Stamina (STA) never increases Vitality or Health**; Stamina modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming damage as **Base Toughness** after highest single-domain Armor DR (`Stability Points = STA + 5`).
- **Personal Hindrance Cap:** Maximum **+10 CP** gained from personal Hindrances at character creation; **Species-level disadvantages and Stigmas are exempt** from this cap.

## **PART 2: THE 5 FUNCTION PACKAGES (25 CP JOB)**

Select **one** Function Package (25 CP) to combine with the chosen Form Package (15 CP):

### **1. The Striker (Combat Focus — 25 CP)**

*Designed to neutralize threats.*

- **Attributes (15 CP):** +2 Strength (or Agility) \[10 CP\], +1 Stamina \[5 CP\].
- **Skills (10 CP):** *Unarmed / Natural Combat* Rank 5 \[5 CP\], *Athletics (Chase/Climb)* Rank 3 \[3 CP\], *Intimidate* Rank 2 \[2 CP\].

### **2. The Guardian (Defense Focus — 25 CP)**

*Designed to shield and protect the operator.*

- **Attributes (15 CP):** +2 Stamina \[10 CP\], +1 Strength \[5 CP\].
- **Skills (6 CP):** *Unarmed (Grapple/Restrain)* Rank 3 \[3 CP\], *Alertness (Spot Danger)* Rank 3 \[3 CP\].
- **Features (4 CP):** *Loyal Protector* (Intercept hit for adjacent master) \[2 CP\], *Armor Proficiency* (Can wear barding/plating) \[2 CP\].

### **3. The Scout (Recon Focus — 25 CP)**

*Designed to observe without being detected.*

- **Attributes (15 CP):** +2 Agility \[10 CP\], +1 Wisdom \[5 CP\].
- **Skills (10 CP):** *Stealth* Rank 5 \[5 CP\], *Alertness* Rank 3 \[3 CP\], *Knowledge (Survival — Tracking)* Rank 2 \[2 CP\].

### **4. The Interface (Tech / Utility Focus — 25 CP)**

*Designed to slice systems, repair hardware, and assist engineering.*

- **Attributes (15 CP):** +2 Intellect \[10 CP\], +1 Agility \[5 CP\].
- **Skills (10 CP):** *Knowledge (Computers / Security)* Rank 4 \[4 CP\], *Vocation (Mechanic — Repair)* Rank 4 \[4 CP\], *Knowledge (Investigation / Search)* Rank 2 \[2 CP\].

### **5. The Mount (Transport Focus — 25 CP)**

*Designed to carry a rider into battle (Requires Medium or Large Size).*

- **Attributes (15 CP):** +2 Strength \[10 CP\], +1 Stamina \[5 CP\].
- **Skills (6 CP):** *Athletics (Run/Jump)* Rank 4 \[4 CP\], *Acrobatics (Balance)* Rank 2 \[2 CP\].
- **Features (4 CP):** *Sure-Footed* (Ignore difficult terrain penalties) \[2 CP\], *Endurance* (Advantage on forced march/fatigue saves) \[2 CP\].

## **PART 3: COMPLETE 40 CP ASSEMBLY EXAMPLES**

- **Example 1 — The Cyber-Hound (40 CP):** *The Predator* (15 CP — Wolf Stats) + *The Guardian* (25 CP — Defense Stats).
- **Example 2 — The Spy Drone (40 CP):** *The Rotor-Drone* (15 CP — Flying, Tiny, Synthetic Immunities) + *The Scout* (25 CP — High Stealth/Alertness).
- **Example 3 — The War-Steed (40 CP):** *The Heavy Beast* (15 CP — Tough, Natural Armor) + *The Mount* (25 CP — Fast, Strong).
- **Example 4 — The Swamp Thrall (40 CP):** *The Plant* (15 CP — Walking Vine, Camouflage) + *The Guardian* (25 CP — Grapple, Protect).
- **Example 5 — The Crystal Familiar (40 CP):** *The Elemental* (15 CP — Hardness, Energy Strike) + *The Striker* (25 CP — High Damage).

## **PART 4: "THRALLS" — TINY BATTLE COMPANIONS COMPENDIUM**

A tactical catalog of ten **Tiny-sized** biological, synthetic, and metaphysical "Battle Pets" across the ten core Species Classifications, pre-calibrated with **Tiny Scale Modifiers** (+4 Combat Modifier to Strike/Defense, +8 Stealth Modifier, -2 Weapon Die Steps, -4 Strength):

### **1. Void-Leech Larva (Type: Aberration)**

- **Origin:** Sha'Nor / Dimensional Rift
- **Visuals:** A floating, segmented worm composed of shifting dark matter and smoke with a lamprey-like maw glowing with pale violet light.
- **Movement:** Hover 30 ft (Anti-Gravity / Levitation)
- **Defense:** **20** (`10 Base + 4 AGI + 4 Size + 2 Displacement`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Phase Blur*:** Exists partially in the Void, imposing **Disadvantage** on incoming physical attacks.
- **Offensive — *Psionic Nip* (Touch):** Inflicts `1d4` Psyche/Mental damage; target must pass a **Willpower Save (CR 12)** or become **Confused / Dazed** for 1 round.
- **Utility — *Mana-Scent*:** Detects active invocations, psionics, or dimensional rifts within 60 ft, glowing brighter as it nears the source.

### **2. Needle-Rat (Type: Beast)**

- **Origin:** Outworlds / Urban Scavenger
- **Visuals:** A wiry, hairless rodent with metallic quills along its spine and large reflective eyes adapted for total darkness.
- **Movement:** Walk/Run 40 ft, Climb 30 ft
- **Defense:** **18** (`10 Base + 4 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Evasion*:** Takes **0 damage** on a successful Reflex Save against Area of Effect attacks.
- **Offensive — *Dart-Quill* (Range 20 ft):** Fires a barbed biological quill dealing `1d2` Piercing damage plus **Neurotoxin** (**Fortitude Save CR 11** or movement speed is halved for `1d4` rounds).
- **Utility — *Scavenger's Nose*:** Grants **Advantage** on Alertness and Knowledge (Survival) checks to locate food, water, explosives, or narcotics.

### **3. Ember-Whelp / Wyrmling (Type: Dragon)**

- **Origin:** Dracon Dynasty
- **Visuals:** A hawk-sized miniature dragon with scales like cooling magma and wisps of smoke curling from its nostrils.
- **Movement:** Fly 50 ft (Winged), Walk 10 ft
- **Defense:** **19** (`10 Base + 3 AGI + 4 Size + 2 Natural Armor`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Magma Scales*:** Complete **Immunity to Pyro/Fire damage**.
- **Offensive — *Cinder Breath* (15 ft Cone):** Inflicts `1d6` Pyro/Fire damage (**Reflex Save CR 13** for half) and ignites flammable materials.
- **Utility — *Thermal Sense*:** Perceives infrared heat signatures through darkness and barriers up to 1 ft thick (**Darksight** + **Thermal Sight**).

### **4. Voltic Wisp (Type: Elemental)**

- **Origin:** High-Energy Nebulae / Mondi Space
- **Visuals:** A crackling sphere of blue-white plasma that orbits its master like a miniature satellite.
- **Movement:** Zip 60 ft (Flight / Short-hop Flicker)
- **Defense:** **22** (`10 Base + 8 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Ionized Form*:** Immune to non-magical Kinetic damage; vulnerable to water immersion and grounding nets.
- **Offensive — *Static Arc* (Range 30 ft):** Jumps between up to 2 targets for `1d4` Voltic damage each (**Advantage** on Strike rolls against metal-armored targets).
- **Utility — *Living Battery*:** Powers TL 2–4 handheld or suit devices indefinitely, or jump-starts a drained vehicle/automata core (1/day).

### **5. Echo-Mote (Type: Entity)**

- **Origin:** Dimensional / Ethereal Plane
- **Visuals:** A floating geometric shard of mirror-glass that reflects an impossible room and rotates silently on a non-Euclidean axis.
- **Movement:** Drift 30 ft (Incorporeal Phasing)
- **Defense:** **18** (`10 Base + 4 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Non-Existent*:** Can turn **Invisible** at will as a Move Action.
- **Offensive — *Memory Spike* (Range 60 ft):** Inflicts `1d2` Psyche damage, and the target must pass a **Willpower Save (CR 12)** or forget the last 6 seconds (**Dazed** for 1 round).
- **Utility — *Telepathic Relay*:** Acts as a mental router, allowing the master to communicate telepathically with any creature the Echo-Mote touches.

### **6. Briar-Sprite (Type: Fey)**

- **Origin:** Deep Wilds / Alterian Gardens
- **Visuals:** A 6-inch humanoid with walnut-bark skin and autumn-leaf wings, armed with a needle-spear.
- **Movement:** Flutter 40 ft
- **Defense:** **20** (`10 Base + 6 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Fey Grace*:** Rolls all Resistance Saves against metaphysical Invocations with **Advantage**.
- **Offensive — *Sleep Dust* (5 ft Burst — 1/Encounter):** Targets in the burst must pass a **Willpower Save (CR 14)** or fall into a magical slumber for `1d4` rounds.
- **Utility — *Nature's Guide*:** Projects bioluminescent *Lights* at will and can unlock any non-electronic wooden or mechanical latch by singing to it.

### **7. Solvent Slug (Type: Ooze)**

- **Origin:** Industrial Sector / Waste Reclamation Vats
- **Visuals:** A translucent, neon-green protoplasmic slug the size of a football that smells of sharp ozone.
- **Movement:** Slither 20 ft, Climb 20 ft (Suction)
- **Defense:** **14** (`10 Base + 0 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Amorphous*:** Immune to Critical Hits, Flanking, and Grappling.
- **Offensive — *Corrosive Spittle* (Range 15 ft):** Deals `1d4` Corrosive damage and reduces the target's Armor DR by **1 point** (cumulative until repaired).
- **Utility — *Matter Eater*:** Dissolves mundane manacles, padlocks, or thin metal grating in **1 round**, and cleans corroded gear instantaneously.

### **8. R-7 "Spyder" Bot (Type: Synthetic)**

- **Origin:** Syndicate / Mekan (Protocol Micro-Chassis)
- **Visuals:** A matte-black mechanical arachnid with a single crimson optic and magnetic-pad legs.
- **Movement:** Wall-Crawl 40 ft
- **Defense:** **18** (`10 Base + 2 AGI + 4 Size + 2 Plating`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Hardened Micro-Frame*:** Immune to Pain, Poison, and Disease; **DR 2**.
- **Offensive — *Shock-Web* (Range 10 ft):** Fires a conductive micro-tether dealing `1d6` Voltic damage and initiating a Grapple (+4 magnetic clamp bonus).
- **Utility — *Data-Siphon*:** Physically jacks into data ports to execute **Knowledge (Computers)** intrusions using its operator's Intellect and skill ranks, or records encrypted audio/video.

### **9. Carrion-Bird / Necro-Raven (Type: Undead)**

- **Origin:** Hostile / Entropic Worlds
- **Visuals:** A skeletal corvid with tattered feathers animated by cold cerulean fire in its eye sockets.
- **Movement:** Fly 40 ft (Silent)
- **Defense:** **16** (`10 Base + 2 AGI + 4 Size`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Dead Nerves*:** Immune to Stun, Fear, Poison, Disease, and Non-Lethal damage.
- **Offensive — *Festering Peck* (Melee):** Deals `1d4` Necrotic/Entropic damage; target must pass a **Fortitude Save (CR 12)** or be unable to recover Health naturally for 24 hours.
- **Utility — *Sense Mortality*:** Automatically detects any living creature below **50% Health** within **1 mile**.

### **10. Spore-Pod Symbiote (Type: Verdant)**

- **Origin:** Auluran Clans / Thorn Groves
- **Visuals:** A walking bulb of fibrous plant matter on root-legs whose blossom opens to reveal crystalline sensory organs.
- **Movement:** Root-Walk 20 ft
- **Defense:** **17** (`10 Base + 1 AGI + 4 Size + 2 Barkskin`)
- **Defensive Trait — *Photosynthetic Regeneration*:** Recovers **1 Health/Structure at the start of every turn** while exposed to natural sunlight or UV lamps.
- **Offensive — *Thorn Burst* (10 ft Radius):** Detonates a spray of needles dealing `1d4` Piercing damage to all adjacent enemies.
- **Utility — *Nutrient Synthesis*:** Grows `1d4` **Restorative Fruits** per day; each fruit restores 1 Health and provides a full day's caloric nourishment.
