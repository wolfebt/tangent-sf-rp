# **04.03 BESTIARY, XENOFAUNA & PROGENITOR COMPENDIUM**

## **I. XENOFAUNA ARCHITECTURE & SCALING RULES**

All non-sapient beasts, xeno-predators, biological swarms, humanoid adversaries, and automated ruin guardians in the Tangent universe are constructed using the **Modular Character Matrix (`99. MODULAR CHARACTER MATRIX.md`)** and **Modular Species Matrix (`99. MODULAR SPECIES MATRIX.md`)**:

1. **Threat Tier Baseline (Tiers 0 – 20):** Establishes the creature's base Attribute bonus, Primary/Secondary Skill Ranks, Action count, base Vitality/Health (`30 + Tier Bonus`), and base Damage Resistance (DR).
2. **Size Scale Multiplier (`operator/1.10 SCALING.md`):** Multiplies the creature's **Weapon/Natural Attack Dice**, **Base Movement Speed**, and **Vitality & Health (or Structure) Pools**:
   - **Tiny (`x0.5` / `-2 die steps`):** +4 Combat Mod, +8 Stealth, -4 Strength.
   - **Small (`x0.66` / `-1 die step`):** +2 Combat Mod, +4 Stealth, -2 Strength.
   - **Medium (`x1` Baseline):** +0 Combat Mod, +0 Stealth, +0 Strength.
   - **Large (`x2`):** -2 Combat Mod, -4 Stealth, +2 Strength, 10 ft Reach.
   - **Huge (`x5`):** -4 Combat Mod, -8 Stealth, +4 Strength, 15 ft Reach.
   - **Gargantuan (`x10`):** -8 Combat Mod, -16 Stealth, +8 Strength, 20 ft Reach.
   - **Colossal (`x20`):** -16 Combat Mod, -32 Stealth, +16 Strength, 25 ft Reach.
3. **Boss & Apex Multipliers:** An **Apex / Alpha** specimen designated as a Boss multiplies its size-scaled Vitality and Health pools by **x2** and gains **Legendary Resilience (1–3/Day)**.

### **Point-Buy Adversary Construction & Threat Scale**

In addition to the Tier Chassis, custom creatures, drones, and NPCs may be constructed directly using the **Character Point (CP)** economy:

- **Fodder (0 – 25 CP):** Weak creatures, untrained civilians, or basic drones. Dangerous only in swarms.
- **Standard (26 – 50 CP):** Competent professionals, dangerous predators, or soldiers. A 1-on-1 match for a starting character.
- **Elite (51 – 100 CP):** Veteran combatants, large alpha predators, or heavy mecha. Requires teamwork to defeat.
- **Boss / Legend (101+ CP):** Major story antagonists, massive distinct entities, or master-tier NPCs.
- **Custom Point-Buy Pricing:** Attributes cost **5 CP per +1 modifier** (and refund **-5 CP per -1 penalty**; no package discount); Skills cost **1 CP per 1 Rank** (**5 CP per 5 Ranks**); Features apply at their listed CP cost.
