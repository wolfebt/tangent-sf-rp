### **1. Eco-Warrior**

- **Archetype Profile:** A fierce defender of the natural world, skilled in combat and survival, willing to fight to protect the environment from exploitation and destruction.
- **80-CP Archetype Chassis Allocation:** Primary Attribute `+3` (`15 CP`), Secondary Attribute `+2` (`10 CP`), 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6 (`24 CP`), 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3 (`18 CP`), plus 2 Signature Traits (`1 CP` each) and 3 Recommended Core Features (`9 CP` at `2 CP`/`3 CP` discounted rate + `2 CP` skill/save rounding = `13 CP`).
- **Recommended Skill Suite:** Athletics, Stealth, Survival, any two Combat skills
- **Signature Traits & Recommended Features:** Guerrilla Tactics: You gain a +2 bonus to Stealth and Survival checks when operating in wilderness or natural environments. Nature's Fury: You can channel the power of nature to enhance your attacks, gaining +2 bonus on any checks related to using environmental hazards or nature as a weapon.

### **2. Empowered Combatant**

- **Archetype Profile:** A combatant adept is a highly skilled warrior who excels in physical combat with the Metafocus abilities being mostly internalized improving their physique. They may possess enhanced strength, agility, and reflexes, allowing them to overpower their opponents. Their abilities could include powerful strikes, unmatched speed, or the ability to endure significant amounts of damage. They may work as mercenaries, bodyguards, or elite soldiers. - Soldier Background is suitable and will likely have several of the Special Ability Features.
- **80-CP Archetype Chassis Allocation:** Primary Attribute `+3` (`15 CP`), Secondary Attribute `+2` (`10 CP`), 4 Essential Skills at Rank 6 (`24 CP`), 6 Supporting Skills at Rank 3 (`18 CP`), plus 2 Signature Traits (`1 CP` each) and 3 Recommended Core Features (`9 CP` at `2 CP`/`3 CP` discounted rate + `2 CP` skill/save rounding = `13 CP`).
- **Recommended Skill Suite:** Athletics, Acrobatics, any three Combat skills.
- **Signature Traits & Recommended Features:** Empowered Strike: Increase damage die by one with your unarmed or natural attacks (ie 1d6 base damage to 2d6). Iron Skin: You gain a +2 bonus to Defense. Empowered Adrenaline: Gain +2 bonus on all attack rolls for up to 5 rounds before a short rest is needed.
