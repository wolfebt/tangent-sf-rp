## **I. THE ASTROGRAPHIC ARCHITECTURE OF KNOWN SPACE**

Known Space in the Tangent universe is divided into four macro-political regions linked by Precursor **Translux Jump Gates**, **Hyperlux Corridors**, and independent **Tangent Drives**:

1. **The Core & Corporate Spires:** High-population, high-law worlds dominated by the **Syndicate**, the **Dracon Dynasty**, and the neutral sanctuary of the **Entari Combine**.
2. **The Imperial & Aeld Reaches:** Ancient sectors contested by the fractured Houses of the **Impyrium Dominion**, the crystal worlds of the **Alterian Enclave**, and the progressive **Ascendancy of the Seventh Reach** beyond the Reach.
3. **The Copia Sector & Free Colonies:** Over forty industrial and agricultural frontier systems governed by the **Coalition of Independent Worlds**, bordering the lawless **Outworlds** and the orbital **Scrapyard**.
4. **The Deep Wilds & Anomaly Zones:** Bio-terraformed expanses held by the **Auluran Clans**, the **Vajar Primalcy**, the **Kitin Star Hive**, the Dyson megastructure of **Mekan Prime**, and quarantined rifts such as **Omin II** and **Sha'Nor** space.

## **III. DETAILED PLANETARY PROFILES (KEY SECTOR HUBS)**

### **1. Premius (The Corporate Core)**
- **TWP String:** `A:8:5:6:10:1:7:4:2` | **Faction:** Incorporated Planetary Syndication (Capital)
- **Geophysical Chassis:** A Large (Size 8, 1.1G) world orbiting a stable Class-G yellow star. Nearly all urban population is concentrated into 300- to 500-story nanocarbon arcologies (Megacities Alpha, Beta, and Gamma) under the **80/20 Preservation Rule**, while heavy manufacturing operates in orbital **Halo** rings.
- **Sociological & Economic Profile:** Home to over 10 billion corporate citizens (Pop 10). Governed directly by the Syndicate Board of Directors (Gov 1), where citizenship is employment and law is enforced by algorithmic surveillance (Law 7–9) and **Syndicate PsiCorp** monitors. Below the gleaming white and cerulean spires lie the **Restriction Zones ("Friction Layers")**, where unsubscribed laborers and native **Premian Mystics** venerating *The Lady* survive.

### **2. Copia ("The Gilded Heart" of the Free Colonies)**
- **TWP String:** `A:7:4:7:9:4:4:3:1` | **Faction:** Coalition of Independent Worlds (Capital)
- **Geophysical Chassis:** An Earth-norm (Size 7, 1.0G) garden-industrial world ringed by orbital grain elevators and heavy fission shipyards.
- **Sociological & Economic Profile:** While nominally the democratic capital of over 40 free frontier systems (Gov 4), Copia is dominated by wealthy clan councils and industrial magnates practicing *"Noble Stewardship."* Copia exports bulk foodstuffs, heavy machinery, and refined Aetherite across the sector while staffing its frontier wars with **Colonial Rangers** (penal legions fitted with neural compliance shunts known as *"The Bit"*).

### **3. Entari Prime ("The Jewel" & The Bastion Atheneum)**
- **TWP String:** `A:7:4:7:9:4:3:4:3` | **Faction:** The Entari Combine (Capital)
- **Geophysical Chassis:** A lush, parametric eco-luxury Garden world (Size 7, 1.0G) where sandstone and amber force-glass towers rise seamlessly from crystal lakes and curated forests.
- **Sociological & Economic Profile:** Transformed by Patron Marcus DeVree into the galaxy's premier neutral sanctuary and financial clearinghouse (Law 3–5). Entari Prime houses the **Bastion Research Atheneum**—the physical data-core of the benevolent Hyper-Intellect **BASTION**—alongside the **Helix-Med Center** and the **Amber-Spire Banking Hub**. Peace is maintained non-lethally by the **Solar Guard** and **Concierge Automata**.

### **4. Altheria ("The Gilded World")**
- **TWP String:** `A:7:4:6:8:12:5:4:4` | **Faction:** Alterian Enclave / Aeld Providence (Capital)
- **Geophysical Chassis:** Orbiting a long-lived Class-K orange star (0.95G), Altheria is a high-resonance (ML 4) world of bioluminescent silver-leaf forests, levitating islands, and towering sunglass spires.
- **Sociological & Economic Profile:** Ruled by a gerontocratic council of elven High Arcanists (Gov 12), Altheria treats magic and physics as a single unified discipline. Its cities harvest stellar and ley-line energy through crystalline solar sails, exporting masterwork Magi-Tech weapons, Sun-Wine, and diplomatic mediation.

### **5. Aurelia (The Imperial Throneworld)**
- **TWP String:** `A:8:4:4:10:11:10:3:3` | **Faction:** Impyrium Dominion (Capital)
- **Geophysical Chassis:** A heavy, world-paved monument planet (1.15G) dominated by Neo-Sumerian brutalist ziggurats, white marble plazas, and miles-high **Zenith Spires**.
- **Sociological & Economic Profile:** Following the vanishings of the bio-engineered **Regi**, Aurelia is locked in a tense bureaucratic cold war between the **House of the Sun**, the **House of the Iron Gate**, and the **House of the Veiled Star** (Gov 11, Law 10). Its legions wield ancient TL 4 Heirloom Luxtech maintained by rote ritual because the Dominion's current industrial base (TL 3) can no longer replicate its own golden-age relics.

# **ALTERIAN CONCLAVE**

Elves are an ancient and long-lived race with intricate politics steeped in traditions. A king or council rules them, depending on the clan structure. There are numerous clans, which have become distant enough to become sub-races of Altierians. The differences between the sub-races are mainly cosmetic and philosophical. Elves have moderate technology, but they incorporate magic into their equipment.

Elves are a proud and noble race, and they take great pride in their heritage. They are also a very spiritual race, and they believe in the power of magic. Elves are skilled warriors, and they are also very knowledgeable about the natural world. They are a peaceful race, but they will fight to defend their homeland and their people.

  - **Royal Clans:** Lanaran, Lansmarr, and Silvermoon (ruling a ¼ of Conclave each) – plus a dozen other minor clans
  - **Territory:** 100+ worlds

**Notable Systems/Planets:**

  - **Lumanara (The Starlight Market):**

      - **Region:** Conclave Border / Lanaran Sector
      - **Stats:** Pop 7 (Mercantile), Tech 3 (Magitech), Meta 4 (Arcane), Law 6 (Trade)
      - **Primary Export:** Exotic Goods, Spells services, Diplomacy
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Aurora Skies)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *Bazaar of the Fates.* As the nearest trade world to the border, it is a melting pot. Mercantile and Appraisal checks gain Advantage. Prices for rare magic items are 10% lower here.
      - **Locations:** The Lanaran Exchange, The Prism Spire, Gate of Whispers.
  - **Silvermoon Prime (The Lunar Spire):**

      - **Region:** Conclave Core
      - **Stats:** Pop 4 (Select), Tech 3 (Magitech), Meta 4 (High Arcana), Law 7 (Traditional)
      - **Primary Export:** Mana Crystals, Silver-Steel, Enchantments
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Eternal Twilight)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *Moonlight Weaver.* Magic cast under the open sky here functions at +1 Caster Level. Non-elves suffer Disadvantage on Stealth checks due to the pervasive luminescence of the flora.
      - **Locations:** Palace of Silver, The Reflection Pools, High Temple of the Moon.
  - **Veridia (The Endless Forest):**

      - **Region:** Conclave Outer Rim
      - **Stats:** Pop 5 (Dispersed), Tech 2 (Naturalist), Meta 4 (Druidic), Law 3 (Tribal)
      - **Primary Export:** Rare Herbs, Exotic Timber, Beasts
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Oxygen Rich)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *The Living Canopy.* The forest is semi-sentient. Survival checks are required to navigate off-path (CR 15). Failure results in the terrain shifting to mislead the traveler.
      - **Locations:** Root-Hold Citadel, The Whispering Grove, Hunters' Rise.
  - **Corastra (The Twin Jewels):**

      - **Region:** Gemini System
      - **Stats:** Pop 6 (Dual-Society), Tech 3 (Standard), Meta 3 (Astrological), Law 5 (Balanced)
      - **Primary Export:** Solar Energy, Gemstones, Art
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Bright)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *Binary Influence.* The binary star system (Castor and Pollux) creates extreme day cycles. Characters not native to Corastra suffer a level of Exhaustion after 48 hours unless they succeed on a Stamina save (CR 12).
      - **Locations:** The Bridge of Two Suns, Castor’s Peak, Pollux Valley.

# **ASCENDANCY**

**The Psionic Architects of the Golden Age**

While the main body of the Impyrium crumbled into rust and stagnation, the Ascendancy seceded to pursue the next stage of human evolution. They believe the "Golden Age" was not just about territory, but about the elevation of the human mind. They have mastered **Psi-Tech**—crystalline circuitry and psycho-reactive materials that allow technology to be controlled by thought alone.

Culturally, they value mental discipline, clarity, and unity. Their cities are neo-classical utopias of white psycholith and conductive gold filigree, designed to resonate with the populace's latent psychic potential. To the Ascendancy, a tool that requires a button press is obsolete; a true machine should simply *know* its master's will.

  - **Territory:** 20+ worlds (The Psionic Sector)
  - **Aesthetic:** Art Deco / Neo-Classical High Tech. White marble structures laced with gold psi-circuitry, floating crystalline spires.

**Notable Worlds:**

  - **Aurelia (The Crystal Spire):** Capital World of the Ascendancy.

      - **Region:** Ascendancy Core
      - **Stats:** Pop 8 (Enlightened), Tech 4 (Psi-Tech), Meta 4 (High Psionic), Law 8 (The Consensus)
      - **Primary Export:** Psi-Crystals, Neural Interfaces, Education
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Psychically Attuned)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *The Noosphere.* A planetary-wide mental network allows for instant, silent communication between citizens. Characters with Psionic ability or Psi-Tech implants gain Advantage on Insight and Investigation checks. Non-psionics feel a constant, headache-inducing "hum" (Disadvantage on Concentration).
      - **Locations:** The Tower of Thought, The Psionic Gardens, The Grand Nexus.
  - **Kastel (The Mind-Bastion):**

      - **Region:** Ascendancy Border
      - **Stats:** Pop 7 (Psi-Militant), Tech 4 (Mind-Weaponry), Meta 4 (Tactical Link), Law 9 (Unity)
      - **Primary Export:** Psi-Knights, Thought-Shields, Will-Forged Alloys
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 1 (Terran / Charged)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *The Gestalt.* Military units here operate with a hive-mind efficiency. Initiative rolls for Ascendancy forces are made with Advantage. Enemy attempts to flank or surprise are negated by the collective awareness.
      - **Locations:** The Citadel of Will, The Null-Field Training Grounds, The Star-Forge.

# **COALITION**

**(AKA The Coalition Of Independent Worlds)**

The Coalition is a loosely united group of independent worlds and colonies. It is supposed to be a democratic republic, but it is often controlled by wealthy interests and is prone to corruption. Worlds and colonies must have a planetary government for admission qualifications, taxing its member worlds but offering the leadership supplemental forces as needed as ‘protection’ or defense force and often helping the leadership with secret policing and general intimidation of the citizens.

The Coalition is the backing agency of the Colonial Rangers, a ‘peace keeping force’ operating on the fringe which is mostly composed of ‘reformed’ criminals and mercenaries from the Dynasty and Syndicate.

  - **Territory:** 20+ worlds

**Notable Worlds:**

  - **Terminus (The Frontier Hub):** Second Capital of the Coalition.

      - **Region:** The Outworlds Border
      - **Stats:** Pop 5 (Medium), Tech 3 (Standard/Scavenged), Meta 2 (Rare/Mistrusted), Law 2 (Low/Wild West)
      - **Primary Export:** Refined Ores, Salvage, "No Questions Asked" Services
      - **Atmosphere:** Type 3 (Arid / Industrial Smog)
      - **Planetary Feature:** *Frontier Justice.* Firearms are legal/expected. Intimidate checks gain +2 Bonus. No public police; security is private.
      - **Locations:** The Rusty Spur, The Boneyard, The Free Senate Dome.

# **DRACON DYNASTY**

The Dracon Dynasty is a feudal republic founded by dragons and ruled by their draconic descendants. It is fairly well-governed, although some worlds within the dynasty have their own issues. Bureaucracy can be incredibly complex, but the dynasty is highly diplomatic. The dynasty has moderate technology, but excels in agriculture and architecture. The Dracon Dynasty is governed by a feudal caste system, with the following ranks  Serfs, Commoners, Knights, Barons, Counts, Marquis, Dukes, Archdukes, and the Chancellor Olivia Draconi, who is the head of the Grand Assembly (proxies of various rulers of the different systems) and the Dracon Family.

The Dracon Dynasty is a powerful force in the galaxy, and its influence is felt on many worlds. It is a complex and fascinating society, and its history is long and storied.

  - **Territory:** 100+ worlds
  - **History:** Claimed 17 centuries of rule by the Dracon Family (actually 800+)
