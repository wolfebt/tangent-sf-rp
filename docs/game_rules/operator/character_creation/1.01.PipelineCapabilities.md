### 1. Technology Level

The **Technology Level (TL)** stage of character creation in Tangent (`99. TECHNOLOGY.md`) involves determining your character's access to and understanding of technology. This is influenced by their species, origin, and faction, and has significant impacts on their skills, wealth, and available equipment:

* **Skill Bonuses (Education):** Characters gain specific Education Skill bonuses based on their technology level, reflecting the foundational literacy, knowledge, and technical expertise associated with that level of technological advancement.
* **Wealth Bonus:** Technology level also provides a **Wealth Score (WS)** modifier, representing the increased access to economic resources, infrastructure, and opportunities that come with advanced technology.
* **Equipment and Improvements:** The availability of equipment, augmentations, and other technological enhancements is directly tied to a character's technology level. Higher technology levels unlock more advanced and powerful options.
* **Skill Access:** The character's technology level may also limit their access to certain skills that require specific high-technology knowledge or training (`Computers`, `Piloting [Starship]`, or advanced `Knowledge [Technology]`) unless upgraded via the *Technologist* feature.

| Tech Level | Era | CP Cost / Refund | Education Skill Bonus | Wealth Modifier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TL 0** | Stone Age (Primitive) | **-30 CP** *(Grants +30 CP)* | `Alertness +2`, `Survival +2`, 3 Skills `+2` (Limited) | **-4 WS** |
| **TL 1** | Metal Age (Industrial) | **-20 CP** *(Grants +20 CP)* | 2 Vocations `+2`, 3–4 Skills `+2` (Limited) | **-2 WS** |
| **TL 2** | Data Age (Digital) | **-10 CP** *(Grants +10 CP)* | **+20 Education Skill Points** (Limited) | **+0 WS** |
| **TL 3** | Space Age (Galactic Baseline) | **0 CP** | **+25 to +30 Education Skill Points** | **+2 WS** |
| **TL 4** | Stellar Age (Enhanced) | **+10 CP** | **+40 Education Skill Points** | **+4 WS** |
| **TL 5** | Galactic Age (Advanced) | **+20 CP** | **+50 Education Skill Points** | **+8 WS** |

#### Walkthrough Example: Xy'larra (Technology Level)

> *Being an Alterian from the Alterian Enclave, Xy'larra is both tech-savvy and magic-adept, seamlessly blending futuristic Space Age tools with arcane arts (**Tech Level 3** at **0 CP**, **+2 WS**). Alterians are known for grace, agility, and magical talent, living in a Space Age society that values both innovation and tradition. She gains **+25 Education Skill Points** for skills of choice and has access to a full range of Meta training from her people due to its widespread use in Elven society.*
> * ***Allocation:** This is a good time to pick up additional foundational skills: she takes **Alertness 5** and **Unarmed 5**, and adds another **+5 each to Academics, Bluff, and Diplomacy** (raising each of those three skills from Rank 5 to Rank 10).*

### 2. Vitality, Health & Structure Pools (Zero Stamina Scaling)

Every biological character possesses two distinct damage buffers—**Vitality** (non-lethal endurance, luck, and fatigue buffer) and **Health** (lethal physical body and life force)—while Synthetics, Constructs, Oozes, and Undead combine both into a single **Structure Points (SP)** pool (`1.01.VitalityHealthStructure.md`):

* **Starting Baseline (`0 CP`):**
  * **Vitality:** **30 points**
  * **Health:** **30 points**
  * **Structure (Synthetics / Constructs):** **60 Structure Points (SP)** (`Vitality + Health` combined)
* **CP Pool Progression (`+5 Points` per `1 CP`):**
  * Spend **1 CP** to increase **Vitality by +5 points** (`+5 Vitality = 1 CP`).
  * Spend **1 CP** to increase **Health by +5 points** (`+5 Health = 1 CP`).
  * Spend **1 CP** to increase **Structure by +5 SP** (`+5 SP = 1 CP`).
  * **Suggested Starting Maximum:** **60 Vitality** and **60 Health** (`6 CP` invested in each pool), or **120 SP** for Synthetics/Constructs (`12 CP`).
* **Zero Stamina Scaling:** The **Stamina (STA)** attribute **never** increases Vitality, Health, or Structure pools. Instead, the Stamina Modifier subtracts point-for-point from incoming wound damage as **Base Toughness** after Armor DR and sets the character's **Stability Points (`STA + 5`)** / negative Health **Threshold of Death (`-STA`)**.

### 3. Hindrances

Players have the option to select **Hindrances** (`1.09 HINDRANCES.md`) for their characters, such as quirks, flaws, obligations, or disabilities. These Hindrances add depth and realism to the character, making them more relatable and nuanced. However, they also come with drawbacks that can hinder the character's abilities or create challenges in certain situations. In exchange for taking on these Hindrances, players are rewarded with additional **Character Points (CP)** that can be used to purchase other aspects of their character, such as skills, features, or equipment:

* **Personal Hindrance Cap (`+10 CP` Maximum):** A character may gain a maximum of **+10 CP** from personal Hindrances during character creation. Players may take additional flaws for narrative roleplay, but cannot gain more than `+10 CP` from them.
* **Species-Level & Stigma Exemption:** Species-level disadvantages and Xeno/Cybernetic Stigmas are accounted for within the Species or Augmentation package and **do not count toward the +10 CP personal Hindrance limit**.

#### Walkthrough Example: Xy'larra (Hindrances)

> *Although she did not have a destitute background, during her travels Xy'larra has gained a **Dependent**—Marla, a young cousin for whom she is responsible, but who happens to be somewhat capable, granting **+3 CP** to add to the character's total (a less capable dependent would garner more points, up to the `+10 CP` personal Hindrance cap). The Marla dependent may evolve into the Companion feature or an entirely separate character in time, but for now she is an Elven teenager traveling aboard ship with all the possible narrative implications of such.*

### 4. Features

The **Features** stage of character creation in Tangent (`1.08 FEATURES.md`) allows you to further customize your character by selecting features that enhance their capabilities. These features can represent a wide range of abilities, from innate talents and learned techniques to technological or metaphysical enhancements:

* **CP Cost (`3 CP` Standard / `2 CP` Recommended):** The default cost for a feature is **3 Character Points (CP)**, but this cost is reduced by **1 CP** (to **2 CP**, with a minimum cost of **1 CP** after all reductions) if the feature is listed as a **Recommended Feature** by the character's species, origin, faction, occupation, or archetype. This encourages players to consider features that complement their character's background and role in the game world.
* **Variety of Options:** Features provide a wide variety of options, such as increased resilience, improved skills or proficiencies, combat maneuvers, or unique abilities that set the character apart from others.
* **Strategic Choices:** Choosing features requires careful consideration, as players must balance the benefits of each feature against its CP cost and its relevance to the character's overall concept and playstyle.

#### Walkthrough Example: Xy'larra (Features)

> *In addition to the **Attractive** and **Educated** features gained from her occupation package, Xy'larra will want more features to focus and expand on her concept. Features cost **3 CP** each unless recommended from a character's aspect (such as occupation, at a minimum cost of **1 CP** after all reductions). In Xy'larra's case, **Skill Features** are recommended by her Representative occupation, reducing the cost of all Skill Features to **2 CP**—guiding her to be more skill-intensive and coupling with many features that grant bonuses and modifiers to skill checks.*
> * *Currently she has the following foundational Features:*
>   * **Attractive** (`+2` appearance bonus to social checks)
>   * **Educated** (`+2` trained bonus to Academics and `+1` trained bonus to all Knowledge checks)
>   * **Awakened x3** (to open **Attune** and the focus skills for the **Mental**, **Entropy**, and **Dimension** Disciplines)

### 5. Skills

The **Skills** stage of character creation in Tangent (`1.07 SKILLS.md`) involves allocating Character Points (CP) to enhance your character's various skills. Each rank of a skill costs a straight **1 CP** (`1 CP = 1 Skill Rank`, `5 CP = 5 Skill Ranks`, zero package discount; maximum starting rank at creation is **Rank 11 [Expert]**), allowing you to improve your character's competencies in different areas. Skills are categorized into five groups:

1. **Physical:** These skills govern physical actions and coordination, such as *Acrobatics, Athletics, Piloting, Riding, Stealth,* and *Thievery*.
2. **Mental:** These skills pertain to intellectual pursuits, analysis, and trade knowledge, including *Academics, Alertness, Computers, Investigation, Survival, Knowledge (Technology, Medicine, Science, Culture, History, Language, Navigation, Tactics),* and *Vocations*.
3. **Social:** These skills focus on interaction, performance, and communication with others, encompassing *Manipulation* (*Barter, Bluff, Diplomacy, Insight, Intimidate, Leadership, Streetwise*) and *Expression*.
4. **Discipline (Metaphysic):** These skills represent specialized training and focus associated with metaphysical practices (*Attune* and the 12 Focus Skills across the 6 Disciplines).
5. **Combat:** These skills determine a character's effectiveness in combat situations, including *Defense, Unarmed, Melee, Ranged, Ballistic, Energy, Heavy Weapons, Heavy Energy,* and *Demolitions*.

> * **Piloting:** `5`
> * **Alertness:** `5`
> * **Academics:** `10`
> * **Knowledge (Culture):** `5`
> * **Knowledge (Navigation):** `5`
> * **Vocation (Ambassador):** `5`
> * **Insight:** `10`
> * **Bluff:** `10`
> * **Diplomacy:** `10`
> * **Defense:** `5`
> * **Melee:** `5`

> * **Attune:** `5` *(Metaphysic Activation Skill)*
> * **Mental (Sense):** `10` *(Mental Discipline — Metaphysic Focus Skill)*

### 6. Augmentations

The **Augmentations** stage of character creation in Tangent (`2.00.Augmentations.md`) is optional and allows players to enhance their characters with permanent augmentations, such as cybernetics, bioware, or meta-tech modifications. These augmentations can offer significant advantages, such as increased attributes, enhanced senses, natural armor, or unique abilities. However, invasive implants also come with anatomical **Node** capacity limits and potential social Stigma drawbacks:

* **Commonplace / Civilian Implants (`0 CP`, `0 Nodes`):** Minor, non-invasive TL 3+ convenience implants (such as a subdermal *Comm Implant*, *ID Chip*, *Body Monitor*, or *Nano Groomers*) cost **0 CP** and **0 Nodes**, acquired via Wealth Score or Credits.
* **Invasive Augmentations (`Augmented` Feature + CP/Node Costs):** The **Augmented** Feature (*Augmented, Heavy, Extreme* — `3 CP` per tier, granting a `6 CP` augmentation purchase credit per tier) is required depending on the stage of augmentation, and each installed augmentation consumes its listed **CP** and anatomical **Nodes** (`10 Usable Nodes = 1 Socket`).

#### Walkthrough Example: Xy'larra (Augmentations)

> *Not wanting heavy surgical invasiveness, Xy'larra outfits herself with a subdermal **Comm Implant** (`CR 10`) and **ID Chip** (`CR 5`) for diplomatic convenience and negligible invasiveness (`0 CP`, `0 Nodes`).*
