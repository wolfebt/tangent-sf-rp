# **Energy Invocations**

The following Invocations belong to the **Energy Discipline**. They rely on the Attune skill for accuracy and CR setting, and the specific Discipline Skill (Energy: Elemental or Energy: Force) for shaping, intensity, and effect resolution.

  
  

## **ELEMENTAL INVOCATIONS**

Parent Skill: Energy

Specialization: Elemental

Description: The conjuration and manipulation of various forms of energy.

Base Damage: 1d6 per Stage.

**Elemental Critical Effects:**

  - **Pyro (Fire):** Burns on Crit. Target takes ½ Damage for the next 2 rounds.
  - **Voltic (Electric):** Shocks on Crit. Target is **Stunned**.
  - **Cryo (Ice):** Freezes on Crit. Target is **Slowed**.
  - **Sonic (Sound):** Booms on Crit. Target is **Stunned**.
  - **Corrosive (Acid):** Dissolution on Crit. Target takes ½ Damage for the next 2 rounds.

  

### **ANALYZE ENERGY**

Classification: Energy (Elemental) | Sensory | [Cantrip] [Analysis]

Parameters:

  - **Base Difficulty:** 5
  - **Time:** Swift Action
  - **Range:** Sight
  - **Area:** Single Source/Effect
  - **Duration:** Instantaneous
  - **Resistance:** None

Effect Description:

Analyzes an energy source or active effect to determine the amount of energy available (potential) or currently being used (kinetic/active).

**Scaling Function:**

  - **Precision:** Detail of analysis increases per Stage (Type -> Exact Magnitude -> Origin).
