## **PART II: COMPLETE UNABRIDGED PROFILES & RULES ENTRIES**

### **VISUAL AND SENSORY GUIDELINES FOR METAPHYSICS**

In the Tangent Universe, the "Meta" is not a single uniform force. It is shaped by the mind and culture of the wielder. A *Thermal Lance* cast by a Mekan logic-priest looks fundamentally different from one cast by a Draconian Sorcerer.

This guide expands on the sensory signatures—Visuals, Audio, and Texture—of the various Meta manifestations.

  

## **I. PSYCHIC MANIFESTATIONS (The Mind)**

*Root Discipline: Mental, Dimension, Energy (Force)*

Psychic power is the imposition of Will upon Reality. It often bypasses the "laws" of physics by simply rewriting local probability.

  

### **A. CONTROLLED (Logic / Telekinetic)**

**Archetypes:** The Mekan, The Analyst, The Tactician.

  - **Concept:** The mind as a computer or architect. Energy is efficient, wasted on nothing.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Defined Geometrics:** Effects manifest as perfect shapes—hexagons, fractals, or grids.
      - **Hard Light:** Shields and projectiles look like solid glass or laser-cut crystal. No smoke, no trail.
      - **Manifestation:** A telekinetic shield appears as a honeycomb wall of force; a mental blast is a perfect spear of light.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Electric Cyan:** The color of raw data.
      - **Circuitry Black:** Negative space that looks like printed lines.
      - **Neon Orange:** Warning/Combat mode indicators.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* High-pitched electronic whines, server hums, the sound of glass snapping.
      - *Texture:* Smooth, frictionless, cold static.

  

### **B. FOCUSED (Discipline / Telepathic)**

**Archetypes:** The Impyrium Centurion, The Monk, The Judge.

  - **Concept:** The mind as a lens. Power is gathered and projected in a singular, overwhelming vector.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Lines & Beams:** Energy moves in straight, unwavering lines.
      - **Solid Color:** No gradients. The energy is dense and opaque (e.g., a sword of pure white energy).
      - **Manifestation:** A "Command" spell manifests as a heavy, crushing weight on the air itself; a "Scan" looks like a flat plane of light passing over an object.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Solar White:** Blindingly bright and pure.
      - **Royal Purple:** Deep, regal, associated with high-level gravity manipulation.
      - **Gold:** Used for "Aura" effects and defensive wards.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* A deep, resonating thrum (like a massive bell), absolute silence.
      - *Texture:* Heavy, oppressive, warm (like sunlight).

  

### **C. WILD (Empathic / Raw)**

**Archetypes:** The Wild Talent, The Mondi, The Feral Psychic.

  - **Concept:** The mind as a storm. Power leaks, bleeds, and reacts to emotion rather than calculation.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Smoky Transitions:** Reality looks like watercolor paint bleeding into water. Edges blur.
      - **Melting Colors:** Colors shift rapidly based on the user's emotion (Red=Anger, Blue=Calm).
      - **Distortion:** Heat hazes, oil-slick rainbows, air warping around the user.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Bruised Violet:** The color of raw psychic force.
      - **Magenta:** Intense emotional output.
      - **Pearlescent:** Shifting, undefined colors.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* Whispering voices, static white noise, a heartbeat sound.
      - *Texture:* Viscous, humid, "pins and needles" sensation.

  
  

## **II. MAGIC MANIFESTATIONS (The Weave)**

*Root Discipline: Energy (Elemental), Matter, Illusion*

Magic is the hacking of the universe's source code using ancient formulas, rituals, or tools.

  

### **A. ARCANE (High Magic / Tech-Magic)**

**Archetypes:** The Alterian Arcanist, The Scholar, The Artificer.

  - **Concept:** Magic as Science. It is complex, formulaic, and elegant.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Runes & Sigils:** Glowing equations float in the air around the caster.
      - **Filigree:** Spells manifest as intricate silver or gold wireframes before filling with energy.
      - **Constellations:** Points of light connecting to form the spell structure.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Platinum Silver:** The standard for neutral arcane energy.
      - **Sapphire Blue:** Used for information and transport magic.
      - **Starlight:** Cold, distant white points.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* Crystalline chimes, the sound of turning pages, resonant harmonics.
      - *Texture:* Sharp, metallic, ozone smell (like a lightning strike).

  

### **B. NATURE (Primal / Verdant)**

**Archetypes:** The Thorn, The Auluran Shaman, The Druid.

  - **Concept:** Magic as Life. It grows, consumes, and cycles.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Motes & Spores:** The air fills with glowing dust or pollen.
      - **Haze:** Bioluminescent mists that obscure the caster.
      - **Rapid Growth:** Spectral vines or roots visible within the energy.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Toxic Green:** For acids and poisons.
      - **Amber:** For preservation and shielding.
      - **Sickly Yellow:** For decay and entropy.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* Rustling leaves, snapping branches, the chittering of insects.
      - *Texture:* Humid, sticky, organic, smell of loam and rain.

  

### **C. VOID (Entropic / Eldritch)**

**Archetypes:** The Sha'Nor, The Necromancer, The Warlock.

  - **Concept:** Magic as Absence. It deletes reality.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Negative Space:** Effects look like "holes" in the world where light cannot exist.
      - **Cracks:** Reality fractures like broken glass around the effect.
      - **Shadow Smoke:** Inky, fluid darkness that moves against the wind.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Vantablack:** Absolute absence of light.
      - **Cold Grey:** The color of ash.
      - **Invisible Distortion:** Only visible by how it bends light behind it.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* The sound of ice cracking, a vacuum sucking in air, unnerving silence.
      - *Texture:* Freezing cold, numbing, "wrongness."

  
  

## **III. DIVINE MANIFESTATIONS (The Will)**

*Root Discipline: Entropy (Order/Chaos), Energy (Radiant)*

Divine power is not about figuring out the universe (Arcane) or bending it (Psychic); it is about *commanding* it through conviction.

  

### **A. CELESTIAL (The Light / Order)**

**Archetypes:** The Ascendancy Judge, The Paladin, The Healer.

  - **Concept:** Authority. The power is undeniable and overwhelming.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Ethereal Rays:** Pillars of light descending from above.
      - **Halos:** Rings of energy forming behind the head or around the hands.
      - **Weightlessness:** Objects and debris float gently around the user.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Burnished Gold:** Warm, metallic, heavy.
      - **Blinding White:** Too bright to look at directly.
      - **Sky Blue:** Peaceful, restorative energy.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* Choir-like harmonics, a single clear trumpet note, thunder.
      - *Texture:* Warm, solid, comforting.

  

### **B. INFERNAL (The Flame / Chaos)**

**Archetypes:** The Cultist, The Destroyer, The Anarchist.

  - **Concept:** Corruption. The power burns and taints what it touches.
  - **Visuals:**
      
      - **Shadow Flame:** Fire that produces darkness instead of light.
      - **Jagged Arcs:** Lightning that moves in erratic, aggressive patterns.
      - **Corrosion:** Surfaces touched by the energy instantly rust or rot.
  - **Palette:**
      
      - **Blood Red:** Deep, visceral crimson.
      - **Sulfur Yellow:** Sickly and bright.
      - **Charcoal:** Burnt edges and ash.
  - **Sensory:**
      
      - *Audio:* Screaming metal, roaring fire, discordant screeching.
      - *Texture:* Burning heat, gritty ash, nauseating.
