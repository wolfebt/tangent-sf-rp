# **2.00.03 AUGMENTATIONS & CYBERNETICS (PART I: CORE, LIMBS, BODY MODS & SURGICAL PROTOCOLS)**

In the Tangent universe, the body is an agnostic chassis—upgradable, interchangeable, and capable of directed evolution. This catalog unifies Cybernetics (Replacements), Bioware (Enhancements), and Nanoware under Tech Levels (TL), Character Points (CP), and Tier 0 UDU Nodes (`10 Usable Nodes = 1 Tier 1 Socket`).

---

## **1. Introduction & Mechanics Overview**

The augmentation system provides a progression from basic prosthetics to god-like nanotech manipulation.

  

### **1. The Economy of Self**

1.  **Character Points (CP)**: Represents neurological and biological capacity. Standard mods cost 2 CP. Specialized traits or simple options cost 1 CP.
2.  **Nodes**: Represents the physical mass and room available in a limb or organ. If a body part's Node capacity is exceeded, the part malfunctions or suffers structural failure. Typically, Structure Points (SP) are matched 1-to-1 with Node Capacity.
3.  **Sockets**: Standardized hardpoints for modular gear and internal upgrades. Limb-based sockets are often pre-determined by the chassis. 1 Socket uses 10 Nodes worth of modifications.
4.  **Credits (Cr)**: The financial cost of hardware and surgery. Calculated via the formula: Value = 10 * 4^(CR / 5).

  

### **1. Tech Level (TL) Progression**

  - **TL 1-2**: Primitive/Functional replacements. Often 0 CP (Prosthetic).
  - **TL 3 (Standard)**: Parity with biology. The galactic baseline.
  - **TL 4 (Enhanced)**: Bioware/Seamless. Mechanic: Immune to disablement from Massive Damage.
  - **TL 5 (Advanced)**: Nanotech/Metaphysical. Mechanic: Costs 1/2 CP (minimum 1).

### **4. External Mounting Hardpoints (External Sockets)**

Characters may attach external mounts for augmentations onto their frame without (or in addition to) limb replacement.

  - **Cost**: 1 CP (Biological) and +2 Wealth CR to the gear's Base CR.
  - **Benefit**: Adds 1 Socket (or 10 Nodes) to the chosen location. Also certain devices may be detachable (+2 CR design cost).
  - **Limit**: A location cannot host more External Sockets than half its base Node capacity.

The following integration adapts the universal capacity, risk-reward overclocking, procedural surgery, and failure-state mechanics directly into the **Tangent RPG System (Matrix 99)**.

  

# **Unified Integration Framework for Tangent RP**

To harmonize physical capacity with neural/spiritual capacity, the Tangent augmentation economy unifies **Node Capacity** (physical volume/mass), **Sockets** (hardpoints), and **Character Points (CP)** (neurological and spiritual tolerance) into a single system.

  

Total Hardware Load  = Sum of Installed Component Nodes (Checked against Location Node Limits)  
Total Neural Strain  = Sum of CP Spent on Active/Passive Augmentations  
Meta-Tech Conversion = 1 Socket = 10 Nodes (Consumes structural volume from the host chassis)  
Economic Baseline    = Value (Cr) = 10 * 4^(CR / 5)  
  

Location Base Node Caps:  
Head: 10 Nodes (1 Socket) | Torso: 50 Nodes (5 Sockets)   
Arms: 30 Nodes each (3 Sockets) | Legs: 40 Nodes each (4 Sockets)  
Full Humanoid Chassis Capacity: 200 Nodes | 20 Sockets | 260 Structure Points (SP)  
  

# **Overclocking & Excess Strain Rules**

A character can deliberately exceed their structural Node limits in a limb/torso or push beyond their standard CP threshold by up to **25%**.

  - **Node Overfill (Structural Stress):** If installed augmentations exceed a location’s Node capacity, the limb/torso suffers structural degradation. The body part's maximum SP is reduced by 2 for every 1 Node over capacity, and physical checks using that location suffer Disadvantage.
  - **CP Overclocking (Neural/Cellular/Resonance Burnout):** For every 1 CP invested over the character's standard CP pool limit, the character loses 5 Maximum Vitality and rolls on the **Operational Overload Table** whenever they suffer a Critical Failure or take hard-counter damage (EMP for Cybernetics, Cytotoxin for Bioware, Null-Void for Meta-Tech).

  
  

# **Surgical Installation & Integration Procedures**

Installing augmentations requires an appropriate facility, surgical/transmutation downtime, and an installation skill check.

  

Installation Check Target CR = Base Item CR + Current Total Installed CP  
  

  - **Cybernetics/Hardware:** Requires an **Engineering (Cybernetics)** or **Medicine (Surgery)** check in a Tech Clinic.
  - **Bioware/Grafts:** Requires a **Medicine (Surgery)** or **Xenobiology** check (or a **Matter: Transmutation Invocation check**) in a Bio-Lab.
  - **Meta-Augs / Inscriptions:** Requires an **Attune / Invocation** check alongside surgery in an Attuned Sanctum or Meditation Forge.

  

## **Installation Outcomes**

  - **Critical Success (Beat CR by 5+):** Masterwork integration. Reduce the total Node footprint by 1 (minimum 1 Node) or increase the component's SP by +20%.
  - **Success (Met CR):** Clean installation. Functions at full operational parameters after the standard recovery downtime.
  - **Marginal Failure (Missed CR by 1–4):** Surgical rejection or wiring fault. The augment is installed but non-functional until recalibrated during downtime, and the patient rolls on the **Acute Surgical Complications Table**.
  - **Critical Failure (Missed CR by 5+):** Catastrophic failure. The component is destroyed/rejected, the patient suffers severe trauma, and rolls on the **Acute Surgical Complications Table** with a **+5 modifier**.
