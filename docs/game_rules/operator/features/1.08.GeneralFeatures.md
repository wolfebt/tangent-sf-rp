## **Assessment**

**Description:** The character has a practiced eye for sizing up opposition. By observing a target's stance, equipment, and demeanor, they can gauge the threat level.

  - **Prerequisite:** Alertness 6, Insight 6
  - **Benefit:** By observing a target for 1 full round, you can determine their relative threat level compared to yourself (Inferior, Equal, or Superior) and estimate their highest Skill Rank category (Novice, Trained, Expert, Master, Pinnacle).
  - **Purpose:** Allows players to gauge if a fight is winnable before initiative is rolled.

  

## **Analyze Weakness**

**Description:** The character applies their assessment to combat, identifying chinks in armor or behavioral tics that can be exploited.

  - **Prerequisite:** Assessment
  - **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make an **Assessment Check** opposed by the target's **Bluff Check**. For every 5 points by which your result exceeds the target's, you gain a cumulative **+1 bonus to Strike and Damage** against that specific target.
  - **Special:** This bonus lasts for the duration of the encounter or until the target successfully leaves your line of sight for more than 1 minute.

  

## **Benefit (Authority)**

**Description:** The character holds a recognized rank within a law enforcement or military organization, granting them legal power and jurisdiction.

  - **Prerequisite:** Associated Occupation (Soldier, Constable, Agent)
  - **Benefit:** You hold a specific rank.

<!-- end list -->

  - *Rank 1:* Deputy / Corporal (Local jurisdiction).
  - *Rank 2:* Officer / Sergeant (Regional jurisdiction).
  - *Rank 3:* Marshal / Lieutenant (Planet-wide jurisdiction).
  - *Rank 4:* Agent / Commander (Interstellar/System-wide jurisdiction).

<!-- end list -->

  - **Mechanics:** Grants **+2 to Intimidate and Diplomacy** when interacting with law-abiding citizens or lower-ranking officials.

  

## **Benefit (Clearance)**

**Description:** The character has official access to restricted information, government facilities, or corporate secrets.

  - **Prerequisite:** Vocation or Faction standing
  - **Benefit:** You possess a security key or biometric clearance.

<!-- end list -->

  - *Rank 1:* Classified (Departmental info).
  - *Rank 2:* Secret (Mission-critical info).
  - *Rank 3:* Top Secret (Strategic info).
  - *Rank 4:* Black Badge (Existential threats / Black Ops).

  

## **Benefit (Equipment)**

**Description:** The character is sponsored by an organization that provides them with gear, rather than purchasing it with personal wealth.

  - **Prerequisite:** Membership in a Faction or Corp
  - **Benefit:** You are issued a package of gear that does not count against your personal Wealth or Debt. Add +4 per rank to wealth for personal property acquisition (not usable by others).

<!-- end list -->

  - *Rank 1:* Standard Kit (Personal Gear, Tools).
  - *Rank 2:* Tactical Package (Weapons, Armor).
  - *Rank 3:* Mobility Package (Personal Vehicle, Heavy Weapons).
  - *Rank 4:* Command Package (Starship, Base of Operations).

  

## **Benefit (Identity)**

**Description:** The character has a meticulously crafted alternate persona, complete with background records, credit history, and identification.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** You gain one complete, verifiable Alternate Identity.
  - **Mechanics:** Checks to uncover your true identity are made at **Disadvantage**. If the identity is "burned" (exposed), this feature is lost until a new one is purchased/established.

  

## **Benefit (Immunity)**

**Description:** The character possesses a legal shield against prosecution for specific actions, usually granted by a government or mega-corp.

  - **Prerequisite:** Special (usually High Status or Agent)
  - **Benefit:**

<!-- end list -->

  - *Rank 1:* Order Immunity (Can claim "Following Orders" for minor infractions).
  - *Rank 2:* Diplomatic Immunity (Cannot be prosecuted by local planetary law).
  - *Rank 3:* Sanctioned (Authorized to commit felonies during mission parameters).
  - *Rank 4:* License to Kill (Authorized to execute targets at discretion).

  

## **Benefit (Status)**

**Description:** The character holds a title of nobility, corporate executive power, or high military command.

  - **Prerequisite:** Special
  - **Benefit:** You gain a Title (e.g., Duke, CEO, Admiral). You gain a **+2 Bonus to Wealth** checks.
  - **Special:** This acts as a \[Ranked\] feature; higher ranks denote higher titles and influence.

  

## **Benefit (Wealth)**

**Description:** The character is independently wealthy, possessing vast investments, inheritance, or liquid assets.

  - **Prerequisite:** None
  - **Benefit:** Increase your **Wealth Score by +3**.
  - **Purpose:** Allows purchasing of higher Tech Level gear or luxury items during character creation and downtime.

  

## **Connected**

**Description:** The character has friends in low (or high) places. They can call in favors for equipment, information, or safe houses.

  - **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1
  - **Benefit:** You have a network of contacts.

<!-- end list -->

  - *Rank 1:* Street/Soldier level (Gangs, Grunts).
  - *Rank 2:* Management/Lieutenant level (Shop owners, Squad leaders).
  - *Rank 3:* Officer/Executive level (City officials, Corp VPs).
  - *Rank 4:* Boss/Director level (Planetary Governors, Crime Lords).

<!-- end list -->

  - **Mechanics:** Make a Charisma check to call in a favor. Difficulty depends on the size of the request.

  

## **Contacts**

**Description:** The character knows who to talk to. They don't necessarily have favors owed, but they know the flow of information.

  - **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1
  - **Benefit:** You may make **Gather Information** checks in **1/2 the typical time**.
  - **Mechanics:** You have a specific reliable contact in a chosen field (Medical, Legal, Criminal) who provides information at **Advantage**.

  

## **Coordinated Assist**

**Description:** The character is a natural team player or squad leader, adept at setting up allies for success.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intellect 1
  - **Benefit:** When using the **Aid Another** action (Skill Synergy), you grant an additional **+1 Bonus** to your ally's check.
  - **Special:** This feature is \[Ranked\] by Intellect. (Rank 2 grants +2, etc.).

  

## **Climber**

**Description:** The character is at home on vertical surfaces, moving with the agility of a spider or monkey.

  - **Prerequisite:** Athletics 1
  - **Benefit:** You gain a **Climb Speed** equal to half your base speed. You may **Take 10** on Climb checks even when rushed or threatened. You retain your Agility bonus to Defense while climbing.

  

## **Distract**

**Description:** The character can cause a diversion so potent that it leaves opponents vulnerable.

  - **Prerequisite:** Charisma 1 or Intellect 1
  - **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make a Bluff or Engineering (for tech distraction) check vs. the Target’s Will.
  - **Success:** The target's attention is totally consumed by the distraction. They are considered **Flat-Footed** against all attacks from characters other than you until the start of your next turn.

  

## **Double Jointed**

**Description:** The character possesses extreme flexibility, able to dislocate joints or contort into impossible shapes.

  - **Prerequisite:** Agility 1
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+4 Bonus** on Acrobatics (Escapology) checks and any check involving fitting into tight spaces.

  

## **Eidetic Memory**

**Description:** The character remembers everything they see or read with fair accuracy.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+4 Bonus** on Memory Checks. You may use **Knowledge Skills Untrained**, as you recall snippets of information you’ve encountered previously.

  

## **Photographic Memory**

**Description:** The character's mind is a perfect recorder. Visual and textual information is stored instantly and permanently.

  - **Prerequisite:** Eidetic Memory, Intellect 4
  - **Benefit:** You recall images and texts perfectly. Study time for **Learning new Skills** is reduced by **50%**.

  

## **Endurance**

**Description:** The character has exceptional cardiovascular health and stamina for long-term exertion.

  - **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1
  - **Benefit:** Gain a **+4 Bonus** on checks to resist non-lethal damage from exhaustion, starvation, thirst, or forced marches. You can hold your breath twice as long as normal.

  

## **Diehard**

**Description:** The character is too stubborn or tough to die when they should.

  - **Prerequisite:** Endurance
  - **Benefit:** When your Health is reduced to 0 or lower (but not dead), you automatically **Stabilize**. You do not lose 1 HP per round. You may continue to take partial actions while at Death's Door, though you risk killing yourself if you take strenuous damage.

  

## **Fast Heal**

**Description:** The character's metabolism repairs tissue at an accelerated rate.

  - **Prerequisite:** Stamina 2
  - **Benefit:** Your natural healing rate is **doubled**. You recover Vitality and Health twice as fast during Rest.
  - **Special:** This feature is \[Ranked\]. Rank 2 = 3x healing, Rank 3 = 4x healing.

  

## **Regeneration**

**Description:** The character's body knits flesh back together in real-time.

  - **Prerequisite:** Fast Heal, Stamina 4
  - **Benefit:** You recover **1 Health point per hour** automatically without resting, provided you are not dead. You can regrow lost limbs and organs over 1d4 weeks.

  

## **Favored Enemy**

**Description:** The character has studied a specific type of creature extensively, knowing their anatomy, tactics, and psychology.

  - **Prerequisite:** Alertness 1
  - **Benefit:** Choose a creature type (e.g., Humanoid \[Human\], Beast, Synthetic, Aberration). You gain a **+2 Bonus** to **Strike**, **Damage**, **Bluff**, **Insight**, and **Survival** checks against them.
  - **Special:** \[Multiple\] Can be taken for different creature types.

  

## **Fearless**

**Description:** Whether through madness, conditioning, or bravery, the character is immune to terror.

  - **Prerequisite:** Will 3+
  - **Benefit:** You are **Immune** to Fear effects (magical, psionic, or mundane intimidation).

  

## **Jack-of-all-trades**

**Description:** The character has picked up a little bit of everything in their travels.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2
  - **Benefit:** You may attempt **any Skill check Untrained**, even those normally requiring training (like Medicine, Engineering, or Piloting).

  

## **Lightning Calculator**

**Description:** The character is a living computer, able to perform complex mathematics instantly.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intellect 1
  - **Benefit:** You perform mathematical calculations at **2x speed**. This applies to Astrogation, Engineering calculations, and Cryptography.

  

## **Mental Alacrity**

**Description:** The character thinks at hyperspeed, allowing them to perform mental tasks while others are still reacting.

  - **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2, Lightning Calculator
  - **Benefit:** You gain an additional **Mental Action** per round, provided you take no Physical actions that round.

<!-- end list -->

  - *Usage:* Useful for issuing commands, hacking, scanning, or using purely mental Psionics.

  

## **Multiplexing**

**Description:** The character's mind can process multiple parallel streams of thought.

  - **Prerequisite:** Mental Alacrity, Intellect 3
  - **Benefit:** Gain **+1 additional Mental Action** per round to your Mental Alacrity pool.
  - **Special:** \[Ranked\] Limited by Intellect modifier.

  

## **Master Plan**

**Description:** The character is a tactical genius who prepares for every eventuality.

  - **Prerequisite:** Logic 6, Intellect 3
  - **Benefit:** If you have time to prepare for an encounter (minimum 1 hour), make a **Logic Check**. For every point your result exceeds CR 10, you create a **Bonus Pool**.
  - **Usage:** During the encounter, you can distribute these points to allies (as a free action) to add to their Attack rolls or Skill checks.
