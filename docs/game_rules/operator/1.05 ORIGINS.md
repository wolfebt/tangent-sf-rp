# 1.05 ORIGINS

A character's Origin in Tangent represents the planetary environment or habitat where they were raised, shaping their foundational skills, traits, worldview, and economic baseline. This origin can be an entire world dedicated to a specific environment, or a particular area or habitat within a more diverse world. For example, a character with an Agricultural origin might come from a planet primarily focused on farming, or from a specific farming community within a larger, more varied world.

## Mechanical Benefits of Origin Selection

* **Origin Skill Package (20 Skill Points):** Every character receives **20 Skill Points (SP)** to distribute among their Origin's Society Skills at **0 CP** (part of the 60-Rank Background Allocation across Faction + Origin + Occupation).
* **Origin Traits (Select 2 Free):** Select **2 Origin Traits** from your Origin's trait list at **0 CP**. Additional Origin Traits may be purchased for **1 CP each** (Origin and Occupation traits are interchangeable if befitting the character concept).
* **Secondary Origin (Optional):** Players may select a secondary Origin to unlock access to its Society Skills and Trait list without gaining additional free skill points or traits, allowing for nuanced and multi-layered character backgrounds.
* **Origin Wealth Score Modifier (WS Mod):** Directly modifies the character's starting Wealth Score (WS) under the Tangent Economic Unified Field Theory (`02 ECONOMY AND WEALTH.md`).

II. CONSOLIDATED ORIGIN TRAITS CATALOG (Select 2 Free | +1 CP per Extra Trait)
01. Agricultural Traits
* Animal Husbandry: +2 to Vocation (Handler) checks with livestock and beasts.
* Botanical Knowledge: +2 to Knowledge (Nature / Botany) checks.
* Community Building: Once per day, reroll a failed Social skill check.
* Green Thumb: Roll checks involving nurturing, growing, or identifying plants at Advantage.
* Mechanical Skills: +2 to Vocation (Mechanic) and Knowledge (Technology) checks on agricultural or heavy machinery.
* Resourcefulness: Once per day, roll any one Skill Check of choice at Advantage.
* Survival Skills: +2 to Knowledge (Survival) checks (+3 in wild growth or heavy foliage).
* Sustainable Practices: +4 to Agriculture-related checks in a prepared grow area.
02. Aquatic Traits
* Adaptability: +2 to one chosen Skill Check (may reassign the chosen skill daily after a Full Rest).
* Aquatic Construction: +2 to Vocation (Architect / Builder / Engineering) checks for water-based or pressurized structures.
* Curiosity: Regain 1 spent Karma Point on the first encounter with a novel creature, location, or technology each session.
* Empathy: +2 to Insight and Vocation (Handler) checks while acting Friendly toward another creature.
* Environmental Awareness: +2 to Alertness checks.
* Sea Piloting: +2 to Piloting checks with submersibles, watercraft, hovercraft, and seaplanes.
* Resourcefulness: Once per day, roll any one Skill Check of choice at Advantage.
* Survival Skills: +2 to Knowledge (Survival) checks (+3 in aquatic or coastal environments).
03. Colony Traits
* Adaptability: +2 to one chosen Skill Check (may reassign daily).
* Community Spirit: +1 additional bonus when using the Aid Another action.
* Frontier Grit: +1 to Fortitude and Willpower Saving Throws.
* Jack-of-All-Trades: May attempt Untrained (Rank 0) skill checks without the -2 unskilled penalty.
* Jury-Rigging: +2 to Vocation (Mechanic) checks for improvised or emergency repairs.
* Resource Management: Roll Wealth Checks for basic survival necessities and rations at Advantage.
* Self-Sufficiency: +1 to maximum Karma Pool (or +2 Knowledge: Survival).
04. Enlightened Traits
* Curiosity: Regain 1 spent Karma Point on the first encounter with a novel metaphysical phenomenon, creature, or relic each session.
* Empathy: +2 to Insight and Vocation (Handler) checks while Friendly.
* Mentorship: Students under the character's tutelage roll Learning / Study Checks at Advantage.
* Open-Mindedness: Reduces all Social / Xeno Stigma penalties by the Enlightened character's Wisdom score (minimum 0) for both parties.
* Peaceful Nature: +2 to Diplomacy checks when mediating peace or de-escalating conflict.
* Problem-Solving Skills: Once per day, reroll a failed Mental Skill Check.
* Shared Wisdom: Roll any check to Aid, Guide, or Instruct an ally at Advantage.
* Spiritual Awareness: +1 to Attune and +1 to one Metafocus Discipline Focus Skill.
05. Hostile Traits
* Apex Vigilance: May Take 10 on Alertness checks even under stress or danger.
* Environmental Resistance: +2 to Fortitude Saves against poison, disease, radiation, and extreme weather.
* Hardened Survivor: +1 to Might and Fortitude Saving Throws.
* Predator's Instinct: +2 to Initiative checks.
* Scavenger's Eye: +2 to Vocation (Salvager) and Knowledge (Survival) checks to locate water, shelter, or usable parts.
* Stalker: May Take 10 on Stealth checks in natural or ruined terrain.
06. Industrial Traits
* Assembly Efficiency: Reduces crafting time by 10 percent (or grants +2 to Crafting/Vocation checks).
* Heavy Lifter: Counts as +1 Strength for lifting, hauling, and operating industrial exo-frames.
* Hazard Conditioning: +2 to Fortitude Saves against smoke, chemical fumes, and industrial heat/noise.
* Machine Affinity: +2 to Knowledge (Technology) and +1 additional bonus from Vocation Tool Kits.
* Shift Endurance: Rolls Fortitude checks against Fatigue from long work shifts at Advantage.
* Union Solidarity: +2 to Social checks with working-class laborers, mechanics, and guild members.
07. Leisure Traits
* Affluent Connections: +1 to Starting Wealth Score (in addition to the +3 Leisure Origin modifier) or Advantage on Gather Information checks in high society.
* Artistic Appreciation: +2 to Knowledge (Appraisal) and Expression checks.
* Charming: May Take 10 on Diplomacy checks in non-combat social settings.
* Cosmopolitan Etiquette: +2 to Etiquette (Charisma) checks and Knowledge (Culture / Nobility).
* High-Stakes Composure: +2 to Bluff checks when gambling or concealing motives at social functions.
* Silver Spoon: Starts with 1 mastercrafted personal luxury item (CR <= WS + 2) at no liquid cost.
08. Militaristic Traits
* Chain of Command: +2 to Leadership and Knowledge (Tactics) checks.
* Combat Training: +2 to one Combat Skill of choice (or +1 Attack with a chosen weapon group).
* Drill Conditioning: +1 to Might and Fortitude Saving Throws.
* Iron Discipline: +2 to Willpower Saving Throws against Fear and Intimidation.
* Rapid Deployment: +2 to Initiative checks.
* Tactical Coordination: Grants an additional +1 bonus when aiding an ally in combat.
09. Research Traits
* Analytical Mind: Once per day, reroll a failed Knowledge (Investigation) or Knowledge (Science) check.
* Data Synthesis: Roll Academics (Research) checks in libraries or databases at Advantage.
* Laboratory Safety: +4 bonus to prototyping checks to avoid chemical or technological mishaps.
* Methodical: Increases the bonus from Meticulous time investment by +1 per step.
* Peer Network: Gather Information checks within academic, scientific, or medical communities are rolled at Advantage.
* Tech Savant: +2 to Computers and Knowledge (Technology) checks.
10. Spacer Traits
* Astrogator's Instinct: +2 to Knowledge (Navigation) and Piloting (Space) checks.
* Damage Control: +2 to Vocation (Mechanic / Engineer) checks to patch hull breaches or ship systems under fire.
* Shipboard Comradeship: +1 additional bonus when aiding crewmates aboard a vessel.
* Vacuum Reflexes: Advantage on Reflex Saves to seal breaches or grab handholds during decompression.
* Zero-G Adaptation: Ignores the standard -4 Zero-G combat and skill penalty (99.Planetary.AstrophysicsAndGeophysics.md).
11. Urban Traits
* Crowd Runner: Ignores Difficult Terrain penalties caused by dense crowds or urban clutter.
* Digital Native: +2 to Computers checks when querying public networks or the Syndicate MESH.
* Fast Talker: +2 to Bluff checks when dealing with bureaucracy, customs, or city security.
* Networked: Roll Streetwise (Gather Information) checks in urban centers at Advantage.
* Street Smart: +2 to Streetwise and urban navigation checks.
* Underbelly Awareness: +2 to Alertness and Insight checks to spot pickpockets, tailing operatives, or urban ambushes.

### ---

**TEMPLATE C: CHARACTER CONCEPT SHEET**

*Designed for asset creation, production pipelines, and character design turnarounds.*

> Multi-angle character reference sheet, cinematic studio lighting, deep neutral gray backdrop with soft spotlighting, five-paneled array, (full body front view:1.5), (full body back view:1.5), (full body right side view:1.5), (full body left side view:1.5), and a detailed (face close-up profile:1.8), seamless blending between panels, maintaining consistent chiaroscuro, specular highlights, and volumetric form across all angles.

### ---

**TEMPLATE D: ARCHITECTURAL ESTABLISHING SHOT**

*Ideal for vast environments, cityscapes, interiors, and scale-heavy world-building.*

### ---

**TEMPLATE E: VEHICLE / MECH DESIGN**
