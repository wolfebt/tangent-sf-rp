### **NATURE**

  - **Ability:** Intellect (or Wisdom for practical application)
  - **Description:** Nature is the skill of understanding and interacting with the natural world. It reflects a character's ability to survive in the wilderness and understand the ecology of different environments. Nature is used whenever a character needs to navigate through the wilderness, whether it's to hunt for food or to avoid danger. This can include things like tracking animals, identifying plants, and surviving in extreme environments. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Identify Creature:** CR 10 + Creature's Rarity/CR.
  - **Foraging/Remedies:** CR 15 to find herbal antidotes or food.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Alien Botany:** Identifying toxic, medicinal, and nutritional properties of wild planetary plants.
      - **Zoology & Beast Behavior:** Predicting predator attack vectors, nesting grounds, and pack dynamics.
      - **Geology & Speleology:** Analyzing rock strata, seismic fault lines, mineral stability, and cavern layouts.
      - **Meteorology & Weather Forecasting:** Predicting dangerous ionic storms, hurricanes, acid rain, and sudden blizzards.
      - **Marine & Aquatic Ecology:** Navigating coastal shoals, identifying predatory sea life, and assessing tides.
      - **Toxicology Sourcing:** Extracting potent venom, paralyzing secretions, and irritants from wild creatures.
      - **Biome Tracking:** Identifying disturbed ground, broken twigs, scat, and animal territory scent posts.
      - **Ecosystem Modeling:** Assessing ecological collapses, invasive species introductions, and bio-cycles.
      - **Spelunking & Underground Survival:** Finding breathable air pockets, navigating cave-ins, and finding subterranean water.
      - **Micro-Ecology & Spores:** Detecting airborne fungal infestations, micro-parasites, and spore clouds.

  

### **NAVIGATION**

  - **Ability:** Intellect (Astrogation) or Wisdom (Survival)
  - **Description:** Navigation is the skill of understanding and using navigational tools. It reflects a character's ability to navigate through different environments, whether it's on land, sea, or air. Navigation is used whenever a character needs to travel to a specific location, whether it's to explore a new area or to deliver a package. This can include things like using a map and compass, piloting a ship, or flying a plane.
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Plot Course:** CR 10 (Familiar) to CR 25 (Uncharted/Hazardous).
  - **Astrogation:** Calculating FTL jumps (CR varies by distance and route stability).

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Astrogation & FTL Calculations:** Plotting jump-vectors through hyperspace, avoiding gravity wells and stars.
      - **Planetary Orienteering:** Traversing wilderness using compasses, star arrays, natural landmarks, and elevation maps.
      - **Cartography & Mapmaking:** Drafting detailed topographical, nautical, and three-dimensional celestial maps.
      - **Dead Reckoning:** Calculating current position based strictly on velocity, time, and previous heading.
      - **Subterranean Navigation:** Mapping complex cavern networks, mine tunnels, and under-city catacombs.
      - **Drift & Anomaly Routing:** Navigating around radiation belts, ion storms, asteroid fields, and spatial debris.
      - **Orbital Mechanics:** Calculating re-entry angles, geosynchronous orbits, and slingshot escape velocities.
      - **Nautical & Marine Navigation:** Charting ocean currents, reefs, depth soundings, and dead-water channels.
      - **Sensor-Blind Navigation:** Flying or driving purely on inertial instruments when sensors are completely jammed.
      - **Waystation & Gate Wayfinding:** Knowing established sector beacon networks, gate corridors, and toll routes.

  

### **NOBILITY**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Nobility is the skill of understanding and interacting with the aristocracy. It reflects a character's ability to navigate a society's social hierarchy and interact with people of high status. Nobility is used whenever a character needs to interact with the aristocracy, whether it's to gain political power or to secure a marriage alliance. This can include things like understanding court etiquette, negotiating with nobles, and identifying noble titles. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Identify Heraldry:** CR 10 (Major House) to CR 25 (Obscure lineage).
  - **Political Insight:** CR 15 to understand current political intrigues.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Imperial Dynasties:** Tracing succession charts, legitimate heirs, and marriage covenants of Great Houses.
      - **Heraldry & Insignia:** Identifying house crests, livery patterns, honor banners, and personal sigils.
      - **Aristocratic Etiquette:** Knowing correct forms of address, seating arrangements, and bow depths for high nobility.
      - **Feudal Law & Land Rights:** Interpreting ancestral planetary charters, fief titles, and aristocratic privileges.
      - **Court Intrigue & Factionalism:** Identifying secret alliances, covert blood feuds, and patronage webs among nobility.
      - **Chivalric Orders:** Knowing the codes, members, sworn oaths, and tournament rights of titled knightly groups.
      - **Diplomatic Immunity Protocols:** Invoking or circumventing noble sovereign immunity and peerage protections.
      - **Precedence Arbitration:** Resolving violent squabbles between visiting lords over status, rank, and parade priority.
      - **Royal Matchmaking:** Calculating political and financial yields of proposed inter-house dynastic marriages.
      - **Private Royal Treasuries:** Understanding the asset holdings, private guards, and sovereign wealth funds of dukes and kings.

  

### **PHYSICS**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Physics is the skill of understanding and applying the laws of physics. It reflects a character's ability to understand the physical world and use that knowledge to solve problems. Physics is used whenever a character needs to understand the behavior of physical objects, whether it's to design a machine or to solve a puzzle. This can include things like calculating trajectories, designing experiments, and understanding the properties of matter and energy. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Solve Problem:** CR 15 to CR 30 depending on complexity.
  - **Analyze Anomaly:** CR 20+ to understand spatial distortions or energy fields.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Astrophysics:** Modeling stellar life cycles, black hole event horizons, neutron stars, and accretion disks.
      - **Quantum Mechanics:** Calculating particle superposition, quantum entanglement communications, and tunneling.
      - **Relativistic Mechanics:** Accounting for time dilation, high-velocity mass expansion, and sub-light acceleration.
      - **Plasma & High-Energy Dynamics:** Regulating magnetic confinement bottles and thermonuclear energy streams.
      - **Gravitational Field Physics:** Calculating grav-plate fields, tidal forces, and artificial gravity generator grids.
      - **Ballistics Trajectory:** Calculating complex gravitational-assist projectile arcs across atmospheric wind shears.
      - **Fluid Dynamics:** Modeling atmospheric drag on hull profiles and turbulent flows through turbines.
      - **Thermodynamics:** Designing heat-sink radiation fins, thermal dissipation grids, and cooling loops.
      - **Subatomic Particle Acceleration:** Understanding antimatter creation, particle beam collation, and cyclotron arrays.
      - **Dimensional Warp Mechanics:** Modeling spatial curvature, Alcubierre bubble metrics, and metric tension.

  

### **RELIGION**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Religion is the skill of understanding and practicing religious beliefs. It reflects a character's ability to understand the beliefs and practices of different religions, and to practice their own religion. Religion is used whenever a character needs to interact with people of different faiths, whether it's to negotiate a peace treaty or to perform a religious ceremony. This can include things like understanding religious texts, performing religious rituals, and interpreting religious symbols.
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Identify Symbol/Rite:** CR 10 (Common) to CR 25 (Secret Cult).
  - **Theological Debate:** Supported by Diplomacy checks.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Pantheons & Cosmic Deities:** Recalling the domains, alignments, and mythologies of recognized sector gods.
      - **Sectarian Cults & Heresies:** Identifying outlawed apocalyptic sects, blood cults, and rogue pseudo-religious factions.
      - **Liturgical Rites & Rituals:** Conducting, interpreting, or disrupting formal consecrations, ordinations, and purifications.
      - **Scriptural Hermeneutics:** Quoting chapter and verse from holy texts to win theological arguments or sway monks.
      - **Funerary Rites & Afterlife Lore:** Knowing the death rites, ossuary designs, and spirit-warding traditions of cultures.
      - **Sacred Symbolism:** Decoding holy icons, divine glyphs, sacred geometries, and ceremonial vestment colors.
      - **Ascendant & Precursor Worship:** Understanding sects that deify ancient alien architects or god-machines.
      - **Prophetic Traditions:** Interpreting ancient religious prophecies, apocalyptic calendars, and omens.
      - **Ecclesiastical Law:** Navigating church property rights, clerical trials, and sanctuary protections.
      - **Exorcism & Cleansing:** Identifying canonical rites to purge metaphysical corruption or unclean entities from holy ground.

  

### **SCIENCE**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Science is the skill of understanding and applying the scientific method. It reflects a character's ability to understand the natural world and use that knowledge to solve problems. Science is used whenever a character needs to understand the behavior of natural phenomena, whether it's to design an experiment or to solve a mystery. This can include things like designing experiments, analyzing data, and interpreting scientific theories. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Lab Work:** CR 15 to perform experiments.
  - **Identify Substance:** CR 15 to CR 25.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Inorganic Chemistry:** Synthesizing acids, industrial catalysts, synthetic polymers, and inert shielding reagents.
      - **Biochemistry:** Analyzing cellular metabolism, amino acid structures, and enzymatic reaction triggers.
      - **Genetics & Cloning:** Splicing DNA, sequencing alien genomes, and managing cellular degradation in clones.
      - **Material Science & Metallurgy:** Testing tensile strength, molecular lattice bonding, and alloy fatigue.
      - **Nanotechnology:** Programming molecular assemblers, managing gray-goo dampeners, and repairing nano-swarms.
      - **Laboratory Procedure:** Operating centrifuges, mass spectrometers, clean-room environments, and electron microscopes.
      - **Atmospheric Chemistry:** Analyzing toxic air balances, terraforming gas mixtures, and greenhouse gas saturation.
      - **Radiological Science:** Measuring rems, half-lives, radioactive isotope decay, and managing fission shielding.
      - **Synthetic Biology:** Designing artificial bacteria to consume plastics, scrub hydrocarbons, or excrete fuels.
      - **Applied Spectroscopy:** Analyzing distant planetary atmospheres and compound solutions using optical light bands.

  

### **SURVIVAL**

  - **Ability:** Wisdom
  - **Description:** Survival is the skill of surviving in extreme environments. It reflects a character's ability to find food, water, and shelter in the wilderness, and to avoid danger. Survival is used whenever a character needs to survive in the wilderness, whether it's to explore a new area or to escape from danger. This can include things like finding food and water, building shelter, and avoiding predators. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Track:** CR varies by surface (Soft ground CR 10, Hard floor CR 20+).
  - **Survival:** CR 15 to provide food/water for self; +2 CR per additional person.
  - **Endure Elements:** Grants bonuses to Fortitude saves against weather.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Foraging:** Sourcing edible roots, insects, fruits, and clean sustenance in untamed wilderness.
      - **Water Procurement:** Locating hidden springs, building solar condensation stills, and testing water toxicity.
      - **Extreme Cold (Arctic):** Building snow shelters, preventing frostbite, and enduring sub-zero blizzards.
      - **Arid Wasteland (Desert):** Conserving bodily moisture, traveling by night, and finding shaded oases.
      - **Jungle & Marshland:** Evading quicksand, combating rot, avoiding parasitic leeches, and crossing bogs.
      - **Emergency Shelter Construction:** Rigging lean-tos, debris huts, windbreaks, and insulated thermal bivouacs.
      - **Zero-G & Vacuum Survival:** Patching suit punctures, surviving depressurization, and conserving suit oxygen.
      - **Subterranean Survival:** Navigating lightless deep caves, avoiding bad air pockets, and scaling internal chasms.
      - **Fire-Building:** Starting cooking and survival fires using flint, friction, or battery sparks in rain and wind.
      - **Wilderness Traps & Snares:** Constructing deadfalls, snare wires, and pit traps to capture wild game for food.

  

### **TACTICS**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Knowledge of strategy, warfare, and small unit operations. It allows a character to predict enemy movements and plan effective ambushes.
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Predict Strategy:** Opposed check vs. Enemy Tactics/Bluff.
  - **Combat Bonus:** A successful check (CR 15+) prior to combat might grant the team a +1 Initiative or Attack bonus for the first round (Architect discretion).

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Close-Quarters Battle (CQB):** Clearing rooms, stacking doors, leapfrogging down corridors, and fatal funnel control.
      - **Squad Fireteam Maneuvers:** Coordinating bounding overwatch, flanking suppression, and tactical withdrawals.
      - **Guerrilla & Asymmetric Warfare:** Setting up hit-and-run ambushes, sabotage supply raids, and partisan insurgencies.
      - **Ship-to-Ship Fleet Tactics:** Coordinating broadsides, torpedo spreads, screen escorts, and crossfire brackets.
      - **Siegecraft & Fortifications:** Establishing defensive kill zones, interlocking fire fields, and breaching fortified walls.
      - **Boarding Actions:** Breaching airlocks, fighting in tight starship corridors, and securing bridge/engineering hubs.
      - **Ambush Planning:** Selecting choke points, camouflage perimeters, and timed kill boxes along transit routes.
      - **Sniper/Spotter Protocols:** Calculating windage, ballistic drop, concealment blinds, and designated marksman security.
      - **Electronic Warfare Coordination:** Synchronizing jamming bursts with kinetic assaults to deafen enemy comms.
      - **Counter-Battery Warfare:** Calculating enemy indirect fire origins from impact craters to direct counter-strikes.

  

### **TECHNOLOGY**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** Technology is the skill of understanding and using advanced technology. It reflects a character's ability to understand the underlying principles of technology and general concept of its usage. 
  - **Mechanics:**

- **Repair:** CR 15 (Simple fix) to CR 30 (Rebuild from scrap).
  - **Knowledge (Technology):** CR depends on device complexity/security rating.
  - **Identify Tech:** CR 10 + Tech Level difference.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Archaic Technology:** Restoring and fabricating muscle-powered, spring, and clockwork mechanical systems.
      - **Advanced Directed Energy Systems:** Calibrating laser lenses, tuning particle coils, and replacing cooling magnets.
      - **Xenotechnology Diagnostics:** Assessing and coaxing function out of inscrutable, non-human technical artifacts.
      - **Robotics & Automata:** Wiring logic boards, replacing servo actuators, and troubleshooting utility droids.
      - **Weapons Technology:** Repairing weapon feed systems, accurizing barrels, and modifying firing pins.
      - **Starship Subsystems:** Diagnosing life support scrubbers, grav-plating, and airlock pressure valves.
      - **Sensor & Scanner Systems:** Calibrating ladar arrays, tuning thermal scanners, and repairing multi-spectrum probes.
      - **Power Generators & Fuel Cells:** Servicing hydrogen fuel cells, compact fusion reactors, and battery banks.
      - **Security Hardware:** Disabling magnetic mag-locks, biometric readers, and laser tripwire systems mechanically.
      - **Emergency Field-Stripping:** Disassembling, cleaning, and unjamming complex machinery while pinned under fire.

  

### **TRADE**

  - **Ability:** Intellect
  - **Description:** The skill of understanding and navigating the complexities of commercial exchange. It reflects a character"s ability to identify market needs, navigate trade routes, understand commercial laws, and manage mercantile relationships. Trade is used whenever a character needs to buy, sell, or transport goods. This can include things like negotiating contracts, assessing commodity values, and understanding trade regulations.
  - **Mechanics:**

- *Analyze Market:* CR 15 to identify trade opportunities, demand, or supply shortages.
  - *Trade Law:* CR 20 to understand tariffs, trade agreements, or restricted goods in a sector.

- **Specialties:** 
      
      - **Commodity Arbitrage:** Buying low in resource-rich frontier sectors and selling high in industrialized core worlds.
      - **Tariff & Customs Evasion:** Structuring cargo routing to legally minimize planetary import taxes and duties.
      - **Black Market Smuggling Networks:** Identifying covert delivery corridors, illicit fences, and bribe payoffs.
      - **Bulk Wholesale Brokering:** Negotiating multi-kiloton contracts for raw durasteel, food grains, and reactor fuel.
      - **Planetary Export Licensing:** Securing legal monopolies, trade charters, and merchant fleet landing passes.
      - **Supply Shortage Exploitation:** Reading war and famine indicators to position medical and ammo cargo ahead of demand.
      - **Currency & Credit Speculation:** Trading local planetary scrip against standard credits to capture exchange spreads.
      - **Merchant Marine Regulations:** Navigating international interstellar laws regarding distress signals and salvaging rights.
      - **Consignment Sales:** Negotiating display, sale percentages, and escrow protection for third-party luxury cargo.
      - **Counter-Embargo Smuggling:** Slipping essential food, fuels, and machine components past military naval blockades.
