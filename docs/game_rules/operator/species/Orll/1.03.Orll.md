# **ORLL (LINEAGE OVERVIEW)**

The Orll are a powerful, barrel-chested humanoid lineage forged by the unforgiving high peaks and freezing mountain woodlands of their ancestral worlds. Recognizable by their prominent upward-curving lower tusks, heavily muscled frames, and thick insulating coats of coarse fur, the Orll combine immense physical strength and resilience with natural light exoskeleton plating.
