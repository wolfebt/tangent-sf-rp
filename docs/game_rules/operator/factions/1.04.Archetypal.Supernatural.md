SUPERNATURAL
I. Detailed Faction Description
* Overview: Secretive factions characterized by their deep connection to supernatural, arcane, or occult forces. They operate in the shadows to protect their kind, maintain the balance of mystical power, and guard dangerous artifacts from the uninitiated.
* Key Themes: The Occult, Dark Fantasy, Hidden Magic, Secret Societies.
* Relationship to Others: Hidden. They view high-tech factions as ignorant children playing with forces they don't understand.
II. Core Identity
* Official Designation: The Hermetic Order / The Coven of [Name]
* Colloquialisms: The Occult / The Witches / The Magus
* Archetype: The Occult
* Capital/Key World: Variable (Hidden pocket dimensions, ancient terrestrial sanctums)
* Driving Mandate: Harness supernatural powers, protect occult artifacts, maintain the balance.
* Motto: "Magic is the blood of the universe."
III. Sociological Profile
* Ideology & Culture: Mysterious, powerful, otherworldly, mystical, secretive.
* Social Structure: Master-apprentice lineages and arcane circles.
* View on Outsiders: Ignorant mundanes who must be protected (or manipulated) from the shadows.
* Law & Order: Arcane oaths enforced by magical curses or geas.
IV. Strategic Assets
* Tech Level (TL): 3- (Rely primarily on magic over machinery)
* Strengths: Unique mystical abilities, access to supernatural resources, deep knowledge of ancient lore, reality manipulation.
* Weaknesses: Vulnerability to specific supernatural weaknesses (e.g., iron, salt), internal divisions, potential for magical corruption.
* Military Doctrine: Curses, ritual magic, summoning, and unseen assassinations.
V. Mechanics (Game Data)
* Prominent Species: Variable (Magic-sensitive species).
* Faction Skill Package (20 Points):
   * Occult (+5)
   * Discipline (Magic) (+5)
   * Attune (+4)
   * Stealth (+3)
   * Insight (+3)
* Typical Archetypes:
   * The Occultist: Uncovering and wielding dangerous, forbidden magical lore.
   * The Mystic: Harnessing deep spiritual power through meditation and ritual.
   * The Guardian: A magically enhanced warrior protecting the secretive covens.
   * The Shapeshifter: Taking on the forms of beasts or other people perfectly.
   * The Warlock: Deriving immense power from a dark, otherworldly pact.
   * The Summoner: Binding extra-dimensional entities to their will.
   * The Coven Leader: Guiding the secret society through wisdom and raw power.
   * The Artifact Hunter: Tracking down dangerous magical items before they fall into the wrong hands.
   * The Shadow-Mage: Manipulating darkness and entropy to hide the society's actions.
   * The Blood-Sorcerer: Using extreme, forbidden rituals to achieve impossible results.
* Recommended Features (1 CP Discount):
   * Awakened: The mandatory fundamental connection to metaphysical magic.
   * Magical Aptitude: A profound, natural resonance with the arcane.
   * Centering Expert: Casting spells flawlessly even when under physical attack.
   * Discipline Focus: Mastering a highly specific, dangerous school of magic.
   * Darksight: Seeing clearly through both mundane and magical darkness.
   * Sense: Detecting the presence of other magical entities or active spells.
   * Telepathy: Communicating silently with other members of the coven.
   * Iron Will: Resisting the mental corruption inherent in dark magic.
   * Evasiveness: Slipping away from direct magical trauma unharmed.
   * Familiar/Companion: Bonded with a supernatural entity or magical beast.
VI. Visual Semiotics (Aesthetics)
* Design Directive: Ethereal, ancient, and shadowy.
* Atmosphere Tokens: Glowing runes, dark shadows, magical artifacts, dusty libraries, hidden dimensional rifts.
* HI-FI INK Guidance:
   * SETTING STYLE: The Occult, Dark Fantasy, Urban Magic.
   * CONTEXT: Glowing runes, dark shadows, magical artifacts, dusty libraries, hidden dimensional rifts.
   * ATMOSPHERE: Ethereal, mysterious, powerful, ancient, secretive.
