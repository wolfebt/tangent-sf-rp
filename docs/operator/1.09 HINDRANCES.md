# **HINDRANCES**

**Hindrances in Tangent are character flaws, quirks, or hindrances that provide additional Build Points (BP) during character creation. These can add depth, realism, and role-playing opportunities to a character, but they also come with drawbacks or limitations.**  

**Types of Hindrances**  
Hindrances can be physical, mental, social, or a combination of these. Examples include phobias, addictions, chronic illnesses, physical disabilities, or even personality traits like impulsiveness or arrogance.

**BP Value**  
Each Hindrance has a BP value assigned to it, reflecting the severity of the drawback it imposes. More significant flaws grant more BP, allowing players to invest in other areas of their character.

**Balancing Act**  
While Hindrances offer a way to gain extra BP, players should be mindful not to overload their characters with too many flaws. A character with numerous severe hindrances might become difficult or unfun to play. The recommended limit is no more than 15 points of Hindrances.

**Role-Playing Opportunities**  
Hindrances can create interesting role-playing opportunities and challenges. Players can explore how their character's flaws affect their interactions with others, their decision-making, and their overall approach to the game world.

**GM Involvement**  
The GM plays a crucial role in approving Hindrances and ensuring they are appropriate for the setting and the character's concept. They may also offer suggestions or work with the player to develop unique Hindrances that fit the character's background and personality.

**Example**  
A character with a fear of heights (Acrophobia) might gain 6 BP, reflecting the significant impact this phobia can have on their actions and choices. This could lead to interesting role-playing moments, such as the character hesitating to climb a tall tower or panicking when crossing a narrow bridge.

Hindrances are an optional but valuable tool for character creation in Tangent. They enable players to create more complex, nuanced, and memorable characters, and they can enrich the role-playing experience by introducing challenges and opportunities for growth.

# **HINDRANCES COMPENDIUM**

## 

## **System Overview**

In the Tangent Role-Playing System, **Hindrances** are negative traits, flaws, or hindrances that a character possesses. Unlike standard features which cost Build Points (BP), Hindrances **grant** additional BP during character creation. This allows players to "buy" more positive traits, skills, or attribute scores by accepting specific limitations.

### 

### **Design Philosophy**

**Depth & Realism:** Hindrances are designed to flesh out a character's personality and history. A character with a phobia, a debt, or a missing limb is often more narrative-rich than a "perfect" character.

**Role-Playing Incentives:** These flaws provide hooks for the Game Master (GM) to create complications, driving the story forward.

**The Balancing Act:** While tempting for the extra points, players are cautioned against taking too many. A character crippled by flaws may become unplayable or a burden to the party.

### 

### **Mechanics & Limits**

**Build Points (BP):** Each Hindrance has a specific BP value (e.g., 2, 3, 6\) based on its severity. These points are added to the character's starting pool.

**Recommended Cap:** The standard limit for Hindrances is **15 BP**. Exceeding this requires GM approval and usually indicates a highly flawed or tragic character concept.

**GM Discretion:** The GM has the final say on whether a Hindrance is valid for the campaign setting. A "Fear of Water" Hindrance is free points in a desert campaign and may be disallowed or reduced in value.

## 

## **Terminology Distinction**

**Important:** Do not confuse **Hindrances (Character Flaws)** with the **Rolling with Hindrance** mechanic.  
**Hindrances (Capital D):** Permanent or semi-permanent character traits chosen at creation (e.g., "Blindness," "Debt").  
**Rolling with Hindrance (lowercase d):** A situational mechanic where a player must roll two d20s and take the **lowest** result.  
*Note:* Some Character Hindrances (like *Impaired Sense*) may cause a player to *Roll with Hindrance* on specific checks.

## 

## **Categories of Hindrances**

Hindrances are grouped into seven primary categories to help define the source of the trouble.

### **I. Background Hindrances**

*Relating to the character's past history, upbringing, or previous entanglements.*  
**Examples:** Dark Secret, Wanted by the Law (Mundane), Non-Monetary Debt, Sworn Enemy, Traumatic Past (Triggers), Unwanted Legacy.

### 

### **II. Mental Hindrances**

*Relating to cognitive function, memory, or intellectual barriers.*  
**Examples:** Poor Memory, Learning Disability, Arrogance, Paranoia, Impaired Reasoning, Low Self-Esteem.

### 

### **III. Metaphysical Hindrances**

*Relating* to magic, psionics, the soul, or supernatural *forces.*  
**Examples:** Curses, Divine Disfavor, Haunted, Magical Vulnerability (e.g., Cold Iron), Geas/Pact restrictions, "Weirdness Magnet."

### 

### **IV. Physical Hindrances**

*Relating to the body, health, and sensory capabilities.*  
**Examples:** Missing Limbs, Blindness, Deafness, Chronic Illness, Frailty, Low Pain Tolerance, Allergies, Physical Deformities.

### 

### **V. Psychological Hindrances**

*Relating* to behavior *patterns, compulsions, and personality restrictions.*  
**Examples:** Phobias, Addictions, Obsessions, Strict Codes of Honor, Vows of Pacifism/Silence, Impulsiveness, Greed, Laziness.

### 

### **VI. Resource Hindrances**

*Relating to assets, wealth, and equipment.*  
**Examples:** Poverty, Massive Financial Debt, Lack of Shelter, Dependency on Rare Substances (Maintenance).

### 

### **VII. Social Hindrances**

*Relating to standing in society, reputation, and relationships.*  
**Examples:** Bad Reputation, Minority/Xeno Stigma, Socially Awkward, Dependent (NPC), Debt of Honor.

## **Addiction**

BP Value: 3 (Minor) / 6 (Moderate) / 9 (Major)  
Category: Psychological / Physical  
The character has a chemical or psychological dependency on a substance or stimulus.  
**Minor (3 BP):** A bad habit (e.g., tobacco, alcohol, mild gambling). The character suffers \-2 to Will saves if they haven't indulged within 24 hours.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** A serious dependency (e.g., hallucinogens, painkillers, hard gambling). If the character goes 24 hours without the substance, they suffer the "Sickened" condition (-2 to all checks) until they indulge.  
**Major (9 BP):** A debilitating addiction (e.g., rare combat stims, neural-jacking). Withdrawal begins after 12 hours. The character suffers Hindrance on all rolls and takes 1 point of Constitution damage per day until they indulge or endure a lengthy detox process.

### 

## **Adversary**

BP Value: 1 to 10 (Variable)  
Category: Social / Background  
Someone, or something, is out to get the character. The BP value depends on the power of the enemy and the frequency of their interference.  
**Individual (1-5 BP):** A rival, a jilted lover, or a singular bounty hunter.  
*1-2 BP:* A nuisance or rival of equal power.  
*3-5 BP:* A superior foe who actively hunts the character.  
**Organization (5-10 BP):** A guild, a mega-corporation (Syndicate), a government (Impyrium), or a cult.  
*5-7 BP:* A local branch or gang.  
*8-10 BP:* A galaxy-spanning entity with nearly unlimited resources.

### 

## **Bodily Age**

BP Value: 5 or 10  
Category: Physical  
The character is outside the "prime" age range for their species, suffering physical limitations in exchange for experience or potential.  
**Child (10 BP):** 7-12ish typical human years.  
*Effect:* **\-1 Strength, \-1 Dexterity, \-1 Stamina** (Choose two). The character is small and easily overlooked but physically undeveloped.  
**Teen (5 BP):** 12-16ish typical human years.  
*Effect:* **\-1 Strength, \-1 Dexterity, or \-1 Stamina** (Choose one). The character is going through awkward growth phases.  
**Old (5 BP):** 60-90ish typical human years.  
*Effect:* **\-1 Strength, \-1 Dexterity, or \-1 Stamina** (Choose one). Joints ache and reflexes slow down.  
**Venerable (10 BP):** 90+ typical human years.  
*Effect:* **\-1 Strength, \-1 Dexterity, \-1 Stamina** (Choose two). The character is frail but likely possesses immense knowledge.

### 

## **Clown**

BP Value: 2  
Category: Social  
The character is a known jokester, prankster, or simply cannot be serious.  
**Effect:** **\-2 Penalty** on all checks to be taken seriously (Intimidation, Persuasion in serious contexts, Command). NPCs will naturally assume the character is lying or joking.

### 

## **Covetous**

BP Value: 3 (Minor) / 6 (Moderate) / 9 (Major)  
Category: Psychological  
The character has an overwhelming desire for something, be it wealth, attention, or power.  
**Types:** Greed (Money), Lechery (Romance/Lust), Ambition (Power/Status), Conspicuousness (Fame/Attention).  
**Mechanic:** When presented with the object of their desire, the character must make a Will Save (DC 15 \+ Severity) to avoid acting on the impulse immediately, regardless of danger or propriety.  
*Minor (3 BP):* Grumbles or complains if denied.  
*Moderate (6 BP):* Will take risks or suffer social faux pas to acquire it.  
*Major (9 BP):* Will steal, betray, or fight to satisfy the craving.

### 

## **Dependent**

BP Value: 3 (Capable) / 6 (Needy) / 9 (Dependent)  
Category: Social  
The character is responsible for the safety and well-being of another person (NPC) who travels with them or requires constant support.  
**Capable (3 BP):** The ward can mostly feed and dress themselves but needs protection in combat (e.g., a spouse, a young apprentice).  
**Needy (6 BP):** The ward requires daily assistance and supervision (e.g., a young child, an elderly relative).  
**Dependent (9 BP):** The ward is helpless or requires expensive medical upkeep/protection (e.g., an infant, someone in a medical stasis pod).

### 

## **Disability**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9 / 18  
Category: Physical  
The character is missing limbs or the use of them.  
**3 BP (Minor):** Missing a Hand or Foot. \-2 to Athletics/Dexterity tasks involving that limb. Reduced movement if foot.  
**6 BP (Major):** Missing a full Limb (Arm or Leg). Cannot use two-handed weapons or shields (if Arm). Movement speed halved (if Leg).  
**9 BP (Severe):** Two Limbs missing or Paraplegic (Legs useless). Requires a wheelchair, hover-chair, or cybernetics (if not bought off with BP).  
**18 BP (Crippling):** Quadriplegic or all limbs missing. The character is essentially a torso and head, requiring a life-support chair or full-body prosthesis to function.

### 

## **Distinctive Features**

BP Value: 3 (Disguisable) / 6 (Not-Disguisable) / 9 (Obvious)  
Category: Physical / Social  
The character stands out in a crowd, making them easy to track and impossible to forget.  
**Disguisable (3 BP):** A large scar, a distinct tattoo, or unique hair color. Can be hidden with a Disguise check.  
**Not-Disguisable (6 BP):** Extra eyes, blue skin in a human city, a robotic voice, or being 7 feet tall. Disguise checks suffer Hindrance.  
**Very Obvious (9 BP):** A glowing aura, made of rock, burning skin, or a monstrous appearance. Stealth and Disguise are effectively impossible in populated areas.

### 

**Emotional Issues**  
BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12  
Category: Psychological  
The character suffers from emotional volatility or regulation issues, making consistent reactions difficult.  
**Minor (3 BP):** Mood swings. The character's emotional state fluctuates, leading to minor social awkwardness or misunderstandings. \-2 penalty to social interactions when under pressure or stressed.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Prone to outbursts or deep withdrawal. The character struggles to contain intense feelings. Must make a Will save (DC 15\) to avoid breaking down, flying into a rage, or freezing up (e.g., *Stunned* for 1 round) when faced with significant emotional triggers or pressure.  
**Major (9 BP):** Debilitating. The character's emotional state frequently overrides rational thought. The character may become Catatonic (Unable to act, *Defenseless*) or Hysterical (Must make a *Charge* or *Attack* against a random target) during combat or high-stress scenes. Will Save DC 20 to act normally. This must be checked at the start of any round where the character takes damage or witnesses a traumatic event.  
**Severe (12 BP):** Crippling instability. The character's emotional state is a liability. They have a permanent \-2 penalty to all Will saves. In any stressful or combat situation, they must make a Will Save (DC 18\) at the start of their turn or suffer from a random, debilitating emotional state (e.g., *Panicked*, *Paralyzed*, or *Staggered* due to overwhelming rage/grief) for the round. They also require constant emotional support or monitoring from allies.

### 

## **Extreme Morality**

BP Value: 4 / 8 / 12  
Category: Psychological / Metaphysical  
The character holds blatant, unshakeable moral views aligned with cosmic forces (Good, Evil, Law, Chaos).  
**Mechanic:** The character is physically incapable (or psychologically unwilling to the point of pain) of acting against their alignment.  
*Minor (4 BP):* Will simply refuse to do it.  
*Moderate (8 BP):* Will actively stop others from doing it.  
*Major (12 BP):* Will violently oppose the antithesis of their morality, even if it means death.

### 

## **Hatred**

BP Value: 3 (Minor) / 6 (Moderate) / 9 (Major)  
Category: Psychological  
The character bears a deep grudge against a specific group (Species, Faction, Organization).  
**Minor (3 BP):** Disgust/Prejudice. \-2 to Social interactions with the target group.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Loathing. Will check required to assist or save them.  
**Major (9 BP):** Violence. Will check required *not* to attack them on sight or sabotage them.

### 

## **Honorable**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Psychological  
The character is bound by a strict personal code of morals, etiquette, or rules of engagement.  
**Minor (3 BP):** General gentlemanly conduct. Won't cheat at cards.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Bushido/Chivalry. Won't attack an unarmed foe, won't lie.  
**Major (9 BP):** Absolute Pacifism or Truth. Cannot break their word or code even to save their own life.

### 

## **Humorless**

BP Value: 2  
Category: Social  
The character takes everything literally.  
**Effect:** **\-2 Penalty** to Insight or Social checks involving nuance, sarcasm, innuendo, or metaphors. They are often the butt of jokes they don't understand.

### 

## **Impaired Sense**

BP Value: 4  
Category: Physical  
One of the character's primary senses is damaged or weak (but not gone).  
**Effect:** **Perception/Alertness Checks are made at Hindrance** when relying on the chosen sense (e.g., Near-Sighted, Hard of Hearing).

### 

## **Lost Sense**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Physical  
One of the character's senses is completely absent.  
**3 BP:** Loss of Taste or Smell. (Immune to scent-based nausea, but cannot detect gas leaks/poison).  
**6 BP:** Loss of Touch (Numbness) or Hearing (Deafness). \-4 to Dexterity (if Numb) or automatically fail listening checks (if Deaf).  
**9 BP:** Loss of Sight (Blindness). The character is Blind. All visual checks fail. Movement is difficult. Combat suffers a 50% miss chance unless compensated by other senses/Meta.

### 

**Mental Issues**  
BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12  
Category: Mental  
General category for neurodivergence or mental blocks that hinder standard operation.  
**Minor (3 BP):** Mild distraction or fixation. \-2 to Concentration.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Confused state under stress. When under a duress or stressful situation, the character suffers a \-2 penalty to all mental checks.  
**Major (9 BP):** Disassociated or Hallucinogenic. The GM may dictate the character sees things that aren't there, or is unable to distinguish fantasy from reality. The character suffers a \-4 penalty to all checks involving Perception or situational awareness.  
**Severe (12 BP):** Catatonic or Paralyzing Anxiety. The character is overcome by their mental block and is unable to take any meaningful action for the duration, or until a specific trigger is removed. They may take only defensive actions (like dodging or bracing) and cannot initiate attacks or complex interactions.

### 

**Minority**  
BP Value: 2+  
Category: Social  
The character belongs to a demographic (Species, Creed, Ethnicity, Gender Identity, or Sexual Orientation) that is marginalized or oppressed in the campaign setting. This marginalization can manifest as systemic oppression, social prejudice, or a combination of both.  
**Effect:** Gains the **Xeno / Social Stigma** modifier. Prices for goods and services are typically higher, law enforcement is generally stricter and more suspicious, and initial NPC reactions are typically "Unfriendly" or "Suspicious," potentially leading to overt discrimination, refusal of service, or unwarranted challenges. In addition, the character may face difficulty in securing certain types of employment or access to specific community areas.

### 

## **Mute**

BP Value: 2  
Category: Physical  
The character cannot speak verbally.  
**Effect:** Cannot cast spells with Verbal components. Cannot communicate without sign language, writing, or telepathy.

### 

## **Nightmares**

BP Value: 2  
Category: Mental  
The character is plagued by horrific dreams.  
**Effect:** Every time the character takes a Long Rest, roll a d10. On a **1**, the character has a night of terror and gains **no benefit from the rest**. They begin the next day Fatigued (-2 to all actions).

### 

## **Obligation**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Social  
The character owes a duty, service, or debt to an organization or individual.  
**Minor (3 BP):** Weekly report-ins or small dues.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Must perform missions on command or pay 25% of all earnings.  
**Major (9 BP):** Total servitude. The character is a thrall, slave, or sworn avatar. They own nothing; everything belongs to their patron.

### 

## **Poor**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Resource  
The character starts with significantly less wealth and carries financial baggage.  
**3 BP:** **\-4 to Wealth Score.** Starts with basic, second-hand gear only.  
**6 BP:** **\-8 to Wealth Score.** Starts with practically nothing.  
**9 BP:** **\-12 to Wealth Score.** The score becomes negative, denoting **Debt**. The character owes money to dangerous people, and any income earned is immediately garnished.

### 

## **Secret**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Background / Social  
The character hides a truth that, if revealed, would cause ruin.  
**Minor (3 BP):** Reputation damage (e.g., "I cheated on my exams").  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Harm or Exile (e.g., "I am actually a spy for the enemy," "I have a criminal record").  
**Major (9 BP):** Death (e.g., "I am the heir to the throne hiding from assassins," "I am a wanted serial killer").

### 

**Social Issues**  
BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9 / 12  
Category: Social  
The character is awkward, shy, abrasive, or simply bad with people. This can manifest as an inability to read social cues, an off-putting demeanor, or a genuine disdain for human interaction.  
**Minor (3 BP):** Shy/Introverted. The character avoids social interaction but is not actively offensive. \-2 to Persuasion checks.  
**Moderate (6 BP):** Abrasive/Rude OR Inept/Clueless. The character either actively offends people or constantly misreads social situations, leading to frequent misunderstandings. \-4 to Diplomacy checks. NPCs start one step lower in attitude (Hostile instead of Unfriendly, Unfriendly instead of Neutral, etc.).  
**Major (9 BP):** Antisocial/Sociopathic OR Repellent Personality. The character either fundamentally cannot empathize or is so outwardly unpleasant that most people immediately dislike them. Cannot naturally improve NPC attitudes through normal interaction; requires significant roleplaying or exceptional circumstances. All social checks (Persuasion, Diplomacy, Intimidation, Deception) are made at Hindrance.  
**Severe (12 BP):** Social Pariah/Ostracized. The character has a notorious reputation or an unavoidable quality that makes social interaction extremely difficult. Most NPCs will refuse to interact with the character unless absolutely necessary, and those who do are immediately suspicious or hostile. All social checks are made at Hindrance, AND the character suffers a flat \-2 penalty on *all* Charisma-based skills, even when used on non-social tasks (e.g., commanding an animal, casting a spell).

### 

## **Technologically Impaired**

BP Value: 10 / 20 / 30  
Category: Mental / Background  
The character is baffled by modern technology. This is common for "Primitive" origins or magical purists.  
**10 BP:** **\-1 Tech Level.** (e.g., A TL 3 character treats all TL 3 items as unfamiliar).  
**20 BP:** **\-2 Tech Level.** (e.g., A TL 3 character struggles with TL 2 items).  
**30 BP:** **\-3 Tech Level.** The character is effectively a Luddite. They cannot use computers, modern weapons, or pilot vehicles without massive penalties.

### 

## **Unlucky**

BP Value: 3 / 6 / 9  
Category: Metaphysical / Karma  
The universe conspires against the character.  
**3 BP:** **\-2 to Karma Pool** maximum.  
**6 BP:** **\-4 to Karma Pool** maximum.  
**9 BP:** **\-6 to Karma Pool** maximum.  
*Note:* This can reduce a Karma Pool to 0 or negative (Karmic Debt), meaning the character starts every session owing the GM bad luck.
