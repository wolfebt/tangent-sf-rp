# **METAPHYSICS & DISCIPLINES**

**The Tangent System Framework**  
In the Tangent universe, "Meta" refers to any ability that defies the standard laws of physics—whether it be Arcane Magic, Psionics, Divine Intervention, or Super-Science. Regardless of the source (The Void, The Weave, The Quantum Field), the mechanics of harnessing this power remain consistent.

## 

## **CORE MECHANICS**

The Metaphysic system relies on a triad of skills: **Attune**, **Discipline**, and **Invocation**.

### 

### **ATTUNE (The Channel)**

Attribute: Varies (Intellect for Mages/Tech, Wisdom for Priests/Psychics, Charisma for Sorcerers/Fey)  
Type: Meta Skill  
Attune represents the character's ability to open a conduit to their power source and shape it safely. It is the "Accuracy" and "Control" stat of the meta system.

* **Setting the Save CR:** When you cast an Invocation that allows a Resistance Check (Reflex, Fortitude, or Will), the CR is determined by your Attune check.  
  * *CR Formula:* 2d10 \+ Attune Rank \+ Ability Mod  
  * *Passive CR:* 10 \+ Attune Rank \+ Ability Mod (Used when taking 10\)  
* **Overcoming Resistance:** If a target has Spell Resistance (SR) or Psionic Dampening, the Attune check must exceed the target's SR.  
* **Maintaining Focus:** Attune is used for Concentration checks when taking damage while sustaining an effect.  
* **Specialties:**  
  * **Conduit Channeling:** Opening your physical body and neural pathways to flow raw meta-energy without self-damage.  
  * **Concentration Under Stress:** Sustaining an active invocation while taking direct ballistic damage or running through fire.  
  * **Spell CR Augmentation:** Tightening the metaphysical weave to make your invocations vastly harder for targets to resist.  
  * **Overcoming Resistance (SR/PR):** Pushing raw energy through an enemy's innate spell resistance or psionic dampeners.  
  * **Runic Circle Scribing:** Drawing physical geometric runes on floors to anchor massive, long-duration rituals safely.  
  * **Metascience Calibration:** Tuning sensory implants and detection crystals to measure ambient magical currents accurately.  
  * **Counter-Magic Shaping:** Reading an enemy's incoming energy conduit and weaving an opposite wave to collapse their spell.  
  * **Ambient Energy Absorption:** Siphoning background radiation, ley line energy, or emotional resonance to fuel casting.  
  * **Reality Anchor Stabilization:** Holding your immediate 10-foot space rigid against planar incursions and reality warping.  
  * **Overcharging Invocations:** Pushing the conduit beyond safe redline limits to maximize damage dice at the risk of personal burnout.

### 

### **DISCIPLINE SKILLS (The Shape)**

Attribute: Varies (Key Ability)  
Type: Meta Skill  
Disciplines are broad categories of reality manipulation. A character must be **Awakened** (via Feature) to learn these. The Rank in a Discipline determines the **Scope** of the effect (Range, Area, Duration) and the "Stage" of power.  
**Skill Stages & Base Potency:**

* **Novice (Ranks 1-5):** Touch to Short Range.  
* **Trained (Ranks 6-10):** Medium Range. Gain \+1d6 Base Effect.  
* **Expert (Ranks 11-15):** Long Range. Gain \+2d6 Base Effect.  
* **Master (Ranks 16-19):** Extreme Range. Gain \+3d6 Base Effect.  
* **Pinnacle (Rank 20):** Line of Sight/Global. Gain \+4d6 Base Effect.

### 

### **INVOCATIONS (The Effect)**

Type: Specialization / Feature  
Invocations are specific "spells" or "techniques" learned within a Discipline.

* **Cost:** 1 Character Point (CP) per Rank.  
* **Potency:** Invocations add **1 die (usually d6) per Rank** to the effect (Damage, Healing, Shielding).  
* **Total Effect:** Base Discipline Stage Dice \+ Invocation Rank Dice.

# **THE DISCIPLINES**

## 

## **DIMENSION**

Focuses: Summoning, Teleportation  
Key Concept: The manipulation of space, time, and planar boundaries.

### 

### **SUMMONING FOCUS**

Calling forth creatures or objects from other coordinates or planes.

* **Mechanic:** Summoned items/creatures appear within Range.  
* **Duration:** 1 Round per Discipline Rank (Sustained). Spending 1 Karma extends this to 1 Minute per Rank.

**Invocations:**

* **Summon Creature:** Calls a creature to fight or aid.  
  * *Effect:* Summons a creature with a Character Point (CP) value equal to 10 \+ (Invocation Rank x 5).  
* **Create Object:** Manifests a temporary item from generic matter.  
  * *Effect:* Creates an object of Simple complexity (CR 0). Rank increases complexity/Tech Level.  
* **Banishment:** Forces a creature back to its home plane or a pocket dimension.  
  * *Effect:* Resisted by Will. On fail, target is removed for 1 round per Rank.  
* **Web:** Pulls strands of sticky dimensional matter to restrain foes.  
  * *Effect:* Area Entangle. Resisted by Reflex/Strength.

### 

### **TELEPORT FOCUS**

Relocation without traversing the intervening space.

* **Mechanic:** Difficulty is based on the Familiarity of the target location.  
* **Familiarity CR:** Very Familiar (5), Seen Once (15), Description Only (25), Blind (30).

**Invocations:**

* **Blink:** Short-range tactical hop.  
  * *Effect:* Move up to 10ft \+ (5ft per Rank) as a Move Action. Does not provoke Attacks of Opportunity.  
* **Gate:** Opens a stable portal for others to pass through.  
  * *Effect:* Sustained portal. Size 5ft x 5ft per Rank.  
* **Teleport Other:** Sends a target to another location.  
  * *Effect:* Touch attack to hit. Resisted by Will. Range determined by Discipline Rank.  
* **Spatial Distortion:** Warps space to increase distance/defense.  
  * *Effect:* Adds \+1 Defense per 2 Ranks against Ranged attacks (displacement).

## 

## **ENERGY**

Focuses: Elemental, Force  
Key Concept: Manipulation of raw energy states and kinetic forces.

### 

### **ELEMENTAL FOCUS**

Conjuration of Pyro (Fire), Cryo (Ice), Voltic (Electric), Sonic, or Corrosive energy.

* **Damage:** 1d6 per Discipline Stage \+ 1d6 per Invocation Rank.  
* **Critical Effects:**  
  * *Pyro:* Burn (1/2 damage next round).  
  * *Cryo:* Freeze (Slow/Snare).  
  * *Voltic:* Shock (Stun).  
  * *Sonic:* Deafen/Stun.  
  * *Corrosive:* Dissolve (Armor DR reduction).

**Invocations:**

* **Blast:** Single target ranged attack.  
  * *Effect:* Standard Damage. High Accuracy (+2 to Attune check).  
* **Burst:** Radius explosion.  
  * *Effect:* Standard Damage in 5ft radius per Rank. Reflex Save for half.  
* **Line/Bolt:** Piercing line attack.  
  * *Effect:* Standard Damage in a 50ft \+ (10ft/Rank) line.  
* **Barrier:** Wall of damaging energy.  
  * *Effect:* Wall 10ft long per Rank. Deals damage to those crossing it.

### 

### **FORCE FOCUS**

Manipulation of pure kinetic energy and invisible constructs.

* **Damage:** 1d8 per Discipline Stage \+ 1d8 per Invocation Rank (Force Damage).

**Invocations:**

* **Telekinesis:** Move objects with the mind.  
  * *Effect:* Move weight 25lbs x Rank.  
* **Force Missile:** Homing shards of force.  
  * *Effect:* Always hits (no Attack roll needed). Lower damage die (d4s).  
* **Force Field:** Personal protection.  
  * *Effect:* Grants Temporary HP equal to 5 x Rank.  
* **Force Wave:** Cone of concussive force.  
  * *Effect:* Knocks targets Prone. Resisted by Fortitude/Might.

## 

## **ENTROPY**

Focuses: Chaos, Order  
Key Concept: The cycle of decay and structure; Life and Death energy.

### 

### **CHAOS FOCUS**

Acceleration of decay, luck manipulation (bad luck), and negative energy.

* **Damage:** 2d6 per Discipline Stage \+ 1d6 per Invocation Rank. (High damage, but dangerous).  
* **Note:** Ignores hardness/DR of non-magical materials.

**Invocations:**

* **Wither/Touch of Death:** Melee touch attack.  
  * *Effect:* Necrotic damage. Heals Undead.  
* **Curse:** Inflicts Disadvantage.  
  * *Effect:* Target rolls at Disadvantage for 1 round per Rank. Resisted by Will.  
* **Corrosion:** Destroys physical armor.  
  * *Effect:* Reduces target DR by 1 per Rank.  
* **Fear:** Induces panic.  
  * *Effect:* Target flees for 1 round per Rank. Resisted by Will.

### 

### **ORDER FOCUS**

Restoring patterns, healing, and stabilization.

* **Healing:** 2d6 per Discipline Stage \+ 1d6 per Invocation Rank.

**Invocations:**

* **Cure:** Restores Health/Vitality.  
  * *Effect:* Touch range healing. Undead take damage.  
* **Stabilize:** Prevents death.  
  * *Effect:* Immediate Action. Target stops losing Health at Death's Door.  
* **Restoration:** Removes status effects (Poison, Disease, Stun).  
  * *Effect:* Difficulty based on the Severity of the ailment.  
* **Ward:** Protection against specific alignments/energy.  
  * *Effect:* \+2 Defense and Saves vs chosen source.

## 

## 

## **ILLUSION**

Focuses: Phantasm, Shadow  
Key Concept: Deception of the senses and the creation of quasi-real matter.

### 

### **PHANTASM FOCUS**

Pure sensory input. Only exists in the mind or as light/sound waves.

* **Mechanic:** Disbelief (Will Save) negates the effect entirely.

**Invocations:**

* **Invisibility:** Bends light around a subject.  
  * *Effect:* Subject is invisible. Attacking breaks effect. 1 min/Rank.  
* **Image:** Creates a visual/auditory scene.  
  * *Effect:* Stationary or scripted illusion. Complexity scales with Rank.  
* **Silence:** Dampens sound waves.  
  * *Effect:* Radius 10ft \+ 5ft/Rank. No sound can pass in or out.  
* **Disguise Self:** Alters perceived appearance.  
  * *Effect:* \+10 to Disguise checks.

### 

### **SHADOW FOCUS**

Infusing illusions with quasi-reality from the Shadow Plane.

* **Mechanic:** Even if disbelieved, Shadow illusions retain 20% reality (and damage).

**Invocations:**

* **Shadow Conjuration:** Mimics a Summoning or Evocation spell.  
  * *Effect:* Duplicates another spell at 50% power.  
* **Shadow Weapon:** Creates a semi-real weapon.  
  * *Effect:* Functions as a specific melee weapon. Deals Psychic/Cold damage.  
* **Obscuring Mist:** Creates heavy fog.  
  * *Effect:* Blocks Line of Sight. Can be solid/sticky (Shadow).

## 

## 

## **MATTER**

Focuses: Enhancement, Transmutation  
Key Concept: Altering the physical properties of existing matter.

### 

### **ENHANCEMENT FOCUS**

Improving the structural integrity or properties of an object or creature.

* **Mechanic:** Buffs and physical upgrades.

**Invocations:**

* **Hardening/Barkskin:** Toughens skin or armor.  
  * *Effect:* Adds \+1 DR per 3 Ranks to armor or natural skin.  
* **Keen Edge:** Sharpens a weapon.  
  * *Effect:* Increases Critical Threat range by 1\.  
* **Physical Boost:** Enhances physical attributes.  
  * *Effect:* Grants \+2 to Str, Dex, or Con for 1 min/Rank.  
* **Repair:** Fixes structural damage.  
  * *Effect:* Restores 1d10 Structure Points per Rank to objects/constructs.

### 

### **TRANSMUTATION FOCUS**

Changing one substance into another or altering state of matter.

* **Mechanic:** Permanent changes usually require high Ranks and Karma expenditure. Combat use is temporary.

**Invocations:**

* **Polymorph/Morph:** Changes biological form.  
  * *Effect:* Subject takes on physical stats of a Beast/Humanoid.  
* **Petrification:** Turns flesh to stone (or vice versa).  
  * *Effect:* Resisted by Fortitude. "Slow" effect on partial success.  
* **Liquid/Gas Form:** Alters caster's state.  
  * *Effect:* Can pass through cracks, resistant to physical damage.  
* **Matter Manipulation:** Shape stone, metal, or wood.  
  * *Effect:* Reshape 1 cubic foot per Rank of raw material.

## 

## 

## **MENTAL**

Focuses: Projection, Sense  
Key Concept: Telepathy, ESP, and mind-over-matter interactions.

### 

### **PROJECTION FOCUS**

Sending thoughts, commands, or psychic damage into another mind.

* **Range:** Extended. 100ft x Rank.

**Invocations:**

* **Telepathy:** Mental communication.  
  * *Effect:* communicate with any language-using creature.  
* **Domination/Command:** Force will upon another.  
  * *Effect:* Target obeys command. Resisted by Will.  
* **Mind Blast:** Overload neurons.  
  * *Effect:* Stun damage. Resisted by Will. 1d6 Psychic damage per Rank.  
* **Memory Edit:** Modify recollections.  
  * *Effect:* Alter last 1 minute per Rank of memories.

### 

### **SENSE FOCUS**

Receiving information from the universe or other minds.

* **Range:** Extended. 100ft x Rank.

**Invocations:**

* **Clairvoyance/Remote Viewing:** See/Hear at a distance.  
  * *Effect:* Project senses to a known location or person.  
* **Precognition:** Glimpse the future.  
  * *Effect:* Gain Advantage on Initiative or a specific Defense check.  
* **Mind Read:** Detect surface thoughts or deep probe.  
  * *Effect:* Resisted by Will.  
* **Detection:** Sense specific signatures (Life, Magic, Tech).  
  * *Effect:* Ping location of target type within range.

# **SKILL USE & MODIFIERS**

### **Focused Task (Concentration)**

Meta users can choose to **Focus** on their invocation.

* **Benefit:** \+2 to Attune/Discipline Check.  
* **Cost:** \-5 to Defense and Perception. Must remain stationary.

### 

### **Rushed vs. Meticulous Casting**

* **Rushed (Swift Action):** \-5 to Attune Check.  
* **Meticulous (Full Round \+):** \+2 to Attune Check per additional round spent channeling (Max \+6).

### 

### **Skill Synergy**

Using a complementary skill to boost the Meta effect.

* *Example:* Using **Anatomy (Knowledge)** to boost a **Wither (Entropy)** spell.  
* *Mechanic:* Roll Anatomy vs CR 10\. For every 5 points over 10, gain \+1 to the Attune/Damage roll.
