# **FEATURES**

**Features (also called Feats) offer a powerful way to customize your character and make them stand out. Think of Features as special abilities or training that go beyond your character's basic attributes (like Strength or Intellect) and skills (like Stealth or Medicine). They represent the unique qualities that make your character who they are, whether it's an innate talent, a learned technique, or a modification gained through technology or magic.**

Cost  
Features are special abilities or training that cost 3 Build Points (BP) each. However, if you choose a Suggested Feature from the list recommended for your character's species, faction, or occupation, the cost is reduced by 1 BP. Keep in mind that even with the discount, Features cannot cost less than 1 BP. You must decide how to spend your BP to acquire the Features most important to your character's concept. 

Advanced Features  
As your character grows and develops, you might unlock access to more advanced Features. These often have prerequisites, meaning you'll need to meet certain requirements before you can take them. This could involve having a specific skill level, possessing another Feature, or fulfilling a particular condition set by the game's rules.

Suggested Features  
To help you choose Features that fit your character's concept, the game provides lists of Suggested Features for different species, factions, and occupations. If you choose a Suggested Feature, its cost is reduced by 1 BP, making it a more affordable option. However, even with this discount, the minimum cost for any Feature is always 1 BP.

**Examples of Features:**  
**Lightning Reflexes**  
Your character reacts faster than most, granting them a bonus to initiative rolls and Reflex saving throws.

**Toughness**  
Your character is more resilient, gaining additional hit points.

**Weapon Specialization**  
Your character is particularly skilled with a specific type of weapon, gaining a bonus to attack rolls and damage with that weapon.

**Night Vision**  
Your character can see in the dark, giving them an advantage in low-light conditions.

These are just a few examples of the many Features available in Tangent. By carefully selecting Features that complement your character's abilities and skills, you can create a truly unique and memorable hero for your adventures.

- ***Multiple** Features have different options and can be taken for different effect, skill, ability.*  
- ***Special** Features are dependent on Storyline and need the GM’s approval*

Ability Features represent a character's raw talent, extensive conditioning, or innate resilience. They directly modify the Core Mechanic (2d10 + Modifiers) by enhancing **Resistance Checks** (Saves) and **Ability Checks**.

## **Great Fortitude**

**Description:** Through vigorous conditioning or natural hardiness, the character has developed a profound resistance to physical ailments. Their body fights off toxins and trauma with exceptional efficiency.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **+2 bonus** on all **Fortitude Checks**.  
  * *Usage:* This applies to resisting poisons, diseases, radiation, physical fatigue, and metabolic hazards.  
* **Special:** This feature is **Ranked**. It may be purchased multiple times, up to a maximum rank equal to the character's Stamina score. The bonuses stack (e.g., Rank 2 grants a +4 total bonus).

### 

  ## **Incredible Fortitude**

  **Description:** The character's physical resilience is legendary. When subjected to physical stress that would cripple a normal being, they find a reserve of strength to persevere.

* **Prerequisite:** Great Fortitude, Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** You may roll all **Fortitude Checks** with **Advantage**.  
  * *Usage:* Roll two sets of 2d10 and keep the highest total when making a Fortitude save.  
* **Special:** This does not replace the bonus from Great Fortitude; you add the bonus to the highest set result.

## **Lightning Reflexes**

**Description:** The character reacts to danger before they are consciously aware of it. Their nerves are wire-taut, allowing them to dodge blasts, catch falling objects, or keep their footing on shifting ground.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1

## **Iron Will**

  * *Usage:* This applies to resisting Fear effects, Mind Control, Charm, Domination, and psychological torture or intimidation.

## **Evasiveness**

**Description:** The character is adept at avoiding the brunt of area attacks or resisting the full efficacy of potent effects.

* **Prerequisite:** Must possess the base Feature for the chosen Save (Lightning Reflexes, Great Fortitude, or Iron Will).  
* **Benefit:** When subjected to an effect that allows a **Resistance Check** for **Partial Effect** (commonly taking half damage on a success), a successful check instead results in **No Effect** (or zero damage).  
  * *Usage:* If a dragon breathes fire (Reflex for half), a character with Evasiveness (Reflex) takes *no damage* if they succeed on the roll.  
* **Special:** This feature acts as a **\[Multiple\]** selection. You must purchase it separately for Reflex, Fortitude, or Will.

### 

  ## **Improved Evasiveness**

  **Description:** Even when the character fails to avoid a danger entirely, their resilience or speed ensures they never take the full brunt of the impact.

* **Prerequisite:** Evasiveness (in the specific Save type).  
* **Benefit:** When subjected to an effect that allows a Resistance Check for Partial Effect, a **Failed Check** results in only the **Partial Effect** (half damage/effect), rather than the full effect.  
  * *Usage:* Combined with Evasiveness, this means the character takes *no damage* on a successful save, and only *half damage* on a failed save.

### 

## **Tolerance**

**Description:** The character's metabolism or psyche processes ongoing trauma much faster than the average being.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1 (for Physical) or Wisdom 1 (for Mental).  
* **Benefit:** When suffering from an ongoing effect (such as poison, disease, paralysis, or mental domination) that allows periodic Resistance Checks to end the effect (e.g., "save every minute"), the time interval between checks is reduced to **1/2 the typical time**.  
  * *Usage:* A poison that normally allows a save once per hour allows this character to save every 30 minutes.  
* **Special:** This feature acts as a **\[Multiple\]** selection. You must choose Physical Tolerance (Fortitude) or Mental Tolerance (Will).

### 

  ## **Improved Tolerance**

  **Description:** The character's recovery rate is supernatural. They can shake off debilitating effects in a fraction of the time it takes others.

* **Prerequisite:** Tolerance (in the specific type).  
* **Benefit:** The time interval between periodic Resistance Checks to end an ongoing effect is reduced to **1/4 the typical time**.  
  * *Usage:* A paralysis effect that allows a save once per round might allow this character to save at the start *and* end of their turn (GM discretion on granular timing), or simply recover four times faster from long-term ailments.

Combat Features represent specialized martial training, tactical awareness, and mastery of weaponry. They directly modify the **Strike vs. Defense** dynamic and the **Action Economy**.

### 

## **Agile Wrestling**

**Description:** The character utilizes leverage, flexibility, and technique rather than brute force to control opponents in close quarters.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1, Unarmed Combat 1  
* **Benefit:** You may use your **Agility** modifier instead of your Strength modifier when making Grapple Checks (both to initiate and maintain a grapple).  
* **Special:** This applies to the grapple check only; damage dealt while grappling is still calculated using Strength unless another feature modifies it.

### 

## **Ambidextrous**

**Description:** The character is naturally gifted or intensely trained to use both hands with equal proficiency.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2  
* **Benefit:** You suffer no penalties for using your off-hand. This eliminates the standard penalty for using a weapon in the secondary hand.  
* **Purpose:** Essential for characters wishing to dual-wield effectively without suffering severe accuracy penalties.

### 

  ## **Multidextrous**

  **Description:** Applicable to species with more than two arms, this feature allows the coordination of multiple limbs in concert.

* **Prerequisite:** Ambidextrous or Agility 4; Species with 3+ arms.  
* **Benefit:** You suffer no penalties for using any of your off-hands. You may wield weapons in all available hands without the standard "off-hand" penalties to Strike.

### 

## **Blind-Fight**

**Description:** The character has learned to fight using auditory cues, air pressure, and instinct, allowing them to engage unseen enemies.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 1  
* **Benefit:** When fighting a target that is concealed, invisible, or obscured (darkness/fog), you may roll your Strike check with **Advantage** to counter the miss chance or penalty usually imposed by the concealment.  
* **Special:** You do not lose your Agility bonus to Defense when attacked by an invisible attacker in melee.

### 

## **Blockade**

**Description:** The character dominates the space around them, making it impossible for enemies to slip by without engaging.

* **Prerequisite:** Strength 2  
* **Benefit:** Enemies cannot move through your threatened area or move past you without engaging in a contest of Strength (Opposed Might Check). If they fail, their movement ends immediately in the square adjacent to you.

### 

## **Burst Attack**

**Description:** The character is trained in controlling the recoil and spray of automatic weaponry to maximize accuracy or saturation.

* **Prerequisite:** Ballistic, Energy, or Heavy Weapon Skill Rank 2  
* **Benefit:** Reduces the penalty for Auto-Fire modes by 2\.  
  * **Short Burst:** When firing a Short Burst (3 rounds), you gain **\+2 Strike** (normally \+1).  
  * **Spray:** You reduce the penalty for wide-angle spraying.

### 

## **Channel Smite**

**Description:** A technique often used by battle-mages or paladins, channeling raw metaphysical energy through a physical vessel.

* **Prerequisite:** Awakened (Energy), Attune 1  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, you may cast a touch-range Invocation (Spell) and deliver it through a melee weapon attack. If the melee attack hits, the target suffers the weapon damage *plus* the effect of the Invocation.  
* **Special:** If the attack misses, the Invocation energy is dissipated and lost.

### 

## **Combat Casting**

**Description:** The character is trained to focus on metaphysical weaving even in the chaos of melee.

* **Prerequisite:** Attune 1  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+4 bonus** to Defense against Attacks of Opportunity caused by casting or using Invocations in a threatened square. You also gain a \+4 bonus to Concentration checks to maintain powers when taking damage.

### 

## **Combat Expertise**

**Description:** The character fights with superior tactical caution, sacrificing aggression for protection.

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** When you declare an attack action, you may choose a number up to your Combat Skill Rank. Subtract that number from your **Strike** (Attack) and add it to your **Defense** until the start of your next turn.

### 

  ## **Improved Disarm**

  **Description:** The character knows how to twist a weapon out of an opponent's grip safely.

* **Prerequisite:** Combat Expertise  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+2 bonus** on Disarm attempts. Attempting to Disarm an opponent does not provoke an Attack of Opportunity.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Disarm**

  **Description:** The character disarms with such force that the weapon is sent flying.  
* **Prerequisite:** Improved Disarm  
* **Benefit:** When you successfully Disarm an opponent, the weapon is knocked **15 feet** away in a random direction (or one of your choice on a critical success), rather than falling at the opponent's feet.

  ### 

  ## **Improved Feint**

  **Description:** The character is a master of misdirection, capable of faking an attack faster than the eye can follow.  
* **Prerequisite:** Combat Expertise, Bluff 1  
* **Benefit:** You may make a Bluff check to Feint in combat as a **Move Action** (normally a Standard Action).  
* **Purpose:** Allows you to Feint and Attack in the same round.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Feint**

  **Description:** The character's feints leave opponents completely off-balance.  
* **Prerequisite:** Improved Feint  
* **Benefit:** Enemies you successfully Feint lose their Agility bonus to Defense for **1 full round** (until the start of your next turn), allowing allies to also benefit from the opening.

  ### 

  ## **Improved Trip**

  **Description:** The character knows how to unbalance opponents safely.  
* **Prerequisite:** Combat Expertise  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+2 bonus** on Trip attempts. Attempting to Trip an opponent does not provoke an Attack of Opportunity.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Trip**

  **Description:** The character follows up a takedown with immediate punishment.  
* **Prerequisite:** Improved Trip  
* **Benefit:** Enemies you successfully Trip provoke an immediate **Attack of Opportunity** from you as they fall.

  ### 

  ## **Whirlwind Attack**

  **Description:** A spinning dance of death that strikes everyone nearby.  
* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2, Combat Expertise, Blitz Attack (Spring Attack)  
* **Benefit:** As a **Full Round Action**, you may make one melee attack against **every** foe within your reach.  
* **Special:** You forfeit any bonus attacks from high skill or other features when using this action; you make one strike per target.

### 

## **Combat Reflexes**

**Description:** The character has exceptional peripheral awareness and reaction time, preventing enemies from exploiting blind spots.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1  
* **Benefit:** You cannot be **Flanked**. Enemies gain no bonus to Strike for positioning on opposite sides of you, and Sneak Attacks that rely on Flanking (but not Flat-Footed) fail to trigger.  
* **Note:** This differs from standard d20 systems (where it grants extra AoOs); in Tangent, this is a defensive situational awareness feature.

### 

## **Combat Specialist**

**Description:** The character knows how to inflict maximum trauma with a specific class of weaponry.

* **Prerequisite:** Combat Skill 6 (Trained)  
* **Benefit:** Increase the **Damage Die type** of one specific weapon group by one step (e.g., d6 becomes d8, d8 becomes d10).  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] May be taken for different weapon groups.

### 

## **Dead Aim**

**Description:** The character trades mobility for extreme precision.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 3, Ranged 6  
* **Benefit:** If you do not move during your turn, you gain a **\+2 bonus to Strike** with Ranged Weapons. This stacks with Aiming actions.

### 

## **Defensive Combat Training**

**Description:** The character has trained to resist grappling and takedowns, using overall combat experience rather than just raw agility.

* **Prerequisite:** Combat Skill 1  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+4 Bonus to Defense** specifically against Combat Maneuvers (Trip, Disarm, Grapple, Bull Rush).

### 

## **Deflect Arrows**

**Description:** The character can knock aside projectiles with a weapon or free hand.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2, Unarmed 1  
* **Benefit:** Once per round, when you would normally be hit by a ranged weapon attack, you may make a Reflex Save (DC equal to the Attack Roll). If successful, you deflect the projectile and take **no damage**. You must be aware of the attack and not flat-footed.

### 

  ## **Snatch Arrows**

  **Description:** The character can catch arrows, bolts, or thrown weapons out of the air.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 4, Deflect Arrows  
* **Benefit:** When using Deflect Arrows, if you have a free hand, you may catch the projectile instead of just deflecting it. You may immediately throw it back as a free attack (if it is a thrown weapon) or drop it.

### 

## **Dodge**

**Description:** The character is adept at dodging blows.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 1  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **\+1 Dodge bonus to Defense**.  
* **Special:** Dodge bonuses stack with other Dodge bonuses (but not with themselves). This bonus is lost if you are Flat-Footed.

### 

  ## **Mobility**

  **Description:** The character moves through the battlefield with ease, weaving through threatened areas.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2, Dodge  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+4 Dodge bonus to Defense** against Attacks of Opportunity caused by moving out of or through a threatened square.

### 

## **Two-Weapon Fighting**

**Description:** The character is skilled in the art of using two weapons simultaneously, coordinating attacks to maintain offense while balancing defensive gaps.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2, Combat Skill 2  
* **Benefit:** You suffer only a \-2 penalty to Strike rolls when using an off-hand weapon (standard penalty is \-4).  
* **Purpose:** The foundation for all dual-wielding combat styles.

### 

  ## **Double Slice**

  **Description:** The character applies full strength to both weapons when dual-wielding.

* **Prerequisite:** Two-Weapon Fighting, Strength 1  
* **Benefit:** Add your full **Strength modifier** to damage rolls with your off-hand weapon (normally, off-hand weapons receive only 1/2 Strength modifier).

  ### 

  ## **Improved Two-Weapon Fighting**

  **Description:** The character's speed allows for more strikes with the off-hand.  
* **Prerequisite:** Agility 3, Combat Skill 6, Two-Weapon Fighting  
* **Benefit:** In addition to the standard single off-hand attack, you gain a **second attack** with your off-hand weapon, albeit at a **\-5 penalty**.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Two-Weapon Fighting**

  **Description:** A blur of steel/energy.  
* **Prerequisite:** Agility 4, Combat Skill 11, Improved Two-Weapon Fighting  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **third attack** with your off-hand weapon, at a **\-10 penalty**.

  ### 

  ## **Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting**

  **Description:** Total mastery of dual-wielding.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 5, Combat Skill 16, Greater Two-Weapon Fighting  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **fourth attack** with your off-hand weapon, at a **\-15 penalty**.

  ### 

## **Power Attack**

**Description:** The character sacrifices precision for raw force, trading attack accuracy for devastating damage.

* **Prerequisite:** Strength 2, Unarmed or Melee 6  
* **Benefit:** When declaring a melee attack, you may choose to take a penalty up to your Combat Skill Rank on your **Strike** (Attack) roll. You add this same amount as a bonus to your **Damage** roll. This choice is made before you roll your attack and lasts until the start of your next turn.  
* **Special:** This bonus to damage is considered a "Power" bonus, which may not stack with other features that grant generic bonus damage.

### 

  ## **Crushing Blow**

  **Description:** The character delivers blows that shatter armor and resolve.

* **Prerequisite:** Strength 2, Power Attack  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make a single melee attack. If successful, the target suffers a **\-2 penalty to Defense** (AC) for 1 round due to the staggering impact.

### 

## **Two-Weapon Defense**

**Description:** The character uses their off-hand weapon to parry and block.

* **Prerequisite:** Two-Weapon Fighting  
* **Benefit:** When wielding two weapons (or a double weapon), gain a **\+1 Shield bonus to Defense**.

### 

  ## **Improved Two-Weapon Defense**

  **Description:** Enhanced parrying techniques.

* **Prerequisite:** Improved Two-Weapon Fighting, Two-Weapon Defense  
* **Benefit:** The Shield bonus to Defense increases to **\+2**.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Two-Weapon Defense**

  **Description:** Superior defensive web of steel.  
* **Prerequisite:** Greater Two-Weapon Fighting, Improved Two-Weapon Defense  
* **Benefit:** The Shield bonus to Defense increases to **\+3**.

### 

  ## **Perfect Two-Weapon Defense**

  **Description:** Impenetrable defense while dual-wielding.

* **Prerequisite:** Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting, Greater Two-Weapon Defense  
* **Benefit:** The Shield bonus to Defense increases to **\+4**.

### 

## **Uncanny Dodge**

**Description:** The character reacts to danger before their senses fully process it.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 4  
* **Benefit:** You retain your **Agility bonus to Defense** even if you are caught **Flat-Footed** or struck by an **Invisible attacker**.  
* **Special:** This does not negate the advantage an attacker gets from Feinting (see Greater Feint).

### 

## **Vital Strike**

**Description:** The character makes a single, devastatingly accurate attack rather than a flurry of blows.

* **Prerequisite:** Combat Skill 6  
* **Benefit:** As a **Standard Action**, make a single attack at your highest bonus. If it hits, deal **two times (2x)** the weapon's damage dice.  
* **Note:** Only the dice are multiplied, not the static bonuses (Strength, specialization, etc.).

### 

  ## **Improved Vital Strike**

  **Description:** A precise strike that targets vital organs.

* **Prerequisite:** Vital Strike, Combat Skill 11  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make a single attack. Deal **three times (3x)** the weapon's damage dice.

  ### 

  ## **Greater Vital Strike**

  **Description:** A killing blow delivered with perfect efficiency.  
* **Prerequisite:** Improved Vital Strike, Combat Skill 16  
* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make a single attack. Deal **four times (4x)** the weapon's damage dice.

### 

## **Weapon Focus**

**Description:** Extensive training with a specific weapon type (e.g., Laser Rifle, Longsword, Plasma Pistol).

* **Prerequisite:** Combat Skill 1  
* **Benefit:** Choose a specific weapon. You gain **\+1 to Strike** with that weapon.  
* **Secondary Effect:** You also gain a specialized perk based on the weapon's damage type:  
  * **Piercing:** \+1 Penetration (ignores 1 DR).  
  * **Slashing:** \+1 Bleed damage.  
  * **Bludgeoning:** \+1 to Stun DC.  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] May be taken for different weapons.

### 

  ## **Weapon Specialization**

  **Description:** The character knows how to inflict maximum damage with their chosen weapon.

* **Prerequisite:** Weapon Focus (in chosen weapon), Combat Skill 6  
* **Benefit:** Gain **\+1 Die of Damage** with the chosen weapon. (e.g., A 2d6 weapon becomes 3d6).  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] This bonus is significantly stronger than standard d20 (+2 static), reflecting the Tangent system's damage scaling.

  ### 

  ## **Imp. Weapon Specialization**

  **Description:** Lethal mastery.  
* **Prerequisite:** Weapon Specialization, Combat Skill 11  
* **Benefit:** Gain **\+2 Dice of Damage** (total) with the chosen weapon.

  ### 

  ## **Grtr. Weapon Specialization**

  **Description:** Peerless lethality.

* **Prerequisite:** Imp. Weapon Specialization, Combat Skill 16  
* **Benefit:** Gain **\+3 Dice of Damage** (total) with the chosen weapon.

## **Weapon Improvisation**

**Description:** The character is dangerous with anything—a bottle, a chair, or a rock.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You suffer **no penalties** for using improvised Archaic weapons (normally \-4).  
* **Purpose:** Allows use of the environment as effective weaponry without training in specific weapon groups.

Discipline Features represent a character's connection to the metaphysical forces of the universe—whether that be the Void, the Quantum Field, Psionic Ether, or Divine Light.

### 

## **Aptitude**

**Description:** The character possesses a natural affinity for specific metaphysical interactions, grasping the nuance of shaping energy more intuitively than others.

* **Prerequisite:** Key Ability Score (Intellect, Wisdom, or Charisma) 1+  
* **Benefit:** Choose two specific **Discipline Skills** (e.g., *Thermal* and *Telekinetic*, or *Biomancy* and *Necromancy*). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to checks with those specific skills.  
* **Purpose:** To demonstrate innate talent in specific areas of magic or psionics without necessarily having broad mastery.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Multiple\]**. It may be selected again to apply to two different Discipline skills.

### 

## **Awakened**

**Description:** The character has opened their mind or soul to a greater reality. This is the fundamental "spark" required to use any form of metaphysics. Without this feature, a character cannot manipulate supernatural forces.

* **Prerequisite:** None (often taken at Character Creation).  
* **Benefit:** You gain access to the **Attune** skill (the universal skill for channeling power) and **2 Discipline Skills** belonging to a specific **Metafocus Discipline** (e.g., Dimension, Energy, Entropy, Illusion, Matter, or Mental).  
  * *Example:* Selecting *Awakened (Energy)* might grant access to *Attune*, *Elemental Focus*, and *Force Focus*.  
* **Purpose:** The entry point for all mages, psychics, and super-powered beings.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Multiple\]**. Taking it a second time allows you to awaken a different Metafocus (e.g., adding *Entropy* to a character who already has *Energy*), granting access to that discipline's specific skills.

### 

## **Centering**

**Description:** The character has learned to stabilize their internal flow of energy, allowing them to channel power with greater control and intensity.

* **Prerequisite:** Attune 6 (Trained), Key Ability Score 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to all **Attune Checks**.  
* **Mechanics:** In Tangent, the **Attune Check** determines the **Difficulty Class (DC)** of the Saving Throw your targets must make. Therefore, this feature directly increases the difficulty of resisting your powers.  
* **Purpose:** Essential for casters who want their spells to be difficult to resist.

### 

  ## **Centering Expert**

  **Description:** The character has achieved a profound state of mental or spiritual equilibrium.

* **Prerequisite:** Centering, Attune 11 (Expert), Key Ability Score 4  
* **Benefit:** The bonus to **Attune Checks** increases to **\+4**.  
* **Special:** This replaces the bonus from *Centering*, it does not stack (it is an upgrade).

  ### 

  ## **Centering Master**

  **Description:** The character's control over their metaphysical connection is absolute; they are a conduit through which power flows without resistance.  
* **Prerequisite:** Centering Expert, Attune 16 (Master), Key Ability Score 6  
* **Benefit:** The bonus to **Attune Checks** increases to **\+6**.

### 

## **Discipline Focus**

**Description:** The character has specialized in shaping a specific type of energy or reality.

* **Prerequisite:** Chosen Discipline Skill 6 (Trained)  
* **Benefit:** Choose one Discipline Skill (e.g., *Summoning*, *Projection*, *Elemental*). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to checks made with that skill.  
* **Mechanics:** In Tangent, the **Discipline Skill Check** determines the **Severity** (Damage, Duration, or Potency) of the effect. This feature directly increases the raw power of the ability.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Multiple\]**; you may take it for different Discipline skills.

### 

  ## **Discipline Expertise**

  **Description:** The character has mastered the nuances of a specific energy form.

* **Prerequisite:** Discipline Focus (in the same skill), Discipline Skill 11 (Expert)  
* **Benefit:** The bonus to the chosen Discipline Skill increases to **\+4**.

  ### 

  ## **Discipline Mastery**

  **Description:** The character is a virtuoso of their chosen art, capable of unleashing devastating power.  
* **Prerequisite:** Discipline Expertise (in the same skill), Discipline Skill 16 (Master)  
* **Benefit:** The bonus to the chosen Discipline Skill increases to **\+6**.

### 

  ## **Discipline Empower**

  **Description:** The character can hold and build a metaphysical charge over time, gathering ambient energy to unleash a single, overwhelming effect.

* **Prerequisite:** Discipline Focus, Attune 6  
* **Benefit:** You may increase the casting time of an Invocation to gather more energy. For every **Full Round** spent channeling (doing nothing but concentrating on the spell), you add **\+1 Effect Die** (or \+5 to the effective Skill Check result for non-damage spells) to the final release.  
* **Limit:** You may channel for a number of rounds equal to your Constitution or Will modifier (whichever is higher). If interrupted (taking damage), you must make a Concentration (Attune) check or lose the energy.

### 

## **Empowered Strike**

**Description:** The character can infuse their physical attacks with raw metaphysical energy. This is the signature ability of "Spellblades," "Paladins," or "Psychic Warriors."

* **Prerequisite:** Attune 6  
* **Benefit:** You may channel energy into a melee or ranged weapon attack. The attack gains **\+1 to Strike** and **\+1 to Damage**.  
* **Effect:** The attack is considered **Enhanced** (Magical/Psionic) for the purpose of bypassing Damage Resistance (DR) that ignores mundane weapons.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Ranked\]**. You may take it multiple times (up to your Attune rank limit). Rank 2 would grant \+2 Strike/Damage, etc.

### 

## **Focused Center**

**Description:** The character's concentration is so absolute that they can channel power reliably even in the heat of battle.

* **Prerequisite:** Centering, Discipline Focus (in all currently used Disciplines)  
* **Benefit:** You may **Take 10** on **Attune Checks** even when threatened, distracted, or in combat conditions.  
* **Purpose:** Guarantees a consistent Save DC for your spells, removing the risk of a low roll making your spells easy to resist.

### 

  ## **Balanced Center**

  **Description:** The character has achieved a perfect equilibrium between the channeling of power (Attune) and the shaping of it (Discipline).

* **Prerequisite:** Centering Expert, Discipline Expertise (in all currently used Disciplines)  
* **Benefit:** You may **Take 10** on **Discipline Skill Checks** even when threatened, distracted, or in combat.  
* **Purpose:** Guarantees consistent Damage or Effect results. You no longer roll for power; you simply apply a reliable standard of devastation.

  ### 

  ## **Harmonious Center**

  **Description:** The character exists in a state of flow with the universe. Power responds to their will instantly and perfectly.  
* **Prerequisite:** Centering Master, Discipline Mastery (in all currently used Disciplines)  
* **Benefit:** You roll **ALL** Metafocus Checks (Attune and Discipline) with **Advantage**.  
* **Mechanics:** Roll two sets of 2d10 and keep the highest total for both your Accuracy/DC (Attune) and your Power/Severity (Discipline).

  ### 

## **Special Ability**

**Description:** The character possesses a singular, innate supernatural power that does not require the broad study of a Discipline. This represents things like a dragon's breath weapon, a mutant's teleportation, or a cyborg's built-in sonic scream.

* **Prerequisite:** None (though usually tied to Species or Origin).  
* **Benefit:** You gain the ability to use **one specific Invocation** (Spell/Power) at **Level 1**.  
* **Mechanics:**  
  * You do not need the *Awakened* feature or the parent Discipline Skill to use this.  
  * This ability functions as a standalone Skill. You can advance it with Skill Points like any other skill (Novice, Trained, etc.) to increase its power.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Multiple\]**. You can take it multiple times to gain different isolated powers.

### 

## **Tech Interface**

**Description:** The character possesses a specialized mental architecture or metaphysical link that allows them to bridge the gap between consciousness and advanced machinery.  
**Prerequisite:** Special (Character must have access to Meta-Tech, Psi-Tech, or Magi-Tech equipment).  
**Benefit:** You can pilot vehicles or operate computer systems using your Intellect score instead of Agility or other physical attributes, provided the device is appropriately designed with a neural or metaphysical interface.  
**Example:** A "Technomancer" with low physical coordination can pilot a starship or hack a high-security mainframe using their mind, performing actions with the speed of thought rather than manual dexterity.  
**Purpose:** To allow Intellect-focused characters to perform roles traditionally reliant on Agility, reflecting a seamless integration with complex systems.  
**Special:** This feature is \[Multiple\]. Each selection applies to a specific category of technology (e.g., Vehicles, Starships, or Network Systems).

## **Versatile Source**

**Description:** The character's understanding of metaphysics is broad enough to bridge different sources of power. A wizard might tap into divine faith, or a psychic might calculate ley-lines like a mathematician.

* **Prerequisite:** Attune 6  
* **Benefit:** You may use a **Secondary Ability Score** to modify your **Attune Checks**.  
  * *Example:* An Intellect-based caster (Mage) with this feature could choose to use their Wisdom modifier for Attune checks instead, perhaps representing a shift from logical weaving to intuitive feeling.  
* **Purpose:** useful for characters with high stats in non-standard casting attributes, or to mitigate temporary Ability Damage to their primary casting stat.

* **Purpose:** Allows players to gauge if a fight is winnable before initiative is rolled.

* **Benefit:** As a Standard Action, make an **Assessment Check** opposed by the target's **Bluff Check**. For every 5 points by which your result exceeds the target's, you gain a cumulative **\+1 bonus to Strike and Damage** against that specific target.

Karma Features represent a character's destiny, luck, divine favor, or sheer force of will. They allow players to manipulate the game mechanics in moments of crisis, spending **Karma Points (KP)** or daily uses to alter outcomes.

### 

## **Beginner's Luck**

**Description:** The character has an uncanny knack for succeeding at things they have no business doing. When inexperienced, they rely on instinct and fortune rather than training.

* **Prerequisite:** The Skill being attempted must be at **Rank 5 (Novice) or lower**.  
* **Benefit:** You may spend **1 Karma Point** to gain **\+5 Temporary Ranks** in a specific skill for the duration of the current scene or encounter.  
  * *Mechanics:* This effectively treats an Untrained (Rank 0\) character as a Novice (Rank 5), or a Novice as a Professional (Rank 6-10) for a short time.  
* **Purpose:** Allows a character to fill a critical role (like piloting a shuttle or hacking a door) in an emergency even if they lack the background for it.

### 

## **Believer**

**Description:** The character possesses a profound faith in a higher power, a philosophy, or a cosmic order. In times of need, they can draw upon this faith to guide their hand.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 1  
* **Benefit:** **Once per day**, you may re-roll any single failed **Skill Check**.  
  * *Mechanics:* This does **not** cost Karma Points. It is a daily ability refreshed after a Full Rest. You must accept the result of the second roll.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Ranked\]**. Each additional rank allows an additional use per day. The maximum rank is limited by your **Wisdom** score.

### 

  ## **Devout**

  **Description:** The character's faith is not just a belief system, but a fundamental force that strengthens their very being.

* **Prerequisite:** Believer, Charisma 1  
* **Benefit:** **Once per day**, you may re-roll any single failed **Ability Check** (e.g., Strength check to lift rubble, Constitution check to hold breath).  
  * *Mechanics:* This is separate from the *Believer* re-rolls. It applies to raw attribute checks, not Skill checks.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Ranked\]**. Each additional rank allows an additional use per day. The maximum rank is limited by your **Charisma** score.

  ### 

  ## **True Faith**

  **Description:** The character is a chosen vessel or a paragon of their belief. When they expend their spiritual energy, the universe bends to ensure their success.  
* **Prerequisite:** Believer, Devout  
* **Benefit:** When you spend a **Karma Point** to modify an action that is directly in service of your Faith or ethos, the numeric effect is **Doubled**.  
  * *Mechanics:* If a Karma spend would normally grant a \+5 bonus, it grants \+10. If the GM rules a Karma spend heals damage or reduces time, that amount is doubled. *Note: This does not double the number of dice rolled for Advantage, but significantly boosts flat modifiers.*

### 

## **Charmed**

**Description:** The character leads a charmed life. Disasters that would doom others seem to miss them by inches.

* **Prerequisite:** Maximum Karma Pool of 4+  
* **Benefit:** **Once per day**, if you roll a **Natural 1** (Fumble/Critical Failure), you may immediately re-roll it without spending a Karma Point.  
  * *Mechanics:* This serves as a free "safety net" against catastrophic failure.

### 

## **Inspiration**

**Description:** The character is the heart of the team. They can lend their own luck and determination to allies, spurring them on to greatness.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 2  
* **Benefit:** You may spend your own **Karma Points** to grant a Karma Benefit to an **Ally**.  
  * *Cost:* The cost is **1 KP (Transfer Cost) \+ The Cost of the Effect**.  
  * *Example:* To give an ally *Surge* (normally 1 KP), you spend 2 KP. To give an ally *Advantage* ("I Got This", normally 1 KP), you spend 2 KP.  
* **Purpose:** Allows support characters to keep heavy hitters active or save allies from bad rolls.

### 

## **Karmic Blessing**

**Description:** The character has a larger destiny than most. Fate has stockpiled more luck for them to use.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** Increase your **Maximum Karma Pool** by **+1 Point** per rank of this feature.  
  * *Default:* Characters start with 3 Max Karma Points by default. Taking Karmic Blessing raises this pool cap (e.g. +1 per rank).  
* **Limit:** Your total Maximum Karma Pool cannot exceed the sum of your Ability Scores (a very high ceiling, rarely reached).  
* **Special:** This feature is **[Ranked / Multiple]**. You may take it multiple times to expand your maximum Karma pool by +1 point per rank.

### 

## **Seize Initiative**

**Description:** The character reacts with blazing speed when chaos erupts, acting before the enemy can blink.

* **Cost:** Spend **1 Karma Point** at the start of combat (before Initiative is rolled).  
* **Benefit:** You automatically go **First** in the initiative order, regardless of your Agility or roll.  
  * *Conflict:* If multiple characters (allies or enemies) use this feature, they are ranked at the top of the order, sorted by their Agility scores.

### 

## **Surge**

**Description:** The character pushes their body and mind beyond standard limits, fitting more action into a moment than should be possible.

* **Cost:** Spend **1 Karma Point** during your turn.  
* **Benefit:** You gain **1 additional Standard Action** this round.  
  * *Usage:* This allows for a Move \+ Attack \+ Attack, or Move \+ Cast Spell \+ Attack. It breaks the standard action economy.

### 

## **Ultimate Effort**

**Description:** The character is a savant or master in a specific field. When they truly focus, failure is impossible.

* **Prerequisite:** None (Must be assigned to a specific Trait/Skill/Attribute).  
* **Cost:** Spend **1 Karma Point**.  
* **Benefit:** Treat a specific **Non-Combat** check as if you rolled a **Natural 20**.  
  * *Mechanics:* You do not roll. You calculate the result as: 20 \+ Skill Rank \+ Attribute Mod \+ Modifiers. This guarantees a critical success on almost any standard difficulty task.  
* **Restriction:** This cannot be used for Attack rolls or Damage rolls. It is intended for heroic feats of strength, brilliant deductions, or masterful diplomacy.  
* **Special:** This feature is **\[Multiple\]**. You must select which trait it applies to when purchased (e.g., *Ultimate Effort: Engineering* or *Ultimate Effort: Strength*).

* **Benefit:** When you use Intimidate to force a target to act, the target **does not become Hostile** toward you afterwards (they remain Indifferent or Unfriendly, but terrified). Bystanders must make an Insight check vs. your Intimidate to even realize a threat was made.

### 

## **Versatile Curator**

**Description:** The character is a font of trivia and data.

* **Prerequisite:** Knowledge 6, Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Knowledge category. For every point of Intellect Bonus you possess, you gain 1 Rank in a Knowledge skill you do not technically have, allowing you to make Trained checks.

### 

## **Versatile Performer**

**Description:** The character is a multi-talented artist.

* **Prerequisite:** Perform 6, Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Performance category. For every point of Intellect Bonus, gain access to another Performance type (Singing, Dancing, Oratory) at your base rank.

### 

## **Versatile Professional**

**Description:** The character knows every job on the crew.

* **Prerequisite:** Vocation 6, Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** You gain "Virtual Skills" in the Vocation category. For every point of Intellect Bonus, gain access to a related Vocation sub-skill without spending BP.  
  * *Example:* A Soldier with Int \+2 might gain Virtual Ranks in *Armorer* and *Demolitions*.

Special Features are often restricted by Species, Origin, or Technology Level. They represent biological realities or extensive body modification rather than simple training.

### 

## **Acute Sense**

**Description:** The character possesses a specific sensory organ that is highly developed, far exceeding the baseline for their species. This could be eagle eyes, a bloodhound’s nose, or sensitive auditory receptors.

* **Prerequisite:** Awareness 1  
* **Benefit:** Choose one sense (Vision, Hearing, Smell, Taste, or Touch). You gain a **\+2 Bonus** to **Awareness Checks** utilizing that sense.  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] You may take this feature multiple times, applying it to a different sense each time.

### 

  ## **Analytical Sense**

  **Description:** The character’s senses function like a tricorder or forensic scanner. They don't just see or smell; they analyze chemical compositions, structural stress points, and thermal gradients naturally.

* **Prerequisite:** Acute Sense (in the corresponding sense), Intellect 2  
* **Benefit:** When using your chosen Acute Sense, you gain specific, detailed scientific information. A smell isn't just "almond-like"; it is "Cyanide, distinct trace."  
* **Mechanics:** You may use **Perception** in place of **Investigation** or **Science** checks to identify substances or analyze clues if the sensory input is available.

  ### 

  ## **Uncanny Sense**

  **Description:** The character’s senses are so sharp they verge on precognition. It is nearly impossible to hide from them.  
* **Prerequisite:** Acute Sense (in the corresponding sense)  
* **Benefit:** You roll all **Awareness Checks** using the chosen sense with **Advantage**.

### 

## **Augmented**

**Description:** The character has undergone invasive surgery to replace or enhance biological systems with cybernetics, biotechnology, or magical prosthetics.

* **Prerequisite:** Available Technology Level (TL 3+)  
* **Benefit:** You are compatible with **Standard Augmentations**.  
* **Special:** Upon taking this feature, you gain a "credit" of **6 Build Points (BP)** worth of Augmentations effectively for free (representing the initial suite of upgrades). Any cost beyond this must be paid with BP or Credits.

### 

  ## **Heavy Augmentations**

  **Description:** The character has replaced significant portions of their body with industrial or military-grade hardware. These modifications are often bulky, obvious, and not designed for social integration.

* **Prerequisite:** Augmented, Stamina 2  
* **Benefit:** You may install **Heavy** class Augmentations (which often have higher DR or Strength bonuses but may impose social penalties). You gain an additional **6 BP** worth of Augmentations.

  ### 

  ## **Extreme Augmentations**

  **Description:** The character is more machine (or bio-construct) than original being. They have replaced the majority of their biological functions.  
* **Prerequisite:** Heavy Augmentations, Stamina 4  
* **Benefit:** You may undergo **Full Body Conversion**. You may install systems that alter your Size category or basic physiology (e.g., tank treads instead of legs). You gain an additional **6 BP** worth of Augmentations.

### 

## **Biotechnology**

**Description:** The character is trained or naturally adapted to use "Living Technology"—grown items, symbiotic armor, and genetically engineered tools (common among Aulurans and Tyranid-like factions).

* **Prerequisite:** Awakened (Psychic) OR Tech Level 4+ society.  
* **Benefit:** You can imprint, operate, and heal Living Technology. Without this feature, Biotech items treat the user as a foreign body and may not function or may even attack the user.

### 

## **Bonded**

**Description:** The character shares a profound, supernatural connection with another specific being.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma 2  
* **Benefit:** You possess a **Tele-Empathic** link with another character (PC or NPC) who also possesses this feature. You can sense their strong emotions and general physical state (pain, unconsciousness) regardless of distance.

### 

## **Companion**

**Description:** The character has a loyal follower, pet, droid, or mount.

* **Prerequisite:** Charisma  
* **Benefit:** You gain a loyal NPC ally built with **40 Build Points**.  
* **Special:** \[Ranked\] Taking this feature again can either upgrade the existing companion (adding \+40 BP) or gain a second companion.

### 

  ## **Familiar**

  **Description:** The companion is not just a pet, but a metaphysical extension of the character's will.

* **Prerequisite:** Bonded, Companion, Awakened  
* **Benefit:** Your Companion gains **\+20 Build Points**. You always know the exact direction and distance to your familiar.

  ### 

  ## **Mind Link**

  **Description:** The bond deepens into a direct mental highway.  
* **Prerequisite:** Familiar or Bonded  
* **Benefit:** Your Companion gains **\+10 Build Points**. You possess a full **Telepathic Link** with the companion. You may share Knowledge Skills; if one knows a fact or language, the other can access it instantly.

### 

  ## **Shared Senses**

  **Description:** The character can project their consciousness into their bond-mate.

* **Prerequisite:** Familiar or Bonded  
* **Benefit:** Your Companion gains **\+10 Build Points**. As a Standard Action, you can shift your sensory input to perceive through your companion's eyes/ears/senses. While doing so, your own body is blind/deaf.

### 

## **Danger Sense**

**Description:** A "sixth sense" or spidey-sense that warns of immediate peril.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 1  
* **Benefit:** You make all **Surprise Checks** and checks to avoid traps at **Advantage**. You are never truly "unaware" of direct danger, allowing you to act in the Surprise round.

### 

## **Dwarfism**

**Description:** The character is significantly smaller than the average for their species.

* **Prerequisite:** Species Trait or Flaw  
* **Benefit:** Your Size Category decreases by one step (e.g., Medium to Small).  
* **Mechanics:** Gain **\+1 to Defense**, **\+4 to Stealth**, and **\+1 to Strike**. Decrease lifting/carrying capacity and movement speed (typically \-10ft).

### 

## **Eldritch Heritage**

**Description:** The character carries the blood of dragons, demons, fey, or gods.

* **Prerequisite:** None (Background)  
* **Benefit:** You gain a specific **Bloodline Power** or minor supernatural trait associated with your ancestor (e.g., minor energy resistance, claws, or a cantrip-level spell usable at will).

### 

## **Energy Resistance**

**Description:** The character's physiology is resistant to a specific form of energy (Fire, Cold, Electricity, Acid, Sonic).

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** Gain **Resistance 5** against one chosen energy type. (Subtract 5 from all damage of that type).  
* **Special:** \[Ranked\] Stacks to Resistance 10, 15, etc., eventually becoming Immunity.

### 

## **Giant**

**Description:** The character is significantly larger than the average for their species.

* **Prerequisite:** Species Trait  
* **Benefit:** Your Size Category increases by one step (e.g., Medium to Large).  
* **Mechanics:** Gain **\+2 Strength**, increased Reach (threat range), and higher carrying capacity. Suffer **\-1 to Defense** and **\-4 to Stealth**.

### 

## **Life Support**

**Description:** The character can survive in environments hostile to their biology.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You can breathe and function comfortably in a specific non-native environment.  
* **Options:** *Aquatic* (Breathe water), *Atmospheric* (Breathe exotic gas mixes), *Vacuum* (Can survive in void for limited time/hold breath 10x longer).

### 

## **Low Light Vision**

**Description:** The character's eyes magnify ambient light.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You can see in dim light (starlight, moonlight) as clearly as if it were bright daylight. You interpret color normally. You still cannot see in absolute darkness.

### 

## **Darksight**

**Description:** The character sees using thermal or other non-visible spectrums.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You can see in total darkness up to 60 feet.  
* **Limitation:** This vision is typically black and white or monochromatic (e.g., heat vision).

### 

## **Natural Weapons**

**Description:** The character possesses claws, fangs, horns, or a tail striker.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** You are always armed. Your Unarmed Strikes deal Lethal damage and the damage die is increased.  
  * *Small/Medium:* 1d4 or 1d6 damage.  
  * *Large:* 1d8 damage.  
* **Special:** \[Ranked\] Can be taken to increase the damage die size.

### 

## **Rage**

**Description:** The character can tap into a primal fury, ignoring safety for raw power.

* **Prerequisite:** None  
* **Benefit:** As a Free Action, enter a Rage.  
  * **Gain:** \+2 Strength, \+2 Stamina (and temporary Health), \+2 Will.  
  * **Suffer:** \-2 Defense. You cannot use skills requiring patience or concentration (most Mental/Tech skills).  
  * **Duration:** Rounds equal to 4 \+ New Stamina Mod. Ends in fatigue.

### 

## **Resistance**

**Description:** The character has a fortified immune system or supernatural resilience.

* **Prerequisite:** Stamina 1  
* **Benefit:** Gain a **\+2 Bonus** to saves against a specific hazard category: **Disease**, **Poison**, **Radiation**, or **Magic**.  
* **Special:** \[Multiple\] Can be taken for different hazards.

### 

## **Scent**

**Description:** The character can detect creatures and objects by smell alone.

* **Prerequisite:** Wisdom 1  
* **Benefit:** You can detect opponents within 30 feet by sense of smell. You can track creatures using **Survival** based on scent trails.

### 

## **Self-Sustaining**

**Description:** The character does not require standard biological fuel.

* **Prerequisite:** Synthetic, Plant, or Undead traits  
* **Benefit:** You do not need to eat or drink. You may rely on solar power, a battery recharge, or magical energy, but you are immune to starvation and dehydration mechanics.

### 

## **Sleepless**

**Description:** The character does not require sleep cycles (though they may need downtime for system diagnostics or meditation).

* **Prerequisite:** Synthetic, Elf, or Special  
* **Benefit:** You are **Immune** to magical sleep effects. You do not need 8 hours of sleep; you remain conscious during rest periods, though you must still engage in light activity to gain the benefits of a Long Rest.

### 

## **Sense**

**Description:** The character can sense a specific type of energy or presence.

* **Prerequisite:** Awakened or Species Trait  
* **Benefit:** You can detect a specific aura (Evil, Magic, Life, Tech, Heat) within a **20 ft Radius** or a **40 ft Cone**. This requires active concentration (Standard Action).

### 

## **Sense, Uncontrolled**

**Description:** The character is constantly bombarded by sensory input.

* **Prerequisite:** Special  
* **Benefit:** As *Sense*, but the range increases to **30 ft Radius / 60 ft Cone**.  
* **Drawback:** It is always active ("Always On"). In areas with overwhelming presence of the sensed type (e.g., sensing Magic in a wizard's tower), the GM may impose distraction penalties.

### 

## **Sneak Attack**

**Description:** The character knows exactly where to strike to deal maximum damage to a vulnerable target.

* **Prerequisite:** Agility 2, Stealth 1  
* **Benefit:** If you attack a target that is **Flat-Footed** or **Flanked**, you deal extra damage.  
* **Mechanics:** Add your **Intellect Modifier** \+ **1d6 extra damage** to the roll.  
* **Special:** \[Ranked\] Each additional rank adds \+1d6 to the damage.

### 

  ## **Bleed**

  **Description:** A dirty fighting technique that opens veins or severs lines.

* **Prerequisite:** Sneak Attack  
* **Benefit:** When you deal Sneak Attack damage, the target also takes **1 point of Bleed damage** per die of Sneak Attack. This damage persists every round until healed/repaired.

  ### 

  ## **Blind**

  **Description:** A precision strike to the eyes or sensors.  
* **Prerequisite:** Sneak Attack  
* **Benefit:** You may trade your Sneak Attack damage dice to inflict the **Blinded** condition on the target for 1d4 rounds.

  ### 

  ## **Cripple**

  **Description:** A brutal strike to the arms or weapon systems.  
* **Prerequisite:** Sneak Attack  
* **Benefit:** You may trade your Sneak Attack damage dice to force the target to drop their weapon or disable one of their arms/manipulators.

  ### 

  ## **Hobble**

  **Description:** A hamstringing strike to the legs or motive systems.  
* **Prerequisite:** Sneak Attack  
* **Benefit:** When you deal Sneak Attack damage, the target's **Movement Speed is reduced by 50%**. A second successful Hobble immobilizes them.

### 

## **Technologist**

**Description:** The character is ahead of the curve, understanding technology that shouldn't exist yet. For a character to advance in their (TL Tech Level) they will require different fields of advanced study, the feature will need to be selected multiple times for various fields (minimum of five).

* **Prerequisite:** Intellect 3, Technology (Knowledge) 11  
* **Benefit:** You treat your effective **Tech Level** as one step higher for the purposes of crafting, repairing, or understanding advanced artifacts in a specific field of study.  
  * Agriculture  
  * Architecture  
  * Biotechnology  
  * Commerce  
  * Communication  
  * Devices  
  * Education  
  * Energy  
  * Manufacturing  
  * Materials  
  * Medicine  
  * Meta Sciences  
  * Science  
  * Society  
  * Synthetic Intelligence  
  * Transportation  
  * Weaponry

### 

## **Telepathy**

**Description:** The character can communicate mind-to-mind.

* **Prerequisite:** Awakened (Mental) or Species Trait  
* **Benefit:** You can communicate mentally with any intelligent lifeform within **Range** (usually 100ft \+).  
* **Constraint:** You must be aware of the subject. If they are unwilling, you cannot force communication without using Discipline powers. Language barriers still apply unless you have the *Universal Translator* power.

### 

## **Wild Speech**

**Description:** The character can speak with the beasts of the wild.

* **Prerequisite:** Nature Affinity, Fey, or Druidic training  
* **Benefit:** You can communicate with animals (Beast type) as if you shared a language. You can use **Social Skills** (Diplomacy, Bluff, Intimidate) on animals with no penalty.
