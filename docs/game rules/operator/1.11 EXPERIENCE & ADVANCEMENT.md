# **EXPERIENCE & ADVANCEMENT**

In the Tangent Science Fantasy Role-Playing system, character progression is driven by tangible narrative milestones, tactical engagement, and memorable roleplaying. Rather than utilizing abstract character levels, Tangent uses an organic, point-buy advancement system governed by **Award Points (AP)**.

Award Points (AP) are awarded for the outstanding actions and achievements of characters and their players during the game. These points serve as a way to recognize and reward exceptional gameplay, encouraging players to engage in creative and immersive roleplaying experiences.

The experience system is designed to enhance the gaming experience by incentivizing players to engage in meaningful and engaging gameplay. By rewarding players for their efforts, the system fosters a sense of accomplishment and encourages players to continue exploring the world of the game.

---

## **THE CORE MECHANICS: AWARD POINTS (AP)**

### **The Exchange Rate (1 AP = 1 BP)**
* **Exchange Rate:** **1 Award Point (AP) = 1 Build Point (BP)**.
* Award Points are spent in the exact same manner as Build Points in character creation, on a strict 1-for-1 basis.
* Characters accumulate AP as a permanent metric of their growth throughout the campaign. Total AP earned increases a character's overall point budget beyond the standard 150 BP starting pool.

### **The Increment Rule (CRITICAL)**
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Increment Rule (CRITICAL):**
> Abilities, skills, or other traits may **ONLY HAVE A 1 POINT INCREMENT OF ANY SCORE PER EXPERIENCE AWARD EVENT**.
> 
> A player cannot receive a 10 AP award and dump all 10 points into a single skill or trait instantly. Growth requires focus, reflection, and balanced training across scenes and chapters. If a player receives 10 AP, they may increase multiple distinct traits by 1 point each, or save the remaining AP for future awards and downtime training.

---

## **AWARD CATEGORIES**

Experience awards fall into three distinct tiers: **Story Awards**, **Session Awards**, and **Epic Ad Hoc Awards**.

```
                           EXPERIENCE AWARDS
                                   |
         +-------------------------+-------------------------+
         |                         |                         |
   STORY AWARDS             SESSION AWARDS              EPIC AWARDS
  (Story & Downtime)       (Post-Session Wrap)       (Ad Hoc In-Game)
   * Chapter: 5-10 AP        * Focus: 0-2 AP           * Epic Feats: 1-5 AP
   * Milestones: 1-3 AP      * Roleplay: 0-2 AP        * Stumping Architect
```

---

## **I. STORY AWARDS**

Story Awards recognize collective narrative breakthroughs, chapter completions, and the resolution of major conflicts. They are generally awarded during narrative transitions and major downtime periods.

### **1. Chapter Completion (5 to 10 AP)**
*(5 to 10 AP — based on the steps and the level of complexity involved, longer and complex chapters awarding more points)*

This award is given when a chapter or major story arc is completed. It acknowledges the players' progress in the narrative and provides an opportunity for character development and reflection during downtime. This is usually a **STORY transition** and represents significant down-time for the characters to train, reflect, and adapt to their evolving universe.

* **Short or Direct Chapter:** 5 to 6 AP (e.g., investigating a single derelict outpost, escorting an envoy across a single star system).
* **Medium or Complex Chapter:** 7 to 8 AP (e.g., infiltrating a syndicate citadel, disabling an planetary shield generator, uncovering an inter-faction conspiracy).
* **Major or Epic Arc Climax:** 9 to 10 AP (e.g., stopping an aetheric planar rupture, surviving a multi-fleet planetary blockade, toppling a sector warlord).

### **2. Overcoming Each Worthy GOAL, VILLAIN, or PLOT (1 to 3 AP)**
*(1, 2, or 3 AP — depending on level of difficulty; these are storyline elements but may be awarded by session)*

This award recognizes players' achievements in overcoming significant obstacles, defeating powerful villains, or unraveling complex plots. It highlights the importance of character growth and the resolution of conflicts.

* **Minor Goal or Lieutenant:** 1 AP (e.g., hacking a secure comms node, subduing a bounty hunter's strike team, securing safe passage).
* **Significant Villain or Threat:** 2 AP (e.g., neutralizing a regional crime boss, banishing an aberrant planar anomaly, cracking an encrypted meta-tech archive).
* **Mastermind, Nemesis, or Crucial Plotline:** 3 AP (e.g., defeating a sector nemesis in tactical combat, resolving a planetary diplomatic crisis without civil war).

> **Architect Example:**
> The Architect might award **5 AP** at the end of a short chapter for its completion and **2 AP** for defeating a major villain. Depending on the pacing and encounter density, villain awards may be granted at the end of the climactic session or tallied together at the chapter's conclusion.

---

## **II. SESSION AWARDS**

Session Awards are evaluated at the conclusion of every individual game session, regardless of whether a major story milestone was achieved. They encourage healthy table dynamics, tactical focus, and immersive roleplaying.

### **1. Proper Game Session & Focused on GAME (0, 1, or 2 AP)**
*(0, 1, or 2 AP)*

This award recognizes players who approach the game with a focus on gameplay mechanics, strategy, and collaborative play. It encourages players to engage actively in combat encounters, environmental puzzles, tactical logistics, and system challenges.

* **0 AP:** Inattentive session, frequent distractions, or lack of mechanical engagement.
* **1 AP:** Good engagement with mechanics, active combat participation, and solid teamwork.
* **2 AP:** Exceptional strategic leadership, innovative utilization of abilities, and proactive problem-solving.

### **2. Roleplaying in CHARACTER (0, 1, or 2 AP)**
*(0, 1, or 2 AP)*

This award is given to players who immerse themselves in their characters' personalities and motivations, actively embodying them during roleplaying interactions. It encourages players to develop their characters' backstories, explore relationships with other characters, and embrace flaws or convictions even when mechanically inconvenient.

* **0 AP:** Pure meta-gaming without character embodiment or roleplay interaction.
* **1 AP:** Consistent in-character decision-making, natural dialogue, and adherence to character concept.
* **2 AP:** Deep, memorable roleplaying moments that elevate the narrative for the whole table, revealing vulnerabilities or forging profound bonds.

> **Session Pacing Standard:**
> * **Standard Award:** **1 to 3 Award Points (AP) per Session** based on pacing and individual achievements.
> * For instance, a player might receive 2 AP for strategically leading the party in battle and 1 AP for a memorable roleplaying interaction (3 AP total for the session).

---

## **III. EPIC ACTIONS, GOOD IDEAS & STUMPING THE ARCHITECT**

### **Epic Ad Hoc Awards (1 to 5 AP)**
*(1 to 5 AP — Ad Hoc with an award determined by the Architect based on each situation. Session Award or possibly Scenario awarded.)*

These awards are given for exceptional, unscripted moments during gameplay, such as epic actions that turn the tide of battle, brilliant creative solutions to insurmountable problems, or surprising the Architect with ingenious tactical twists. They encourage players to think outside the box and bring their imaginations to life.

* **1 to 2 AP:** A remarkably clever tactic (e.g., using an atmospheric venting system to clear a toxic cloud; setting a bait maneuver that tricks enemy gunners).
* **3 to 4 AP:** A dramatic turning of the tide (e.g., sacrificing a ship component in a high-risk maneuver to save an allied vessel; pulling off an impossible social gamble under duress).
* **5 AP:** Stumping the Architect / Campaign-defining genius. The player conceives a complex, unforeseen plan that saves the city, the party, and the plot while making the Architect scramble to figure out the rolls. *Consider granting the character 5 AP right after the dust settles. It happens.*

## **SPENDING AWARD POINTS (DOWNTIME & ADVANCEMENT)**

Because Award Points translate directly to Build Points (1 AP = 1 BP), they follow the standard Tangent character creation costs, subject to **The Increment Rule**:

| Character Dimension | Cost in AP | Increment Limit | Notes & Requirements |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Skill Rank** | **1 AP per +1 Rank** | Max +1 Rank per award | Limited by standard skill rank caps (Novice to Pinnacle). Requires training context. |
| **Attribute Check** | **1 AP per +1 Score** | Max +1 Score per award | Directly improves Might, Reflex, Fortitude, Reason, Willpower, or Etiquette checks. |
| **Primary Attribute** | **5 AP per +1 Score** | Max +1 Score per award | Increases STR, AGI, STA, INT, WIS, or CHA. Simultaneously raises linked checks by +2. |
| **Feature / Perk** | **3 AP** *(2 AP if recommended)* | 1 Feature per award | Must meet prerequisites. 1 AP discount applies if on Origin, Faction, or Species list. |
| **Vitality Buffer** | **1 AP per +5 Vitality** | Max +5 Vitality per award | Increases non-lethal stamina and shielding cushion. |
| **Health Buffer** | **1 AP per +5 Health** | Max +5 Health per award | Increases structural physical trauma threshold. |
| **Specialization** | **1 AP per Rank** | Max +1 Rank per award | Narrow focus bonus (e.g., Submachine Guns, Astrogation, Xenobiology). |
| **Awakened Discipline** | **5 AP** | Max 1 Discipline per award | Unlocks access to a new Metafocus Discipline. |
| **Invocation / Spell** | **1 to 3 AP** | 1 Invocation per award | Varies by Stage (Novice to Pinnacle) within an Awakened discipline. |

## **EXPERIENCE DEBT & REVIVIFICATION**

In the Tangent system, cheating death comes at a profound price—known as **The High Cost of Dying**.

When a fallen character is brought back to life via advanced medical cybernetics (TL5+) or high-order Metaphysics (Revivification):
1. The character immediately loses **ALL remaining Karma Points** (resets to 0).
2. The character suffers a **-5 Experience Debt** due to existential and psychological trauma.

### **Paying Off Experience Debt**
* Whenever a character with Experience Debt receives an Experience Award, the debt must be addressed.
* The player may allocate earned AP on a 1-for-1 basis to reduce their Experience Debt before (or alongside) spending AP on traits.
* Alternatively, if the character cannot or chooses not to pay down the debt with incoming AP, the debt is settled by reducing an existing trait or traits by an equivalent value.
* Once the -5 Experience Debt reaches 0, normal advancement proceeds unhindered.

---

## **EXPERIENCE RECORD-KEEPING & FOLIO INTEGRATION**

The Persona Folio tracks three interconnected metrics to maintain complete transparency:

1. **Total AP Earned:** The cumulative lifetime total of all Award Points granted to the character. Added to the starting 150 BP pool, this determines the character's **Total Point Budget**.
2. **Unspent / Available AP:** Award Points currently on hand waiting to be allocated during downtime.
3. **Experience Debt:** Any outstanding revivification trauma debits requiring repayment.
4. **Experience Award Log:** A chronological ledger noting the date, session number, award category (Story, Session, or Epic), point value, and Architect's narrative rationale.
