# **ENLIGHTENED**

Certain worlds have particularly strong ley lines, special crystal formations, atmospheric anomalies, or other features that make them more metaphysically active. This could be in the form of a variety of metaphysical energies or patterns, but it is still a common occurrence.  
Structures on these worlds may have a wide range of designs based on creativity, ability, and wants or needs.  
Adept Occupations (and Metafocus Disciplines) are more common in these worlds.

## **Traits**

### *Empathy*

Has a deep sense of empathy and compassion for others

*\+2 Insight and \+2 Handle Animal Checks while being Friendly*

&nbsp;

### *Curiosity*

The character has a natural curiosity about the world around them, which drives them to explore and discover new things in the aquatic environment.

*Regain a Karma Point on the first encounter when dealing with something new during the session \- creature, area, technology, etc.*

&nbsp;

### *Mentorship*

May take on the role of a mentor or guide, sharing their knowledge and wisdom with others to help them grow and develop

*Those under the Enlightened character’s tutelage may make Study Checks at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Open-Mindedness*

Open to new ideas, perspectives, and experiences

*Reduce all Stigma Penalties with others by the Enlightened character’s Wisdom score for both parties*

&nbsp;

### *Peaceful Nature*

Seeks peace and harmony in all aspects of life

*\+2 to Diplomacy for peaceful meditations*

&nbsp;

### *Problem-Solving Skills*

Excels at problem-solving and critical thinking

*Once per Day may reroll a Mental Skill Check*

&nbsp;

### *Shared Wisdom*

Possesses wisdom beyond their years

*Any Check to Aid, Guide or Instruct another (granting them a bonus) may be made at Advantage*

&nbsp;

### *Spiritual Awareness*

May have a strong connection to spirituality or metaphysical concepts

*\+1 to Attune and one Metafocus Skill*

## **Origin Scenery & Visual Synthesis**

## **04\. ENLIGHTENED**

*Magic, psionics, weird science, crystals, temples.*

### **IMPYRIUM**

* **CONTEXT:** Massive mausoleums where psychic echoes of ancestors are stored. Gold-plated sarcophagi, burning incense, red velvet, psionic inquisitors.  
* **STYLE:** Sanctum of the Ancestors, Gothic Occult.  
* **PALETTE:** Gold, Velvet Red, Psionic Purple, Shadow.  
* **TOKENS:** Skulls, candles, incense, heavy robes, whispering.

### **SYNDICATE**

* **CONTEXT:** Sterile white rooms where psychics are plugged into servers. "Magic" is quantified as data. Holographic pentagrams, neural-jacks, containment fields.  
* **STYLE:** Psionic Data-Center, Lab Magic.  
* **PALETTE:** Lab White, Hologram Cyan, Data Blue, Chrome.  
* **TOKENS:** Server racks, cables, holograms, glass, sterility.

### **DYNASTY**

* **CONTEXT:** Volcanic vents or high peaks. Ancient stone altars dedicated to elemental dragons. Magma flows, lightning rods, shamans in scale armor.  
* **STYLE:** Dragon Shrine, Elemental Temple.  
* **PALETTE:** Magma Red, Basalt Black, Lightning Blue, Ash.  
* **TOKENS:** Fire, stone, altars, dragon statues, storms.

### **OUTWORLDS**

* **CONTEXT:** A place where reality is broken. Floating islands, purple lightning storms, scavengers hunting for artifacts in the distortion zones.  
* **STYLE:** Wild Magic Zone, Stalker Zone.  
* **PALETTE:** Distortion Purple, Sickly Green, Rust, Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Floating rocks, lightning, anomalies, junk, danger.

### **COALITION**

* **CONTEXT:** Hidden basements in gritty cities. Neon-lit summoning circles, black market spell-tech, smoky opium-den vibe with spirits.  
* **STYLE:** Occult Underground, Cyber-Witchcraft.  
* **PALETTE:** Neon Pink, Shadow Black, Candle Yellow, Smoke Grey.  
* **TOKENS:** Graffiti runes, candles, tech-scraps, smoke, shadows.

### **AULURAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Massive spirit-trees with glowing white bark. Whispering winds, floating pollen that acts as psychic dust. Ancestral spirits manifest as animals.  
* **STYLE:** Grove of Souls, Spirit Forest.  
* **PALETTE:** Ghostly White, Pale Blue, Forest Green, Bioluminescence.  
* **TOKENS:** Giant trees, spirits, pollen, glowing bark, peace.

### **ALTERIAN ENCLAVE**

* **CONTEXT:** Floating towers of ivory and gold. Books flying on their own. Impossible geometry, spiraling staircases into the sky. The peak of magical civilization.  
* **STYLE:** High Arcane Academy, Magic Spire.  
* **PALETTE:** Ivory, Gold, Royal Blue, Magic Sparkles.  
* **TOKENS:** Floating books, crystals, towers, stars, elegance.

### **ASCENDANCY**

* **CONTEXT:** Crystalline skyscrapers that resonate with mental energy. Meditation gardens, levitation pads, clean lines, focus on mental clarity.  
* **STYLE:** Psionic Spire, Jedi Temple.  
* **PALETTE:** Crystal White, sky Blue, Silver, Soft Light.  
* **TOKENS:** Crystals, meditation, floating pads, clean geometry.

### **ENTARI COMBINE**

* **CONTEXT:** A museum-city of artifacts from all races. Curators in silk robes, magical displays, open knowledge, glass vaults.  
* **STYLE:** Archive of Wonders, Galactic Museum.  
* **PALETTE:** Warm Stone, Glass, Artifact Gold, Soft Lighting.  
* **TOKENS:** Display cases, artifacts, holograms, libraries, study.

### **MEKAN**

* **CONTEXT:** Magic visualized as complex fractals. Hard-light constructs, mathematical sigils floating in the air. "Magic" is just uncompiled code.  
* **STYLE:** The Equation, Digital Sorcery.  
* **PALETTE:** Neon Purple, Grid Black, Fractal White.  
* **TOKENS:** Floating shapes, numbers, grids, hard light, logic.
