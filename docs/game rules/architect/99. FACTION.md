# **FACTION FRAMEWORK**

## 

## **Introduction: The Sociological Chassis**

In the Tangent universe, a Faction is not merely a political boundary; it is a **Sociological Chassis** that defines a character's worldview, resources, and operational parameters. Just as a Species defines biological capabilities, the Faction defines cultural software: education, prejudices, equipment standards, and economic standing.  
This framework synthesizes **Narrative Architecture**, **Game Mechanics (Bonuses/Wealth)**, and **Visual Semiotics (AI Prompts)** into a unified design document. It ensures that every faction entry serves as a functional "Source of Truth" for World Building and Character Creation.

## 

## 

## **PART I: THE CONSTRUCTION PROTOCOL**

To ensure consistency across the Tangent Universe, all factions must be built using this four-phase protocol. This ensures that a "Militaristic" faction in one sector functions mechanically similarly to one in another, even if their flavor differs.

### 

### **PHASE 1: ARCHETYPE SELECTION**

*Select one primary archetype to define the faction's mechanical baseline. This determines their Skill Package focus and default Features.*

| Archetype | Focus | Key Skill Groups | Implied Wealth |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Militaristic** | Conquest / Defense | Combat (Any), Tactics, Leadership | Moderate (+1 to \+2) |
| **Corporate / Mercantile** | Profit / Efficiency | Administration, Negotiation, Vocation | High (+3 to \+4) |
| **Religious / Cult** | Dogma / Purity | Academics (Theology), Insight, Social | Variable (0 to \+4) |
| **Technological** | Innovation / Logic | Science, Engineering, Computers | High (+2 to \+5) |
| **Criminal / Syndicate** | Illicit Gain / Control | Streetwise, Stealth, Intimidate | Variable (Black Market) |
| **Exploration / Academic** | Discovery / Knowledge | Piloting, Survival, Sciences | Moderate (+1 to \+2) |
| **Agrarian / Colony** | Survival / Growth | Nature, Vocation, Fortitude | Low (0 to \+1) |
| **Isolationist / Alien** | Preservation / Xenophobia | Stealth, Awareness, Cultural | Variable |

### 

### **PHASE 2: MECHANICAL SKELETON**

*Define the hard numbers that govern the faction's power.*

#### 

#### **1\. Technological Level (TL)**

*Reference: 05.01 TECHNOLOGY*  
**TL 0-1 (Primitive/Archaic):** Pre-industrial or low-tech. (Wealth Modifier: \-4 to \-2)  
**TL 2 (Industrial):** Combustion engines, projectile weapons. (Wealth Modifier: 0\)  
**TL 3 (Stellar \- Standard):** FTL, Energy weapons, standard shields. (Wealth Modifier: \+2)  
**TL 4 (Advanced):** Anti-grav, Nanotech, High-Energy fields. (Wealth Modifier: \+4)  
**TL 5 (Metaphysical/Post-Scarcity):** Polymatter, Psi-Tech, Reality manipulation. (Wealth Modifier: \+8)

#### 

#### **2\. Economic Standing (Wealth Modifier)**

Reference: 02 PROPERTY  
Determine the faction's global Wealth Modifier. This bonus is applied to the Wealth Score of characters belonging to this faction.  
**Struggling (0):** Outworlds, Refugees, Scavengers.  
**Stable (+1 to \+2):** Dynasty, Auluran, Standard Colonies.  
**Affluent (+3):** Alterian, Impyrium, Entari.  
**Hyper-Wealthy (+4 to \+6):** Syndicate, Mekan.

#### 

#### **3\. Faction Skill Package (20 Points)**

Reference: 01.03 FACTIONS & 01.08 SKILLS  
Create a curated list of skills that represents the faction's "Basic Training."  
**Rule:** Distribute **20 Skill Points** among 4-6 relevant skills.  
**Example (Militaristic):** Firearms (5), Tactics (5), Athletics (5), Discipline (5).

#### 

#### **4\. Faction Features (Select 2\)**

Reference: 01.07 FEATURES  
Select two features that characters from this faction may take at a discount (1 BP instead of 3 BP).  
**Examples:** *Authority*, *Clearance*, *Wealthy*, *Contacts*, *Combat Trained*.

### 

### **PHASE 3: NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE**

*Define the "Cultural Software" and governance.*  
**Governance:** How do they rule? (e.g., Corporatocracy, Feudal Monarchy, Hive Mind).  
**Law Level:** How strict is the control? (e.g., Lawless, Moderate, Police State).  
**Sociological Hooks:** What defines their daily life? (e.g., "Subscription Service" for the Syndicate, "Caste System" for the Dynasty).

### 

### **PHASE 4: KINETIC SEMIOTICS (AESTHETICS)**

*Define the physical look of the faction using the "Chassis & Skin" philosophy.*  
**Architecture:** What shapes define them? (e.g., Hexagons, Spires, Domes, Brutalist Blocks).  
**Materials:** What are things made of? (e.g., White Ceramic, Rusted Steel, Living Wood, Black Chrome).  
**Lighting:** What is the mood? (e.g., Neon, Sunlight, Torchlight, Bioluminescence).
