#### **Expanded Tier Descriptions:**

* **Tiers 0-2 (Civilian/Adept/Militia):** Everyday populace, unaugmented wildlife, or standard grunt-level security. They possess rudimentary to basic professional training in their daily vocation but are largely ineffective in a sustained firefight against true professionals. Dangerous in groups, manageable alone.  
* **Tiers 3-5 (Trooper/Standard/Professional):** Career soldiers, experienced bounty hunters, and advanced predatory wildlife. They utilize cover, flanking maneuvers, and fully understand their weaponry. They represent the standard threat level for an engaged and active combat zone. (Note: Tier 5 is the mathematical equivalent of a starting 150 CP Player Character).  
* **Tiers 6-9 (Veteran/Specialist/Elite/Operative):** Hardened survivors, squad commanders, special forces operatives, and master martial artists. Their high attributes and Expert-level skills make them highly lethal one-on-one. They often possess Augmentations, advanced Features, and superior-tier gear.  
* **Tiers 10-13 (Champion/Warlord/Commander/General):** The threshold of Boss-level adversaries or legendary Allies. Faction generals, ancient beasts, or heavily modified cyborgs. They have vast health pools (+50 to \+65, doubled for Boss status) and specialized, devastating attacks. Their mastery of skills means they dictate the terms of engagement and rarely miss.  
* **Tiers 14-16 (Paragon/High Lord/Heroic):** Characters that can subjugate entire cities alone. Grand Masters of metaphysical disciplines, apex Dragons, or highly advanced Synthesis constructs. Beings that require an entire coordinated strike team and extensive, multi-stage planning to successfully engage.  
* **Tiers 17-19 (Legend/Ascendant/Demigod):** Myths made manifest and beings of immense, localized power. Avatars of the U'Nonn, rogue AI that have consumed planetary computing networks, or ancient figures whose very presence warps the local environment.  
* **Tier 20 (Cosmic/Mythic):** Reality-warping beings. The sleeping Ila, Cosmic horrors, or literal gods. They possess Pinnacle skills (Rank 30+) and operate completely beyond the standard laws of physics, mortality, and the battlefield.

#### **Deriving Stats from the Base Chassis:**

* **Skill Allocation:** The chassis provides a Primary and Secondary Skill Rank. Apply the Primary Rank to the first "Key Skill" listed in the character's chosen Role Package. Apply the Secondary Rank to all remaining Key Skills in that package.  
* **Initiative:** Agility Mod \+ Alertness Rank.  
* **Attack Bonus:** Primary Attribute Mod \+ Key Offensive Skill Rank (as dictated by Role).  
* **Defense:** 10 \+ Agility Mod \+ Defense Skill Rank \+ Armor Modifiers.  
* **Saves (Will/Fort/Ref):** 10 \+ Attribute Mod \+ Feature Bonus.  
* **Base Movement:** 30ft Groundspeed (unless modified by Species Scale, Faction traits, or Role features such as the Mobility Specialist).  
* **Wealth Score:** A character's base Wealth Score is equal to double their Threat Tier, representing their escalating influence and access to superior tier gear. *Exceptions:* Companions do not generate a Wealth Score; they are completely dependent on the Player Character for equipment and liquidity. Furthermore, feral beasts, mindless constructs, and cosmic anomalies do not utilize Wealth Scores.  
* **Vitality & Health (or Structure):** All characters possess a Base Vitality of 30 and Base Health of 30\. The "Vit/Health Bonus" column is added directly to both of these base values to determine maximum pools. Synthetics and Mecha do not possess Vitality; to calculate their Structure Points (SP), combine the total values of the Vitality and Health pools (Vitality \+ Health \= SP).  
* **Size & Multiplier Stacking:** The scaling multipliers from the Scaling Framework apply universally to all life pools. For biological characters, multiply their final calculated Vitality and Health totals by their Size Scale Multiplier (e.g., Tiny x0.5, Large x2, Huge x5). *Note:* If a character possesses multiple multipliers (e.g., a Large Boss), calculate the Size Multiplier *first* to establish the physical baseline, then apply the Designation Multiplier (Boss x2 / Mastermind x3+) to that new total. (Multipliers are multiplicative).  
* **Invocation Stages:** When calculating the damage or scale of a metaphysical Invocation, convert the character's Discipline Skill Rank (20 maximum) plus the Invocation Rank (10 maximum) into Stages: level 1 to 5 \- Novice (Stage 1), 6 to 11 \- Trained (Stage 2), 12 \- 15 \- Expert (Stage 3), 16 to 20 \- Master (Stage 4), 21 to 25 \- Grand Master (Stage 5), 26 to 30 \- Pinnacle (Stage 6).  
* **Essence Economy:** Manifesting an Invocation at its base learned Stage costs 0 Essence (it is powered by ambient resonance). Pushing an Invocation to cast at a *higher* Stage than the character's current Skill Rank (any Invocation check over 10 difficulty), or maintaining multiple sustained effects simultaneously, costs 1 Essence per additional Stage/Effect per round.
