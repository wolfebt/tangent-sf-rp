## **The Epistemology of Material Assets in Role-Playing Systems**

The construction of a coherent speculative fiction ecosystem requires a rigorous integration of narrative philosophy, visual aesthetics, and mechanical function.1 Within the Tangent universe, the definition of "equipment" extends significantly beyond the utilitarian metrics of damage output or cargo capacity.2 Gear represents the essential connective tissue bridging the individual actor and a hostile, multi-dimensional reality.3 It is a kinetic declaration of technological ethos, cultural values, and economic leverage.2  
Historically, the design of economic and material systems within tabletop role-playing games has suffered from a fundamental fracture between narrative ambition and mechanical execution.4 This economic dissonance arises when the disparate currencies of a game—time, experience, liquid wealth, and crafting effort—fail to exchange at consistent, logical rates.4 Legacy systems frequently rely on linear pricing models derived from arbitrary utility balance rather than intrinsic complexity, leading to ludonarrative failures where the acquisition of a starship and the forging of a sword operate on entirely incompatible mathematical realities.4  
The Tangent Equipment Matrix resolves this dissonance by synthesizing a unified mechanical engine that standardizes creation, modification, and valuation across all physical assets.3 It establishes a definitive source of truth by dividing all material objects into two conceptual layers.3 The first layer is the "Agnostic Chassis," the fundamental, physics-compliant engineering skeleton governed strictly by mass, scale, and complexity.2 The second layer is the "Cultural Skin," the aesthetic and functional overlay that aligns the machine with a specific faction’s technological paradigm.2  
Crucially, this matrix strictly delineates the externalization of capability from the internalization of the self.3 While the '99 \- AUGMENTATIONS MATRIX' governs the microscopic, biological realities of physiological modification and cybernetic rejection, the Equipment Matrix governs the macroscopic world.3 External equipment operates independently of biological capacity, interacting instead with the universal laws of spatial displacement and economic valuation.3

## **The Universal Displacement Unit and the Tier 1 Protocol**

To ensure absolute mathematical consistency across personal inventory, vehicular engineering, and architectural design, the matrix relies upon the Universal Displacement Unit (UDU) hierarchy.2 The UDU system is the spatial foundation of the Tangent universe, dictating capacity limits from the microscopic to the cosmic.6  
The '99 \- AUGMENTATIONS MATRIX' establishes "Nodes" as Tier 0\.5 A Node represents less than 10 grams of mass, serving as the granular unit for internal biological strain and cybernetic installation.5 The Equipment Matrix, however, operates strictly at Tier 1 and above, bypassing biological strain entirely.3 The absolute floor for external equipment is the Socket.3

### 

### **The Socket: The Foundation of Tier 1**

A Socket represents the fundamental unit of personal-scale spatial displacement, equating to less than 1 kilogram (2.2 lbs) of mass or functional complexity.2 The Socket dictates a character's carrying capacity, the number of modifications a weapon chassis can support, and the granular integration of external components.3  
A Standard Fit means a Socket accepts 1 standard modification, attachment, or component. However, items may also feature a single **Minor Option** (e.g., a laser sight, cosmetic light, or concealed pocket) which utilizes negligible bulk and does not consume a Socket.3  
The UDU hierarchy scales exponentially from this baseline to accommodate larger entities and structures without relying on disjointed conversion tables.2 While preserving the absolute physical displacement metrics of 10g, 1kg, 100kg, and 10 tons, the matrix introduces a consistent 10:1 integration bottleneck across all scales.5 Just as the biological protocol dictates that only 10 Nodes are actively usable per 1 Socket, this bottleneck is replicated at the macro-scales: a Mount allows for exactly 10 Usable Sockets, and a Module allows for exactly 10 Usable Mounts.5

| SCALE CATEGORY | DEFINITION AND CAPACITY | INTEGRATION BOTTLENECK |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Tier 0: Nodes (Implant Scale)** | A "Node" represents less than 10 grams of mass.  | 10 Usable Nodes \= 1 Socket |
| **Tier 1: Socket (Personal)** | The baseline unit for handheld items, weapon modifications, and cybernetic sub-routines. Maximum 1 kg displacement. | — |
| **Tier 2: Mount (Mecha/Vehicle)** | The baseline unit for vehicular weapons, engines, and heavy cargo. Maximum 100 kg displacement. | 1 Mount \= 10 Usable Sockets |
| **Tier 3: Module (Architecture)** | The baseline unit for structural rooms, capital ship hangars, and planetary facilities. Maximum 10 Tons displacement. | 1 Module \= 10 Usable Mounts |

This strict hierarchy ensures seamless cross-scale integration.3 A Mecha (Tier 2\) or a Structure (Tier 3\) can store or equip Personal-scale items (Tier 1\) taking up mathematically accurate space.6 While a single vehicle Mount designated as a utility bay possesses 100 kilograms of volumetric displacement, its active capacity is capped at precisely 10 Usable Sockets worth of personal equipment.2 Furthermore, garaging rules remain perfectly consistent: storing a Medium-sized vehicle requires 0.1 Modules of architectural space, while a Huge Mecha requires exactly 1 full Module.1

### 

### **Tier 2: The Mount (Hardpoints & Sub-Scale Gear)**

The **Mount** is a sub-scale hardpoint (Maximum 100 kg displacement). In architectural and capital-ship design, Mounts are used for functional components that do not require an entire room. This includes exterior structural armor plating, energy shield emitters, defense turrets, sensor dishes, and localized utility nodes.

### 

### **Tier 3: The Module (Rooms & Facilities)**

The **Module** is the baseline unit of macro-scale structural volume (representing roughly 400 square feet of functional space, or a 20x20 ft area, up to a Maximum 10 Tons displacement). In architectural terms, a Module represents a physical room or a massive internal facility—such as a generator room, a barracks, a medical bay, or a vehicle hangar. A building's total Size and Height determine how many Modules it can hold.

### **Dimensional Footprint and Base Complexity**

Every discrete piece of equipment is assigned a Size Category, which dictates its default capacity in Sockets, its base Structure Points (HP), and its baseline Crafting Difficulty Class (DC).3 This establishes the unflavored mechanical reality of the Agnostic Chassis before technological era or cultural skin is applied.2

Items classified as "Fine" possess a mass so negligible they do not consume standard Socket capacity for inventory management.3 They may serve as minor aesthetic additions or highly miniaturized components, though they remain subject to standard durability constraints.3

## **The Matrix of Eras and Technological Sub-Strata**

The efficiency, durability, and operational methodology of an item's chassis are explicitly gated by its Tech Level (TL) and its resulting Complexity Stage.3 The matrix of "Eras and Comparative Technology" serves as the definitive guideline for understanding the availability and sophistication of equipment across the galaxy.9  
Technological progress in Tangent is not a rigid ladder, but a fluid spectrum deeply influenced by resource availability, environmental pressures, and cultural dogma.9 To provide a baseline mechanical standard before exploring the nuanced sub-strata, all materials and passive traits are broadly categorized by their primary era.3

| TL | ERA | MATERIAL COMPOSITION | PASSIVE TRAIT |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 1-2 | Analog | Stamped Metal, Plastic | None |
| 3 | Digital | Aluminum, Composite | **Modular:** Universal ports; share batteries/charge. |
| 4 | Enhanced | Smart-Fabric, Nanocarbon | **Self-Repairing:** Regens 1 HP/hour. Mastery of Shields/Hover/Directed Energy. |
| 5 | Advanced | Polymatter, Hard-Light | **Morphic / Weightless:** Instant reconfiguration and functional mass projection. |

To accurately represent the nuance of innovation within these eras, each Tech Level is further divided into three distinct sub-strata: Nascent (TL-), representing experimental and bulky prototypes; Standard (TL), representing refined and mass-produced baselines; and Advanced (TL+), representing optimized miniaturization bordering on the capabilities of the next era.9

### 

### **TL 0: The Stone Age (Primitive / Pre-Industrial)**

Operating entirely outside of complex mechanical dependence, TL 0 equipment relies on inherent magic, survival strategies, or deep cultural dogma that rejects fragility in favor of organic strength.9 Materials consist of unworked stone, bone, wood, and animal hides in the Nascent stage, advancing to bronze, iron, and masonry in the Standard stage.9 Equipment at this tier possesses the "Degrading" trait, breaking easily under sustained stress, and offers minimal Damage Resistance (DR).6

### 

### **TL 1: The Metal Age (Industrial / Mechanical)**

Marking the transition from the Iron Age to the Industrial Revolution, TL 1 is characterized by the harnessing of fire for foundries, allowing for mass metallurgy.9 Power sources shift from muscle and wind to coal, steam, and early fossil fuels.9 Nascent technologies include intricate clockwork mechanisms akin to the Antikythera mechanism, complex architectural domes, alchemy, plate armor, and crossbows, as well as stationary steam engines and the printing press.9 Standard innovations introduce internal combustion, early photography, and rudimentary vaccines.9 Advanced TL 1+ borders on the digital, featuring direct current (DC) electricity, radio, biplanes, early X-Rays, and chemical weapons.9 Equipment here is heavy, highly durable, but imposes significant check penalties due to its unrefined ergonomics.6

### 

### **TL 2: The Data Age (Digital / Information)**

Comparable to modern Earth, TL 2 spans from early telegraphy to the colonization of the local solar system.9 Power generation relies on advanced hydrocarbons, fission, and renewable infrastructure.9 The era is defined by global connectivity, digital networks, and the miniaturization of electronics.9 Nascent tech includes jet engines, orbital rocketry, and fission bombs.9 The Standard era perfects GPS, reusable rockets, and genetic sequencing.9 The Advanced TL 2+ tier borders on cybernetic integration, featuring quantum prototypes, functional virtual reality, nascent artificial intelligence algorithms, and prototype railguns.9 Protective equipment utilizes kevlar, ceramic plates, and advanced alloys, possessing a "Ballistic Weave" trait that highly resists piercing kinetic trauma.6

### 

### **TL 3: The Space Age (Stellar / The Gate Era)**

The dawn of interstellar expansion, breaking the bonds of the home star system.9 Power is generated via Thorium reactors and Hydrogen fuel cells.9 A critical limitation of this era is that artificial gravity remains achievable only via spin or thrust; manipulation of gravitons is not yet mastered.9 TL 3 represents the galactic baseline for common trade, featuring reaction mass space flight (Ionic or Fusion), memory materials with limited shape retention, and molecular nanotechnology.9 Advanced TL 3+ pushes the limits of thermodynamics with the discovery of Tangent Space for faster-than-light travel, holographic transceivers, and energy shields that impose massive power drains.9 TL 3 equipment is defined by the "Modular" trait, featuring universal data ports, standard charging protocols, and vacuum-ready hermetic seals.3

### 

### **TL 4: The Stellar Age (Galactic / The Warp Era)**

The domain of high-tier galactic factions, characterized by a practical mastery over matter and force where standard physics are treated as a mere suggestion.9 Power generation shifts to antimatter, cold fusion, and Aetherium integration.9 This era masters gravity with artificial grav-plating, inertial dampeners, and repulsorlift hover technology.9 Starships feature independent Tangent Drives for FTL travel without relying on fixed gate networks.9 Biological limitations are bypassed via "Clinical Immortality" cellular treatments and trans-neural interfaces that turn the biological brain into a supercomputer terminal.9  
Material science at TL 4 is defined by programmable materials engineered at the nanoscale.9 Equipment utilizes smart-fabrics, nanocarbon weaves, and phase-shift armor.3 All TL 4 equipment inherently possesses the "Self-Repairing" trait, regenerating 1 Structure Point per hour due to localized nanotech, while kinetic weaponry becomes entirely obsolete unless chemically or magnetically hyper-accelerated to keep pace with directed energy and plasma.6

### 

### **TL 5: The Galactic Age (Cosmic / The Singularity)**

The realm of "God-Like" power, representing the apex of material science.9 The pillars of this era are matter-energy conversion (replicators), dimensional compression (folding space), and absolute gravitonic control.9 The distinction between hardware and software vanishes completely.9 Equipment at this tier relies heavily on two exotic technologies: Polymatter and Holophotonics.3  
Polymatter is a synthetic, programmable substrate existing in a hyper-potential state.9 It is composed of true liquid metal held in a magnetic suspension that reacts instantaneously to telepathic or digital commands.3 Equipment forged from Polymatter possesses the "Morphic" trait, allowing it to reshape as a Free Action into complex mechanical forms, such as shifting from a sensory scanner into a lethal weapon.3 Polymatter also utilizes Base Substance Exchange, integrating ambient foreign matter (rock, steel scrap) directly into its matrix to increase its mass or repair structural damage.3  
Holophotonics, developed by highly advanced entities, consist of programmable hard-light constructs.3 Unlike visual illusions, these are tactile, functional photons capable of exerting kinetic force, bearing immense weight, and cutting through steel.3 Holophotonic gear grants the "Weightless" trait.3 The physical emitter is often the size of a piece of Fine jewelry (0 Sockets), carrying zero bulk when inactive, but projecting full functional mass when triggered.3

## **Complexity Gates and the Skill Economy**

A highly advanced piece of equipment is only as effective as the operator manipulating it.3 The matrix introduces "Complexity Gates" to simulate the difficulty of mastering disparate technological tiers, directly linking the item's Crafting DC to the Skill Rank required for safe operation.3

|  | CLASSIFICATION | CRAFT DC | SKILL  | EFFECT OF OPERATIONAL INCOMPETENCE |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **T1** | **Simple** | DC 0 \- 9 | Untrained (0+) | None. The item is universally intuitive. |
| **T2** | **Standard** | DC 10 \- 14 | Trained (6+) | The user suffers Disadvantage on all operational skill checks. |
| **T3** | **Advanced** | DC 15 \- 19 | Expert (11+) | Cannot activate secondary or active abilities; passive functions only. |
| **T4** | **Experimental** | DC 20+ | Master (16+) | Cannot operate the item safely, severe risk of catastrophic Glitch. |

The calculation of saving throws against standard equipment—such as resisting the blast of an explosive round or the voltage of a stun module—is mathematically anchored to this complexity.3 Unless specifically overridden by the item's stat block, standard Equipment Save DCs are calculated as 10 plus twice the Item Tier, plus the Operator's Key Ability Modifier.3 This ensures that the efficacy of gear scales logically with both the operator's proficiency and the item's intrinsic sophistication.3  
Furthermore, characters possessing the "Technologist" feature may operate equipment ahead of their era's standard TL, requiring intense specialization across multiple fields (e.g., Biotechnology, Energy, Weaponry).9 Without this feature, attempting to repair or modify a device above the character's native Technology Level incurs a severe \-5 penalty per level of difference.9

## **The Cultural Skin: Manufacturer Paradigms**

While the Agnostic Chassis and its Tech Level dictate the underlying mathematics and material capabilities, the "Cultural Skin" dictates the narrative aesthetics and specific mechanical nuances.2 A small pistol (2 Sockets, TL 3\) manufactured by the Syndicate is statistically and operationally distinct from the identical chassis manufactured by the Auluran.3 The Manufacturer Paradigm applies a specific aesthetic overlay and a unique mechanical trait, transforming the vehicle, weapon, or tool into a mobile artifact of its originating civilization.2

### 

### **The Syndicate (Corporate Futurism)**

Functioning as a corporate utopia, the Syndicate prioritizes "Technical Facilitation" and a highly curated user-first experience.2 Equipment aesthetics mirror an "Apple Store Cathedral"—hyper-efficient, seamless, sterile, and heavily branded with corporate iconography.2 Construction utilizes matte white composites, brushed aluminum, and ergonomic contouring with cyan holographic data-streams.2  
Syndicate gear possesses the "Integrated System" mechanical trait.3 The equipment is constantly tethered to the "Mesh" network, granting operators a \+2 bonus to Knowledge checks (Computers, Technology, Language) when interfacing with local networks.3 However, this interconnectivity comes with significant drawbacks for non-citizens; features are frequently locked behind subscription paywalls, and failure to tithe the corporation results in the gear entering "Penance Mode," severely reducing functionality.2

### 

### **The Impyrium (Heirloom Industrial)**

The Impyrium operates under a philosophy of "Enduring Dominance," where technology is maintained through rote tradition and religious ritual rather than genuine scientific understanding.2 Equipment exhibits a "Celestial Neo-Sumerian" aesthetic—massive, brutalist structures clad in gold filigree, polished white marble, and purity seals.2 Vehicles and armor appear exceptionally heavy, prioritizing slow but unstoppable momentum.2  
Impyrium gear possesses the "Archeotech" mechanical trait, granting it \+2 Damage Resistance (DR) and \+2 Hardness due to its ancient, over-engineered reinforcement.3 However, this gear relies exclusively on "Relic Sockets." An Impyrium item outright rejects modern or alien commercial modifications, accepting only authorized, ancient archeotech components, rendering it highly inflexible in the field.2

### 

### **The Dracon Dynasty (Feudal Protection)**

The Dracon Dynasty adheres to a philosophy of "Noblesse Oblige," where equipment is explicitly designed to protect the user and project absolute authority.2 Their aesthetic is described as "High-Fantasy Industrial," featuring heavy, blocky silhouettes reminiscent of castles or keeps.2 Interiors prioritize warmth, utilizing orange and gold lighting, tapestries, and heraldic crests, while exteriors feature dragon-inspired motifs such as sculpted scales.2  
Dracon Dynasty gear is synonymous with the "Bulwark" trait.6 Their weapons heavily favor devastating kinetic and thermal output, while their armor and vehicular plating prioritize structural integrity over speed, granting a \+20% bonus to Structure Points (SP) and an inherent \+1 DR.2 Their starships are constructed as "Void Castles," designed to weather overwhelming sieges.2

### 

### **The Ascendancy (Enlightened Innovation)**

In direct contrast to the stagnant Impyrium, the Ascendancy operates on a philosophy of "Adaptive Progress," innovating rapidly.2 Their design language is "Smart City Solarpunk," characterized by clean curves, expansive glass canopies, and pristine white hulls accented with blue energy emissions.2  
Ascendancy technology is highly user-friendly, heavily shielded, and designed for agility and precision rather than raw stopping power.2 Their vehicles feature variable-geometry wings and high-efficiency ion drives, and their tactical doctrine uniquely integrates sophisticated non-lethal weapon options alongside standard lethal armaments, reflecting a hopeful, forward-looking ethos.2

### 

### **The Coalition & Outworlds (Scrappy Industrial)**

Operating on the fringes of the galaxy, the Coalition embraces "Survival Pragmatism".2 Their aesthetic is "Space Western Noir," defined by the reality that nothing is new and everything is repurposed.2 Equipment consists of scavenged parts, asymmetrical hulls, exposed wiring, welded patch-jobs, and ubiquitous hazard stripes.2 Due to sporadic maintenance capabilities, redundancy is prioritized over elegance.2  
Coalition equipment possesses the "Jury-Rigged" mechanical trait.3 These scavenged items are significantly cheaper to acquire, applying a \-5 DC reduction to all acquisition and crafting checks.3 However, this affordability brings extreme unreliability: on a Natural 1 skill check, the item breaks (permanently disabling a Socket) or triggers a catastrophic failure, such as inflicting 1d4 Fire damage to the operator.3 Their weaponry heavily favors cheap, loud ballistics and oversized engines bolted haphazardly onto aging frames.2

### 

### **The Alterian Enclave (Botanical Engineering)**

The Alterians treat magic as a rigorous science seamlessly integrated into their engineering, following a philosophy termed "The Long View," where vehicles and tools are works of art designed to endure for centuries.2 Their aesthetic is "Interstellar Art Nouveau," combining impossible grace, silent propulsion, solar sails, and white ceramic fused intimately with living wood.2  
Alterian gear possesses the "Photosynthesis" and "Naturalist" traits.3 Equipment acts as a solar capacitor, with energy weapons (such as their signature Sun-Lances) and powered tools automatically regenerating charges and ammunition when exposed to direct sunlight.2 The organic resonance of the living wood grants the user a \+2 bonus to Nature and Survival checks.3 Their vehicles eschew traditional armor plating, utilizing open, airy cockpit designs protected entirely by advanced force fields.2

### 

### **The Auluran (Symbiotic Growth)**

The Auluran species follows a "Biological Imperative." Their technology is not forged in foundries but grown in vats.2 The aesthetic is a "Bioluminescent Jungle," featuring chitinous hulls, vascular corridors, pearlescent finishes, and fluid, organic shapes entirely devoid of hard angles.2 Auluran gear feels warm to the touch and operates as a living organism.3  
Auluran equipment possesses the "Regeneration" and "Symbiosis" traits.3 The gear actively heals itself, repairing 2 Structure Points per round without the need for mechanical repair.3 Furthermore, a user can expend 1 Karma to instantly trigger the Symbiosis mechanic, forcing the biological gear to rapidly bond and adapt to a new environmental hazard.3 Their weaponry utilizes spore and acid-based payloads, operated via neural-link control interfaces that require deep biological compatibility.2

### 

### **The Mekan (Mechanical Perfection)**

Operating entirely on "The Code," the Mekan believe that form must follow function instantaneously.2 Their aesthetic is one of "Fractal Perfection," utilizing perfect geometric shapes (spheres, tetrahedrons), liquid metal hulls, and components held in suspension by powerful magnetic fields.2  
Mekan equipment relies heavily on TL 5 Polymatter.3 This grants the "Integrated Tools" trait, allowing the equipment to autonomously shift its physical form to aid the user, granting a \+2 bonus to Vocation checks.3 In combat, a Mekan vehicle might reconfigure its entire shape mid-maneuver to adapt to an incoming threat, and their tactical doctrine relies on "Swarm" operations where many small, polymorphic units coalesce to act as a single massive entity.2

### 

### **The Entari Combine (Cosmopolitan Trade)**

The Entari prioritize "Ethical Efficiency," designing technology specifically for trade, high-end diplomacy, and supreme comfort.2 Their aesthetic represents a "Galactic Senate" ideal—a highly curated blend of the best features appropriated from other factions, smoothed over with a luxury eco-finish.2  
Entari equipment utilizes silent, seamless nanoweave materials, visually communicating immense wealth and precision.6 Their vehicles feature universal docking rings and advanced translation suites, maintaining non-threatening silhouettes by utilizing entirely concealed weaponry, presenting a peaceful facade that belies deadly defensive capabilities.2

### 

### **The Thorn (Primal Growth)**

Emphasizing primal growth, Thorn technology is wrapped in bark-plate, vine-wrapped tech, and glowing chlorophyll veins, making it rough, durable, and naturally camouflaged.3 Thorn gear grants the "Rooted" trait, providing a \+2 bonus to resist Trip or Bull Rush attempts, along with a "Camouflage" trait granting a \+2 Stealth bonus in wilderness environments.3

### 

### **The Kitin (Hive-Resin)**

Utilizing secreted hive-resin composites arranged in organic structures with green vein-piping, Kitin gear is lightweight and incredibly rigid.3 It possesses the "Hive-Link" trait, inherently integrating with telepathic hive-mind communications, and the "Lightweight" trait, meaning their armor counts as one category lighter for movement penalties.3

## **The Tangent Standard Economics**

The most significant advancement in the Equipment Matrix is the complete eradication of economic dissonance—the historically persistent fracture between the narrative abstraction of wealth and the mechanical reality of crafting and buying items.4 In legacy RPG systems, the cost to craft an item is derived from its market price, which is often assigned arbitrarily based on perceived combat utility rather than engineering complexity.4 This results in linear pricing models that fail catastrophically in science fiction settings; a starship is not merely a very large rifle, it represents an order-of-magnitude leap in manufacturing difficulty.4  
The Tangent Standard Economics posits a singular, mathematically rigorous solution: **Complexity Determines Value**.4 The market price of an item is simply the societal aggregate of the skill (Crafting DC) and time required to produce it.4

### 

### **The Tangent Standard Curve (TSC)**

Because the gap between a simple survival tool and a dimensional jump-gate is logarithmic, the cost scaling must be exponential.4 The market price of every physical asset within the Tangent galaxy is generated using the Tangent Standard Curve (TSC) formula. This formula dictates that an item's value in Credits equals a baseline value of 10 Credits (representing the simplest manufactured good or scrap metal at DC 0\) multiplied by a growth factor of 4, raised to the power of the item's Crafting DC divided by 5\.4 The growth factor of 4 ensures that value inherently quadruples across every tier interval of 5 DC.4  
By strictly adhering to this calculation, all pricing debates are eliminated. This mathematical rigidity allows Game Masters to value any new, homebrew, or precursor artifact instantly simply by assigning it a Crafting DC based on its operational complexity.4

### **Wealth Score and Purchasing Power**

Character economic power is quantified by the Wealth Score (WS), a static rating of economic leverage representing credit rating, active investments, salary, and social capital.4 The '99 \- AUGMENTATIONS MATRIX' utilizes Build Points (BP) for biological tolerance; the Equipment Matrix utilizes Wealth Score for material acquisition, translating a character's intrinsic potential (BP invested at character creation) into extrinsic economic power.4  
The integration of WS and the TSC operates on **The Golden Rule of Tangent Wealth**: A character may automatically purchase any item with a Crafting DC equal to or less than their Wealth Score without depleting liquid Credits or reducing their baseline Wealth Score.4 This aligns the abstraction of "lifestyle" with the concrete math of item acquisition: Purchase DC \= Crafting DC.4

### 

### **The Liquidity Gap and Friction Mechanics**

A common failure point in abstract wealth systems is the "infinite money loop," where players attempt to use their passive Wealth Score to acquire high-value items for free and immediately sell them for infinite cash.4 Tangent mitigates this via strict friction mechanics, ensuring "Loot" (liquid Credits) remains a vital gameplay reward at all tiers.4  
**The Liquidity Constraint (The Gap Rule):** When a character seeks an item with a DC exceeding their Wealth Score, their passive income cannot cover the transaction.4 They must bridge the gap with liquid Credits.3 The required liquid cost is calculated by subtracting the Credit value of the character's Wealth Score from the Credit value of the desired item's DC.4  
For example, if an Affluent character (WS 15, Auto-Buy Limit \~640 Cr) wishes to purchase an advanced Stealth Suit (DC 18, Value \~1,470 Cr), the character applies their WS 15 leverage to cover the base lifestyle cost, leaving a remainder of 830 Credits that must be paid in liquid cash from their adventuring savings.4  
**Liquidity Drag (The Fence Rate):** To further prevent market abuse, the economy imposes Liquidity Drag.4 In real-world economics and RPG simulations, the "Buy" price and "Sell" price are never identical due to fencing effort, market saturation, and legality.4 The Sell Price of an item is heavily reduced:

* Legal goods sell at 50% of Total Value.4  
* Black market or stolen goods sell at 20-25%.4  
* Scrap sells at 10%.4

Because the raw materials required to craft an item also cost 50% of the item's Total Value, a character who buys materials and crafts an item for immediate resale operates at a 0% profit margin.4 Profit is only possible through adventuring, scavenging materials for free, or utilizing specialized merchant skills.4

## **The Productivity Engine and Macro-Economics**

Because the Tangent Standard Curve dictates that value scales exponentially, a linear crafting system (e.g., generating 50 credits of value per day) would require literal centuries to build high-end assets, ending campaigns before items are finished.4 Tangent solves the "Time vs. Cost" paradox through the Productivity Engine, converting effort into Productivity Points (PP).4

### 

### **The Productivity Formula**

To craft an item, the creator must accumulate a Target PP equal exactly to the item's Credit Value.4 Daily progress is generated based on the crafter's skill variance and the tier of their infrastructure.4 A creator's Daily Progress in Productivity Points is calculated by taking their Craft Check Result, subtracting 10, and multiplying that number by the tool's Tier Multiplier.4  
The Tool Tier Multiplier is the critical variable that allows the timeline to scale exponentially alongside the item's value 4:

* **Tier 0 (Improvised):** x1 Multiplier. Bare hands, stone tools.4  
* **Tier 1 (Basic):** x10 Multiplier. Handheld power tools, garage kit.4  
* **Tier 2 (Advanced):** x50 Multiplier. Professional machine shop.4  
* **Tier 3 (Industrial):** x200 Multiplier. Automated factory lines.4  
* **Tier 4 (Nanoforge):** x1,000 Multiplier. Molecular assembly swarms.4  
* **Bio (Cultivation):** x1,000 Multiplier. Hyper-Growth Vats (Auluran/Kitin) where items are grown in accelerated nutrient tanks. Requires Medicine/Nature and Engineering checks.3  
* **Tier 5 (Genesis):** x5,000 Multiplier. Thought-responsive Polymatter loom, Holophotonics or Wish-level metaphysical fabrication.3

Under this engine, a Mastercraft Titan Mech Suit (DC 30, Target PP \= 40,960) would take a hobbyist with Basic Tools (x10) over 409 days to build.4 However, an advanced engineering character utilizing a Nanoforge (x1,000) with a high check result can materialize the identical Mech Suit in less than two days.4

### 

### **Macro-Scale Construction and Faction Labor Pools**

For astronomical projects like a Dreadnought (DC 50, Value \~10.5 Million Cr) or a Titanic Arcology (DC 60+, Value \~167 Million Cr), individual crafting is physically impossible.1 Factions utilize **Labor Pools** to execute macro-scale construction.4 The Daily PP output is simply the sum of individual workers.4  
A shipyard employing 1,000 engineers operating Industrial (x200) tools can generate 1,000,000 PP per day, completing a capital ship in roughly 10.5 days.4 This macro-economic logic drives factional warfare; maintaining these shipyards requires an uninterrupted flow of massive liquid capital (5.25 Million Cr in raw materials per ship), forcing factions to fight for territorial resources to keep their production engines fed.4

## **Offensive and Defensive Hardware Systems**

The Equipment Matrix provides a unified structure for the creation, classification, and modification of weapons and armor.6 Moving beyond simple item lists, the matrix defines the "DNA" of combat gear—its component parts, resource requirements, and capacity for evolution through Sockets.6

### 

### **Weapon Anatomy and Scaling Mechanics**

Every weapon in the Tangent system is defined by a standard Stat Block detailing its Core Identity (Name, Classification, Size Category, Tech Level), Combat Statistics (Damage, Range, Penetration), and Logistics (Ammunition, Bulk, Sockets, Craft DC).7  
A critical component of the Tangent combat engine is the replacement of arbitrary "type-based" multipliers with "size-based" logic, determined by the Scaling Modifier.7

* **Damage and Structure:** Weapon damage dice are strictly multiplied by the Scaling Modifier.7 A Huge creature (x5 Scaling) deals five times the base damage dice with its weapons, and its Structure Points are multiplied by five.7 Importantly, no damage is arbitrarily reduced due to a target being a larger size; larger targets simply possess proportionally increased Structure Points and Damage Reduction to absorb the impact.8  
* **Distance and Area:** Movement speed, weapon ranges, and area-of-effect parameters are multiplied by the Scaling Modifier.7  
* **Starship Proximity Damage:** At Enormous scales (x40) and above, weapons are so overwhelmingly powerful that they utilize Proximity Damage.7 Overwhelming attacks from capital ships often vaporize small targets instantly and deal 1/10th indirect damage (splash effect, debris) to small targets not directly hit, within a radius equal to half the attacker's Strength Modifier in feet.7

**Relative Combat Modifiers:** The Combat Modifier adjusts a creature's Agility, Attack, and Defense scores based on the relative size of their opponent.7 When a Large attacker targets a Small defender, the attacker suffers a significant penalty (subtracting the target's modifier from their own), reflecting the difficulty of hitting a nimble, diminutive target with massive weaponry.7 Conversely, when a Tiny attacker targets a Small defender, they gain a moderate advantage.7

### 

### **Protective Equipment and Armor Construction**

Armor serves as a destructible resource—a second skin with its own structural integrity, socket capacity, and resistance tier.6 Armor construction is defined by three primary factors 6:

* **Chassis Class (Base Matrix):** Dictates the base capacity for integrated Sockets, structural integrity (SP), and maximum dexterity.6 Classes range from Light (Clothing/Mesh, 1 Socket, 10 SP) to Heavy (Assault Shells, 4 Sockets, 40 SP) to Powered Exoskeletons (6+ Sockets, 120 SP).6  
* **Coverage (Layer Scaling):** A multiplier applied to the Chassis Class based on how much of the body is protected.6 Partial coverage (vests/greaves) halves SP and Sockets, while Bulwark coverage (excessive layering) doubles SP but halves Socket capacity and severely restricts mobility.6  
* **Materials (Tech Level):** Dictates the base Damage Resistance (DR) percentage and applies a final multiplier to the Structure Points.6 TL 1 Iron offers 50% DR and a x0.5 SP multiplier, whereas TL 4 Nanocarbon offers 125% DR and a x2.0 SP multiplier.6

Armor modules can be installed into Sockets to radically alter operational capability.6 Common modules include Ablative Foam (emergency SP restoration), Cloaking devices (+20 Stealth while stationary), Enviro-Seals (hermetic vacuum protection), and Grav-Chutes (inertial dampeners negating falling damage).6 Personal Field Generators (Shields) provide a regenerative buffer of Ablative Points (AP), requiring 2 Sockets and granting 20 AP that recharges at 5 AP per round.6

## **Meta-Tech Integration: Synthesizing Science and Sorcery**

In the Tangent Universe, the divide between science and sorcery is treated purely as a matter of origin, not effect.10 Meta-Tech represents the engineering necessary to accommodate reality-warping patterns within physical Sockets.10 This integration is governed by the Meta-Conversion capacity system, which utilizes the UDU hierarchy.10  
The type of UDU capacity used (Socket, Mount, or Module) dictates the Operational Scale of the effect, while the Power Level is determined by the Skill Rank installed during creation.10

| PHYSICAL UNIT | OPERATIONAL SCALE | CAPACITY LIMIT FOR INVOCATIONS |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| 1 Socket | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 10 (Trained Effect) |
| 2 Sockets | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 20 (Master Effect) |
| 3 Sockets | Personal Scale | Holds up to Rank 30 (Pinnacle Effect) |
| 1 Mount | Vehicle Scale | Holds any Rank up to Pinnacle (Rank 30\) |
| 1 Module | Strategic Scale | Holds any Rank up to Pinnacle (Rank 30\) |

The Principle of Scale dictates that an Invocation installed in a Mount is fundamentally more potent than the identical spell in a Socket.10 A Rank 10 Teleport installed in a personal suit (1 Socket) moves an individual 5 miles; the same Rank 10 Teleport installed in a Mecha (1 Mount) moves a 10-ton vehicle 25 miles.10

### 

### **Passive Enhancement vs. Active Imbuement**

Meta-Tech serves three distinct engineering goals:

1. **Enhancement (Passive):** The application of Matter or Energy disciplines to permanently improve the physical properties of a device.10 These effects are "Always On" and do not require an action to activate.10 Examples include an *Energy Sheath* (adding \+1d6 elemental damage to a blade), *Ghost-Strike* (allowing a weapon to phase through physical DR), or *Featherweight* (altering local gravity to make armor count as a lighter weight category).10 Each Socket allocated to an Enhancement increases the item's Crafting DC by \+5, effectively quadrupling its market value.10  
2. **Imbuement (Active):** Hard-coding a specific metaphysical Invocation (spell) into a machine, allowing a non-Awakened user to cast the effect via a physical trigger.10 The device's statistics become fixed constants based on the engineering quality.10 The Crafting DC for an Imbuement is calculated as 15 plus the Invocation Rank plus the Tech Level Modifier.10 A "Blink Belt" (TL 4\) imbued with a limited Rank 10 Teleport occupies 1 Socket and possesses a Craft DC of 20\.10  
3. **Interface (Symbiotic):** Devices that amplify the user's natural metaphysical skills.10 When an Awakened user channels through an Amplifier (such as an Elemental Focus Cannon taking up 1 Mount), the Invocation's output is multiplied by the Chassis Scale.10 A spell cast through a Huge Mecha (x5 Scale) deals five times the damage and expands its area of effect proportionately.8

Meta-Tech fabrication requires highly specialized conductive materials—such as Aetherium for active Imbuements or Resonance Crystals for passive Enhancements—which are extremely rare and always constitute 50% of the item's exorbitant final value.10

## **Specialized Gear and Utility Ecosystems**

Beyond combat and architecture, the Equipment Matrix codifies the rules for exploration, survival, and computational problem-solving, ensuring that all gear interfaces seamlessly with core gameplay loops.3

### 

### **Advanced Grown Technologies (Biotech Protocols)**

Factions utilizing grown biotechnology subvert traditional battery mechanics, requiring distinct operational rules.2 Grown tech is subject to three core protocols 3:

1. **Symbiosis (Bonding):** Living gear must genetically and bio-electrically "bond" with a host over a 24-hour attunement period. Failure to attune results in a persistent \-2 operational penalty across all checks.3  
2. **Sustenance (Feeding):** Grown technology cannot be recharged via a power grid; it requires nutrient consumption.3 Minor items (Diminutive/Tiny size) feed harmlessly on the user's waste heat and dead skin flakes.3 Heavy weapons and armor must be fully submerged in a Nutrient Solution (costing 10 Cr per dose) once per week, lest they degrade and gain the "Broken" (Starving) condition.3  
3. **Bio-Repair:** Damage to living gear is healed via Medicine or Xenobiology checks rather than Engineering.3 A healthy host naturally facilitates the automatic regeneration of 1 Structure Point per hour for their bonded equipment.3

**Specific Factional Innovations:**

1. **Empathic Carapace (Auluran):** Reactive armor that adapts when struck by energy (Fire/Cold/Elec), granting DR \+5 against that specific energy type for the remainder of the Scene.3  
2. **Chorist Node (Auluran):** A grown neural-graft transceiver. Allows Telepathy (1 mile radius) with other Node users and grants Advantage on Empathy checks.3  
3. **Sunglass Laminates (Alterian):** Crystalline structures grown from silica and light. Acts as a solar capacitor, recharging energy weapons automatically in sunlight.3  
4. **Hive-Resin (Kitin):** A secreted construction material lighter than plasteel but self-sealing. Structures grant \+2 to Stealth (sound dampening) inside them.3

### 

### **Programmable Matter and Utility Devices**

Beyond the organic growth of the Auluran, high-tech factions utilize advanced material science for everyday utility.3 Programmable Matter, often referred to as "Picotech" (TL 4, DC 25), consists of a canister of smart sand or fluid metal that can be commanded to reshape into any Simple tool, requiring user instruction to shift forms.3 Visual utility is provided by Image Projectors, which scale from TL 2 (DC 5\) units projecting 2D images with sound, up to TL 3 (DC 10\) Holo Emitters capable of projecting localized 3D holograms.3

### 

### **Computational Assets and Software Systems**

Computers and digital decks are defined by their Processor Rating (PR).3 A Basic Processor (TL 3, DC 10\) operates at PR 0, capable only of simple calculations and data storage.3 Advanced Processors (TL 5, DC 25\) reach PR 4 and can host robust Persona AIs, possessing pseudo-intelligence and a Charisma score equal to their PR.3  
Computers run specialized software to aid users.3 **Expert Software** (ranging from DC 10 to DC 30\) grants a direct \+1 to \+5 Aid Bonus to specific Skill checks.3 **Operation Software** allows a user to automatically "Take 10" on specific skill checks when the software is active, critically reducing the risk of catastrophic failure during routine high-stakes operations, such as orbital astrogation or systemic hacking.3

### 

### **Surveillance and Communications**

Information gathering and transmission are categorized into three distinct operational spheres.3 Passive Sensors range from simple TL 2 (DC 5\) Rangefinders and Cameras to TL 3 (DC 10\) Holo-Cams.3 Comm Units dictate connection scale: Audio/Visual radios (TL 2, DC 5\) handle standard comms, Holographic transceivers (TL 3, DC 10\) project 3D representations, and Telepathic arrays (TL 4/5, DC 25\) allow for planetary-scale communication requiring the Telepathy skill.3 To protect this data, Counter-Intel gear includes TL 2 (DC 10\) Scrambler Units and advanced TL 4 (DC 20\) Encryption Suites.3

### 

### **Vocation Tools and Facilities**

Vocation tools provide direct Equipment Bonuses to relevant skill checks, scaling rapidly with the facility's volumetric footprint and the number of users supported.3

* **Basic Tools:** A portable belt or pack (DC 5\) that requires Trained skill but offers no intrinsic bonus, supporting 1 user.3  
* **Full Set:** A semi-portable caddy (DC 10\) requiring Trained skill, granting a \+2 Equipment Bonus and supporting 2 users.3  
* **Shop/Lab:** A dedicated room (DC 20\) requiring Expert skill, granting a \+4 Equipment Bonus and supporting 5 users.3  
* **Facility:** A building or campus (DC 30\) requiring Master skill, granting a \+6 Equipment Bonus and supporting 10 or more users.3

Specific tool systems integrate directly into this hierarchy.3 Diagnostics Systems and Databases grant scaling bonuses (+2 at TL 3, \+4 at TL 4, and \+6 at TL 5\) to Repair and Knowledge checks respectively.3 Multi-Tools replicate non-powered tools at TL 3 (DC 10), powered tools at TL 4 (DC 15, \+2 Bonus), and become fully polymorphic at TL 5 (DC 25, \+4 Bonus).3  
Medical tools follow these exact scaling rules. A Field Surgery Pack acts as a basic kit (Tiny, DC 10\) granting a \+2 Equipment Bonus to Medicine checks. A Portable Nurse is a TL 3 unit running Expert Software that acts as an automated secondary aid. An Autodoc is a TL 4 (DC 20\) fully autonomous robotic surgeon capable of running Operation Software independently. **Operating Theaters** are Medium-sized room installations (DC 20\) granting a \+4 Equipment Bonus. Finally, a Medical Lab requires a Facility-sized architectural Module (DC 30), granting a \+6 Equipment Bonus and capable of supporting multiple trauma patients simultaneously.

### 

### **Environmental and Survival Gear**

Exploration equipment interfaces directly with the core survival mechanics, specifically the degradation and restoration of the Supply Die.3 Advanced purifiers and survival canteens allow characters to actively restore degraded Supply Dice in the field without returning to civilization.3  
To mitigate extreme climates, explorers utilize **Enviro Fields**, a TL 3 (DC 15\) deployable generator that establishes a 20ft radius of Environmental Protection Rating (EPR) 1, shielding occupants from hostile weather and extreme temperatures.3 For vertical traversal, climbing gear scales significantly by era: Standard gear (TL 2, DC 5\) relies on ropes and pitons, Improved gear (TL 3, DC 10\) grants a \+1 bonus per Athletics skill stage, and Electro-Adhesive grips (TL 4, DC 15\) allow a user to automatically Take 10 on climb checks.3 At the apex of traversal technology, **Grav Attenuators**, a TL 5 (DC 25\) mobility device, grant a user Advantage on all climbing and traversal checks by entirely dampening localized gravity.3

## **Equipment Modification and Mastercrafting**

Equipment in Tangent is inherently modular, capable of evolving alongside the character through the utilization of Sockets.7 Weapons, armor, and utility gear possess a baseline number of Sockets derived from their Size Category (e.g., a Tiny pistol holds 1 Socket, a Medium rifle holds 4 Sockets, a Large heavy weapon holds 6).7  
A Socket allows for the installation of optics, barrel attachments, biometric locks, or payload alterations.7 Installing a Sniper Scope consumes 1 Socket and increases Range Increment by 50% while penalizing short-range strikes.7 Installing an Under-barrel Grenade launcher consumes 2 Sockets.7

* **Standard Upgrades:** Each mundane module costs its listed Socket amount and increases the Crafting DC by \+5 (or \+10 for TL4/TL5 items).  
* **Mastercrafting (Bonus Sockets):** High-quality manufacturing allows armor to exceed standard Socket limits. For every 5 points an Acquisition or Crafting check exceeds the item's base DC, the item gains 1 Bonus Socket (up to a maximum of 1 per TL of the item).  
* **Socket Compression:** If a build requires more capacity, advanced engineering can compress the hardware at a severe premium.  
  * *Efficient Design:* \-25% Socket cost (Round up). Adds \+5 DC.  
  * *Miniaturized:* \-50% Socket cost (Round up). Requires Tech Level \+1 over item base. Adds \+10 DC.  
  * *Integrated:* 0 Sockets (Fused to chassis). Cannot be removed. Adds \+5 DC.

**Meta-Tech Integration (Synthesizing Science and Sorcery)**  
Meta-Tech represents the engineering necessary to accommodate reality-warping patterns within physical Sockets. The type of UDU capacity used dictates the Operational Scale, while the Power Level is determined by the Skill Rank installed during creation.

* **1 Socket:** Holds up to Rank 10 (Trained Effect).  
* **2 Sockets:** Holds up to Rank 20 (Master Effect).  
* **3 Sockets:** Holds up to Rank 30 (Pinnacle Effect).

Meta-Tech fabrication requires highly specialized conductive materials (e.g., Aetherium or Resonance Crystals), which are extremely rare and always constitute 50% of the item's exorbitant final value.

* **Enhancement (Passive):** Permanently improves the physical properties of the armor (e.g., *Ghost-Strike* phasing, or *Featherweight* gravity alteration). These are "Always On." Each Socket allocated to an Enhancement increases the item's Crafting DC by \+5.  
* **Imbuement (Active):** Hard-coding a specific metaphysical Invocation into the armor, allowing a non-Awakened user to cast the effect via a physical trigger. The Crafting DC is calculated as: **15 \+ Invocation Rank \+ TL Modifier** *(TL3: \+0, TL4: \+2, TL5: \+5)*.  
  *(Example: A "Blink Suit" imbued with a Rank 10 Teleport at TL4 occupies 1 Socket and possesses a Craft DC of 27).*  
* **Consumable Modifier:** If a Meta-Tech module is designed for a single use (e.g., a one-time emergency planar shift that burns out the crystals), the final Meta-Tech DC is reduced by 10\.  
* **Interface (Symbiotic):** Devices that amplify an Awakened user's natural skills.

## **Conclusion: The Synthesized Material Reality**

The Tangent Equipment Matrix resolves the chaos of standard RPG inventory management and the historical failures of linear game economies by replacing arbitrary item lists with a highly scalable, mathematically rigorous taxonomy.3 By anchoring every physical asset—from the lowest-tier scavenger's blade to a highly synchronized Mekan Polymatter array—to the Universal Displacement Unit (Sockets) and the exponential value scaling of the Tangent Standard Curve, the matrix creates a seamless continuity of physics, logistics, and economics.3  
Through the explicit division of internal biological alterations (Scale 0/Nodes) and external material acquisition (Scale 1/Sockets), both players and game designers possess a definitive, irrefutable source of truth.3 An item's Tech Level, Material Composition, Manufacturer Skin, and Meta-Tech Sockets are not mere flavor text; they are explicit mechanical vectors that profoundly dictate the item's utility, its acquisition path, and its narrative footprint within the galaxy.3 This matrix ensures that the material reality of the Tangent universe is as robust, adaptable, and meticulously engineered as the technology it seeks to simulate.
