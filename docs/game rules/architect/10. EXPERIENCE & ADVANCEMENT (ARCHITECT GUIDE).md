# **10. EXPERIENCE & ADVANCEMENT (ARCHITECT GUIDE)**

# **THE ARCHITECT'S GUIDE TO EXPERIENCE & CHARACTER PROGRESSION**

As the Architect of a Tangent Science Fantasy campaign, managing character growth is one of your most effective narrative and mechanical tools. Progression in Tangent is organic, non-linear, and directly tied to the actions, ingenuity, and roleplaying choices of your players.

This guide provides the Architect with pacing models, award assessment checklists, downtime adjudication, and guidance on maintaining mechanical equilibrium as operative power increases.

---

## **I. THE THREE PACING MODELS**

Depending on the genre, lethality, and narrative scope of your campaign, choose one of the following progression paces:

| Campaign Style | Session Award (Per Player) | Story Arc / Chapter Award | Progression Feel | Best Fit Genre |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Gritty / Survival** | **0 to 1 AP** | **3 to 5 AP** | Slow, hard-won growth. Every point counts. | Low-tech survival, horror, deep-space isolation. |
| **Standard / Heroic** | **1 to 3 AP** | **5 to 8 AP** | Steady, balanced advancement across attributes & skills. | Space opera, syndicate intrigue, planetary exploration. |
| **Cinematic / High-Powered**| **2 to 4 AP** | **8 to 10 AP** | Rapid evolution into sector-renowned legends. | Epic military sci-fi, high-order metaphysics, mecha warfare. |

---

## **II. POST-SESSION AWARD CHECKLIST**

At the conclusion of each session (or immediately prior to the next session during your recap), walk through this simple two-question rubric for each player:

### **1. Tactical & Mechanical Engagement (0 to 2 AP)**
* Did the player actively engage with the game mechanics, combat tactics, or environmental puzzles?
* Did they work collaboratively with the team or display strategic leadership?
  * **0 AP:** Inactive or distracted throughout the session.
  * **1 AP:** Solid participation, executed turns effectively, supported the team.
  * **2 AP:** Outstanding tactical thinking, innovative use of skills or equipment, led or rescued allies.

### **2. Roleplaying in Character (0 to 2 AP)**
* Did the player embody their character's distinct personality, speech, quirks, and convictions?
* Did they lean into their character's Hindrances or personal stakes, even when suboptimal?
  * **0 AP:** Pure mechanical play with no roleplaying presence.
  * **1 AP:** Maintained character consistency, made decisions in-character.
  * **2 AP:** Delivered memorable narrative scenes, provoked meaningful table banter, or made profound character-defining choices.

## **III. ADJUDICATING CHAPTER & STORY AWARDS**

Story Awards represent major narrative milestones. They are granted to the entire party upon concluding a distinct narrative arc or chapter:

### **Chapter Completion (5 to 10 AP)**
* **Short Chapter (5-6 AP):** 1 to 2 sessions covering a singular objective (e.g., escaping a compromised orbital station).
* **Medium Chapter (7-8 AP):** 3 to 5 sessions with multiple branching encounters, investigation, and climax (e.g., investigating a syndicate conspiracy across multiple worlds).
* **Epic Chapter / Milestone (9-10 AP):** 6+ sessions culminating in a sector-altering confrontation (e.g., halting a planetary extinction event or defeating an ancient entity).

### **Worthy Goals, Villains & Plots (1 to 3 AP)**
* **Minor Goal or Lieutenant (1 AP):** Overcoming a formidable lieutenant, bypassing complex security grids, or cracking a crucial code.
* **Significant Threat (2 AP):** Defeating a recurrent antagonist, neutralizing an apex bioweapon, or mediating an armed conflict.
* **Nemesis / Campaign Climax (3 AP):** Defeating the primary architect of a major villainous scheme or unraveling a galaxy-spanning plot.

---

## **IV. AD HOC EPIC AWARDS & STUMPING THE ARCHITECT**

Occasionally, a player will pull off a maneuver that completely upends the scenario, outsmarts your preparations, or provides an unforgettable cinematic triumph.

* **Award Range:** **1 to 5 AP** (awarded immediately when the dust settles).
* **Guiding Principle:** Reward genuine lateral thinking and creative problem-solving—not merely lucky dice rolls.
* **The "Stumping the Architect" Clause:** If a player devises a strategy so clever and grounded in the game's physics/lore that you have to pause, laugh, and figure out how to rule it—reward them with **3 to 5 AP**. It acknowledges their contribution to the shared fiction.

## **V. ENFORCING THE INCREMENT RULE**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Increment Rule:**
> *Abilities, skills, or traits may only have a 1-point increment of any score per experience award.*

### **Why This Rule Matters to the Architect:**
1. **Prevents Specialization Runaway:** Without this rule, a player receiving an 8 AP chapter reward could immediately jump a combat skill by 8 ranks, breaking combat encounter math.
2. **Encourages Well-Rounded Operatives:** Players are naturally encouraged to branch into secondary skills, attribute checks, and defensive vitals.
3. **Narrative Justification:** Genuine mastery takes time. Jumping 1 rank per award mirrors training and reflection.

### **How to Arbitrate Spending:**
* If a player receives **7 AP**, they can spend:
  * 1 AP for +1 to Alertness
  * 1 AP for +1 to Reflex Check
  * 1 AP for +1 to Melee Combat
  * 3 AP for 1 new Feature
  * 1 AP for +5 Vitality
  *(Each distinct dimension receives at most a +1 increment).*
* Remaining points can be held in reserve as **Unspent AP** on the character sheet.

## **VI. ENCOUNTER BALANCING WITH AP**

In the Tangent system, non-player adversaries and monsters are designed directly on the **Build Point (BP)** economy (see *05. BESTIARY*). 

Because **1 AP = 1 BP**, tracking party power is mathematically transparent:
* **Starting Operative:** 150 BP equivalent.
* **Veteran Operative (+25 AP):** 175 BP equivalent.
* **Heroic Operative (+50 AP):** 200 BP equivalent.
* **Legendary Operative (+100 AP):** 250 BP equivalent.

When crafting encounters for a veteran party:
1. Increase adversary BP budget proportionally by adding tactical skills, cyberware nodes, or higher attribute check scores.
2. Introduce adversaries with complementary counter-tactics rather than simply inflating enemy Health pools.
3. Use the Bestiary's Elite and Swarm templates to challenge higher-AP parties dynamically.

---

## **VII. MANAGING REVIVIFICATION & EXPERIENCE DEBT**

When a character dies and is revivified (via TL5 medicine or high-level Metaphysics):
* The character resets to **0 Karma**.
* The character takes **-5 Experience Debt**.

### **Architect Best Practices for Debt:**
* Remind the player of the existential weight of surviving death. The trauma should be reflected in roleplay.
* When experience is next awarded, the player has the choice to dedicate their earned AP directly toward eliminating the debt.
* If a player wants to advance their skills immediately without paying the debt, they must accept a permanent or semi-permanent -1 reduction in another trait to balance the psychic/biological deficit.
