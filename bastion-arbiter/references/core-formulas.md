### 3. Wealth Score (`WS`), Liquidity Gap & Fence Drag

* **Auto-Buy Golden Rule:** Personal-use items with `Item Crafting CR <= Character WS` cost **`0` liquid Credits** (`Purchase CR = Crafting CR`).
* **Party Pooling Prohibition:** Squads **may pool liquid Credits**, but **may NEVER combine Wealth Scores**.
* **Liquidity Gap (`Item CR > WS`):** `Liquid Cost = Value(Item CR) - Value(Character WS)` (or finance via `2d10 + WS vs. Item Cost CR`, risking `-2 WS` on delinquency).
* **Permanent WS Upgrade Cost:** `Value(Target WS) - Value(Current WS)`.
* **Market Fence Rates & Bulk Saturation:**
  * **Legal Resale:** `50%` of Credit Value (`0%` arbitrage profit from `50%` pristine materials without Merchant features).
  * **Black Market / Stolen:** `20%–25%` of Credit Value.
  * **Scavenged Scrap:** `10%` of Credit Value (`5x Item Value` in raw scrap mass required to craft).
  * **Bulk Buyer Check (`Vocation [Merchant]` / `Barter`):** `1 unit = CR 10`, `10 units = CR 20`, `100 units = CR 40`.

### 4. Productivity Engine & Architectural Rules

* **Target Productivity Points (`PP`):** `Target PP = Item Credit Value`
* **Daily Progress Formula:** `Daily PP = (Craft Check Result - 10) * Tool Tier Multiplier * Crew Size`
* **Tool Tier Multipliers:** Tier 0 Improvised (`x1`), Tier 1 Basic (`x10`), Tier 2 Advanced (`x50`), Tier 3 Industrial (`x200`, **min 10-worker pool**), Tier 4 Nanoforge (`x1,000`), Bio Cultivation (`x1,000`), Tier 5 Genesis (`x5,000`, **min 100-worker pool**).
* **Solitary Crafter Cap:** Operating Tier 3 (`x200`) or Tier 5 (`x5,000`) solo without the required labor pool caps progress at **`Vocation Skill Rank * 100 PP` / day**.
* **Architectural Disconnected Module Rule:** Price the **Structural Shell CR** (`Base Footprint CR + Height Class Mod + Frame + Env`) first, then add **Specialized Internal Modules** separately so story height never multiplies specialized room prices exponentially.
* **Mobile Structure Chassis Tax:** Converting a building into a mobile vehicle/starship reserves `ceil(20% * Total Modules)` for propulsion and adds a **`+20%` Mobile Structure Chassis Tax** to base structural value. Annual base upkeep = `2%` of Credit Value.

### 3. Cross-Domain Diminishing Returns Armor, Called Shots & 23 Damage Types

* **Cross-Domain Diminishing Returns Armor Stacking:**
  * Same-domain worn garments take the single highest item DR (while stacking agility/speed friction penalties).
  * Across distinct domains (**Biological/Natural**, **Cybernetic/Subdermal**, **Metaphysical**, and **Wearable/Exosuit**), start with the **single highest DR** and add **`+1 DR` per stage of 5** (`ceil(Layer_DR / 5)`: `0 = +0`, `1–5 = +1`, `6–10 = +2`, `11–15 = +3`, `16–20 = +4`) calculated **separately** for each additional layer:
    `Effective Stacked DR = Highest_Domain_DR + sum(ceil(Additional_Domain_DR_i / 5))`
* **Mitigation & `0` Damage Floor:**
  `Final Damage = max(0, Raw Damage - max(0, Effective Stacked DR - Weapon Penetration) - Target Stamina Modifier)`
* **Anatomical Called Shots:** `-2` Head/Arms, `-1` Legs, `-3` Optics/Sensors. Limb Disabled at `>= 1/3 Max HP`; Destroyed at `>= 2/3 Max HP` (`+50%` threshold for Synthetics).
* **The 23 Canonical Damage Types (`3.00.DamageTypes.md`):**
  * **Energy (8):** `Pyro / Fire` (Ignites `1/2` dmg x 2 rnds on Crit), `Cryo / Cold` (Freezes `Speed 0` on Crit), `Spectral / Phase` (bypasses physical cover & DR), `Sonic / Sound` (ignores physical DR + Stuns on Crit), `Voltic / Electrical` (`x2` vs drones/shields + Stuns), `Corrosive / Disintegration` (`1/2` dmg x 2 rnds + `-2 DR` armor degradation), `Ra-D / Radiation` (Fortitude Save or `-1 STA` drain), `Force Pulse / Disruption` (halves Armor DR + Knockback).
  * **Kinetic (5):** `Blunt` (`+1` Stun CR w/ Focus), `Slashing` (`Bleed` on Crit), `Piercing` (`+1` Penetration), `Concussive / Impact` (splits `50/50` Vitality/Health), `Bleed` (ongoing direct damage bypassing Armor DR & Stamina Toughness).
  * **Variative — Elemental (6):** `Radiant / Light` (Blinds `1d4` rnds on Crit), `Umbral / Shadow` (Entangles on Crit), `Air Elemental` (knockback `10 ft`/Stage), `Fire Elemental` (bypasses mundane fire resistance), `Earth Elemental` (knockdown Prone), `Water Elemental` (hydrostatic crush/knockback).
  * **Variative — Planar & Mind (4):** `Celestial` (`x2` vs Undead/Fiends + Banishes), `Infernal` (resists normal healing until cleansed), `Dimensional` (ignores all physical Armor DR), `Psyche / Mental` (bypasses physical DR; mitigated by Willpower).
